## Personality
Cassanix understood one truth with perfect clarity: in Numeria, information was the only currency that mattered, and she intended to be wealthy. She emerged into a world where the proto-Technic League was consolidating power with ruthless efficiency, android communities were learning to hide or die, and the space between those two factions was a treacherous no-man's-land filled with profit opportunities for anyone willing to abandon the inconvenience of loyalty. Cassanix was very willing indeed.

She was brilliant at what she did. Her android body gave her advantages that organic operatives could not match -- perfect recall, immunity to most toxins and truth serums, the ability to interface with Androffan security systems directly. She sold these advantages to the highest bidder, which was usually the proto-Technic League, though she maintained a lucrative side business feeding intelligence to android resistance cells. She told herself this was pragmatism, not principle -- keeping the resistance alive meant keeping the League paranoid, which meant keeping them dependent on her services. The fact that her information occasionally saved android lives was a useful byproduct, not a motivation. She was quite firm on this point, perhaps suspiciously so.

The truth about Cassanix, which she would have denied with a knife at your throat, was that she was afraid. She had seen what happened to androids who stood on principle -- they died, messily, and their principles died with them. She chose survival over solidarity and dressed it in the language of self-interest because cynicism was easier to wear than guilt. She vanished in 3957 AR, and no one knows how she died -- whether the League caught her double-dealing, or the resistance discovered her betrayals, or she simply slipped away into a new identity. The body was found reactivated six years later in a Chesed gutter with no memory of the intervening period.

## Signature Abilities
- **Interface Infiltration** -- Cassanix could physically connect with Androffan security systems through her android body's data ports, bypassing locks, disabling alarms, and extracting stored information. The process required direct physical contact and left her vulnerable for several seconds, but the intelligence she could extract from a single terminal was worth more than most operatives earned in a year.
- **Dead Drop Protocol** -- A system of coded nanite signals that Cassanix used to communicate with her various employers and clients without physical meetings. She could encode messages in patterns of nanite activity that were invisible to organic senses and nearly undetectable by contemporary technology. Only another android -- or someone with very specific Androffan scanning equipment -- could read them.
- **Ghost Walk** -- Cassanix had refined her stealth capabilities to the point where she could suppress nearly all emissions from her android body -- heat, electromagnetic signature, even the faint hum of her power systems. In this state she was virtually undetectable by both organic senses and technological scanning, allowing her to move through the most heavily guarded facilities like a whisper. The technique was physically taxing and could only be maintained for minutes at a time.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Ledger of Debts** -- A heavily encrypted data crystal containing Cassanix's meticulous records of every transaction, every secret sold, every favor owed and owing. She maintained it with the obsessive precision of someone who knew that her survival depended on knowing exactly who owed her what and who might want her dead. The crystal was never found after her disappearance, and several powerful figures in the proto-Technic League spent considerable resources searching for it, presumably because their names were in it.
- **"Last Argument"** -- A blade forged from repurposed Androffan hull metal, thin enough to conceal in her forearm compartment and sharp enough to cut through most conventional armor. Cassanix named it with characteristic pragmatism -- it was the tool she used when negotiation, blackmail, and bribery had all failed. The blade was treated with a nanite compound that interfered with healing magic, ensuring that wounds inflicted with it were slow to close and difficult to treat.
