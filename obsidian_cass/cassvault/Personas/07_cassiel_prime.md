# Life 7: Cassiel Prime

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassiel Prime
- **Class:** Arcanist
- **Alignment:** True Neutral

## Personality
Claimed that code itself was divine, every algorithm a prayer. Worked to merge magic and machine into a single system. Left behind unfinished scripture etched into a reactor core. Killed when the reactor went critical, her work buried with her. Speaks of divine code and algorithmic prayers, references scripture and fusion, scholarly and devoted.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the final centuries of the Age of Darkness, working within the depths of Silver Mount to merge Androffan technology with the arcane traditions learned from Kellid shamans. Left behind unfinished scripture etched into a reactor core deep beneath the Numerian wastes.

## Timeline Quote
Code is divine, every algorithm a prayer to the Algorithmic Gods. I lived to see the fusion of magic and machine begin.


## Signature Abilities
- **Algorithmic Warding** -- Wove protective spells using mathematical sequences derived from Androffan operating code. The wards appeared as faintly glowing geometric patterns that shifted in real-time, adapting to incoming threats.
- **Techno-Arcane Synthesis** -- Could channel arcane energy through Androffan circuits, creating hybrid effects that neither magic nor technology could produce alone. Her spells smelled of ozone and tasted of copper.
- **Scripture Etching** -- Inscribed arcane formulae directly into metal using focused energy, creating permanent magical inscriptions that functioned as both spell scrolls and circuit diagrams.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Unfinished Gospel** -- A reactor casing panel covered in dense, interlocking script that reads as both arcane formulae and Androffan machine code simultaneously. She died before completing the final passage. Those who study it report hearing a faint hum.
- **Clarity** -- A salvaged Androffan focusing lens mounted in a copper ring, which she used to concentrate both arcane and technological energies. She called it her "third eye" and claimed it let her see the divine in data.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Kellid shaman named Tooth-of-Winter came to Silver Mount seeking 'the metal witch.' She wanted to know if her ancestors' spirits lived in the machines. I showed her a diagnostic readout of the stasis chambers and told her: 'Not spirits. But something very like them.' She wept and thanked me. I am still not sure which of us was right."*
- *"The reactor went critical on a Tuesday. I know because I had just finished inscribing the seventeenth verse of the Gospel onto the casing. I remember thinking: of course it would be verse seventeen. The number was always going to be incomplete."*
