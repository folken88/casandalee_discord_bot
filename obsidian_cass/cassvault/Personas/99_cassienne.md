# Life 99: Cassienne

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassienne
- **Class:** Skald
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Life Number:** 99
- **Birth Year:** 3504
- **Death Year:** 3537

## Personality
Cassienne was a liar, a thief, and the most honest person the android body ever housed -- because she never once pretended to be anything else. She woke into a world that was already telling stories about her, or rather about the "android with many souls," and she found the stories laughably wrong. Too clean, too noble, too symmetrical. The real history of the body was messy and contradictory -- saints followed by monsters, heroes followed by hermits -- and the folklore had sanded all the edges off. Cassienne decided that if the world was going to tell her story wrong, she might as well be the one telling it. So she picked up a drum, tuned her voice to carry across a Kellid campfire, and became the definitive chronicler of her own mythology. The fact that she made half of it up was, in her view, beside the point.

She traveled the length of Numeria for three decades, performing in every settlement from Torch to Starfall. She was a magnificent performer -- her voice could shift from a whisper that silenced a crowded tavern to a battle-cry that made warriors reach for their swords. She told tales of the body's past lives with such conviction that even she sometimes forgot which parts she had invented. Life 14 became a dragon-slayer. Life 37 became a lover of a goddess. Life 72 became a trickster who outwitted Brigh herself. The real versions of those lives were far more mundane, but Cassienne understood something fundamental about truth: people do not remember what happened. They remember what they felt when they were told what happened. And she made them feel wonder.

The danger of being a mythmaker is that myths have a way of growing beyond their maker. By the time Cassienne was in her thirties, her embellished stories had taken on lives of their own. Kellid shamans cited them as sacred oral history. Tech-cult scholars debated their accuracy as though they were primary sources. And Cassienne, who had started as a performer, found herself becoming a prophet against her will -- the very thing Life 95 had been on purpose. The irony was not lost on her. She died on the road between Chesed and Hajoth Hakados, ambushed by bandits who had heard that the "storytelling android" carried treasures from a hundred lifetimes. She carried nothing but a drum and a head full of stories, but they killed her anyway. Her last words, according to the caravan guard who found her, were a punchline to a joke no one else had heard.

## Timeline Quote
I never lied about who we were. I lied about what happened to us, which is an entirely different sin. The truth of a hundred lives is too dull for a campfire. So I gave them fire instead.

## Signature Abilities
- **The Raging Verse** -- Cassienne's skald song could whip listeners into a frenzy not through magic alone but through sheer narrative momentum. She would begin a tale slowly, building tension until her audience was leaning forward in their seats, then unleash the climax with a shout that carried genuine arcane power. Allies caught in the verse fought with reckless joy, as though they were characters in one of her stories.
- **Stolen Memory** -- By drawing on fragmented impressions from the body's past lives, Cassienne could improvise tales with such specificity that listeners believed she had personally witnessed events centuries before her birth. This was not true recall but a blend of genuine memory echoes and her own extraordinary talent for extrapolation. Even those who knew she was fabricating found themselves unable to identify which details were real.
- **The Revision** -- Cassienne could retroactively alter the "narrative" of a recent event through a combination of bardic magic and sheer charismatic force. A failed negotiation became a calculated gambit. A clumsy retreat became a tactical withdrawal. She could not change what had happened, but she could change how everyone present remembered feeling about it -- and in practice, that amounted to the same thing.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Drum of Echoes** -- A Kellid war drum with a head made from the hide of a Numerian auroch, given to Cassienne by a shaman who said it "already knew her songs." The drum had an unusual resonance -- when struck, it produced a faint harmonic undertone that seemed to shift depending on which story Cassienne was telling, as though it were accompanying her from memory.
- **The Apocrypha** -- A leather-bound book in which Cassienne kept her "true" notes -- the actual, unembellished memories she could access from past lives, alongside annotations on which parts she had altered for her performances. It was the one honest thing she owned, and she guarded it with her life. It was not found on her body after her death and has never been recovered.
