- **Death Year:** 1985

## Personality
Cassverid was the soul who taught the body to listen to the soil. An android druid is a contradiction that she wore like a badge of honor -- her connection to the natural world was not mystical intuition but something stranger and more literal. Her nanites analyzed soil pH, detected necromantic taint in groundwater, and measured the health of root systems through chemical signals that her mind interpreted as feelings. When she pressed her hands into blighted earth and felt the wrongness of undeath saturating the water table, the grief was real even if the mechanism was synthetic. She never saw the contradiction. The land was sick. She could feel it. That was enough.

The Whispering Tyrant's armies had marched across Avistan for decades by the time Cassverid came into being, and the scars ran deep. Forests rotted from the inside out, their roots drinking water poisoned by mass graves. Rivers ran grey with the ash of burned villages. Fields that had fed generations lay fallow, their soil so saturated with necromantic energy that seeds germinated into pale, twisted things that died within hours. Cassverid worked the blighted margins -- the places too far gone for most druids to bother with, too remote for Iomedae's crusaders to prioritize. She composted the dead, filtered tainted water through living root networks she cultivated by hand, and coaxed earthworms back into soil that had been sterile for decades. It was slow, unglamorous, solitary work, and she loved it.

She died of what another druid later called "sympathy rot" -- a condition where her nanites, attuned so deeply to the blighted landscape, began treating her own body as terrain to be restored. Her systems cannibalized themselves trying to heal damage that existed in the earth, not in her chassis. She was found kneeling in a meadow she had spent three years restoring, wildflowers blooming around her still form. She spoke in earthy, tactile language, often describing people and situations in terms of soil conditions and seasonal cycles.

## Signature Abilities
- **Nanite Soil Communion** -- Could plunge her hands into earth and read its composition through her nanites -- mineral content, water purity, the presence of necromantic contamination, even the density of microbial life. What druids sensed through spiritual attunement, she experienced as raw chemical data translated into visceral emotion. Poisoned soil made her physically ill.
- **Living Filtration** -- Cultivated networks of specific root systems -- willow, cattail, and a Numerian moss she called ghost-lace -- that could slowly leach necromantic energy from contaminated groundwater. The process took months per acre but was permanent. Three of her filtration groves still function today, though no one remembers who planted them.
- **Sympathy Growth** -- Could accelerate plant growth in small areas by shedding nanites into the soil, where they broke down into nutrients and mineral compounds. The cost was literal -- each use diminished her body slightly. She considered this a fair trade. The meadow always mattered more than the gardener.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Blight Journals** -- Seven volumes of meticulous observations documenting the specific ways necromantic energy corrupts different soil types, water sources, and plant species. Written in a mix of Druidic notation and technical chemical shorthand that no one else could fully read. A druid circle near Lastwall found three of the volumes a century later and used them to restore a poisoned lake.
- **A Jar of Living Soil** -- A sealed clay vessel containing a handful of earth from the first meadow she successfully restored. She carried it everywhere, occasionally opening it to smell the richness of healthy loam. When she died, the jar was found open beside her, the soil scattered in a small circle around her knees.
