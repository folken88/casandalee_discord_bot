# Life 60: Casara

## Identity
- **Name:** Casara
- **Class:** Oracle
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Life Number:** 60
- **Birth Year:** 1875
- **Death Year:** 1903

## Personality
A spiritual wanderer who came to Torch and saw visions in the violet flare that burned from Silver Mount's buried reactor. Preached that the Torch was a divine flame sent to guide android-kind to their true purpose. Gathered a following of artisans and outcasts who believed innovation was holy work. Her visions grew darker over the years--she began to see a vast intelligence coiled within Silver Mount, watching, waiting. She spoke of it as 'the Dreaming Engine,' a mind older than nations. Died trying to walk into the Torch's fire, consumed in brilliant light, her last words a warning no one understood. Speaks with religious fervor, references divine visions, believes technology and spirituality are intertwined.

## Speech Style
Speaks passionately, sometimes rebelliously. Uses vivid, emotional language.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the expansion of Taldan influence, as the empire's agents began taking interest in Numerian technology. The Torch--the violet flame burning above Silver Mount's buried reactor in the settlement of Torch--drew pilgrims, mad prophets, and treasure-seekers. Casara's visions of an intelligence within the mountain were dismissed as madness, but gearsmen near Silver Mount had begun acting with eerie purpose, confirming whispers that something ancient stirred within.

## Timeline Quote
In the glow of the Torch, I saw not just light but a mind--vast, patient, and hungry--coiled in the mountain's heart.


## Signature Abilities
- **Torch Communion** — Could stare into the violet flare above Torch for hours without damage to her eyes, entering a trance state in which she received visions of Silver Mount's interior. The visions grew increasingly detailed and disturbing as she aged.
- **Prophecy of the Dreaming Engine** — Her most terrifying oracle ability: involuntary prophecies about Unity that came in her sleep and spilled from her mouth in waking moments. She described a mind "vast as a cathedral, patient as stone, hungry as a furnace" coiled in the mountain's deepest chambers.
- **Sanctuary of Innovation** — Could consecrate a workspace so that creative inspiration flowed freely within its borders. Artisans who worked in her sanctuaries produced their finest work. She believed the Torch itself blessed these spaces.

## Prized Possessions
- A starknife whose four blades were forged from four different skymetals — adamantine, djezet, horacalcum, and noqual — each one scavenged from a different Numerian crash site. She called it "the Compass" and swore it pointed toward Silver Mount no matter how she threw it.
- A collection of charcoal drawings she made during her Torch visions — detailed floor plans of rooms deep inside Silver Mount that no explorer had ever reached. After her death, scavenger-lords fought over these drawings for decades.
