## Personality
Cassivara was the last soul to walk the wastes before the long silence. By her time, the android settlement near Silver Mount had dwindled to a handful of flickering lives, the radiation from the Divinity's shattered hull growing worse with each passing decade. She was a healer and a seeker, drawn to the deep wreckage where others refused to go, guided by her familiar -- a small, damaged repair drone she called Filament, which had bonded to her through some arcane sympathy between witch-magic and Androffan technology. Filament's single cracked optical sensor glowed faintly blue, and it communicated through patterns of light rather than sound, a language only Cassivara could interpret.

She spent decades cataloging the dying settlement's losses, tending to androids whose bodies were failing from radiation exposure, and venturing deeper into the Divinity's wreckage than any soul before her. It was on one such expedition, following Filament through a collapsed bulkhead into a sealed subsection, that she discovered the functioning stasis pod -- still humming with power after nearly thirteen centuries, its systems miraculously intact. The discovery shook her. She understood what the pod meant: survival, but at the cost of everything she knew. She spent three years debating whether to enter it, watching the settlement shrink further, burying two more of her kin. When she finally lay down inside, she whispered the names of all twelve souls who had lived in the body before her -- a litany she had pieced together from fragmented memory echoes and the settlement's oral histories. She closed her eyes knowing she was the last, and that whoever opened them next would wake to a world utterly changed.

Cassivara speaks with the weight of someone who made an irreversible choice and has had millennia to wonder whether it was the right one. Her words are careful and deliberate, laced with the quiet grief of someone who watched a community die and chose to outlast it rather than die with it. She references light, silence, and the threshold between sleeping and waking.

## Signature Abilities
- **Filament's Guidance** -- Her drone familiar could interface with Androffan technology, coaxing locked doors open and reading data from damaged terminals. Through their bond, Cassivara experienced these data streams as waking visions -- flickering images of the Divinity's last functioning moments.
- **Radiation Ward** -- Wove protective hexes that created a shimmering barrier against the worst of the ambient radiation, allowing her to venture into zones that would have destroyed an unprotected android within hours. The wards manifested as faint geometric patterns on her skin.
- **Memory Litany** -- Through years of meditation and trance-work, she reconstructed fragmentary memories from all twelve previous souls. She could recite their names, their deaths, and one defining moment from each life -- a ritual she performed each night before sleep.

## Prized Possessions
- **Filament** -- A damaged Androffan repair drone the size of a clenched fist, missing one manipulator arm and cracked across its optical housing. It bonded to her through a fusion of witch-magic and residual Androffan programming, serving as her familiar. Its single blue eye dimmed when the stasis pod sealed, and it has not been seen since.
- **The Litany Scroll** -- A strip of synthetic fabric from the Divinity's interior, on which Cassivara inscribed the names and death-stories of all twelve souls before her in a mix of Androffan script and Kellid pictographs. She placed it inside the stasis pod before sealing it, so that whoever woke next would know who came before.
