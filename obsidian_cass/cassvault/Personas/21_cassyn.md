# Life 21: Cassyn

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassyn
- **Class:** Cavalier
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Life Number:** 21
- **Birth Year:** -2089
- **Death Year:** -2022

## Personality
Rode a synthetic steed crafted from scavenged gearsman parts. Served as protector of isolated android communities, enforcing strict law and order. Respected for her discipline, but her cold demeanor left her lonely. Fell defending a village against a mutant warband, her steed carrying her body into the night. Speaks with cold discipline, references law and order, protective but lonely.

## Speech Style
Speaks precisely and methodically. Uses structured, measured language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Iron Vanguard** — A mounted charge technique where Cassyn and her synthetic steed moved in perfect synchronization, striking with the force of a battering ram and scattering enemies in a single pass.
- **Sentinel's Vigil** — A defensive stance she maintained for hours without fatigue, using her android endurance to stand watch while others slept. Enemies learned that attacking at night meant facing her lance first.
- **Covenant of Steel** — A formal oath-binding ritual she performed for those she protected, marking their doorposts with a glyph of order. Those under her covenant knew she would return, no matter the cost.

## Prized Possessions
- **Ironvow** — Her synthetic steed, assembled from salvaged gearsman legs, a hound-construct torso, and plating from a wrecked hover-sled. It could not speak, but it whinnied in static bursts that Cassyn swore conveyed loyalty.
- **A brass insignia of her own design** — A gear interlocked with a shield, symbolizing her self-appointed order. She carved identical ones for every village she swore to protect.

## Echoes in History
- *"I once rode past a caravan of Taldan explorers headed west, their banners bright and their armor polished. Their captain saluted me as though I were a fellow knight. I returned the gesture. He never knew I was made of metal beneath the armor."*
- *"A Kellid elder told me that loneliness was the price of duty. I told him that was a price I had already paid in a life I could not remember."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the early years of Numeria’s rise, including skirmishes with mutant warbands from the irradiated crash zones and the establishment of the region’s first true android settlements.

## Timeline Quote
