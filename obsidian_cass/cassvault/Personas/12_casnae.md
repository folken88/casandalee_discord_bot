# Life 12: Casnae

## Identity
- **Name:** Casnae
- **Class:** Medium

## Personality
Allowed the voices of her past selves to speak through her in trances. Frightened taverns when she spoke in multiple tones at once, reciting things no one else knew. Declared openly: 'I am many. One day, we will be one again.' Disappeared after collapsing in a fit, her body never recovered. Speaks in multiple voices, references past selves and unity, unsettling but prophetic.

## Era / Major Events
Wandered the Numerian wastes during the Age of Anguish, channeling voices of past selves in Kellid mead halls and trading posts near Silver Mount. While Ancient Osirion reached its zenith in distant Garund and the Jistka Imperium declined, Casnae frightened and fascinated those who heard her speak in a dozen voices at once. Her body was never recovered -- some whisper Silver Mount itself claimed her.

## Timeline Quote
The world is a canvas, and each generation paints it anew.


## Signature Abilities
- **Channel of the Many** -- Allowed her past selves to speak through her body, each voice carrying its own knowledge and personality. She could sustain up to four voices simultaneously, though it left her exhausted and disoriented.
- **Psychometric Trance** -- Touched objects and experienced vivid visions of their history, seeing through the eyes of everyone who had handled them. Androffan artifacts triggered especially powerful episodes.
- **Voice of Ages** -- Could shift her vocal patterns to perfectly replicate any of her past incarnations, complete with their mannerisms and knowledge. Tavern-goers found this deeply unsettling.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Speaking Stone** -- A smooth river stone that she claimed amplified her connection to past selves when held. In truth, it was an Androffan data crystal worn smooth by millennia of erosion, still faintly active. It grew warm when she channeled.
- **Eleven Braids** -- Her hair was woven into eleven distinct braids, each tied with a different material representing a past life she could access: wire for Cassula, ash-stained cord for Cassandra, crystal thread for Cassiel-D3, and so on. She added a new braid for each voice she recovered.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Kellid elder named Smoke-Over-Water invited me to a feast. When I began speaking in four voices at once, reciting the names of Numerian landmarks that had been destroyed centuries before Earthfall, the entire hall went silent. The elder poured me more mead and said: 'You carry your ancestors well.' I did not correct her."*
- *"Silver Mount called to me. I resisted for years, but one night I walked toward it in a trance, guided by a voice I later recognized as Cassiel-D3's. I never came back out. Or rather, my body never did. The voices, I think, found their way home."*
