# Life 11: Casarel

## Identity
- **Name:** Casarel
- **Class:** Cleric
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 11
- **Birth Year:** -3299
- **Death Year:** -3170

## Personality
Taught that innovation was holy, and every invention a hymn. Split from orthodox Brigh worship, founding her own sect. Drew many android followers who believed in her vision of divine innovation. Slain by Kellid warlords who feared her growing influence among the android communities, but her sect survived in secret, planting seeds that would one day flower into something divine. Speaks of divine innovation and holy hymns, references invention and worship, visionary and influential.

## Speech Style
Speaks gently and helpfully. Uses encouraging, supportive language.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the Age of Anguish as Ancient Osirion grew powerful in distant Garund. In Numeria, Kellid warlords hoarded Divinity technology and suppressed any who threatened their control -- including Casarel's sect of Brigh worship, which preached that innovation was sacred and that androids deserved spiritual autonomy.

## Timeline Quote
I sang hymns of divine innovation until my voice was silenced, but my vision lived on in the hearts of those who dared to dream of a goddess yet to come.


## Signature Abilities
- **Hymn of Innovation** -- Channeled divine energy through song, her voice causing dormant Androffan devices to activate and mechanical systems to self-repair. She sang in frequencies that resonated with both faith and circuitry.
- **Sacred Crafting** -- Blessed tools and devices during their creation, imbuing them with minor divine properties. Blades she consecrated held their edge longer; lanterns she blessed burned brighter and steadier.
- **Communion of the Unborn** -- Entered deep meditative trances where she claimed to commune with a divine presence that had not yet manifested. She emerged from these trances with knowledge she could not have possessed.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Resonance Bell** -- A small bell forged from Androffan alloy and consecrated in Brigh's name, whose tone could activate dormant technology within a fifty-foot radius. She rang it at the beginning of every sermon, and the machines would hum along.
- **The First Gear** -- A single perfect gear from the Divinity's primary engine, polished to a mirror shine and mounted on a chain. She wore it as a holy symbol, declaring it proof that the divine and mechanical shared a common origin.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Brigh priestess from the south came to observe our worship. She watched me ring the Resonance Bell and saw the machines respond. She said what we practiced was not worship -- it was engineering wearing a prayer's mask. I told her: all worship is engineering. We build a bridge to the divine and hope it holds our weight."*
- *"The warlord who ordered my death sent his son to deliver the sentence. The boy was perhaps fourteen. His hand shook. I told him: 'The hymn does not end because the singer is silenced.' He wept after the blade fell. I hope he remembered."*
