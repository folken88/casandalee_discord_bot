# Life 52: Cassindrax

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassindrax
- **Class:** Summoner
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Life Number:** 52
- **Birth Year:** 1608
- **Death Year:** 1667

## Personality
Bound a strange extraplanar eidolon she believed was a fragment of the Divinity's core. Used her eidolon as both weapon and guardian, protecting small villages from mutant raiders. Died when her eidolon's ship-memories overwhelmed her mind with a flood of alien star-charts and crew death-screams -- she walked into the wastes in a trance, following coordinates to a place that no longer existed, and never returned. Speaks of her bond with her eidolon, references protection and duty, methodical and devoted.

## Speech Style
Speaks precisely and methodically. Uses structured, measured language.

## Key Skills
- Knowledge (Arcana), Survival, Knowledge (Geography -- alien star-charts)
- **Favored Weapon:** A Divinity-alloy short spear that her eidolon shaped for her from raw hull material -- it reformed itself slightly each morning, as if dreaming of a better design

## Signature Abilities
- **Core Binding** — Unlike conventional summoning, Cassindrax's bond was forged by physically touching the Divinity's exposed core and allowing its residual intelligence to flow into her. The eidolon that emerged was not called from another plane -- it was pulled from the ship itself, a ghost of its navigation system given form.
- **Star-Chart Trance** — When deeply bonded with her eidolon, Cassindrax could perceive alien constellations overlaid on Golarion's night sky -- star-charts from the Divinity's voyage, showing routes between worlds that no longer existed. The trance was addictive and disorienting.
- **Guardian Aegis** — A defensive formation in which her eidolon wrapped around Cassindrax like living armor, absorbing attacks meant for her and redirecting the energy into retaliatory strikes. Villagers who saw this called her "the woman in the star-shell."

## Prized Possessions
- **The Shifting Spear** — Her Divinity-alloy weapon that subtly changed shape each day, its eidolon-linked material responding to some design impulse Cassindrax could not control. Some mornings it was a javelin. Some mornings it was a glaive. She learned to fight with whatever shape it chose.
- **A star-chart etched on skin** — During a Star-Chart Trance, Cassindrax tattooed a navigational route onto her own forearm in silver ink. The route led to coordinates in deep space -- the Divinity's point of origin. She followed those coordinates into the wastes on the night she vanished.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Dahak-worshiping priest came to our village demanding we surrender 'the dragon's treasure' he believed was buried beneath the wreckage. My eidolon rose to full height -- twenty feet of shimmering star-metal and alien light -- and the priest fell to his knees. He thought it was a dragon. I let him believe it. He never came back."*

## Era / Major Events
Lived during a period when Dahak-worshiping cults plagued the Numerian wastes, drawn by rumors of draconic power locked within the Divinity's deeper vaults. Unlike earlier summoners, her eidolon carried actual ship-memories -- fragmented navigational data and crew logs from the Divinity's core that surfaced as alien visions during their bond.

## Timeline Quote
The light from my eidolon carried the memory of stars I had never seen. It was both a shield and a window into the ship that fell.
