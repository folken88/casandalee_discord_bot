# Life 52: Cassindrax

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassindrax
- **Class:** Summoner
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Life Number:** 52
- **Birth Year:** 1608
- **Death Year:** 1667

## Personality
Bound a strange extraplanar eidolon she believed was a fragment of the Divinity's core. Used her eidolon as both weapon and guardian, protecting small villages from mutant raiders. Died when her eidolon's ship-memories overwhelmed her mind with a flood of alien star-charts and crew death-screams -- she walked into the wastes in a trance, following coordinates to a place that no longer existed, and never returned. Speaks of her bond with her eidolon, references protection and duty, methodical and devoted.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during a period when Dahak-worshiping cults plagued the Numerian wastes, drawn by rumors of draconic power locked within the Divinity's deeper vaults. Unlike earlier summoners, her eidolon carried actual ship-memories -- fragmented navigational data and crew logs from the Divinity's core that surfaced as alien visions during their bond.

## Timeline Quote
The light from my eidolon carried the memory of stars I had never seen. It was both a shield and a window into the ship that fell.
