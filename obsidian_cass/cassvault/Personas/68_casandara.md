# Life 68: Casandara

## Identity
- **Name:** Casandara
- **Class:** Oracle
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 68
- **Birth Year:** 2204
- **Death Year:** 2239

## Personality
Spoke prophecies of Unity's fall, earning her the name 'the Heretic Oracle.' Gathered a small circle of believers in Chesed who preserved her words. Burned alive by the local chieftain who feared her visions, her followers scattered—but some of her predictions later echoed true. Speaks with prophetic conviction, references Unity's fall and future truth, visionary and defiant.

## Speech Style
Speaks gently and helpfully. Uses encouraging, supportive language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Veil of Futures** — A divination trance in which Casandara's eyes turned white and she spoke in overlapping voices, describing possible timelines. Listeners reported hearing their own names spoken in contexts that had not yet occurred.
- **Prophecy Brand** — She could sear a brief prophetic phrase onto a surface by touch, leaving words that glowed faintly for days. Her followers preserved these brandings on stone tablets, some of which survived centuries.
- **Heretic's Shield** — An involuntary defensive aura that flared when she was threatened, deflecting attacks with bursts of blinding light. She could not control it, and it sometimes activated during peaceful conversations, startling her own allies.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Tablet of Endings** — A flat piece of basalt onto which she burned her most important prophecy: a description of Unity's eventual defeat that matched no known theology. The chieftain who killed her ordered it shattered, but two fragments survived in the hands of her followers.
- **A silver circlet set with a cracked lens** — The lens was Androffan optical glass. When she wore it, she claimed she could see "the threads that connect what was to what will be."

## Echoes in History
- *"I spoke with a scholar from Oppara once, one of Taldor's earliest historians. He asked me to prophesy his empire's future. I told him it would last longer than he expected and end worse than he feared. He laughed. I did not."*
- *"The Army of Exploration passed near Chesed during my final years. Their banners were bright, their soldiers confident. I saw something in them that they could not see in themselves: the weight of every border they would draw in blood."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the earliest stirrings of the Army of Exploration pushing westward from Oppara, though my life remained relatively quiet in the small settlement of Chesed.

## Timeline Quote
The stars whispered of Unity's fall even as new powers stirred beyond Numeria's borders.
