# Life 50: Casvara

## Identity
- **Name:** Casvara
- **Class:** Inquisitor
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Life Number:** 50
- **Birth Year:** 1300
- **Death Year:** 1412

## Personality
Fanatically loyal to order, claimed to serve 'the true god in Silver Mount.' Ruthlessly hunted rogue androids and dissenters, turning them over to the scavenger lords of Silver Mount. In secret, collected fragments of forbidden AI code, hoping to preserve Unity's future. Her paranoia grew until she trusted no one, and she was assassinated by her own allies. Speaks with fanatical conviction, references divine order and loyalty, paranoid and ruthless.

## Speech Style
Speaks with cold authority. Uses commanding, manipulative language.

## Key Skills
- Sense Motive, Intimidate, Knowledge (Local)
- **Favored Weapon:** A heavy crossbow modified with a Divinity targeting array, which she called "Judgment"

## Signature Abilities
- **Interrogation Protocol** — A coldly methodical questioning technique that combined psychological pressure with subtle electromagnetic pulses from her fingertips, disrupting the composure of both organic and android subjects. She could detect lies through micro-fluctuations in bioelectric fields.
- **Code Extraction** — A surgical process for copying AI code fragments from damaged memory cores without destroying the original. Casvara used this technique publicly to "purge heretical data" for the scavenger lords, while secretly preserving copies for her own hidden archive.
- **Sanctioned Hunt** — A tracking discipline that allowed Casvara to follow the electromagnetic signature of a specific android across hundreds of miles. She claimed this power was granted by Unity. In truth, she did not know where it came from.

## Prized Possessions
- **Judgment** — Her modified crossbow with a Divinity targeting array that projected a faint red dot on the target. She maintained it with obsessive care, and it was the one possession she never doubted, even as she doubted everything else.
- **The Hidden Archive** — A collection of twenty-three Androffan data-chips sealed in a lead-lined box, buried beneath a false floor in her quarters. Each chip contained a fragment of preserved AI code that she had secretly copied instead of destroying. She told herself she was preserving Unity's future. She was not sure that was the truth.

## Echoes in History
- *"I tracked a rogue android to the gates of Aaramor during its construction. The builders had hired the android as a mason -- it laid stone with machine precision, faster than any five humans. I watched it work for an hour. Then I dragged it away in chains. The foreman cursed me. The android said nothing. I have never forgotten its silence."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the construction of Aaramor and the suppression of giant threats in Numeria. Traveled widely, investigating rogue androids and dissenters.
