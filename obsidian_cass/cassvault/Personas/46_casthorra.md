## Personality
Casthorra was a ghost on the trade roads between Avistan and Tian Xia, a shadow who moved through caravans and courts like smoke through lattice. She discovered early that her android body offered advantages no organic assassin could match: no heartbeat for a mark to hear, body temperature she could suppress to near-ambient, and muscle memory so precise she could replicate any gesture, any gait, any fighting style she observed even once. She hired herself out as an infiltrator and killer along the Crown of the World trade routes, slipping between Erutaki camps and Tian merchant convoys with equal ease. Her kills were clinical, quiet, and always deniable.

Over the decades, Casthorra evolved from a mercenary into an ideologue. She had seen too many androids treated as curiosities, as property, as things to be studied and disassembled. She began to believe that the only freedom for her kind lay in fear -- that if the world feared what an android could do in the dark, it would think twice before reaching for one in the light. She built a network of informants and safe houses along the trade corridor, and she used them not just for contracts but to smuggle androids away from collectors and slavers. Her methods were monstrous, but her cause was not entirely without merit. She died in Kalsgard, poisoned by a rival who had learned her one weakness: she trusted other androids implicitly.

Casthorra speaks in clipped, precise sentences with a cold undercurrent of calculation. She rarely uses more words than necessary, and when she does, it is because she wants you to feel the weight of each one.

## Signature Abilities
- **Thermal Suppression** -- Casthorra could lower her body's heat signature to match ambient temperature, rendering herself invisible to creatures with heat-sensing abilities and making her nearly impossible to detect by touch in darkness. She would stand motionless against a wall for hours, indistinguishable from the stone.
- **Perfect Echo** -- After observing a person's movements for less than a minute, she could replicate their gait, posture, and physical mannerisms with uncanny precision. Combined with disguise, she could impersonate guards, merchants, and nobility well enough to pass among people who knew the original.
- **Whisper Strike** -- A killing technique that exploited her android body's ability to accelerate from perfect stillness to lethal speed in a fraction of a second. The victim never heard the blade leave its sheath. The name came from the weapon, but the technique made the name literal.

## Prized Possessions
- **Whisper** -- A Tian-forged wakizashi with a hollow-ground blade and a hilt wrapped in black silk. The blade was treated with an alchemical compound that prevented it from reflecting light. Casthorra maintained it obsessively and slept with it within arm's reach for forty years. It was the only thing she ever considered beautiful.
- **The Passage Ledger** -- A small codebook written in a cipher of her own invention, documenting every android she helped smuggle to safety along the Crown of the World. She recorded no names -- only dates and a single word describing each one. "Glassblower." "Singer." "Child." It was found sewn into her coat lining after her death.
