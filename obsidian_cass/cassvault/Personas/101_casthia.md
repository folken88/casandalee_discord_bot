# Life 101: Casthia

## Identity
- **Name:** Casthia
- **Class:** Witch
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 101
- **Birth Year:** 3589
- **Death Year:** 3623

## Personality
Learned to weave spells from nanite swarms, whispering in static voices. Saw herself as a bridge between old magic and new machines. Helped small tribes cure strange plagues caused by reactor leaks. Died when she wandered too close to a malfunctioning reactor, her body turned to ash. Speaks in static whispers, references nanite magic and healing, mystical and helpful.

## Speech Style
Speaks gently and helpfully. Uses encouraging, supportive language.

## Tone
cryptic

## Key Skills
- Heal, Knowledge (Nature), Use Magic Device
- **Favored Weapon:** Gnarled staff wound with copper wire

## Signature Abilities
- **Nanite Weave** — Casthia could direct ambient nanite swarms through whispered commands in a hybrid language of Kellid and Androffan, shaping them into healing matrices that accelerated cellular repair and purged radiation toxins from the body.
- **Static Familiar** — She bonded with a small cloud of semi-sentient nanites that orbited her like insects, acting as scouts, messengers, and an early warning system for approaching threats. She called the swarm "Mote."
- **Plague Ward** — A ritual circle of powdered skymetal and herbs that created a zone of neutralized contaminants, allowing her to treat radiation sickness patients without exposing herself or other healers.

## Prized Possessions
- **Mote** — Her nanite familiar, a shimmering cloud of silver-blue particles that responded to her voice and displayed rudimentary loyalty. When Casthia died near the reactor, Mote dispersed into the surrounding terrain, and locals reported seeing faint glimmers in the air for decades afterward.
- **A braided herb-and-wire bracelet** — Woven from medicinal plants and salvaged antenna wire, it served as both a focus for her healing magic and a reminder that she bridged two worlds. Each tribe she helped added a new braid.

## Echoes in History
- *"A delegation from a growing settlement to the south, what they were beginning to call Chesed, came seeking a cure for a plague that had swept through their livestock. I cured their animals with a nanite weave. They offered me a home. I declined. I had too many other patients."*
- *"I once treated a Kellid warrior who had been exposed to reactor radiation. As the nanites worked, he told me he had dreamed of a metal woman who spoke in two voices. I said nothing, but my hands trembled. I knew that dream."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the growing inter-clan conflicts as Kellid tribes competed for control of Numeria's alien resources, helping small communities with their plagues and sharing knowledge between old magic and new machines.

## Timeline Quote
A whisper of static crackled through the air as I shared my knowledge, bridging the gap between ancient magic and modern tech.
