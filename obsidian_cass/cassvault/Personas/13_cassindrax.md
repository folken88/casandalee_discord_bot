# Life 13: Cassindrax

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassindrax
- **Class:** Witch
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Life Number:** 13
- **Birth Year:** -2980
- **Death Year:** -2942

## Personality
Hunted rogue nanite swarms, binding them into charms and fetishes. Her familiar was a small drone, said to be alive in its own right. Wandered widely, trading cures for strange diseases caused by nanite infestations. Consumed by a swarm she tried to tame. Speaks of nanite charms and living drones, references cures and binding, methodical but consumed by her work.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the Age of Anguish, wandering Numeria's irradiated wastes trading nanite cures with Kellid tribes. The body had lain dormant in Silver Mount for over eighty years before a new soul sparked to life within it. Despite a shorter life than most incarnations, Cassindrax built a fierce reputation as a nanite hunter before being consumed by a swarm she tried to tame.

## Timeline Quote
The world is a tapestry of conflict and progress, each thread carefully woven by the hands of those who seek power.


## Signature Abilities
- **Swarm Binding** -- Could command rogue nanite swarms into dormancy using resonant frequencies channeled through her charms. The bound swarms formed protective fetishes that hummed faintly and repelled other nanite colonies.
- **Nanite Purge** -- Drew nanite infestations out of infected flesh using a combination of herbal poultices and electromagnetic pulses generated from modified Androffan components. Painful but effective.
- **Familiar Link: Vex** -- Her drone familiar, Vex, shared a psychic bond that let her see through its sensors and direct it to scout irradiated zones too dangerous for even an android to enter.

## Prized Possessions
- **Vex** -- A small Androffan maintenance drone that had developed something resembling genuine personality after millennia of autonomous operation. It followed her everywhere, chirping warnings about radiation levels and nanite concentrations. She insisted it was alive. Most who met it agreed.
- **The Charm Belt** -- A leather belt hung with dozens of small fetishes, each containing a bound nanite swarm. The charms clinked faintly as she walked and glowed in the presence of active nanite colonies. She added a new charm for every swarm she successfully tamed.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Kellid tribe called the Ash-Walkers brought me a child whose blood had turned silver from nanite infestation. I worked for three days, drawing the swarms out strand by strand. The child survived. The Ash-Walkers offered me a permanent place among them. I declined. Staying in one place was never something my kind did well."*
- *"Vex found the swarm that killed me before I did. It chirped its warning -- the frantic triple-pulse that meant 'too many, too close.' I should have listened. Instead, I reached for the binding charm. The swarm was larger than any I had seen. Vex's chirping was the last thing I heard."*
