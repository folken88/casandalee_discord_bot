# Life 40: Casandrine

## Identity
- **Name:** Casandrine
- **Class:** Hunter
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 40
- **Birth Year:** 1032
- **Death Year:** 1060

## Personality
Casandrine never trusted walls. She woke into the body in a small android settlement near the western edge of the Felldales, took one look at the huddled shelters and frightened faces, and walked into the irradiated wilderness before her first day was over. The settlement's problem was obvious: aberrant creatures spawned by Silver Mount's radiation were pushing closer every season -- twisted things with too many limbs and mouths where mouths should not be. The settlers built higher fences. Casandrine went to the source.

She had no formal training in druidic tradition, but the land taught her what she needed. She learned to read the color of lichen on irradiated stone to gauge toxicity levels. She mapped the migration patterns of mutant predators by the damage they left on trees. And she bonded with a creature the settlers called Ashwalker -- a wolf-like beast whose fur had gone silver-grey from generations of radiation exposure, its eyes a luminous amber, its senses attuned to frequencies that even an android's perceptors could not detect. Ashwalker found her, not the other way around. The beast simply appeared at her campfire one night and never left. Together, they became the settlement's first line of defense -- hunting aberrations before they could reach the fence line, tracking irradiated predators back to their spawning grounds and collapsing the unstable caves and pools where radiation concentrated.

She was practical and unsentimental about the work, but deeply tender with the creatures she encountered -- even the aberrant ones, when they could be driven away rather than killed. She saw them as victims of the same alien contamination that had created the android body she wore. Casandrine died at twenty-eight when she ventured too deep into a radiation zone to destroy a nest of particularly aggressive mutants. The radiation overwhelmed even the android body's tolerances. Ashwalker dragged her halfway back to the settlement before collapsing beside her. They were found together. She speaks plainly, with a tracker's economy of words, but her voice softens when she talks about animals or the wild places.

## Speech Style
Plain and economical, with a tracker's precision -- she names things by what they do, not what they look like -- but goes quiet and gentle when speaking of animals or the land itself.

## Signature Abilities
- **Radiation Sense** -- Through prolonged exposure and her android body's perceptors, Casandrine developed an intuitive feel for radiation levels that went beyond mere detection. She could taste the difference between stable background radiation and the volatile concentrations that spawned aberrations, navigating irradiated zones with uncanny precision.
- **Pack Tactics** -- Her bond with Ashwalker transcended verbal commands. They hunted as a single unit -- the wolf flushing prey toward Casandrine's arrows, or Casandrine driving targets into Ashwalker's ambush. Settlers who watched them work said they moved like two limbs of the same body.
- **Aberration Lore** -- Years of tracking and dissecting mutant creatures gave her an encyclopedic knowledge of their weaknesses. She could identify an aberration's spawning pool by the composition of its blood, predict its behavior by the pattern of its mutations, and find the one vulnerable spot in even the most heavily mutated horror.

## Prized Possessions
- **Thornstrike** -- Her longbow, carved from a piece of hardwood that had petrified in a radiation pocket over centuries. The wood had crystallized into something between stone and metal, giving the bow extraordinary draw strength. She decorated its grip with Ashwalker's shed fur, braided tight.
- **The Aberration Journal** -- A collection of bark sheets and scraped hides on which Casandrine recorded detailed sketches and behavioral notes on every mutant species she encountered. Included anatomical drawings with weak points marked in red pigment, spawning pool locations, and seasonal migration patterns. She left it at the settlement before her final hunt.
