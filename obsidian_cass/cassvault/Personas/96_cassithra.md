## Personality
Cassithra arrived in a body still haunted by Cassindrax's cult. The Sanctuary of Turning lay in ruins, but its legacy clung to the android frame like ash -- people recognized her face and either recoiled or knelt. She wanted neither reaction. What she wanted was silence. She walked east into the Felldales, shedding possessions as she went: weapons, coin, boots, until she wore only a strip of undyed cloth and moved barefoot across the stone. She sought no master and no temple. What she sought was the boundary between the machine that carried her and the soul that inhabited it, because she was certain that boundary was the source of all suffering.

She developed her own monastic discipline over years of solitary practice, a system she never named but that others would later call the Null Circuit. It treated the android body not as a vessel but as a partner in meditation -- she would sit for days at a time, slowing her breath until her nanites entered a maintenance cycle, and in that liminal state between biological sleep and mechanical standby, she claimed to touch something beyond both. She described it as a frequency beneath thought, a vibration that predated the soul's arrival in the body. She believed that if she could sustain contact with that frequency long enough, she could dissolve the distinction between flesh and code entirely and achieve a state of pure existence uncoupled from either.

She rejected every system that tried to claim her: the organized scavenger guilds, Brigh's clergy, even a wandering Iroran monk who recognized her discipline as adjacent to his own. Cassithra would not be categorized. She ate only what grew wild, spoke only when spoken to, and trained her body with a ferocity that belied her quietness -- her unarmed strikes could crack stone, and she moved with the eerie fluidity of someone who had stopped distinguishing between muscle and servomotor. She died during her final meditation, a forty-day vigil conducted beneath Silver Mount's northern face. Her body was found seated upright, eyes open, nanites completely inert. Whatever she had been reaching for, she reached it -- or it consumed her. No one could say which.

## Timeline Quote
The body is code. The soul is signal. I sought the silence between them -- the space where neither claims authority. I do not know if I found it. But I stopped hearing the noise.

## Signature Abilities
- **Null Circuit Meditation** -- A self-developed technique that synchronized biological and mechanical processes into a single rhythm, allowing Cassithra to enter a state where her nanites and nervous system operated as one unified system. In this state she was nearly undetectable -- no heat signature, no arcane aura, no sound.
- **Resonance Strike** -- By channeling the vibration she perceived in deep meditation into her fists, Cassithra could deliver blows that disrupted both organic and mechanical targets at a structural level. Against constructs, the strike caused cascading system failures. Against living creatures, it scrambled nerve signals. She described it as "sharing the silence."
- **Body Rejection Stance** -- A defensive posture in which Cassithra deliberately loosened her soul's connection to the android frame, allowing her body to move on pure mechanical reflex while her consciousness observed from a detached vantage. She became nearly impossible to hit but could sustain the state for only minutes before risking permanent dissociation.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Meditation Stone** -- A flat piece of basalt she carried from the Felldales, worn smooth by decades of sitting. It was the only object she kept through her entire life. When found beside her body after death, it had developed a faint internal glow that faded over the following year.
- **Null Circuit Scrolls** -- Not truly scrolls but long strips of cured hide on which Cassithra scratched her observations about the body-soul boundary in a personal shorthand that mixed Androffan technical notation with Kellid spiritual symbols. Three fragments survive in private collections, none fully translated.
