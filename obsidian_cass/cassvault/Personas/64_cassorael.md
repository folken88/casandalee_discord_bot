# Life 64: Cassorael

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassorael
- **Class:** Sorcerer
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Life Number:** 64
- **Birth Year:** 2036
- **Death Year:** 2068

## Personality
Wild, unhinged, and chaotic, drew power directly from malfunctioning reactors and strange radiation. Worshiped entropy, seeing destruction as a sacred act. Reigned as a petty warlord in Scrapwall, demanding offerings of scrap and bone. Was devoured by her own unstable nanite swarms and corrupted gearsmen in a fit of chaos. Speaks with manic energy, references entropy and destruction, unpredictable and dangerous.

Speaks with aggressive energy. Uses threatening, dominating language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Reactor Siphon** — Cassorael could draw raw power directly from malfunctioning Divinity reactors by plunging her hands into their coolant streams. The energy suffused her body with a violet glow and amplified her sorcery to devastating levels, at the cost of slowly degrading her own systems.
- **Entropic Cascade** — A channeled blast of unstable radiation that she shaped through sheer willpower into arcs of destructive force. No two casts looked the same -- sometimes lightning, sometimes fire, sometimes a wave of disintegration. She did not control the form. She only controlled the target.
- **Scrapwall Dominion** — Less a spell than a reputation made manifest: Cassorael's presence in Scrapwall was so feared that corrupted gearsmen and nanite swarms responded to her emotional state, becoming more aggressive when she raged and dormant when she was calm. She believed this was worship. It may have been something stranger.

## Prized Possessions
- **A reactor-core fragment** — A chunk of unstable Divinity reactor material she kept embedded in her chest cavity, replacing one of her original power cells. It gave her immense sorcerous power and slowly killed her. She called it "my heart" and laughed when people flinched.
- **The Bone Throne** — A seat in the center of Scrapwall assembled from the metallic bones of dismantled gearsmen and the actual bones of challengers she had defeated. She did not sit in it often. She preferred to stand where people could see the reactor light pulsing through her chest.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Taldan trade delegation came to Scrapwall once, seeking to negotiate skymetal exports for the Grand Prince's armories. I melted their lead negotiator's sword from across the room and told them my export terms were 'leave or burn.' They left. I kept the molten sword as a paperweight."*

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the height of Taldor's imperial power, as its far-flung provinces grew restless under distant rule. In Numeria, tribal warlords and machine-cultists warred over salvage territories, and Scrapwall's reputation as a lawless haven attracted the desperate and the mad.
