# Life 63: Cass

## Identity
- **Name:** Cass
- **Class:** Ranger
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Life Number:** 63
- **Birth Year:** 1990
- **Death Year:** 2030

## Personality
A survivor and loner who roamed Numeria's wastes, haunted by dreams she could not explain. Learned to hunt, track, and live off the scorched land. Collected scrap and wreckage from crash sites, instinctively drawn back to the bones of the Divinity though she had no rational reason for the compulsion. Known as the 'Ghost in the Scraplands' by Kellid tribes who left offerings at her campsites. The dreams grew worse as she aged--vivid visions of being aboard a great ship, of stars wheeling outside viewports, of calculating trajectories in a language she did not speak. She mistook these for memories of a life she must have lived and could not reconcile them with her actual existence. Died quietly in the Scraplands, curled against a fragment of alien hull, whispering coordinates in Androffan. Speaks of survival, tracking, and the harsh beauty of wasteland life, tinged with confused longing for a past that was not hers.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during an era when the Technic League had become a recognized power in Numeria, though not yet the iron-fisted rulers they would become. The League was actively recruiting--or capturing--androids for study. Cass avoided them by living in the deep Scraplands, but she was plagued by dreams of the Divinity's original voyage, memories bleeding through Casandalee's broken rebirth cycle. She never understood that these visions were echoes of her first self--she believed she was going mad.

## Timeline Quote
The wasteland is unforgiving, but it whispers secrets to those who listen carefully.


## Signature Abilities
- **Ghost Walk** — Could move through the Scraplands so silently that even augmented gearsmen lost her trail. Kellid trackers who tried to follow her reported that her footprints simply stopped in the middle of open ground, as if she had been lifted into the sky.
- **Wreckage Dowsing** — Felt an involuntary pull toward fragments of Divinity wreckage. She could not explain it — her body simply walked toward crash sites the way water runs downhill. She followed this instinct across hundreds of miles without questioning it.
- **Starlight Whisper** — On clear nights, lying against alien hull fragments, she could hear faint transmissions — static, fragments of data, and once, unmistakably, a voice speaking Androffan. She never learned Androffan. She understood it anyway.

## Prized Possessions
- A longbow made from alien polymer she found in a Divinity debris field, strung with braided wire salvaged from the same site. It was lighter and stronger than any bow the Kellids had ever seen. She never named it — she was not sentimental about tools. But she slept with it within arm's reach every night.
- A hull fragment the size of her palm, worn smooth from years of handling. She carried it in her pocket and rubbed it when the dreams got bad. When she died, it was clenched in her fist.
