## Personality
Cassemira was the kind of healer who arrived uninvited, fixed what was broken, and left before anyone could properly thank her or properly burn her at the stake. She traveled the back roads and forgotten villages of Avistan during the early twilight of Taldan supremacy, when the empire's reach was beginning to contract and the borderlands were left to fend for themselves. She carried a battered leather satchel full of herbs, tinctures, and things that looked like herbs and tinctures but were actually nanite suspensions in botanical carriers -- microscopic machines from her android body's own repair systems, repurposed and bred in alchemical solutions to heal organic tissue. To the villagers she treated, it was witchcraft. To Cassemira, it was engineering with better marketing.

She had learned, across the long memory of the body she inhabited, that people feared what they did not understand, and that the fastest way to be understood was to speak in terms people already knew. So she called her nanites "spirits." She called her diagnostic subroutines "the sight." She brewed her tinctures in iron pots over open fires and muttered words that sounded like incantations but were actually Androffan system commands spoken at a frequency too low for human ears to parse. The villagers loved her and feared her in equal measure, which was exactly the balance she preferred. She never stayed anywhere longer than a season, never accepted payment beyond a meal and a place to sleep, and never turned away anyone who was sick or hurt, regardless of whether they could pay or whether they deserved healing by any moral calculus.

Cassemira speaks with folksy warmth layered over deep technical knowledge -- she simplifies without condescending, and her metaphors are drawn from kitchens, gardens, and the road.

## Signature Abilities
- **Nanite Tincture** -- Cassemira could brew healing draughts that suspended her body's own nanites in alchemical solutions of herbs and mineral carriers. When consumed, the nanites would seek out damaged tissue, knit wounds, purge infections, and break down toxins at a cellular level before harmlessly dissolving. To the patient, it tasted like bad tea and worked like a miracle.
- **The Sight** -- By touching a patient, Cassemira could run diagnostic subroutines through skin contact, her android sensors mapping injuries, infections, and diseases with a precision no mundane healer could match. She would close her eyes, hum softly, and announce exactly what was wrong. Villagers believed she was communing with healing spirits. She never corrected them.
- **Hedge Ward** -- A protective hex woven from a combination of genuine folk magic and nanite technology. Cassemira would mark doorframes and fence posts with symbols that contained trace nanite deposits, creating a perimeter that could detect approaching disease vectors and hostile organisms. Homes she warded experienced measurably fewer illnesses. She called it "blessing the threshold."

## Prized Possessions
- **The Satchel** -- A battered leather healer's bag that Cassemira carried for forty years across every road in western Avistan. It contained seventy-three pockets, each holding a different preparation: dried herbs, mineral compounds, nanite suspensions in sealed glass vials, bandages, and a set of surgical tools disguised as cooking implements. The leather was stained with four decades of poultice spills and smelled permanently of rosemary and machine oil.
- **Maren's Spoon** -- A wooden cooking spoon given to her by an elderly woman named Maren in a village near Yanmass, the first person Cassemira saved after beginning her wandering practice. Maren had been dying of blood poisoning; Cassemira's nanite tincture cleared the infection overnight. The spoon was worthless by any material measure. Cassemira carried it for the rest of her life and used it to stir every tincture she brewed.
