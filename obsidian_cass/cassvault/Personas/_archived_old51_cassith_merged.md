# Life 51: Cassirath

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassirath
- **Class:** Witch
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 51
- **Birth Year:** 1469
- **Death Year:** 1548

## Personality
Haunted by fragmentary dreams of a past self who burned at the Torch -- dreams she could not explain but could not ignore. Where that earlier life had tried to build a temple openly, Cassirath worked in shadows, weaving protective hexes around android settlements and teaching innovation through whispered councils. Her familiar was a clockwork raven assembled from Divinity scrap. When a petty techno-tyrant razed her hidden workshop, she did not fight -- she scattered her students across Numeria, ensuring her knowledge survived even as she did not. Found dead at the base of the Torch, her clockwork raven still perched on her shoulder, eyes dimming.

## Speech Style
Speaks in quiet, deliberate tones. Uses metaphors of weaving, shadows, and hidden patterns.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during an era when petty techno-tyrants and organized scavenger guilds tightened their grip on Numerian salvage operations. Far to the south, the Taldan empire began showing the first hairline cracks of its long decline, and Keleshite traders brought new ideas along the eastern trade routes. Around the Torch, the settlement had grown into a true town, drawing smiths and inventors from across the region.

## Key Skills
- Spellcraft, Knowledge (Arcana), Craft (Clockwork)
- **Favored Weapon:** A hex-etched dagger made from Divinity hull plating, used to inscribe protective wards

## Signature Abilities
- **Warding Weave** — A complex hex pattern Cassirath could inscribe around a settlement in a single night, using ground skymetal powder and her own nanite-infused blood. The weave disrupted hostile technological scanning and confused the navigation systems of gearsmen sent to locate her charges.
- **Whispering Council** — A ritual of communal instruction in which Cassirath and her students sat in a circle, each holding a fragment of salvaged circuitry. Through the ritual, she could transmit knowledge directly -- not as words, but as intuitive understanding, bypassing the need for dangerous written records.
- **Raven's Eye** — Through her clockwork raven familiar, Cassirath could perceive threats from miles away. The raven's Divinity-crystal eyes recorded everything they saw, and Cassirath could replay these recordings by touching its head -- a crude but effective surveillance network.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Clockwork Raven** — Assembled from Divinity scrap with obsidian-chip eyes, this familiar was more than a construct -- Cassirath believed it contained a fragment of awareness, a tiny spark that made it loyal beyond mere programming. It perched on her shoulder even after her death, its eyes slowly dimming over days.
- **A hex-pattern quilt** — Sewn from scraps of fabric donated by her students, each patch embroidered with a different protective ward. She wrapped herself in it during rituals and claimed it kept the dreams of past lives from overwhelming her. Under each patch, she had hidden a slip of paper bearing the name and location of one of her scattered students.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Keleshite merchant passed through our region selling 'bottled lightning' from the east -- alchemical curiosities that crackled and sparked. I bought three bottles and used them to power my raven for a month. The merchant asked me how I knew they would work for that purpose. I said I dreamed it. He looked at me like I was mad. He was not entirely wrong."*

## Timeline Quote
The flame remembers what the flesh forgets. I will not build another temple for them to burn -- I will plant seeds they cannot find.
