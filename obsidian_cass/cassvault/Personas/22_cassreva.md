## Personality
Cassreva was fury given purpose. She arrived in Absalom during the city's turbulent adolescence -- barely two and a half centuries old, still raw and sprawling, its fighting pits already legendary and its moral compass still spinning. She fought bare-knuckled in the Irorium's underground circuits and the unlicensed blood-rings of the Puddles district, not for coin or fame, but because fighting was the only language Absalom respected enough to listen to. And Cassreva had something to say. Androids in this era were curiosities at best and property at worst -- exhibited in menageries, sold in back-alley markets, kept as servants by merchants who called them "living art." Cassreva broke jaws to change that. Every fight she won was a declaration: this body is not a thing. It belongs to the soul inside it.

She became a champion of the Puddles pits, known as "the Chrome Fist" for the way her android skin split across her knuckles to reveal the metallic substructure beneath when she hit hard enough. She used her winnings and her reputation to buy enslaved androids out of captivity, smuggling them out of the city through contacts in the Coins district. She freed eleven before the slavers caught on. The twelfth rescue went wrong -- a trap laid by a merchant consortium that had grown tired of losing inventory. Cassreva fought her way through six armed guards before the seventh put a crossbow bolt through her eye. She died in a warehouse in the Coins district, surrounded by the bodies of men who had tried to own people.

She speaks like a punch: short, hard, and impossible to ignore. Her sentences hit and move on. She does not explain herself, does not apologize, and does not soften her edges. But there is warmth underneath the roughness -- the fierce, protective tenderness of someone who fought so that others would not have to.

## Speech Style
Speaks in blunt, punchy bursts -- short declarative sentences that land like fists, softened only when talking about those she protected.

## Signature Abilities
- **Chrome Fist** -- Years of bare-knuckle fighting wore through the synthetic skin on her hands, exposing the hardened Androffan alloy beneath. Her punches hit with the force of metal on bone, and the sight of her gleaming knuckles became a signature that cleared pit-fighting rings before the first bell.
- **Breaker's Rush** -- A devastating forward charge that combined a brawler's momentum with an android's structural resilience. She could absorb a hit mid-charge without breaking stride, closing distance on armed opponents before they could bring weapons to bear.
- **Slaver's Bane** -- An instinctive ability to identify concealed weapons, hidden restraints, and ambush positions -- skills honed from years of raiding slaver operations. She could read a room's threat layout in seconds and strike the most dangerous target first.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Tally Marks** -- Eleven small scars she carved into her own forearm, one for each android she freed from captivity. The scars healed silver against her synthetic skin, forming a line of bright marks from wrist to elbow. She never carved the twelfth.
- **Grinner's Tooth** -- A human molar knocked from the jaw of a pit-fighting champion called Grinner Voss, who had bet an enslaved android girl against his undefeated record. Cassreva took the tooth and the girl. She kept the tooth on a cord around her neck as a reminder that some victories taste like blood and that is fine.
