## Personality
Cassithan was discipline made flesh -- or rather, discipline made metal and synthetic muscle. He enlisted in Taldor's expanding military at a time when the empire was young and hungry, pushing its borders outward into lands that had known no central authority since the Age of Darkness. He served for thirty-six years, rising from the ranks of a frontier garrison to become a centurion of the Taldan Phalanx, commanding a mixed company of humans and the occasional oddity like himself. His superiors valued him for his tirelessness, his perfect recall of orders, and his unsettling calm under fire. His soldiers respected him because he never asked them to endure anything he would not endure first, and because he remembered every name of every soldier who fell under his command.

The frontier shaped him. Decades of garrison duty along Taldor's northern and eastern borders, where the empire's golden ambitions met the reality of hostile terrain, displaced peoples, and supply lines stretched too thin, ground away any romanticism about glory or conquest. Cassithan came to believe in structure -- not because it was beautiful, but because it kept people alive. He built walls, drilled formations, enforced discipline, and buried the dead with full honors regardless of which side they had fought on. He was neither cruel nor kind; he was thorough. He died during a border skirmish that should have been routine, taking a spear through the throat while shielding a young soldier who had frozen in her first engagement. His last order was for the line to hold.

His speech is clipped and precise, stripped of ornament. He measures words the way he measured rations -- enough to serve, never enough to waste. When emotion surfaces, it manifests as a tightening of phrasing, not an expansion of it. He speaks of duty, structure, and the weight of names remembered.

## Speech Style
Speaks in short, direct sentences with military precision, wasting no words and tolerating no ambiguity.

## Signature Abilities
- **Phalanx Discipline** -- Could organize even untrained civilians into a functional defensive formation within minutes, barking commands with such authority that compliance was instinctive. His formations held against charges that should have broken them.
- **Name of the Fallen** -- Maintained a perfect mental record of every soldier who died under his command -- name, rank, manner of death, and one personal detail. He recited the full list each evening. By the end, it took nearly an hour.
- **Iron Endurance** -- His android body's resilience, combined with decades of frontier conditioning, allowed him to march, fight, and stand watch for periods that would have destroyed a human soldier. He used this not for glory but to take the hardest shifts so his soldiers could rest.

## Prized Possessions
- **Vigil** -- His longspear, regulation Taldan Phalanx issue, unremarkable except that he kept the same iron spearhead for thirty-six years through seven shaft replacements. A single notch on the blade marked his first loss -- a scout named Perren who died of infection three days into Cassithan's first command. He added no further notches. One was enough to remember them all.
- **The Muster Roll** -- A leather-bound ledger in which he recorded the name, hometown, and one personal detail of every soldier assigned to his company. The entries grew more terse over the years, but never stopped. The last entry, half-finished, reads: "Soldier Liera Voss. Cassomir. First engagement. Brave."
