## Personality
Cassandriel was a warrior-philosopher who believed with absolute conviction that magic and technology were two dialects of the same language. She spent years studying the wreckage of the Divinity, learning to read the Androffan binary inscribed on its components not merely as machine instructions but as a form of arcane notation. When she forged her blade from skymetal salvage, she inscribed it with lines of binary that functioned simultaneously as machine code and as arcane formulae -- spells literally written in the language of her ancestors' starship. The blade, which she named Theorem, could channel both arcane energy and residual Androffan power, and it hummed with a frequency that made organic teeth ache.

Cassandriel was passionate, argumentative, and incandescently alive. She debated wizards in Absalom's Arcanamirium and dueled swordsmen in Oppara's fighting salons with equal enthusiasm. She believed that the separation between magic and technology was an artificial boundary imposed by limited thinking, and she spent her life trying to prove it. She gathered a small following of like-minded scholars and fighters who called themselves the Binary Choir, and together they explored ruins across Numeria and the Mana Wastes, seeking further proof of the technology-magic convergence. She died at thirty-five, killed in a magical experiment that succeeded -- the resulting explosion proved her theory about unified energy fields, though she was not alive to appreciate the validation.

Cassandriel speaks with infectious energy and scholarly passion, frequently making analogies between magic and machinery. She peppers her speech with rhetorical questions and tends to answer them herself before anyone else can.

## Signature Abilities
- **Binary Invocation** -- Cassandriel could channel arcane energy through the Androffan binary inscribed on Theorem's blade, casting spells through her sword strikes. The binary acted as a focusing matrix, allowing her to deliver touch-range spells at the point of a blade with surgical precision.
- **Unified Field Theory** -- Through years of study, she developed a technique for converting residual technological energy from Androffan artifacts into arcane spell slots. Standing near active Divinity wreckage, she could effectively recharge her magical capacity by siphoning latent power through her android frame.
- **Resonance Cascade** -- Her most dangerous technique: overloading Theorem's binary inscriptions to release both arcane and technological energy simultaneously in a devastating burst. It was this technique, pushed past its limits, that killed her. Those who witnessed it said the light was beautiful.

## Prized Possessions
- **Theorem** -- A longsword forged from a single piece of horacalcum-alloy skymetal recovered from a Divinity debris field in the Felldales. Every flat surface of the blade was inscribed with microscopic binary code that Cassandriel spent three years writing -- each line a functional arcane formula expressed in Androffan machine language. The blade resonated at a frequency of 432 hertz when active, and left faint blue afterimages in the air when swung.
- **The Convergence Codex** -- A leather-bound journal filled with Cassandriel's research notes, written in a hybrid notation that used Androffan binary, Thassilonian arcane shorthand, and her own symbolic language. The Binary Choir preserved it after her death and continued her work for another century. Three of the spells she theorized were eventually proven functional by other magi.
