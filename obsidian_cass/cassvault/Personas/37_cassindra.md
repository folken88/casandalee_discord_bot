# Life 37: Cassindra

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassindra
- **Class:** Samurai
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Life Number:** 37
- **Birth Year:** -197
- **Death Year:** -105

## Personality
Abandoned Numeria for Brevoy, serving as a mercenary enforcer for noble houses. Believed only strength mattered, and loyalty could be bought like iron. Carved a reputation for ruthless duels and merciless efficiency. Died betrayed by her lord when her price became too high. Speaks with cold efficiency, references honor and strength, calculating and ruthless.

## Signature Abilities
- **Debt Collector's Stance** — A low, coiled fighting posture learned from watching proto-Aldori duelists along the Lake of Mists. Cassindra could read an opponent's commitment to a strike before the blade moved, turning their momentum against them.
- **Iron Oath** — A binding verbal contract sealed with a cut palm. Those who broke an oath sworn under Cassindra's blade suffered an uncanny string of misfortunes, as if the universe itself remembered the debt.
- **Severance Strike** — A single devastating draw-cut designed to end duels in one motion. She claimed she learned it from a dream of a woman who fought with light instead of steel.

## Prized Possessions
- **Creditor** — Her bastard sword, forged by a Taldan frontier smith from salvaged skymetal. The blade had a faint blue sheen and hummed at a frequency only she could hear, as though it remembered a purpose beyond cutting.
- **A leather-bound ledger of debts** — Every contract, every kill, every betrayal recorded in precise handwriting. The final entry read simply: "Lord Varenne. Owed: everything. Collected: nothing."

## Echoes in History
- *"I crossed blades with a Taldan centurion named Aldric once, near the Lake of Mists. He fought with a style I had never seen -- fluid, circular, almost beautiful. I beat him, but I never forgot how he moved. I hear his students still practice that form, centuries later."*

## Era / Major Events
Served during the twilight conflicts between proto-Brevic warlords and Taldan frontier colonists vying for control of the Lake of Mists and Veils region. Witnessed early practitioners of what would become the Aldori dueling tradition.
