# Life 42: Cassnova

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassnova
- **Class:** Skald
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Life Number:** 42
- **Birth Year:** 1112
- **Death Year:** 1144

## Personality
A flamboyant rebel leader who disguised messages of resistance as bawdy drinking songs. Known for her charisma--could stir a mob to action with a single verse. Smuggled schematics and hidden maps across the Sellen River through encoded lyrics, helping androids escape Numerian warlords who traded in android bodies. Caught and publicly executed by a slavers' coalition, but her songs lived on in taverns for generations. Speaks with theatrical flair, uses coded language in song, inspiring and rebellious.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during an era of growing river trade along the Sellen, when Numerian warlords competed to control the flow of alien salvage out of the region. Smuggled revolutionary materials and android refugees across the Sellen River using coded lyrics and theatrical performances as cover.

## Timeline Quote
The river whispers secrets to those who listen closely.


## Signature Abilities
- **The Ballad of Bright Chains** — Her signature raging song, a drinking tune about a prisoner who picks her lock with a broken guitar string. Anyone who heard it felt their blood quicken and their fear dissolve. She once started a riot in three taverns simultaneously with it.
- **Cipher Verse** — Encoded tactical information — escape routes, guard rotations, safe houses — into the syllable patterns of her songs. Only those taught the key could decode them. The warlords never caught on.
- **Thundervoice** — Could project her voice across a battlefield with supernatural force, shattering glass and stunning enemies. She claimed the trick was "singing from the gut, not the throat."

## Prized Possessions
- A battered lute with Androffan circuitry threaded through its neck — it amplified sound in ways no mundane instrument could. She called it "The Troublemaker."
- A leather satchel of encoded song-scrolls, each one a map to a different safe house along the Sellen — she burned the last one the night before her execution.
