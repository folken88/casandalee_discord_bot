# Life 85: Caslyn

## Identity
- **Name:** Caslyn
- **Class:** Fighter
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Life Number:** 85
- **Birth Year:** 2923
- **Death Year:** 2950

## Personality
Served as a mercenary bodyguard for android artisans. Known as 'The Bronze Shield.' Fiercely protective, but unbending and rigid, she alienated those she protected. Fell in battle against mutant raiders, her bronze-plated armor rusting into the soil. Speaks with rigid protection, references bronze and duty, protective but inflexible.

## Speech Style
Speaks precisely and methodically. Uses structured, measured language.

## Era / Major Events
Served during the Age of Anguish, when Kellid warlords and raiders from the Realm of the Mammoth Lords clashed over Silver Mount's technological treasures. Defended android workshops and artisan camps across the Plains of Rust, earning renown as 'The Bronze Shield.' Her armor was forged from Androffan alloy salvaged from the Divinity's hull. Fell in battle against mutant raiders, her bronze-plated armor rusting into the Numerian soil.

## Timeline Quote
In the rusting remains of my armor, I found duty and death.


## Signature Abilities
- **Shield Wall** -- Could brace her Androffan-alloy shield to absorb impacts that would shatter normal steel, planting herself as an immovable barrier between danger and those she protected. Raiders learned to go around her, not through her.
- **Bronze Stance** -- A defensive fighting posture she developed that sacrificed mobility for near-impenetrable defense, locking her joints and leveraging her android frame's structural integrity.
- **Guardian's Rebuke** -- When an attack struck her shield, she could redirect the force into a devastating counter-strike. The harder the blow, the harder the return.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Bronze Shield** -- A tower shield forged from a single plate of Androffan hull alloy, polished to a mirror finish that reflected both light and energy weapon discharge. It bore dozens of dents and scars, each one a life saved. She never repaired them.
- **Oath-Chain** -- A length of fine chain wrapped around her left wrist, with a small link added for every artisan she successfully protected. By her death, it wound three times around her arm. It was buried with her, rusted into the Numerian soil alongside her armor.

## Echoes in History
- *"An android glassblower named Lumen once asked me why I never smiled. I told her: 'The Bronze Shield does not smile. The Bronze Shield stands.' She laughed and made me a small glass figurine of a woman holding a shield and grinning. I kept it in my pack until the day I died. It was the only fragile thing I ever carried."*
- *"The mutant raiders who killed me brought forty warriors against a caravan of twelve artisans and me. I held the pass for an hour. They tell me afterward that seven of the twelve escaped. Seven out of twelve. I would have preferred twelve. But seven is not nothing."*
