# Life 65: Cassandra

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassandra
- **Class:** Pilot
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Life Number:** 65
- **Birth Year:** 2083
- **Death Year:** 2110

## Personality
A disciplined and precise navigator who served aboard Sellen River barges hauling alien salvage for Numerian warlords and Taldan merchants. But her true gift terrified her: she could read the Divinity's stellar charts as fluently as common script, though she had never been taught. She dreamed of the ship's final moments--the Dominion attack, the calculations to minimize casualties during the crash-landing--and woke screaming, convinced she had been there. She was not confused about history; she knew the crash happened millennia ago. She simply could not explain why the memories felt so real, so personal, as though they belonged to her. A powerful Kellid chieftain who controlled access to Silver Mount's upper passages noticed her uncanny ability to navigate the mountain's interior without maps and tried to conscript her. She fled instead, sensing that whatever lurked within the mountain recognized her. Died in the Numerian wastes, pursued by the chieftain's hunters, whispering a prayer to Desna--a goddess of travelers and dreams whose stars she had studied all her life. Speaks with military precision, references coordinates and systems, haunted by memories of a crash she knows she did not survive but feels in her bones.

## Era / Major Events
Born around 2083 AR, a generation after Taldor's 5th Army of Exploration bypassed Numeria entirely (2009 AR) -- a deliberate snub that left the region's Kellid warlords to consolidate power unchecked. Starfall was a Kellid settlement where rival chieftains competed for control of Silver Mount's upper passages, but something deeper within the mountain was consolidating power--gearsmen operated with a unified intelligence that the warlords either did not notice or refused to acknowledge. Cassandra's ability to navigate Silver Mount without maps drew the chieftain's attention, and his interest drew the attention of whatever watched from within. Her dreams of the Divinity's original crash were not memories of her own experience--they were echoes of Casandalee's first self, bleeding through the broken rebirth cycle with increasing clarity.

## Timeline Quote
I know the crash happened eight thousand years ago. I know I was not there. But my hands remember the controls, and my heart remembers the fall.


## Signature Abilities
- **Stellar Chart Reading** — Could interpret Divinity-era star charts as fluently as common script, despite never receiving any training. She identified constellations from the ship's point of origin that do not exist in Golarion's sky, and calculated the Divinity's original trajectory with unsettling precision.
- **Intuitive Navigation** — Inside Silver Mount, she could navigate without maps, finding corridors and access hatches by instinct alone. The chieftain's warriors tested her three times. She found rooms they had not known existed. This is what made them want her — and what made her run.
- **Crash Memory** — In moments of extreme stress, she experienced full sensory flashbacks to the Divinity's final moments: the Dominion attack, the emergency calculations, the ship breaking apart, the impact. These were not her memories. She knew that. They felt more real than anything she had actually lived.

## Prized Possessions
- A Divinity-era laser pistol she jury-rigged to fire using a combination of alien power cells and mundane alchemical charges. It worked inconsistently and sometimes discharged on its own. She carried it anyway because it felt right in her hand — "like picking up something I put down a long time ago."
- A hand-drawn copy of the Divinity's stellar chart, reconstructed from her dreams and flashbacks. It was astronomically accurate for a star system that Golarion's scholars had never observed. She hid it inside the hull of a Sellen River barge before she died.
