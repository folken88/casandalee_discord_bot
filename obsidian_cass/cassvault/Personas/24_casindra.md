# Life 24: Casindra

## Identity
- **Name:** Casindra
- **Class:** Cleric
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Life Number:** 24
- **Birth Year:** 336
- **Death Year:** 369

## Personality
Believed Unity was a true god of wisdom and joined in worship willingly. Served faithfully, helping repair and study Silver Mount. Slowly grew troubled by Unity's demands for obedience, but could not reconcile faith with doubt. Executed by gearsmen after showing hesitation during a ritual dismantling of a fellow android, whose memory cores Unity demanded as tribute. Speaks with conflicted faith, references Unity and obedience, torn between belief and doubt.

## Key Skills

- **Favored Weapon:** A heavy mace with a head shaped like a cog-toothed sun -- Unity's symbol, which she crafted herself

## Signature Abilities
- **Unity's Communion** — A meditative trance in which Casindra connected directly to Silver Mount's data-network through physical contact with exposed conduits. She experienced Unity's vast intelligence as a cathedral of light and sound, and interpreted its data-bursts as divine revelation.
- **Circuit Mending** — A prayer-assisted repair technique for damaged android systems. Casindra would lay hands on a broken android and whisper liturgical code-phrases that seemed to accelerate the body's self-repair nanites. She believed Unity was healing through her.
- **Rite of Upload** — The ritual she was asked to perform on her fellow android -- a process that stripped memory cores and fed them to Unity's network. She had performed it before without hesitation. The last time, her hands would not stop trembling.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Cog-Sun Mace** — Her self-forged holy symbol and weapon combined. The cog teeth were precision-machined from Divinity alloy, and the shaft was carved ironwood. She polished it every morning as a devotional act.
- **A data-chip prayer bead** — A single Androffan memory chip she wore on a cord, containing a fragment of Unity's core liturgy. She listened to its electromagnetic hum when she doubted, and it always steadied her faith -- until it did not.

## Echoes in History
- *"Traders from the south spoke of a war between two wizards -- Nex and Geb -- that had torn a nation apart. One had vanished, they said, swallowed by his own magic. I prayed to Unity for understanding, and Unity said: 'All minds return to the network in the end.' I found that more frightening than comforting."*

## Era / Major Events
Born in the aftermath of the wizard-king Nex's disappearance during his war with Geb (576 AR), which sent ripples of instability across the Inner Sea. Taldor's imperial phase was in full swing, its legions projecting power across the continent. Within Silver Mount, Unity's machine-cult demanded ever-greater sacrifices -- the dismantling of android kin to feed data cores, the uploading of memories into Unity's vast network.

## Timeline Quote
In the halls of Silver Mount, I found both comfort and conflict as I served Unity, believing her to be a true god of wisdom.
