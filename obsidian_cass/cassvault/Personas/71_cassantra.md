# Life 71: Cassantra

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassantra
- **Class:** Archivist
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Life Number:** 71
- **Birth Year:** 2329
- **Death Year:** 2363

## Personality
Collected 'death-songs' of androids—the static emissions released at their last moments. Compiled them into a library of strange melodies, believing they revealed truths about the soul. Respected as eccentric but harmless by tribes who sheltered her. Vanished during a nanite storm; her library still waits, hidden in a cave. Speaks of death-songs and static melodies, references soul truths and libraries, mystical and scholarly.

## Speech Style
Speaks calmly and objectively. Uses balanced, philosophical language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Death-Song Transcription** — Cassantra could attune herself to the electromagnetic emissions of a dying android, recording the "song" in a notation system she invented. Each transcription captured not just sound but emotional resonance, preserving something she believed was the android's departing soul.
- **Harmonic Analysis** — By comparing multiple death-songs, she could identify patterns and extract hidden data, occasionally recovering fragments of Androffan code or personal memories embedded in the static.
- **Resonance Ward** — A protective field generated by playing back a death-song at specific frequencies, creating an area where hostile nanites became dormant and radiation effects were temporarily suppressed.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Resonance Library** — Hundreds of thin metal plates etched with her death-song transcriptions, stored in a cave near the Sellen River headwaters. Each plate was catalogued with the name, designation, and final words of the android whose song it preserved. It remains hidden.
- **An Androffan tuning fork** — Salvaged from a ship's calibration kit, it produced a tone at the exact frequency of an android's baseline hum. She used it to begin every transcription session.

## Echoes in History
- *"I heard rumors of the war between Nex and Geb from traders who traveled the southern routes. Two archmages destroying each other over pride. I wondered: if I recorded their death-songs, would they sound different from anyone else's? I suspect not."*
- *"A Kellid oral historian once visited my cave and listened to the death-songs for three days. When she left, she told me I was keeping a graveyard. I told her I was keeping a choir."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the consolidation of Kellid war-chiefs in Southwest Numeria, and heard distant rumors of the growing conflict between Nex and Geb far to the south.

## Timeline Quote
