## Timeline Quote

## Signature Abilities
- **Encrypted Psalms** -- Sang in frequencies that contained hidden data, encoding Androffan histories and technical schematics into melodies that only androids could fully decode. To human ears, they were hauntingly beautiful but meaningless.
- **Bardic Recall** -- Could access memories from fourteen past lives through musical triggers, each life associated with a specific melody. Playing the right tune unlocked knowledge she had no other way to reach.
- **Dirge of Defiance** -- A war-song that bolstered android resolve and disrupted the control signals of hostile constructs simultaneously. Kellid warlords who heard it said the air itself trembled.

## Prized Possessions
- **Starfall Lute** -- A stringed instrument built from Androffan wire and a resonating chamber carved from Silver Mount's hull plating. Its tone was unlike any terrestrial instrument -- clear, metallic, and faintly reverberant, as if the notes echoed in a much larger space than the room they were played in.
- **The Song Scrolls** -- Sheets of treated hide inscribed with musical notation that doubled as Androffan code. Each scroll contained one psalm -- one encrypted history. She carried fourteen when she died, one for each life she remembered.

## Echoes in History
- *"I played the Starfall Lute in the trading post at Starfall's edge. A Kellid child sat at my feet and listened for hours. When I finished, she asked me to teach her the song. I taught her the melody but not the code. Years later, I heard a Kellid woman humming it in a market. The tune had survived, even if its meaning had not."*
- *"The warlord who ordered my death said my songs were 'inciting rebellion.' I told him: 'I am singing history. If your rule cannot survive the truth of the past, the problem is not my music.' He did not appreciate the distinction."*
