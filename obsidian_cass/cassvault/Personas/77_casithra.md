# Life 77: Casithra

## Identity
- **Name:** Casithra
- **Class:** Spiritualist
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 77
- **Birth Year:** 2593
- **Death Year:** 2615

## Personality
Bonded with the ghost of a former life—her own past incarnation. Lived with two voices in one body, often speaking in unsettling dialogue. Helped wandering androids recover lost memories, offering fragments of herself as guidance. Vanished during a ritual, leaving only static echoes in the air. Speaks in dual voices, references past lives and memory fragments, unsettling but helpful.

## Speech Style
Speaks gently and helpfully. Uses encouraging, supportive language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Phantom Communion** — Casithra could summon the echo of a past incarnation as a translucent phantom companion, speaking through it in a voice not her own. Those who witnessed it described hearing two conversations at once.
- **Memory Graft** — By touching an android's forehead, she could transfer fragments of her own remembered experiences into their consciousness, restoring lost identity data or simply offering comfort through shared memory.
- **Static Requiem** — A mourning ritual that drew ambient electromagnetic energy into a visible aurora, honoring the dead with light that hummed at the exact frequency of an android's final emission.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Twinned Mask** — A half-face mask carved from the shell of a broken holographic projector. When worn, it occasionally displayed a second face overlapping her own, flickering in and out. She claimed it was "the other one."
- **A journal written in two handwritings** — The left pages in Casithra's neat script, the right pages in an older, shakier hand she attributed to "the voice before me."

## Echoes in History
- *"I met a Sarkorian shaman once, far to the north. She recognized the phantom at my side before I introduced it. 'You carry your ancestor,' she said. 'No,' I told her. 'I carry myself.' She understood immediately."*
- *"There was a Kellid chief who lost his daughter to a nanite plague. I could not save her, but I grafted her last memory into a crystal so he could hear her laugh one final time. He broke it within a year. He said he could not bear to hear it and not hold her."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the increasing consolidation of Kellid tribal confederations and the early stirrings of Sarkorian shamanic traditions to the north, as well as the initial conflicts between Numerian tribes over control of crash sites.

## Timeline Quote
The echoes of my past life still whisper in my ears, guiding me through this world of shadows and light.
