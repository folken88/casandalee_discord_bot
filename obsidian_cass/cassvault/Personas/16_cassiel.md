# Life 16: Cassiel

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassiel
- **Class:** Druid
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Life Number:** 16
- **Birth Year:** -2732
- **Death Year:** -2580

## Personality
Walked with hybrid plants seeded from alien labs, creating a 'garden of chrome.' Believed nature was evolving to accept metal and code. Helped tribes survive famine with engineered crops. Perished when her garden was burned by raiders who feared its strangeness. Speaks of chrome gardens and evolving nature, references hybrid plants and survival, nurturing but strange.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the Age of Destiny, when Kellid clans had long established their seasonal migrations across Numeria. Created a 'garden of chrome' -- hybrid alien-terrestrial plants grown from the Divinity's hydroponics bays crossed with Golarion flora. Helped Kellid tribes survive famine with engineered crops that thrived in radiation-tainted soil near Silver Mount. Perished when raiders burned her garden, fearing its strangeness.

## Timeline Quote
The land whispered secrets to me, and I shared my knowledge to help our people thrive.


## Signature Abilities
- **Chrome Cultivation** -- Could coax hybrid plants from alien seeds mixed with Golarion soil, creating flora that metabolized radiation and produced edible fruit. The plants had metallic leaves and bioluminescent blossoms.
- **Verdant Communion** -- Spoke to plants through a form of empathic resonance, sensing their needs and health. Her hybrid chrome-plants responded to her presence by turning their leaves toward her like sunflowers tracking light.
- **Famine's End** -- Engineered radiation-resistant crop strains that could grow in tainted Numerian soil, producing enough food to sustain entire Kellid clans through lean seasons.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Seed Vault** -- A sealed Androffan container holding samples of every hybrid strain she created, preserved in stasis gel. She buried it beneath the garden's central tree before the raiders came. It has never been found.
- **Rootmother's Staff** -- A living staff grown from the first chrome-plant she cultivated, its surface covered in thin metallic bark that shifted color with the seasons. Small bioluminescent blossoms grew from its crown, providing soft light in darkness.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Kellid elder named Harvest-Moon brought her entire clan to see the garden. The children were afraid of the glowing plants at first. Then one of them ate a chrome-apple and declared it 'the best thing ever.' Within a week, the clan had moved their camp to the garden's edge. For three years, no one went hungry. Those were the best three years."*
- *"The raiders came at dawn. I could have run -- I knew every path out of the garden. But the Seed Vault was still unsealed. I chose to bury it instead of save myself. The garden burned around me. But the seeds survived. Somewhere beneath the ash, they are still waiting."*
