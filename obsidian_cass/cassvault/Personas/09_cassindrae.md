## Era / Major Events
Roamed the outskirts of Silver Mount during the final decades of the Age of Darkness and witnessed the sun's return in -3470 AR -- the end of nearly eight centuries of twilight. Mapped hidden tunnels and passages through the Divinity's wreckage, avoiding autonomous defense constructs that still patrolled the corridors. Carved secret symbols into the rocks to guide future rebels. Killed by a rogue defense construct, but her trail-marks endured for centuries.

## Timeline Quote
The mountains hold many secrets, if one knows where to look.


## Signature Abilities
- **Pathmaker's Mark** -- Carved subtle trail-signs into stone using a vibrating Androffan cutting tool, creating symbols only androids could interpret. The marks indicated safe passages, water sources, and gearsman patrol routes.
- **Terrain Ghosting** -- Moved through the tunnels and wreckage of Silver Mount without triggering automated defense systems, having memorized their sensor patterns over years of careful observation.
- **Favored Enemy: Construct** -- Knew the weak points of every gearsman and defense drone in Silver Mount's arsenal. Could disable a patrol unit in under six seconds using the right tool in the right joint.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Etching Blade** -- A modified Androffan cutting tool repurposed as both weapon and trail-marker. Its vibrating edge could carve through stone and metal alike, leaving the distinctive Pathmaker symbols that guided rebels for centuries after her death.
- **The Route Map** -- A roll of treated hide marked with every passage, shaft, and maintenance tunnel she discovered in Silver Mount's outer sections. Annotated with gearsman patrol schedules, radiation zones, and hidden water sources. She kept it in a waterproof Androffan container strapped to her thigh.

## Echoes in History
- *"I watched the sun return. After centuries of ash-sky, the first true dawn broke over the Plains of Rust in -3470. I was perched on a ridge above Silver Mount, checking a sensor array. The light hit the wreckage and it blazed like a fallen star. I sat there until noon, just watching shadows move properly for the first time in eight hundred years."*
- *"A Kellid scout named Ferris followed my trail-marks for weeks before he realized they were not natural formations. When he found me, he was furious. Then curious. We mapped the eastern tunnels together for a season. He was the only human who ever learned to read my signs."*
