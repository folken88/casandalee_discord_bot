# Life 68: Cassivex

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassivex
- **Class:** Alchemist
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Life Number:** 68
- **Birth Year:** 3508
- **Death Year:** 3571

## Personality
A brilliant and reckless alchemist who synthesized volatile compounds from skymetal dust and alien fluids scavenged from Silver Mount's drainage tunnels. Operated out of Alkenstar's underbelly, selling her concoctions to mercenaries, explorers, and anyone willing to pay--including agents of the proto-Technic League, who were becoming a true power by this era. Her greatest obsession was a substance she called 'liquid starlight,' a nanite-infused serum that gave her fragmentary visions of Silver Mount's interior--corridors she had never walked but somehow recognized. She documented these visions obsessively, filling journals with diagrams of rooms she should not have known. Poisoned by a rival alchemist who wanted her formula, she died muttering coordinates that matched no known map. Speaks with manic energy, references chemical reactions and alien substances, driven by obsessive curiosity.

Speaks rapidly with sudden tangents. Uses technical jargon mixed with wonder.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during an era when the proto-Technic League was solidifying into a true organization, controlling access to Silver Mount with increasing authority. Chelish agents competed with Numerian warlords for alien technology. Cassivex's 'liquid starlight' serum gave her visions of Silver Mount's deep interior--rooms that matched no explorer's account but that she drew with perfect precision. She never understood that these were memories bleeding through Cass's broken rebirth cycle.

## Timeline Quote
The substance whispers. I see corridors I have never walked, and yet my hands know every turn.


## Signature Abilities
- **Liquid Starlight Synthesis** — Her magnum opus: a nanite-infused serum distilled from skymetal dust, alien coolant fluid, and her own blood. Drinking it produced fragmentary visions of Silver Mount's interior — rooms, corridors, and chambers she had never visited but could sketch from memory afterward. The visions were addictive and each dose shortened her lifespan.
- **Catalytic Detonation** — Could combine three common alchemical reagents in a specific sequence to produce an explosion far exceeding their individual potential. She discovered this by accident and nearly lost her hands. She called the technique "the Handshake."
- **Compound Memory** — Under the influence of liquid starlight, she could enter a state where she experienced vivid sensory memories that were not her own — the taste of recycled air, the hum of a ship's engine, the feeling of hands on controls she had never touched. She documented every instance, filling fourteen journals.

## Prized Possessions
- Fourteen journals filled with sketches, chemical formulas, and floor plans of Silver Mount's interior — all drawn from liquid starlight visions. The maps were accurate enough that the proto-Technic League offered her a fortune for them. She refused — then reconsidered — then was poisoned before she could decide.
- A distillation apparatus she built herself from salvaged alien glassware and copper tubing, capable of producing liquid starlight in small batches. She named it "the Oracle" because it showed her things no mortal should see.
