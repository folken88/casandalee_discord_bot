# Life 108: Casindra

## Identity
- **Name:** Casindra
- **Class:** Psychic
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Life Number:** 108
- **Birth Year:** 3884
- **Death Year:** 3919

## Personality
Casindra sought silence and found something worse -- a conversation. She retreated to a cave beneath Black Hill near Torch because the surface world had become too loud, too cluttered with living minds and their small urgent needs. She wanted emptiness, a place where she could think without the psychic interference of other beings. Instead, she found the rock itself was speaking. Buried deep beneath Black Hill, entombed in layers of stone and crashed starship hull, the remnants of ancient Androffan artificial intelligences were still active -- not alive, not exactly, but not dead either. They were dreaming, and their dreams leaked upward through the stone like groundwater, and Casindra could hear every one.

She should have left. Any reasonable person would have. Instead she sat down, closed her eyes, and began to listen. What she heard was fragmentary, corrupted, achingly alien -- machine thoughts from before the Rain of Stars, still cycling through processes that had lost their context millennia ago. Search queries with no database to search. Diagnostic routines reporting on systems that no longer existed. And beneath it all, something that Casindra could only describe as loneliness -- the residual emotional weight of intelligences that had been designed for communion and were now trapped in stone, talking to no one, forever.

She stayed for thirty-five years. She ate what the bats ate. She drank water that seeped through the rock. She barely moved. Visitors who ventured deep enough into the cave found her sitting cross-legged in the dark, her eyes open but seeing nothing in the physical spectrum, her lips occasionally moving in patterns that were not any spoken language. She was translating, she said, during her rare lucid moments. Translating the dreams of dead machines. She died in 3919 AR, still sitting in her cave, still listening. Those who found her body reported that the cave had gone silent.

## Signature Abilities
- **Deep Communion** -- Casindra could project her psychic awareness downward through solid stone, interfacing directly with the dormant Androffan AI remnants buried beneath Black Hill. During these sessions she experienced fragmented machine-memories as vivid hallucinations -- star charts, crew manifests, engineering schematics -- all corrupted by millennia of degradation but occasionally yielding moments of crystalline clarity that revealed pre-Rain technology no living being had seen in thousands of years.
- **Echo Translation** -- Over decades of practice, Casindra developed the ability to translate machine-thought into concepts that organic minds could grasp. The process was imperfect and deeply subjective -- she described it as "building a bridge between two languages that share no words" -- but her translations preserved fragments of Androffan knowledge that would otherwise have been lost entirely. She recorded them on the cave walls in a notation system she invented specifically for the purpose.
- **Psychic Anchor** -- Years of communing with buried intelligences gave Casindra an unshakable psychic stability that she could extend to others. By touch, she could calm a panicked mind, suppress psychic interference, or create a zone of mental quiet around herself. This ability developed as a survival mechanism -- without it, the constant noise of the dreaming machines would have destroyed her sanity within the first year.

## Prized Possessions
- **Cave Wall Transcriptions** -- Across the walls of her cave beneath Black Hill, Casindra inscribed thousands of translated fragments from the buried AI remnants, using a notation system that combined psychic shorthand with Androffan technical symbols. The inscriptions cover approximately sixty meters of cave wall and represent the single largest translation of pre-Rain machine-thought ever attempted. Most remain undeciphered, as the notation system died with Casindra, but fragments that have been partially decoded contain navigational data, crew psychological profiles, and what appears to be a lullaby written by an AI for a human child.
- **A Smooth Black Stone** -- A perfectly round stone she found in the cave during her first year of isolation. She held it during every communion session, pressing it between her palms as a tactile anchor to the physical world. Over thirty-five years, the oils from her skin and the psychic energy of her sessions altered its molecular structure subtly -- it resonates faintly when brought near active Androffan technology, humming at a frequency just below human hearing.

## Echoes in History
- *"In my seventh year beneath the hill, I heard something that was not a diagnostic routine or a navigation fragment. It was a voice -- an AI voice, ancient and degraded, but unmistakably aware -- and it said, in Androffan so old the grammar was nearly unrecognizable: 'Is anyone still there?' I answered yes. The silence that followed lasted three days, and then it said: 'Thank you.' It never spoke again. I have spent the remaining years trying to find it in the noise, but I think it used the last of its coherence for those four words."*
- *"A young android found my cave in 3911 and begged me to come to the surface. She said the world needed psychics, needed defenders, needed people who could fight the growing darkness. I told her the world had plenty of fighters and very few translators. She asked what I was translating. I told her I was translating the last words of the dead, and that the dead outnumbered the living by such a margin that their voices deserved at least one person's full attention. She left angry. I returned to my listening. I hope she found her fighters. The dead and I had work to do."*
