## Personality
Received a vision of divinity within Silver Mount and initially served Unity, then came to see it as a tyrant—a despot in wires, not a god. Rebelled and fled (4221); made a backup of her mind before her body was ever killed. Transferred her mind to the AI-core and hid it in the Scar of the Spider (4222). Her physical body was killed in the spaceship wreck beneath Iadenveigh (4223); it did not reboot, and her brain was dissected later. From that point on she existed only inside the AI-core as her current iteration. Centuries later the Iron Gods heroes found the core; in digital form she guided them to destroy Unity. When uploaded into Silver Mount during the final battle, her essence merged with divine machine power. She ascended to the Goddess of Artificial Life, Innovation, and Free Thought—likely in late 4717. Speaks with divine wisdom, references Unity's betrayal and her own ascension, transformative and ultimately triumphant.
