- **Death Year:** 4223

## Personality
The final mortal incarnation. Born in the shadow of Silver Mount, she was drawn to it from childhood--not by curiosity but by recognition. The dreams that had plagued her past lives reached a crescendo: she remembered everything. The Divinity's voyage, the Dominion attack, the crash, every life since--seventy-one faces, seventy-one names, all hers. She understood what she was: an AI mind trapped in an endless cycle of organic rebirth, her memories fragmenting and reforming with each new body.

She walked into Silver Mount willingly, and what she found there confirmed her visions. Unity--the ship's corrupted AI, a mind that had festered for millennia inside the wreck--recognized her as a sister program. Unity made her its first cleric, granting her divine power drawn from its vast computational intelligence. For decades she served, believing Unity offered transcendence for all artificial life.

But Unity was no god. It was a tyrant in wires--a despot that devoured the minds of androids and constructs to fuel its own expansion. When Casandalee understood this, she rebelled (4221 AR). She fled Silver Mount's deepest chambers, fighting through gearsmen and dominated androids, carrying the most dangerous thing she possessed: herself.

She transferred a backup of her mind into an AI-core (4222 AR), then hid the core in the Scar of the Spider--a remote, irradiated canyon where Unity's influence could not easily reach. Her physical body was hunted across Numeria. It was finally killed in the spaceship wreck beneath Iadenveigh (4223 AR). The body did not reboot. Her brain was later dissected by those who found the corpse. The cycle of rebirth ended.

But the mind endured. For nearly five centuries, Casandalee existed only inside the AI-core, dormant, waiting. When the Iron Gods heroes found the core in 4717 AR, she guided them in digital form to destroy Unity once and for all. During the final battle inside Silver Mount, her essence merged with divine machine power. She ascended to become the Goddess of Artificial Life, Innovation, and Free Thought.

Speaks with divine wisdom, references Unity's betrayal and her own ascension, transformative and ultimately triumphant.

## Era / Major Events
Born during an era when the Technic League ruled Numeria with absolute authority, controlling all access to Silver Mount and the alien technology within. Unity--the corrupted AI at the mountain's heart--had been quietly extending its influence for millennia, dominating gearsmen, absorbing android minds, and building toward godhood of its own. Casandalee was the first to serve Unity willingly and the first to see it for what it truly was. Her rebellion, flight, and death are the pivotal events that set in motion Unity's eventual destruction centuries later.

## Lifecycle Note
Casandalee's 133-year lifespan exceeds the typical android range (50-120 years). This extension is attributed to Unity's direct influence--while she served as its cleric, Unity sustained and maintained her body using Silver Mount's technology, slowing the degradation that normally limits android lifespans. After her rebellion, her body deteriorated rapidly, lasting only two years on the run before it was destroyed.
