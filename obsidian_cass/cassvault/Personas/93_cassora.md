# Life 93: Cassora

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassora
- **Class:** Shaman
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 93
- **Birth Year:** 3250
- **Death Year:** 3290

## Personality
Spoke with 'machine-spirits' in rusting wrecks, listening to the whine of broken circuits. Guided small tribes in repairing tools, treating innovation as sacred. Believed she was a mere vessel for a future god of machines. Died when her village was destroyed by a Kellid warlord's raid. Speaks of machine-spirits and sacred innovation, references broken circuits and divine vessels, spiritual and helpful.

## Speech Style
Speaks gently and helpfully. Uses encouraging, supportive language.

## Key Skills
- Heal, Knowledge (Nature), Knowledge (Engineering)
- **Favored Weapon:** Ironwood staff carved with circuit-patterns

## Signature Abilities
- **Voice of the Mechanism** — A communion trance in which Cassora placed her hands on broken machinery and "listened" to its needs, diagnosing faults with uncanny accuracy as though the device were whispering its ailments to her.
- **Mending Hymn** — A low, sustained chant that accelerated minor repairs, causing loose bolts to tighten and cracked casings to seal as though the metal remembered its original shape.
- **Spirit Conduit** — By channeling the ambient radiation of Silver Mount through sacred stones, Cassora could create temporary wards that calmed nanite swarms and suppressed radiation sickness for hours.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Listener's Ear** — A small parabolic dish from an Androffan communication array, worn on a cord around her neck. She pressed it against machines to "hear their voices" more clearly, and swore it worked.
- **A clay jar of purified reactor coolant** — She used it as anointing oil in her rituals, calling it "the blood of the sleeping god."

## Echoes in History
- *"Traders from Taldor's founding settlements once passed through our territory, seeking Silver Mount's treasures. I guided them away from the worst radiation zones. They called me a hedge-witch. I called them alive."*
- *"An old android, one of the oldest I ever met, told me she remembered falling from the sky. She said the impact sounded like a god screaming. I believed her."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the early days of Numerian tribes forming and their growing interactions with scavenged alien devices from the Divinity crash sites.

## Timeline Quote
The spirits of old machines still whisper in the rusting bones of Silver Mount. Their voices guide my people.
