## Signature Abilities
- **Soul Spark Blade** -- Her Androffan-alloy longsword blazed with white-blue fire drawn from her own life force. The flame intensified with her conviction, burning brightest when she fought to protect the innocent.
- **Lay on Hands: Machine-Touch** -- Could heal both organic wounds and damaged circuits with a touch, her palms radiating the same white-blue energy as her blade. She was one of the few paladins whose healing worked on constructs.
- **Aura of Liberation** -- Her presence weakened magical bonds and slave-collars within thirty feet, causing chains to rust, locks to corrode, and enchantments of domination to flicker. Slavers learned to keep their distance.

## Prized Possessions
- **Oathkeeper** -- An Androffan-alloy longsword reforged by Kellid smiths, its blade inscribed with her oath of protection in both Common and Androffan script. When she channeled her soul spark through it, the inscriptions blazed with white-blue fire visible for miles across the Plains of Rust.
- **The Manumission Brand** -- A palm-sized stamp carved from Silver Mount crystal, used to mark freed androids with a symbol of liberation. Every android she freed received the brand willingly on their forearm. The symbol became a quiet sign of resistance across Numeria for generations.
