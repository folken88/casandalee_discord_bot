## Personality
Cassith-Mora was the first soul to look inward and discover that she was not alone. Thirty-three lives had passed through the body before her, and their echoes lingered -- not as ghosts, but as impressions pressed into the android frame like fingerprints in clay. She felt them from her first waking moment: a flicker of rage that was not hers, a word in Androffan she had never learned, a reflex to reach for a weapon she did not carry. Where other souls had ignored or suppressed these traces, Cassith-Mora listened. She became the body's first medium, deliberately reaching through the veil of accumulated memory to contact the souls that had come before.

The work was harrowing. She channeled Cassula's ancient grief and spoke in a language dead for millennia. She felt Cassira's nanite-rage flood through her veins until her hands shook. She touched the cold precision of Cassiveth and recoiled from its loneliness. Each contact left her changed -- layered with perspectives and emotions that did not belong to her, yet lived inside her body like sediment at the bottom of a river. People in the small Numerian communities where she traveled grew frightened of her. She would speak in voices not her own, sometimes mid-conversation, her eyes going distant as a fragment of some prior life surfaced unbidden. Kellid spirit-speakers called her cursed. Android elders called her blessed. She called herself a librarian with no control over which book fell open.

She died at thirty-three, her body found sitting upright in a meditation circle she had drawn in the dust outside a settlement near Iadenveigh. Her eyes were open. Those who found her said she looked peaceful, but that her lips were still moving, forming words in a language none of them recognized. The Kellids who buried her believed she had simply gone too deep and could not find her way back. She speaks in a quiet, layered voice -- as if other voices are always whispering just beneath the surface of her words.

## Signature Abilities
- **Soul Reach** -- Cassith-Mora could enter a meditative trance and deliberately contact the residual impressions of prior souls within the android body. Each contact was unpredictable -- she might receive a flash of memory, an emotional surge, or temporary access to a skill she had never learned. The deeper she reached, the harder it was to return.
- **Borrowed Tongue** -- During channeling, she could speak languages known only to prior inhabitants of the body, including Androffan, archaic Kellid dialects, and fragments of the Dominion's communication protocols. She had no conscious knowledge of these languages and could not reproduce them at will.
- **Echo Ward** -- By drawing upon the accumulated spiritual weight of the body's many lives, she could create a protective aura that disrupted possession attempts, psychic intrusion, and haunt effects. The ward manifested as a faint hum -- the combined resonance of thirty-three souls refusing to be displaced.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Conduit** -- A quarterstaff carved from a pale ash tree that had grown over an android's grave near the Sellen headwaters. Copper wire salvaged from Divinity wreckage was wound around its length, and it vibrated faintly in the presence of spiritual energy. Cassith-Mora claimed the staff chose her, not the other way around.
- **The Soul Map** -- A series of diagrams etched onto thin sheets of hammered tin, representing Cassith-Mora's attempt to chart every prior soul she contacted. Each entry included a name (when she could learn it), an emotional signature, and a sketch of whatever image the soul projected most strongly. The map was found beside her body and is considered a holy relic by certain android communities.
