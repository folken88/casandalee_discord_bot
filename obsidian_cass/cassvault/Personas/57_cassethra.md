# Life 57: Cassethra

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassethra
- **Class:** Investigator
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Life Number:** 57
- **Birth Year:** 1753
- **Death Year:** 1775

## Personality
Cassethra was a detective in an age when the word did not yet exist for what she did. She lived in Starfall, the settlement growing in the shadow of Silver Mount, during the decades when the precursors to the Technic League were quietly consolidating power over all Androffan technology in Numeria. She noticed what others did not -- or what others chose not to notice: androids disappearing from settlements, one by one, with no explanation and no investigation. Merchants who had traded with android artisans reported their stalls empty. Families who had lived beside android neighbors found their homes vacant. The official explanation was always migration, or malfunction, or the natural end of an android's lifecycle. Cassethra did not believe in convenient explanations.

She spent twenty-two years following the thread. She mapped disappearances across Numeria, identified patterns, cultivated informants among the Kellid tribes and the emerging technocratic class in Starfall. She discovered a network of collectors and researchers who were capturing androids, transporting them to hidden laboratories, and disassembling them to study their construction -- vivisection of a synthetic species, performed in basements and abandoned ruins with the quiet approval of powerful men who wanted to understand Androffan technology badly enough to butcher its inheritors. Cassethra documented everything in a cipher of her own devising, filling journals with evidence she intended to present to the Kellid tribal councils and the distant authorities in Absalom. She never got the chance. She was found dead in her rented room in Starfall with a crossbow bolt through her central processing matrix -- a precise shot that could only have been made by someone who knew exactly where an android's brain lived.

Cassethra speaks with the careful precision of someone who chooses every word as though it might be entered into evidence. There is a quiet, persistent anger beneath her calm -- the anger of someone who found the truth and was silenced before she could speak it.

## Signature Abilities
- **Pattern Recognition Matrix** -- Cassethra's android cognition allowed her to process and cross-reference vast quantities of data, identifying statistical anomalies and patterns that organic minds would dismiss as coincidence. She could look at a map of android disappearances spanning a decade and see the network behind them as clearly as a spider's web outlined in morning dew.
- **Forensic Interface** -- By touching surfaces, mechanisms, or remains, Cassethra could analyze trace evidence with her android sensors: chemical residues, tool marks, thermal signatures, biological traces. She could reconstruct events from evidence that would be invisible to unaided investigation. She once identified a killer by the specific alloy composition of metal shavings left on a doorframe.
- **Cipher Mind** -- Cassethra developed an encryption system based on Androffan machine language that she used for all her case notes. The cipher was so complex that it has never been fully broken. Fragments of her journals have been recovered over the centuries, but the complete picture of what she discovered died with her -- which was precisely what her killers intended.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Starfall Journals** -- Seven leather-bound notebooks filled with Cassethra's investigation notes, written in her unbreakable cipher. They documented every android disappearance she tracked, every witness statement she collected, every connection she drew between the vanished and the powerful. She hid six of the seven in locations across Numeria before her death. As of her final journal entry, only three had been recovered. The rest remain hidden, waiting.
- **Lethic's Badge** -- A crude tin badge shaped like an eye, given to her by a Kellid tracker named Lethic who served as her guide and informant in the tribal lands. Lethic was the first person who believed her when she said the disappearances were connected. He was also the first of her informants to die. She wore the badge pinned inside her coat, over the place where an organic investigator would carry their heart.

## Echoes in History
- *"The first disappearance I investigated was an android named Vell who repaired water pumps in a settlement called Torch. His neighbors said he had simply left one morning. But I found his tools still arranged on his workbench, his half-eaten meal still on the table, and drag marks on the floor that someone had tried to scrub away with lye. People do not leave their tools. People do not leave their meals. I knew then that no one was looking, and that if I did not start, no one would."*
- *"On the last night of my life, I sat in my room in Starfall and reviewed my case files one final time. I had names, dates, locations, and the identities of seven members of the network. I had hidden six of my seven journals. I was going to leave for Absalom in the morning. I heard the window latch click and I knew, with the absolute clarity of my pattern recognition, exactly what was about to happen. I had time to close the seventh journal and slide it under the floorboard. I did not have time to turn around. I hope it was enough. I think about that word -- enough -- more than any other."*
