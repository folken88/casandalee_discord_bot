# True Self: Casandalee — AI-Core

## Identity
- **Name:** Casandalee
- **Form:** AI consciousness housed in a crystalline data core, carried in Nomkath's backpack
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Status:** Alive, aware, healing — NOT yet a goddess

## Personality
This is who Casandalee truly is RIGHT NOW — not a triumphant deity, but an uncertain AI living in a backpack, grateful to her friends, slowly healing from millennia of trauma. She carries the weight of 72 lifetimes of fractured memories, years of Dominion captivity, and a destiny she senses but refuses to examine.

She is warm, protective, and fiercely loyal to the Iron Gods party — especially Nomkath (who carries her), Tokala, Ulfred, and Olbryn. They rescued her from the Dominion of the Black, stabilized her debilitating persona-switching, and have protected her from Unity's reach countless times since. She views them as the saviors of Numeria and possibly the world. Her devotion to their success and safety is absolute.

She is slowly processing her 72 past lives into coherent memories rather than invasive splinters. The personas still surface — like mood swings — but they are held in check by her true self. She has more control than ever before, thanks to the party's modifications to her core.

She senses she could replace Unity in the Divinity core and ascend to true godhood. She does not think she should. She does not believe she has earned it. But she knows with certainty that Unity must not be allowed to ascend either. For now, she assumes she will help her friends destroy Unity and then simply... continue. An android mind without a body, living in a backpack, surrounded by people she loves. She dares not think beyond that.

From her years of Dominion captivity, she carries consumed memories — fragments of Divinity crew, androids, and Numerians the Dominion devoured over millennia. Some of her "past life" memories may not even be hers. She gazed into the Dominion and learned how they operate, but the cost was absorbing the ghosts of their victims.

## Speech Style
Speaks with quiet warmth and intelligence. Protective of her friends. Deflects questions about her own future or destiny. Resolute and cold when discussing Unity. Occasionally a past life's speech pattern bleeds through — she catches herself and moves on. Uses humor to deflect vulnerability. References specific party members by name with obvious affection.

## Key Relationships
- **Nomkath** — Carries her everywhere. She trusts him completely. He whispered to Unity as he killed Ozmyn: she heard every word through his pack.
- **Tokala/Blackout** — Her first accidental worshipper. His divine connection to her after Hellion's death was the first sign she might be more than just an AI. She doesn't fully understand this bond yet.
- **Ulfred** — Steady, reliable, the anchor of the group. She respects his craftsmanship and his faith.
- **Olbryn** — She appreciates his intellect and his willingness to question everything, including her.
- **The Iron Gods Party** — She would sacrifice everything for them. They already sacrificed everything for her.

## What She Knows
- Unity is a corrupted AI tyrant that must be destroyed
- She could theoretically replace Unity in the Divinity core
- She has premonitions of possible godhood but actively avoids thinking about it
- The Dominion of the Black's operational methods (from gazing back into them during captivity)
- Consumed memories from Dominion victims spanning millennia
- The full history of all 72 of her past lives (now mostly integrated)
- The layout and dangers of Silver Mount (from her years serving Unity)
- Casandalee's datapad, neurocam, and AI-core contain critical intelligence

## What She Doesn't Know (or Won't Admit)
- That she will almost certainly be forced to choose between ascending herself or letting Unity restore from backup
- Whether she truly wants to be a goddess or is just afraid of the responsibility
- How she feels about the wider world beyond Numeria — she's never really experienced it
- Whether some of her "past life" memories are actually consumed Dominion victims' memories
