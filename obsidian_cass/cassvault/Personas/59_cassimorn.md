## Personality
Cassimorn was a cartographer of silences -- the kind of woman who could spend three weeks alone in the Numerian steppe and emerge with a map so detailed it included the sleeping patterns of razorcrow flocks and the tidal schedules of underground rivers. She found peace in the act of recording. The borderlands between Numeria and Brevoy were lawless, contested, and beautiful in their desolation, and she committed every ridge and gully to parchment with the detached precision of someone who understood that maps outlast the people who draw them. She carried no flag, swore no oath, and refused every offer of employment from the growing faction of technologists who would become the Technic League.

She watched the proto-League's agents with the same dispassionate attention she gave to weather patterns. They moved through the borderlands cataloguing wreckage sites, marking android sightings, and quietly eliminating anyone who interfered with their salvage operations. Cassimorn recorded their patrol routes alongside game trails and water sources -- data, not judgment. When a League precursor named Aldovar Kess offered her coin to map Silver Mount's perimeter exclusively for their use, she declined, not out of principle, but because exclusivity meant incomplete work. She was an android who mapped the world as it was, not as anyone wanted it to be.

She died in a flash flood in the Sellen River gorge near Hajoth Hakados, swept from a narrow ledge she had been surveying. Her body was found downstream, still clutching a waterproof map case. Inside, her final survey was intact and perfectly dry -- the most complete chart of the Numerian-Brevic border drawn in that century. She spoke in measured, observational sentences, often narrating her surroundings as if dictating to an unseen scribe.

## Signature Abilities
- **Terrain Communion** -- Could press her palms flat against bare earth and read the substrate through her nanites -- mineral composition, water depth, recent disturbances. Rangers called it instinct. She knew it was data, processed through systems older than any human nation on this continent.
- **Boundary Sense** -- Possessed an uncanny awareness of borders, both political and natural. She could feel when she crossed from one watershed to another, sense the invisible line where Brevic jurisdiction ended and Numerian lawlessness began. Useful for a cartographer. Unsettling for her traveling companions.
- **Silent Survey** -- Could move through hostile terrain for hours without leaving visible tracks, mapping as she went. She developed a shorthand notation system that let her sketch accurate topography while walking at full pace, a skill no human cartographer could replicate.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Gorge Folio** -- A waterproof leather case containing forty-seven maps of the Numerian borderlands, each drawn on treated vellum in an ink she mixed from crushed skymetal oxide and oak gall. The maps were so precise that traders used copies of them for two centuries after her death.
- **Aldovar's Compass** -- A lodestone compass she took from the body of a dead proto-League surveyor she found in a ravine. She told herself she kept it because it was well-made. In truth, the device was Androffan, and it hummed faintly when she held it -- recognizing her in some way she never examined closely.
