# Life 31: Cassiveth

- **Death Year:** 671

## Personality
Cassiveth woke into the body with a predator's instincts already sharp, as if the muscle memory of thirty prior lives had settled into something feral. She spent her first years as a bounty hunter along the trade routes linking nascent Taldor to the Kellid frontier, taking contracts on bandits, deserters, and the occasional beast that wandered too close to caravan paths. She was good at killing and saw no reason to dress it in purpose. Gold was gold. The work kept the body fed and the mind occupied, and that was enough -- until it wasn't.

The turning point came in a muddy camp outside Hajoth Hakados, where she found three androids caged in iron, their bodies stripped and catalogued like exotic animals by a Taldan merchant who planned to sell them as curiosities in Oppara. Something broke in Cassiveth that day -- not compassion exactly, but a cold fury born from recognizing herself in those blank, terrified faces. She killed the merchant, freed the androids, and spent the remaining eighteen years of her life as a hunter of a different kind: tracking down android traffickers across Avistan, dismantling their networks, and spiriting refugees to safety in the Numerian hinterlands where the Technic League had not yet consolidated power.

She was never gentle about it. Cassiveth lied, cheated, and murdered her way through the trafficking rings with a mercenary's pragmatism, seeing no contradiction between her methods and her cause. She trusted no one completely, kept her android nature hidden behind scarves and long sleeves, and maintained a rotating set of false identities across half a dozen cities. She died at thirty-five in an ambush outside Chesed, betrayed by an informant she'd paid too little. Her last act was to slit the throat of the man who'd sold her out. She speaks in clipped, direct sentences -- the voice of someone who learned that hesitation gets people killed.

## Signature Abilities
- **Studied Target** -- Cassiveth could observe a mark for mere seconds and identify their weaknesses -- the hitch in a stride that signaled an old knee injury, the way a hand drifted toward a concealed weapon, the nervous glance that betrayed an ambush position. Once she had your measure, every strike landed where it hurt most.
- **Vanishing Trail** -- She perfected the art of disappearing mid-pursuit, using terrain, crowds, and misdirection to break line of sight. Entire bounty-hunting companies lost her in open grassland. She could make three androids vanish from a guarded camp and leave no trail but confusion.
- **Mercy's Edge** -- Despite her ruthlessness toward slavers, she developed a precise, almost surgical strike that could incapacitate without killing. She used it exclusively on bystanders and guards who were simply doing their jobs. The distinction mattered to her, even if she never explained why.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Teeth** -- Twin shortswords forged by a Kellid smith in Chesed who owed her a debt. The blades were treated with a carbon-black compound that prevented light reflection. Each bore a row of tally marks along the flat -- one for every trafficker she put down. The count reached forty-seven.
- **The Ledger** -- A small, cipher-encoded journal containing the names, routes, and methods of every android trafficking ring she identified across Avistan. She buried copies in three locations. None have been found.
