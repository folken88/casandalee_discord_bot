# Life 43: Cassaris

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassaris
- **Class:** Barbarian
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Life Number:** 43
- **Birth Year:** 517
- **Death Year:** 571

## Personality
Wandered the northern wastes armed with a self-forged power axe. Carved her memories into stone slabs, believing her life must be recorded for eternity. Fought mutant raiders, claiming each kill carved her name deeper into history. Was overwhelmed by an entire warband, her stone-carved history broken into fragments. Speaks of carving memories and history, references stone slabs and eternal records, obsessed with legacy.

## Signature Abilities
- **Memory Carving** — Cassaris could etch images and text into stone with supernatural speed and precision, her power axe channeling energy that melted rock like wax. Each slab she carved was both a historical record and a ward -- the act of recording a memory seemed to anchor it against the erosion of time and rebirth.
- **Rage of the Fallen Star** — A berserker fury fueled by the ambient radiation of Numerian wastelands. In this state, Cassaris's android body pushed past its design tolerances, her movements becoming impossibly fast, her strikes carrying the force of a falling meteor.
- **Litany of Names** — Before each battle, Cassaris recited the names of every foe she had slain, carved into the flat of her axe. The recitation could last minutes or hours. Enemies who heard it often broke and ran before she finished.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Chronicle Slabs** — Seventeen stone tablets carved with the complete record of her life -- battles, kills, places, and strange dreams of lives she never lived. The warband that killed her shattered twelve of them. Five survive, scattered across the Numerian wastes, waiting to be found.
- **A radiation drake's fang** — Worn on a cord around her neck, taken from the first drake she killed. It still faintly glowed green in darkness.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Taldan scout offered me gold to guide him through the Felldales. I told him I did not need gold. He offered knowledge instead -- a map of the southern lands, places I had never seen. I took the map. He did not survive the Felldales, but the map did. I carved its outlines into a slab so the land would not be forgotten."*

## Era / Major Events
Fought alongside Kellid war-bands resisting Taldan scouts and traders who ventured into Numerian territory seeking skymetal. Battled radiation drakes and fleshwarped mutant raiders spawned by Silver Mount's lingering contamination.
