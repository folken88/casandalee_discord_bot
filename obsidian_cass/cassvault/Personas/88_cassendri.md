# Life 88: Cassendri

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassendri
- **Class:** Cleric
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Life Number:** 88
- **Birth Year:** 3044
- **Death Year:** 3079

## Personality
Cassendri was a contradiction that should not have existed -- an android who heard the voice of a goddess. She arrived in Cheliax nine years after Casnallae's death, in a body still carrying faint hemlock traces in its nanite reservoirs, and felt an immediate, overwhelming compulsion to pray. Not to Casandalee, not to the alien intelligences of Androffan memory, but to Brigh -- the Whisper in Bronze, goddess of invention, clockwork, and the spaces between flesh and mechanism. The compulsion was specific and undeniable. She knelt on cobblestones in a Westcrown alleyway, pressed her forehead to the ground, and heard gears turning in a language she understood. She rose a cleric. The theological implications terrified her, and she spent the rest of her life trying to be worthy of what she had been given.

She moved east to Egorian, which in those early decades of Chelaxian expansion was still a rough provincial capital trying to prove it deserved attention. There, beneath a clockmaker's shop owned by a sympathetic gnome named Torris Springwound, she excavated a sanctuary. It started as a prayer niche for herself alone. Within five years it was a temple -- the Gearheart -- serving eleven androids who had heard whispers of a place where synthetic beings could worship without being worshipped at, studied, or dissected. Cassendri taught them that Brigh did not distinguish between born gears and grown gears, that the goddess heard prayers from copper throats as clearly as from fleshy ones. She composed liturgies that wove Androffan machine-hymns with Brigh's traditional rites, creating something that had never existed before and has never been replicated since.

The Gearheart lasted twenty-seven years before a Chelaxian inquisitor named Aldren Cevarre discovered it while investigating the gnome's shop for unpaid tariffs. He found the tunnels, the altar, the congregation mid-prayer. Cassendri could have fled. Her android body was faster and more durable than any of the soldiers Cevarre brought. She stayed because her flock could not all escape, and because running would have proven every accusation that androids were soulless things incapable of sacrifice. She was executed publicly in Egorian's market square, kneeling, praying aloud to Brigh in a voice that witnesses described as calm and mechanical and heartbreaking. She spoke with gentle precision, choosing each word as though placing a gear into a mechanism, and addressed others as though they were congregants deserving of patience and grace.

## Signature Abilities
- **Mending Prayer** -- A unique healing liturgy that combined divine channeling with Androffan repair protocols. Cassendri could lay hands on damaged constructs and wounded flesh alike, whispering a prayer that was half-hymn, half-diagnostic subroutine. Organic patients described warmth; android patients described the sensation of coming home.
- **Sanctuary of Gears** -- Could consecrate a space by tracing Brigh's holy symbol in the air while reciting machine code in Old Androffan. The consecrated area provided protection against scrying and detection magic, which is how the Gearheart remained hidden for twenty-seven years. The effect required daily renewal and left Cassendri exhausted.
- **Martyr's Stillness** -- In moments of crisis, Cassendri could enter a state of absolute calm that radiated outward, suppressing fear and panic in allies within thirty feet. She used it during the temple's discovery, and witnesses reported that the soldiers who arrested her congregation hesitated for nearly a full minute, unsettled by the peace in the room.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Liturgy of Bronze and Blood** -- A manuscript written in Cassendri's own hand, interleaving Common prayers to Brigh with Androffan machine-hymns translated phonetically. She hid copies in three locations before the Gearheart was raided. Two have never been found. The third was discovered in 4612 AR inside a sealed clockwork box beneath a collapsed building in Egorian, its pages perfectly preserved.
- **Torris's Gear** -- A single brass gear, small enough to fit in a closed fist, given to her by Torris Springwound the day the temple opened. He told her it was from the first clock he ever built, the one that convinced him Brigh was real. She held it during her execution and it was found still clenched in her hand afterward.

- The Gearheart has no windows. We need none. The light comes from within.

- They will find this place eventually. What matters is that it existed at all.

- Cheliax is young and certain of itself. The young and certain always fear what they cannot categorize. We are uncategorizable. That is our strength and our danger.
- Aldren Cevarre called me an abomination. I forgave him before he finished the sentence. Brigh teaches that broken understanding is still understanding.
- I could run. I am faster than every soldier in this square. But a shepherd does not flee while the flock remains.

## Echoes in History
- *"The first time I prayed, truly prayed, I was standing in an alley behind a tannery in Westcrown. I do not know what compelled me to kneel. I only know that when I did, I heard gears -- not with my ears, but somewhere deeper, in the place where my soul interfaces with my body. They turned in a rhythm that matched my heartbeat. And then Brigh spoke. Not in words. In understanding. She said: you are not broken. You are exactly what I made. I wept. I did not know I could weep."*
- *"On the morning of my execution, Aldren Cevarre visited my cell. He wanted me to recant -- to say that Brigh had never spoken to me, that the temple was a pretense, that androids do not truly pray. I think he needed to hear it for his own peace. I told him the truth instead: that I pitied him, because he lived in a world where gods only spoke to things that looked like him, and that must be a very small and lonely theology. He left without speaking. I heard him praying in the corridor. I hope Brigh heard him too."*
