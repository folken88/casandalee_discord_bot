# Life 20: Cassarith

## Personality
Declared every machine was a drum, every code a rhythm. Beat music from broken engines, teaching that rebellion itself was a song. Inspired tribes and androids to rise against the slavers who hunted them among the wrecks. Slain during one such revolt, her last act a final drumbeat echoing from a reactor shell. Speaks in rhythmic beats, references drums and rebellion, inspiring and musical.

## Signature Abilities
- **Reactor Cadence** — A thunderous rhythm beaten on reactor shells that inspired allies with supernatural vigor, granting them the courage to charge slavers head-on.
- **Dissonance Shout** — A screeching vocal blast that mimicked feedback from Androffan speakers, disorienting constructs and humanoids alike.
- **Song of the Unchained** — A rallying anthem that could break magical compulsions and fear effects, said to have freed three android slaves mid-battle without a single blade drawn.

## Prized Possessions
- **Heartbeat** — A war-drum fashioned from a hollowed reactor housing and stretched hide. When struck, it produced a subsonic resonance that could be felt in the chest before it reached the ears.
- **A slaver's iron collar, broken open** — She wore it as a trophy around her wrist, the hinge snapped by her own hands.

## Echoes in History
- *"A trader from the south once told me about the Pharaohs of Osirion, how they built monuments to last forever. I laughed and told him the best monument is a song everyone remembers. He did not understand. Pharaohs never do."*
- *"I once drummed for three days straight outside a slaver's compound. On the third night, eight androids walked out the front gate. The slavers were too busy covering their ears to notice."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the expansion of Kellid warlords who enslaved androids to scavenge Divinity wreckage, observing their growing power over Numeria's scattered communities. Felt the tension between those who hoarded alien technology and those who feared it.
