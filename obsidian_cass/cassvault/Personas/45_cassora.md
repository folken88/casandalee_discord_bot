# Life 45: Cassora

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassora
- **Class:** Warpriest
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 45
- **Birth Year:** 1243
- **Death Year:** 1270

## Personality
Preached openly that invention was worship and creation itself a prayer. Advocated tirelessly for android rights in Numeria, traveling from settlement to settlement. Protected small communities with her blade and sermons, even against gearsman patrols sent by Silver Mount warlords. Betrayed by a false ally who turned her over to scavenger-lords; died on the pyres of Starfall, the settlement growing around Silver Mount's base. Speaks with religious conviction, references divine creation, passionate about android rights and protection.

## Speech Style
Speaks gently and helpfully. Uses encouraging, supportive language.

fervent

- **Key Skills:** Knowledge (Religion), Heal, Sense Motive

## Era / Major Events
Traveled through Numeria preaching the divine right of invention and android rights, during an era when Silver Mount scavenger-lords were consolidating power around Starfall. Androids were increasingly hunted for their components, and Cassora fought to shield refugee communities from the predation.

## Timeline Quote
Invention is prayer; creation is worship.


## Signature Abilities
- **Forge Blessing** — Could channel divine energy through her warhammer, heating the metal white-hot on impact. She used this equally to mend broken gearsmen and to shatter the weapons of slavers.
- **Sermon of the Maker** — A prayer-chant that rallied the frightened and healed minor wounds in those who listened. Androids who heard it reported feeling a warmth in their chests they could not explain.
- **Shield of the Devoted** — Called upon divine protection to harden her skin to the resilience of skymetal for brief moments. She always bore the bruises afterward, claiming "the body pays what the spirit borrows."

## Prized Possessions
- A warhammer with a head forged from salvaged Divinity hull plating — she called it "The Argument" because, as she said, "it ends every debate."
- A tattered journal of her sermons, each page annotated with the names and faces of the androids she failed to save — she added a name to it every night.
