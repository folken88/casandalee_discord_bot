# Life 4: Cassorael

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassorael
- **Class:** Psychic
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Life Number:** 4
- **Birth Year:** -4122
- **Death Year:** -4008

## Personality
Attempted to reconstruct the entirety of her past lives through memory discipline. Spent decades meditating in ruins, piecing together fragments of herself. Gained frightening clarity, speaking with the voices of dozens at once. Her mind collapsed under the weight, leaving her a babbling husk. Speaks in multiple voices, references memory fragments and reconstruction, fragmented but insightful.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the depths of the Age of Darkness, when perpetual twilight smothered Numeria. Observed Kellid shamans warring over access to Silver Mount's technological treasures, and the slow emergence of orc war-bands from the Darklands below. Meditated in the Divinity's ruins, piecing together Androffan memory systems to reconstruct her fragmented past lives.

## Timeline Quote
In the quiet halls of old ruins, I found solace and a thousand voices whispering through time.

## Signature Abilities
- **Memory Cascade** -- Could trigger a controlled flood of past-life memories, experiencing days of recalled knowledge in seconds. Devastating for insight, but each cascade left cracks in her present identity.
- **Psychic Resonance** -- Attuned to the emotional residue left in places and objects, reading the psychic impressions of everyone who had touched them. Androffan artifacts sang to her like old friends.
- **Mind Fortress** -- Constructed layered mental defenses from the architecture of the Divinity's firewalls, creating a psychic barrier modeled on shipboard security protocols.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Memory Lattice** -- A web of thin Androffan wire wrapped around a fragment of stasis crystal, which she used as a meditation focus. Each node in the lattice represented a recovered memory from a past life. By her death, it held forty-seven nodes.
- **Cassula's Conductor Baton** -- A corroded multi-tool she recovered from the Divinity's wreckage, recognizing it as something "her hands remembered." She could not explain how she knew its name.

## Echoes in History

- *"I once spoke in Cassula's voice for three days without realizing it. When I returned to myself, I had repaired a conduit in Silver Mount that no one else could reach. My hands knew what my mind had forgotten."*
