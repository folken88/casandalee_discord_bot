## Personality
Known as 'The One Who Never Missed.' Patrolled the wilds to protect caravans and android enclaves from raiders, wielding a salvaged Androffan beam-rifle. A stoic wanderer, feared by bandits for her unerring aim. Slain in a raider ambush near the Plains of Rust—her rifle still said to be perfectly balanced, passed from hand to hand. Speaks with stoic precision, references aim and protection, methodical and reliable.

## Signature Abilities
- **Dead-Eye Protocol** — A targeting subroutine Cassrin developed by interfacing with her beam-rifle's damaged targeting computer. She entered a trance-like state where her breathing and heartbeat synchronized with the weapon's charge cycle, producing shots of inhuman precision.
- **Suppressive Arc** — A sweeping beam discharge that created a wall of searing light across a corridor or pass, forcing enemies into cover and buying time for those she protected to retreat.
- **Field Strip Communion** — A meditative maintenance ritual where she disassembled, cleaned, and reassembled Whisper in total darkness by touch alone. She performed it nightly, and claimed the rifle "spoke" to her during these sessions.

## Prized Possessions
- **Whisper** — Her Androffan beam-rifle, salvaged from a Divinity wreck-site and painstakingly maintained across decades. Its targeting display was cracked but functional, and its energy cell had been replaced with a jury-rigged skymetal capacitor. Said to be "perfectly balanced," it passed from hand to hand after her death, but no one else could shoot it as well.
- **A carved bone tally-stick** — One notch for every caravan she escorted safely. Forty-seven notches. No notches for the ones she lost, because she remembered those without needing to count.

## Echoes in History
