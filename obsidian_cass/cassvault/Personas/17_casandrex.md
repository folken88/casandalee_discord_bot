# Life 17: Casandrex

## Identity
- **Name:** Casandrex
- **Class:** Swashbuckler
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 17
- **Birth Year:** 46
- **Death Year:** 69

## Personality
Casandrex was a blade of light in an age of ambition, a wandering duelist who crossed the Inner Sea with nothing but a rapier, a grin, and an android body that drew stares in every port. Born only a century and a half after the body woke from its nearly three-thousand-year sleep, Casandrex inherited a form that remembered ancient Numeria but found itself in a world dominated by Taldor's golden banners and the freshly-founded walls of Absalom. Everything was new. The languages had changed, the gods were different, the stars had shifted in their courses. Casandrex responded to this overwhelming newness the only way that made sense: by laughing at it and picking fights.

He dueled his way from Oppara to Absalom, from the docks of Cassomir to the merchant squares of Almas, never for money and rarely for malice. He fought because the ring of steel was the one constant he could find in a world that bore no resemblance to the body's oldest memories. He was charming and reckless, quick to defend the underdog and quicker to challenge anyone who mistook his android nature for weakness. Taldan nobles found him infuriating and magnetic in equal measure. He won forty-seven duels before his forty-eighth opponent -- a Taldan blade-lord named Corvius Asterion -- ran him through with a poisoned blade during what was supposed to be a duel to first blood. Casandrex died at twenty-three, laughing, because Corvius had cheated and that struck him as deeply, cosmically funny.

His voice is quick and bright, full of momentum and deflection. He makes jokes when he is hurting, turns compliments into challenges, and treats every conversation like a bout -- parry, riposte, touch. Beneath the bravado is a soul that was overwhelmed by how much the world had changed and chose spectacle over grief.

## Speech Style

## Signature Abilities
- **Riposte of the Unnamed** -- A signature counter-attack that turned an opponent's lunge into an opening. Casandrex would sidestep at the last instant and deliver a precise cut to the wrist, disarming without maiming. He called it "unnamed" because he refused to name a technique he was still perfecting.
- **The Laughing Guard** -- A deceptively casual defensive stance that baited aggressive opponents into overextending. Casandrex would lean back with his off-hand raised as though applauding, drawing his opponent forward into a trap of angles.
- **Crowd's Favor** -- An instinctive ability to play to an audience during combat, turning duels into spectacles that drew cheering crowds. The attention energized him, sharpening his reflexes the louder the crowd became.

## Prized Possessions
- **Applause** -- A rapier of Taldan make, its swept hilt etched with silver filigree depicting a laurel wreath. Won in his ninth duel from a minor Opparan noble who had wagered the blade against Casandrex revealing his "true nature." Casandrex won, took the sword, and told the noble his true nature was "impatient."
- **The Tally Cord** -- A braided leather cord worn at his wrist, with forty-seven small knots tied along its length, one for each duel won. He was tying the forty-eighth when he died. The knot was never finished.
