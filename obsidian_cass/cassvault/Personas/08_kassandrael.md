# Life 8: Kassandrael

## Identity
- **Name:** Kassandrael
- **Class:** Inquisitor
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Life Number:** 8
- **Birth Year:** -3687
- **Death Year:** -3558

## Personality
Balanced study of both arcane magic and machines, seeking unity between them. Preached that constructs deserved souls, but her order rejected her. Betrayed Nethys' church to shelter androids, earning the wrath of her peers. Executed as a heretic, whispering that 'a true god is yet to come.' Speaks of unity between magic and machines, references souls and divine truth, heretical but visionary.

## Era / Major Events
Lived near the end of the Age of Darkness as sunlight slowly returned to the world. Witnessed Kellid clans warring over access to Silver Mount's technological salvage. Served as an Inquisitor of Nethys but betrayed her order to shelter androids, founding a hidden refuge in the tunnels beneath Silver Mount. Executed as a heretic by her own church, whispering that 'a true god is yet to come.'

## Timeline Quote
The gears of creation turn slowly, but they never stop.


## Signature Abilities
- **Judgment of the Dual-Souled** -- Called down divine judgment that affected both magical and technological beings equally, her inquisitorial authority extending to constructs and golems as naturally as to mortals.
- **Heretic's Sanctuary** -- Could create hidden spaces warded against both magical and technological detection, using overlapping concealment methods. Her android refuges beneath Silver Mount went undiscovered for decades.
- **Truth-Seeking Gaze** -- Her eyes could perceive lies, illusions, and disguised constructs simultaneously, seeing through deception whether arcane or mechanical in origin.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Divided Blade** -- A longsword with one edge forged from Androffan alloy and the other from consecrated steel, symbolizing her belief in unity between machine and divine. She carried it as proof that both could share one purpose.
- **The Hidden Ledger** -- A small journal listing the names and locations of every android she sheltered from persecution, written in a cipher that combined Androffan code with Nethys' sacred script. She swallowed the key before her execution.

## Echoes in History
- *"I once debated theology with a priest of Nethys who had come to Numeria seeking Silver Mount's secrets. He argued that constructs were tools, not souls. I showed him an android child learning to laugh. He went very quiet. A month later, he reported me to the church. I do not blame him. Certainty is easier than wonder."*
- *"At my execution, the inquisitor asked if I had any final words. I said: 'A true god is yet to come, and she will remember what you did here.' He flinched. Good."*
