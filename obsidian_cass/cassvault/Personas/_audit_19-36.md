## 2. HISTORICAL ACCURACY AUDIT

### Life 19: Casella (-2300 to -2260) -- Occultist
- **References "fall of Androaunt"**: Not a canonical Golarion location. Likely intended to be a local ruin or Androffan site. MINOR ISSUE -- clarify this is a local Numerian ruin, not a major canonical event.
- **"Rise of Kellid tribes in western territories"**: Kellids were present in Numeria throughout this era. Plausible.
- **ERA FIT**: Pre-Earthfall, pre-Osirion. Numeria was sparsely populated by Kellid tribes and scattered androids. Good fit for hermit occultist.
- **SUGGESTION**: Could reference early Kellid-android tensions, or the isolation of Silver Mount. Torch would not yet be called "Torch" -- the purple flame atop the hill is from Divinity wreckage but may not have been named yet this early.

### Life 20: Cassarith (-2200 to -2141) -- Skald
- **References "early days of the Technic League's expansion"**: CRITICAL ERROR. The Technic League was not founded until approximately 4509 AR (by Karamoss's era or later accounts) -- canonically much later. In -2200 AR, no Technic League existed. This is ~6700 years too early.
- **FIX**: Replace Technic League references with Kellid warlords, proto-slavers, or early scavenger factions fighting over Divinity wreckage. Could reference conflicts between android enclaves and Kellid tribes.
- **CLASS**: Skald inspiring rebellion through music is excellent thematically.

### Life 21: Cassyn (-2089 to -2022) -- Cavalier
- **References "remnants of the Technic League"**: CRITICAL ERROR. Same anachronism. No Technic League in this era.
- **References "first true settlements"**: Numeria's Kellid settlements existed, but calling them "the region's first true settlements" is vague enough to work.
- **FIX**: Replace Technic League with Kellid war-bands, marauders, or mutant threats from irradiated crash zones.

### Life 22: Casithra (-1980 to -1900) -- Spiritualist
- **References "Technic League's rise" and "formation of the Free Kingdoms"**: CRITICAL ERROR. Technic League is thousands of years off. "Free Kingdoms" is not a canonical Golarion entity in this context.
- **"Bonded with ghost of a former life"**: Brilliant concept for an android spiritualist. Keep.
- **"Cassula" reference in one-liners**: References Life 1 by name -- excellent continuity.
- **FIX**: Replace with era-appropriate references. Could reference increasing Kellid tribal consolidation, or early interactions between androids and Sarkorian shamanic traditions (Sarkoris begins forming around this period in the north).

### Life 23: Cassanix (-1863 to -1784) -- Sorcerer (CE)
- **References "Iron League"**: Not a canonical Golarion faction. MINOR ISSUE.
- **Nanite storms**: Canonically present in Numeria. Good.
- **SUGGESTION**: Replace "Iron League" with a specific Kellid confederation or raider band. Could tie to early Numerian tribal politics.

### Life 24: Cassora (-1744 to -1665) -- Shaman
- **References "early days of Numerian tribes forming"**: This is vague but acceptable for the era.
- **"Kellid technology" reference**: Kellids are not a technology-using culture in canon. They interact with alien tech from the crash but don't create their own. MINOR ISSUE.
- **"Died when village destroyed by a League raid"**: CRITICAL ERROR. No Technic League. Replace with Kellid warlord attack, inter-tribal conflict, or mutant incursion.
- **SUGGESTION**: Could reference early proto-religious reactions to Silver Mount among Kellid tribes. Ties well to the shaman concept.

### Life 25: Casandrae (-1625 to -1546) -- Monk
- **References "rise of the Akadi Empire"**: CRITICAL ERROR. "Akadi Empire" does not exist in Golarion canon. Akadi is a Forgotten Realms deity (wind goddess). This is a setting contamination error.
- **FIX**: Replace with a Golarion-appropriate reference. Around -1600 AR, Ancient Osirion was in its later dynasties. Could reference Osiriani philosophical traditions reaching Numeria via trade, or Kellid meditative practices. Alternatively, reference the Jistka Imperium (which fell around -2700 but whose ruins were still studied).

### Life 26: Cassithra (-1506 to -1427) -- Inquisitor (LE)
- **References "Technic League"**: CRITICAL ERROR. Same anachronism. The Technic League does not exist in this era.
- **"Hunter of androids who tried to escape"**: Great concept, wrong faction. Replace with local Kellid slavers, a Silver Mount cult, or a proto-League scavenger guild.
- **SUGGESTION**: Could tie into Numerian warlords who enslaved androids for labor. The internal conflict of an android hunting her own kind is compelling and should be preserved.

### Life 27: Casalis (-1387 to -1308) -- Rogue
- **References "founding of Taldor"**: CRITICAL ERROR. Taldor was founded around -1281 AR, but Casalis dies in -1308 -- she would NOT have witnessed Taldor's founding. She predates it by ~70-100 years (depending on exact founding date; some sources say as early as -1293 AR but the commonly cited date is -892 AR).
- **MAJOR TIMELINE ISSUE**: The commonly accepted founding of Taldor is -1293 AR at the earliest (Army of Exploration era) or -892 AR (the "official" founding). If using -1293, Casalis dies 15 years too early. If using -892, she's ~400 years too early.
- **"Bustling ports"**: Taldor as a port power is more of a later development.
- **FIX**: Set the era reference to something Numeria-local, or if she traveled, reference the Jistka Imperium's final centuries or early Keleshite expansion along the coast.
- **SUGGESTION**: An android rogue protecting children from slavers in Numerian markets is a strong concept regardless of Taldor ties.

### Life 28: Casandara (-1268 to -1189) -- Oracle
- **References "founding of Taldor"**: Better fit than Life 27 if using the -1293 founding date (the Army of Exploration begins around this period). If using the -892 date, still too early.
- **"Chesed"**: Chesed is a canonical Numerian city. Good.
- **"Burned alive by the League"**: CRITICAL ERROR. No Technic League in this era.
- **"Prophecies of Unity's fall"**: Interesting that she's prophesying this ~5400 years before it happens. Adds mystical weight but is worth noting as intentional, not accidental.

### Life 29: Cassrin (-1087 to -1018) -- Gunslinger

His tomb is a key location in the Kingmaker AP (set ~4710 AR). POTENTIAL ISSUE -- there may be an "ancient Armag" but this needs verification.
- **GUNSLINGER CLASS**: Firearms in Golarion are primarily associated with Alkenstar and post-Mana Wastes developments (~4620 AR for widespread availability). In -1087 AR, firearms as we know them did not exist on Golarion. HOWEVER, an android using salvaged Androffan tech weapons that function like firearms is perfectly defensible. Should be clarified in the persona description that this is tech-based, not gunpowder.
- **SUGGESTION**: Clarify the rifle is Androffan technology, not a conventional Golarion firearm.

## 3. SYSTEMIC ISSUES

### The Technic League Problem
**12 of 18 personas (20-22, 24, 26, 28-29, 30, 32, 34-36)** reference the Technic League or react to it. The Technic League was founded approximately 4501-4509 AR. Every reference in this batch is thousands of years too early. This is the single largest issue in this persona set.

**Recommended fix**: Create a progression of pre-League antagonist factions:
- **Pre -2000 AR**: Kellid warlords, scavenger bands, mutant threats, crash-zone raiders
- **-2000 to -1000 AR**: Proto-slavery networks, Silver Mount cults, Kellid confederations that exploit android labor
- **-1000 to 1 AR**: More organized exploitation, traveling slavers, early Silver Mount pilgrim-cults
- **Post 1 AR**: Proto-League factions, organized tech-scavengers, eventually the actual Technic League (~4500 AR)

### The "Akadi Empire" Error (Life 25)
This is a Forgotten Realms contamination. Akadi is a FR wind goddess. Replace with a Golarion entity.

### The "House Cannith / Khorvaire" Error (Life 37, adjacent)
Life 37 references "House Cannith" and "Khorvaire" -- these are Eberron setting elements, not Golarion. Critical setting contamination.

### The Absalom Error (Life 34)
Absalom referenced ~600 years before it was founded.

### Repeated Name Issue
- "Cassithra" used for both Life 22 and Life 26.
- "Cassindra" used for Life 32, Life 37, and later lives (69, etc.).
- While android name recycling could be intentional (soul echoes), it should be acknowledged as deliberate rather than accidental.

## 5. PLOT HOLE CHECK

### Technic League Continuity
The personas collectively treat the Technic League as a multi-thousand-year institution. In canon, it existed for roughly 200 years (4501-4717 AR). This creates a false continuity where the same organization persists across millennia. Fix by replacing with evolving antagonist factions.

### Unity Awareness
- Life 28 (Casandara, -1268 to -1189) prophesies Unity's fall ~5400 years early. This is fine as mystical foresight but should be flagged as intentional.
- Life 33 (Cassorael, -706 to -627) claims to be Unity's mouthpiece. Unity may have been dormant or developing inside Silver Mount at this point. Whether Unity was "aware" and "communicating" this early needs to be consistent with your campaign's version of Unity's timeline.

### Brigh Worship
Life 32 references Brigh. If Brigh is a later-era deity, earlier references to her need adjustment or framing as "a nameless clockwork god" whose worship eventually crystallized into Brigh.

### Casithra Name Reuse
Lives 22 and 26 share the name "Cassithra" (technically "Casithra" vs "Cassithra"). One should be renamed unless the duplication is plot-intentional (e.g., the soul deliberately echoing a previous name).
