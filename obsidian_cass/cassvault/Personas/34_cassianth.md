# Life 34: Cassianth

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassianth
- **Class:** Kineticist
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Life Number:** 34
- **Birth Year:** -605
- **Death Year:** -475

## Personality
Drew raw power from malfunctioning reactors, her skin glowing with plasma burns. Wandered the growing junkyard wastelands near Silver Mount, offering her volatile strength as a mercenary. Believed her body was only a vessel for cosmic flame. Died when her power flared uncontrollably, incinerating half a bandit camp. Speaks with volatile energy, references cosmic flame and power, unpredictable and destructive.

## Signature Abilities
- **Reactor Siphon** — Cassianth could draw energy directly from malfunctioning reactors and radiation sources, converting ambient power into kinetic force channeled through her fists and feet. The process left her skin cracked and glowing.
- **Plasma Bloom** — An explosive release of stored energy in a spherical burst, incinerating everything within arm's reach. She used it as a last resort, as it damaged her own body as much as her targets.
- **Ember Walk** — A movement technique where she superheated the ground beneath her feet, leaving glowing footprints and making pursuit dangerous. She could cross irradiated zones that would kill unprotected travelers.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Scorch Journal** — A fireproof metal notebook in which she sketched the locations of every reactor, radiation zone, and energy source she discovered across the wastelands. The maps were invaluable to scavengers, but she never shared them freely.
- **A pair of insulated gauntlets** — Woven from heat-resistant fibers salvaged from a Divinity escape pod. They cracked and reformed constantly, mirroring the state of her own body.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Silver Mount cultist once tried to recruit me, promising power beyond imagining. I showed him my hands, glowing with reactor-fire, and asked him what power he thought I was lacking. He left quickly."*
- *"I met a Kellid firewalker who danced through bonfires as a spiritual trial. I told her I walked through something hotter every day. She asked if it was a trial. I told her it was just Tuesday."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the growing power of Silver Mount scavenger cults and the radiation-scarred wastelands expanding around the crash sites. Traveled the desolate stretches between Numeria's scattered settlements, selling her destructive talents to the highest bidder.
