# Life 105: Casalune

## Identity
- **Name:** Casalune
- **Class:** Witch
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 105
- **Birth Year:** 3763
- **Death Year:** 3790

## Personality
Casalune was the kind of person who would spend three hours coaxing a wounded fox out from under a bush and consider it time well spent. She settled on the edges of the Felldales -- that ragged stretch of wild borderland where civilization frayed into something older and stranger -- and built herself a cottage from salvaged timber and Androffan hull plating. She kept a garden of medicinal herbs interspersed with half-functional Numerian devices, and visitors could never quite tell where the botany ended and the technology began. Neither could she, and she preferred it that way.

Her magic was a hybrid thing, drawn from a patron she described only as "the Compiler" -- an entity or intelligence she communed with through her familiar, a mechanical raven she had rebuilt from scavenged parts and animated with a sliver of nanite-code. Through this patron she accessed a style of witchcraft that blurred the line between spell and program, hex and subroutine. She used it to heal -- stitching torn flesh with threads of guided nanites, purging infections with targeted micro-enchantments, repairing damaged automata with whispered incantations that were half prayer and half machine instruction. She never charged for her services and never turned anyone away, regardless of species.

Her most improbable achievement was teaching a local goblin tribe rudimentary binary. It started as a joke -- she was trying to explain to their chief why her raven kept clicking at them -- but the goblins took to it with alarming enthusiasm, adopting binary sequences as a secret language for their scouts. Within a year, the Ashteeth tribe was coordinating ambushes using patterns of torch-flashes that spelled out instructions in ones and zeroes. Casalune found this simultaneously delightful and slightly concerning. She died peacefully in 3790 AR, in her garden, surrounded by the mechanical animals she had repaired over the decades. The goblins mourned her for a full season and left offerings of shiny things on her grave for years afterward.

## Signature Abilities
- **Nanite Mending Hex** -- Casalune could weave her nanites into a targeted healing matrix, guiding them with witch-magic to repair both organic tissue and mechanical components. The process looked like fine silver threads emerging from her fingertips and sinking into the wound, knitting flesh or re-soldering connections with equal facility. She used this on everything from broken bird wings to damaged construct limbs, treating the distinction between living and mechanical as largely academic.
- **The Compiler's Whisper** -- Through her patron, Casalune could commune with the residual intelligence in Androffan technology, coaxing damaged devices into partial function or extracting fragments of stored data. She experienced this as a gentle conversation rather than a forceful extraction, and insisted that the machines appreciated being asked nicely. Whether this was literally true or simply effective methodology, her success rate with ancient technology was remarkable.
- **Binary Ward** -- A protective hex expressed as a sequence of ones and zeroes that Casalune could inscribe on surfaces or weave into the air. The pattern created a field that disrupted hostile nanite activity and interfered with technological surveillance. The Ashteeth goblins learned a simplified version and used it to mark their territory, though they understood it as "the click-witch's keep-away signs" rather than as a mathematical ward.

## Prized Possessions
- **Copperbeak** -- Her familiar, a mechanical raven assembled from at least seven different Androffan devices and held together with witch-magic, spit, and stubbornness. Copperbeak could speak three languages (Common, Androffan, and a creditable approximation of Goblin), served as Casalune's conduit to her patron, and had a personality best described as "opinionated." It continued to function for several years after her death before finally going still on the roof of her cottage, facing east.
- **The Patchwork Bestiary** -- A hand-illustrated journal documenting every creature Casalune had healed, both organic and mechanical, with detailed notes on their anatomy, temperament, and preferred treats. The margins are filled with sketches of goblins attempting binary and mechanical foxes sleeping in sunbeams. It is one of the few genuinely joyful documents to survive from this era of Numerian history.

## Echoes in History
- *"The Ashteeth chief came to me one winter with a broken automaton he had found in a ravine. It was ancient -- Androffan, pre-crash, barely functional. He asked if I could fix it. I spent three weeks on it, Copperbeak translating the machine's fading diagnostics while I replaced corroded components with whatever I could scavenge. When it finally powered on, it said a single word in Androffan -- 'home' -- and then went dark again forever. The goblin chief patted its casing and said, in perfectly accented binary, 'Same.' I never asked him what he meant. I think I already knew."*
- *"My garden was always full of visitors. Not just patients -- residents. A three-legged fox who refused to leave after I healed her. Two mechanical beetles I had repaired that now pollinated my herbs more efficiently than any bee. A goblin scout who came every Tuesday to practice binary and steal my bread. I used to sit on my porch in the evenings and watch them all coexisting -- organic, mechanical, goblin, fox -- and think: this is what the world is supposed to look like. This small, ridiculous, patchwork thing. This is it."*
