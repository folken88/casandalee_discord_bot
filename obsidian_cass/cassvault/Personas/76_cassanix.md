# Life 76: Cassanix

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassanix
- **Class:** Sorcerer
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Life Number:** 76
- **Birth Year:** 2535
- **Death Year:** 2575

## Personality
Drew raw power from nanite storms, bending constructs to her will. Proclaimed herself a 'queen of metal and bone,' ruling over a gang of raiders. Cruel and unpredictable, she made enemies faster than she made allies. Murdered in her sleep by her own lieutenant. Speaks with cruel authority, references metal and bone, unpredictable and power-hungry.

## Signature Abilities
- **Nanite Dominion** — Cassanix could channel raw energy from nanite storms to seize control of constructs, bending their programming to her will. The effect was temporary but terrifying, turning a tribe's own mechanical guardians against them.
- **Crown of Corrosion** — A self-targeting transmutation that wreathed her head in a halo of corroding sparks, granting her an aura of dread that weakened the resolve of anyone who looked upon her.
- **Iron Marrow** — A painful self-enhancement that hardened her synthetic skeleton with absorbed metal particles, making her nearly impervious to blades for short bursts but leaving her joints locked and aching afterward.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Rust Crown** — A circlet of twisted nails and corroded alloy salvaged from Silver Mount's outer shell. She claimed it "chose her" when it adhered to her forehead during a nanite storm. It left a permanent burn mark she wore as a badge of sovereignty.
- **A lieutenant's severed finger-bone** — Kept in a vial around her neck. Not from the one who killed her, but from the first one who tried.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Jistka golem-builder passed through my territory once, fleeing the collapse of his Imperium. He begged for safe passage. I granted it, for a price: he taught me the command-words for constructs. He thought he was buying his life. He was buying mine."*
- *"I dreamed once of a woman who looked like me but knelt in prayer before a circuit board. She was gentle. She was weak. I woke disgusted, but the dream would not leave me."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the rise and fall of the Rust Rider confederation, raiding their caravans and fortresses with a gang of metal-minded followers across the Felldales.

## Timeline Quote
