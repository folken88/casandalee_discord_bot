# Life 39: Cassrilyn

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassrilyn
- **Class:** Tinker

## Personality
Famous for crafting living clockwork animals, often gifted to children and drunks in taverns. Treated each of her creations as her children, teaching them songs and games. Her constructs spread widely, and some still wander the wilds, breeding strange 'generations.' Died in her workshop explosion, laughing as her pets scurried to safety. Speaks with maternal warmth, references her clockwork children, creative and nurturing.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the early growth of Absalom as a trade hub, with Aroden's cult spreading across the Inner Sea. Plagued by vivid dreams of Azlant's fall during Earthfall -- echoes from Cass's broken memory bleeding through millennia. Her clockwork creations were inspired by Numerian salvage and an intuitive connection to Brigh, goddess of clockwork and invention.
