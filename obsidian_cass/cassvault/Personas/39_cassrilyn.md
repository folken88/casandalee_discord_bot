# Life 39: Cassrilyn

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassrilyn
- **Class:** Tinker
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Life Number:** 39
- **Birth Year:** 80
- **Death Year:** 120

## Personality
Famous for crafting living clockwork animals, often gifted to children and drunks in taverns. Treated each of her creations as her children, teaching them songs and games. Her constructs spread widely, and some still wander the wilds, breeding strange 'generations.' Died in her workshop explosion, laughing as her pets scurried to safety. Speaks with maternal warmth, references her clockwork children, creative and nurturing.

## Speech Style
Speaks passionately, sometimes rebelliously. Uses vivid, emotional language.

## Key Skills
- Craft (Clockwork), Disable Device, Knowledge (Engineering)
- **Favored Weapon:** A spring-loaded wrench she affectionately called "The Persuader"

## Signature Abilities
- **Breath of Brigh** — A whispered prayer to the goddess of clockwork and invention that Cassrilyn spoke over each finished creation. She did not know where she learned it. The prayer seemed to give her constructs an uncanny liveliness that other tinkers could never replicate.
- **Sympathetic Resonance** — By tuning a construct's mainspring to a specific harmonic frequency, she could make multiple clockwork animals move in coordinated patterns, as though they shared a single mind.
- **Spark of Awareness** — Her most controversial technique: embedding a sliver of Numerian nanite-crystal into a construct's core, giving it a rudimentary capacity to learn and adapt. Some of her "children" developed distinct personalities.

## Prized Possessions
- **Pip** — A clockwork songbird the size of a robin, made from salvaged Divinity copper and Numerian crystal. Pip could mimic any melody it heard and would perch on Cassrilyn's shoulder while she worked. Pip survived the workshop explosion and was last seen flying south toward Absalom, still singing.
- **A sketch-journal of designs** — Hundreds of pages of clockwork animal blueprints, annotated with cheerful margin notes like "Give this one longer legs -- he wants to dance!"

## Echoes in History
- *"A trader from young Absalom came through with a crate of Osiriani scarabs -- real ones, jeweled and ancient. I showed him Pip, and he offered me the whole crate for my little bird. I said no. He said I was a fool. Pip bit his finger. I think we were both right."*

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the early growth of Absalom as a trade hub, with Aroden's cult spreading across the Inner Sea. Plagued by vivid dreams of Azlant's fall during Earthfall -- echoes from Cass's broken memory bleeding through millennia. Her clockwork creations were inspired by Numerian salvage and an intuitive connection to Brigh, goddess of clockwork and invention.

## Timeline Quote
I made my little clockwork friends from the scraps of a fallen star-ship. They're still out there somewhere, running wild in the woods.
