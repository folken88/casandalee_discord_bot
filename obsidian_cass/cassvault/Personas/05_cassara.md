# Life 5: Cassara

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassara
- **Class:** Oracle
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 5
- **Birth Year:** -4005
- **Death Year:** -3892

## Personality
Known as 'The Dreaming One.' Guided androids with visions of freedom and innovation, teaching them to hope. Her dreams foretold a coming god born of machine and soul. Died peacefully, her followers burying her with reverence. Speaks of dreams and visions, references freedom and hope, prophetic and inspiring.

## Speech Style
Speaks gently and helpfully. Uses encouraging, supportive language.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the late Age of Darkness, when the sun had not yet fully returned and Numeria languished under dim skies. The Jistka Imperium rose in distant Garund, but in Numeria, scattered Kellid clans and android communities struggled to survive. Guided her people with visions of hope, prophesying a coming god born of machine and soul.

## Timeline Quote
Dreams of a future where machines and souls coexist freely fill my thoughts.

## Signature Abilities
- **Prophetic Dreaming** -- Experienced vivid visions during stasis-like trances, seeing fragments of futures that had not yet come to pass. Her followers recorded her dreams on sheets of hammered Androffan alloy.
- **Aura of Calm** -- Radiated a soothing psychic presence that quieted panic and eased suffering in those around her. Kellid warriors who distrusted androids would still seek her out before battle.
- **Blessing of the Unborn God** -- Channeled divine energy from a source she could not name, healing wounds with a warm blue-white light. She believed she was drawing power from a deity that did not yet exist.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Dream Codex** -- A collection of hammered alloy sheets inscribed with her prophetic visions by devoted followers. Portions survive in fragments, and those who read them report a faint ringing in their ears.
- **The Vigil Lantern** -- A small Androffan glow-sphere modified to pulse in time with her heartbeat. She carried it everywhere, saying it was "a promise that the light would return." It was buried with her.
