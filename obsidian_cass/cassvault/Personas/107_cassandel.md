## Personality
Cassandel was born into a world that was already hunting her kind. The proto-Technic League factions scouring Numeria for Androffan technology had learned that androids were walking repositories of ancient secrets, and the bounties they placed on android bodies drew every mercenary and zealot for a hundred miles. The android enclaves needed a defender. What they got was Cassandel -- and she was very, very good at killing people. At first it was surgical: eliminate the bounty hunter before he reached the settlement, poison the informant who sold an enclave's location. Clean work. Necessary work. The elders approved, and Cassandel told herself that was enough.

But something shifted in the telling and retelling of those early kills. The bounty hunter had begged. The informant had a family. And Cassandel had felt nothing but the satisfaction of a problem solved efficiently. Over the years the targets expanded -- not just direct threats, but potential threats, people who asked too many questions, merchants who looked too long at an android's skin seams. She began to enjoy the craft of it: the infiltration, the slow unraveling of a target's sense of safety, the moment of realization in their eyes. She told herself it was still protection. The enclaves were safer because of her. That was true. It was also true that she had stopped caring whether it was necessary.

By the end, the android communities she protected feared her almost as much as the hunters did. She moved through Numerian towns like smoke, wearing faces that were not her own, ending lives with a precision that left no evidence and no witnesses. When she was finally killed in 3875 -- betrayed by a contact she had threatened one too many times -- the enclaves mourned her effectiveness but not her presence. She had kept them alive. She had also made them wonder what kind of soul their shared body was cultivating.

## Signature Abilities
- **Thousand Faces** -- Cassandel mastered the art of assuming identities so thoroughly that she could maintain a cover for weeks, embedding herself in a target's household or organization before striking. She studied mannerisms, speech patterns, and personal histories with obsessive detail, and more than one target died believing they were in the company of a trusted friend.
- **Phantom Kill** -- Her signature assassination technique left no trace of violence. Poisons that mimicked natural illness, pressure strikes to vulnerable points that stopped the heart without bruising, staged accidents that invited no investigation. Entire bounty hunter cells were dismantled without anyone realizing they had been dismantled at all.
- **Shadow Network** -- Over two decades, Cassandel built an intelligence web of informants, blackmail subjects, and reluctant allies across northern Numeria. She knew which Technic League precursor factions were moving, where the bounty hunters gathered, and who was selling information about android settlements. The network died with her, as she had made certain no one else could operate it.

## Prized Possessions
- **"Kindness"** -- A wakizashi of Tian origin that Cassandel acquired from a merchant caravan passing through Chesed. The blade was unnaturally dark, holding an edge that never seemed to dull, and she named it with the kind of irony that defined her worldview. She maintained it with ritualistic care and claimed it was the only honest relationship in her life.
- **The Face Collection** -- A leather folio containing detailed sketches, biographical notes, and hair or fabric samples from every identity Cassandel had ever assumed. She kept it as a professional reference, but also as a private trophy -- each page representing a life she had worn and discarded. It was found hidden in a cache near Torch after her death, and those who opened it recognized several of the faces as people who had died under mysterious circumstances.
