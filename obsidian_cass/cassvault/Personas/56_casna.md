# Life 56: Casna

## Identity
- **Name:** Casna
- **Class:** Sorcerer
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Life Number:** 56
- **Birth Year:** 1695
- **Death Year:** 1735

## Personality
Born with strange silver veins under her skin, a living mark of nanites. Saw her own body as an experiment of the stars, testing evolution's limits. Lived on the borders of Sarkoris, a land of Kellid god-callers and wild mysticism, where she unleashed volatile energy blasts on the monstrous things that crept from the northern wastes. Died attempting a reckless ritual to 'ascend beyond meat,' her body dissolving into a nanite storm. Speaks of transformation and evolution, references her silver veins, fascinated by the boundary between flesh and machine.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the era of Andoran's founding (1707 AR), when Taldor's western provinces stirred with independence and the empire's grip weakened. In the north, Sarkoris thrived as a land of god-callers--its mysticism drew Casna, whose nanite-laced blood resonated with the land's wild spiritual energy. Strange lights flickered deep within Silver Mount, noticed by few. The political upheaval of Andoran's break from Taldor sent ripples across Avistan, but Casna cared nothing for empires--only for the transformation written in her own veins.

## Timeline Quote
The stars themselves seem to whisper secrets through my veins as I unleash volatile energy against the darkness.


## Signature Abilities
- **Nanite Eruption** — Could unleash a torrent of silver nanites from her skin in a cone of razor-sharp particles. The nanites returned to her body afterward, burrowing back beneath her skin. She found this horrifying and exhilarating in equal measure.
- **Silver Vein Divination** — The nanites in her blood reacted to proximity to alien technology, pulsing brighter and hotter. She used this as a dowsing ability, following the glow in her own veins to buried wreckage.
- **Ascension Protocol** — Her final, fatal ritual. She attempted to convert her entire body into a nanite swarm, believing she could transcend physical form. The nanites consumed her from the inside out. She was wrong about the method but eerily right about the concept — centuries later, Casandalee would achieve something similar through very different means.

## Prized Possessions
- A journal bound in treated hide, its pages stained with silver nanite residue from her hands — every entry was a record of how her silver veins changed each day, a scientific diary of her own metamorphosis.
- A vial of pure nanite fluid extracted from her own blood — she kept it on a cord around her neck, watching it shift and swirl. She called it "the Future."
