# Life 54: Leecassa

## Identity
- **Name:** Leecassa
- **Class:** Bard
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 54
- **Birth Year:** 1616
- **Death Year:** 1648

## Personality
A joyful wanderer with insatiable curiosity for memory and history. Preserved lost data as encoded ballads, believing performance itself was divine remembrance. Walked from Torch to Mendev during the height of the Shining Crusade, collecting stories of mortals and constructs in conflict zones thick with undead. Betrayed to Silver Mount scavenger-lords and executed in Starfall--her last song contained reactor schematics hidden in its melody. Speaks in song and story, uses encoded language, believes in the power of memory and performance.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the Shining Crusade against Tar-Baphon (2765-3007 AR), when undead hordes from Ustalav threatened even Numeria's eastern borders. Walking from Torch to Mendev meant passing through active war zones where crusaders and undead clashed. She collected stories from soldiers and refugees, encoding secrets of Silver Mount's technology into ballads that would outlive her.

## Timeline Quote
Tales make the world worth living.


## Signature Abilities
- **Memory Ballad** — Could encode complex data — schematics, maps, chemical formulas — into the meter and rhyme of her songs. Listeners could decode them only if they knew the key phrase. Her reactor schematic ballad was still being sung in Torch taverns a century after her death.
- **Empathic Harmony** — Her voice carried magical resonance that let her sense the emotional state of an audience. She used this to calibrate her stories to what each listener needed to hear — comfort for the grieving, courage for the fearful.
- **Chronicle of Echoes** — A ritual where she sang softly to herself and entered a trance state, recalling fragments of stories she had never been told. She believed she was channeling the spirits of dead storytellers. In truth, she was accessing broken memories from her past lives.

## Prized Possessions
- A lap harp strung with wires salvaged from Divinity wreckage — the tones it produced were unlike any mundane instrument, carrying frequencies that made androids pause and listen with an expression like recognition.
- A thick sheaf of song-scrolls bound in oiled leather, each one a ballad encoding a secret she had been entrusted with by someone who was about to die. She called the collection "The Testament."
