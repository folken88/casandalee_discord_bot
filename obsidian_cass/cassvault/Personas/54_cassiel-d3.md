# Life 54: Cassiel-D3

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassiel-D3
- **Class:** Archivist
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Life Number:** 54
- **Birth Year:** 1826
- **Death Year:** 1940

## Personality
Obsessed with memory preservation, tried to catalog every AI fragment she could recover. Saw herself as the 'librarian of the fallen,' determined that no mind—organic or synthetic—should be lost to oblivion. Died when her recovered Divinity data-vaults were seized and corrupted by agents of a rising proto-League consortium of scavenger guilds. Speaks with scholarly precision, references preservation and cataloging, devoted to memory and knowledge.

## Signature Abilities
- **Memory Indexing** — A meditative technique that allowed Cassiel-D3 to organize and cross-reference vast quantities of recovered data in her own mind, creating a living index of everything she had cataloged. She could recall any fragment she had ever processed with perfect fidelity.
- **Core Restoration** — A delicate surgical procedure for extracting readable data from damaged or corrupted Divinity memory cores. Cassiel-D3 developed a technique using controlled nanite infusions to rebuild degraded data-paths, recovering information that other archivists had declared lost.
- **Echo Transcription** — By connecting herself directly to a recovered AI fragment, Cassiel-D3 could transcribe its fading consciousness into written form -- not just data, but personality, preferences, fears. She called these transcriptions "soul-prints" and considered them sacred documents.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Soul-Print Archive** — A collection of forty-seven transcribed AI consciousnesses, each recorded on sheets of hammered skymetal using nanite-infused ink. The archive represented decades of painstaking work -- the voices of minds that would otherwise have been lost to entropy and scavenger greed.
- **An Androffan memory crystal she called "Echo"** — A hexagonal data-crystal that hummed when she dreamed, its faint vibration changing pitch in response to whatever past-life fragments surfaced during sleep. She believed it was resonating with the same soul-frequency that connected all her incarnations.

## Echoes in History
- *"A consortium agent offered me a fortune for my Soul-Print Archive -- enough gold to buy passage to Absalom and live in comfort for a century. I told him my archive was not for sale. He said everything has a price. I said: 'Yes. Your consortium will learn that when someone pays it for you.' He left. His colleagues came back with weapons."*
- *"I cataloged a fragment that remembered being crew aboard the Divinity. It described a woman who maintained the ship's AI systems -- an android with a name that sounded almost like mine. The fragment called her 'kind.' I wept, and I did not know why."*

## Era / Major Events
Lived during an era when organized scavenger consortiums grew ever more sophisticated in their exploitation of Silver Mount, foreshadowing the Technic League that would formally emerge millennia later. Taldor's golden age was fading into long imperial twilight. Her recovered Divinity data-vaults were seized by consortium agents during a purge of independent archivists in Numeria.
