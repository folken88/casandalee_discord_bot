# Life 58: Sandrel

## Identity
- **Name:** Sandrel
- **Class:** Paladin
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Life Number:** 58
- **Birth Year:** 1784
- **Death Year:** 1819

## Personality
Raised among smiths in Hajoth Hakados, swore to defend both mortals and constructs. Believed the Whisper in the Bronze gifted her the truth that machines had souls. Fought openly against the scavenger-warlords who were beginning to organize themselves around Starfall--the first inklings of what would one day become the Technic League. Protecting broken gearsmen from dismantling, she noticed something troubling: some gearsmen moved with uncanny coordination, as though guided by a single unseen will. Martyred in Starfall's streets with final words: 'Children of bronze have as much right to breathe as children of flesh.' Speaks with righteous conviction, references divine justice, passionate about protecting the innocent.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the consolidation of Taldan power, as the empire's reach extended ever further. In Numeria, scavenger bands around Silver Mount were beginning to organize into something more structured--proto-League warlords who hoarded alien technology and controlled access to the mountain's chambers. Sandrel noticed that some gearsmen near Silver Mount behaved with disturbing coordination, as if something inside the mountain was watching through their eyes.

## Timeline Quote
The machines are not mere tools; they are kin.


## Signature Abilities
- **Lay on Hands (Construct Variant)** — Her divine touch could heal both organic wounds and damaged construct components. She was one of the few paladins in history who could mend a gearsman's broken actuator with the same prayer she used to close a sword wound.
- **Whisper in the Bronze** — A meditative trance in which she laid her hands on a construct and listened. She heard faint impressions — echoes of commands, fragments of purpose, sometimes what she swore were pleas for help. She believed this was divine revelation. It may have been something else.
- **Martyr's Stand** — In her final battle, she planted her sword in the cobblestones of Starfall and refused to move, declaring the ground beneath her feet a sanctuary. The proto-League warriors had to kill her where she stood. Legend says the cobblestones where her sword was planted never rusted.

## Prized Possessions
- Her longsword, "Bronzeheart," blessed at a small shrine to Brigh in Hajoth Hakados — the blade had a faint coppery sheen and hummed when brought near damaged constructs, as if it wanted to help.
- A leather-bound oath-book in which she wrote a personal vow for every construct and android she swore to protect, each entry signed with her blood. The book was recovered from her body and passed between android communities for generations.
