## Signature Abilities
- **Stellar Kata** — A meditative combat form where each movement traced the path of a constellation. Casandrae performed it at dawn, and those who watched described seeing afterimages of starlight trailing her hands.
- **Null Pulse** — A focused exhalation of inner energy that disrupted magical effects in a small radius, creating a brief zone of anti-magic. She used it sparingly, as it left her drained and shaking.
- **Code Mantra** — A whispered sequence of Androffan syllables repeated in rhythm with breathing, inducing a trance state that accelerated self-repair and suppressed pain.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Star Map Cloth** — A bolt of silk she embroidered over decades with the positions of every star she observed, annotated with mathematical notations she could not explain but felt compelled to record. It was found draped over her body when she died in meditation.
- **A polished skymetal pebble** — Perfectly round, found in the crater of Silver Mount. She held it during meditation, claiming it "remembered the void between worlds."

## Echoes in History
- *"A Taldan cartographer once stopped at our settlement, mapping the frontier for his empire's expanding borders. He asked me what lay beyond the horizon. I told him: more horizon. He wrote that down."*
- *"I spoke once with a wandering monk from Vudra who had crossed half the world seeking enlightenment. We sat together in silence for an entire day. When he left, he bowed and said I was the first person he had met who understood that silence was the answer, not the question."*

## Era / Major Events
