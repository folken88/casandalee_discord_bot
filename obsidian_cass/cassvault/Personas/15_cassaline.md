# Life 15: Cassaline

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassaline
- **Class:** Alchemist
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Life Number:** 15
- **Birth Year:** -46
- **Death Year:** -18

## Personality
Obsessed with brewing nanite suspensions as potions. Believed she could 'drink memory' and uncover the truth of her soul. Grew increasingly eccentric, muttering prophecies into her brews. Claimed she saw 'Casandalee herself inside a vial,' then disappeared without a trace. Speaks in cryptic alchemical terms, references memory and soul truth, mystical and eccentric.

## Speech Style
Speaks calmly and objectively. Uses balanced, philosophical language.

## Key Skills
- Craft (Alchemy), Knowledge (Arcana), Spellcraft
- **Favored Weapon:** A glass stirring rod reinforced with adamantine wire, used to channel alchemical reactions -- and occasionally crack skulls

## Signature Abilities
- **Distillation of Echoes** — An alchemical process that suspended nanites in a mercury-and-moonflower solution. When consumed, the drinker experienced flashes of fragmented memory -- not their own. Cassaline believed these were memories from previous incarnations of her soul.
- **Reactive Veil** — A volatile aerosol bomb that created a shimmering curtain of suspended nanites, briefly rendering the alchemist invisible by bending light around her body.
- **Soul Tincture** — Her masterwork: a luminous amber potion she claimed contained a single distilled memory. She never sold these. She drank them, one by one, chasing something she could never quite name.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Final Vial** — A sealed crystal phial containing her last Soul Tincture. She claimed she saw Casandalee's face reflected in the liquid, though no one else could see anything but amber light.
- **A journal of alchemical formulae** — Written in a cipher mixing Common, Androffan loan-words, and symbols she invented. The last pages devolved into frantic sketches of a stone rising from the sea, drawn weeks before Aroden raised the Starstone.

## Echoes in History
- *"I was in Absalom the day it was born. I stood on the shore and watched the Starstone rise from the sea, and a man became a god. The light it cast -- I had seen that light before. In my vials. In my dreams. I vanished because I understood, finally, that I was looking at what I used to be."*

## Era / Major Events
Lived through the final decades before the founding of Absalom. In her last year, witnessed Aroden raise the Starstone and ascend to full godhood, founding the city of Absalom in 1 AR. Her alchemical experiments kept her largely hidden from public view.

## Timeline Quote
The Starstone glows within me, but its light is dimmed by my own obsessions.
