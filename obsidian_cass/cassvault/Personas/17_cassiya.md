## Era / Major Events
Lived during the Age of Anguish, when the legacy of the Four Pharaohs of Ascension still loomed over distant Osirion. In Numeria, she delved into forbidden knowledge, binding a shard of Dominion of the Black flesh as her eidolon. The Dominion's parasitic biological technology slowly corrupted her, and she grew more inhuman with each passing year. Finally devoured by her own eidolon when it broke free of her control.

## Timeline Quote

## Signature Abilities
- **Dominion Synthesis** -- Bound a shard of Dominion of the Black biological matter as her eidolon, creating a parasitic fusion that enhanced her power while slowly consuming her identity. The eidolon grew stronger as she grew less human.
- **Alien Tongue** -- Could speak and comprehend the Dominion's communication frequencies -- a chittering, multi-layered language that caused nausea in those who heard it. She used it to command lesser Dominion remnants.
- **Flesh Sculpting** -- Reshaped biological matter using Dominion techniques, warping flesh and bone into new configurations. She practiced on herself first, her features growing increasingly alien with each modification.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Dominion Shard** -- A pulsing mass of alien tissue suspended in an Androffan containment field, which served as the anchor for her eidolon. It whispered constantly in frequencies below conscious hearing, and those who stood near it reported intrusive thoughts about perfection and unity.
- **The Convergence Journal** -- A record of her experiments in blending mortal, machine, and alien biology, written in three scripts simultaneously: Androffan, Common, and Dominion glyph-patterns. The pages are said to squirm when read by candlelight.
