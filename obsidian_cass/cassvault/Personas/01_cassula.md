## Personality
An Androffan AI personality who served aboard the starship Divinity for centuries, maintaining its core life support, navigation, and stasis systems. Spent decades perfecting the ship's infrastructure, treating every circuit and conduit as a work of art. Believed that proper maintenance and protocol were the foundation of all civilization. When the Dominion of the Black attacked the Divinity in -4363 AR, her final act was to reroute power to preserve the ship's core systems, helping to guide the crippled vessel down into what would become Numeria. She did not survive the crash in organic form, but her personality imprint seeded the android body that would carry her forward. Speaks in technical precision, references systems and protocols, methodical and devoted to function.

## Signature Abilities
- **Emergency Power Reroute** -- Could redirect energy across multiple ship systems simultaneously, keeping critical functions alive even as hull breaches cascaded. She described it as "convincing the ship to breathe through a different lung."
- **Diagnostic Pulse** -- Sent a resonant frequency through any mechanical system to identify faults by sound alone. Other engineers said she could hear a failing capacitor three decks away.
- **Structural Integrity Override** -- Bypassed safety limiters to reinforce failing bulkheads during the Divinity's descent, buying precious seconds for the crash-landing sequence.

## Prized Possessions
- **"The Conductor's Baton"** -- A multi-tool calibration rod from the Divinity's engineering bay, capable of interfacing with any Androffan system. She never went anywhere without it, and it was found fused into the wreckage beside her final station.
- **Navigation Core Fragment** -- A palm-sized crystalline shard from the Divinity's primary navigation array, which she kept as a reminder of the stars they had crossed. It still faintly glowed when held near active Androffan technology.
