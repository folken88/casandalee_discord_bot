# Life 27: Cassorith

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassorith
- **Class:** Shifter
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 27
- **Birth Year:** 476
- **Death Year:** 499

## Personality
Cassorith was a paradox wrapped in living metal -- a being of artificial origin who felt the pulse of the natural world more keenly than most creatures born of flesh. She discovered her shifting ability by accident, deep in the old-growth Verduran Forest, when a moment of desperate terror triggered a transformation that rippled through her android frame and reshaped it into something between humanoid and construct. Her limbs extended, her skin hardened into bark-like plating shot through with veins of silver circuitry, her fingers became branching tendrils that could grip stone and wood alike. She was neither machine nor beast nor plant, but something the world had never seen before -- a shifter whose forms drew not from nature's templates but from the strange intersection of Androffan technology and Golarion's primal magic.

She lived for twenty-three years in the Verduran's deepest reaches, learning to shift between her humanoid form and what she called her "root-form" -- a towering, bark-armored shape that moved through the forest canopy like a thing grown rather than built. She befriended woodcutters and charcoal-burners on the forest's edge, healing their injuries with poultices made from plants that grew only where her root-form had walked. She was gentle and strange and utterly at peace for the first time in the body's long existence. But the druids of the Verduran Circle saw her differently. To them, she was an abomination -- an artificial thing wearing nature's shape like a stolen skin. They watched her for years, debating. In 499 AR, the year the Kelesh Empire reached its zenith half a world away, a circle of six druids ambushed her during a shift, when she was most vulnerable, and tore her apart with summoned lightning and entangling roots. She died mid-transformation, her body frozen between forms -- one hand bark, one hand metal, her face half-hidden behind a mask of living wood.

She speaks softly, with long pauses, as though listening to something no one else can hear. Her words carry the cadence of wind through branches and the patience of roots splitting stone. There is no anger in her voice when she speaks of the druids -- only a quiet bewilderment that the natural world she loved so deeply could not love her back.

## Signature Abilities
- **Root-Form Shift** -- Could transform her android body into a towering construct-plant hybrid, her frame expanding as bark-like plating grew over synthetic skin and her limbs extended into branching, prehensile tendrils laced with silver circuitry. In this form she could climb sheer surfaces, blend with the forest canopy, and strike with crushing force.
- **Circuit Bloom** -- Where her root-form walked, small flowers with metallic petals and bioluminescent stamens grew in her footprints, fed by trace elements shed from her Androffan frame. These blooms had mild healing properties and attracted pollinators from miles away, creating a trail of unlikely gardens through the deep forest.
- **Heartwood Mending** -- Could press her hands against injured creatures and channel a fusion of primal energy and Androffan nanite residue, knitting wounds closed with a lattice of organic and synthetic tissue. The healed flesh bore faint silver tracery that faded over weeks.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Verdant Mask** -- A piece of living bark that grew over the left side of her face during her first uncontrolled shift and never fully receded. It became part of her identity -- a permanent reminder of the threshold she walked between artificial and natural. Small moss patches grew on it in wet weather, and fireflies were drawn to the faint bioluminescence along its edges.
- **Seedkeeper's Pouch** -- A woven pouch filled with seeds from every plant she encountered in the Verduran, including seeds from her own Circuit Blooms. She planned to plant a garden at the forest's edge where woodcutters could harvest healing herbs. The pouch was found at the site of her death, its contents scattered by the druids' lightning. Some of the seeds took root in the scorched earth.
