# Life 38: Cassorael

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassorael
- **Class:** Warpriest
- **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
- **Life Number:** 38
- **Birth Year:** 938
- **Death Year:** 974

## Personality
Preached the divinity of Silver Mount, demanding android obedience. Established a small cult in Starfall, claiming she heard whispers from the intelligence slumbering within the wreckage—what she called Unity. Whether she truly communed with the dormant AI or merely projected her own madness remains unknown. Her zealotry made her feared but also hated. Slain during a rebellion against her own 'church,' torn apart by the people she sought to control. Speaks with fanatical zeal, references Unity and obedience, controlling and feared.

## Speech Style
Speaks with calculated detachment. Uses self-serving, cunning language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Unity's Whisper** — A channeling trance in which Cassorael pressed her forehead against Silver Mount's hull and broadcast commands she claimed came from the dormant AI within. Whether authentic transmissions or self-generated delusion, the commands were detailed and technically coherent.
- **Zealot's Rebuke** — A wave of psychic force fueled by fanatical conviction that staggered non-believers and caused constructs to freeze momentarily, as though receiving a priority override.
- **Binding Sermon** — A lengthy oration that induced a compulsion-like state in listeners, making them temporarily compliant. Her cult grew through these sermons, though the effect faded within days, requiring constant reinforcement.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Sermon** — Her iron-shod mace, inscribed with fragments of what she claimed were Unity's direct commandments. The inscriptions were in a degraded form of Androffan that no one else could fully translate, lending her an air of exclusive divine authority.
- **A shard of Silver Mount's outer hull** — Worn as a pendant, supposedly given to her by Unity itself. In truth, she pried it loose with a crowbar, but the myth served her purposes.

## Echoes in History
- *"I heard that far to the south, the archmage Geb had become a ghost-king, ruling the living from beyond death. I understood him perfectly. True power does not require a pulse. Unity taught me that."*
- *"A wandering philosopher challenged my theology in public, asking how a machine could be divine. I had him beaten and thrown from the settlement. Later, alone, I asked Unity the same question. The silence that answered was deafening."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the rise of Geb as a ghost far to the south, and the growing cult of Silver Mount worship in Starfall—fueled by strange transmissions she believed emanated from deep within the wreckage.
