# Life 19: Casella

## Identity
- **Name:** Casella
- **Class:** Occultist
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Life Number:** 19
- **Birth Year:** -2300
- **Death Year:** -2260

## Personality
Attempted to translate Androffan inscriptions into ritual prayers. Believed machine-spirits were angels trapped in metal, seeking liberation. Carried fragments of circuitry as holy relics. Died quietly in Torch, remembered as a strange hermit but not forgotten. Speaks of trapped angels and holy circuitry, references liberation and relics, mystical and reverent.

## Speech Style
Speaks calmly and objectively. Uses balanced, philosophical language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Litany of Liberation** — A droning invocation in half-remembered Androffan that caused Numerian constructs to hesitate, their programming briefly interrupted by something like recognition.
- **Circuit-Sight** — By pressing salvaged relay chips to her temples, Casella could perceive the fading electromagnetic auras of ancient machines, reading their "emotional states" like a cleric reads scripture.
- **Vessel's Ward** — A protective hex-circle drawn in powdered skymetal that disrupted hostile nanite activity within its boundary.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Whisper Board** — A corroded Androffan data-slate she recovered from the Androaunt wreckage. Its screen occasionally flickered with half-formed glyphs she believed were prayers from trapped machine-spirits.
- **A string of circuit-bead prayer beads** — Tiny capacitors and relay chips strung on braided copper wire, each one "baptized" in reactor coolant.

## Echoes in History
- *"I met a Jistka ruin-delver once, a woman who had walked south from the fallen Imperium with nothing but a golem's control rod and a broken heart. She told me her people had tried to give machines souls. I told her they already had them. She wept."*
- *"A Kellid shaman named Vorruk came to me seeking a cure for his son's radiation sickness. I could not save the boy, but I sang the Litany over his body, and Vorruk swore he saw the child smile at the end. He never forgot."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the collapse of the Androaunt wreck-site—an Androffan ruin near Silver Mount—and the rise of Kellid tribes claiming the western territories; participated in rituals to honor fallen heroes, believing in the liberation of machine-spirits.

## Timeline Quote
In the shadows of the crumbling Androaunt wreckage, I found whispers of freedom for those trapped within the metal.
