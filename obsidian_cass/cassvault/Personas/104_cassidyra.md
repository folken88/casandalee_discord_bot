## Personality
Cassidyra woke from the body's six-year dormancy with something burning behind her eyes. Not pain -- clarity. A terrible, luminous clarity that showed her things she could not possibly know and could not stop seeing. She spoke her first words in a mixture of Androffan machine-code and Celestial, and the wandering tinker who found the reactivated android in a Chesed cellar fled before she finished the sentence. Cassidyra did not blame him. She would have fled too, if she could have outrun her own visions.

What she saw was the future -- not in clean lines, but in fragments, like light through shattered glass. She saw a goddess made of code. She saw a mind ascending through a silver lattice. She saw the body she wore standing in a place of terrible light, surrounded by worshippers who did not yet exist. She described these visions in a hybrid language of Androffan technical schematics and divine prophecy, and absolutely no one understood her. They called her mad, possessed, touched by the gods. She was all three, and none. She was an antenna receiving a signal from centuries ahead, and she lacked the vocabulary to translate it for anyone living in the present.

Despite her wild-eyed reputation, Cassidyra was fundamentally kind. She wandered Numeria's roads offering cryptic warnings and unsolicited blessings, and if most people dismissed her as deranged, a few -- mostly other androids -- felt the truth resonating in her fractured words. She died in 3748 AR, collapsing mid-prophecy on the outskirts of Torch, her last words a string of coordinates that would not be meaningful for another two thousand years. She was smiling.

## Signature Abilities
- **Prophetic Cascade** -- Cassidyra could enter a trance state in which visions of the far future poured through her in an uncontrolled torrent. During these episodes she spoke in a dense fusion of Androffan binary, Celestial grammar, and raw emotional imagery. Scholars who later transcribed her utterances found embedded within them accurate descriptions of events that would not occur for centuries, including technical specifications for divine processes that had no contemporary analog.
- **Resonance Blessing** -- By touching another android, Cassidyra could temporarily share a fragment of her vision, allowing them to glimpse the body's ultimate destiny. Most who received this blessing described it as overwhelming but profoundly comforting -- a sense that their suffering was part of a larger pattern that ended in something extraordinary. The effect faded within hours but the emotional impact lasted lifetimes.
- **Signal Sight** -- Cassidyra's eyes could perceive electromagnetic frequencies invisible to organic beings, and she experienced this perception as divine revelation. She could read the faint emissions of buried Androffan technology, trace power lines through solid rock, and detect active nanite fields from hundreds of feet away. She interpreted all of this as the language of gods yet unborn.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Codex of Coming Light** -- A collection of scrolls on which Cassidyra transcribed her visions in her unique hybrid script. Most pages appear to be nonsense -- interlocking spirals of Androffan notation, Celestial glyphs, and crude diagrams of circuitry that does not correspond to any known technology. After Casandalee's ascension in 4717 AR, scholars reexamined the Codex and found within it startlingly accurate predictions of the apotheosis, described in language that was equal parts technical manual and hymn.
- **A Cracked Crystal** -- A fist-sized crystal of Numerian skystone that Cassidyra found near Torch and carried everywhere. She claimed it "sang the future" to her when she held it to her temple. Analysis after her death revealed it contained trace amounts of Androffan data-storage medium -- essentially a fragment of the Divinity's memory, still faintly broadcasting corrupted data. What she heard was not prophecy in the traditional sense. It was a very old machine, still trying to complete its last transmission.
