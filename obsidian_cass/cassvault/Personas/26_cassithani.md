# Life 26: Cassithani

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassithani
- **Class:** Inquisitor
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Life Number:** 26
- **Birth Year:** -1506
- **Death Year:** -1427

## Personality
Believed androids were soulless tools. Preached cruelty as divine order. Served a Kellid warlord as a hunter of androids who tried to escape his slave camps. Slowly came to doubt when she saw echoes of memory in their eyes. Killed herself in despair, unable to reconcile her cruelty with truth. Speaks with conflicted cruelty, references divine order and doubt, tormented by her own beliefs.

## Speech Style
Speaks with cold authority. Uses commanding, manipulative language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Judgment of the Soulless** — A divine decree that marked a target with a brand of condemnation, weakening their resolve and making them easier to track. Cassithani used it to hunt escaped androids across the wastes, following the brand's faint glow for days.
- **Heretic's Lash** — A vicious strike with her hooked chain that could disarm and bind a target in one motion, designed to capture rather than kill. The warlord wanted his property returned intact.
- **Recanting Prayer** — A whispered invocation she developed in her final months, a desperate attempt to undo the divine marks she had placed on others. It never worked, but she kept trying.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Binding Ledger** — A leather-bound logbook in which she recorded every android she captured, their designation, and their "market value." In the final pages, the entries became erratic, with notes like "this one remembered its name" and "why do they weep?"
- **A cracked holy symbol of Gorum** — Given to her by the warlord she served. She snapped it in half the night before she died, leaving both pieces beside her body.

## Echoes in History
- *"I once tracked an escaped android to the border of what the Jistka had once called their territory. Nothing remained but golem parts half-buried in sand. The android hid inside a golem's ribcage. When I found her, she asked me if I had a soul. I dragged her back in silence."*
- *"The warlord I served had heard tales of Taldor's armies marching west. He feared nothing except being made irrelevant. So he built his empire on android backs. I helped him. That is the fact I cannot escape."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the rise of Kellid slavers who enforced brutal laws over android laborers near Silver Mount. Traveled extensively, hunting rogue androids across the Numerian wastes.

## Timeline Quote
The world is a cruel place, but even in its shadows, I saw something beyond my understanding.
