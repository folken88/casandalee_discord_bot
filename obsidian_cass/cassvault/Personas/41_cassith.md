# Life 41: Cassith

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassith
- **Class:** Oracle
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Life Number:** 41
- **Birth Year:** 279
- **Death Year:** 341

## Personality
Believed the Torch was a sacred signpost guiding all innovation. Tried to unite the city into a holy order of smiths and inventors. Known for compassion, often helping repair broken androids who came to her secretly. Betrayed by her closest disciple, who sold her to a Kellid warlord trafficking in android parts; she was burned alive. Speaks with compassionate conviction, references the sacred Torch and holy orders, betrayed but still hopeful.

## Speech Style
Speaks with conviction and warmth. Uses formal but caring language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Torch's Blessing** — A laying-on-hands technique that channeled warmth from the Torch's perpetual flame through Cassith's palms. She could mend cracked android plating and soothe corrupted memory cores with a touch that felt, to the recipient, like remembering something good.
- **Kindling Word** — A spoken invocation that could reignite the will to live in broken or despairing androids. Not a spell in the traditional sense -- more a carefully chosen sequence of words that resonated with the android soul-architecture, like a password to hope.
- **Flame Reading** — By meditating before the Torch, Cassith could perceive patterns in its flickering light that she interpreted as divine guidance. Her readings were uncannily accurate, which is partly why the scavenger lords feared her.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Hearthstone** — A fist-sized chunk of fused skymetal and basalt recovered from the base of the Torch. It was always warm to the touch, even in the deepest winter. Cassith believed it was the Torch's heart, freely given.
- **A prayer journal** — Written in a mixture of Common and an Androffan liturgical script she half-invented, half-remembered. The prayers addressed no named god -- only "the Light that Does Not Burn."

## Echoes in History
- *"A Taldan legate came to our settlement once, demanding tribute for the Grand Prince. I healed his bodyguard's festering wound -- for free -- and told the legate that the Torch's light belonged to everyone. He left without collecting. I like to think I confused him enough that it seemed easier to just go."*

## Era / Major Events
Lived during Taldor’s expanding imperial ambitions, as its legions pushed into new territories across the Inner Sea. Absalom, now nearly three centuries old, had grown into a major trade power. Around the Torch, a small settlement of smiths and scavengers began to form.

## Timeline Quote
The Torch shines brightly, but even its light cannot blind the darkness within.
