# Life 26: Castriel

## Identity
- **Name:** Castriel
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Life Number:** 26

## Personality
A brash, violent mercenary who lived for destruction. Modified herself into a living arsenal—grafting weapons into her arms, chest, even her jaw. Took contracts across Numeria, leaving entire camps smoking behind her. Self-destructed during a duel when her volatile power-cores overheated. Speaks with violent bravado, references weapons and destruction, aggressive and self-destructive.

## Key Skills

## Signature Abilities
- **Overclocked Draw** — A combat technique that pushed her grafted weapons beyond safe power thresholds for a devastating burst-fire. Each use risked catastrophic feedback, but Castriel considered the risk the best part.
- **Walking Arsenal** — Castriel's body was a layered weapons platform: arc pistols in her forearms, a plasma cutter in her jaw, concussion grenades in compartments along her ribcage. She could engage multiple targets simultaneously by activating different weapon systems in sequence.
- **Scorched Earth Protocol** — A last-resort self-destruct sequence she designed for herself: overloading all her volatile power cores simultaneously in a devastating explosion. She always said she would never use it. She always knew she would.

## Prized Possessions
- **Argument and Rebuttal** — Her twin arc pistols, salvaged from a Divinity armory and permanently grafted into her forearm housings. Each had been modified so extensively they were more Castriel than Divinity. She polished them with reactor coolant and spoke to them like old friends.
- **A scorched playing card** — The queen of swords from a Taldan card deck, the only surviving memento of the first warlord she killed. She kept it tucked behind Argument's barrel housing, where it slowly charred from residual heat.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Kellid warlord named Brakka the Unyielding hired me to clear a radiation cult from his territory. I did it in three hours. He asked me to stay as his champion. I told him champions do not charge by the hour. He did not think that was funny. I did."*

## Era / Major Events
Lived during an era of Kellid warlord politics and the growing scavenger economy around Silver Mount. Took contracts from petty techno-tyrants fighting over salvage rights. Her grafted weapons were Numerian arc pistols and plasma cutters, not conventional firearms. Participated in a skirmish against a radiation cult during a contract gone wrong.
