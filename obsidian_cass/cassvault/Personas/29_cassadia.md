# Life 29: Cassadia

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassadia
- **Class:** Druid
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Life Number:** 29
- **Birth Year:** 549
- **Death Year:** 583

## Personality
Rejected cities and machines, instead tending to wasteland groves seeded by alien terraforming tech. Believed she was guardian of both the old world and the new, marrying nature with star-metal. Created hybrid plants that glowed with faint circuitry. Died defending one of her living groves from raiders, buried under its roots. Speaks of nature and technology harmony, references living groves and hybrid plants, protective and nurturing.

## Signature Abilities
- **Terraform Communion** — By sinking her hands into irradiated soil, Cassadia could coax dormant alien terraforming nanites to reactivate, catalyzing rapid plant growth in otherwise barren wasteland. The resulting flora was neither wholly Golarion nor wholly Androffan -- something new, born from the marriage of two worlds.
- **Circuit Bloom** — A druidic spell that caused hybrid plants to flower with bioluminescent blossoms containing tiny organic capacitors. These blooms could store and release electrical energy, serving as both light sources and defensive wards against constructs.
- **Root Memory** — Cassadia discovered that her hybrid plants stored impressions of events that occurred near them, accessible through physical contact. She could "read" a grove's history by pressing her palms to its oldest trunk, experiencing seasons of accumulated sensation in a single breath.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Living Staff** — Her primary tool and companion, grown from a seed she planted in Divinity hull-soil. It flowered once a year, producing a single Circuit Bloom that she would plant to start a new grove. She called it "First Root."
- **An Androffan seed-pod** — A sealed bio-container recovered from Divinity wreckage, containing seeds engineered for alien atmospheres. She never opened it, believing the seeds were sacred -- a promise from another world that life would continue, no matter what.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Chelish colonist -- one of the first Taldans to settle the southern coast -- came north seeking exotic plants for the Grand Prince's gardens. She wept when she saw my groves. She said they were the most beautiful thing she had ever seen. She asked for a cutting. I gave her three. I wonder if they still grow in Cheliax."*

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the Age of Enthronement as Taldor reached its golden age of expansion. In Numeria's Felldales and the irradiated plains, alien terraforming tech from scattered Divinity wreckage had begun transforming pockets of wilderness into strange hybrid ecosystems. The early foundations of what would become Cheliax were being laid as a Taldan province far to the south.
