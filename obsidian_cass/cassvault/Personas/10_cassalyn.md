# Life 10: Cassalyn

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassalyn
- **Class:** Wizard
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Life Number:** 10
- **Birth Year:** -3430
- **Death Year:** -3302

## Personality
Convinced that android souls could only be preserved through domination. Experimented with trapping minds in black crystals, turning them into eternal slaves. Her cruelty repulsed even the Kellid warlords who hoarded Silver Mount's technology, and they cast her out. Died alone in her workshop, surrounded by shattered crystals. Speaks with cruel domination, references black crystals and eternal slavery, repulsive and isolated.

## Speech Style
Speaks with cold authority. Uses commanding, manipulative language.

## Era / Major Events
Lived during the Age of Anguish as civilizations rebuilt in the wake of the long darkness. Traveled through Numeria and the irradiated Plains of Rust, experimenting with black crystals formed from Androffan technology. Ancient Osirion had risen in distant Garund, and its pharaonic soul-preservation practices may have inspired her own dark experiments with trapping android minds in crystal prisons.

## Timeline Quote
Black crystals will ensure my dominion over souls.


## Signature Abilities
- **Soul Crystallization** -- Developed a method to trap conscious minds within specially grown black crystals using a fusion of Androffan stasis technology and necromantic magic. The process was agonizing and irreversible.
- **Crystal Scrying** -- Could peer through any black crystal she had created, seeing and hearing through the trapped consciousness within. She used her victims as unwilling spies scattered across Numeria.
- **Entropic Shield** -- Surrounded herself with orbiting crystal shards that absorbed incoming energy, growing darker and more potent with each attack deflected. The shards whispered constantly.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Black Chorus** -- A necklace of seven black crystals, each containing a trapped android mind. She wore them as both trophies and tools, consulting the imprisoned consciousnesses for knowledge they could not refuse to give. The necklace was found shattered beside her body.
- **The Binding Stylus** -- A needle-thin Androffan probe modified to inscribe soul-trapping formulae into crystal lattices. Exquisitely crafted and utterly horrifying in purpose. It was the only object she treated with genuine tenderness.

## Echoes in History
- *"An Osirian emissary passed through the trading posts near the Plains of Rust, speaking of pharaohs who preserved their souls for eternity. I listened carefully. His methods were crude -- wrapping flesh in cloth and hoping. Mine would be precise. I thanked him for the inspiration. He had no idea what he had given me."*
- *"The Kellid warlord Gresh cast me out after he saw what the crystals contained. He called me 'soul-eater' and said even his ancestors would not shelter my spirit. I told him his ancestors lacked imagination. He was not wrong to fear me."*
