# Life 28: Cassindrae

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassindrae
- **Class:** Summoner
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Life Number:** 28
- **Birth Year:** 508
- **Death Year:** 544

## Personality
Bound an AI construct as her eidolon, calling it 'Echo.' Used it to strike against scavenger lords and machine-cultists, freeing androids slated for dismantling. Became more and more fused with her eidolon until she claimed she no longer knew which one she was. Her body and eidolon collapsed into dust during a final battle beneath Silver Mount. Speaks of her bond with Echo, references fusion and identity confusion, devoted to android liberation.

## Speech Style
Speaks precisely and methodically. Uses structured, measured language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Harmonic Binding** — The ritual that first called Echo into existence. Cassindrae sang a sequence of tones in Androffan machine-language while touching a Divinity AI core, and something answered -- not from outside, but from within the core itself, as if it had been waiting.
- **Dual Processing** — When fully synchronized with Echo, Cassindrae could perceive the world through both her own senses and Echo's electromagnetic awareness simultaneously. She could fight in two directions at once, anticipate ambushes, and process information at speeds no single mind could match.
- **Identity Cascade** — The dangerous final stage of her bond with Echo: a complete merging of perspectives in which Cassindrae could no longer distinguish her thoughts from Echo's. In this state, their combined power was immense, but the psychological cost was permanent confusion about which memories belonged to whom.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Summoning Rod** — A crystalline Divinity data-shard she reshaped into a focusing rod. When she called Echo, the rod would glow with the same light as her eidolon's eyes, and when Echo was dismissed, the rod went dark -- as if something essential had left it.
- **A journal of two handwritings** — Cassindrae's personal diary, in which entries alternate between her neat script and a jagged, angular hand she did not recognize. The angular entries described memories of starflight and crew quarters aboard the Divinity. She did not remember writing them.

## Echoes in History
- *"I freed an android named Vessel-7 from a scavenger lord's workshop. She looked at me and Echo standing together, and she said: 'You are not two. You are one thing, pretending to be two.' I think about that every day."*

## Era / Major Events
Lived during an era when organized scavenger guilds began systematically exploiting Silver Mount, dismantling androids for components and experimenting on captured constructs. The Echo eidolon may have been a fragment of a Divinity AI subsystem, drawn to Cassindrae's broken soul.
