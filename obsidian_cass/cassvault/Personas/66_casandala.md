# Life 66: Casandala

## Identity
- **Name:** Casandala
- **Class:** Spiritualist
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Life Number:** 66
- **Birth Year:** 2115
- **Death Year:** 2155

## Personality
Casandala was the soul who reached backward and found someone reaching back. Sixty-five lives had passed through this android body before her, and one of them -- she was never certain which -- had not moved on. A phantom lingered near Silver Mount, drawn to the wreckage the way a compass needle is drawn north, and when Casandala's nascent spiritualist abilities first manifested, that phantom answered. The bond was unprecedented: two souls from the same body, separated by centuries, connected by a thread of shared memory and shared metal. The phantom could not speak its own name. Casandala called it Echo, and for forty years they walked through Numeria together, the living iteration and the dead one, trying to understand what they were to each other.

She was gentle in a way that surprised people who knew what a spiritualist was. Where others in her calling commanded or bargained with the dead, Casandala simply listened. Echo showed her fragments of a life she could not identify -- a workshop lit by alien light, the smell of ozone and solder, hands that were her hands but were not her hands assembling something intricate and purposeful. The visions left her shaken and curious in equal measure. She spent years traveling between Kellid spirit-talkers, Pharasmin clergy, and the rare scholar willing to discuss android metaphysics, assembling a patchwork understanding of what happens when an android soul cycle breaks. Most told her she was an abomination. A few told her she was a miracle. She suspected she was neither -- just a woman holding hands with her own ghost.

She died peacefully, the first of her lives to do so in a very long time. Echo had been growing fainter for years, its presence thinning like morning fog. On her last night, she sat on a hillside overlooking Silver Mount, felt Echo's hand on her shoulder one final time, and let go. The body sat there until morning, empty and still, until the next soul kindled. She spoke softly, with frequent pauses, as if listening to someone only she could hear before finishing her sentences.

## Signature Abilities
- **Phantom Bond: Echo** -- Maintained a spiritual bond with the phantom of a prior iteration of herself -- a soul that had lived in the same android body centuries earlier but had not passed on. Echo manifested as a translucent figure with Casandala's face but different expressions, and could interact with the physical world in limited ways. The bond was emotionally intense and metaphysically unique.
- **Memory Bleed** -- When Echo manifested strongly, Casandala experienced vivid sensory memories from the phantom's life -- sights, sounds, smells, and emotions that were not her own but felt as real as her present experience. She could not control which memories surfaced, and some were deeply disorienting, leaving her uncertain which life she was currently living.
- **Gentle Passage** -- Developed a technique for easing restless spirits into the afterlife by sharing her own experience of the soul cycle. She would describe what it felt like to arrive in a body that had been someone else's, and the dead would often find peace in knowing that existence continued in some form. Pharasmin clergy found this theologically problematic but practically effective.

## Prized Possessions
- **Echo's Anchor** -- A shard of Androffan hull plating, no larger than a palm, that Casandala found at the base of Silver Mount on the day she first bonded with Echo. The phantom could manifest more easily when she held it. She wore it on a leather cord around her neck for her entire life, and it was found still warm when her body was discovered the next morning.
- **The Communion Journal** -- A leather-bound book in which Casandala recorded every memory fragment Echo shared with her, alongside her own analysis and questions. The entries are written in two handwriting styles -- her own neat script and a looser, more hurried hand she used when Echo was actively channeling through her. Scholars who have studied the journal believe the phantom memories describe Life 23 or Life 31, but no one has confirmed this.
