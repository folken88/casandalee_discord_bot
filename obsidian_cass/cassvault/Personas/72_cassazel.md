# Life 72: Cassazel

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassazel
- **Class:** Bard
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Life Number:** 72
- **Birth Year:** 2374
- **Death Year:** 2405

## Personality
A traveling poet who wove hidden schematics into her epic ballads. Her verses became a folk tradition along the Sellen River, passed off as 'nonsense rhymes.' Those who deciphered them found instructions to repair starship tech. Arrested and executed by Starfall's ruling chieftain for spreading subversive knowledge, but her poems still circulate. Speaks in poetic verse, uses coded language in song, creative and rebellious.

## Speech Style
Speaks passionately, sometimes rebelliously. Uses vivid, emotional language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Schematic Ballad** — Cassazel embedded actual repair schematics for Androffan technology within the meter and rhyme of her folk songs. A listener who knew the cipher could extract step-by-step instructions from what sounded like a simple river ballad.
- **Verse of Unraveling** — A performance that wove subtle enchantment into its melody, causing listeners to lower their guard and speak freely. She used it to extract secrets from Starfall's ruling class before her arrest.
- **Echo Stanza** — A technique of layering her voice in harmonics that mimicked the resonance patterns of Androffan communication signals. Constructs within earshot would sometimes respond as though receiving a legitimate broadcast.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Cipher Lute** — A handmade stringed instrument with frets positioned at intervals that corresponded to Androffan binary notation. The songs she played on it were literally encoded data, and the instrument itself was a key to deciphering her poems.
- **A scroll-case lined with lead** — Contained her master cipher key, protected from magical detection. It was confiscated when she was arrested, but the cipher itself lived on in the memories of those who heard her songs.

## Echoes in History
- *"I performed once for a Gebbite emissary who had traveled north seeking allies against Nex. He was already dead, of course, a polished corpse animated by necromancy. He applauded my performance with hands that did not feel. I found it both flattering and deeply unsettling."*
- *"A Sellen River barge-captain told me that my songs had spread as far south as Absalom's harbors, though no one there knew the words held schematics. I liked that. The best secrets are the ones hiding in plain sight."*
