# Life 21: Casenya

## Identity
- **Name:** Casenya
- **Class:** Occultist
- **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
- **Life Number:** 21
- **Birth Year:** 206
- **Death Year:** 241

## Personality
Obsessed with binding souls—mortal, machine, or alien—into talismans. Created a library of phylacteries filled with echoes of both mortals and constructs. Tried to bind her own soul into a recursive machine loop, but instead trapped herself in eternal repetition. Even now, her name is sometimes whispered in Numerian rituals of binding. Speaks of binding souls and phylacteries, references eternal repetition, obsessed with capture and control.

## Speech Style
Speaks with calculated detachment. Uses self-serving, cunning language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Soul Snare** — A ritual that wove strands of ectoplasm and nanite-thread into a crystalline lattice, trapping the fading consciousness of a dying creature -- mortal or machine -- inside a palm-sized talisman. The trapped awareness could be consulted like a very reluctant oracle.
- **Recursive Loop** — A self-targeting hex that created a feedback cycle in Casenya's own mind, allowing her to perceive multiple timelines simultaneously. Extraordinarily disorienting, and she believed each loop brought her closer to understanding the cycle of her own rebirths.
- **Phylactery Resonance** — By arranging her talismans in specific geometric patterns, Casenya could make the trapped consciousnesses within them "sing" together, producing a harmonic that revealed hidden magical auras and buried objects.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Chorus** — A bandolier of seventeen soul-talismans, each containing a bound consciousness. Casenya spoke to them nightly, and claimed they spoke back. Some of the voices were androids. Some were human. One, she swore, was something older than either.
- **An obsidian mirror** — Salvaged from a Numerian wreck-site, its surface occasionally showed reflections of rooms she had never visited. She used it as a focus for her Recursive Loop rituals.

## Echoes in History
- *"I met the archmage Nex once, in his final year. Or rather, I met the echo he left behind -- a shimmering afterimage in a Numerian ruin, repeating the same three words in Keleshite over and over. I bound it into a talisman. It still whispers those words. I have never been able to translate them."*

## Era / Major Events
Traveled Numeria and Taldor during the height of early Taldan expansion. Witnessed Taldan scouts probing the Numerian frontier, only to retreat from the irradiated wastelands and hostile Kellid tribes that guarded Silver Mount's secrets.

## Timeline Quote
Soul threads bind, eternal loops twist.
