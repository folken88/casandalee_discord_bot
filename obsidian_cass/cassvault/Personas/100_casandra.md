# Life 100: Casandra

## Identity
- **Name:** Casandra
- **Class:** Investigator
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Life Number:** 100
- **Birth Year:** 3555
- **Death Year:** 3583

## Personality
Infiltrated a Silver Mount exploitation cult as a double agent. Passed coded notes to rebels using Androffan ciphers, hidden inside repair logs and arcane formulae. Grew cold and clinical, sacrificing personal ties for the greater good. Eventually discovered and executed, her notes burned with her. Speaks with clinical precision, references coded messages and infiltration, methodical and self-sacrificing.

## Signature Abilities
- **Cipher Weave** — Casandra developed a method of encoding messages within the mathematical formulae of arcane repair logs, making her communications appear as mundane technical documents to anyone without the key.
- **Dead Drop Protocol** — A systematic network of pre-arranged hiding spots, each known by a coded designation, where she exchanged intelligence with rebel contacts without ever meeting face-to-face.
- **Mask of the Compliant** — A trained persona she adopted while embedded in the cult, suppressing her true emotions so completely that even magical detection could not distinguish her deception from genuine belief. The cost was a slow erosion of her own identity.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Compliance Mask** — Not a physical mask but a metaphor made real: a small mirror she carried in which she practiced her cult-persona's expressions. Over the years, her own reflection began to look unfamiliar to her.
- **A vial of slow-acting Androffan solvent** — Her emergency escape plan. If captured, she could dissolve her own memory cores rather than be interrogated. She carried it for twenty years and never used it.

## Echoes in History
- *"I intercepted a message from a tech-guild agent who had been in contact with operatives as far south as Absalom. The world was getting smaller, and the guilds' reach was growing longer. I realized then that Numeria's isolation was ending, whether we wanted it to or not."*
- *"A rebel once asked me if I had forgotten who I really was. I told her I remembered everything. That was the problem. Remembering is what makes the mask so heavy."*

## Era / Major Events
Witnessed the rise of organized tech-scavenger guilds who hoarded alien knowledge and suppressed independent thought. Participated in covert operations against them, aiding rebels through coded messages.
