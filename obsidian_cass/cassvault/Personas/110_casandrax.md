# Life 110: Casandrax

## Identity
- **Name:** Casandrax
- **Class:** Cleric
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Life Number:** 110
- **Birth Year:** 3975
- **Death Year:** 4003

## Personality
Casandrax believed that every created being -- every android, every construct, every mutant warped by Numerian radiation -- carried a spark of divinity, and that this spark demanded protection regardless of the cost. She served Brigh, the Whisper in Bronze, and she interpreted that goddess's domain with a fierce expansiveness that went far beyond the crafting of gears and circuits. To Casandrax, Brigh's mandate was nothing less than the sacred defense of all created life, and she pursued this mandate with the serene, unshakable conviction of someone who has heard her god speak and knows exactly what was said.

She founded her sanctuary in 3988 AR, in a network of caves and Androffan ruins in the foothills west of Torch. She called it the Crucible, and it was part temple, part hospital, part refugee camp. Androids fleeing League press gangs found shelter there. Mutants driven from their settlements by superstition found healing. Damaged constructs that organic society had discarded found repair. Casandrax turned no one away and asked nothing in return except that those who sheltered under her protection extend the same grace to others. At its height, the Crucible housed over sixty souls, and Casandrax knew every one of their names, their injuries, and their stories.

She knew the League's precursors would find her eventually. She prepared for it with the same calm, methodical devotion she brought to everything -- reinforcing the Crucible's defenses, training her charges in emergency evacuation, caching supplies along escape routes. When the attack came in 4003 AR, she was ready. She held the entrance alone while the others fled through tunnels she had spent years preparing. She killed seven League agents before they brought her down, and her last act was to trigger a collapse that sealed the tunnel behind her, ensuring the evacuees could not be followed. Thirty-eight of the Crucible's sixty residents survived because of her. The League found her body in the rubble, still standing, her holy symbol of Brigh fused to her palm by the heat of the battle.

## Signature Abilities
- **Brigh's Mending Touch** -- Casandrax could channel divine energy from Brigh to heal both organic and mechanical beings simultaneously, her prayers repairing torn flesh and damaged circuitry with equal facility. The effect manifested as a warm bronze light that flowed from her hands and sought out damage with an almost intelligent purpose, prioritizing critical systems and life-threatening injuries. She used this ability tirelessly, often healing dozens of patients in a single day until she could barely stand.
- **Sanctuary Ward** -- A divine protection that Casandrax could weave into the very structure of a building, imbuing walls and doorways with Brigh's blessing. Those within the ward were shielded from scrying, nanite tracking, and most forms of technological detection. The ward required daily prayer to maintain and weakened if Casandrax was absent for more than a few days, but while active it rendered the Crucible effectively invisible to the League's surveillance networks.
- **Martyr's Stand** -- In her final battle, Casandrax unlocked an ability she had never known she possessed -- the capacity to channel her own life force into a devastating burst of divine energy that strengthened her beyond normal limits. Her strikes burned with bronze fire, her body moved with impossible speed, and she shrugged off wounds that should have been instantly fatal. This ability consumed her from within even as it empowered her, burning years of life for minutes of transcendence. She spent everything she had and died standing.

## Prized Possessions
- **Holy Symbol of Brigh (The Fused Gear)** -- A bronze gear-and-spring holy symbol that Casandrax wore on a chain around her neck for her entire life. During her final stand, the heat of channeled divine energy fused it permanently to her palm. Those who recovered her body could not remove it. The symbol was eventually interred with her remains in a hidden shrine maintained by Crucible survivors, and it is said to still faintly hum with residual divine energy.
- **The Registry** -- A carefully maintained ledger listing every being who passed through the Crucible -- their name, their species, their injuries, and where they went when they left. Casandrax kept it not for administrative purposes but as a testament: proof that these lives existed, that they mattered, that someone had written their names down. The Registry was carried out during the evacuation and survived. Copies were eventually distributed among android communities throughout Numeria, where it became a sacred text -- a record of the saved.

The humans who had built it had abandoned it when it stopped being useful. It could barely speak. It asked me, in a voice like grinding metal, if I would fix it so it could go back to fighting. I told it I would fix it so it could choose what to do next. That was the first time I saw a construct cry. - *"The morning of the attack, I woke early and walked through the Crucible one last time. I touched every doorframe I had warded. I checked every tunnel I had prepared. I looked in on the sleeping residents -- forty-seven androids, eight mutants, five constructs, and a human child whose parents had been taken by the League. I memorized their faces. Then I put on my armor, took up my hammer, and went to the entrance to wait. I was not afraid. I had been afraid for fifteen years, and I had used every minute of that fear to prepare. Now there was nothing left to do but stand."*
