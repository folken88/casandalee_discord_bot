## Personality
The first android incarnation of Cassula's imprint, awakening in the wreckage of the Divinity one year after the crash. Retained fragments of the navigator's tactical code and an instinctive understanding of the ship's systems. In -4294 AR, she witnessed Earthfall -- the Starstone's impact that destroyed Azlant and Thassilon. Even in landlocked Numeria, the sky darkened for years, tsunamis of dust and ash swept across the land, and the Age of Darkness began. Having survived one cataclysm from space and another from the ground, she became deeply fatalistic. Spent her final decades guiding scattered survivors through the first terrible years of perpetual twilight. Speaks with tactical precision, references navigation and duty, heroic and methodical.
