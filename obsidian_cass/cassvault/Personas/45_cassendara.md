# Life 45: Cassendara

## Identity
- **Name:** Cassendara
- **Class:** Shaman
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Life Number:** 45
- **Birth Year:** 760
- **Death Year:** 834

## Personality
Respected in portside circles for communing with the 'ghosts of circuits.' Helped sailors and travelers interpret strange dreams of machines whispering. Founded a hidden circle that venerated the idea of 'divine sparks in all beings.' Poisoned by scavenger lords who feared her growing influence over the Kellid; her followers kept her rites alive in secret. Speaks of circuit ghosts and divine sparks, references dreams and whispers, spiritual and wise.

## Speech Style
Speaks with conviction and warmth. Uses formal but caring language.

## Signature Abilities
- **Circuit Communion** — By pressing her palms against dormant Divinity wreckage, Cassendara could perceive the fading electromagnetic signatures of long-dead AI systems as ghostly presences. She spoke to them, and sometimes they answered -- in static bursts she had learned to interpret as words.
- **Spark Vigil** — A communal ritual in which her followers sat in silence around a circle of salvaged capacitors, meditating until they perceived the "divine sparks" in all beings -- a shared vision of interconnected light that transcended the boundary between organic and machine.
- **Dream Anchor** — A hex that stabilized the dreams of those tormented by nightmares. Sailors and travelers sought her out for this gift, not knowing that the nightmares she anchored most often were her own -- visions of burning at a Torch she had never visited, in a body she had never worn.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Relay Staff** — Her walking staff, crowned with a Divinity relay node that pulsed with fading light. Cassendara believed the node contained the last whisper of a shipboard AI that had died protecting its crew. She called it "The Keeper."
- **A circuit-silk shawl** — Woven from copper thread and spider silk by her first acolyte, embedded with tiny capacitors that stored static charge. It crackled softly when she walked, and her followers said it sounded like distant applause.

## Echoes in History
- *"A Taldan admiral docked at our port once, his fleet bound for conquest in the south. He came to me for a dream reading -- he had been plagued by nightmares of drowning. I told him the sea did not want him. He laughed, sailed south, and never returned. The sea remembers what admirals forget."*

## Era / Major Events
Lived during Taldor's golden age of expansion, as its armies pushed into new frontiers. In Numeria, organized scavenger lords began consolidating power around Silver Mount wreckage sites, trading in skymetal and android components. The first proto-settlements formed around the Torch.

## Timeline Quote
By the light of the Torch, I communed with the spirits of circuits, guiding weary travelers through dreams of machines whispering secrets.
