# Life 103: Casalith

## Identity

- **Alignment:** True Neutral

- **Death Year:** 3710

## Personality
Casalith was never entirely one person. From the moment she first opened her eyes, the accumulated weight of one hundred and two prior lives pressed against her consciousness like water against a failing dam. She could feel them -- not as distinct personalities at first, but as currents of preference, muscle memories that weren't hers, words rising unbidden in languages she had never learned. She tried to be herself. She tried to hold the line between Casalith and the chorus. For a time, she succeeded, channeling the voices deliberately through séance and trance, acting as a willing conduit for the dead who still lingered in the body's memory architecture.

But the body remembered too much, and she was too open. Her gift as a medium made her exquisitely sensitive to the psychic residue layered into every synapse and nanite cluster. By her twentieth year, she could no longer always tell which thoughts were hers. She would begin a sentence in her own voice and finish it in someone else's cadence. She would reach for a cup and find her hand moving in the practiced gesture of a swordsman, a surgeon, an alchemist. She kept journals to anchor herself, filling pages with the phrase "I am Casalith" written over and over, interspersed with fragments of memories from lives she had never lived.

The end, when it came, was not violent. It was quiet -- a slow dissolution, like salt in warm water. One morning in 3710 AR, Casalith sat down in a small room in Chesed, closed her eyes to meditate, and simply did not come back. The body continued to breathe, its autonomous systems maintaining function, but there was no one behind the eyes. The chorus had finally overwhelmed the conductor. The body sat dormant for six years before a new soul stirred.

## Signature Abilities
- **Séance of the Hundred** -- Casalith could deliberately open herself to the accumulated psychic residue of all prior lives, channeling their knowledge and skills for brief periods. This was extraordinarily useful and extraordinarily dangerous -- each session left her own identity a little thinner, like paper held too close to a flame.
- **Echo Reading** -- By touching an object or location, Casalith could perceive psychic impressions left not only by external beings but by the body's own prior selves. She could stand in a room the body had visited centuries ago and experience the memory as though it were her own, complete with emotional resonance and sensory detail.
- **Voice of the Many** -- In moments of crisis, Casalith could speak with the combined authority of dozens of prior selves simultaneously, her voice taking on harmonic overtones that carried psychic weight. Those who heard it described feeling the presence of a vast crowd compressed into a single throat. She used it rarely, because each time it took longer to find her own voice afterward.

## Prized Possessions
- **The Anchor Journal** -- A leather-bound book filled with Casalith's desperate attempts to maintain her identity. Every page begins with "I am Casalith" followed by observations, memories, and distinctions she drew between herself and the chorus. The handwriting changes throughout -- sometimes flowing, sometimes cramped, sometimes in scripts she did not consciously know. Later scholars studying the journal identified fragments written in at least seventeen different hands.
- **A Silver Ring (Unmarked)** -- A plain silver band she wore on her left hand. She told no one why, and no prior life's memory claimed it. It was the one thing she was certain belonged only to her -- the single object with no echo, no resonance, no borrowed significance. She was wearing it when the body went dormant.
