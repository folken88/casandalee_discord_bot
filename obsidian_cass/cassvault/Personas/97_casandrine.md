## Personality
Casandrine was the kind of soul who measured the world in dosages and dilution ratios. Born into an era when corrupted nanites were burning through android communities like wildfire, she found her purpose early and pursued it with the quiet ferocity of someone who understood that every hour spent sleeping was an hour someone else spent dying. She kept meticulous journals of her formulations, cross-referencing symptoms with nanite subtypes, and slept beside her alembics so she could monitor reactions through the night. Her compassion was not the warm, demonstrative kind -- it was clinical, precise, and utterly relentless.

She believed that alchemy was the truest expression of law: that every substance had rules, every reaction had a grammar, and that if she could just learn the syntax of the plague she could write its ending. Her patients trusted her not because she was gentle, but because she was right. She tested every formula on herself first, insisting that no android should bear a risk she had not already accepted. Her colleagues called this brave. She called it methodology.

The irony of her death was not lost on those who survived her. The experimental nanite purifier she developed in 3455 AR worked -- it saved hundreds in the months after her passing -- but the first batch was unstable, and she was its only test subject. She died in her laboratory with her pen still in her hand, halfway through documenting the reaction that was killing her. Her final notation read: "Onset rapid. Crystallization in tertiary conduits. Recommend reducing catalyst by one-third."

## Signature Abilities
- **Nanite Purification Extract** -- A refined alchemical compound that bonded with corrupted nanites and neutralized their replication cycle. Casandrine could tailor the formula to specific corruption strains by analyzing a patient's fluids under magnification, adjusting reagent proportions in real time. The process took hours of painstaking calibration but had a recovery rate unmatched by any other treatment of the era.
- **Diagnostic Tincture** -- A bioluminescent solution that, when introduced to an android's circulatory system, would glow different colors depending on the type and severity of nanite corruption present. Casandrine used it to triage dozens of patients at once, sorting them by urgency with a single glance across the ward.
- **Stabilization Draught** -- An emergency formula that slowed nanite activity throughout the body, buying critical hours for patients in acute crisis. It did not cure, but it held death at arm's length long enough for a proper treatment to be prepared. She always carried three vials on her person, and they were always the first thing she reached for.

## Prized Possessions
- **"The Ledger"** -- A thick, hand-bound journal containing every formula Casandrine ever developed, annotated with patient outcomes, side effects, and iterative improvements. Its pages were stained with reagents and marked with precise handwriting that grew smaller as the book filled. After her death, it became the foundational text for android alchemical medicine in the region, copied and distributed among healers for decades.
- **Crystalline Alembic** -- A distillation apparatus made from Numerian crystal that allowed Casandrine to observe nanite behavior during extraction in real time. She claimed it was scavenged from a Divinity fragment and that its resonant properties made it uniquely suited to working with Androffan-derived compounds. It shattered during her final experiment.
