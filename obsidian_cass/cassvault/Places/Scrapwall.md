# Scrapwall

**Region:** Numeria | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A massive junkyard settlement in the Numerian wastes, built from the debris of crashed starship wreckage. Scrapwall is a lawless town controlled by various gangs and warlords who salvage technology from the ruins. It was the base of operations for Hellion, an AI that had left Silver Mount and set itself up as a false god worshipped by the local orc and human gangs. The Iron Gods party traveled here to confront Hellion, who was ultimately destroyed by Maginnis's pistol "Justice" on 4716.09.02.
