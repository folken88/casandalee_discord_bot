# Carrion Hill

**Region:** Ustalav | **City:** Unknown

## Description

Location associated with guards whose equipment was found inside aberrations; A town under repeated attack (twice daily for a week). Has a rampant infestation of purple/black roaches locally called 'carrying beetles.' Known for local beer made from these roaches. Town has bars and local guards.; A town being attacked by aberrations that carry off bodies of residents. The creatures are non-native and appear to have arrived through a dimensional gate.; A town built into a hill that has existed in many forms over centuries. Was a major eastern trade hub under Tarbuff's dictatorship, built up during the Shining Crusade, then war torn and leveled, and rebuilt again. Currently built over an extensive sewer system from the Shining Crusade era
