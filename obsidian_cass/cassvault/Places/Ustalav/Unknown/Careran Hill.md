# Careran Hill

**Region:** Ustalav | **City:** Unknown

## Description

A hill with the most stable, flattest spot that has been converted into a barracks. Contains foundation stones centuries old with multiple construction layers. Houses a cell block with a trap door in the northwest room leading to a basement.
