# Cockroach Town

**Region:** Ustalav | **City:** Unknown

## Description

A town built of cockroaches, described as resembling Hobbiton from Lord of the Rings. Appears peaceful from a distance (600-700 ft away) but becomes increasingly unsettling the closer one gets. Players are warned not to drink the beer or water there.
