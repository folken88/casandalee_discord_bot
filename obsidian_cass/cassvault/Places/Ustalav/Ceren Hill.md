# Ceren Hill

**Region:** Ustalav | **City:** Ceren Hill

## Description

A town on the brink of collapse and madness, ruled by three elected Keepers who wear sigil rings. The town has a history of misfortune, conspiracy, and worse befalling its leaders. Keepers who abandon the town while wearing the amulet are compelled to return by whispering voices and shifting streets. The town appears to have ancient tentacle-related history.
