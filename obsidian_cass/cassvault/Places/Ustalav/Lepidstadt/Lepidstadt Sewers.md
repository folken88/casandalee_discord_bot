# Lepidstadt Sewers

**Region:** Ustalav | **City:** Lepidstadt

## Description

Underground sewer system beneath Lepidstadt where the party is heading to hunt creatures; Underground sewer system with abnormal airflow, water bubbling from walls, steam vents with valves, and evidence of beetle brew distillery operations. Contains hostile creatures (gooners). Smells of typical sewer waste with faint hints of beetle brew.; Underground sewer system filled with flammable gases, containing a tube-like structure. Currently infested with dangerous creatures including a purple minotaur and squid-like monsters
