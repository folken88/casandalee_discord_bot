# Ancient Sewer System

**Region:** Ustalav | **City:** Lepidstadt

## Description

An ancient sewer with superior brickwork compared to street level, foul-smelling, containing large enchanted copper pipes (3-4 foot diameter) with valves. Connected to Lanke Street via a hole opened by a basilisk. Foggy conditions to the south.
