# Sumprat Tavern

**Region:** Ustalav | **City:** Unknown (rural town)

## Description

A depressing tavern filled with downtrodden patrons showing signs of alcohol abuse and despair. Described as a very gross place run by idiots. Contains city guards and various townspeople in poor condition; A tavern in Kerrion Hill with a bar, local beer selection (five copper per beer), and live gnome bard entertainment. Keeper Hive appears to be associated with the establishment.
