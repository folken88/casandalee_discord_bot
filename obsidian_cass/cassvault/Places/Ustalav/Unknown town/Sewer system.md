# Sewer system

**Region:** Ustalav | **City:** Unknown town

## Description

Extensive underground sewer network with multiple tunnels flowing west toward Miden Swamp. Poorly maintained with fallen bricks and debris. Contains flowing sewage. Three main tunnel directions: east (too small for large creatures), west (too small, minimal use), and south (heavily used by the basilisk); Underground sewer system beneath Lepidstadt containing aberrations including purple nerple basilisks and harpies. Contains acid and noxious gases.; Underground tunnel system beneath Lepidstadt where the party is currently exploring; contains invisible creatures and smells of beetle brew; Underground sewer with flowing sewage/poop water. Contains dangerous creatures. Brewery is located 25 feet northeast through a wall.; Underground sewer with a metal grate enchanted to prevent rust. Contains spilled chemicals and water. Slick and difficult to climb.
