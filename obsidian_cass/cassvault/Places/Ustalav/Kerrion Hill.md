# Kerrion Hill

**Region:** Ustalav | **City:** Kerrion Hill

## Description

A swampy, hot, extremely humid location in southern Louisiana-like west Florida setting. Known for sump beetles (purplish-pink iridescent cockroaches) that are ground up and used in fabrics, walls, and roof tiles, giving the area a distinctive purple-pink coloration. Home to the Sumprat Tavern.; A town with a West Filth neighborhood containing a bar where the party is staying. Described as a rough area
