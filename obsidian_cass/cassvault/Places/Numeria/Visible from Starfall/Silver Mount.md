# Silver Mount

**Region:** Numeria | **City:** Visible from Starfall

## Description

A massive artificial structure disguised as a mountain. Lower 90% appears as typical Numerian rock (light brown to beige, resembling Utah landscape). Upper portion is a perfectly formed silver object over a mile long, revealed to be a technological construct or ship. Visible from Starfall on one side.
