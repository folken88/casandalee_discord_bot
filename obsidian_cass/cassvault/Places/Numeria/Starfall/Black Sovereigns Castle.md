# Black Sovereigns Castle

**Region:** Numeria | **City:** Starfall

## Description

Multi-floor fortress. Top floor: Black Sovereign's quarters and lounge. Second floor: Garton's Lab (off-limits), Hill Giant quarters, Hall of History (statues), Sovereign's Lounge (dining/drinking), Guard quarters with dozens of beds. First floor: Main hall filled with Hill Giants.
