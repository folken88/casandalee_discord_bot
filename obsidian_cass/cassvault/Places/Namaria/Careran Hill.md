# Careran Hill

**Region:** Namaria | **City:** Careran Hill

## Description

A deteriorating town described as a 'hell hole' with disappearing residents, destroyed houses, and a heavily intoxicated population. The water and beer smell of sulfur. Located on the route between Califus and Silvermount
