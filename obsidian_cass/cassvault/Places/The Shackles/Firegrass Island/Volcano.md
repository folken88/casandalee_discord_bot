# Volcano

**Region:** The Shackles | **City:** Firegrass Island

## Description

Extremely dangerous volcanic area with poisonous vog, intense heat (requires at least 10 fire resistance), and lava fountains reaching 1000 feet into the air. Located between Goatshead and the main settlement, accessible via south side approach. Contains rare supernatural lichen that only grows in hot, poisonous atmospheres.; Contains lyken and creatures related to the salamander family; accessible from the south side; requires resist heat spell to survive; lyken can be harvested
