# Goatshead Port

**Region:** The Shackles | **City:** Firegrass Island

## Description

A port settlement on Firegrass Island where the party has been operating. Leadership is willing to purchase and maintain the TPK as a defense and scout ship.; Port where the party sells the TPK (The Pillager's Knife) for a good price; Good place to hire people for crew; Major port with large pier capable of docking multiple ships and performing dock repairs on several vessels simultaneously; Port settlement with skilled smiths capable of enhancing magical weapons like the Iron Clutch of the Devil to +2 and adding elemental properties. Thragorn operates here as a craftsperson who can create magic items.; Port where multiple ships are being maintained. Basmara's Courage, Bold Folly, and Winks are going into dry dock here.
