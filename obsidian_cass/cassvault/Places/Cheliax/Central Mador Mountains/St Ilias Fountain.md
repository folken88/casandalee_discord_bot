# St Ilias Fountain

**Region:** Cheliax | **City:** Central Mador Mountains

## Description

A sacred site devoted to Iomedae and her first saint, Ilia. Located in a rock-ringed veil along the southern ridge of the Menor mountains. Features Seruan Pool as the entrance to an underground complex. Contains a temple behind a raging waterfall with a glowing crystal-clear pool. The water has supernatural light that fades within 24 hours of being taken from the site. The Asmodeans have redacted most written information about it.
