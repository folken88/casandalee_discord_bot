# Waterfall

**Region:** Cheliax | **City:** Unknown - remote location

## Description

A large waterfall dropping approximately 40 feet off a flat rock cliff into a clear pool below. Located in a remote part of the woods with no clear foot paths due to recent war. Radiates extremely high magical aura. No visible glow during midday.; 90 ft away to the Northwest, leads to temple. Can be traversed by flying, swimming, or climbing (DC 30 climb check per 10 ft). Water has spread across forest from magical fountain.
