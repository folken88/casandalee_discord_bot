# Dyar Fortress

**Region:** Cheliax | **City:** Unknown

## Description

Multi-level fortress with north and south towers, three levels. North side features a 100-foot diameter atrium with 5-foot wide windows overlooking a river. Contains a map room with a large map of Cheliax and chess pieces representing armies. Ground floor north has a key-shaped table (symbol of Avadar), stairs to docks, and balconies. South side has arrow slit windows and a jail cell.
