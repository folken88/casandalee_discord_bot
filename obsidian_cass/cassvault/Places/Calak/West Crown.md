# West Crown

**Region:** Calak | **City:** West Crown

## Description

Former capital of Calak, now contains an old Temple to Iomedae that has not been worshiped at for 50 years. Currently serves as the headquarters of the Glorious Reclamation with hundreds of soldiers, dozens of clerics and wizards, and powerful angels.
