# Sniper Arrow

**Type:** loot (ammunition) | **Owner:** Rodney (Chris) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

An arrow that hums when nocked. For each round held nocked while moving no more than 30 feet, the wielder gains a stacking +1 bonus to hit (maximum +5).

Also grants Improved Critical, providing a +4 bonus to confirm critical hits.
