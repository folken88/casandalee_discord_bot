# Shudderwood Jons

**Owner:** Storgrim | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Wondrous item (clothing)

Divinely crafted short-pants of the finest woven jean, blessed with remarkable durability and flexibility. Reduces the wearer's armor check penalty by 1. For shapeshifters, the Jons stretch and adapt seamlessly to any bipedal form, maintaining their fit and modesty through any transformation. Rumored to have originated in Shudderwood, where such garments are a practical necessity for the lycanthropic locals.
