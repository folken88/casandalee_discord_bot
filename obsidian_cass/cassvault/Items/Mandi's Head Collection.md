# Mandi's Head Collection

**Owner:** Kate Blackwood (CC), Rhyarca (SS) | **Player:** Mandi

Mandi's characters have a well-known habit of collecting severed heads in their backpacks for use with the *Speak with Dead* spell — which Mandi calls **"Speak with Head"** (the terms are interchangeable in this group). The heads are stored in Carriers by Calvin containers ("The Body Bag" for Rhyarca, "Northgarde Thicc Boi" for Kate).

## Known Heads

### Carrion Crown
- **Auren Vrood's Head** — Head of the Whispering Way field commander, Wizard/Necromancer 14. Currently in the party backpack after his defeat at Feldgrau. A valuable intelligence source via Speak with Dead.
- **King of Skanktown's Head** — Found in Clickboom Hideout Storage.

### Skull & Shackles (Rhyarca's Body Bag — confirmed inventory)
- **Humanoid Skulls (Nerds)** x7 — Generic humanoid skulls, 7 lbs total
- **Chelish Agent Skull** x1 — Skull of a Chelish intelligence operative
- **Tiefling Diabolist of Asmodeus Head** x1 — Head of an Asmodean cultist
- **Windward Jungle Ranger Head** x2 — Heads from jungle rangers encountered on Windward Isle
- **Lord of Gol-Khazak Skull** x1 — Skull of the dwarven lord of Gol-Khazak
Total: 12 heads/skulls in The Body Bag

### Hells Vengeance
- **Severed Dragon Head** — Head of Parnoneryx, the great Gold Dragon and Champion of the Glorious Reclamation. Stored in the Armored Luxury Carriage.

## Notes

The practice of carrying severed heads for *Speak with Dead* is a running theme across Mandi's characters and has become a group in-joke. There is even a session titled "Speak with Skull & Shackles" (2025-05-29) referencing this habit. Players have discussed carrying severed heads in bags across multiple campaigns.
