# Stolen Winds

**Owner:** Bujon | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Codex of Stolen Winds (spellbook/artifact)

A tome bound in blue sharkskin, containing the accumulated weather-magic of unknown origin. Allows the reader to cast Control Weather once per week. The cover depicts a ship caught in a violent storm -- an image that changes subtly each time the book is opened, as if the storm is alive and shifting. The pages within are written in a flowing script that seems to move like wind across the paper.
