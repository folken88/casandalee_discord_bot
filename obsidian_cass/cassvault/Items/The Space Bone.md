# The Space Bone

**Type:** weapon (club/exotic) | **Owner:** Luna (Rye) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A femur-sized object that exists as many simultaneous dimensional occurrences of itself. Its appearance is that of ever-moving space riddled with storms -- looking at it is like staring into a window to somewhere else entirely. Strikes land as multidimensional impacts, hitting across planes at once. Nobody fully understands what it is, including its wielder.
