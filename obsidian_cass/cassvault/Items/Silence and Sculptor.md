# Silence & Sculptor

**Type:** weapon (paired daggers) | **Owner:** Nomkath (Daemon) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A pair of bonded daggers that must stay within 10 feet of each other or become non-magical. Each blade has a living eye set into the pommel that tracks movement, granting +2 to Perception and initiative checks. On a confirmed critical hit, they impose a DC 18 silence effect on the target. Both daggers feature a built-in injection system for delivering poisons or alchemical substances. Unsettling to look at, worse to be cut by.
