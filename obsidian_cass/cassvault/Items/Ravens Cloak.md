# Ravens Cloak

**Type:** equipment (cloak) | **Owner:** Nomkath (Daemon) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

Blackened leather lined with mithral chain and woven with black feathers. Grants Resist Cold 10 and Resist Fire 10, plus a +3 resistance bonus to saves. Once per day: Feather Fall. Once per day: Bypass -- cloaks the wearer's weapons in shadow, changing their damage type to negative energy for 10 rounds. The cloak of someone who operates between the spaces where light doesn't reach.
