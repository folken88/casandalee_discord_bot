# Katya's Blade

**Owner:** Rhyarca | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** +1 Longsword (Finesse, Bane: Demons and Devils)

A finely balanced +1 longsword that can be used with Weapon Finesse. Bears the Bane enchantment against demons and devils, flaring with holy light when drawn against fiendish foes. Originally crafted as a wedding gift for a half-elven Andoran noblewoman named Katya, the blade's elegant design reflects both elven craftsmanship and Andoran ideals of liberty and justice.
