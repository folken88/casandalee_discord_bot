# Besmara's Bicorne

**Owner:** Holden | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Wondrous item (hat)

A large pirate bicorne hat blessed by Besmara, the Pirate Queen. Grants a +2 bonus to Acrobatics and Climb checks. If the wearer claims Besmara as their patron deity, the hat floats to the water's surface at a rate of 30 ft per round when submerged -- and brings the wearer with it. The hat is weathered but never seems to get any more worn, and always sits at a jaunty angle no matter how rough the seas.
