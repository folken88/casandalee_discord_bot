# Amulet of the Echo Line

**Owner:** Vaughan | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Wondrous item (amulet)

An iron disk bearing the image of a defiant dwarf, worn on a chain around the neck. Grants the wearer a +4 bonus against fear effects and a +2 bonus to concentration checks. Once per day, the amulet allows the wearer to stay conscious for 1 extra round when reduced to 0 HP, channeling the stubborn endurance of the dwarven warriors it commemorates.
