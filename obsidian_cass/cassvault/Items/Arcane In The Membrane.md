# Arcane In The Membrane

**Type:** equipment (book) | **Owner:** Kovira (Sydney) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

A book that grants +5 to Knowledge (Arcana) checks. Contains hundreds of blank spellbook pages for recording spells.

Once per day, the wielder can quick-change one prepared spell as a full-round action while holding the book open.
