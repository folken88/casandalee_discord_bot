# Revenge

**Type:** weapon (revolver) | **Owner:** Maginnis (Hans) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A six-chambered revolver and the second of Maginnis's paired sidearms, alongside [[Justice]]. Revenge is invisible to everyone except its wielder -- holster included. It has perfect balance; a natural 1 merely locks the mechanism rather than misfiring, clearable with a move action. The gun you never see coming.
