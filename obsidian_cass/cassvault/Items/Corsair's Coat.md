# Corsair's Coat

**Owner:** Holden | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Mithral chain shirt +1 (as weathered leather coat)

A weathered leather longcoat that conceals a layer of mithral chain beneath its battered exterior. Functions as a mithral chain shirt +1 while looking like nothing more than a well-worn sailor's coat. Grants a +2 bonus to Acrobatics checks and a +4 bonus to CMD. The coat's leather is salt-stained and scarred from countless voyages, but the hidden mithral links beneath are flawless.
