# Great Buffalo Hunt (GBH)

**Owner:** Rodney "Danger" Smith (Chris) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Orcish Hornbow (massive compound bow) | +2 enhancement
**Aura:** Evocation | CL 15 | **Price:** 12,500 gp
**Requirement:** +4 STR bonus minimum to wield

## Description
Made from the horns of a great buffalo, this Orcish-Hornbow is a massive compound bow. It requires custom ammunition — regular arrows snap from the greater forces. Properly built GBH arrows must be enchanted at least +1 to survive being fired. On impact, they explode in a small but potent 2-foot diameter orange fireball. The noise of impacts can be heard for miles — easily as loud as most firearms.

## Attack Modes

### Standard Attack
- Range: 60 ft (5 increments) | Ammo: Custom GBH arrows
- Damage: 2d6 piercing + 1d6 fire/force, 20/x3
- On crit: +2d10 fire/force

### Bullseye Single Shot
- +4 attack bonus, 19-20/x2, +2d6 piercing on non-crit

### Explosive Shot (WIS mod/day)
- Range: 100 ft | 10-foot radius AoE
- Damage: 1d6 piercing + 1d6 bludgeoning + 1d6 force + 1d6 fire
- Reflex save halves damage and negates "Glitter-Cone" effect (-20 stealth for 2 rounds)

## Custom Ammunition Types
- GBH Shafts (standard, 83 in stock)
- Phase Locking Arrows (6)
- Arrow of the Crusader (5)
- Arrow of Undead Bane (6)
- Ghost Touch Arrows (2)
- Arrow +2 (7)
- Hushing Arrow, Greater (1)
