# Backlash

**Owner:** Towa | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Returning black-steel kunai

A black-steel kunai forged in Alkenstar. When thrown, it returns to the wielder's hand automatically, pulled back by an arcane tether embedded in the metal. The kunai's dark finish absorbs light, making it difficult to track in flight, and the return mechanism trails a faint streak of dark energy.
