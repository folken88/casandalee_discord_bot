# Beak of the Void Raven

**Type:** equipment (helm) | **Owner:** Mr Brow (Enrique) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A helm forged from voidsteel with blackgold circuitry, shaped like a monstrous raven's head. Salvaged from the wreckage of a crashed alien war automaton and reforged by a barbarian shaman who understood nothing of its technology but everything about its power. The Technic League cataloged it as "Artifact 77-D: Raven AI Host Interface" before it disappeared from their vaults.
