# Lammas Aeternum

**Type:** weapon (elven curved blade) | **Owner:** Femmik (Harrison) | **Campaign:** Hells Rebels

An elven curved blade with a storied past. Its former owner, Lord Aeturno of Pangolais, used it to kill demons until they returned the favor. The blade endures where its wielder did not.
