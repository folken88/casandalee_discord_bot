# Diadem of Devotion

**Type:** equipment (circlet) | **Owner:** Dinvaya (Josh) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

A silver circlet bearing two inscriptions:
- **Outer band:** "Where loss and love intertwine / True enlightenment we find."
- **Inner band (scratched):** "I will never get over you."

The diadem radiates no magic until placed on a good-aligned positive channeler, at which point it begins to glow and float.
