# Tally Buckle

**Owner:** Holden | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Belt of the Duelist

A belt buckle that keeps count. Upon defeating or successfully riposting a worthy opponent, the Tally Buckle casts Blur on the wearer, shrouding them in a haze of displaced images as if the victory itself fuels the next defense. The buckle bears tiny notch-marks that appear and fade with each activation.
