# Guards of the Silent People

**Type:** equipment (bracers) | **Owner:** Olbryn (Josh) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

Bracers of Armor +6 with a constant Silent Spell metamagic effect applied to the wearer's casting. If the wearer already possesses the Silent Spell feat, they instead gain 2 free silent-spell uses per day without increasing spell level. Magic without words -- ideal for a caster who prefers not to announce their intentions.
