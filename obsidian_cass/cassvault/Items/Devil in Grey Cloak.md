# Devil in Grey Cloak

**Owner:** Rodney "Danger" Smith (Chris) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Cloak (shoulders slot) | CL 12 | **Aura:** Abjuration
**Price:** 42,000 gp

## Description
A cloak made from the famous Devil In Grey — a legendary winter wolf. The image on the cloak shows a huge wolf with blue eyes.

## Properties
- **Cold Resistance 30**
- **+4 enhancement bonus to Constitution** (functions as Belt of CON +4)
- **+4 resistance bonus to all saving throws** (functions as Cloak of Resistance +4)
- **Leaves no tracks** (trackless step effect)

## Notes
This is one of the most valuable items in the CC party, combining three major item slots' worth of bonuses into a single shoulders-slot item. Chris's ranger Rodney benefits enormously from the CON boost and saves while maintaining his wilderness tracking theme.
