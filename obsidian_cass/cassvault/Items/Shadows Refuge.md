# Shadows Refuge

**Owner:** Towa | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Umberhulk plate armor

Plate armor crafted from umberhulk chitin, with a dark purple tinted glass visor and full-body coverage. The armor includes a deployable full hood that, when raised, projects an illusion of black smoke over the wearer's eyes, obscuring their gaze and lending an intimidating, faceless appearance. The chitin plates are surprisingly light for their durability and carry a faint iridescent sheen.
