# Maven's Precision

**Owner:** Rhyarca | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Darkwood heavy crossbow

A darkwood heavy crossbow carved with intricate depictions of harpies in flight along the stock and limbs. Once belonged to Maven, a renowned harpy sniper whose accuracy was legendary among the Shackles. The darkwood construction makes it lighter than a standard heavy crossbow, and the harpy carvings seem to shift subtly in dim light.
