# Arinyark Full Plate

**Type:** equipment (armor) | **Owner:** Akradenn (Tim) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

Full plate forged from a rare mithral/arinyark alloy that allows full spellcasting without arcane spell failure. The outer surface has a reflective gloss, while the interior features a kevlar-like lining that provides additional protection against directed spells and energy weapons. Technology and magic fused into something Numeria rarely produces: armor that actually works for everyone.
