# Justice

**Type:** weapon (revolver) | **Owner:** Maginnis (Hans) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A six-chambered revolver and one of Maginnis's paired sidearms, the other being [[Revenge]]. Justice is a +2 seeking weapon -- its barrel subtly tugs toward its target, negating miss chances from concealment. Where Revenge hides, Justice finds.
