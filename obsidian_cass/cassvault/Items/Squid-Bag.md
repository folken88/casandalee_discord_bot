# Squid-Bag (h'ntai)

**Owner:** Storgrim | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Sahuagin Bag of Holding (Type 1)

A Sahuagin-crafted Bag of Holding made from treated squid hide and lined with tiny, semi-animate tentacles. The bag protects all contents from water exposure and actively dries any soaked items placed within. However, the tentacles are "grabby" -- placing items inside is a free action as the tentacles eagerly snatch things inward, but drawing items out usually takes a full-round action as the tentacles reluctantly release their grip.
