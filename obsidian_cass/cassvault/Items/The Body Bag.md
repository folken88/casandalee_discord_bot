# The Body Bag

**Owner:** Rhyarca | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Extradimensional container ("Carriers by Calvin")

A luxury container from the "Carriers by Calvin" line -- a dimensional pocket capable of holding up to 100 cubic feet of material while reducing the weight of its contents by 75%. Outwardly stylish and well-crafted, it belies the grim practicality suggested by its nickname. The Body Bag has proven invaluable for transporting loot, supplies, and the occasional literal body.
