# Panorama Celestiale

**Type:** equipment (ring) | **Owner:** Ulfred (Kip) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A ring with a visible universe through a tear in its circumference -- looking into it reveals stars, nebulae, and the slow rotation of celestial bodies. Grants +5 Perception, constant True Seeing, constant Fly, at-will Feather Fall, and an innate awareness of the planetary axis. The cosmos on your finger.
