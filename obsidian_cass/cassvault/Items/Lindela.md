# Lindela

**Owner:** Kate Blackwood (Mandi) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Ring of Protection +2 with tactical power | Slot: Ring
**Aura:** Abjuration | CL 6 | **Price:** 11,000 gp

## Inscription
Inscribed in Osiriani: *"Never interrupt your enemy when they are making a mistake."*

## Properties
- **+2 deflection bonus to AC** (standard Ring of Protection)

## Tactical Power
When the wearer casts a spell against a target that **fails their Will save**, the wearer can instantly cast a **defensive buff spell** on themselves — provided the buff can be cast without verbal or somatic components.

This enables Kate to layer defensive buffs reactively during offensive spellstrike combos, making her Mindblade Kensai fighting style even more fluid.
