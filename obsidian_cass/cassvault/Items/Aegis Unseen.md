# Aegis Unseen

**Owner:** Kestrel 'Kez' Ironbloom (Storgrim's cohort) | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** +1 Heavy Steel Shield

A heavy steel shield inscribed with runes honoring nameless, forgotten soldiers. At its center is the emblem of a closed eye. The shield grants a +4 bonus against flanking attacks, as if the bearer can sense threats from unseen angles. The runes glow faintly when enemies attempt to circle behind the wielder.
