# Dragonclan Bone Mask

**Type:** equipment (mask) | **Owner:** Dismas (Harrison) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

- +2 to Intimidate checks
- Stormsight out to 30 feet
- No concentration checks required due to weather conditions
