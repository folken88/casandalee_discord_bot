# Broken Sword of Pharasma

**Owner:** Kovira (Sydney) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Ancient Pharasma short sword with reassembled shards | +3 enhancement
**Aura:** Necromancy (powerful) | CL 20 | Finesse weapon

## Description
This blade bears the old symbol of Pharasma that has not been used by her followers since the Shining Crusade. The blade was snapped off just above the guard despite the weapon's superior construction. It radiates powerful necromantic magic. Kovira has been reassembling it by finding and reattaching shards.
