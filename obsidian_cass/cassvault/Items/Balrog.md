# Balrog

**Owner:** Ser Toche | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** +1 Keen Elven Curved Blade

An elven curved blade with a razor edge and a hunger for the righteous. Bears the enchantment "Infernal Hunger": on a successful hit against a lawful good creature, the wielder gains temporary hit points equal to half the damage dealt (maximum 10 temporary HP). The blade's edge seems to drink in light, and faint Infernal script traces along the fuller.
