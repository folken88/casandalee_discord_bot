# Phase Spider Bracers

**Type:** equipment (bracers) | **Owner:** Kate Blackwood (Mandi) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

A silver bracer shaped like a phase spider. Functions as Bracers of Armor +2.

Automatically activates Feather Fall via ethereal webs when the wearer falls. The webs are only touchable by creatures with ethereal sensitivity.
