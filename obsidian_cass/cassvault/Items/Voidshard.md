# Voidshard

**Type:** weapon (dwarven 1h axe) | **Owner:** Ulfred (Kip) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A dwarven one-handed axe, exotic to non-dwarves. Functionally equivalent to a +4 magic weapon. The handle is crafted from glaucite and features a variant spell-storing property. Hardness 30 -- nearly indestructible. A weapon built to endure whatever Numeria throws at it.
