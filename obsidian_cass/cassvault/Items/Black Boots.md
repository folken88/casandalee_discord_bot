# Black Boots

**Type:** loot | **Owner:** Kai Gin (Graham) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

Boots that produce no sound, leave no marks, and carry no scent. They provide excellent grip on all surfaces.

Grants the Fast Stealth rogue talent. If the wearer already has Fast Stealth, the first 10 feet of movement becomes a "shift" that passes through solid objects.
