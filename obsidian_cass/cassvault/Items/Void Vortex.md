# Void Vortex

**Type:** weapon (composite bow) | **Owner:** Luna (Rye) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

An extraplanar bow created by a Voidlord for an elven archer friend. A +2 composite bow that summons endless ammunition from nothing. Each arrow strikes as piercing, force, electricity, or negative energy -- whichever deals the most damage to the target. The bow decides; the archer just aims.
