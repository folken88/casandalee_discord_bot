# Bangarangs

**Owner:** Holden | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Set of bronze rings (wondrous item)

A set of bronze rings inscribed with Infernal runes, worn in pairs on the wrists and ankles. The rings grant different bonuses depending on placement:

- **Wrists:** +2 to Parry & Riposte, grants Quick Draw.
- **Ankles:** +10 ft to base speed, grants Improved Initiative.
- **Either placement:** Once per day as a swift action, the wearer can activate Haste on themselves.

The Infernal runes glow faintly when their abilities are active, and the rings resize to fit any wearer.
