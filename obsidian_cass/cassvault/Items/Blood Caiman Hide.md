# Blood Caiman Hide

**Owner:** Shared (party item) | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Hide armor (buoyant, aquatic)

Hide armor crafted from the skin of a blood caiman, naturally buoyant and designed for aquatic use. The armor imposes no Swim check penalty (unlike standard hide armor) and grants a bonus to Swim checks. The scales retain a reddish tint and are remarkably water-resistant, shedding moisture almost instantly. Ideal for pirates and sailors who expect to end up in the water regularly.
