# Time and Patience are the strongest of all warriors

**Type:** weapon (greatsword) | **Owner:** The Nameless Wanderer | **Campaign:** Hells Vengeance

An immense five-foot greatsword. The name is the item's actual name -- not a description, not a title, but the words inscribed upon a blade too large for most mortals to lift. Carried by the Nameless Wanderer, who has both the time and the patience the name demands.
