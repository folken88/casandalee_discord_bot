# Championship Belt

**Type:** equipment (belt) | **Owner:** Freya (Betty) | **Campaign:** Hells Vengeance

A famous dwarven artifact -- a championship belt that grows in power as its wearer defeats progressively larger giant types. Grants +1 diplomacy with dwarves by default. Progressive bonuses unlock upon defeating specific giants. Current progress: "Hill Giant: +2 STR - Defeated Beef at Checkers."
