# Tyrant's Band

**Type:** Ring | **CL:** 5 | **Aura:** Abjuration | **Price:** 2,000 gp
**Owner:** [[Auren Vrood]]

A ring forged from black steel and set with six deep emerald gems. The centerpiece is a skull with its mouth gagged -- an emblem reflecting the Whispering Way's reverence for silence and secrets, and their goal of serving [[Tar Baphon, The Whispering Tyrant]]. Each gem represents an aspect of the Tyrant's power.

## Abilities

**Base:** Ring of Protection +1 (deflection bonus to AC). The aura occasionally manifests as emerald sparks dancing along the wearer's forearm when in danger.

### Veil of Silence (1/day)
Casts Silence (2nd level) with a twist: the wearer and up to two chosen allies within the area can communicate silently via telepathy. Others nearby hear only unintelligible whispers. Duration: 5 minutes. 20 ft radius.

### Necrotic Navigation (3 charges/day, replenish at dawn)
- Spend 1 charge: Detect Undead at will for 10 minutes, can sense through walls and barriers.
- Spend all remaining charges (1/week): Conjure a **spectral guide** -- a hooded figure visible only to the wearer, leading them through the safest or most direct path to their destination. The path may not be morally straightforward, but ensures survival.
