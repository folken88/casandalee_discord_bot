# Brogan Tharnak

**Owner:** Storgrim | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Enchanted glass weapon

A weapon forged from enchanted glass, deceptively resilient and razor-edged. Bears the enchantment known as "Brogan's Oath":

- **Brogan's Oath:** When the wielder makes a saving throw against fear from an enemy of at least their level and succeeds, the weapon casts True Strike on the wielder (lasts 2 rounds).
- **Brogan's Shame:** If the wielder fails a fear saving throw, the weapon rebels -- all attack rolls of 1 through 5 automatically miss for the next 24 hours.

The glass blade refracts light in strange patterns, and those who look closely swear they can see a face snarling within.
