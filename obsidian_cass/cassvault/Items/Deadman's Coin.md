# Deadman's Coin

**Owner:** Storgrim | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Wondrous item (supernatural)

A tarnished coin of uncertain origin that clings to its bearer with grim tenacity. Once per day, when the bearer is dropped below 0 HP, they may flip a coin. If they call the correct side, they are healed (1d4)d8 HP. This effect can prevent death, snatching the bearer back from the brink at the last possible moment. The coin grows warm to the touch when its power activates, and faintly cold for the rest of the day once spent.
