# Tidewater Side Piece

**Owner:** Storgrim | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Ring (signet)

An ornate signet ring depicting a crashing wave, masterfully crafted in silver and deep blue enamel. Issued only to the current favored consort of Pirate Queen Lady Smythee of Tidewater Rock. The bearer enjoys the Lady's favor and the political protections that come with it -- a signal to all who recognize the ring that its wearer stands under the Queen's personal regard.
