# Judge & Jury

**Type:** weapon (dual pistols) | **Owner:** William Gaspar (Tim) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

A pair of matched pistols wielded together.

- **Judge:** Grants +2 to attack when both pistols fire at the same target.
- **Jury:** Grants +4 to saving throws against the target's abilities.
- **Continuance:** On a kill or coup de grace, both pistols are fully repaired and reloaded instantly.
