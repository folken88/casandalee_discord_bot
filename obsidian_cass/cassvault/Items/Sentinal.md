# Sentinal

**Owner:** Sha-Feng | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Dwarven Sentinel Hammer (Lucerne Hammer)

A dwarven lucerne hammer forged in the halls of Durnheim. Originally wielded by Justicar Thurik Stoneguard, this weapon embodies dwarven justice and vigilance. The haft is reinforced iron-bound oak, and the hammer head bears the Stoneguard clan sigil. Sentinal (the dwarven spelling preserved on the weapon itself) is balanced for both crushing blows and precise hooking strikes.
