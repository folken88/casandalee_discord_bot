# Silence (Shadow's Silence)

**Owner:** Kai Gin (Graham) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** +1 keen kukri | CL 10
**Material:** Rare Shadow Plane metal

## Description
Forged in the dark underbelly of Golarion, the Shadow's Silence was crafted by a cabal of necromancers in service to the Whispering Way. The blade is made from a rare metal mined from the depths of the Shadow Plane. The cabal created a series of these daggers, each intended for their most skilled assassins. It is both a weapon and a symbol of the assassin's pledge to the Whispering Way.

## Properties
- +1 keen kukri: 1d4 slashing, 15-20/x2 (keen expanded crit range)
- Critical Focus: +4 to confirm

## Shadow Vanish (1/day)
When in dim light or darkness, wielder can use a **swift action** to become invisible (as per Invisibility spell) for **3 rounds**. Allows approach or escape after a strike.

## Silent Wound
When a successful **sneak attack** is made with this weapon, the sound of the strike and any resulting wound is **completely silent**, preventing nearby enemies from being alerted by noise.

## Combat Stats
- Attack: DEX-based, 1d4 slashing, 15-20/x2
- Conditional: Sneak Attack dice (Rogue/Arcane Trickster levels)
