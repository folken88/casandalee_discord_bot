# Anaconda Coil

**Type:** equipment (belt) | **Owner:** Draymus (Harrison) | **Campaign:** Hells Vengeance

A belt that sets STR to a minimum of 16, grants +4 to grapple checks, +10 to Escape Artist, and constrict damage of 2d6+STR modifier. Once per day, the wearer can use Beast Shape to become an anaconda with unlimited duration. The belt wraps around you; you wrap around everything else.
