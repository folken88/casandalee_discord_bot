# The Reach

**Owner:** Rodney "Danger" Smith (Chris) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** 4-form transforming mithral weapon | +2 enhancement, Keen
**Material:** Mithral alloy (Numerian artifice + Ustalavian smithing) | CL 6
**Weight:** 6 lbs (light for its size) | **Price:** 22,000 gp

## History
Forged a century ago for Numeria's greatest warrior, **Zalren the Undefeated**, a master of all weapons and combat styles. Zalren believed adaptability and precision were the keys to defeating any foe. Legend holds that the Black Sovereign himself trained under Zalren in his youth. Crafted by Numerian artificers who blended Silver Mount technology with Ustalavian smithing traditions.

## Swift Morphing (Swift Action)
The wielder can change form by pressing hidden buttons in the handle. No delay, no AoO.

## Four Forms
| Form | Category | Damage | Crit | Properties |
|------|----------|--------|------|------------|
| **Shortsword** | Light, 1H | 1d6 S | 17-20/x2 | Compact, agile |
| **Longsword** | 1H | 1d8 S | 17-20/x2 | Balanced, versatile |
| **Greatsword** | 2H | 2d6 S | 17-20/x2 | Overwhelming strikes |
| **Partisan** | 2H, Reach | 1d10 S | 17-20/x2 | 10-ft reach polearm |

All forms: +2 enhancement, Keen (17-20 crit range), strikes as silver and magic for DR.

## Scabbard
Accompanied by a bespoke scabbard keeping the weapon flat against the spine. Can be drawn one-handed from either side. +5 to hide with a loose cloak or coat.
