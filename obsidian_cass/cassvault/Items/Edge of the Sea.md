# Edge of the Sea

**Type:** weapon (katana) | **Owner:** Boudicca | **Campaign:** Hells Rebels

A +2 Icy Burst Katana that grants at-will waterbreathing and a 30-foot swim speed. Celestial runes along the blade form a poem about Kintargo Bay. A weapon that carries the sea with it wherever it goes.
