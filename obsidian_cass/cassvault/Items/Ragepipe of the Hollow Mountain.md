# Ragepipe of the Hollow Mountain

**Type:** equipment (war horn) | **Owner:** Ulfred (Kip) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A massive war horn carved from the horn of a fiendish dire ram, etched with primal symbols of rage and conquest. When sounded, it invigorates allies and terrifies foes. The kind of instrument that makes an entire battlefield stop and listen.
