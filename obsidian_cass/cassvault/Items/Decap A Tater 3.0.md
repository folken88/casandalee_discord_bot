# Decap A Tater 3.0

**Owner:** Kate Blackwood (Mandi) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** +1 keen wakizashi/shortsword | CL 6
**Material:** Glaucite alloy (alien origin, over 7,000 years old, not from Golarion)
**Aura:** Evocation

## Description
This blade is forged of a Glaucite alloy beyond modification by most Inner Sea smithies. Scrying spells indicate it is over 7,000 years old and does not originate on Golarion. The blade has an unknown power source and radiates no magic or external energy until swung or thrust in skillful use. It exhibits a soft blue-white glow while in use, not bright enough to be seen at distance.

## Properties
- +1 keen wakizashi: 1d6 slashing/piercing, 18-20/x2
- Bypasses DR as **adamantine** due to laser-sharp blade that never needs sharpening
- Finesse weapon (DEX-based attacks)
- Critical Focus: +4 + INT mod to confirm crits

## Elemental Conditionals (can switch between)
- **Flaming:** +1d6 fire, +1d10 fire on crit
- **Frost:** +1d6 cold, +1d10 cold on crit
- **Shock:** +1d6 electric, +1d10 electric on crit (default active)

## Spellstrike Modes (Kate is a Mindblade Kensai Magus)
- **Spellstrike:** Normal attack + channeled spell (Mind Thrust, Frigid Touch, Vampiric Touch, Shocking Grasp)
- **Perfect Spellstrike:** 18-20/x3 crit multiplier, same spell options
- Intensified Shocking Grasp: (magus level)d6 electric damage
