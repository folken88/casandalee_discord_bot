# Oath-Cinder

**Owner:** Storgrim | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Dwarven fact book (self-destructing)

A dwarven fact book -- a compact ledger used to record oaths, debts, and sworn testimony. Equipped with a pull-tab mechanism that, when pulled, ignites the book's interior to destroy all contents beyond recovery. In its current state, the pull-tab was partially pulled at some point but failed to fully ignite due to moisture damage, leaving the contents partially intact but the self-destruct mechanism compromised.
