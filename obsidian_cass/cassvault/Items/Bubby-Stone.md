# Bubby-Stone

**Owner:** Storgrim | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Wondrous item (elemental)

A palm-sized azure gemstone gifted by (or stolen from) the elemental lords of air. When held in the mouth, it emits a steady stream of breathable air bubbles, granting up to 10 minutes of underwater breathing. The stone recharges when exposed to direct sunlight. Its surface is cool to the touch and faintly effervescent, even when dry.
