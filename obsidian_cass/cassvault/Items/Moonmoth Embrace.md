# Moonmoth Embrace

**Owner:** Rhyarca | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** +1 Mithral-lined Studded Leather Armor

Black leather armor with bronze filigree depicting a moth symbol sacred to Desna. The interior is lined with mithral mesh, granting it the lightness and flexibility of mithral construction while maintaining the appearance of mundane studded leather. The armor carries Desna's favor -- the moth emblem glows faintly under moonlight and starlight.
