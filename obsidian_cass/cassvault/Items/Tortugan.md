# Tortugan, the Walking War-Cannon

**Owner:** Vaughan | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Level 8 Construct (40,000 gp market price)

A giant tortoise construct of reinforced steel and brass, with a cannon built into its head. Tortugan is a walking siege engine -- slow, heavily armored, and capable of devastating ranged bombardment. Its shell provides cover for nearby allies, and its cannon head can be loaded and fired like a standard siege weapon. The construct follows simple commands and can traverse most terrain, though it is not known for its speed. A marvel of engineering and a terrifying addition to any pirate fleet's arsenal.
