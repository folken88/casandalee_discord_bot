# Maelstrom

**Owner:** Bujon | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Wondrous item (amulet)

A silver amulet shaped like a lightning bolt, crackling with faint static energy. Enhances the wearer's lightning and thunder magic, and improves their reaction to danger -- sharpening reflexes as if charged by the storm itself. The amulet hums during thunderstorms and sparks faintly when the wearer is in peril.
