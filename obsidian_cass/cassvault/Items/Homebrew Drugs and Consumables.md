# Homebrew Drugs & Consumables

**Type:** consumable (drugs) | **Owner:** shared | **Campaign:** All campaigns

A collection of homebrew drugs and consumables appearing across campaigns. Each provides a mechanical benefit alongside a memorable side effect.
