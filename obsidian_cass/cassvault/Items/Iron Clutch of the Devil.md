# Iron Clutch of the Devil

**Owner:** Sha-Feng | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Prosthetic arm (+1 magic weapon for unarmed strikes)

A Chelish prosthetic arm forged from black iron and infused with infernal energy. Bonding the arm to a new wielder is an agonizing 10-minute process that leaves permanent scorch marks where flesh meets metal. Once attached, the arm functions as part of the wearer's body and counts as a +1 magic weapon for unarmed strikes. The fingers articulate with unnatural precision, and the joints occasionally glow with faint hellfire.
