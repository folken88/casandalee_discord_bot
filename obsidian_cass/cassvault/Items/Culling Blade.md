# Culling Blade

**Owner:** Towa | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Chelish Wakizashi

A Chelish wakizashi with a mirror-finish blade and a crimson hilt wrapped in dark silk. The blade is said to "cut through silence itself" -- its edge is so fine that strikes produce no sound until the wound opens. The mirror finish occasionally reflects things that aren't there, and the crimson hilt darkens with use.
