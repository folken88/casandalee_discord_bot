# Maura's Wayfinder

**Type:** equipment (wayfinder) | **Owner:** Olbryn (Josh) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A gold-alloy wayfinder container. Inside, a sketch of a handsome man holding pistols with the inscription: "To my darling Maura, may you always find your way to me." Functions as a 3rd-level Pearl of Power. The compass is semi-functional -- it points somewhere, but not north. Whether it points toward anything meaningful is an open question.
