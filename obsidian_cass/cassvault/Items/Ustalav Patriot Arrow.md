# Ustalav Patriot Arrow

**Type:** loot (ammunition) | **Owner:** Rodney (Chris) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

A patriotic arrow of Ustalavian make. If the wielder is Ustalavian and the target is a traitor, the arrow ignores concealment up to 80% and can make up to two 90-degree turns in flight to reach its target.

Produces a flash of light on strike.
