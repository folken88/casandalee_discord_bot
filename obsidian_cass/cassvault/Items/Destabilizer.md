# Destabilizer

**Type:** weapon (mithral heavy mace) | **Owner:** Dinvaya (Josh) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

A mithral heavy mace with upheld hands of gold-plated steel. A persistent prestidigitation effect keeps it perpetually untarnished.

Previously wielded by the legendary Hamdad the Defiant. Fighting near Hamdad was said to be as dangerous as being his enemy.
