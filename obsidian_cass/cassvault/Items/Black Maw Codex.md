# Black Maw Codex

**Type:** loot (grimoire) | **Owner:** Kate Blackwood (Mandi) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

An ancient grimoire bound in unnatural hide. The grotesque tentacled cover shifts and writhes when unobserved, and its eyes glow in the presence of those who seek forbidden knowledge.

Can summon an Ancient Horror once per day.
