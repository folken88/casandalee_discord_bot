# Radiance

**Owner:** Vaughan | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Aldori Dueling Sword (Finesse)

An Aldori dueling sword bearing an inscription about the Sun's embrace. The blade gleams with an inner light, and its balance is so perfect that it can be used with Weapon Finesse despite its size. Radiance represents the Aldori tradition of elegant, precise swordplay -- a weapon for duelists who value speed and technique over brute force.
