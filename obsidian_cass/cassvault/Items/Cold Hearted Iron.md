# Cold Hearted Iron

**Type:** weapon (cold-iron blade) | **Owner:** Luna (Rye) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A cold-iron blade carried by one of the most hated warriors in Numerian history. A +4 Keen Furious weapon with a toggleable frost enchantment. The blade radiates evil and bears the skull symbol of its original owner. A weapon of terrible provenance that has found new purpose -- though its nature hasn't changed.
