# Rovadra

**Owner:** Dismas Aevrett (Harrison) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Lever-action precision rifle | +2 enhancement | Exotic weapon
**Material:** Glaucite alloy (Numerian) with gold-and-mithril dragon wrapping
**Aura:** Evocation (Good) | CL 14 | **Price:** 24,000 gp

## Description
This lever-action precision rifle is of unknown origins. Many modifications have been made with most parts replaced by glaucite. The most recent modification is a gold-and-mithril-alloy wrapping in the shape of a dragon with its mouth as the barrel. The weapon radiates good and very potent magic.

## Inscription
*Beloved Rovadra, / Immortal golden scales in flight, / Rolling golden curls by night. / She soared across the field of war, / And nightly came unto my door. / Felled at last by Tyrant's word, / Just one last time her love I heard. / She gave her soul unto my steel, / So her wrath our foes could feel.*

(Rovadra was a gold dragon whose soul was bound into the rifle after she was killed by Tar Baphon, the Whispering Tyrant.)

## Fear Aura
When fired, a muffled roar is heard and a wave of terror passes over the wielder. **DC 28 Will Save vs fear** or wielder drops the weapon and is frightened for 1 round.

## Attack Modes

### Bite of Rovadra (Ranged Attack)
- Range: 80 ft (5 increments) | Ammo: .308 cartridges | Capacity: 5
- Damage: 1d12 B/P, 19-20/x3
- Conditionals: Smiting Shot (Paladin level + CHA mod positive), Flaming (1d6 fire), Bane (Holy 2d6 / Axiomatic 2d6 / Deadslayer 2d6), Scope (+2 hit / -2 AC)

### Breath of Rovadra (Cone Attack)
- Action: Standard | Uses: CHA mod/day
- Effect: 30-foot cone of gold and orange fire
- Damage: (level/2)d10 fire/positive
- Wielder must make DC 28 Fear Save

### Tail of Rovadra (Melee)
- Range: Reach | Damage: 1d6 B + 1d6 fire/positive
- Used as a quarterstaff due to sturdy construction

## Scope
+2 to hit, -2 AC for the round. Requires concentration and steady hand.

## Construction
Nearly indestructible due to magical reinforcement and unknown energy. Cannot be fitted with a bayonet.
