# Polytool of Brigh

**Owner:** Dinvaya (Josh) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Transforming multi-tool / weapon | +1 enhancement | Light simple weapon
**Aura:** Abjuration | CL 7 | **Price:** 2,000 gp

## Description
A holy object of Brigh, goddess of invention. This item can function as a wide variety of tools and several weapon types. It transforms between configurations.

## Transformation
- **Swift action** with Knowledge (Engineering) DC 10
- **Full-round action** with Intelligence check DC 8 (if check fails)

## Tool Functions
- Thieves' tools
- Basic tools for any operation
- Blacksmithing tools (basic set)
- Functions as masterwork tools in all modes

## Skill Bonuses (while equipped)
- +5 circumstance to Disable Device
- +5 circumstance to Knowledge (Engineering)
- +5 circumstance to Craft
- +5 circumstance to Profession

## Weapon Mode
- 1d6 S/P/B damage, 20/x2
- Nonlethal capable
- Light weapon
