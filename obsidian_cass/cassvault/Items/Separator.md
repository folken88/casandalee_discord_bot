# Separator

**Type:** Greatsword (Large, +2) | **CL:** 6 | **Aura:** Evocation | **Price:** 8,350 gp
**Owner:** [[Sirona|Sirona, Deva of the First Circle]]

Larger and heavier than a conventional greatsword. Strikes as cold-iron, silver, and magic.

## Properties
- **Damage:** 2d6 (Large size) slashing | **Crit:** 17-20/x2
- Counts as cold-iron, silver, and magic for DR purposes
- Grants the **Cleave** feat to a competent wielder
- With Vital Strike feats, enables **Cleaving Smash** (Weapon Trick: 2h)

### Cleaving Smash
When using Cleave, add the additional damage from Vital Strike to both initial and secondary attacks. With Greater Vital Strike, add Improved Vital Strike damage to both instead.

### Enchantments (per conditionals)
- **Holy:** +2d6 positive energy damage vs evil
- **Shocking Burst:** +1d6 electric, +1d10 electric on crit
