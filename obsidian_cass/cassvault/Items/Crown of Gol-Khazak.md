# Crown of Gol-Khazak

**Owner:** Rhyarca | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Wondrous item (crown)

The former regalia of King Dorn Helbraaz of Gol-Khazak, a ruined subterranean kingdom. The crown enables its wearer to use Command Undead once per day at the wearer's character level. The crown is forged of tarnished silver and black iron, set with a single pale gem that pulses with necromantic energy when the Command Undead ability is invoked.
