# That Will Buff Out

**Type:** equipment (book) | **Owner:** Dinvaya (Josh) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

An engineering reference book that grants +5 to Knowledge (Engineering) checks.

Once per day, it deploys a full blacksmithing setup complete with a retractable table and forge.

The author's note inside reads simply: "God Dammit."
