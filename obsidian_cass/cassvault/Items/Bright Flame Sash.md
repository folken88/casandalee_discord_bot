# Bright Flame Sash

**Owner:** Vaughan | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Wondrous item (sash)

A crimson sash that trails glowing motes of light when the wearer moves. Grants a +5 ft bonus to base speed. Once per day during Spell Combat, the wearer may cast Bladed Dash or Greater Bladed Dash as part of their spell combat routine. The motes flare brightly during the dash, leaving a streak of flame-like afterimages.
