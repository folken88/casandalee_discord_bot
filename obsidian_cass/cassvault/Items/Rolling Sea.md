# Rolling Sea

**Owner:** Sha-Feng | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** +1 Steel Knuckles (Sonic)

Steel knuckles engraved with storm clouds and lightning bolts. A +1 magic weapon that deals an additional 1d6 sonic damage on each hit. The wielder can also deliver an area-of-effect sonic attack by punching the ground with both fists simultaneously, sending a shockwave of thunder outward. The knuckles crackle with static electricity and hum with barely contained energy.
