# Nocturne (Nocturne's Fang)

**Type:** +4 Mithral Aldori Dueling Sword | **CL:** 19 | **Aura:** Evocation | **Price:** 32,020 gp
**Owner:** [[Luvick Siervage]]

Forged in the shadow of [[Caliphas]] centuries before the rise of the [[The Whispering Tyrant|Whispering Tyrant]]. Originally the ceremonial blade of the elven-blooded wardens of Ustalav's old noble courts. When Luvick fell to vampirism but retained his will and dignity, he reforged the blade in secret, embedding it with the will to defy domination and resist the undead tyrant.

## Properties
- **Damage:** 2d6 slashing | **Crit:** 15-20/x3
- +4 enhancement bonus
- Mithral construction (reduced weight, superior speed)
- Finessable (uses DEX for attack and damage)
- **+4 insight bonus to initiative**
- **+2 competence bonus to AC** (Aldori Dueling Mastery style)
- Can sneak attack (3d6 precision conditional)
