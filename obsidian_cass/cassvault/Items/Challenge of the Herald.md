# Challenge of the Herald

**Type:** equipment (artifact) | **Owner:** Elfrip (Anna) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

A Shining Crusade artifact worn by many champions over the ages. When a wearer was killed in battle, their allies would pick it up and continue the fight.

The last mortal to wear the Challenge of the Herald was Iomedae herself, before she took the Test of the Starstone and ascended to godhood.
