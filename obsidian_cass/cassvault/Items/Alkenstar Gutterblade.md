# Alkenstar Gutterblade

**Owner:** Towa | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** +1 Wakizashi (folding)

A +1 wakizashi engineered in Alkenstar that folds compactly into its hilt. The blade can be deployed as a swift action -- a series of metallic clicks and a flash of arcane energy mark the transition from concealed hilt to fully extended weapon. When folded, it resembles an ornate metal baton, making it easy to conceal and carry without arousing suspicion.
