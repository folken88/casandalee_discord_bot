# Gunslinger's Coat

**Type:** equipment (clothing) | **Owner:** Maginnis (Hans) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A long black coat bearing the inscription: "For my darling Ayrton, against the road, the law, and the elements." The coat benefits from persistent prestidigitation, keeping itself perpetually clean regardless of the filth its wearer walks through. A hidden back-holster grants a +5 bonus to Sleight of Hand checks to conceal a weapon.
