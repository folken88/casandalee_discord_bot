# Book of Terrors

**Type:** equipment (book) | **Owner:** Jason (Charles) | **Campaign:** Hells Vengeance

A book only readable by its claimed owner, containing 9 chapters. Write the true name of any creature of Hell, Heaven, or Abaddon within its pages to gain a once-per-month Planar Binding with no conditions attached. The creature simply appears, bound. Nine chapters, nine names, and an awful lot of leverage over the planes.
