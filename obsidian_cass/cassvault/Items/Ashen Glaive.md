# Ashen Glaive

**Type:** weapon | **Owner:** William Gaspar (Tim) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

Forged during the Shining Crusade, the Ashen Glaive ignores the incorporeal nature of its targets and devastates undead. The weapon was forged from the smoldering ruin of a greater lich destroyed by the holy fire of Arazni herself.

The glaive can transform into a tattoo of smoke and fire on the wielder's body, allowing it to be stored and summoned at will.
