# Null Blade

**Type:** weapon (one-handed blade) | **Owner:** Nomkath (Daemon) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A +2 keen one-handed blade that resolves attacks against touch AC rather than normal AC. Further enhanced by [[Ozmyn Zaidow]]'s magus abilities. Armor means nothing to this weapon -- it finds the gaps that aren't there.
