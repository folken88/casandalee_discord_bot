# Curator

**Owner:** William Gaspar (Tim) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Blue and gold mithral alloy hand-and-a-half sword | +3 enhancement
**Wielding:** 2H with martial proficiency (1.5x STR), or 1H with exotic proficiency (katana/bastard sword)
**Strikes as:** Magic, Gold, Silver for DR purposes | CL 17

## Engraving — War Prayers
The blade is engraved with war-prayers that shift and change constantly, drawing from deities and heroes of good or lawful alignment. Functions as a Book of War Prayers but requires only 1 minute (instead of 10) to receive the SuperBless buff.

## Enhancements (blade held upright before face)
- **Bless**, **Prayer**, and **Magic Circle against Evil** cast time reduced to **swift action**
- Their effective radii are **doubled**

## Offensive
- **Bane** effects empowered: +3 to hit, 3d6 damage (instead of standard +2/2d6)
- **Smite Evil** confirmed critical triggers a free **Lay on Hands** on the wielder
- **Channel Positive Energy** grants healed allies +1 AC per dice healed, lasting until wielder's next turn

## Defensive
- Wielder treated as having **Crane Style** feat
- "Fight Defensively" penalty reduced from -4 to -2
- Additional +1 dodge bonus to AC when using Fight Defensively or Total Defense

## Bracing
- If wielder braces against a charge, readied swing inflicts **double weapon dice and double strength damage**

## Combat Modes
- 1H Attack: 1d10 slashing, 19-20/x2
- 2H Attack: 1d10 slashing, 19-20/x2, 1.5x STR
- Brace vs Charge: 2d10 slashing, 2x STR
- 1H Dual Wield: 1d10 slashing, -2 attack penalty
- Conditionals: Bane (+3/3d6+3), Vs Undead (+1 sacred/+1 positive), Flames of the Faithful (1d6 fire + 1d10 fire on non-crit)
