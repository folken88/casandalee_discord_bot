# DVL-10 Saboteur

**Type:** weapon (silenced rifle) | **Owner:** Nomkath (Daemon) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A silenced rifle designed for precision assassination. When deployed with its bipod and the wielder maintains concentration, it enables sneak attacks at up to 3 range increments. Equipped with a Vudu Scope that halves range penalties. The weapon of choice for someone who wants their target dead before anyone hears the shot.
