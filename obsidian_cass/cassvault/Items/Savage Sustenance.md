# Savage Sustenance

**Owner:** Sha-Feng | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Cookbook (wondrous item)

A cookbook written for carnivorous barbarians, bound in thick hide with crude but enthusiastic illustrations. Grants a +5 bonus to cooking checks when used as a reference. The foreword reads: *"Conquer your hunger with a symphony of carnivorous decadence."* Recipes emphasize meat in all forms -- raw, seared, smoked, and occasionally still moving. Despite its savage presentation, the techniques within are surprisingly sophisticated.
