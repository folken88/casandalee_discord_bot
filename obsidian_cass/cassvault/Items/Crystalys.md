# Crystalys

**Type:** weapon (morphic blade) | **Owner:** Mr Brow (Enrique) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A crystalline blade that can take any known sword form -- longsword, scimitar, rapier, greatsword -- requiring a full-round action and a DC 25 Spellcraft check to reshape. Additionally increases the DC of cold spells cast by its wielder by 2. The blade shifts and refracts light differently depending on its current form.
