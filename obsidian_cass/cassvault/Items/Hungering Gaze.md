# Hungering Gaze

**Type:** weapon | **Owner:** Kai Gin (Graham) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

Forged in the Eye-Forges of Uzul'Zeth, where thoughts become metal and madness is currency. A living eye set between the blades never blinks, twitching toward future prey.

Previously wielded by the eldritch minotaur Vaugron the Labyrinth-Tearer.
