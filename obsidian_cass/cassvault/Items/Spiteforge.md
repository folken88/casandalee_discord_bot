# Spiteforge

**Type:** equipment (ring) | **Owner:** Akradenn (Tim) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A ring containing the imprisoned soul of a dethroned earthen warlock king, trapped within for a hundred generations. The soul still rages. Whatever power the ring grants comes at the cost of wearing something that hates you for existing.
