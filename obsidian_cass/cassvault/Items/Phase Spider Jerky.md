# Phase Spider Jerky

**Owner:** Shared (party item) | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Consumable (magical food)

Dried and seasoned strips of phase spider meat. Within 24 hours of eating a piece, the consumer can use Dimension Door to teleport up to 60 feet. The jerky has a bitter, ozone-like aftertaste and leaves a faint tingling sensation in the teeth. Supply is limited and cannot be easily replenished.
