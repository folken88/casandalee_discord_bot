# Wandering Dreamer Boots

**Owner:** Storgrim | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Wondrous item (boots)

Purplish-brown leather boots tooled with Desna's flight constellations in silver thread. When the wearer moves at least 10 feet in combat while attacking or being attacked, the boots release a pulse of radiant energy. The constellation patterns glow faintly during movement, leaving brief afterimages of starlight in the wearer's wake.
