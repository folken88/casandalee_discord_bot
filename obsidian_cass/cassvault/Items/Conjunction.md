# Conjunction

**Type:** equipment (ring/band) | **Owner:** Bruce (Chris) | **Campaign:** Hells Vengeance

A band set with 7 heavenly bodies. After casting a Line-effect spell, the wearer can use a free action to Dimension Door to anywhere along the line. Additionally, targets who failed their save against the line spell can be relocated along it. Turns a line of fire into a line of transit.
