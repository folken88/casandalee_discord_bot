# All Seeing Eye Ring

**Type:** equipment (ring) | **Owner:** Celeb (Tim) | **Campaign:** Hells Rebels

Appears to be a Ring of Spell Storing, but functions as a counterspell engine. The wearer stores a spell of 1st through 6th level into the ring. If that exact spell is cast targeting the wearer, the ring automatically counters it -- no action required. The best defense is making your enemy's offense disappear.
