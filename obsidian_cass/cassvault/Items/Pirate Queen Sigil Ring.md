# Pirate Queen Sigil Ring

**Type:** equipment (ring) | **Owner:** Dismas (Harrison) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

A ring made from fused firearm bullets. Functions as a Ring of Protection +1.

Twice per day, the wearer can store regained grit points (up to 2 stored). Stored grit can be spent for various effects beyond normal grit usage.
