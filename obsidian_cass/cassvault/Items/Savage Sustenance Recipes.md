# Savage Sustenance Recipes

**Type:** loot (recipe collection) | **Owner:** shared | **Campaign:** All campaigns

The full recipe collection from the [[Savage Sustenance]] cookbook brand, appearing across multiple campaigns. Each recipe provides temporary buffs, questionable nutrition, and unforgettable flavor.

## Recipes

| Recipe | Description |
|---|---|
| **Blood Caiman Tacos** | Reptilian street food. Crunchy, bloody, surprisingly good with lime. |
| **Tater Tot Hot Dish** | Midwestern comfort food adapted for adventurers. Warm, filling, zero sophistication. |
| **Wyvern Cassoulet** | Slow-cooked wyvern meat in a rich bean stew. The poison gland adds a kick if you leave it in. |
| **Barbarian Banana Bread** | Dense, sweet, baked in a shield over a campfire. Fuels rage and breakfast equally. |
| **Valkyrie Berry Crumble** | Tart wild berries under a buttery oat crust. Allegedly blessed by warrior spirits. |
| **Chicken Noodle Hot Tub** | An entire cauldron of chicken noodle soup. You eat it. You sit in it. Both are acceptable. |
| **Troll Blood Stew** | Regenerating stew -- the leftovers grow back overnight. Tastes like iron and regret. |
| **Miso Beef Ramen Delight** | Rich bone broth with thick noodles and seared beef. The one recipe everyone agrees is actually good. |
| **Swamp Egg Goulash** | Eggs of uncertain provenance in a thick paprika sauce. Don't ask what laid them. |
| **Double Death Bomb Chorizo Poutine Fries** | Spiced chorizo over fries and cheese curds, drowned in gravy. Named for the constitution save required after eating it. |
