# Crookit

**Owner:** Storgrim | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** +1 Returning Throwing Axe

A stout dwarven throwing axe etched with storm-call sigils and angular dwarven runes along the haft. Crookit can only be thrown once per round, but it returns automatically to the wielder's hand after each throw, arcing back through the air with unnatural precision. The axe hums faintly when gripped, as if eager to be loosed.
