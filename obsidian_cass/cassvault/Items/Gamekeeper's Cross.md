# Gamekeeper's Cross

**Type:** equipment (amulet) | **Owner:** Rodney (Chris) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown

A wooden amulet bearing a Varisian inscription about a were-bear uncle whose grave grew a tree. Functions as an Amulet of Natural Armor +1.

When combined with Barkskin, also casts Enlarge Person on the wearer.
