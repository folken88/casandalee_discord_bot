# Deathsight

**Owner:** Kovira (Sydney) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Technological goggles / extra-sensory augmentation device | Slot: Eyes | CL 15
**Aura:** Divination | **Price:** 13,000 gp

## Description
A technological marvel of extra-sensory augmentation that hones in on weak points of any visible target. Can focus on structural integrity, operational components, or other vital areas.

## Aimed Spells (Touch/Ranged Touch)
- Dice size **reduced by one** (e.g., d6 becomes d4)
- Critical threat range and multiplier **expanded by 1**
- Example: Scorching Ray 4d6, 20/x2 becomes 4d4, 19-20/x3

## Area Spells (AoE)
- Radius/length **halved** (20' radius becomes 10', etc.)
- Dice size **increased by one** (d6 becomes d8)
- Spellstat modifier added to final damage
- Example: Fireball 20' radius d6/level becomes 10' radius d8/level + INT mod

## Passive Bonuses
- +5 circumstance bonus to sight-based Perception
- Detects and highlights **Invisible**, **Blurred**, **Displaced**, and **Ethereal** targets
- Ethereal targets may still be immune to material-plane effects

## Contingency: Re-Peek
**Trigger:** Confirmed critical hit with a spell
**Action:** Immediate action (usable only in that instant)
**Effect:** 1-round Vanish effect + Dimension Door 1d6 x 5 feet (variable by caster level x 5') to an open square in field of vision toward or beyond the crit target

## Kovira's Custom Spells Using Deathsight
- Deathsight Fireball (modified AoE)
- Deathsight Scorching Ray (modified aimed)
- Deathsight Shocking Grasp (modified touch)
- Deathsight Lightning Bolt (modified AoE)
- Deathsight Magic Missile w/ Broken Blade Shards (combined effects)
