# Tonbokiri

**Type:** weapon (fauchard) | **Owner:** Azwraith | **Campaign:** Hells Rebels

A +1 Keen Fauchard that inflicts nightmares on those it cuts and grants +5 to Knowledge (History) checks related to the Chelaxian Civil War. In combat, the wielder hears whispered words -- "Kill" and "Blood" -- urging violence. The weapon progresses based on total kills, growing more powerful as its body count rises. A polearm with opinions.
