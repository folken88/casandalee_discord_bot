# God Complex

**Owner:** Dinvaya (Josh) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Book of forensic pathology and healing | Slotless wondrous item
**Price:** 12,500 gp

## Description
A book on various medical and forensic principles with supporting data and tables. Has a number of potent healing scrolls in its later pages, though many pages have been torn out over the years.

## Passive Bonus
- +5 circumstance bonus on Heal checks when used to "take 10" or consulted for at least 10 minutes
- +5 circumstance bonus on cause-of-death investigations (forensic pathology)

## Scrolls Contained
1. **Raise Dead** (1 use) — Restores life, subject gains 2 permanent negative levels
2. **Resurrection** (1 use) — Full restoration, 1 permanent negative level, heals 42,069 HP (joke value by GM)
3. **Greater Restoration** (1 use) — Dispels all negative levels, ability damage, mental effects, fatigue
