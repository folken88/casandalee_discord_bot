# Ugokasenai (不動 — "Immovable")

**Owner:** William Gaspar (Tim) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** +2 Deathless O-Yoroi (heavy armor) | CL 7
**AC:** +10 (8 base + 2 enhancement) | Max DEX +2 | ACP -6 | Spell Failure 35%
**Aura:** Abjuration | **Price:** 29,800 gp | Weight: 45 lbs

## Inscription
Inside the faceplate: **自分の役割を知る** (Jibun no yakuwari o shiru) — "Know your role."

Part of a mantra recited by the Tian-Xia Crusaders who joined the war against Tar Baphon a thousand years ago. They were fearless, durable, and wore armor so heavy they were immovable — but also vulnerable. The mantra reminds the wearer to do their job while remembering their limitations within the greater army.

## Deathless Property
- Absorbs the **first 10 points of negative energy damage** every round
- **25% chance** to negate any negative levels incurred (roll 1d4, negated on a 3)

## Adamantine Plating
- **DR 3/adamantine** — reduces all incoming physical damage by 3 unless the weapon is adamantine quality or greater

## Deflect Arrows
- Heavy plating with a slick process against projectiles grants the **Deflect Arrows** feat effect
- Same plating causes **-4 penalty to ranged weapons** (does not apply to crossbows or firearms)

## Historical Significance
Worn by Tian-Xia Crusaders during the Shining Crusade against the Whispering Tyrant. These warriors were the immovable anchor of the crusading army — durable frontline soldiers who held the line while others maneuvered.
