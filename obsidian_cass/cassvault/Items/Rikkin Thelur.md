# Rikkin Thelur

**Type:** weapon (war hammer) | **Owner:** Ulfred (Kip) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

"Chief Advisor" in Dwarven. A +2 War Hammer of Impact (2d6, 20/x3). The hammer head features twin grimacing dwarf faces carved in exacting detail. When swung with force, the weapon creates an unnerving low whistle that precedes each blow -- the Chief Advisor announcing its counsel.
