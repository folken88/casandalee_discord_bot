# Life Giving Amulet

**Type:** equipment (amulet) | **Owner:** Mr Brow (Enrique) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

Once per day, automatically casts Greater Infernal Healing when the wearer drops to 0 HP. If the wearer is instantly killed, the amulet shatters and casts Breath of Life as a final act. A last resort that only works once -- after that, you're on your own.
