# Thundering Silencer

**Owner:** Dismas Aevrett (Harrison) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Bolt-action rifle (Mosin-Nagant M1891 base) | +1 enhancement | Modern Firearm
**Material:** Numerian artifact | CL 6 | **Price:** 8,800 gp

## Description
An ancient artifact of Numeria. A bolt-action precision rifle with unholy enchantment.

## Combat Stats
- Range: 110 ft (10 increments) | 2-handed
- Damage: 1d10 B/P, 19-20/x4 + 2d6 Unholy (on non-crit)
- Critical Focus: +4 to confirm

## Attack Modes
- **Standard Attack:** Full iterative attacks, 1d10 + 2d6 unholy
- **Bullseye Shot:** +4 attack bonus, same damage

## Vital Shot
- +2d10 B/P/negative damage on non-crit hits

## Note
Dismas wields this alongside Rovadra, switching between the unholy Thundering Silencer and the holy Rovadra depending on the target.
