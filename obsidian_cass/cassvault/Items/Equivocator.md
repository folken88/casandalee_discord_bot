# Equivocator

**Owner:** Kai Gin (Graham) | **Campaign:** Carrion Crown
**Type:** Ultra-heavy glaucite revolver | +2 enhancement
**Material:** Glaucite (Numerian metal) | **Aura:** Transmutation | CL 17
**Price:** 14,300 gp | Capacity: 6 rounds (.357 Northgarde Magnum Special)

## Description
Forged from a mysterious Numerian Metal, this ultra-heavy revolver takes a strong arm to even hold, and a strong body to discharge without falling over. Its mechanism is purely focused on delivering a steady, potent shot, and gives zero thought to comfort or recoil management.

## Combat Stats
- Range: 50 ft (5 increments) | Ammo: .357 cartridges
- Damage: 1d12 B/P + 1d6 positive, 19-20/x4
- Critical Focus: +4 to confirm
- Reload: Move action (refills 6 rounds)

## Conditionals
- **Bane:** +2 hit, 2d6+2 untyped damage
- **Vs Undead:** +1 sacred hit, +1 positive damage
- **Flames of the Faithful:** +1d6 fire + 1d10 fire on non-crit

## Vital Shot Mode
- Smite: +CHA mod (profane) to attack, +Paladin level to damage
- Vital Shot: +2d12 B/P/negative on non-crit
