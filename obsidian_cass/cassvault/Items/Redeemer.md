# Redeemer

**Type:** weapon (greatsword) | **Owner:** Gabriel (Josh) | **Campaign:** Hells Rebels

Formerly known as "Corruption." A +2 Flaming Greatsword forged from green glass that burns with green flame. The blade radiates evil -- but in the hands of a Lawful Good wielder, it becomes aligned Lawful Good and strikes as adamantine. The sword progresses: on level-up it grows stronger, killing undead grants Ghost Touch, and after dealing 666 cumulative damage to evil creatures it becomes Holy. A weapon in the process of being saved.
