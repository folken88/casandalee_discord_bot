# The Bank of Besmara

**Owner:** Rhyarca | **Campaign:** Skull & Shackles
**Type:** Wondrous item (locket)

A gold chain locket shaped like a coin, sacred to Besmara the Pirate Queen. The locket cannot be located by divination or finding-spells, and grants the wearer a +5 bonus to Sleight of Hand checks. It functions as a bag of holding for coins only -- an extradimensional space that stores currency of any denomination. The perfect pirate's savings account, invisible to magical auditors.
