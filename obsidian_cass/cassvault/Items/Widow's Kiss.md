# Widow's Kiss

**Type:** equipment (bracer) | **Owner:** Maginnis (Hans) | **Campaign:** Iron Gods

A bracer that projects a short-range silence spell. Anything the wearer touches or holds produces dramatically less sound -- locks picked without a click, screams muffled before they begin. The perfect tool for a gunslinger who prefers not to announce his presence.
