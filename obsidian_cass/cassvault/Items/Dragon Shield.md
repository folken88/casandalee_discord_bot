# Dragon Shield

**Type:** weapon (shield) | **Owner:** J'Mal (Tim) | **Campaign:** Hells Vengeance

A darkwood round shield inlaid with gold twin dragons. Normally worn on the back, it detaches and hovers before its wielder when danger looms -- a shield that decides for itself when it's needed.
