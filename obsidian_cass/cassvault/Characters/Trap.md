# Trap

**Class:** Unknown
**STR** 10 | **DEX** 10 | **CON** 10 | **INT** 10 | **WIS** 10 | **CHA** 10

## Key Equipment
- **Light Crossbow** (weapon, equipped) — Load: Loading a light crossbow is a move action that provokes attacks of opportunity.  You draw a light crossbow back by pulling a lever. Normally, operating a light crossbow requires two hands. However, you can shoot, but not load, a light crossbow with one hand at a –2 penalty on attack rolls. You can shoot a light crossbow with each hand, but you take a penalty on attack rolls as if attacking with two light weapons. This penalty is cumulative with the penalty for one-handed firing.
- **Shortspear** (weapon, equipped) — A shortspear is about 3 feet in length, making it a suitable thrown weapon.
