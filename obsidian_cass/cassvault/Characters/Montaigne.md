# Montaigne

**Class:** Unknown
**STR** 10 | **DEX** 10 | **CON** 10 | **INT** 10 | **WIS** 10 | **CHA** 10

## Key Equipment
- **Techno Wizard Ring** (equipment, equipped) — The Techno Wizard Ring offers both defense and insight. It protects its wearer with potent magic from premium enchanters as well as enhancing investigative magics. Available only at Montaigne!  Functions as +5 ring of protection and the following spells have double their normal range when cast by the wearer: Detect Chaos Detect Evil Detect Good Detect Law Detect Magic Detect Magic, Greater Detect Metal Detect Radiation Detect Secret Doors Detect Thoughts Detect Undead Technomancy
- **Outfit, Noble's** (equipment, equipped) — Source PZO1110  These clothes are designed specifically to be expensive and gaudy. Precious metals and gems are worked into the clothing. A would-be noble also needs a signet ring and jewelry (worth at least 100 gp) to accessorize this outfit.  As this outfit does not cost 10 gp or less, player characters can not choose this outfit for free when first beginning play.
- **Montaigne Turtleneck** (equipment, equipped) — Make a statement with the Montaigne Turtleneck - a stunning emerald necklace that offers unparalleled protection. Using the most potent magics, this necklace will toughen your skin to the consistency of alloyed steel chainmail. Never fear injury again with the Montaigne Turtleneck on. Upgrade your style and protect yourself at the same time - get your Montaigne Turtleneck today!
- **Matriarch's Tiara** (equipment, equipped) — Crafted for and worn by a Drow Matriarch, this incredibly potent item has immensely powerful magic woven about it to aid the wearer in any task. Unlock your ultimate spellcasting potential with this incredible artifact from Montaigne!
- **Poncho of the Golden Seal** (equipment, equipped) — An artifact from a long-dead civilization, this Poncho was once the item of station for high ranking diplomats, Lords, and Ladies.  It protects the wearer against a wide variety of unpleasant situations and also enhances their stature with those who know. Found only at Montaigne!
- **Titan Belt** (equipment, equipped) — This belt is said to have been crafted for a Storm Giant Prince. How it arrived in Numeria, none can say! But you could only find such powerful artifacts at Montaigne!  It grants the wearer incredible strength and stamina, making armor and shields feel light as a feather!
- **Spin-A-Trix** (equipment) — A holy artifact to the followers of Besmara and other gambling-affiliated deities, this item is truly an avatar of chaos. Wearing the item disables the following magical bonuses on the wearer, though the effects resume if Spin-A-Trix is taken off: AC Deflection, AC Natural Armor, Saves Resistance. The item then provides similar bonuses (luck) but the potency of the enchantment is ever changing.
- **Cowboy Belt** (equipment, equipped) — More than just cosplay, this belt greatly enhances the wearers reflexes and aim! It also protects any pistol holstered within it and up to 4 knives with a constant Mending, Prestidigitation, and Rapid Repair's up to 5hp to these items per day. Of course such an exotic item could only be found at Montaigne's of Starfall!
