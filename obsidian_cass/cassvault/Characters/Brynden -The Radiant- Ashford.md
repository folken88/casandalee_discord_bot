# Brynden "The Radiant" Ashford

**Race:** Human | **Class:** Paladin 10 | **HP:** 20
**STR** 15 | **DEX** 12 | **CON** 12 | **INT** 10 | **WIS** 12 | **CHA** 15

## Key Equipment
- **Ring of Protection +1** (equipment, equipped) — This ring offers continual magical protection in the form of a deflection bonus of +1 to +5 to AC. ConstructionRequirements Forge Ring, shield of faith, caster must be of a level at least three times higher than the bonus of the ring; Cost 1,000 gp
- **Amulet of Natural Armor +1** (equipment, equipped) — This amulet, usually containing some type of magically preserved monster hide or other natural armor—such as bone, horn, carapace, or beast scales—toughens the wearer’s body and flesh, giving him an enhancement bonus to his natural armor from +1 to +5, depending on the kind of amulet. ConstructionRequirements Craft Wondrous Item, barkskin, creator's caster level must be at least three times the amulet's bonus; Cost 1,000 gp
- **Inheritor's Greatsword** (weapon, equipped) — A masterfully crafted blade made in homage to the inheritor's own, from the time before her ascension. This blade functions as a holy symbol for any Lawful-Good Divine Caster.
- **Cloak of Resistance +1** (equipment, equipped) — This garment offers magic protection in the form of a +1 to +5 resistance bonus on all saving throws (Fortitude, Reflex, and Will). ConstructionRequirements Craft Wondrous Item, resistance, creator's caster level must be at least three times the cloak's bonus; Cost 500 gp
- **The Horn of Northgarde** (loot, equipped) — Sounding a horn requires a DC 10 Perform (wind instruments) check and can convey concepts such as "Attack!", "Help!", "Advance!", "Retreat!", "Fire!", and, "Alarm!" The report of a signal horn can be clearly heard (Perception DC 0) up to a half-mile distant. For each quarter-mile beyond, Perception checks to hear the horn suffer a -1 penalty.
