## Signature Maneuvers

### The Elfrope
A party maneuver where Elfrip casts *Levitate* on multiple party members, then uses his flight ability (Wings of Fire revelation) to tow them through the air like a rope line. Commonly used to cross rivers and other terrain obstacles. Named for its inventor. There is even a session named after it: "Carrion Crown - Elfrope 2024 03 03".
