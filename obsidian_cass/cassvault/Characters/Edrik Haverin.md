## Background
Caliphas noble and aristocrat. Publicly known as a respectable member of the upper class. Privately, he recovers Shining Crusade artifacts for the Whispering Way through the city's underground smuggling network.

## GM-Only: Plot Role
- Ordered the hit on the Anotep uncle to recover specific artifacts Anotep was fencing
- The ransacked rooms at the Anotep villa were his people searching for items
- Works for the Whispering Way in exchange for promised immortality (vampirism or lichdom)
- Not a true believer — a coward making a transaction. Fears death above all.
- The cult considers him disposable and will discard him when no longer useful
- Maintains a villa/mansion in Caliphas where stolen artifacts are stored before cult handoff
- The acid mutilation on Anotep's body was cult methodology — Haverin may not have ordered it specifically

## Personality
Paranoid, slippery, and self-serving. Presents as a cultured aristocrat but underneath is a frightened man desperate to cheat death. High DEX and rogue abilities reflect someone who has survived by being faster and more cunning than his enemies, not by fighting fair.
