## Key Equipment
- **Cutlass** (weapon, equipped) — This short, curved sword is favored by seafarers for closequarters combat. It is treated as a scimitar for any effects that apply to scimitars.
- **Studded Leather Armor** (equipment, equipped) — An improved form of leather armor, studded leather armor is covered with dozens of metal protuberances. While these rounded studs offer little defense individually, in the numbers they are arrayed in upon such armor, they help catch lethal edges and channel them away from vital spots. The rigidity caused by the additional metal does, however, result in less mobility than is afforded by a suit of normal leather armor.

- **Light Crossbow** (weapon, equipped) — Load: Loading a light crossbow is a move action that provokes attacks of opportunity.  You draw a light crossbow back by pulling a lever.
