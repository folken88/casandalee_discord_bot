# Boudicca

**Race:** Half-Elf | **Class:** Magus 12 | **HP:** 120
**STR** 10 | **DEX** 17 | **CON** 14 | **INT** 15 | **WIS** 12 | **CHA** 8

## Key Equipment
- **Spellbook** (loot) — A spellbook has 100 pages of parchment, and each spell takes up one page per spell level (one page each for 0-level spells). A newly purchased spellbook contains no spells. A wizard character begins play with a spellbook containing the spells he knows.
- **Cloak of Resistance +2** (equipment, equipped) — Aura moderate abjuration; CL 6th  This garment offers magic protection in the form of a +1 to +5 resistance bonus on all saving throws (Fortitude, Reflex, and Will).
- **The Gunslinger +2 Dex & Con** (equipment, equipped) — This tattoo grants the wearer an enhancement bonus to two physical ability scores of +2, +4, or +6. Treat this as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the belt is worn. These bonuses are chosen when the tattoo created and cannot be changed. It also grants a +2 competence bonus on all dex-based skill checks. ConstructionRequirements Craft Wondrous Item, bear's endurance, cat's grace; Cost 6,000 gp
- **Ring of Protection +2** (equipment, equipped) — Aura moderate abjuration; CL 6th  This ring offers continual magical protection in the form of a deflection bonus of +1 to +5 to AC.
- **Edge of the Sea** (weapon, equipped) — Powers: +2 Icy Burst Katana, grants owner at-will waterbreathing and touch of the sea (30' swim speed).  Runes on the blade are written in celestial and appear to be part of a poem:  1) She sought restoration in the cool waters of Kintargo Bay.  2) She honed her edge upon the white stones of knowledge.  3) Lo! She heard the song of the city in her ears and then her heart. 4) Standing upon the hill in the temple's great hall, she prayed to the Last of the First. 5) As the King Tide crashed agains
- **The Librarian +2 (Int & Wis)** (equipment, equipped) — The tattoo grants the wearer an enhancement bonus to two mental ability scores of +2, +4, or +6. Treat this as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the tattoo is worn. These bonuses are chosen when the tattoo is created and cannot be changed. If the tattoo grants a bonus to Intelligence, it also grants skill ranks as a headband of vast intelligence. ConstructionRequirements Craft Wondrous Item, fox's cunning, owl's wisdom; Cost 6,000 gp
