# Chief Bello

**Gender:** Male (he/him).

Leader of the cursed undead Boali who haunt the ruins of Boali and the Soul
Well. Retained more of his mind than most of his people — enough to rule them,
and enough to speak.

- When Ser scouted the Soul Well under magical concealment (Desnus 4717), Bello
  could not see her but sensed her presence, threatened that he and his people
  would eat her if they found her, and told her: **"The Boali are twice cursed."**
- Commanded the undead who remained at the Well while the horde hunted at night.
- Defeated by the Kill-Steal crew during their assault on the Soul Well, after
  the Charau-Ka lured much of the horde away.

What Bello meant by "twice cursed" is not fully established in the record.
