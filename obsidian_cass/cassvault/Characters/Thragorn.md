# Thragorn




## Session Appearances

### Shackles - Drow Envy 2026 03 05
A druid who runs a shop/hut. The party visits him to potentially purchase reincarnation services. He has a pet Tyrannosaur and is described as 'a little loose in the morals' and 'grumpy' but willing to trade magic items at 50% value (100% for Spear of the Mangrove Watch).; A smith/craftsperson at Goatshead who can enhance weapons and create magic items. Can upgrade the Iron Clutch of the Devil to +2 and add fire damage, or create a flying magic item from harpy feathers.
