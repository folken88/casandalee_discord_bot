## Key Equipment
- **Amulet of Natural Armor +1** (equipment, equipped) — This amulet, usually containing some type of magically preserved monster hide or other natural armor—such as bone, horn, carapace, or beast scales—toughens the wearer’s body and flesh, giving him an enhancement bonus to his natural armor from +1 to +5, depending on the kind of amulet.
- **Wand of Infernal Healing** (consumable, equipped) — You anoint a wounded creature with devil's blood or unholy water, giving it fast healing 1. This ability cannot repair damage caused by silver weapons, good-aligned weapons, or spells or effects with the good descriptor. The target detects as an evil creature for the duration of the spell and can sense the evil of the magic, though this has no long-term effect on the target's alignment.
- **Wand of Remove Disease** (consumable, equipped) — Remove disease can cure all diseases from which the subject is suffering. You must make a caster level check (1d20 + caster level) against the DC of each disease affecting the target. Success means that the disease is cured. The spell also kills some hazards and parasites, including green slime and others. Since the spell's duration is instantaneous, it does not prevent reinfection after a new exposure to the same disease at a later date.
- **Whisper Skin** (equipment, equipped) — Blacksteel chainmail shirt with integrated fully articulated greaves. When properly size-adjusted for a medium-sized wearer, this functions as a mithral chain shirt yet offers far greater protection.  Depending on the wearers general health and experience, the item allows them a limited form of travel through the shadow-realm.  This armor was likely crafted for one of the Whispering Tyrant's most skilled and trusted assassins.
- **Cloak of Resistance +1** (equipment, equipped) — This garment offers magic protection in the form of a +1 to +5 resistance bonus on all saving throws (Fortitude, Reflex, and Will).
- **Ring of Protection +1** (equipment, equipped) — This ring offers continual magical protection in the form of a deflection bonus of +1 to +5 to AC.
