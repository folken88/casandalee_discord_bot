## Key Equipment
- **Wirejack Tendons (Mark I)** (equipment, equipped) — Description Wirejack tendons not only strengthen and enhance existing reflexes, but also enhance the transmission of nerve impulses to surrounding musculature. Once installed, wirejack tendons grant an enhancement bonus to Dexterity.
- **Implant Mesh (Mark I)** (equipment, equipped) — Description  An implant mesh is a cybernetic augmentation that enhanced other cybernetic implants, notably those that heighten their wearers' physical abilities. If used to enhance cyberfiber musclesTG, a thoracic nanite chamberTG, or wirejack tendonsTG, an implant mesh must be installed on that piece of cyberware before the item is implanted in a body.      This implant mesh supports up to 3 physical body mods with a maximum bonus of +2.
- **Cyberfiber Muscles (Mark I)** (equipment, equipped) — Description Cyberfiber muscles are powerful synthetic muscles that augment but do not replace existing muscle mass in a creature. Once installed, cyberfiber muscles grant an enhancement bonus to Strength. This enhancement bonus alters price, implantation value, install DCs, and Craft DCs as listed below.
- **Dermal Plating (Mark I)** (equipment, equipped) — Description These plates exist under the skin and provide increased resistance to physical attacks. They are undetectable normally, but are visible when struck and for a few moments afterwords.
- **Cybernetic Legs (Mark I)** (equipment, equipped) — Description A cybernetic leg completely replaces a normal leg, from the hip to the foot. It can take the place of a leg lost to a severing wound. The leg is fully functional and no different in appearance from a regular leg, although cyberart can be added to the leg at no additional cost. A cybernetic leg does not increase a user's Strength score. If both legs are replaced, they increase the user's base land speed by 10 feet and grant a +5 bonus to CMD against trip attempts and a +5 circumstance
