# Goblin Seaman

**Race:** Goblin | **Class:** Rogue (Unchained) 7 | **HP:** 45
**STR** 10 | **DEX** 17 | **CON** 12 | **INT** 10 | **WIS** 10 | **CHA** 14

## Key Equipment
- **Studded Leather Armor MW** (equipment, equipped) — An improved form of leather armor, studded leather armor is covered with dozens of metal protuberances. While these rounded studs offer little defense individually, in the numbers they are arrayed in upon such armor, they help catch lethal edges and channel them away from vital spots. The rigidity caused by the additional metal does, however, result in less mobility than is afforded by a suit of normal leather armor.
- **Dagger MW** (weapon, equipped) — A dagger has a blade that is about 1 foot in length.  Benefit: You get a +2 bonus on Sleight of Hand skill checks made to conceal a dagger on your body (see Using Skills).
