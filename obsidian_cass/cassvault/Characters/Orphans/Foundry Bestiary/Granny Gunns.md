## Key Equipment
- **Granny Gunn's Bracers of Armor +4** (equipment, equipped) — These beautifully ornate bracers, crafted with intricate floral patterns and delicate engravings, appear entirely decorative at first glance. However, they are imbued with powerful protective magic. Once worn by the legendary Granny Gunn, a revered protector and matron of her village, these bracers provide the wearer with a continuous mage armor effect, enveloping them in an invisible field of force. Despite their elegant appearance, these bracers are a formidable defense in any adventurer's ars
- **Sea-Service Pistol** (weapon, equipped) — This masterfully crafted flintlock-style pistol, issued exclusively to officers of the Chelish Navy, represents the height of their martial craftsmanship. The weapon's stock is made from lacquered darkheart rosewood and inlaid with infernal gold filigree, its grip engraved with the winged crown of House Thrune. Despite appearing archaic to Alkenstar engineers or Technic League scholars, it is a weapon of status, discipline, and deadly precision in Cheliax.
- **Pirate's Ring of Protection +3** (equipment, equipped) — This ring, once worn by Granny Gunns in the Shackles, is a tarnished silver band adorned with a small sapphire that glimmers like the sea under moonlight. It was taken from the severed finger of one of her many vanquished foes, a testament to her ruthless prowess on the high seas. The ring is a prized possession among pirates, rumored to bring the wearer supernatural protection against all manner of threats.
