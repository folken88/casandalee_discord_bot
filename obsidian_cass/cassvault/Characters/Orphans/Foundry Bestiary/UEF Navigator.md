**Race:** Drow Noble | **Class:** Seaman 1 / Investigator 9 | **HP:** 100
**STR** 10 | **DEX** 15 | **CON** 14 | **INT** 17 | **WIS** 10 | **CHA** 10

## Key Equipment
- **Formula Book** (loot) — A formula book has 100 pages of parchment, and each alchemical extract formula takes up one page per extract level. A newly purchased formula book contains no extract formulas. An alchemist character begins play with a formula book containing the extract formulae he knows (Advanced Player's Guide 32). This is the alchemist equivalent of a spellbook for wizards (a spellbook and a formula book are essentially the same item).
