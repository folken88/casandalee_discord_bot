**Race:** Human | **Class:** Wizard 10 | **HP:** 16

## Key Equipment
- **Carrion Hill Keeper's Sigil** (equipment, equipped) — "To stand against the rot, one must be harder than the stone beneath it." Forged from blackened silver and set against a background of deep violet stone, the Carrion Hill Keeper’s Sigil is more than just an amulet—it is a symbol of authority, a ward against the city's endless decay, and a mark of duty worn by the three elected Keepers of Carrion Hill. Each of the three sigils has passed through the hands of countless rulers, warriors, and priests over the centuries, yet the town remains ever on 
- **Spellbook** (loot) — A spellbook has 100 pages of parchment, and each spell takes up one page per spell level (one page each for 0-level spells). A newly purchased spellbook contains no spells. A wizard character begins play with a spellbook containing the spells he knows.
- **Dark Red Diamond** (loot) — The gem is cut in a round shape, and when it refracts light it looks as if a fire is embedded in it. In the right light, these polished gems resemble droplets of blood. They usually come in dark red to brownish-red colors. They are also known as the most expensive variant of a diamond. It is speculated that when a spell is cast with its help, part of the caster's soul gets consumed along with the diamond.
- **Cloak of Resistance +2** (equipment, equipped) — This garment offers magic protection in the form of a +1 to +5 resistance bonus on all saving throws (Fortitude, Reflex, and Will). ConstructionRequirements Craft Wondrous Item, resistance, creator's caster level must be at least three times the cloak's bonus; Cost 2,000 gp
- **Torch** (loot) — Price 1 cp; Weight 1 lb.  A torch burns for 1 hour, shedding normal light in a 20-foot radius and increasing the light level by one step for an additional 20 feet beyond that area (darkness becomes dim light and dim light becomes normal light). A torch does not increase the light level in normal light or bright light. If a torch is used in combat, treat it as a one-handed improvised weapon that deals bludgeoning damage equal to that of a gauntlet of its size, plus 1 point of fire damage.
