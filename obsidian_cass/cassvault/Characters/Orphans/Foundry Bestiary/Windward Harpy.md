- **Dragon Heart** (loot) — Dragon Heart is a pendant fit for an emperor, a masterpiece typically accompanied by its counterpart, the Dragon's Eyes earrings. The jewelry pieces are crafted from the same rare gemstone sharing the Dragon Heart name. Due to the gem's extreme rarity, only the most famous gem cutters and jewelers are permitted to cut and set these stones. The work made transforms raw rarity into symbols of godlike power.
- **Agate** (loot) — The agate ring is rumored to attract wealth and prosperity. This belief may stem from Druidic traditions, which hold that the agate stone shares a strong connection and bond with the surrounding earth. Among the highest class of commoners, however, there exists a strict code of conduct regarding which finger and hand the agate gem should be worn on, a practice that changes depending on the day.
- **Gold Bangle** (loot) — Gold Bangle is a bracelet crafted from pure gold, designed to symbolize the highest class of nobility and the pinnacle of societal status. Forged by skilled blacksmiths using precise molds, each piece is then handed over to renowned goldsmiths who meticulously finish it with ornate detailing. These bangles are typically priced highly, which makes them frequent targets for thieves and swindlers seeking to profit from their value.
- **Silver Goblet** (loot) — Silver goblets, along with other pieces such as plates, platters, cutlery, and eating utensils, are ever-present in a noble household. These items are often commissioned in bulk, typically from a dedicated smithy or the local blacksmith. In most cases, the noble provides the silver ingots required for the commission, having full control over the final product.
