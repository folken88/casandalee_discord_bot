## Key Equipment
- **Torch** (loot) — Price 1 cp; Weight 1 lb.  A torch burns for 1 hour, shedding normal light in a 20-foot radius and increasing the light level by one step for an additional 20 feet beyond that area (darkness becomes dim light and dim light becomes normal light). A torch does not increase the light level in normal light or bright light. If a torch is used in combat, treat it as a one-handed improvised weapon that deals bludgeoning damage equal to that of a gauntlet of its size, plus 1 point of fire damage.
- **Potion of Cure Light Wounds** (consumable, equipped) — When laying your hand upon a living creature, you channel positive energy that cures 1d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level (maximum +5). Since undead are powered by negative energy, this spell deals damage to them instead of curing their wounds. An undead creature can apply spell resistance, and can attempt a Will save to take half damage.
- **Reaper Scythes** (weapon, equipped) — This pair of scythes are joined by a chain between them. As such, they can be treated as a dual-weapon, or if one is used as an anchor, the other can be thrown and retrieved in a single standard action with a 15' range.
- **Archivist** (weapon, equipped) — This blade is a mix of advanced technology, potent magic, and a master thief's cleverness. It functions as an adamantine short sword though it can be wielded by either Shortsword or Dagger proficiency. A trained wielder can alter the shape of the blade completely, allowing it to be used as thieves tools that yield a +5 bonus instead of the +2 of masterwork.
- **Potion of Vanish** (consumable, equipped) — This spell functions like invisibility, except the effect only lasts for 1 round per caster level (maximum of 5 rounds). Like invisibility, the spell immediately ends if the subject attacks any creature.
- **Studded Leather Armor** (equipment, equipped) — An improved form of leather armor, studded leather armor is covered with dozens of metal protuberances. While these rounded studs offer little defense individually, in the numbers they are arrayed in upon such armor, they help catch lethal edges and channel them away from vital spots. The rigidity caused by the additional metal does, however, result in less mobility than is afforded by a suit of normal leather armor.
