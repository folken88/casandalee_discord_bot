# Caliphas City Guide

> Capital of Ustalav. Setting for both Justice Gorls (4707 AR) and portions of Carrion Crown (4717 AR).

## Restoration Park

### Restoration Park: A Tranquil Facade in Ustalav's CapitalRestoration Park in Caliphas presents a seemingly tranquil space within the bustling city. It is a meticulously maintained park, offering respite from the often grim atmosphere of Ustalav. However, a closer look reveals a more complex and layered narrative.
- **Location:** The park is situated within the shadow of Castle Stryithe, a significant landmark in Caliphas. This proximity suggests a potential connection between the park and the castle's history or inhabitants.
- **Creation:** The park was established by Caliphas's aristocrats to cover a section of the city's buried level, effectively sealing off underground sewers.
- This detail hints at a deliberate attempt to conceal aspects of Caliphas's past or possibly some unsettling secrets lurking beneath the city's surface.- **Warden:** A half-elf druid named Merrick Sais serves as the warden of Restoration Park. She is particularly fond of the park's nocturnal beauty, preferring to tend to it under the moonlight.
- **A Hunting Ground:** Merrick's nocturnal habits inadvertently exposed her to the vampires who use the park as a hunting ground. Her fondness for the park's nighttime tranquility tragically led to her demise at the hands of these predators.
- This highlights the ever-present danger lurking beneath Ustalav's surface, even in seemingly peaceful locations like a city park.- **Key Features:**
- **Park Entrance:** The park officially remains open from dawn until dusk, but lacks a gate to prevent nighttime visitors. This accessibility adds to the vulnerability of those, like Merrick, who are drawn to the park's nighttime ambiance.- **Sesasgia Caliphvaso Memorial:** This alabaster statue commemorates Sesasgia Caliphvaso, the countess who re-established Caliphas after the Whispering Tyrant's defeat. Its presence serves as a reminder of the city's tumultuous past and the enduring legacy of its leaders.- **Standing Stones:** Merrick constructed three standing stones, marking the park as her sacred grove. These stones bear faded bloodstains, mistakenly attributed by most citizens to druidic rituals. The bloodstains serve as a subtle indication of the park's darker side, a reminder of the vampires' presence.- **Reflecting Pool:** This historically significant pool features columns depicting Ustalavic soldiers confronting various threats, including the undead knights of the Whispering Tyrant. It serves as a visual representation of Ustalav's ongoing struggle against darkness and the enduring threat posed by the Whispering Tyrant.Restoration Park embodies the duality that characterizes Caliphas and Ustalav as a whole. It offers a facade of tranquility and beauty while concealing a history of darkness and danger. This reinforces the theme of Ustalav as a nation where appearances can be deceiving and where even seemingly safe havens may harbor sinister secrets.

## Hound's Tooth

### The Hound's Tooth: A Working-Class Haven in CaliphasHound's Tooth is a popular tavern in Caliphas that caters primarily to the city's working class. Unlike some of the more opulent establishments discussed in our conversation, like Lethean Manor or The Majesty, the Hound's Tooth offers a more down-to-earth atmosphere.
- **Clientele:** Craftsmen, stevedores, and other laborers frequent the Hound's Tooth, seeking respite from their daily toils.- **Ambiance:** The tavern is not known for its refined atmosphere or gourmet cuisine.- **Affordability:** Patrons appreciate the Hound's Tooth for its cheap drinks, which are only slightly diluted.**Shaid Boxmon**, a former constable, owns and operates the Hound's Tooth. He resides above the tavern with his wife and two teenage sons.
- **Protective Nature:** Shaid is fiercely protective of his family and those he considers worthy of his loyalty.- **Insight into Caliphas's Underbelly:** Due to his past as a constable, Shaid possesses a deeper understanding of Caliphas's criminal underworld than he readily shares.- **Intuition and Action:** He is known to forcefully eject patrons from his establishment based solely on a sense of unease, demonstrating his reliance on intuition and his proactive approach to maintaining order.The Hound's Tooth represents a microcosm of Caliphas's working-class population, providing a space for them to unwind and connect. Shaid Boxmon's background as a former constable adds another layer to the tavern's narrative, hinting at a possible connection to the city's darker side.

## Palace of Voices

### Palace of Voices: A Stage for Sibling RivalryThe Palace of Voices in Caliphas is the city's royal theater and opera house, a place of high culture and entertainment for the Ustalavic elite. It's a place where, just like in some of the other locations we've discussed, like Lethean Manor, the city's elite gather. However, unlike the mystery surrounding Lethean Manor's events, the Palace of Voices is known for its artistic performances.
- **Ownership:** The Palace of Voices is owned by siblings Paulo and Marrian Carameizta, who inherited it from their parents.- **A Divided Leadership:** Although both siblings share a love for the theater, they have a deep-seated animosity towards each other and clash over how to manage the establishment.- **The Guiding Force:** In the midst of this sibling rivalry, the theater staff and performers look to conductor **Aldentor Rudono** for guidance.
- Rudono's talent and leadership have driven the Palace of Voices to achieve success season after season, further establishing its prestige in Ustalav.- He maintains a dynamic relationship with Headmistress Calmadra Vhalikackos of the Karcau Opera, described as a "love-hate" connection, which suggests a complex and perhaps competitive dynamic between the two institutions.The Palace of Voices represents a cultural cornerstone in Ustalav, a place where artistic brilliance thrives despite the undercurrent of familial discord. Aldentor Rudono's role as a stabilizing force amidst the Carameizta siblings' conflict showcases the enduring power of art and passion even in the face of personal conflicts.

## Maiden's Choir Cathedral

### Maiden's Choir: Caliphas's Royal ChurchThe sources characterize the Maiden's Choir as a significant religious institution in Caliphas. The cathedral serves as the royal church of Pharasma and holds a prominent position within the city's religious landscape.
- **Historical Significance:** The Maiden's Choir is an ancient structure that predates the reign of the Whispering Tyrant, enduring even during his oppressive rule. This suggests the cathedral holds deep historical and cultural importance for the people of Caliphas.- **Architectural Features:** The sources highlight the cathedral's impressive dome, a prominent architectural feature that likely contributes to the building's grandeur.- **Leadership:** Mother Verith Thestia, a cleric of Pharasma, presides over the Maiden's Choir. Her leadership role suggests a strong adherence to the goddess's tenets within the cathedral's community.- **Zetiah Mardhalas:** Zetiah, a merciless inquisitor, assists Mother Thestia. Her presence highlights the cathedral's commitment to upholding Pharasma's principles and confronting any potential threats to the faith.- **Rumors of a Hidden Remnant:** Some whispers suggest a horrifying remnant of the Whispering Tyrant's rule remains concealed within the cathedral's catacombs. This rumor adds an element of mystery and potential danger to the Maiden's Choir, contrasting with its outward appearance as a place of worship.- **Denial by Authorities:** Both Mother Thestia and Zetiah Mardhalas deny the rumors of a hidden remnant. Whether this denial reflects a genuine lack of knowledge or a deliberate attempt to conceal a dangerous secret remains unclear.The sources present the Maiden's Choir as a place of worship deeply ingrained in Caliphas's history and culture. However, the rumors of a hidden remnant from the Whispering Tyrant's reign introduce a sense of unease and a potential for conflict beneath the cathedral's seemingly devout facade.

## Brookman's Alley

### Brookman's Alley: Caliphas's Haunted StreetAn infamous location in Caliphas steeped in urban legends and a chilling reputation for paranormal activity.
- **Reputation:** Brookman's Alley is widely considered the most haunted street in all of Caliphas. This reputation likely deters most citizens from venturing into the alley, especially at night.- **The Legend of the Skipping Squares:** A specific urban legend associated with the alley involves blood bubbling up from the cobblestones on nights with a full moon, forming the pattern of a child's skipping squares. According to the legend, anyone passing through the alley on such a night must tread upon the squares in the correct order to avoid attracting the attention of the resident ghosts.- **Conflicting Identities of the Ghosts:** The legend offers conflicting accounts regarding the identities of the ghosts haunting the alley. Some stories claim the ghosts are two sisters named Yarri and Yevette who met a mysterious demise while playing. Others believe the alley is haunted by a single ghost, that of a Kellid boy whom the sisters stoned to death. This ambiguity adds to the mystery and fear surrounding the alley.The sources depict Brookman's Alley as a place imbued with a sense of dread and supernatural intrigue. The urban legends surrounding the alley, particularly the tale of the blood-soaked skipping squares, highlight Caliphas's fascination with the macabre and the city's tendency to embrace tales of the supernatural. The conflicting accounts of the ghosts' identities add another layer of mystery to the alley, leaving room for speculation and personal interpretation.

## Castle Borgoffi

Castle Borgoffi: Caliphas's Rusting Sword
Castle Borgoffi in Caliphas is described in the sources as the city's "rusting sword" in contrast to Castle Golbanze, which is considered Caliphas's "shield against threats from the sea."1 This heavily armed fortress boasts an impressive array of weaponry.

Defensive Armaments: The castle is fortified with ballistae and mangonels, capable of launching hull-perforating spears, alchemist’s fire bombs, and casks of quicklime.1
Offensive Capabilities: Ritambros Deymarian, the castle’s eccentric master of arms, is an alchemist who continually develops new weapons of war using unusual materials such as molds from the Darklands, undead body parts, and diseased rats.2
Despite its considerable arsenal, Castle Borgoffi sees limited action outside of scheduled exercises.2

Limited Engagement: Colonel Joelm Colmar, the commanding officer of the fortress, views his position as a monotonous dead-end. He spends much of his time tending to his two dozen pet ravens, kept in a coop on the castle’s roof.3
Castle Borgoffi stands as a testament to Caliphas's preparedness for conflict, even if such conflict is infrequent. The sources suggest that the city's true battles are fought in the political and social arenas, rather than on the open battlefield.

## Havenguard Lunatic Asylum

Havenguard Lunatic Asylum: A Beacon of Hope in Grim Ustalav
A place misunderstood by many citizens. Although they see it as a prison or a frightening place, it actually serves as a sanctuary for the mentally ill. The asylum's founder, **Dr. Beaurigmand Trice**, is a venture-captain of the Pathfinder Society and a healer who utilizes unconventional methods to treat his patients.
●
**Unconventional Healing:** Dr. Trice focuses on treating mental illnesses without resorting to divine magic, making his approach unique in the religiously devout nation of Ustalav.
●
**Pathfinder Connection:** Havenguard stands out for hosting Ustalav's largest Pathfinder lodge, demonstrating Dr. Trice's commitment to finding help for patients beyond traditional medical practices.
**Cereis Linas**, a sorcerer, assists Dr. Trice in managing the asylum and its patients, highlighting the collaborative nature of care provided at Havenguard**2**.
●
**Challenging Cases:** One notable patient, **Mr. Tembs**, adds complexity to their work. Mr. Tembs is a witch with a history of violence and possesses multiple personalities, presenting a unique challenge for Dr. Trice and Cereis.
Havenguard Lunatic Asylum is a place where compassion and unconventional healing practices intersect. Despite its association with mental illness, the asylum, under the leadership of Dr. Trice, strives to provide solace and support for those struggling with mental afflictions.

## Lethean Manor

Lethean Manor: Countess Carmilla's Seat of Power and Mystery
Lethean Manor, as detailed in the sources, is a lavish palace in Caliphas, constructed with striking crimson flowers and ruby glass. This serves as the residence of Countess Carmilla Caliphvaso, the powerful ruler of both Caliphas city and county. The Countess holds a position of great influence in Ustalav, and her manor reflects her status and enigmatic nature.
●
**Lavish Social Events:** Lethean Manor frequently hosts grand galas, renowned as prestigious events that draw attendees from all strata of Caliphas society, including members of the lower classes who have caught the Countess's eye.** **These events provide a glimpse into the Countess's power and her unconventional approach to social interactions.
●
**Mysterious After-Effects:** The galas held at Lethean Manor are shrouded in an air of mystery. Guests often find themselves unable to recall the details of the extravagant festivities, and some attendees have even vanished without a trace. This suggests a darker side to the Countess and the events that transpire within her opulent residence.
The sources hint at a possible connection between the name Lethean Manor and Lethe, the river of forgetfulness in Greek mythology. This connection could explain the memory loss experienced by guests attending the Countess's gatherings.
The sources paint Lethean Manor as a place of beauty and intrigue, yet also one of potential danger. Countess Carmilla Caliphvaso remains a figure of fascination and fear, her motives and the true nature of her gatherings at Lethean Manor remaining largely unknown.

## Reaping Rock

### Reaping Rock: Caliphas's Protective Barrier and Ship GraveyardA prominent geographical feature near the city of Caliphas, serving as a natural breakwater that shelters the city harbor. The cliffs offer protection from the storms that frequently batter Lake Encarthan, playing a crucial role in safeguarding Caliphas's naval and merchant fleets.
However, the Reaping Rock is also a site of danger and maritime disaster. Its hidden rocks, combined with unpredictable winds and currents, have spelled doom for countless ships, earning the area its grim name.
- **Dual Nature:** The Reaping Rock embodies a duality, symbolizing both protection and peril. While it safeguards the city's ships from the wrath of the lake, it also poses a constant threat to navigation.
- **Historical Significance:** The sources suggest that the Reaping Rock has played a role in Caliphas's history for a considerable time, with numerous shipwrecks littering its length.
- **Infamous Wrecks:** The sources highlight two particularly notable shipwrecks:
- **The Silver Star:** This ship met its end due to a heartbroken navigator, adding an element of human tragedy to the site's history.- **The Trackless:** This vessel vanished 40 years prior to its reappearance during a storm in 4710 AR. The ship wrecked upon the Reaping Rock with no trace of its crew, adding an eerie mystery to the location.- **Symbolism:** The Reaping Rock's name itself evokes imagery of death and the relentless cycle of life and demise. The cliffs, with their jagged edges and the skeletal remains of ships scattered along their base, serve as a visual reminder of the power of nature and the fragility of human endeavors.
The Reaping Rock stands as a testament to the complex relationship between Caliphas and the natural world. It provides a vital service to the city while simultaneously posing a constant threat, serving as a reminder of the ever-present dangers lurking beneath the surface of Lake Encarthan. The shipwrecks, particularly those shrouded in mystery or tragedy, contribute to the aura of unease and the sense of the macabre that pervades Caliphas.

## Cairns

This suggests a strong sense of community and resourcefulness, with families adapting to challenging living conditions.
- **Brynran Khoraseid:** The community is led by Brynran Khoraseid, a noseless crone and oracle, who is regarded as a grandmother figure by many. Her role as an oracle suggests a connection to the spiritual realm and potentially to the ancient Kellid faiths practiced by the community.
- **Potential for Conflict:** The presence of outlawed Kellid faiths, even in symbolic form, hints at a potential for conflict with Ustalav's dominant Pharasmin culture. This tension could manifest as social prejudice or even persecution, as suggested by the description of elves facing prejudice from Ustalav's Varisian inhabitants.
The sources present the Cairns as a resilient community striving to preserve their Kellid heritage within the bustling, and often unforgiving, environment of Caliphas. The community's adaptation to limited space, their reverence for their elder leader, and the presence of outlawed religious symbols all point to a distinct cultural identity that persists despite the pressures of assimilation.
