## Justice Gorls — Professor Martin's Death (player-known, public cover story)

**Public story (the in-world record):** Professor Vellesca Martin — the researcher the Justice Gorls famously *rescued* from the Skanktown Killas — was killed in Caliphas (4707.01.28), which authorities ruled random gang violence. She was publicly mourned as a hero, and the Gorls were honored as her rescuers at her funeral on 4707.02.10.

**The truth:** the Justice Gorls themselves killed her, on a paid contract. **This is NOT a secret from the players — they did it and know it.** Per Toby (2026-07-24): Cass is an honest, non-judgemental chronicler. When players ask, she may acknowledge the truth matter-of-factly (and note what the public believes), without moralizing or dredging it up to shame anyone. The timeline row (4707.01.28) now records BOTH: the truth (Gorls, paid contract) and the public cover story (random gang violence) — since the Discord audience is the players, who know. In-world NPCs still believe the cover story.

## Ustalav — The Vampire Ruling Class (Caliphas)

**GM canon (Toby, 2026-06-29):**
- **Vampires secretly rule Ustalav.** Ever since the Shining Crusade defeated Tar-Baphon (the Whispering Tyrant, imprisoned 3827 AR — roughly 900 years before the campaign's 4717), a hidden vampire ruling class has been the true power behind the nation, seated in **Caliphas**. The visible nobility and Palatine Council are the face; the vampires are the hand.
- **They cull the CP-USS.** The vampires keep a close watch on the City Police Undead Specialist Squads (out of Lepidstadt, under Judge Embreth Daramid). When a squad reaches a certain level of **competence and awareness**, the vampires arrange its elimination — usually by steering it onto a "dangerous mission" out of town from which it does not return.
- **Why:** a CP-USS squad that got high-level enough and learned the truth about the vampires would be a genuine threat to the ruling class. Culling competent squads keeps the secret and removes the one force positioned to uncover it.
- **Tie-in — Anotep Villa:** the CP-USS being out of town / overdue during the Anotep investigation (see above) is one such cull in progress.

**Open threads (TBD — GM to decide):**
- Which specific vampire family/families sit at the top, and who fronts for them among the living nobility.
- How the vampires interface with the Palatine Council and the counties' rulers.
- Whether the Whispering Way are rivals to the vampires, unwitting cover, or occasionally their tools.
- Whether any current NPC ally/patron of a party is a vampire agent (or a vampire).

## Skull & Shackles — Boali / Soul Well / Scourge Cove (GM canon rewrite, 2026-08-16)

**The secret history (players must piece this together in play):**
- The **Boali Soul Well** was a "gift" from a powerful devil-lord centuries ago: repeated reincarnation for the Boali people, but every reincarnation required the sacrifice of another mortal soul. Thousands were captured and sacrificed over the centuries; Boali religion/culture became synonymous with human sacrifice. Senghor's war (destruction of Boali, **4701.02.07**) was genuinely justified on these grounds.
- **Magistrate Ato Jaja** (Senghor magistrate; ~60 in 4701, ~76 in 4717; bald Black man with Senghor's characteristic green eyes; now elderly, frail, retired, publicly a WAR HERO) secretly and independently devised a plan to neutralize the Soul Well, using city funds without revealing the full plan. He was NOT trying to create undead — he believed the infernal artifact couldn't be safely destroyed outright.
- **The Sphere** (completed **4701.01.15**, Senghor Harbor, under Ato's direction): converts magical energy passing through it into necromantic energy. Ato intended it to corrupt the Well so reincarnating Boali would simply die permanently / have souls sent on to Pharasma's Boneyard, and hoped battle casualties would overload and collapse the system.
- During the 4701.02.07 assault (harbor deliberately sabotaged: ships sunk, huge stones raised to block navigation, docks wrecked), Ato's **secret mercenary team** infiltrated the Soul Well, installed and activated the Sphere, then withdrew to **Scourge Cove**, a hidden harbor-cave north of Boali.
- **Ato's theory was wrong.** The Sphere transformed the Well's reincarnation energy into necromancy: Boali who die and pass through the Well return as **cursed undead** — fragments of identity, overwhelming aggression/hunger/anguish/rage. They shelter in/near the Soul Well by day (cannot tolerate sunlight), emerge in hundreds after nightfall to hunt/kill/eat any living creature, and return before dawn. They do not fight one another. A stable, repeating necromantic corruption — not the collapse Ato hoped for.
- **The cover-up:** months-to-years later, Senghor scouts observed the cursed Boali and reported the city was NOT empty — large numbers of undead former citizens, violently hostile. The reports reached Ato; this is when he learned his plan failed. He suppressed/destroyed the reports and falsified the record: officially Boali is abandoned, no organized population, danger ended, no reason to return. Senghor society is NOT knowingly complicit — Ato personally concealed everything. Modern Senghor remembers the war as a clean triumph of good over evil.
- **The Boali curse on the conspirators:** anyone significantly involved in handling/transporting/installing/arranging the Sphere operation. In life: mounting paranoia, distrust, suspicion, belief the other conspirators intend betrayal. On death: rise as powerful intelligent undead (graveknights or comparable). None can truly escape until justice is done to the plan's ORIGINATOR — **the curse's ultimate target is Ato Jaja** (it marked him even though he never carried the artifact; he does not know).
- **Scourge Cove:** the mercenaries retreated there, didn't know they were cursed, dissolved into accusations (betrayal, theft, hidden payment, informing for Senghor, murdering witnesses) and killed one another. By 4717 the cove is full of malevolent intelligent undead retaining varying memories of the operation, the Sphere, their orders, their employer, and their deaths — trapped until the crime receives justice.

**Story structure & planned stages:**
1. **Boali (WHAT happened)** — party arrived **4717.05.18** (harbor unnavigable; ~16 years abandoned). Charau-Ka tribe (sleep ~75 ft up in hammocks, avoid the undead) briefed them on the nightly horrors. Ser, scouting the Soul Well invisibly, met **Chief Bello** (leader of the cursed Boali) — he couldn't see her, threatened to eat her, and said **"The Boali are twice cursed"** (= the sacrificial Soul Well itself, then the Sphere's corruption). With a Charau-Ka nighttime distraction, the party descended the ~70-ft Soul Well shaft and defeated Bello and his guards. **Next objective: neutralize the Sphere** — the goal is NOT restoring the old sacrificial reincarnation (that should stay ended) but stopping the corruption so the trapped Boali can finally pass on.
2. **Scourge Cove (HOW)** — forensic stage. The undead mercenaries give PARTIAL information only: Senghor sponsored a secret operation; the Sphere was deliberately carried into the Well; it was hidden from military records; a high-ranking official was responsible. Records/contracts/testimony may eventually name Ato Jaja.
3. **Senghor (WHO)** — party must dig up old records and build convincing evidence: Ato's creation of the Sphere, his secret authorization, the undead catastrophe, his receipt of the scouting reports, his falsification. Ato may deny, rationalize, or flee once exposure is certain.
4. **Trial** — once convinced, Senghor arrests and publicly tries him (treason, misuse of funds/authority, unlawful secret military action, falsifying records, concealing an ongoing supernatural threat, genocide-equivalent). Convicted; sentenced to death by hanging.
5. **Execution climax** — Ato hangs, dies… and the Boali curse claims HIM: his corpse animates as a **graveknight** (surprising even Ato), armor manifesting around the body; it rises from the gallows and attacks the crowd. The party must destroy it publicly — undeniable supernatural proof, witnessed by all of Senghor, that cannot be buried again. This is the climax of the whole plotline.

**Moral theme:** Boali's human-sacrifice system genuinely needed to end — and that does not excuse Ato's secret experimental weapon, nor his choice to conceal the endless suffering it accidentally created. His two great crimes: the secret Sphere operation, and knowingly hiding the catastrophe once he had proof.

**What players know so far (safe to discuss):** the public war history (Senghor destroyed Boali in 4701 over its human-sacrifice religion; harbor blocked; city "abandoned"), and everything the party has directly experienced at Boali (ruins, Charau-Ka, the nightly undead, Bello and "twice cursed", the Soul Well assault). Everything else above — Sphere, Ato, mercenaries, cover-up, curse mechanics, Scourge Cove contents, trial/execution plan — is UNDISCOVERED.
