## Justice Gorls — Anotep Villa Murder

**GM Intent (Toby's notes, 2026-04-10):**
- Uncle Anotep was killed by the **Whispering Way**
- His face was defiled with acid as part of their methods (preventing speak with dead, or ritual significance)
- The Whispering Way is seeking specific artifacts that Anotep fenced for someone
- The exact artifacts they're after and who Anotep was fencing for is TBD (GM needs to decide)
- The uncle crawled to the Aroden soul anchor bust while dying so his soul could be captured — he may have known who killed him and can reveal it via speak with dead
- **CP-USS** (City Police Undead Specialist Squad — "SEUS" in the older note was a garbled transcription) being conveniently out of town and overdue is connected: the squad is away on a dangerous mission where it is being killed off. This is an instance of the **vampire cull** (see "Ustalav — The Vampire Ruling Class" below) — Caliphas being left without its undead specialists during the Anotep investigation is not a coincidence. (Whispering Way interference may also be a contributing factor.)
- The ransacked rooms suggest the killers were searching for specific items and left in a hurry

**What players know so far:**
- Uncle died, ruled suicide by Calops
- Servants were killed and rose as zombies
- Attic: dying person crawled to soul anchor, acid poured on face, body removed by third party
- House full of necromancy artifacts
- Khep Anotep hired them to investigate

**What players DON'T know:**
- Whispering Way involvement
- What specific artifacts were being sought
- Connection to the broader CC Whispering Way plotline (which takes place 10 years later in 4717)

## Justice Gorls — Professor Martin's Death (player-known, public cover story)

**Public story (the in-world record):** Professor Vellesca Martin — the researcher the Justice Gorls famously *rescued* from the Skanktown Killas — was killed in Caliphas (4707.01.28), which authorities ruled random gang violence. She was publicly mourned as a hero, and the Gorls were honored as her rescuers at her funeral on 4707.02.10.

**The truth:** the Justice Gorls themselves killed her, on a paid contract. **This is NOT a secret from the players — they did it and know it.** Per Toby (2026-07-24): Cass is an honest, non-judgemental chronicler. When players ask, she may acknowledge the truth matter-of-factly (and note what the public believes), without moralizing or dredging it up to shame anyone. The timeline row (4707.01.28) now records BOTH: the truth (Gorls, paid contract) and the public cover story (random gang violence) — since the Discord audience is the players, who know. In-world NPCs still believe the cover story.

## Ustalav — The Vampire Ruling Class (Caliphas)

**GM canon (Toby, 2026-06-29):**
- **Vampires secretly rule Ustalav.** Ever since the Shining Crusade defeated Tar-Baphon (the Whispering Tyrant, imprisoned 3827 AR — roughly 900 years before the campaign's 4717), a hidden vampire ruling class has been the true power behind the nation, seated in **Caliphas**. The visible nobility and Palatine Council are the face; the vampires are the hand.
- **They cull the CP-USS.** The vampires keep a close watch on the City Police Undead Specialist Squads (out of Lepidstadt, under Judge Embreth Daramid). When a squad reaches a certain level of **competence and awareness**, the vampires arrange its elimination — usually by steering it onto a "dangerous mission" out of town from which it does not return.
- **Why:** a CP-USS squad that got high-level enough and learned the truth about the vampires would be a genuine threat to the ruling class. Culling competent squads keeps the secret and removes the one force positioned to uncover it.
- **Tie-in — Anotep Villa:** the CP-USS being out of town / overdue during the Anotep investigation (see above) is one such cull in progress.

**Open threads (TBD — GM to decide):**
- Which specific vampire family/families sit at the top, and who fronts for them among the living nobility.
- How the vampires interface with the Palatine Council and the counties' rulers.
- Whether the Whispering Way are rivals to the vampires, unwitting cover, or occasionally their tools.
- Whether any current NPC ally/patron of a party is a vampire agent (or a vampire).
