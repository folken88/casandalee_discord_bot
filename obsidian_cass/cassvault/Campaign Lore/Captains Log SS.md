# Captain's Log

## 18th of Desnus

We had another keelhauling today. A gnome named Gricket was put to death for mutinous intent.  I've never seen so many sharks devouring a corpse.  They must enjoy the taste of gnome above others. I may test this theory with Conchobar when he ceases to be entertaining.  Something must be done to show these dogs that mutiny is suicide. Perhaps making Scourge bosun could  kill two fish with one spear, seeing as he's been such a shit quartermaster.

## 20th of Desnus

We raided a fishing village on the coast of Motaku. A parasite must've plagued the trees, because they had hardly anything to spare. But Besmara's fortune is as sure as the tides because this village happened to be keeping a Rahadoumi prisoner. Seeing as we are light on crew, we took him as well.

## 23rd of Desnus

Plugg has been keeping a close watch on the Rahadoumi. Says he hasn't spoken once but does his job well. Best to leave him in the Shackles of Compliance and keep him on fishing duty for a few more days.

## 26th of Desnus

The Rahadoumi has proven to be an incredible acquisition. A loyal, hardworking sailor is like three whores and an alehouse in times like these. He scales the ropes like a goblin monkey, and I can already tell he has a keen penchant for violence. If he kills as well as he climbs,  he could be the master of arms I've been sorely lacking. If only he would open his bloody mouth

## 28th of Desnus

Another rat was caught snooping around in my armory! This one smells Sargavan and swears he has no coconspirators. Kipper will pry the truth from underneath his fingernails. The rest of the crew will go down to half rations without grog until they can demonstrate some goddamn loyalty! Any thieving is now 20 lashes and then the sweatbox. And if anyone but Riaris even sniffs that armory I will peel them like a carrot.

## 29th of Desnus

Port Peril at last, and the end to so many of my problems...
