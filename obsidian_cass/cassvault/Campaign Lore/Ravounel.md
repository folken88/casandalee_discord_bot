# Ravounel

## Region

Map of the former Chelish province of Ravounel, now a new country of the same name.

## Lord Mayor Jilia Bainilus

Also said to have died and been reborn around the battle for Kintargo, Jilia is the beloved ruler of Kintargo, and now the temporary ruler of the fledgeling country of Ravounel.

## Gabriel

A huge man nearly 7' tall, said to have died on bleakbridge during the battle for kintargo. But it is also said that he is alive again thanks to the grace of the queen of delights, a powerful witch from the nearby city of Vyre.

## Femmik

A battle-dancer of Saranrae, Femmik is one of the warriors who liberated Kintargo from Barzillai Thrune.

## Calvin Carpenter

The famed luxury leatherworker calls Kintargo his home, and is said to have ties to the revoluntionaries.
