# CC-TG Campaign Combination

GM notes on how the Carrion Crown and Tyrant's Grasp adventure paths are combined.

## Overview

Carrion Crown is based on [[Adivion Adrissant]] building the Carrion Crown and putting it on, becoming some 'version' of [[Tar Baphon, The Whispering Tyrant|Tar Baphon]], whom the party then wipes out.

In Tyrant's Grasp, Tar Baphon escapes on his own and takes over Lastwall and the Isle of Dread.

[[Arazni]] escapes Geb and comes north to stop Tar Baphon, accompanied by Gebbite guards.
