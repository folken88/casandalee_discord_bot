# The Whispering Way

An ancient necromantic cult devoted to [[Tar Baphon, The Whispering Tyrant]]. More than just a cabal of necromancers -- undeath is their fountain of youth.

## Foundry Lore (Manifesto Mortiari)

The Whispering Way is practically a religion unto themselves. Tar Baphon's cult has endured centuries without him. Their only assets have been virtually limitless funds, fanatical devotion to their secret cause, and knowledge. The Esoteric Order of the [[Palatine Eye]] hunts them but does not exhaust every resource against the cult.

Their goal in Carrion Crown: constructing the **Carrion Crown**, a proto-phylactery vessel intended to transform a mortal into an unliving tyrant -- a hybrid crown of bone, alchemical toxin, and preserved organ. [[Adivion Adrissant]] seeks to build it and become a version of Tar Baphon.

Key operatives:
- **[[Auren Vrood]]** -- Wizard/Necromancer 14 (Agent of the Grave), field commander. Currently deceased (head in party backpack).
- **[[Adivion Adrissant]]** -- Magus (Kensai) 14, the mastermind behind the Carrion Crown plot.
- WW Annihilators, Blades, Practitioners, and Shanks serve as rank-and-file cultists.
