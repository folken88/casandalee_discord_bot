# Boali and Senghor

## The public history

**Boali** was a city on the Garundi coast whose religion centered on an ancient
magical site, the **Boali Soul Well**, said to grant its people a form of
repeated reincarnation. That gift had a monstrous price: each reincarnation
required the sacrifice of another mortal soul. Over centuries the Boali captured
and sacrificed thousands, and their culture became infamous for human sacrifice.

**Senghor** eventually learned the full extent of these practices and went to
war. On **7 Calistril, 4701**, Senghor attacked and destroyed Boali: the harbor
was deliberately rendered unusable (ships sunk in the approaches, enormous
stones raised to block navigation), the docks and much of the city were wrecked,
and organized Boali resistance was destroyed. Senghor remembers the war as a
justified triumph over an evil institution, and its official records state that
Boali was left abandoned, with no surviving population and no remaining danger.

## What the Kill-Steal crew found (4717)

The official record is wrong about the "abandoned" part. When the crew reached
the ruins on **18 Desnus, 4717** (approaching by small boats — the blocked
harbor still cannot take a ship), they found the city overgrown, shattered — and
NOT empty:

- By night, hundreds of **cursed undead Boali** pour out from the direction of
  the Soul Well, howling through the ruined streets, attacking and devouring any
  living thing they find. They cannot tolerate sunlight and return to the Well
  before dawn. They retain fragments of who they were, twisted by hunger and rage.
- A tribe of **Charau-Ka** (intelligent baboon-folk) roams the area and survives
  by sleeping some 75 feet up in jungle hammocks, out of the creatures' reach.
  They told the crew about the nightly horrors.
- Scouting the Soul Well while magically concealed, **Ser** encountered
  **Chief Bello**, leader of the cursed Boali — he could not see her, but knew
  someone was there, threatened to eat her, and said: **"The Boali are twice
  cursed."**
- With the Charau-Ka drawing the horde away one night, the crew descended the
  Soul Well — a shaft roughly 70 feet down in the jungle outside the city — and
  defeated Chief Bello and his guards.
- At the heart of the Well sits a strange **sphere-like artifact** that appears
  to be corrupting the site. Its origin is unknown. The crew's aim is to end the
  corruption so the trapped Boali can finally pass on — not to restore the old
  sacrificial reincarnation, which deserved its ending.

How the Boali became undead, who (if anyone) placed the sphere, and what
"twice cursed" fully means — the record does not yet establish.
