# Eliouren Elven Enclave

A hidden elven settlement in Ustalav, discovered during the Carrion Crown campaign.

## Foundry Lore

The elves retreated here in the face of rising human kingdoms and the [[The Whispering Tyrant|Whispering Tyrant]]'s reign. They practice druidic and illusionist magic, cloaking their presence from outsiders and keeping their sacred glades hidden.

The enclave has a shop, a speaker, a warden, and villagers represented in the Foundry world.
