# Sekolah, The Shark God

### **Sekolah, the Great Shark**

### **Titles & Epithets:**

*The Sea Devil*

*The Great Shark*

*Father of All Predators*

*The Bloodcurrent*

### **Alignment:**

**Lawful Evil** – Sekolah represents the **brutal order of predation**, where the strong dominate and the weak are consumed.

### **Domains:**

**Evil**, **Law**, **Water**, **War**

### **Favored Weapon:**

**Trident** – often stylized as **jagged or barbed**, representing the tearing jaws of a shark.

### **Appearance:**

Sekolah appears as a **massive, terrifying shark**, often **blood-colored or black**, with **unknowable golden eyes** and a mouth large enough to swallow whales whole.

### **Dogma:**

**Survival of the Fittest** – the weak exist to feed the strong. Mercy is weakness.

**Hunt and Kill Without Hesitation** – bloodshed is sacred, especially in Sekolah’s name.

**Claim What Is Yours** – land, sea, treasure, flesh—it belongs to those strong enough to take it.

**The Surface Must Drown** – sahuagin are superior; all surface dwellers are prey or tools.

### **Clergy & Worship:**

Sekolah’s clerics are known as **Bloodpriests**, and higher-ranking ones are sometimes called **Sharkbinders** or **Abyssal Prophets**.

Worship includes **ritual bloodletting**, **live sacrifices**, and **tournaments of combat**, sometimes even against other sahuagin.

Holy sites are often located in **submerged trenches**, **blood pits**, or **shark-infested coral spires**.

### **Holy Symbol:**

A **stylized shark tooth**, often **dripping blood** or surrounded by waves.

Others use a **three-pointed crimson trident**, echoing both his **weapon** and the **jagged dorsal fins** of sharks.

### **Rivalries:**

**Despises sea elves, merfolk, and surface races**, especially **those who worship gods of peace or nature** (like Gozreh).

Sometimes **in opposition to Dagon**, the demon lord of the sea, though they may be allied if it serves greater bloodshed.
