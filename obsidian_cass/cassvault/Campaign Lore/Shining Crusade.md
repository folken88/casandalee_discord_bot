# Shining Crusade

The Shining Crusade (3801-3827 AR) was the great war to defeat [[Tar Baphon, The Whispering Tyrant]] and free Ustalav from his undead dominion.

## Foundry Lore (Shining Crusade Lore Journal)

**Hamdad** was a legendary warrior of the Crusade, the 4th son of Ustalavian Nobility. Trained from birth as a knight and bodyguard for his older siblings. By the time of the Crusades, all his family was dead on the executioner's block at the hands of the Tyrant.

The Crusade secured a beachhead in Ustalav at modern-day Vauntil (3801 AR). Ustalav's armies, supported by the church of [[Pharasma]], launched a crusade against the lich. The Pharasmin bishop Prince Adamondais Virholt, bearing the holy mace Raven's Head, led the crusaders to Gallowspire.

## Key Artifacts

- **The Challenge of the Herald** -- A helmet once worn by Iomedae when she was Herald of Aroden. Re-discovered by [[Sirona]] (Dame Sirona Thorne).

## The Unnamed Tomb

Lies under the city of Feldgrau, has existed for a thousand years since the Tyrant was thrown down. Recently subject to major seismic events during a war with a neighboring province. The East hall is severely flooded. Protected by a Private Sanctum -- mortals who do not worship Pharasma, Saranrae, Torag, or Iomedae will find it very difficult to locate.
