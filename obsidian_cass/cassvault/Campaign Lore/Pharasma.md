# Pharasma

The Goddess of Death, Fate, Prophecy, and Birth. Central deity opposing the undead in the Carrion Crown campaign.

## Foundry Lore

### Dea Pharasma Nua
A temple or sacred site visited by CP-USS. The party received a blessing of Pharasma for 5 days, along with a vision of the peril of Northgarde.

### Role in the Crusades
Ustalav's armies were supported by the church of Pharasma during the [[Shining Crusade]]. The Pharasmin bishop Prince Adamondais Virholt bore the holy mace Raven's Head against [[Tar Baphon, The Whispering Tyrant|Tar Baphon]].

### Professor Lorrimor Connection
Professor Lorrimor noted that the Temple of Pharasma would have a list of everyone who died the night of the Harrowstone fire.

### Bat Symbolism
Unlike the birds and butterflies holy to Pharasma and Desna, bats are commonly associated with Urgathoa and blamed for spreading maladies.
