# Vaughn's Log

## Day 1

I am among pirates. This seems familiar. 

We have taken a second ship. This second ship is named The Man's Promise. We secured it in a battle. Its captain was beheaded, made into a trophy to secure the submission of his crew. This feels familiar, too. Familiar, and wrong. Still, it is effective.

I am to go with the trophy ship. It is Captained by a Mr. Plug. His first-mate is a Master Scourge. I can tell that my new friends hate these men. I think some bad shit happened to them at the hands of Plug and Scourge.

They tried to take the Sword. The Sword would not let them. They believe it is cursed. That doesn't feel accurate; unless the Sword didn't like you. Then there would be no greater curse in any Hell.

We should be going West. We are going Northwest. This was supposed to be a three-week trip to sell the ship and link up with the Wyrmwood after that.

I am told we are planning a Mayonnaise.

We believe that we are near Rickety Squib. It is northwest of us. Do Plug and Scourge know where they're going?

---

We spoke in the Bilges. It seems that Plug and Scourge are planning to steal this ship and make it their own. My new friends are not going to be a part of this new crew. They will be killed. 

Unless we kill them first.

There are whispers of whispers. Plans to align allies. We have two-to-three days before we arrive at Rickety Squib's. What a stupid fucking name. In two to three days, we will slay these two; or be slain by them.
