# Manifesto Mortiari

A philosophical text found in the Carrion Crown campaign, expounding the beliefs and worldview of [[The Whispering Way]] and [[Tar Baphon, The Whispering Tyrant]].

## Key Sections

### On the Whispering Way
Tar Baphon's cult has endured centuries without him. Their assets: virtually limitless funds, fanatical devotion to their secret cause, and knowledge. The [[Palatine Eye]] hunts them but does not commit everything against the cult. There are truly skilled and talented individuals in the group.

### On the Tyrant
Tar Baphon is described as the least trusting individual to ever exist. He takes the philosophy of distrust to an extreme -- the peasantry cannot be trusted, neither can anyone else.

### On the Lawbringers
The Pharasmans, Iomedaeans, followers of Abaddar, Hell Knights, etc. are all fervently pursuing 'Order' while simultaneously guaranteeing chaos in their realms. Where there is free-will, there is danger.

### On the Shining Crusade
Due to Tar Baphon's paranoia and planning, the [[Shining Crusade]] found his armor thick and seamless, his plans deep and confusing, and their offensives anticipated and weakened or halted for many years despite superior numbers.
