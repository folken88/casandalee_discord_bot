# Caliphas

The capital city of Ustalav, key location in the later books of Carrion Crown.

## Foundry Lore (Caliphas Information Journals)

**Restoration Park** presents a seemingly tranquil space within the bustling city, offering respite from the grim atmosphere of Ustalav. Standing Stones constructed by Merrick (who re-established Caliphas after the [[The Whispering Tyrant|Whispering Tyrant]]'s defeat) serve as reminders of the city's tumultuous past.

### History
- 4288 AR: Numerous blatant vampire attacks led to a widespread purge of the city's undead. Hundreds of vampires killed.
- 4670 AR: Weakness and mismanagement led to political instability.

### Key NPCs in Caliphas
- **[[Adivion Adrissant]]** -- The main villain, based in Caliphas
- **[[Luvick Siervage]]** -- Elder vampire ally, operates from Caliphas
- **Antoinette Borden** -- Connected to the Borden Family operations

### The Borden Family
A powerful family with holdings including:
- **Bloodkiss Winery**, east of Caliphas
- **Warehouse Company Ownership**: Controls three heavily guarded warehouses in the south docks

### The Job
A contract issued by Antoinette Borden in Caliphas -- 110,000 GP pay, deadline Sunrise Abadius 24th 4717.
