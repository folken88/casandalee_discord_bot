# Caromarc Family

A noble family of Ustalav, central to the Trial of the Beast arc of Carrion Crown.

## Key Members

- **[[Count Caromarc]]** -- The Count, creator of the Beast of Lepidstadt
- **[[Clarissa Ellsa Caromarc]]** -- The Count's daughter. Ghoran Druid 11/Barbarian 1. Level 12, HP 147. Carries a Starmetal Shard Spear and Warrant: The Whispering Way. Wild shapes into hawk, vegetiger, axolotyl, beastmode, and promethean forms.
