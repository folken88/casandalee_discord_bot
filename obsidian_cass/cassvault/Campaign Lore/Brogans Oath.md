# Brogan's Oath

## Brogan's Oath 01 - Coward

*I sleep through weakness, wake for the dour,
Brace the soldier, betray the coward.*

Your weapon grants you a constant +2 morale bonus vs Fear saves.

When you make a fear save from an enemy of at least your level, your weapon casts True Strike on you. This effect lasts for up to 2 rounds.

If you fail a fear save, this weapon auto-misses on a roll of 1-5 for 24 hours.

This axe functions as +1 magic per oath known by its wielder.

Every day, Brogan's Oath returns to masterwork unless the oath is repeated in it's entirety, one oath per round.

## Brogan's Oath 02 - All

*Where we fight together, our enemies fall
None can owe me, for I give all.*

You gain use of the feat: Intercept Charge while the weapon is in your hand. When this action is used for the first time in a given day, the weapon casts Longstrider on you with your level as caster level.

If your charge causes you to absorb damage that would have downed or killed an ally, the weapon casts Weapon of Awe at your level as caster level.

## Brogan's Oath 03

*Stone for the loyal, sand for the craven,
Oak for the battlefield, swaying grass for the haven.*

Any allies within your touch range may ask you to make a fort save on their behalf. They must accept your result. This power can be used once per day per weapon bonus.

## Brogan's Oath 04

*Weather fire, storm, and cold, 
Where cowards break, heroes hold.*

Once per day, the first time you are targeted by an attack that deals Fire, Electrical, bleeding or inflict-wounds damage, or bone damage such as Boneshaker , your weapon absorbs up to 20 points of this damage and becomes enchanted with that damage type for the next hour.

Fire -> 1d6 Fire Damage, 2d10 on confirmed crit.

Electrical -> 1d6 Shock Damage, 2d10 on confirmed crit.

Blood -> Vampiric, 1d4 negative energy damage which heals you, 2d4 on confirmed crit.

Bone -> Boneshattering 1d6 Force, 2d10 on confirmed crit.

## Brogan's Oath 05

*Sky Father, Earth Mother, 
Forge Wife, Soldier Brother. 
*
If any ally within 30 feet of you is knocked unconscious or killed, the weapon casts Haste on you for 2 rounds per weapon bonus. If you were already hasted, the weapon casts Dimension Door which can only be used to immediately move you closer to the downed ally.
