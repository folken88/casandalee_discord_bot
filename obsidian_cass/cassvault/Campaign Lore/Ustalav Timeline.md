# Ustalav Timeline

Key historical dates from the Foundry VTT "Ustalav Timeline" journal.

## Major Events

- **~3200 AR**: Tar Baphon returns to life as a Lich, the [[The Whispering Tyrant|Whispering Tyrant]]. He unites the orc tribes of Belkzen. A wizard called Socorro leads an uprising in Carrion Hill, butchering thousands in his name.
- **3204 AR**: Prince Ardurras II involved in ongoing conflicts.
- **3801 AR**: The [[Shining Crusade]] secures a beachhead in Ustalav at modern-day Vauntil.
- **~3827 AR**: The Crusade concludes. Ustalav's armies, supported by the church of [[Pharasma]], launched the crusade. Pharasmin bishop Prince Adamondais Virholt bore the holy mace Raven's Head.
- **3999 AR**: The Esoteric Order of the [[Palatine Eye]] is active. A mob in Vieland disrupts a midnight orgiastic rite.
- **4288 AR**: Numerous blatant vampire attacks in [[Caliphas]] lead to a widespread purge. Hundreds of vampires killed.
- **4670 AR**: Weakness and mismanagement lead to political instability.
