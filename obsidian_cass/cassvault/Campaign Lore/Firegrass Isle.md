# Firegrass Isle

## The Legend

Nearly 200 years ago, the Chelish navy managed to trap the infamous buccaneer Knotbeard in a long, narrow cove of an island far south, within spitting distance of the Fever Sea. Four ships faced off against his lone, battered barkentine, the *Mother o’ Seasons*. Knotbeard and his crew knew escape was impossible, so as soon as the full complement of Chelish marines swarmed aboard, the pirate captain tossed a torch into the hold. Within seconds, angry flames erupted from the portholes.

The marines made a desperate attempt to extinguish the blaze, but the fire spread with alarming speed. The inferno soon reached a crate of volatile alchemical powders, triggering an explosion that obliterated Knotbeard’s ship and three of the Chelish warships. The fourth ran aground, and the flames spread to the dry grass covering the rocky island, consuming every living thing.

Of the 17 Chelish sailors who survived the initial disaster, only four emaciated souls endured long enough to be rescued by a passing ship a week and a half later. This fierce and deadly battle earned the place its grim name: Firegrass Isle.

## Goatshead

Only 70 years ago, a trio of pirate captains settled on the island. These buccaneers resented even the minor restrictions imposed by the Pirate Council in Port Peril and quickly grew weary of adhering to its rules. What chafed them most was the anti-piracy pact with Sargava. With a small settlement established on Firegrass Isle, these captains and their crews began flagrantly violating the treaty.

Before long, the Council blamed them for all pirate misbehavior targeting Sargavan ships, far exceeding what their three ships could reasonably have committed. Made scapegoats for every Sargavan complaint, the captains embraced the insult, adopting a flag that displayed a white goat’s head on a black field. Their settlement took the name *Goatshead* in kind.

### Goatshead

The captains who founded the settlement on Firegrass Isle are long dead, but Goatshead continues to thrive. Today, three vessels call the port home, and the bustling town boasts a population of over 400 souls. It features three taverns, two inns, and half a dozen large warehouses. Merchants often arrive eager to barter for stolen Sargavan goods at bargain prices. Rumor has it that the Pathfinders are also interested in Goatshead as a potential base of operations, though their reasons remain unclear.

Goatshead is a rough-and-tumble place. Its “government” is summarized by a charter nailed with a dagger to the wall of Walleye’s Rum Room, the oldest tavern in town. The document, scrawled in the childish script of a semiliterate, reads:
*“No lawss, nor lords, evry won is hez oon mastr—We ar the lass baschun of freedym.”*
It is signed with three shaky X’s, a testament to the founders’ lack of education and frequent drunkenness.

The three captains who now rule Firegrass Isle are not much different from their long-dead predecessors.
