# Iomedae In A Fire

The team's destructive campaign continues through Kantaria: burning docks, rioting at markets, storming the North tower, ambushing militia patrols, burning the West Guard tower, burglarizing the labor guild, stealing artifacts from Narikopolus Manor, murdering prison guards and freeing inmates, and skinning the lady warden alive.

Summoned angels from heaven aid the Glorious Reclamation but are defeated or driven off. The team, exhausted and wounded, flees to plague-ridden caves after fireballs destroy the Uskwood.

While recovering, they continue operations: securing mines around Kantaria to cut off rebellion income, destroying a Lamashtu cult, assassinating key merchants and ship captains supporting the rebellion, attacking perimeter forts limiting rebel patrol range, defrauding and seducing a distant royal cousin, killing royal family members and their guards.

They reanimate Glorious Reclamation corpses as undead shock troops to assault Valor's Fastness. The exterior guards fall. The team kills their way through heroes, clerics of light, and paladins of Iomedae.

Reaching the tallest tower, they kill a powerful mage, battle-hardened war priest, and Angel Knight. Finally facing the Fourth Sword Knight and his Archon bodyguard, they nearly defeat him. He steps off the tower into open air, but they dive after him, saving his life, taking him prisoner, and crushing his rebellion.
