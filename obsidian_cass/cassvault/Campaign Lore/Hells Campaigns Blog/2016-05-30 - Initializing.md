# Initializing

The blog announces playing two simultaneous Pathfinder adventure paths with the same players, in the same country and timeframe, involving the same rebellion. One party supports the rebellion (Hell's Rebels), one supports Cheliax's government (Hell's Vengeance). Both begin in Kintargo.

## Hell's Vengeance Party (Government/Evil)

- **Freya** - LE Half-Elf Samurai/Noble
- **Jamal (J'Mal)** - NE Hobgoblin Rogue (Knife-Master)
- **Draymus Mortiar** - LE Dhampir Necromancer
- **El Guapo** - NE Human Swashbuckler/Pirate
- **Jason** - LE Tiefling (Oni-Spawn) Cleric of Asmodeus

## Hell's Rebels Party (Rebellion/Good)

- **Boudicca** - NG Half-Elf Magus (Kensai) - Freya's identical twin sister
- **Femick (Femmick)** - NG Ifrit Bard (Dawnflower)/Inquisitor (Infiltrator/Immolator)
- **Eol** - LN Samsaran Theurge/Monk
- **Azwraith** - NG Human Phalanx Soldier
- **Domli** - LN Dwarf Evoker

Both campaigns are set in Kintargo, Cheliax, during 4716 AR. The two parties exist in the same world at the same time, on opposite sides of a revolution.
