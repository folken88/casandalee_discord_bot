# Player-Written Backstory: Boudicca / Freya Kusanagi

Written by E. Williams, this character backstory details the birth and early childhood of twin half-elf sisters Boudicca and Freya Kusanagi, born to Solei (half-elf) and Noyuda (Tian) in Cheliax.

The narrative describes their infancy, their father's pride in having warrior daughters despite preferring sons, and their early sword training. It reveals growing tension between Solei and Noyuda regarding his involvement in Asmodeus-driven military campaigns, which Solei opposes on moral grounds.

## The Separation

The pivotal moment occurs when Solei, unable to convince her husband to abandon the cause, makes an agonizing choice: she flees with only Boudicca, abandoning Freya in her sleep. Solei hopes Noyuda will keep Freya's memory of her mother and twin alive, intending to eventually reunite them. She escapes into the woods with her handmaiden and a waiting carriage, unable to look back.

This backstory explains the central dramatic tension between both campaigns: Boudicca (HR, good-aligned Magus) and Freya (HV, lawful-evil Samurai) are identical twin sisters, separated as infants, now fighting on opposite sides of a revolution. One raised by their elven mother away from Cheliax, the other raised by their Tian father in service to Asmodeus.
