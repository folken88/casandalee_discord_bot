# Right Proudly High

Poetic verse heard among Kintargo patriots on the 25th of Neth, 4716. Celebrates the raising of the flag of war in Kintargo-town, preferring death under a Ravounel sky to imprisonment in the Holding House. Describes Silver Ravens emerging to fight while naval forces sailed through fog.

Part of the revolutionary poetry series marking the Battle for Kintargo.
