# This May Be the Last Time We Are Seen

Gabriel's inspirational speech to the Silver Ravens before the Battle for Kintargo. Delivered at the Opera House on the 25th of Neth 4716.

The speech references the historical Silver Ravens and their legacy of protecting Kintargo, calls for revolution against oppression, and concludes with a call to action against enemies at the Opera House.

Attributed to Gabriel, Son of Kintargo. This rallying cry precedes the final battle for the city's liberation.
