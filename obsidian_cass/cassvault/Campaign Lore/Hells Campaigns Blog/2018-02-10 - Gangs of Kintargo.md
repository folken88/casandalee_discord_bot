# Gangs of Kintargo

The city of Kintargo faces civil unrest following murders of Dottari officers. The Silver Ravens investigate clues suggesting a secret lair beneath the old museum "Hocum's Fantasmagorum" that belonged to the Sacred Order of Archivists. Rexus Victocora hopes his parents survive and provides a mithral key.

The team battles animated ape skeletons, scarlet spiders, undead mermaids, and zombies of burned Kintargo citizens. They discover a signet ring matching the Rexus family crest on one corpse, indicating victims were burned alive and animated.

The party defeats Asmodean Redactors led by Cleric Raul in the library section. After interrogation, Raul reveals a secret door beneath an Aroden statue leads to the sub-basement. He's allowed to leave under promise of departing Kintargo.

In the underground complex, they encounter a creature composed entirely of book pages who requests they not proceed. They defeat him and continue through the Sacred Order's halls, fighting more Redactors in running battles.

A meditation garden contains Nox, a half-demon half-human, serving as right-hand to Kintargo's lord. She possesses demonic strength, regeneration, and can teleport via black smoke. Despite her power, the team defeats her through coordinated effort, though she escapes via teleportation.

Rexus learns his parents were cruelly killed. He enters silent mourning for a day, then emerges transformed -- determined to bankrupt his family fortune and remove Thrune from his city. He invites the Silver Ravens to his parents' funeral.
