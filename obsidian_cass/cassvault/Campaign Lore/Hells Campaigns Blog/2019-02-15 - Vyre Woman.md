# Vyre Woman

The Silver Ravens' journey to the coastal city of Vyre while laying low from Thrune forces. They acquire the Lucky Bones as their new headquarters, forge alliances with the Acisazi Elves and Vyre leadership, rescue orphan property deeds, defend a temple, and conduct social maneuvering among noble circles.

Covers events from late September to early November 4716. The Silver Ravens expand their network and resources while the heat from their activities in Kintargo cools down.
