# Safe to Dance

The Silver Ravens rescue missing member Hetamon from a succubus demon (his mother), recruit new allies Morfak, Chucko, and Streya, and discover the Ruby Masquerade plot.

They explore secret areas beneath the Opera House including the Silver Vault, and rescue the petrified Shensen. The team develops elaborate planning for the Masquerade event using waterbreathing, infiltration, and secret tunnels.

This sets up the climactic Ruby Masquerade event at the Kintargo Opera House.
