# Boudicca's Dream

Boudicca aboard the *Scourge of Belial* just outside Vyre, wakes from this dream on the evening of the first of Neth (November), 4716.

A vivid dream sequence where Boudicca charges up a circular stone staircase while holding a bloody katana. She encounters an armored angelic figure wielding a light-wreathed longsword and battles her alongside her team, ultimately executing the opponent.

Continuing upward, she reaches a tower roof where she confronts a man in ornate armor gazing at a burning city below. Despite his magical defenses and guilt-stricken demeanor, she overwhelms him in combat. He jumps from the tower in despair, pursued by a dark-red shadowed ally attempting capture.

The dream ends with Boudicca observing her blood-spattered reflection in a mirror, her expression showing satisfied anger before she awakens.

This dream foreshadows the final battle at Valor's Fastness -- Boudicca is dreaming what her twin sister Freya experienced in the HV campaign's assault on the Fourth Sword Knight.
