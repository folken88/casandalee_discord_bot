# Friend Finder

Laria Longroad requests the team investigate her missing smuggler friend Nan Comerivos, who operates from the basement connected to an old hidden temple to Calistria.

Exploring underground, the team discovers a foul-smelling crossroads. A large water pool contains a bloated corpse against a small dock. A gigantic sewer crocodile erupts from the water attacking Azwraith's head. He instinctively drives his fist into its eye.

Eol casts Color Spray point-blank, stunning the beast. They defeat it and examine the corpse -- a human male missing one leg and arm, wearing magical Daredevil Boots. Boudicca guts the crocodile searching for the other boot. Femick wears them, improving his combat positioning for flanking.

Femick scouts ahead and encounters a dire corby. Three tengu appear, introduced by Korva as escaped slaves freed by Nan. A corby they freed became unstable and attacked Nan, killing him. They've been losing people to the crocodile slowly.

Korva and her tengu companions agree to help the team's rebellion against Thrune and slavery. The Silver Ravens begin organizing formally with officers and divided duties.

Returning to the coffeehouse, Femick diplomatically informs Laria that Nan was strangled by a corby and then eaten by a giant croc. She thanks them for discovering his fate and securing the basement.
