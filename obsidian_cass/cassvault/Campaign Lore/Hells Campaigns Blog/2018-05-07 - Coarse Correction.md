# Coarse Correction

Setrona Sabinus, owner of the Tooth and Nail bar, requests the Silver Ravens locate her missing cousin Octavio, highest-ranking Hellknight of the Order of the Torrent since the Night of Ashes. She believes him alive despite his disappearance. She reveals Barzillai Thrune outlawed the Order, seized their citadel, and arrested accessible members.

The team navigates Kintargo's new tax system on gates and bridges by hiring riverboat captain Hank Marlow, who cheerfully transports them across the Yolubilis River aboard his fishing vessel, the Fortune Star. They seek the *Scourge of Belial* at the docks, meeting first mate X.O., a half-elven gunslinger.

Captain Cassius emerges -- a former acquaintance from Rexus's parents' funeral. He agrees to secretly transport them south but requests a favor: delivering a discreet message across town tomorrow.

Rowed through the night to the Shrine of Saint Senex (patron of drowned sailors), they navigate past the shrine's guardians and descend iron ladders into underground chambers. They discover a room filled with dead sailors and one very-much-alive hellknight.

Octavio, employing constant lie detection as a Hellknight ability, trusts the party. He explains the Order of the Torrent rescued kidnapping victims -- their outlawing by Thrune is illegal, permitting him to partially disregard the unjust law. He proposes a deal: free his arrested hellknights from illegal detention, and the Order joins the Silver Ravens in resistance.

Intelligence-gathering proves difficult -- no records exist of named, ranked hellknights in official systems, suggesting illegal black-site incarceration. They eventually identify the Holding House, a secret Dottari prison in Southern Kintargo.

Femmik devises a plan using stolen identities "Captain Yuri" and "Corporal Foxworthy." He arrives at 1 PM announcing prisoner intake at 4 PM. At the appointed hour, Boudicca, Femmik, and Azwraith, disguised as Dottari, are led into the cell block where guards immediately club Femmik unconscious. Azwraith is subdued similarly.

Boudicca escapes into obscuring mist, reduces herself to one foot tall, and renders herself invisible, slipping through a cell port to hide. Extensive guard searches fail finding her. The party languishes in darkness with severely tortured civilians warning them about "she" -- the facility's torturer.

The torturer emerges: a tall, hairless woman with pale grey skin, numerous head spikes, twisting facial tattoos, and multiple daggers embedded painlessly in her body. She subjects Femmik and Azwraith to extended torture on a stretching rack, repeatedly healing them to repeat the process as she questions them within an aura of truth. They resist by remaining completely silent, denying her any answers.
