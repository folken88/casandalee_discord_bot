# Constructive Dismissals

The team delivers Archbaron decrees throughout Longacre, including one firing Sheriff Rhona Staelish. They attend a sermon where the priestess displays the Angel Knight's helmet and discusses rebellion.

The party disrupts the crowd through intimidation and persuasion. When delivering the dismissal, Rhona leaves quietly but four deputies ambush them outside the jail. The team defeats the deputies and confines them.

They advance to level 2 and prepare for a meeting with the Archbaron.
