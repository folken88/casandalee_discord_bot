# A Taste of Vengeance

The Ruby Masquerade event at the Opera House. The Silver Ravens make elaborate preparations, infiltrating as guests and guards. The Dance of the Damned competition takes place before Thrune forces disguised as false Silver Ravens launch a surprise attack.

Multiple combat scenes erupt across the Opera House as allies fight alongside the Ravens. Beneath the Opera House, they discover a vampire identified as the rightful Lord Mayor Jilia Bainilus, who is resurrected by the party.

The Song of Silver activation location is found. This event becomes known as the Ruby Massacre -- Lord Mayor Barzillai Thrune kills 101 citizens while two hundred are rescued by the Silver Ravens. The discovery of the true Lord Mayor negates Thrune's legal authority over Kintargo.
