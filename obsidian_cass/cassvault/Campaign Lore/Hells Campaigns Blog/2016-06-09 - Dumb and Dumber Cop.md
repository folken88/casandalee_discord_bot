# Dumb and Dumber Cop

The team visits Archbaron Fex and swears the Hellfire Compact. During an unusual oath-taking ceremony, the tiefling cleric Jason performs an unorthodox gesture that charms the Archbaron. Razelago reveals his true demonic form -- an accuser devil -- shocking the party.

They receive information about Riley Kels and Bolgart operating illegally. After confronting Bolgart at the tavern and capturing Riley, the team discovers Bolgart's wereboar curse when he transforms in the stocks.

The party spreads rumors about his curse while establishing an informant network at the docks.
