# Fuck-It List

Freya and her team receive a lieutenant colonel promotion and new mission from their queen. They're tasked with gathering rare ingredients to create a tathlum -- a weapon of unknown destructive power featuring enhanced variants of items historically used by Kellid Barbarians.

## Journey Through Nidal

The team travels north through Nidal, locating and defeating Cave-Giants. They pursue frost giants through mountains between Isger and Molthune, navigating past the Wight Mother's massive undead army and discovering an ancient dragon skeleton of apparent divine origin with its own magical suppression field.

## Negotiating with Frost Giants

After negotiating with the frost giant leader Skadi through formal debate, they agree to remove a nuisance in exchange for passage and artifacts. They retrieve a magically reinforced coffin from a remorhaz, discovering it contains **Lady Violetta Pavel**, a Nidalese vampire diplomat trapped in ice for nearly a year.

## Planning the Citadel Assault

The post concludes with the team planning an assault on Citadel Dinyar, which the Glorious Reclamation captured nearly a year prior. They've learned of a secret door from a surviving hellknight-signifier and hope to find imprisoned allies.
