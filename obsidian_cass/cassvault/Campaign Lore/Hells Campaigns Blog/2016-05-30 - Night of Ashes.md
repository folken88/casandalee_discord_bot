# Night of Ashes

The Hell's Vengeance party begins in Kintargo during martial law following a rebellion. Freya, a noble Samurai, receives orders for a covert operation alongside an Asmodean cleric. They assemble a team for the "Night of Ashes" -- coordinated strikes across the city.

Their specific target is a secret temple to Sarenrae beneath the Silver Star music shop owned by actress Shensen. They defeat the temple's inhabitants, including Viggo, a Dawnflower Swordmaster, and replace his scimitar with a rapier to obscure the evidence. The temple is dismantled and the building burned.

Freya arrives with guards to declare the fire accidental and collects 100gp payment from a contractor named "Nox," a female human wielding a Glaive.

From a safehouse, they watch three fires blaze across the city before being transported by covered wagon to *The Western Star* heading to Remesiana.

This is the pivotal event that begins both campaigns -- the Night of Ashes destroys multiple rebel safe houses and temples across Kintargo in coordinated strikes ordered by the new Lord Mayor Barzillai Thrune.
