# Barfight Stratagems

The good-aligned party in Kintargo is recruited by Femick to address goblin banditry threatening Sarenrae worshippers. After interviewing a survivor and bonding with mercenaries in a tavern brawl (which Femick breaks up in disguise), they locate the goblins' cave. They eliminate the goblin captain, an elderly former-slave hobgoblin.

Returning to Kintargo, they discover the Silver Star Music Shop burned to the ground with Viggo and other allies dead inside. The authorities deny access and call it accidental. This is the HR party's first direct encounter with the aftermath of the Night of Ashes.
