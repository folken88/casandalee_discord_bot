# The Audacity of Nope

The good-aligned party in Kintargo awakens to find their city transformed under new Lord Mayor Barzillai Thrune's rule. His proclamations include absurd prohibitions on specific items and animals. Protests erupt in Aria Park against increasingly restrictive decrees.

The party meets Rexus Victocora, who recruits them after they defend him from attackers. He offers what remains of his resources in exchange for assistance against the oppressive regime. This marks the formal beginning of the Silver Ravens resistance movement.
