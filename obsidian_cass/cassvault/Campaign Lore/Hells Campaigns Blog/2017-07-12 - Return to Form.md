# Return to Form

Kintargo's mood shifts as people grow restless under martial law's curfews and restrictions. Rumors spread that the Silver Ravens survived the Night of Ashes and fight injustice.

Hidden beneath Laria Longroad's Coffeehouse, the team plans carefully. Tengu spies report gang activity and troop movements. Rexus connects them with sympathetic shops for off-books purchases.

An oddity emerges: nightly brawls at Clenchjaw's bar, previously peaceful due to the owner's martial prowess. Strange mishaps -- tipping mugs, chairs leaning back unexpectedly -- lead to fights. They find the owner asleep and discover a hidden stash in the rafters. A faerie dragon named Vendelfek reveals himself, claiming the treasure as his own. His former home burned during the Night of Ashes, and he's invisibly causing mischief to combat boredom. The team invites him to join them at the Wasp Nest.

Laria reports a customer was robbed outside her coffeehouse by the Red Jills -- mostly female tieflings wearing red and black cloaks sometimes mistaken for Dottari. The team sets a trap, defeats them non-lethally, and learns they operate from connected rooftops.

Eol, Furion (a feral-shaman druid), and Gabriel (a towering man in plate armor with a greatsword, drawn by vision) assault the Red Jills lair. Gabriel falls thirty feet into an alley losing his sword. Celeb and Furion disable archers.

The Red Jills' leader Scarplume, a strix-sorcerer, devastates them with shocking grasp and scorching ray. Gabriel recovers and re-climbs. The team beheads Scarplume. Many Red Jills accept joining the Silver Ravens.

They "apprehend" Chelish Citizens Group thugs and discover a protection racket in Northwestern Kintargo. Femick impersonates Corporal "Jimmy Dux," learning Dottari methods and stealing over 2,000 gold. He and Boudicca return the money with Silver Raven marks.

Azwraith befriends dock workers and arranges shipping captured thugs and soldiers out on departing vessels.

More Dottari including Lieutenant Yuri Maru arrive at the coffeehouse searching for the secret door. The team ambushes and captures them, learning they received an anonymous tip. The Ravens realize they're watched and may have an internal enemy.
