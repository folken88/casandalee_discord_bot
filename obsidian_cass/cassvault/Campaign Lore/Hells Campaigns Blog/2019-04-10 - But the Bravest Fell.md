# But the Bravest Fell

Poetic verse whispered through smoke and tears by Ravounel patriots on the 25th of Neth, 4716. Reflects on brave warriors who died during the winter season's ending, their sacrifice enabling freedom's light to shine through darkness.

A memorial piece for those who fell in the Battle for Kintargo.
