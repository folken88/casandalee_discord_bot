# Down to Earth

The party advances on Fort Arego to confront a portal to Hell controlled by Archbaron Fex, who plans to sacrifice their souls to a devil named Visperthul. They battle Glorious Reclamation forces, descend into abandoned military dungeons encountering various infernal denizens, and locate the Inferno Gate surrounded by molten lava trenches.

Meeting Visperthul, they learn Fex intended betrayal of both them and the devil through a phlegmatic talisman. Visperthul proposes an alliance: defeat Fex and surrender the talisman, and he'll close the portal permanently.

Combat erupts as Fex arrives. The party faces confusion spells, stinking clouds, chain lightning, and mind domination. Reese, confronted with unconscious allies and controlled companions, seizes the talisman and jumps through the portal into Hell itself. There, his mind and body assaulted by infernal forces, he negotiates with Visperthul, swearing his party's unconditional fealty in exchange for aid defeating Fex.

Visperthul returns with Reese, summons bone devils, and together they overcome Fex's defenses. The powerful wizard dies. However, the party now faces consequences of their devil bargain, particularly regarding Reese's hastily signed contract. Corrine arrives from Egorian with a servant of the Queen as the Glorious Reclamation battalion attacks Fort Arego's upper levels.

Visperthul negotiates with Queen Thrune through intermediaries, agreeing to close the portal permanently if the party remains as his "servants" in Cheliax. They're teleported to Hotel Barato in the capital.

Jason reports to the Temple of Asmodeus and is stripped of his rank for betraying Asmodeus and Cheliax. Freya faces public discharge, stripped of military rank and Hellknight status.

During Freya's walk of shame through crowds throwing refuse, Hellknight Zara Orcelani intervenes, silencing the mob through supernatural command. When a tomato strikes both women, Zara executes the perpetrator with her bastard sword, demonstrating the absolute authority of her station. The party escapes to their cheap hotel.

The next day, Reese experiences visions of a fountain meeting where a stranger recounts a tailor's tale: her family will be murdered and displayed in her shop's window using her own garments by Thursday. The party, despite Jamal's reluctance, executes this dark plan before traveling to Remesiana to repay further debts.
