# Closing the Gap

The Silver Ravens' mission to prevent military reinforcements reaching Kintargo by destroying Menador Keep. They infiltrate via luxury cart disguise, navigate dwarven fortress defenses including an Iron Golem and Archons, and activate the fortress's self-destruct mechanism.

They escape and execute Lucian Thrune. Dated 10th-14th of Neth, 4716.

With Menador Keep destroyed, Thrune's ability to funnel reinforcements into Kintargo is severely compromised, setting the stage for the final confrontation.
