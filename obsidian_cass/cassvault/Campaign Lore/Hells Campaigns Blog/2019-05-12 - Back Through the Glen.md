# Back Through the Glen, I Rode Again

Poetic verse whispered through smoke and tears by Kintargo patriots on the 25th of Neth, 4716. Reflects on separation from valiant men and commitment to their memory despite never seeing them again, with themes of loss, prayer, and slavery's end.

Part of the revolutionary poetry series marking the Battle for Kintargo.
