# Chief Scout's Log

Military-style log entries from Chief Scout Korva Fushi covering November 24-25, 4716, documenting the Ruby Massacre and the battle's opening movements.

## November 24 - The Ruby Massacre

Lord Mayor Barzillai Thrune ordered the Dottari to seal Kintargo Opera House during a performance, resulting in 101 civilian deaths. Shensen was discovered petrified in the basement and freed. Lord Mayor Jilia Bainilus was discovered magically enslaved in an underground cavern, her release negating Thrune's authority.

## November 25 - Battle for Kintargo

The entries are organized geographically and chronologically, detailing:

- Five Chelish warships entering Kintargo Harbor with the *Scourge of Belial* refusing their flag signals
- Military redeployments between Kintargo Castle and Keep
- Dottari lockdown measures
- Various skirmishes in Old Kintargo including the deaths of two Silver Guard members
- The destruction of the *Scourge of Belial*
- Battles at multiple locations including the Holding House assault by Hellknights
- Naval engagement with mysterious magical forces
- The final confrontation at Aroden Plaza involving dragon attacks

A detailed tactical record of the Battle for Kintargo from the Silver Ravens' intelligence network perspective.
