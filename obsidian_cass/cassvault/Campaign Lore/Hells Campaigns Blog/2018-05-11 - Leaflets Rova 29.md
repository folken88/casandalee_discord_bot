# Kintargo Leaflets - 29th of Rova 4716

Image post depicting propaganda materials distributed in Kintargo on the 29th of Rova 4716. These leaflets are found by the thousand in the city streets before dawn. The Dottari collect and burn them, demanding that citizens surrender them if they are seen holding them.

Continued Silver Ravens propaganda campaign against Lord Mayor Barzillai Thrune's regime.
