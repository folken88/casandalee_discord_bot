# Thunderous Passing of Titans

Presented as excerpts from "Ravounel: A History," this post provides historical context for Kintargo's century-long struggle against Cheliax's Asmodean influence.

The narrative traces events from the initial swearing of allegiance to Asmodeus through the eventual independence achieved by the Silver Ravens. It describes how initial resistance in the north gradually transformed into organized rebellion.

The conflict between Hellknights and Iomedaean paladins over a sacred sword escalated into the "Glorious Reclamation" -- a force that marched through Cheliax while it struggled with internal panic and military reallocation.

## Lord Mayor Barzillai Thrune

His cruel reign in Kintargo is detailed, showing his initial charm -- enjoying opera and fine living -- contrasting sharply with his increasingly tyrannical curfews and public humiliations. The text describes his eventual descent into wanting to burn the city, his blue dragon, and finally his defeat by the combined forces of Silver Ravens and Hellknights of the Torrent, aided mysteriously by an angelic figure.

An in-world historical document providing the macro perspective on both campaigns' events.
