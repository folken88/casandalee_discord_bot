# Let's Start With Murder

A young Ifrit named Femick, orphaned and adopted by the underground church of Sarenrae, returns from an errand one day after the Night of Ashes to find his home destroyed. Among the charred remains he discovers his teachers and friends dead, including Viggo, his Dawnflower Swordmaster mentor. On Viggo's corpse, he finds a charred rapier instead of Viggo's signature scimitar.

Femick investigates and learns that a woman with long black hair and red eastern armor led city guards in fighting the fire, quickly declaring it accidental arson. His inquiries reveal no official record of this woman or the guards present that night, though the neighborhood remains under trespassing prohibition.

This is the Hell's Rebels perspective of the Night of Ashes -- Femick discovers the aftermath of the attack that the Hell's Vengeance party carried out. The woman in red armor is Freya, and the missing scimitar was swapped by the HV party to cover their tracks.
