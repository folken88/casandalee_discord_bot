# A Vacation from Virtue

Commander Freya Kusanagi and her team work as agents for the Chelaxian Queen. They infiltrate criminal organizations, battle corrupt nobles, attend high society events including the Night of the Pale Party, and duel Hellknight Vokadus Fex.

Their adventures span across Cheliax including stops in Belde, Hinji, Corentyn, and Pezzack. The HV party operates as a covert spy team carrying out the Queen's will across the nation while the Silver Ravens prepare for their final stand in Kintargo.
