# Mid-day Cowboy

Following Oppian's capture and the Kantaria Rebellion's destruction, Zara Orcelani rides to the capital to craft Freya's Hell Knight Plate. Archbaron Fex promises to deliver the war criminal within five days.

The team transports Oppian via carriage down the South Road. Freya leads on horseback while Bob and El Guapo drive, with Jason, J'Mal, Draymus, and Corrine inside. Reese flies overhead watching.

A roadblock appears with eight Glorious Reclamation soldiers, four mounted Sword Knights, and two Iomedaean Clerics. Draymus and Jason respond with fireballs killing soldiers. Freya charges the knights on her war horse. Reese eliminates both clerics with arrows. The party continues south.

Reaching Egorian mid-morning, they deliver Oppian to Dottari guards. Eight Egorian Knights on war horses escort them through Fort Adivian. Jason resupplies at the Midnight Temple, the greatest temple to Asmodeus on the material plane.

At Falco Manor in Thorntown, Lady Tria Falco welcomes them. Freya insists the party sleep together for watch rotation.

J'Mal and El Guapo investigate a crooked city guard from a previous visit who was murdered, likely by Red Mantis Assassins. J'Mal contacts his mentor Jaquen, who has captured the guard alive. Jaquen offers a contract: kill a minor noble bard at the scheduled public execution for 5,000gp, with 5,000gp bonus for public execution.

Freya and Jason testify at Oppian's six-hour trial. He's declared guilty. Zara escorts Freya to Thumbtown's blacksmith district, revealing the adamantine Hell Knight plate she crafted is complete. The armor fits perfectly. The smith reveals Zara worked over 20 hours daily for five days, rarely requesting food or water. He names it "Zara Geitta," meaning "Zara watches over you" in his native language.

Draymus searches poorer districts seeking vagrants, finding two. He casts Phantasmal Killer on one, who dies of fright, then raises him as a zombie puppet named "Joe Kek," commandeering an antique wheelchair as disguise.

At the public execution, J'Mal and El Guapo locate a young noble suspected of rebel sympathy. J'Mal becomes visible and beheads him with both sawtooth sabers. Four bodyguards with shocking grasp enchanted longswords attack. Three strikes devastate J'Mal before he vanishes ethereally.

Freya arrives and kills two bodyguards. Draymus holds the fourth with Hold-Person. El Guapo executes him. Jason stabilizes and heals J'Mal. Freya manages the Dottari response by framing victims as rebel sympathizers planning disruption.
