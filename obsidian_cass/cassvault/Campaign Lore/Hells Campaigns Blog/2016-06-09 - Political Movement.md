# Political Movement

The team investigates the open Western Gate and battles a protective fey creature called an Ovinnik. Later, someone attacks the jail and frees Riley Kels while nearly killing Cimri.

The Archbaron orders executions. The team arrests Bo Gramel and schedules her execution. During the public execution, rioters attack while a figure in silver plate armor -- revealed as Riley Kels in disguise -- appears claiming divine support.

The party crushes the rebellion and displays both Bo and Riley's severed heads publicly. The HV party firmly establishes itself as enforcers of Thrune's will in Longacre.
