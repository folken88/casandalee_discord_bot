# Killing Strangers

Freya's team continues completing an assassination list of ritual components. After escaping a forest with four stolen unicorn horns, they return to Senara to regroup and identify next targets.

En route, they encounter a Glorious Reclamation prison wagon demanding contraband inspection, resulting in brief brutal combat. They defeat a Hound Archon and multiple paladins, freeing the prisoner -- a powerful Magus named Orn from Nidal -- who joins their cause.

Upon reaching Senara, they notice increased guard patrols. Consulting the Black Dagger thieves guild for reconnaissance, they confirm an angel resides in the local graveyard.

They assault the cemetery, defeat the angel, and remove its heart. This triggers Glorious Reclamation alert, placing the city under total lockdown. The team now has unicorn horns and an angel's heart for their dark ritual.
