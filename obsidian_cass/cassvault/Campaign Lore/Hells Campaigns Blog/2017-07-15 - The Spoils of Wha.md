# The Spoils of Wha?

Kantaria's docks remain damaged from the rebellion. Valor's Fastness, the temple to Iomedae, has been desecrated. The townsfolk turn to Freya's team for leadership. Linton Demeer becomes the new mayor, reforming the city council.

Razelago informs the party that Archbaron Fex is pleased, and the prisoner's public execution will reflect well on everyone. Each team member receives generous rewards plus 10,600gp back pay.

**Rewards:**
- **Draymus**: Ring of Wizardry Type 1, increasing daily first-level spells from six to ten.
- **J'Mal**: Mosswater Tunic, a +3 studded leather tunic providing five rounds of ethereal jaunt daily.
- **Reese**: Silent Sentry, a +2 frost bow with built-in magical ammunition and ability to create silent sentry posts marking passage through designated areas.
- **Jason**: The Dragon Helm, a former possession of a powerful tiefling cleric. Functions as headband of wisdom +2 and provides three daily uses of rod of lesser-maximize or extension.
- **Freya**: Custom adamantite armor crafted from liberated ore, fitted exclusively to her form. Excellent AC, damage reduction, modest dexterity bonus, minimal armor check penalty with full mobility.

Loredana Viorica collects her father's insurance on the Uskwood and returns to Nidal. She signs over the property deed to Freya, J'Mal, Jason, and Draymus as co-owners.
