# Unrest for the Wicked

Kintargo, known as the "Silver City," faces increasing murders and disappearances under martial law. Citizens experience fires, killings, gang wars, and taxation without government protection beyond curfews and regulations.

Femick infiltrates the Dottari as Lieutenant Yuri, learning that the Hellknight Order of the Rack has occupied parts of the keep under Paracount Thrune's direction. He files reports claiming the Longroad Coffeehouse investigation proved fruitless and steals patrol route information.

Rexus arranges gear supply through Falco Steel, meeting owner Falco and his granddaughter Lore, who performs smithing while Gramps enchants items.

The team targets a salt granary holding dissidents as slave labor. Boudicca spots someone following them. A figure identifying herself as "the Silver Raven" appears, sharing their liberation goals. She joins their efforts.

They storm the granary building, with Femick and the Silver Raven engaging front guards while Gabriel and Azwraith block the barracks hallway. The dwarven leader Kossrani is killed. Prisoners are freed and Laria helps find them safe shelter.

The next day, the Chelish Citizens Group plans public execution of Clenchjaw at an old fountain. Gabriel, Boudicca, and Celeb intervene. Celeb casts sleep on outer thugs while Boudicca uses color-spray. Gabriel appeals to the crowd for mercy, calming them. Clenchjaw is healed and freed.

During a planned Dottari patrol robbery, Boudicca kills one officer and others die attempting escape. The team flees without collecting stolen money, triggering massive lockdown. Femick goes undercover as Lt. Yuri again, manipulating patrol assignments to protect the Longroad Coffeehouse neighborhood where Laria operates.
