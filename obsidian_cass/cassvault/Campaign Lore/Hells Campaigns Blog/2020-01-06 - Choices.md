# Choices

The HV party becomes "Trusted Agents of Thrune" and receives missions in Cheliax's capital, Egorian.

## The Noble Game

They receive an unwanted assignment: befriend two competing nobles -- **Archcountess Levisvia Vasvion** and **Paracount Thalgano Sethic** -- vying for the queen's favor. After mixed results with odd jobs, the team accelerates their plan. They side with Vasvion, systematically destroying Sethic's businesses, exposing corrupt judges, preventing an assassination attempt on Vasvion's fiance Nicolo Alazario, and ultimately driving Sethic underground.

## Crushing the Bellflower Network

They create an elaborate trap using a rented river-barge advertised as an all-night party with a halfling fighting ring. When actual halfling liberators arrive to free captives, a desperate battle ensues, forcing the team to escape using a recall word and regroup at their hideout. They subsequently summon earth elementals to level a Bellflower safehouse.

## The Dark Pilgrimage

With the nobles pacified, the queen rewards them with authority and a new task: a ritual pilgrimage to sacrifice a "friend of the queen" to Asmodeus at standing stones in the Barrowood. The team selects Sethic, infiltrates his mansion, discovers he's tortured a man named Bob Applewood, and after an intense battle, captures Sethic.

They perform the pilgrimage while facing attacks from Glorious Reclamation scouts and rogue Asmodean clerics. Jason dies during the final confrontation but is resurrected by the pit fiend **Gorthoklekk**, who appears when the sacrifice is completed. The queen herself arrives to thank them, rewarding them with promotion to Bound Agents of Thrune and magical equipment.

## The Turning Point

The Glorious Reclamation has taken Westcrown, and the Silver Ravens have killed Lord Mayor Barzillai Thrune in Kintargo, threatening to declare independence. The team prepares for a confrontation with the rebels -- setting up the endgame collision between both campaigns.
