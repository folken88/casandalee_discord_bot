# The Battle for Kintargo

The historically-styled narrative describes the Battle for Kintargo following the Ruby Massacre, when Lord Mayor Barzillai Thrune killed 101 citizens while two hundred were rescued by the Silver Ravens.

## The Morning After

Citizens communicate through shuttered windows while Dottari patrols increase. Citizens plan to gather at Aroden Plaza, hoping to make their voices heard despite military mobilization and bridge closures.

## Confrontations Across the City

- **Captain Hank Marlow** defended by mysterious sea elves
- Patrons of the **Tooth and Nail Tavern** scattered by stink bombs
- **Laria Longroads** rescued by a seven-foot wolf-man wielding a gold and silver blade
- **Captain Sargaeta** of the *Scourge of Belial* dies as the ship burns to the waterline under dragon fire

## The Silver Ravens Attack

The Ravens take Bleakbridge and reveal the Hellknights of the Torrent as allies. The Ravens protect the Alabaster Academy from a fire that's quickly extinguished.

The Chelish Navy intervenes but finds their ships targeted by sea creatures, lightning, and mysterious forces -- including references to a "great grey whale" and an orca.

## Lord Mayor Jilia Bainilus Returns

Lord Mayor Jilia Bainilus reveals herself at protests and helps calm the crowd before violence escalates further.

## The Final Confrontation at Aroden Plaza

The Ravens stand with the Lord Mayor, Hellknights, and unexpected allies including the Queen of Delights and "Bujon: Storm of Cheliax."

An angelic figure manifests, transforms into Gabriel, and together with Azwraith, they lead the charge. Despite dragon attacks and spellcaster barrages from Asmodean forces, the Ravens prevail.

## Casualties

- Frisky Jim and Zea from the orphanage are killed
- Rivozair retreats to the Temple of Asmodeus
- Thrune forces withdraw, leaving Kintargo in the hands of its citizens and the Silver Ravens

The culmination of the Hell's Rebels campaign -- Kintargo is liberated.
