# In The Shit

The evil-aligned party disembarks from a pleasure cruise at Port Remesiana and travels to Longacre. Their first mission involves robbing the Louslik Tannery. Jamal immerses himself in a waste pit for cover but is discovered. The team uses diplomacy rather than violence to secure the payroll.

They meet their mysterious employer Razelago as a silhouette. That night, Jamal encounters a drowned ghost in the bathroom -- an ectoplasmic undead woman created by tragedy.

Razelago assigns them to disrupt a rebellious sermon. The HV party begins their work as agents of Archbaron Fex in Longacre.
