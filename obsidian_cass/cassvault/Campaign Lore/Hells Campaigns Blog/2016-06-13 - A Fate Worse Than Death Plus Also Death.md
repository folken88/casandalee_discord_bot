# A Fate Worse Than Death, Plus Also: Death

This post documents the HR campaign's beginning in Kintargo. The party investigates the Fair Fortune Livery, discovering murdered workers animated as undead in lye pits. They recover supplies and Silver Raven figurines, learning the rebellion is severely damaged.

Rexus introduces them to Laria Longroad, who offers refuge in exchange for clearing underground tunnels beneath her coffeehouse. The team advances to level 2.

The Silver Ravens begin to take shape as a formal resistance organization, operating from the hidden "Wasp Nest" beneath Laria Longroad's Coffeehouse.
