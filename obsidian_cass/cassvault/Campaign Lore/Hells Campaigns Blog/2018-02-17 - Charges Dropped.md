# Charges Dropped

Prosecutors investigating J'Mal's involvement in Duke Fellatio's death see their case collapse when all witnesses disappear or perish in fire on a single night. A mistrial motion is denied by Judges of Asmodeus, settling the matter. Freya receives orders from Razelago, Archbaron Fex's right-hand devil, departing with her team for the river town of Senara.

The team understands their mission: collect ritual components their benefactor claims will close an errant Hell portal in the Whisperwood near Senara. An uncontrolled portal permitting unrestricted devil access troubles even Thrune's government. Fex believes eliminating this danger will please the Queen.

Fex has already determined these components exist locally, partly thanks to Glorious Reclamation presence. An Infernal Talisman he left at his Senara vacation home was previously stolen in burglary. The team settles into the Fex residence and begins hunting the local thieves guild.

El Guapo throws a house party, inviting mid-ranking rebel garrison members. Draymus and Jason conduct stealth operations on a local orphanage -- former thieves guild location -- spotting three orphans who subsequently vanish.

They locate the Black Dagger thieves guild beneath the city via sewers. Through surprise attacks (Glitterdust and Black Tentacles spells), they overcome the guild, securing the returned talisman and coercing them into regular intelligence reports for Thrune.

The party visits the nearby Hellknight Citadel, pays respects, learns new spells, and gathers Whisperwood intelligence.

Freya decides to assault the unicorns next. The party travels in their upgraded black carriage pulled by four war-horses, with Bob as driver. While camping, Whisperwood dangers attack -- spiders, ettercaps, giants, and bears, with one bear nearly fatally mauling Bob before J'Mal and El Guapo rescue him.

Finding the unicorn "Blessing," they assault the ancestral glade, killing three unicorns. They camp there and face united counterattack from surviving unicorns, stone giants, owlbears, and swan maidens. Forced to retreat, J'Mal combat-loots a unicorn head, extracting its horn to reach four horns total -- completing a second objective. Next, they hunt angel hearts.
