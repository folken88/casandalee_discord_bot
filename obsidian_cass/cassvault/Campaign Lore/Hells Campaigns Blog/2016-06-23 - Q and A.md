# Q & A

The party thwarts thieves at Longacre Armory while a fire breaks out at the jail. El Guapo arrives and intervenes, discovering two women fighting inside -- Rhona and Cimri Staelish.

After Rhona escapes, the party investigates Fort Estazano, where they encounter a halfling archer and a manticore guardian. Following a secret path into the Whisperwood forest, they discover "The Court of Spears," a hidden settlement used by the Glorious Reclamation rebels.

The session concludes with the party preparing for an assault on the Court of Spears.
