# Postcards from a Bloodbath

Following their previous victory, the team handles cleanup and administrative duties. Jason becomes the new church pastor and removes religious artifacts. Cimri Staelish is promoted to sheriff. Jamal completes Red Mantis Assassin training.

Doctor Rohalendi warns them of suspicious illegal activity. The party sets up an ambush at the armory, where they encounter Bo and Dent Gramel leading rebels attempting a heist. After defeating the group, the wrongly accused elderly woman is now rightfully branded a traitor.

The HV party consolidates power in Longacre, installing loyalists in key positions.
