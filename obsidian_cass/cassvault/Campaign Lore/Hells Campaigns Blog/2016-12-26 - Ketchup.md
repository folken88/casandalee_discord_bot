# Ketchup

Field logs from Captain Freya Kusanagi detail recent operations. The Kintargo operation succeeded with all targets eliminated and no losses. She relocated following completion.

Arriving in Longacre on 8 Arodus 4716, Freya began working for Archbaron Fex. She identified and publicly punished Glorious Reclamation agents, learning they'd secured a ruined fort outside town.

Investigating rebel shipments revealed their true base: "The Court of Spears," an old druidic tree-town. The assault killed all rebels except "Angel of Longacre" Lencia Visserene, a Paladin of Iomedae with winged armor featuring feather-fall capability.

The operation resulted in over thirty rebel deaths, twelve captured and punished, with a handful escaping. Archbaron Fex granted four weeks' furlough before assigning her to Kantaria.

While traveling north, bandits with a Glorious Reclamation letter of marque attacked. Their leader "Sancho" revealed they were funded to target government caravans and military groups. The threat was eliminated.

In Egorian, armed robbers specifically targeted the team at their inn. Five were killed, one escaped. A staff member who scouted the team was also missing. The inn owner comped their bill due to staff involvement.

By 25 Rova, Freya was officially hired as security for Loredana Viorica at the Uskwood Inn. Initial rebel force assessments identified three guard towers, Valor's Fastness temple, Narikopolus Manor, and Hardship's Hearth prison.

Investigation revealed the imprisoned HVT was Hell Knight Zara Orcelani. She was extracted and hidden at the Uskwood. Kantaria Docks was destroyed, eliminating a major rebellion income source with expected two-month repair time. The South Guard Tower was damaged with expected two-week repair time.
