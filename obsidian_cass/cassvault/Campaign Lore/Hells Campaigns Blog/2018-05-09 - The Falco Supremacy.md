# The Falco Supremacy

Lore Falco orchestrates an elaborate prison rescue during Kintargo's martial law. The city suffers under Dottari patrols, mandatory bridge taxes, and mass arrests. Falco's imprisoned friends motivate desperate action.

She begins at the city library researching the Holding House Prison's architecture, studying blueprints with assistance from an old soul trapped in a young man's body who remembers rather than learns history.

Using charm magic, she captures and interrogates Guard Captain Durl Haggard, extracting detailed facility knowledge while maintaining his compliance through wine and manipulation. She researches municipal records for days, identifying prisoner serial numbers and locating missing team members including a kensai who evaded capture for three days.

Falco creates elaborate false documentation: transfer requests, duty rosters, and release forms, using stolen official seals and artificially aging the papers. She assembles allies for coordinated action. Tengu scouts organize a massive anti-curfew protest in the guard district, occupying reinforcement attention. A nobleman leads eight hundred angry citizens in thundering demonstration.

Falco constructs fake Dottari uniforms, disguising herself as Captain Haggard using padding, prosthetics, makeup, vocal mimicry, and posture training. She and her corporals approach the Holding House, bully sentries, manipulate the desk sergeant, and gain cell block access through authoritative deception.

They discover systematic torture of prisoners, including daily cycles of near-lethal injury followed by magical healing. Falco drops guards with sleep magic and begins liberation. She finds her three imprisoned friends and four additional hellknight captives. She also discovers numerous tortured civilians requiring rescue. She distributes healing potions, air-bubble enchantments, and portable weapons she personally assembled and disassembled from her armor.

When guards return, violent combat erupts. She executes opponents with perfectly-thrown silver daggers while her corporals engage with collapsible weapons. They battle the facility's torturer -- a terrifying devil with pale grey skin, protruding spikes, twisting facial tattoos, and multiple self-embedded daggers.

After defeating the devil, they raid the office for confiscated items and weapons. Fighting their way out against desk staff and hellknights, they doff disguises and burn uniforms as evidence.

The nobleman's eight hundred citizens surround them as they exit, providing camouflage. Every freed prisoner receives cloaks and protest signs, merging seamlessly into the mob. After one block, they divert into a sympathetic bar where a warm meal, wine, and beds await ten people. The proprietor comments, "I love it when a plan comes together."

After settling freed prisoners and destroying evidence, Falco relaxes with drink and bath, contemplating her next project.
