# Excerpt from Finagle's Artifact Almanac

Documents the Admiral's Jacket worn by Femmik Embersword during the Battle for Kintargo in 4716. The artifact initially appeared unremarkable despite bloodstaining, but after cleaning, its green color darkened to blue matching Kintargo Bay waters, and it lengthened into a full Admiral's Coat. Two new buttons subsequently appeared.

The entry notes that successive owners observed the cufflinks bearing the Dawnflower symbol, suggesting later modification. Though experts credit Calvin Carpenter Leatherworks for the accompanying Admiral's Bicorn Hat, believers maintain the Dawnflower herself changed the sigils in recognition of Embersword's heroism and faith during that period.
