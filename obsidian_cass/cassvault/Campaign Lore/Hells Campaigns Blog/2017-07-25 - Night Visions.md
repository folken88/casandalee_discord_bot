# Night Visions

A vivid nightmare from Femick Embersword's perspective describes watching Kintargo burn. Multiple fires consume the docks, shops, and his beloved establishment -- "the Badger." He dives from a four-story balcony, glides to escape walls, then sprints through neighborhoods toward the fire.

Finding the Badger consumed in flames, Femick crosses the river and races toward the southern fires near the opera house. Dottari soldiers with Thrune sashes approach. Femick's scimitar "Stirling" quickly kills two and wounds a third. When asked where Thrune is, the soldier spits that he's at the opera house.

At the opera house, Femick encounters Dottari with crossbows on balconies. He transforms Stirling into a longbow, fires burning silver arrows eliminating six guards. The weapon returns to sword form, then splits into two.

More guards emerge. A demonic woman -- Thrune's bodyguard -- charges with a glaive. She possesses devastating strength, destroying seating with each blow. Femick feints, drawing her lunge, then his blade passes through her heart.

The Count stands on a balcony, casting a restraining spell that paralyzes Femick. Wings flap behind him. Something sharp strikes his leg, hip, and neck. Heat drains away, replaced by numbing cold. His heartbeat slows and stops.

Femick wakes in panic, checking that his heart still beats.
