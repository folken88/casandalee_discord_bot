# Kintargo Leaflets - 12th of Rova 4716

Image post depicting propaganda materials distributed in Kintargo. These leaflets are found by the thousand in the city streets before dawn on the 12th of Rova 4716. The Dottari collect and burn them, demanding that citizens surrender them if they are seen holding them.

Part of the Silver Ravens' propaganda campaign against Lord Mayor Barzillai Thrune's regime.
