# Catalyst

A funeral for Rexus Victocora's parents, Porcia and Aneran, occurs on their family's tiny island in Kintargo bay on a cloudy, misty morning. The island, barely two hundred feet wide, features a single stone memorial platform with four ten-foot pillars carved with ancestral names. The Silver Ravens attend as observers, positioned vigilantly around the island's perimeter.

Upon arrival, Caleb detects magic revealing the island exists under "Hallow," creating a constant zone of truth. A woman named Abigail plays ancient stringed instruments, singing mortality laments as the ceremony begins. Gabriel and Furion -- contrasting in shining gilded plate versus tattooed bare-chested druid -- carry clay urns containing ashes.

Gabriel removes his gauntlet, slices his palm, and drips blood into urns while speaking a celestial prayer. Furion kneels, drives his hands into bare ground, and concentrates intensely. Island vegetation sways in unison as roots pierce the urns, absorbing ashes as two saplings explosively grow to two feet, sprouting tiny leaves. Furion speaks in Druidic, committing the bodies to the earth.

Rexus honors his parents' city service and generosity, openly wearing a Silver Raven pin. He proclaims all present are friends -- thus enemies of the current regime -- and introduces important contacts:

- **Laria** introduces **Jonas Reikman**, appearing as river pirate but actually from Kantaria, where he and his employer **Oppian Nevilindor** (Fifth Sword-Knight of the Glorious Reclamation) seek revolutionary salvation from Thrune influence.
- **Clenchjaw**, local bar-owner and bouncer, mingles with Zea. Those familiar with him detect subtle faerie dragon Vendelfek.
- **Captain Cassius Sargaeta** of the pirate-hunting *Scourge of Belial* arrives -- respected throughout Cheliax but confined to his ship by Thrune's eighth proclamation prohibiting non-native captains from leaving vessels. He suggests the Silver Ravens run "errands" in the city.

Rowing toward shore, the party witnesses Dottari arresting Clenchjaw, Zea, and other attendees. The Ravens respond instantly and violently. Furion erupts from water into the street as Caleb casts Bull's Strength. Gabriel receives Enlarge Person, rising nearly twelve feet tall, drawing an enormous greatsword and roaring at guardsmen.

Boudicca casts Glitterdust, blinding and coating Dottari in sparkles. Furion and Gabriel devastate opponents. Boudicca freezes an apparent leader with Frigid Touch. Azwraith controls the cramped battlefield with polearm positioning.

Fleeing guardsmen are intercepted by Gabriel crashing through abandoned buildings. Caleb casts Grease on Clenchjaw's shackles, freeing him to pummel guards. Azwraith disguises the scene with false clues implicating invented local gangs. The Silver Ravens return to their secret hideout -- the Wasp Nest beneath Laria Longroad's Coffeehouse.
