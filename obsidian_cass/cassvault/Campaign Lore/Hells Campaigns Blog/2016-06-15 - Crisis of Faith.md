# Crisis of Faith

The team discovers missing executed prisoners and severed heads, leading them to the Church of Iomedae. They breach beautiful stained glass windows depicting sacred acts and engage multiple defenders inside.

A resurrection spell transforms Riley Kels into a ghast. The party defeats Sword Knight Allamar and her forces. When they destroy the altar, a shadowy figure emerges -- a trapped warrior who offers either death or knowledge as payment.

Jamal accepts training as a Red Mantis Assassin from this mysterious figure. This marks the beginning of J'Mal's path to becoming a dual-wielding sawtooth saber assassin.
