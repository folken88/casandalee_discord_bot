# Pirates and Old Ladies

The HV party travels aboard *The Western Star* to Pezzack, where they fence stolen musical instruments from the Silver Star shop. El Guapo, a pirate party member with bounties in Chelish ports, is recognized and ambushed by three thugs while weaponless -- Jamal intervenes and saves him. Confined to the ship, the party encounters desperate pirates trailing their vessel.

They feign weakness, lure the pirates into boarding, and defeat them in combat, killing the enemy captain. Operating both ships with skeleton crews, they sail to Corentyn where they successfully trade the captured pirate ship to the Chelaxian government, turn in prisoners for bounty money (planting the stolen Scimitar of Sarenrae on the pirate captain's corpse as evidence), and purchase superior equipment.

They sail toward Remesiana to meet contact Cimri Staelish for work under someone called Razelago.
