# Esoteric Order of the Palatine Eye

A secret society in Ustalav that opposes [[The Whispering Way]].

## Foundry Lore

The Palatine Eye hunts the Whispering Way but does not exhaust every single resource against the cult (per the Manifesto Mortiari).

Active since at least 3999 AR per the Ustalav Timeline. [[Judge Embreth Darymid]] appears connected to the Order.

A **Manual of the Palatine Eye** exists as a journal entry in the Foundry world.
