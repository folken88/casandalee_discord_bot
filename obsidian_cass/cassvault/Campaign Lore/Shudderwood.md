# Shudderwood

A vast forest in Ustalav, home to multiple werewolf tribes. Central to Book 3 of Carrion Crown.

## Foundry Lore (Shudderwood Lore Journal)

The assassination of the werewolf packlord by **Mathus Mordrinacht** and [[The Whispering Way]] threw all the tribes of the Shudderwood into disarray, not just the Vollensag.

### Werewolf Tribes

**The Prince's Wolves** -- Originally tasked by a prince to rid his lands of agents of the [[The Whispering Tyrant|Whispering Tyrant]] centuries ago. Maintain a tight pack structure, spend time in small family groups performing or pickpocketing.

**The Blackwoods** -- Led by [[Kate Blackwood]]. Have regular dealings with Ardis, [[Caliphas]], Courtaud, and Lepidstadt by way of river trade. Offer safe-escort through Shudderwood to travelers or visitors to Ascanor Lodge. Spend most of their time in full wolf form.

### Key Events
- **Demon Wolves** sighted in the Shudderwood
- **Thundering Tyrant** sighted in West Shudderwood near the standing stones
