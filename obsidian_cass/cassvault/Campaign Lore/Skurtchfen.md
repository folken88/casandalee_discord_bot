# Skurtchfen

A swamp settlement of goblins and swampfolk, relevant to the Carrion Crown campaign.

## Foundry Lore

**Population:** ~150 goblins and swampfolk hybrids. Subject to magical fluctuations common in the area even before [[The Whispering Way]]'s intrusion.

### The Carrion Crown

Skurtchfen contained a **partially completed Carrion Crown** -- the proto-phylactery vessel intended to transform a mortal into an unliving tyrant. A hybrid crown of bone, alchemical toxin, and preserved organ. Even incomplete, the Crown leaks necrotic essence.
