# Campaign SS Session Summary: "Shackles - Trap Cards"

The party's day began productively as they completed their rat-extermination duties in the ship's bilges and collected their payment from Sha-Feng and Croc at the quartermaster store. With coin in hand, the crew outfitted themselves with essential gear including bucklers, studded leather armor, rope, and potions. Meanwhile, Ser-Toche cultivated a relationship with Ambrose Kroop by performing a Chinese poem about wine and blossoms, successfully charming the quartermaster. The crew also discussed recent activities, including the capture of a Chelish-bound Sargovan vessel, though some crew members made disparaging comments about the Sargovan people.

Emboldened by their newfound resources and growing familiarity with the ship, the party began investigating the officers' quarters with an eye toward theft. Ser-Toche scouted the area while Holden spent 20-30 minutes carefully picking the lock to Scourge and Kipper's quarters using sticky tar from the ship's boards to manipulate the lock pins. However, their reconnaissance proved dangerous—upon entering the room and attempting to disable a trapped chest, a spring-loaded harpoon trap fired, dealing significant damage and constitution loss to Holden. Simultaneously, Josh's character triggered a poison trap while picking a lock in the captain's quarters, suffering devastating poison damage that reduced him to unconsciousness and death.

With two party members down, Ser-Toche created a desperate distraction by performing an elaborate song and dance on a table, drawing crew attention away from the officers' quarters. Under cover of this performance, Riviera successfully infiltrated the officer's room to rescue the wounded Holden, administering a potion to stabilize him. The party also discovered a wounded Tengu with a harpoon embedded in its body; they carefully removed the weapon by cutting the rope and pulling it through, stabilizing the victim with another potion.

As the party attempted to conceal evidence of their activities and escape with their discoveries, they encountered Slippery Syl Lonegan, who confronted them about being in the "monster sculptures room." When Riviera's diplomatic attempts failed to silence her, the party made a fateful decision. A rogue character stabbed Syl in the back for 13 damage on the gun deck, then placed a harpoon in the wound to stage the scene as an accident. Using a successful healing check (result 22), the party made the wound appear consistent with harpoon damage rather than a blade strike, effectively framing the death as a tragic accident. The party maintained operational security by moving through the ship in coordinated fashion, with some members kept deliberately uninformed to provide plausible deniability should their activities be discovered.
