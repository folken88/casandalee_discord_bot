## Summary

# Iron Gods Campaign Session Summary: "Shots in the Dark"

The party found themselves in a desperate firefight within an underground facility, separated from enemies by an iron wall barrier. As hostile forces unleashed shotgun blasts from the other side, the party's situation deteriorated when multiple members fell under confusion effects. Olbryn attempted to dispel the magical afflictions using powerful fourth-level spell slots, but both attempts failed due to poor rolls. The confusion spread chaos through the ranks—Nomkath struggled to decide between melee combat and spellcasting, while Ulfred, similarly affected, cast Air Geyser on his own ally Olbryn in desperation, launching the wizard ten feet upward into the ceiling and leaving him prone and injured. Meanwhile, Azuma resisted a powerful DC-22 will save spell from the enemies, demonstrating her mental fortitude.

The party's situation grew more dire as a massive lightning bolt AOE tore through the hallways, striking multiple confused party members. Kath McGinnis found themselves trapped in ghost form, controlled by The Dominion and unable to recognize their surroundings, until a mysterious warm presence with a gentle touch guided them back to their body and freedom. Eventually, Akradenn succeeded where others had failed, dispelling the confusion effects that plagued the group and restoring clarity to the battlefield.

With their minds cleared, the party launched a coordinated counteroffensive. Ulfred, equipped with jet boots, charged down the hallway at tremendous speed and slammed into a group of dangerous aliens with his hammer, using Eagle Soul to negate the impact damage. The party encountered a horrifying array of enemies: brain-like creatures wielding chainsaws, enormous spiders, heavily armored natalgu, and psionic robots. A massive green claw burst through an iron wall, nearly tearing it apart. Ulfred landed a devastating critical hit with an ice shard gun that caused one creature to explode from internal damage, while Akradenn's spiritual weapon found a hole in the iron wall to deliver a critical strike against a natalgu, cracking open its armored shell.

The battle culminated in the defeat of a massive dragon creature—the apparent major villain of the encounter. The party concentrated their laser-like attacks on a single point of the dragon's body, causing it to heave sideways before its head opened up. Through sustained melee attacks from Ulfred, Olbryn, and coordinated strikes from the entire party, they finally brought down the heavily armored and magically protected beast. During the chaos, a bandolier of cartridges was dropped near the incapacitated Zuma, and Maginnis recovered a pistol and bandolier from Asuma after the battle concluded.

Among the spoils of victory, the party discovered a data pad in a backpack—a device smaller than expected that appeared as flat glass when powered off. The discovery sparked speculation among the group about its potential connection to Casandalee, hinting at larger mysteries yet to be unraveled in the Iron Gods campaign.
