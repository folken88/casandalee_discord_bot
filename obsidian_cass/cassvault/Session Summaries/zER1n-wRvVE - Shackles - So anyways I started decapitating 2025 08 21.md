## Summary

The crew barely survived a ship-to-ship battle with a small Chelish messenger ship in the previous session. This session begins with the aftermath: putting out fires, looting the Chelish vessel, and distributing custom Chelish weapons and equipment. The party discovers a captain's parcel containing intelligence about Operation Crimson Doctrine, Operation Dusk Milk (food sabotage against Tidewater Rock), the HMS Fury Unyielding (a lost Chelish warship), and the Chelish blockade around Tidewater Rock. Rhyarca uses Speak with Dead on the Chelish Inquisitor to interrogate her, though she resists several questions. Sha-Feng collects heads in a bag and takes an Asmodean holy symbol.
