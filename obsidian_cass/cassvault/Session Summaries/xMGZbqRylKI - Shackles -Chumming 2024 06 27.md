# Session Summary: "Shackles - Chumming" (4717.02.01)

The party's fifth day aboard the *Wormwood* began as it had before—with grueling labor, verbal abuse from Master Scourge, and the constant threat of the captain's wrath. The monotony was broken when Master Scourge heard mysterious noises emanating from below deck. After consulting with Captain Harrigan, the officers altered the ship's course westward to parallel a line of small islands. Bujon, released from his week-long confinement in the ship's box, was given a grim opportunity for redemption: use his wind-manipulation abilities to adjust the ship's course or face further imprisonment. He succeeded, shifting the breeze fifteen degrees and causing the *Wormwood* to lurch as the wheel was corrected. The crew's mood remained tense as they sailed deeper into the Shackles.

As the ship approached the island chain, Holden spotted a distinctive formation of spike-like rocks jutting from the sea approximately one mile to the west. The sight was grim: multiple wrecked vessels lay run aground on the rocks, their hulls shattered and masts snapped. Most ominously, one ship flew a yellow plague flag—the universal warning of disease and death. Master Scourge confirmed the flag's meaning and consulted with Captain Harrigan below deck. The captain's decision was swift and brutal: a boarding party would investigate the plague ship to determine if salvage was possible. Storgrim, Holden, Riviera, and Bujon were selected for the mission, with Storgrim receiving explicit orders to kill or abandon any infected crew members they encountered.

The party armed themselves with cutlasses, daggers, and whatever armor they possessed, receiving grim warnings from their crewmates as they prepared. The crew saluted them solemnly as they rowed away from the *Wormwood* in a dinghy, the gesture suggesting this was likely a one-way mission. During the row, Buhan—one of their fellow crew members—revealed his preference for piracy over military service, though he expressed uncertainty about the current suicide mission. As they approached the rocky island cluster, the stench of rotting flesh grew overwhelming. The party discovered not one spike-like formation but five black stone fingers protruding from the ocean, with four to five wrecked ships of varying ages caught between them. Large sharks—likely hammerheads or great whites—cruised the waters, drawn by the scent of death.

Upon landing on the beach, the party discovered two decomposed corpses approximately twenty feet up the slope from the waterline. Investigation revealed the bodies belonged to malnourished Marines who had apparently committed suicide by cutting their own throats, likely driven to desperation by scurvy and starvation after being trapped on the island for an extended period. The corpses bore signs of having scavenged supplies from the surrounding shipwrecks in a desperate attempt to survive. Among the bodies, the party discovered several magical items: Holden retrieved a Besmara-blessed pirate hat that radiated minor magic and granted bonuses to acrobatics, climbing, and pirate knowledge checks, along with the ability to automatically float to the water's surface. Riviera recovered a composite longbow, interesting boots, a bronze neck medallion with an inscription, and a mysterious metal bar with a grip. As the party ventured deeper into the wreck site, they encountered skeletal remains and zombie-like creatures, which they dispatched with cutlasses and ranged attacks. The true nature of the plague ship and what horrors awaited them deeper within the wreckage remained to be discovered.
