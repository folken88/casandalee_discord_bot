## Summary

The party attends a rigged robot fight in Starfall to gain the favor of Technic League Captain Acroden, then races to defend a Southwest tower where Captain Elias is stationed against an incoming robot strike force. The session features a large-scale tactical battle against Meridons, a Juggernaut, repair drones, and other gearsmen, with the party employing anti-tech fields, wall of force, and heavy buffing to hold the position.
