## Summary

# Iron Gods Campaign Session Summary: "Calamitous Flailing"

Following their victory against the Dominion of the Black's northern patrol—a horrifying force of giant orcs and kasatha piloted by intellect devours—the party resolved to strike at a second enemy patrol group. They loaded into their six-wheeled Nomad transport and drove south through the Scar of the Spider, successfully crossing a treacherous river with a skilled 24-point ride check. However, as they drove loudly through a valley searching for their quarry, the landscape itself seemed to turn against them. A psychic assault of screaming, thundering noise crashed into their minds, forcing multiple party members to resist confusion effects. The vehicle's driver executed a perfect emergency maneuver—a controlled power slide between two trees—bringing the transport to a sudden halt as the party scrambled to exit. Olbryn remained trapped inside, confused and striking himself repeatedly, while Akradenn made the questionable tactical decision to cast Invisibility on Asuma rather than attempt to dispel the mental effects.

The ambush erupted with devastating force. A lightning bolt struck the party as they disembarked, and from the southwestern woods came the snarl of something massive. Orcs with great axes burst through the undergrowth in a frenzied charge, their bodies clearly puppeted by the Dominion's intellect devours. A towering, muscle-bound orc performed a devastating power attack against Zuma, drawing blood across her hide armor. The party responded with coordinated ferocity: Asuma fired three icicle shots through an enemy's neck and body; Caulky conjured a Wall of Thorns to slow the orc advance; and Nomkath unleashed heaving earth and entangle spells that proved devastatingly effective. One orc became entangled around the throat, vomiting repeatedly before a brain creature ejected from its skull. Another orc in a mechanical suit sank into the ground as Nomkath's spells crushed it, its head exploding from the pressure.

The battle descended into controlled chaos as the party's superior tactics overwhelmed the Dominion's forces. Tokala's entangling magic claimed victims; Isuma delivered a critical strike for 96 damage that obliterated a brain creature; and Croctaw wielded an adamantium chainsaw blade to annihilate a subterranean creature. Multiple enemies were run down and exploded beneath the party's six-wheeled transport as Isuma and Maginnis drove it over them repeatedly. Throughout the carnage, whispered voices called out party members' names—a psychological tactic that proved ineffective against their determination. When the dust settled, the party had systematically dismantled the second Dominion patrol, though the cost of their victory remained to be fully assessed. Akradenn earned a mysterious tattoo for their excessive application of roll spells during the encounter, a strange reward for overkill that hinted at forces beyond their understanding.
