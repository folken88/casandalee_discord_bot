## Summary

The party was deep in Gol Khazak (meaning "Howling Mines"), an underwater dwarven dungeon where they had cut a deal with the local inhabitants to clear undead. Bujon was dead, Sha-Feng captured, and the remaining crew pressed on with replacements. Riviera returned to the ship to recruit Vaughan and Kwami as reinforcements. Vaughan consecrated the base camp area and cast Discovery Torch on Storgrim. The new crew member Sorak (a combat-obsessed loner who constantly stood uncomfortably close to everyone and oozed slime) clashed with Vaughan's personal space preferences. The party explored collapsed dwarven tunnels, identified deliberate architectural collapses, and found signs of an infectious disease that turned dead dwarves into a variant of grave knights ("grave evenings"). They discovered a body near an underground pond where the temperature dropped dramatically, and a dwarven voice echoed the word for "trespass" before combat began against what appeared to be a cold-based undead guardian.
