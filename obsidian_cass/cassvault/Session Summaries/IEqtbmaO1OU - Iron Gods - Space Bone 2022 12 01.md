# Iron Gods Campaign Session Summary: Space Bone (December 1, 2022)

The party found themselves pulled into a Dominion mind space within the Black Hill Caves, where consciousness exists simultaneously in both the physical world and the alien realm. This surreal environment became the stage for one of their most harrowing encounters yet: they faced exact duplicates of themselves. These doppelgangers proved devastatingly effective, adapting their tactics in real-time as they learned new abilities from observing the party members in combat. The battle turned dire when Doppel Nomkath and Doppel Crocoda brought Ulfred down to death's door; only Akradenn's quick intervention with healing magic saved him from permanent demise. Though the party ultimately prevailed—destroying Doppel McGinnis with 47 points of electrical damage, Olbryn's critical chainsaw strike killing a cat-man doppelganger for 63 damage, and Auburn's electrical assault obliterating a giant aquatic ape—the victory came at great cost.

After regrouping and healing the unconscious Nomkath, the party explored deeper into the cave system. They discovered glowing blue symbols etched into the stone floor and encountered memory storage barriers—visions imprinted into the very rock itself. One vision depicted a massive desert battle between two armies; another showed a cat folk desperately attempting to disarm a grenade detonator. Most disturbing was the discovery of disintegrated bodies in a 35-foot-wide chamber: Maginnis and Crocod had been reduced to ash, their fate unknown. A black glass wall displayed another vision of someone disarming grenades before fading to reveal a passage northward.

The party realized they had entered a Dominion knowledge vault—an ancient facility where the very architecture functioned as puzzles. Hallways and barriers required understanding of specific information to pass through. Ulfred examined a wall depicting a historical battle and had to determine which side would emerge victorious to progress. The facility's design separated party members, forcing them to navigate its passages independently before reuniting in various hallways. One particularly striking moment came when Ulfred examined a wall that opened into a thick jungle, revealing a vision of a spear-wielding man striking a charging predator directly between the eyes—another fragment of alien or ancient history preserved in this strange vault.

As the session concluded, the party stood at the threshold of further mysteries within the Dominion facility. They had acquired several powerful items from a holographic chest, including a transforming sword hilt that becomes a +1 Keen returning weapon usable in virtual spaces, and Nomkath obtained Higgs Boson goggles granting invisibility and enhanced perception in virtual environments. Olbryn discovered a fear orb that could be equipped as armor, providing full plate protection without encumbrance. The vault's puzzles and visions suggested they were uncovering crucial knowledge about the Dominion's past—knowledge that might prove essential to understanding the greater threat they faced.
