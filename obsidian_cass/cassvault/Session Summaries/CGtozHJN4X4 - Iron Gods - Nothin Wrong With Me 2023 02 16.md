## Summary

The party wakes in the Dead Block of Starfall's Kill Box district and begins gathering intelligence on the city's power structure. Nomkath scouts southeast and learns about local gangs -- the Junkers and the V's -- who have a truce, and discovers the Dead Block was abandoned after a gang fight months ago. The party investigates the abandoned neighborhood as a potential safe house, disguising themselves before venturing into populated areas. They discuss long-term plans for operating in Starfall, ranging from serious intelligence gathering to jokes about a "10-year infiltration plan."
