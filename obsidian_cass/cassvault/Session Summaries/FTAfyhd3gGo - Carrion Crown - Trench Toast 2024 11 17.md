## Summary

CP-USS stayed at the Penitent Hearth Inn where they encountered Gabriel, a massive winged celestial paladin heading to the World Wound. Sylvia the innkeeper confirmed the Whispering Way cultists passed through roughly two weeks ago with undead followers, including one on the path to lichdom. The demon wolves also passed through as paying customers. After departing south, the party discovered a cursed battlefield near Feldgrau scarred by Shining Crusade trench warfare and a more recent massacre. At nightfall they engaged a large force of ghost soldiers--burned caster types using cold/negative energy AOE and armored dual-wielding melee fighters draining Constitution on touch. Dismas and Rodney Danger Smith arrived mid-combat to reinforce.
