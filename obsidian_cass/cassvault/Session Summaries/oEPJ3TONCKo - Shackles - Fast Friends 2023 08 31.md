# Session Summary: "Shackles - Fast Friends"

The party's evening began innocently enough at the Groggy Rock bar in Port Peril, where a tall, lanky pirate approached them with an invitation to play Liar's Dice. What started as a simple game of chance quickly escalated into a raucous drinking competition, complete with increasingly absurd acrobatic challenges—cartwheels, table jumps, and other drunken feats. The bartender led them through a hidden passage (accessed via a triple knock on an ale barrel) into a secret underground chamber where the games continued in earnest. As the night wore on and the drinks flowed, the party members grew progressively more intoxicated, eventually collapsing into a delirious slumber. They would later discover the drinks had been laced with tachet oil—a drug with a distinctive nutmeg aftertaste.

The party awoke in darkness, shackled in a cage aboard the pirate ship *Wormwood*. Their heads pounding and their freedom stripped away, they quickly learned they had been pressed into service as crew members for Captain Barnabas Harrigan, one of the Shackles' most notorious pirate captains. In an adjacent cell, they discovered a fellow prisoner named Jakes Magpie, who had been imprisoned for suspected mutiny a week prior. Through conversation with Magpie and other crew members, the party learned that multiple mutiny attempts had been made against Harrigan, all failing due to poor coordination and his extensive network of loyal informants. The captain maintained iron-fisted control through fear and strategic punishment.

That morning, the entire crew was summoned to the top deck for a grim spectacle of justice. Captain Harrigan himself descended from the aft castle to oversee proceedings. Jakes Magpie was brought before them, accused of thievery, lying, and mutinous intent. Captain Plugg, the ship's master-at-arms, sentenced him to keel hauling—a brutal execution in which the condemned is dragged beneath the ship by rope, suffering catastrophic injuries. Magpie did not survive the ordeal. Harrigan then addressed the assembled crew, noting this was the third person in two months caught attempting to break into the armory, and announced that future theft would result in confinement in the sweat box—a small, airless compartment used as torture.

With the execution complete, the party's true initiation into the crew began. They were uncuffed and ordered to participate in a climbing competition up the ship's rigging to demonstrate their abilities. Ser-Toche proved the most capable, reaching the Crow's Nest first and earning assignment as the ship's new rigger. Sha-Feng demonstrated culinary skills during a subsequent cooking test and was assigned as cook's mate under Ambrose Kroop. Meanwhile, a mysterious ally named Sandara Quinn began distributing contraband throughout the crew—including Heaven's Sting (a family heirloom retrieved for Alister), formula books, an alchemy kit, scale mail, and thieves' tools. The party had survived their first day aboard the *Wormwood*, but their real struggles were only beginning.
