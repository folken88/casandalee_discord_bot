# Iron Gods Campaign Journal - Session: Autonomy Threatened

## A Restless Night and Mysterious Disappearance

After a harrowing encounter with river monsters and a profitable exploration of the lake depths, the party returned to their campsite to rest and recover. The morning of the third day dawned peacefully enough, with the group taking time to tend to their wounds and discuss their next moves. However, tensions simmered beneath the surface as the party debated a troubling subject: the fate of Casandalee and the android religious sect. The discussion revealed a fundamental philosophical divide—should the party enforce traditional ways upon those who wish to abandon them, or should they respect individual autonomy? This moral quandary would prove prescient, as events that night would force the issue in the most violent way possible.

## The Ambush

During the third watch, Tokala noticed an unsettling absence: Heresick had vanished from camp without explanation. Before the party could investigate, the night erupted in gunfire. A coordinated assault descended upon the sleeping camp from multiple directions—beautiful female-presenting androids with glowing orange eyes, bobbed hair, and nurse-like uniforms emerged from the darkness, whispering "face the wall, lover" as they unleashed volleys of non-lethal stun pistol fire. The attack was devastatingly effective. Croctaw took four point-blank shots, absorbing 64 damage before collapsing into unconsciousness. Tokala suffered an even worse fate, taking 65 damage and falling immediately. Alfred was knocked prone and battered. The remaining conscious party members—Ulfred, Olbryn, Nomkath, Maginnis, Akradenn, Luna, and Brow—scrambled to respond.

## Combat and Capture

Olbryn reacted swiftly, teleporting Nomkath to safety before casting haste on the remaining fighters and greater invisibility on the ranger. Maginnis unleashed Justice, his powerful weapon, against the eastern android attacker, dealing significant construct-bane damage—yet the android showed no pain response despite visible wounds. Nomkath threw a black powder grenade at two enemies, but both evaded with inhuman precision. Croctaw landed multiple melee strikes against the lead female combatant before being struck by a devastating aerial kung fu knee drop from twenty feet above, leaving him stunned and vulnerable. The androids fought with mechanical precision and acrobatic prowess, their coordinated assault overwhelming the party's defenses. One by one, party members fell to the relentless barrage of non-lethal fire. Olbryn was staggered by over 30 damage of blaster fire after failing his will saves. Maginnis was brought to negative two hit points by a female combatant who dropped from the trees. As consciousness faded for the final defenders, the party realized they had been systematically captured by forces far more advanced than anything they had previously encountered.
