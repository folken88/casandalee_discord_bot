# Session Summary: The Pillager's Knife - Bad People Diarrhea Expedition

The party set their sights on an unexplored region of Bonewrack Isle colloquially known as "Bad People Diarrhea," rumored to contain gold and inhabited by ghouls. After debating whether to sail or travel overland, they chose to sail, with Holden executing a masterful navigation check (rolling 30-31) to safely guide the ship around treacherous reefs. Before proceeding inland, the party made strategic decisions about their crew: they dropped off unwanted captives on a nearby island with basic supplies and weapons, and left behind some crew members to establish a small settlement with cleared fields for food production. They also planned to inform the Kofi tribe about enemies who had been cutting down their sacred trees.

Upon reaching the swampy area known as Bad People Diarrhea, the party discovered a shack built on tree stumps surrounded by impenetrable murky water and fog. The terrain proved treacherous, forcing them to navigate by climbing trees, piers, and boat structures to avoid the water below. When Holden opened the door to the main hut, he discovered a horrifying scene: bodies and blood covering the interior, with boxes and additional doorways leading deeper into the structure. The party soon encountered multiple ghouls emerging from a pile of corpses, who attacked Holden aggressively with claws and teeth.

The combat that followed was brutal and decisive. Riviera demonstrated exceptional skill, executing a precision sneak attack that drove her sword straight through a crab's shell opening, and later severing a ghoul's head before setting its body ablaze. Sha-Feng proved equally lethal, delivering a devastating knee strike to a female ghoul that collapsed her forehead and flattened her skull. The party systematically eliminated the undead threats, with one creature being slashed cleanly through the body before turning to ash. During this expedition, the party also acquired several magical items including a Ring of Lu (+3 bonus to Navigator checks) for Holden, a Natural Armor Amulet for Riviera, and a Dead Man's Coin—an item rumored to save one from death itself.
