## Summary

The party ventures into the Windward Jungle and encounters two major combat encounters: first, a swarm of giant poisonous centipedes with hidden archers, and second, a coordinated ambush by lizardfolk rangers and more centipedes in a valley. Despite heavy damage and casualties, the party prevails through tactical spellcasting (particularly Buhan's lightning bolts), melee prowess, and teamwork. The party discovers the enemies are staging from a nearby cave and must decide whether to pursue or summon reinforcements.
