The party set out from Bahari Kofi Village on a rescue mission to save kidnapped crew members from grindylows on Bonewrack Isle. After trading gunpowder for cumquats and acquiring giant frog meat from combat encounters in the swamps, they tracked mysterious screams to a coastal hut where they discovered a hung ghoul that had been slowly deteriorating.
