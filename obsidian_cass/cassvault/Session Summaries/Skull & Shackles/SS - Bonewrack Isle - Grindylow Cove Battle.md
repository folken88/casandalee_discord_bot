## Summary

The party scouts a grindylow-infested cove on Bonewrack Isle using Riviera's scouting flight. After careful planning, they execute an ambush on the beach, with Sarah performing an acrobatic dive attack on a shaman. The fight escalates as the party faces approximately 26 grindylows and multiple shamans. Key turning points include Riviera's alchemist fire igniting a floating trash pile as a distraction, Bujon's strategic use of obscuring mist and entangle spells, and Sha-Feng being nearly drowned before being rescued by Holden. The party ultimately defeats the grindylow force, though Sha-Feng is severely wounded and the prisoners' location remains unknown, likely in underwater caves.
