## Summary

The party's dinghy is attacked by sahuagin warriors and a massive hammerhead shark, forcing them into an underwater battle shrouded in deeper darkness. After intense combat with multiple sharks and sahuagin, the party defeats most enemies but Sha-Feng is captured and carried away by a large shark. Upon surfacing, the party learns sahuagin territory is nearby and interrogates three captured sahuagin prisoners using speak with dead to gather intelligence about a potential undead threat in an underwater dwarven tomb.
