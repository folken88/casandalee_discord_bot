# Drow Envy

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=1TVkAiSCsT8)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Storgrim, Holden, Sha-Feng, Ser-Toche, Vaughan, Riviera, Bujon, Rhyarca

## Summary

The party negotiates the division of cargo and ships from the recent battle, deciding to take the Chelish frigate Veil of Dhelm and sell the TPK to Goat's Head. After acquiring a skeleton crew from defeated Chelish sailors and resolving ship finances, they sail to Goat's Head where Riviera is reincarnated as a drow. The group then sails to Rickety Squibs to begin repairs on the Veil, with plans to acquire flying boots via a volcano expedition before heading to new regions including Sangor and Vidrian.
