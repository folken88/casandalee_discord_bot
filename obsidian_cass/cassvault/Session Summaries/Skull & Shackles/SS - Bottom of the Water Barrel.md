# Bottom of the Water Barrel

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=TBMtprvj3a0)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Holden, Storgrim, Sha-Feng, Ser-Toche, Vaughan, Riviera, Bujon, Rhyarca

## Summary

The party navigates a severe supernatural storm that drives the Man's Promise onto a coral reef. During the storm, grindylows attack the ship and several crew members are lost overboard, including Rosie and Kabar. The party successfully repels the attackers and manages to slow the ship before it completely wrecks. They are then sent ashore in a dinghy to collect fresh water, where they encounter the Bahari Kofi—bird-like native inhabitants—who inform them that grindylows have captured four of their crew members and plan to sacrifice them at the full moon in two days.
