## Summary

The party explores a ghoul-infested location on Bonewrack Isle called 'Bad People Diarrhea,' defeats several undead creatures in combat, and recovers significant treasure including potions, gems, and gold. They sail to Rickety Squibs where they rename their ship to 'The Pillager's Knife' (TPK), negotiate with Rickety Hake for repairs, and begin acquiring equipment and magical items at local shops.
