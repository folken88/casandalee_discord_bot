## Summary

The party returns from the library to find the TPK under attack by Sahuagin. A large underwater battle ensues in near-total darkness, with the party fighting Sahuagin warriors, a Sahuagin shaman/leader, and commanded sharks. [[Sha-Feng]] is eventually separated from the group, dragged toward the island by the shark.
