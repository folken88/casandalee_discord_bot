# Speak with Skull — Gol Khazak Delving & Undead Combat

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=2AERbrEysxQ)

## Summary

The party continues exploring the sunken dwarven settlement of Gol Khazak after rescuing Sha-Feng. They clear the south storage room and southern dormitory, then push into the mushroom farm area where they encounter and defeat three undead dwarves, a soldier with reach, a cleric paragon, and the heavily-armored Lord of Gol Khazak wielding enchanted axes. After a chaotic combat involving blindness, grappling, and strategic positioning, the party loots significant treasures including enchanted weapons, documents in an oath-sender, and a crown. They learn the facility was a secret Alcenar dwarven outpost building an advanced submersible vessel, now destroyed. Sorak reveals the Sahuagin know of these underwater ships and that Tidewater Rock is under siege.
