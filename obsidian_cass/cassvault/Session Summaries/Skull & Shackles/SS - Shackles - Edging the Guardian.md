## Summary

The session begins with the crew interrogating a captured Bronze Fleet prisoner, who reveals that the Bronze Fleet is conducting a massive slave run, filling ships to overcapacity and meeting at the southwest end of the Eye to sail to Cheliax. The fleet consists of roughly six galleons, seven or eight frigates, and numerous smaller ships, with a Chelish Navy escort. After extracting intel, [[Storgrim]] executes the prisoner. The crew tows the captured Bronze Fleet brigantine back to [[Goatshead Port]], negotiates a sale with [[Bent Beak]] (settling on 3,200 gold plus a feast and a copy of "The Cumbers of Ustalav"), and splits loot among party and crew. The party then sets sail on patrol, heading west past [[Island 418]] and [[Skinny Whale Island]] to investigate a treasure map X on an island in the Arrowhead Chain. They find abandoned dinghies with coal dust and mining equipment traces on the beach, follow a cleared jungle path with precisely-cut trees, climb a cliff using remnants of an elevator system, and discover a reinforced mine entrance. Inside the cave, they find multiple crushed skeletons and a stone golem guardian standing before a magical metal aperture in the wall. The golem radiates magic similar to [[Bilge]] and [[Tortugan]]'s construction. When [[Bujon]] attempts to use Mage Hand to retrieve loot from the bodies, the golem unleashes a sleep effect that drops most of the party. The session ends on this cliffhanger, with the golem poised to stomp the sleeping party members.
