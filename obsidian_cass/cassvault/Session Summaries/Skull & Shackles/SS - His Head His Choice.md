## Summary

The party completes the final chambers of Gol Khazak, defeating undead workers and triggering a fungal plague when Riviera's gunshot detonates contaminated mushroom spores. Sha-Feng is beheaded by a zombie in combat and dies. The party recovers his head, burns the mushroom farm, and negotiates with the Sahuagin to leave peacefully. They travel to the druid Thraorn on Firegrass Island to reincarnate Sha-Feng, who rolls a goblin (specifically a hobgoblin) on the reincarnation table. The party levels up to 6th level.
