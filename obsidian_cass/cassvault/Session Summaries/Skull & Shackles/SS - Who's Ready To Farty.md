# Who's Ready To Farty

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=NzmNCw3Di0Q)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Holden, Storgrim, Sha-Feng, Bujon, Rhyarca

## Summary

The party resupplies at Firegrass Island (Goatshead Port) after Sha-Feng's reincarnation, selling loot and banking gold. They learn of a blockade at Tidewater Rock and sail to investigate. En route, they spot a Chelish supply ship at dawn and pursue it, choosing to engage rather than let it escape. Combat begins with Buhan casting stinking cloud to great effect, nauseating two of the enemy's most dangerous casters. The boarding party makes heavy use of phase spider jerky buffs and dimension door tactics to devastating effect, systematically eliminating the Chelish crew.
