## Summary

The party sails the TPK from Island 418 toward Goat's Head Port on Firegrass Island to meet with the whalers and collect payment. Upon arrival, they discover the port under attack by slavers—a galley full of slave-catchers supported by two distant gallions chasing away the local defender ship. The party intervenes in a large combined combat involving PCs, crew, town militia, and whalers against the slaver forces on the docks and in the town, ultimately defeating the slavers and freeing the enslaved people chained to the slaver galley.
