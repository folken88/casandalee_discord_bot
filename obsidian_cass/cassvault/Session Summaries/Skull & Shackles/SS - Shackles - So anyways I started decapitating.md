# Shackles - So anyways I started decapitating

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=zER1n-wRvVE)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Holden, Storgrim, Sha-Feng, Ser-Toche, Vaughan, Riviera, Bujon, Rhyarca

## Summary

The party defeats a Chelish messenger ship in combat, interrogates the corpses using Speak with Dead to learn about Operation Crimson Doctrine (a planned invasion of the Shackles), and discovers details about sabotaged food supplies at Tidewater Rock. They scuttle the defeated ship and sail to Windward Island to investigate a shipwreck, then row ashore at Half Moon Beach where they discover the corpse of a female operative with severed legs and find a mysterious cursed mask and maracas.
