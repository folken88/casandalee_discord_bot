# Shackles - Chumming (2024-06-27)

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=xMGZbqRylKI)

## Summary

The party is pressed into service aboard the Wormwood and sent on a dangerous reconnaissance mission to a cursed island graveyard filled with wrecked ships. They discover two dead sailors and loot their gear, then explore multiple derelict vessels, encountering and fighting off skeletal undead and a mysterious zombie. The party collects various magical items and supplies while learning painful lessons about low-level combat.
