# Shackles - Impress Me Spice Rack

**Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=hvOCLgD38XU)
**Date Aired:** 2025-02-06

## Summary

The party returns to [[Goatshead Port]] on [[Firegrass Island]] after rescuing survivors from Bronze Fleet slavers at a ruined temple. They deliver slaver heads as proof and receive a wand of cure moderate wounds from [[Bent Beak]]. The crew then ventures into the island's interior to rescue additional captives taken by a giant. They discover a vast planted forest of saplings surrounding a massive tree, encounter a herd of enormous beasts called Tukkar, and meet [[Thragorn]], a forest giant druid living inside the great tree with his invisible tyrannosaurus animal companion, [[Loa]]. [[Sha-Feng]] negotiates with [[Thragorn]] by offering whiskey and hallucinogenic wildflower petals, then cooks a mackerel cassoulet for the giant using his pantry ingredients. In exchange for slaver corpses to feed [[Loa]], [[Thragorn]] releases his captives. The party strikes a longer-term deal: they will eventually procure three galleons to transport [[Thragorn]], [[Loa]], and his Tukkar herd to the mainland. [[Thragorn]] gives each party member one item from his shop as a token of good faith. The party also spots a yellow-sailed sloop being chased by a Bronze Fleet brigantine in the distance, and [[Rhyarca]] recalls intelligence about an alliance between the Bronze Fleet and Cheliax.

## NPCs

- [[Thragorn]] — Forest giant druid (~200 years old), lives in a great hollow tree on Firegrass Island with his invisible tyrannosaurus [[Loa]] and a herd of Tukkar. Chaotic neutral. Vegetarian but feeds meat to [[Loa]]. Wants to leave the island eventually.
- [[Loa]] — Thragorn's tyrannosaurus animal companion. Can turn invisible, see invisible, and pass without trace. Blue-gray with war paint and tattoos.
- [[Bent Beak]] — Goatshead Port leader (Australian-accented). Provides supplies and bounty info.
- [[Lil-Vaughan]] — Monkey adopted by [[Riviera]] as familiar. Has been pickpocketing ~24 gold from various people. Intelligence boosted to 8.
