## Summary

The party returned to Firegrass Island to rescue kidnapped crew members and encountered a massive forest giant druid named Thror and his invisible tyrannosaur companion Loa. After negotiating with combat, cooking, and diplomacy, the party traded slaver corpses for safe passage and magical items. They learned Thror needs transport for himself, Loa, and a herd of tuskar creatures to the mainland—a task requiring multiple galleons. They also spotted a Bronze Fleet brigantine chasing a yellow-sailed sloop, hinting at increased slaver activity in the region.
