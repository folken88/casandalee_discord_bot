## Summary

The party is attacked during a celebration at Tidewater Rock's mead hall by Chelish assassins and summoners. A prolonged three-hour combat ensues involving invisible assassins, summoned imps, two powerful spellcasters (a Flame Oracle and a Diabolist), and multiple monks. The party successfully defends the hall, protects Lady Augusta (the local ruler), and defeats the Chelish strike team, though some enemy casters escape via teleportation. Post-combat, they discover a scroll of Raise Dead written by a cleric of Asmodeus on one of the fallen enemies.
