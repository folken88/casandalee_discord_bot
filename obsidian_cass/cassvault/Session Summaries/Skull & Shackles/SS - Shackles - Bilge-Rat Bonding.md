# Shackles - Bilge-Rat Bonding 2023 09 07

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=9dByfRgEAGA)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Storgrim, Holden, Riviera, Sha-Feng, Ser-Toche, Alexios

## Summary

The party continues their early days as press-ganged crew aboard the Wormwood, covering days three and four of service. After dealing with the aftermath of a brawl with Tilly and her cronies, the crew performs their daily ship duties while working to build alliances among the NPC crew. Ser-Toche bonds with Crimson Cogward over dark tales, Sha-Feng wins over the ship's cook Ambrose with poetry, and Riviera returns Rosie's fiddle. When something bites young Jack Scrimshaw in the bilges, Scourge sends the party below to exterminate the threat. They fight dire rats and bilge spiders, clear the hold, and scavenge weapons and supplies from the cargo.

## Key Events

- The party drags Tilly Brackett up to the top deck and throws her at Scourge's feet to explain why they are late. Scourge issues a warning but Storgrim volunteers to take lashes alongside the four attackers, resulting in six non-lethal damage from punishment at dusk.
  - Characters: Storgrim, Holden, Ser-Toche
  - Location: Wormwood, top deck
- Alexios pickpockets all four of Tilly's group during the fight aftermath, recovering 60 gold total (15 per person).
  - Characters: Alexios
  - Location: Wormwood, below deck
- Day three ship duties: Ser-Toche is assigned to the bilges and bonds with Crimson Cogward by telling dark tales, gaining a rapport bonus. Riviera works as a runner delivering messages and visits Grog the quartermaster to acquire Rosie's fiddle and a rapier. Sha-Feng works as cook's mate alongside the belligerently drunk Ambrose and impresses him by reciting poetry in Tien. Holden works the upper rigging with ease. Alexios works in the bilges alongside Holden and Cogward.
  - Characters: Ser-Toche, Riviera, Sha-Feng, Holden, Alexios
  - Location: Wormwood
- Riviera returns Rosie's fiddle to her in the evening, improving Rosie's disposition. Rosie plays a song that is surprisingly pleasant.
  - Characters: Riviera, Rosie
  - Location: Wormwood
- Nighttime activities: Ser-Toche steals from a crew locker (15 gold, alchemist's fire, antitoxin, bullseye lantern). Riviera steals from crew (15 gold, bicorn hat, tanglefoot bag, centipede poison, gems). Holden bonds with Barefoot Samms Toppin over drinks, rolling a 31 diplomacy. Storgrim bonds with fellow dwarf Narwhal Tate over rum.
  - Characters: Ser-Toche, Riviera, Holden, Storgrim
  - Location: Wormwood
- Alexios secures a regular food supply for his wolf companion Antonina through negotiation with Grog the quartermaster.
  - Characters: Alexios, Grog
  - Location: Wormwood, quartermaster store
- Day four: Jack Scrimshaw runs up from the bilges reporting something bit him. Scourge orders the party to go exterminate whatever is down there. The party fights two dire rats and three bilge spiders. Holden takes spider poison (one point of strength damage). Sha-Feng is bitten by a spider but resists the poison. The party defeats all the vermin.
  - Characters: Storgrim, Holden, Riviera, Sha-Feng, Ser-Toche, Alexios
  - Location: Wormwood, bilges
- After the bilge fight, the party finds discarded supplies in the cargo hold: leather armor (taken by Sha-Feng), a buckler, a hand axe, three heavy maces (one converted to a club for Alexios), a tanglefoot bag, a tinder twig, holy water, and 21 gold 12 silver (split among the party).
  - Characters: Storgrim, Holden, Riviera, Sha-Feng, Ser-Toche, Alexios
  - Location: Wormwood, bilges/cargo hold
