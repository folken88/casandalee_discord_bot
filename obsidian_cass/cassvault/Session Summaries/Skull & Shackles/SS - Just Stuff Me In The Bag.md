# Just Stuff Me In The Bag

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=f5kGKkWunho)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Storgrim, Holden, Buhan, Riarcha, Riviera, Sorak

## Summary

The party explores an underwater dwarven ruin (Gol Kazak) discovered by Riviera's scouting. They encounter the Sahuagin leader Sorak, who proposes an alliance to defeat undead dwarves ("kill twice") that infest the ruins. After clearing a chamber of worker zombies and armored undead soldiers, Buhan is killed by a critical hit from a dwarven greataxe while heroically protecting Riarcha. The party secures significant loot including magical items and begins planning the next phase of the dungeon.
