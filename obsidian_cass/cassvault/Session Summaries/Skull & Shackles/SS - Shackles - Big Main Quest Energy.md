## Summary

The party is at [[Rickety Squibs]] with their captured Chelish frigate undergoing a squib job (disguise refit). [[Ser-Toche]] (who was left at [[Tidewater Rock]]) has stowed away back aboard her old ship. [[Rickety Squibs]]' crew finds the Chelish frigate unusual — designed to carry a giant device no longer part of the ship's superstructure, with distinctive split sails and unmistakable Chelish cannons that cannot be disguised. The party shops, gets tattoos (including [[Rhyarca]]'s "Queen Kitty" operative tattoo for +2 DEX/stealth), and equips crew members. [[Bilge]] causes confusion by trying to undo [[Rickety Squibs]]' repair work, insisting on repairing things himself. The locals share news of a group of seven "nerds" (academics/investigators with no wilderness skills) who arrived about a week ago and wandered into the jungle to "investigate." The party decides to track them. Following hex-stone paths (older than the dwarven ruins, possibly Azlanti) deeper into the jungle, they track the nerds off the path into dense vegetation. [[Sha-Feng]] scouts ahead, identifying seven sets of tracks (two with military boots, five with soft city boots). They discover a well-worn trail made by hundreds of clawed bipedal reptilian feet, a crude log bridge over a river near a large waterfall, and follow the nerds' trail westward into what appears to be lizardfolk territory.
