# Shackles - Fast Friends

## Summary

The party is drugged and press-ganged aboard the Wormwood, captained by Barnabas Harrigan. After waking in the brig, they meet [[Jakes Magpie]], a fellow prisoner sentenced to death for attempted mutiny. He advises them to bide their time and wait for a prize ship opportunity. Jakes is keel-hauled and killed. The party is assigned shipboard duties and begins working to build alliances among the crew during their first days aboard.

## Key Events

- The session opens with a game of Liar's Dice at a tavern called the Groggy Rock in Port Peril, where [[Master Scourge]] lures the party into a back room
- The drinks are laced with oil of taggit (a knockout drug); the party fails their fortitude saves and wakes up shackled in the brig of the Wormwood
- They meet [[Jakes Magpie]] in the adjacent cell, who tells them he attempted mutiny and expects to be executed; he has a daughter back in Sargava
- Jakes advises the party to wait for Barnabas to capture a prize ship and get themselves assigned to its skeleton crew as a means of escape
- Jakes is keel-hauled and killed before the crew as punishment for thievery, laziness, and mutinous intent
- [[Mr. Plugg]] assigns the party jobs: [[Ser-Toche]] wins the rigging climb and becomes the new rigger; [[Sha-Feng]] becomes the cook's mate under [[Ambrose]]
- [[Holden]] meets [[Sandara Quinn]], a recently press-ganged cleric of Besmara, who returns his family sword Heaven's Sting and smuggles back [[Riviera]]'s formula book and alchemy kit
- [[Storgrim]] befriends [[Shivika]], a brawler-type crew member, bonding over fighting
- [[Ser-Toche]] befriends [[Slippery Syl Lonigan]], catching her when she nearly falls from the rigging
- [[Riviera]] meets [[Rosie Cusswell]], a halfling who had her fiddle stolen, and offers to help recover it
- [[Sha-Feng]] works diligently as cook's mate, successfully hunting turtles for the galley
- Alexios (a Druid with a wolf companion) finds his wolf Nina caged in the bilges
- The GM introduces the ship action system: daytime duties plus optional actions (influence NPCs, sneak, shop, shirk duties)
