## Summary

The party explored a jungle cave on Windward Island infested with giant centipedes and lizard folk rangers led by a powerful matriarch called 'Queen Blossom.' After a brutal combat where several party members were nearly killed (including Storgrim being attacked by a massive 30-40 foot centipede and Bujon being knocked unconscious), the party defeated the creatures and the surviving elder lizard ranger. They discovered the remains of Chellish sailors (likely from the Rizzen) and collected various items including magical weapons. The session ended with the party deciding to recruit the surviving lizard ranger to their crew and harvesting centipede eggs that can be used as healing items.
