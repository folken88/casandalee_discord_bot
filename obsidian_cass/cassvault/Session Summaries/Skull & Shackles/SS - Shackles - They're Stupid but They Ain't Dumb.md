## Summary

The party rescues their kidnapped crew from a grindylow lair on Bonewrack Isle, discovering an underwater cave system and battling a giant grindylow and its brood mother spellcaster. After freeing Sandara, Rosie, and other crew members, they recover significant treasure including coins, a magical corsair's coat, a maelstrom necklace, and a vindictive harpoon. With their mission accomplished, they prepare to return to the ship with fresh water.
