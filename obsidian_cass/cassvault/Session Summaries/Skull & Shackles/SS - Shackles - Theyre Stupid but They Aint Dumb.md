# Shackles - Theyre Stupid but They Aint Dumb

## Summary

After the beach battle, the party heals up and sends Ser-Toche on an underwater scouting mission into the grindylow lair beneath Riptide Cove. She discovers a honeycomb of volcanic caves and coral formations with a stream of bubbles indicating air pockets below. The party follows her in, navigating the underwater tunnels to reach an air-filled grindylow cavern. They fight through the remaining grindylows, including a brood mother, and rescue the captured crew -- Sandara, Rosie, Kabar, and Foras Jack. They recover a massive treasure hoard including nearly 1,900 gold coins, a Corsair's Coat (magical mithril chain shirt), a Maelstrom Necklace (evocation-boosting amulet given to Bujon), and a Vindictive Harpoon. The grindylows are nearly exterminated from the island. The party plans to return to the Man's Promise.
