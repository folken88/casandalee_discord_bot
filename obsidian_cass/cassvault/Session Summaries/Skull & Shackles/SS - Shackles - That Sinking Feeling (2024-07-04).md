## Summary

The party explores a graveyard of wrecked ships and discovers Tim's new character, a mysterious amnesiac swordsman, trapped on a corpse-laden hulk. After rescuing him and fighting off aggressive reef claws and giant crabs, they loot the wrecks and find significant treasure including the enchanted Dwarven war-axe Thorac, various magical wands, and military supplies. They must now decide what treasure to hide from their officers before returning to the ship.
