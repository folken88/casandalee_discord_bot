## Summary

The party is deep in the underwater dungeon of [[Gol Khazak]], the lost dwarven settlement, allied uneasily with [[Zorak]] and the Sahuagin against undead dwarves infesting the ruins. The session opens with the party battered and low on spells, seeking rest. [[Zorak]] offers to have his shamans heal the party and protect them while they rest, in exchange for the party clearing the rest of the dungeon. The Sahuagin shaman heals [[Zorak]] first, then [[Kwami]], then [[Holden]] — pointedly leaving [[Storgrim]] and others for last. [[Zorak]] offers to have the Sahuagin reincarnate [[Bujon]] (who was slain in combat) for the "astronomical" price of a magical item worth over 2,000 gold. [[Storgrim]] barely contains his laughter at the low price and trades a +1 shield. [[Bujon]] is reincarnated by the Sahuagin as an Iku-Turso (eel-man). The party then continues clearing the dungeon, fighting undead dwarves (ghouls/zombies) in the ruined mess hall and other chambers. [[Zorak]] charges into combat impetuously. The undead include dwarven ghouls with hammers, a female ghoul with a rapier, and various zombies emerging from rubble. [[Storgrim]]'s samurai challenge mechanic and [[Bujon]]'s first combat as an Iku-Turso are highlights. The party discovers a dining hall with ancient dwarven skeletons and three large unidentified magical creature skeletons (later identified as "Guang Bakuloids"). [[Sha-Feng]] rests at camp recovering from injuries, reporting back later.
