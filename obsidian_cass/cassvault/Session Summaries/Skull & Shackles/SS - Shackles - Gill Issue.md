## Summary

The party rests in Gol Khazak after recent battles, during which Bujon is killed and the Sahuagin offer to reincarnate him in exchange for magical payment. Bujon agrees and is reincarnated as an Iku-Turso (eel man) with significant mechanical and personality changes. The party then clears additional chambers of the dungeon, including a dining hall with giant skeletal remains, an armory, and a storage room, while dealing with undead dwarven soldiers and discovering valuable loot including dwarven weapons, armor, and a magically-enhanced spellbook.
