# Shackles - Speak with Skull 2025 05 29

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=2AERbrEysxQ)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Storgrim, Holden, Vaughan, Rhyarca, Sha-Feng

## Summary

The TPK crew continues clearing the dwarven settlement of Gol Khazak alongside their Sahuagin allies. Sha-Feng is returned to the party after the Sahuagin discover he cannot cook underwater. The party removes a slave collar from Sha-Feng, explores a deliberately destroyed forge room, and discovers that the dwarves of Gol Khazak may have been an Alkenstar expedition -- technologically advanced and far from home. Multiple combats against undead dwarven workers, soldiers, and a paragon (cleric) ensue, culminating in a fight against the Lord of Gol Khazak, who wields dual throwing axes and continues fighting headless after Sorak decapitates him. The party uses tripping tactics, grappling, and spiritual weapon to overcome the remaining undead in obscuring mist conditions.

## Key Events

- Sha-Feng is returned to the party by the Sahuagin because he could not cook underwater. He still has a slave collar that absorbs attack spells and puts the wearer to sleep. The Sahuagin shamans remove it by casting hold person (DC 23) to trigger the collar's sleep function, then removing it while he's unconscious.
  - Characters: Sha-Feng, Sorak, Storgrim
  - Location: Gol Khazak
  - Timeline: Correlates to 4717.04.12 area events
- Storgrim and the party examine a deliberately destroyed forge room. The blast patterns are 15+ years old and intentional. An advanced metal forge was destroyed on purpose, and metal ingots were cleared before the destruction. The technology level is unusually advanced for dwarves.
  - Characters: Storgrim, Holden
  - Location: Gol Khazak, destroyed forge room
- Storgrim identifies the dwarves as potentially from Alkenstar -- a primarily dwarven settlement near the Mana Waste where magic is unsafe to use, leading to advanced technology including rifles, mechanical and chemical engines, and limited control over electricity. The Alkenstar clan is very secretive and separated from other dwarves for centuries.
  - Characters: Storgrim
  - Location: Gol Khazak
- Storgrim notes a connection: Broen's oath (related to his axe) has an inscription in Gol Khazak, meaning two completely different dwarf clans had a connection to Broen.
  - Characters: Storgrim
  - Location: Gol Khazak
- Sha-Feng privately tells the party in Mayonnaise (their secret hand-sign language) that the Sahuagin guards who held him are untrustworthy and should be killed if it can be made to look like an accident.
  - Characters: Sha-Feng, Storgrim
  - Location: Gol Khazak
- Multiple combats against undead dwarven workers and soldiers in the mushroom farm area. Kwami uses tripping tactics effectively. Sorak continues his challenge escalation, reaching challenge level 4 after killing the Lord of Gol Khazak.
  - Characters: Holden, Storgrim, Kwami, Sorak, Vaughan, Sha-Feng
  - Location: Gol Khazak, mushroom farm area
- The paragon (undead dwarven cleric) casts blindness/deafness on Rhyarca, permanently blinding her until remove blindness is cast. The Sahuagin shaman arrives and casts remove blindness to cure her.
  - Characters: Rhyarca, Sorak, Sahuagin Shaman
  - Location: Gol Khazak
- The Lord of Gol Khazak -- a heavily armored undead with a golden crown, dual axes (one a returning throwing axe) -- charges in, crits Storgrim for about two-thirds of his HP. Sorak decapitates him with a 33-point slash, but the lord's headless body sits up and continues fighting until Vaughan's spiritual weapon finishes him off.
  - Characters: Storgrim, Sorak, Vaughan, Holden
  - Location: Gol Khazak, mushroom farm area
- The remaining paragon casts obscuring mist, creating 20% miss chance at close range and total concealment at distance. Kwami uses martial flexibility to gain Blindfight and successfully grapples the paragon, holding him still for the party to finish off.
  - Characters: Kwami, Holden, Vaughan, Sorak
  - Location: Gol Khazak
- The paragon casts a negative energy burst hitting multiple party members before being destroyed.
  - Characters: Party
  - Location: Gol Khazak
