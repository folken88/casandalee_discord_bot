## Summary

After a successful boarding action against a Raduni ship and looting a shipwreck island, the party is assigned to a prize crew on the captured Man's Promise under the command of plug and Scourge, whom they despise. During the voyage to Port Peril, they discover the ship is being diverted to Rickety Squibs—likely for renaming and reselling rather than scrapping as the captain ordered. Recognizing this as a brewing mutiny by their hated commanders, the party begins secretly recruiting trusted crew members (Ambrose the chef, Albaer, and Sandara) to launch their own counter-mutiny. Meanwhile, Sha-Feng beats fips chamlet nearly to death in the cargo hold, and mysterious objects on V's cursed sword terrify both plug and Scourge.
