# Shackles - No Concept of Personal Space 2025 04 24

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=PPTFwACxYgs)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Storgrim, Holden, Vaughan, Rhyarca, Riviera, Sha-Feng

## Summary

The TPK crew continues exploring the underwater dwarven settlement of Gol Khazak (also called Kazak, meaning "howling minds"), fighting alongside their Sahuagin ally Sorak. With Buhan dead and Sha-Feng captured, Riviera brings replacement crew members Kwami (a monk) and Von down into the dungeon. The party battles a powerful dwarven wraith lord in ancient black and gold armor, multiple undead dwarven soldiers and workers, and suffers a fear effect that panics several party members. Vaughan uses positive energy spells and his sentient sword Radiance to damage the undead. Sorak proves a formidable if abrasive ally, constantly standing too close to Vaughan and lacking any concept of personal space. The party loots significant magical items from the wraith and a dead adventurer.
