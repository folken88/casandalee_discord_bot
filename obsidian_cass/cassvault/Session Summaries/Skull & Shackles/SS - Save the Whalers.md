## Summary

The Pillager's Knife departed Rickety Squibs with a newly assembled crew. The party encountered a stranded whaler ship being raided by pirates holding prisoners captive on a nearby beach. The party engaged in combat with the raiders, boarding their dingy and then the whaler itself, with significant assistance from ship cannons operated by the crew. Combat was ongoing at session end.
