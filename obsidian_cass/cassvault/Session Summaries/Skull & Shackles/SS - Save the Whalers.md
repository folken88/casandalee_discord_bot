## Summary

The party departs Rickety Squibs aboard the Pillager's Knife with a newly assembled crew and encounters a whaler ship that has been seized by pirates on a sandbar. After some initial planning and preparation (including extensive monkey management), they launch a coordinated assault in a dinghy while their ship provides cannon support, quickly overwhelming the pirates and saving the captured whalers.
