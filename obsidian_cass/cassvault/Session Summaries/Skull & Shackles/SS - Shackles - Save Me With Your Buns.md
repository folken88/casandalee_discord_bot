## Summary

The party defeats assassin vines in a poison field on Bonewrack Isle, harvesting resources and discovering valuable loot including scrolls, potions, and a mysterious infernal compass. They then execute a coordinated mutiny against the Man's Promise command crew, using disguises and ambush tactics to neutralize officers including Master Scourge, Mr. Plug, and others. The operation succeeds with significant casualties on the command side.
