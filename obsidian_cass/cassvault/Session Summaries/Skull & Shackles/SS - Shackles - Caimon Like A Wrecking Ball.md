# Shackles - Caimon Like A Wrecking Ball

## Summary

At Rickety Squibs, the party levels to 4 and does extensive shopping while their ship (the Man's Promise) is being refitted. [[Storgrim]] addresses the crew, offering anyone who wants to leave a small purse and safe passage to the next port -- establishing the crew is not press-ganged. They accept a mission from Rickety to clear the western road of dangerous caiman and bandits threatening supply shipments to the southern garrison. The party marches west along a jungle trail, discovering that recent flooding has changed the terrain. They investigate caiman activity near a submerged section of the trail.
