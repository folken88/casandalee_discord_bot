## Summary

The party cleared a section of the western road near Rickety Squibs of a caiman infestation, defeating multiple sun caymans in two separate combat encounters. They then encountered a group of shipwrecked sailors from the privateer vessel 'Sunrise' living in the jungle, initially engaged them in combat but negotiated peace after realizing they were escaped Chelish slaves. The party offered them provisional work at Rickety Squibs with the possibility of joining the Pillager's Knife crew pending evaluation.
