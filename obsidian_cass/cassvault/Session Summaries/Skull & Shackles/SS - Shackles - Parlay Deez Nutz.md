## Summary

After the naval battle, the party rests at Firegrass Island and then pursues Bronze Fleet slavers who have taken captives into the island's interior. Bent Beak describes the island's hazards including the death vog (volcanic fog) from the barely active volcano at the center. The crew tracks the slavers uphill to a massive ruined stone building, possibly an ancient temple. A brutal firefight ensues inside the ruins against Bronze Fleet soldiers, a goblin swashbuckler, and an officer in a cowboy hat with goggles. Three of six captives are killed by the slavers during the fight. The party defeats all enemies, rescues the surviving captives, and discovers a sarcophagus containing an ancient knight of Milani. Inside they find two powerful magic items: the Bank of Besmara (a holy symbol/locket that functions as a bag of holding for coins and scales as an amulet of natural armor) and the Bangarang (bracers that boost parry/riposte and grant haste).
