## Summary

The party engaged in a major ship-to-ship combat against Bronze Fleet slavers attempting to capture the Red Courage, resulting in significant casualties on both sides. After a hard-fought victory that left two beloved party members dead (Storgrim and Sha-Feng), the crew secured a valuable prize galley. The party spent virtually all available gold to resurrect their fallen comrades using Raise Dead scrolls, leaving them temporarily broke but operationally intact. They accepted a patrol contract from Goats Head Port to defend Firegrass Island for 2-3 weeks in exchange for wages, supplies, and a share of the prize ship's eventual sale.
