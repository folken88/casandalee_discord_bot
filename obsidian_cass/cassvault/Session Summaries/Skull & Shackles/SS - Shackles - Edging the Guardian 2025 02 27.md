## Summary

The party interrogates a captured Bronze Fleet sailor and learns of a major slaver operation involving Chelaxian forces planning to meet in the southwestern Shackles within a month. After selling loot and restocking at Goats Head, the crew sets sail to investigate a mysterious treasure map pointing to Arrowhead Chain Islands. Upon landing, they discover abandoned dinghies with coal dust and evidence of mining equipment, leading them to an abandoned cave system with an elaborate pulley system. Inside, they find four crushed corpses and a massive 8-foot stone golem guarding a mysterious metal portal—which they accidentally trigger while investigating with a mage hand, causing the golem to cast sleep on most of the party.
