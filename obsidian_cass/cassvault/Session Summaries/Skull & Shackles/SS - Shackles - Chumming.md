# Shackles - Chumming

## Summary

On the fifth day aboard the Wormwood, the party spots a cluster of wrecked ships on rocky islets flying a yellow plague flag. Master Scourge sends the party (minus Sha-Feng) to investigate via dinghy. They explore the shipwreck graveyard, finding the remains of stranded sailors who cut their own throats after running out of food. The party discovers significant loot including magical boots, a Besmara-blessed pirate hat, a pipe bomb, deadly poison, and a magical satchel. They fight off skeletons emerging from a bone pile aboard one of the wrecked frigates. [[Bujon]] is introduced to the campaign, having been locked in the Wormwood's box for a week for talking too much.

## Key Events

- [[Bujon]] is introduced: he was kept in "the box" for a week and released to use his Alter Winds ability to change the ship's wind direction
- [[Holden]] spots the yellow plague flag on wrecked ships and identifies it as signaling illness
- The party is sent via dinghy to investigate; sharks are spotted circling beneath them
- [[Riviera]] identifies a large shark (14-15 feet) from her knowledge nature check
- They find two dead sailors in matching uniforms who cut their own throats after being stranded and starving
- [[Holden]] picks up a Besmara-blessed pirate hat from one corpse; it grants +2 on acrobatics and climb checks
- [[Riviera]] identifies numerous items: boots of landing, a Numerian pry bar (+6 strength checks to open doors, also a +1 magic club), lung taker poison (worth 1000 gold), and a magical satchel (reduces item weight by 75%)
- The party fights four skeletons that emerge from a bone pile on a wrecked frigate; they learn slashing and piercing do minimal damage to skeletons
- [[Storgrim]] uses the Numerian pry bar to effectively smash skeletons; [[Bujon]] destroys one with Shocking Grasp
- [[Riviera]] shoots a skeleton with her musket, finishing the combat
- The party finds Masterwork studded leather armor, cutlasses, and a Masterwork rapier in the bone pile
- They also find items from distant lands: Sherwood jorts (magical shorts that reduce armor check penalty), an enchanter starter kit, and sparkling water from Ustalav

## Combat

- **Skeletons (x4)** at the wrecked frigate bone pile. DR 5/bludgeoning made slashing and piercing weapons nearly useless. Storgrim used the pry bar, Bujon used Shocking Grasp, and Riviera used her musket to dispatch them.
