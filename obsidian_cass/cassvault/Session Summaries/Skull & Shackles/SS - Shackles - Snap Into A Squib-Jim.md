## Summary

The party cleared a dangerous road near Rickety Squibs, defended a watchtower crew, and cooked excellent cayman barbecue. Upon returning to town, they encountered the mischievous monkey 'Little Vaughan' (now nicknamed 'Jack'), who had stolen a Numerian vibro-knife and a cookbook. The group spent time upgrading their equipment with blood cayman hide armor and discussed new ship combat mechanics. They discovered an unconscious drunk monkey in a bush and now carry it aboard their ship.
