# Shackles - Snap Into A Squib-Jim

## Summary

The party completes clearing the western road and arrives at the southern garrison watchtower on the Rickety Squibs peninsula. They deliver the news that the path is clear, cook caiman barbecue for the garrison crew, and spend the night. During the night watch, [[Bujon]] spots a massive Chelish man-of-war sailing past in a distant storm -- a terrifying sight far beyond the TPK's ability to challenge. The next morning, the party heads back toward Rickety Squibs, passing through the dwarven ruins and squabble monkey territory. The GM introduces the ship combat system and crew positioning mechanics.
