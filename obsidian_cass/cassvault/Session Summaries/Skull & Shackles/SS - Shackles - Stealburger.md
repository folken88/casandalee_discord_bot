# Shackles - Stealburger

## Summary

The party finishes a boarding action against a Rahadoumi ship alongside the Wormwood crew, then celebrates a victory feast. The captured ship is named the Man's Promise. The party is assigned to its prize crew under the hated officers Plugg and Scourge, who plan to sail the ship to Port Peril to sell it. However, Bujon discovers they are sailing the wrong direction -- toward Rickety Squibs, a pirate dry dock where ships are disguised and renamed. The party begins plotting a mutiny, which they code-name "mayonnaise" to avoid saying the real word aloud. They recruit Albear and Sandara to their side, while Ambrose the cook cryptically warns Sha-Feng to "unfigure" what he knows.
