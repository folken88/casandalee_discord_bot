## Summary

The party completed exploration of Gol Khazak, fighting undead and encountering a diseased mushroom farm. Sha-Feng was killed by an undead creature wielding a polearm during combat and his head was severed. The party burned the mushroom farm, retrieved Sha-Feng's remains, and traveled to a druid named Thraorn to arrange reincarnation. Sha-Feng was reincarnated as a hobgoblin after rolling on the expanded reincarnation table.
