# Shackles - Just Dont Hit The Rope

## Summary

The party (minus Vaughan and Riviera, who stay behind filling water barrels) sets out across Bonewrack Isle to rescue crew captured by the grindylows. They buy kumquats from the Bahari Kofi and trek through swamp and jungle. Along the way they discover a sunken ship's crow's nest visible offshore. On the western coast they find the "Castaway Stockade" -- an abandoned hut on stilts with a hanging corpse and a note reading "stay away." The corpse turns out to be a ghoul, which attacks when disturbed. The party fights and destroys it, then investigates the hut, finding a chest with minor magic items. Screams are heard from inside, confirming the island's dangers.
