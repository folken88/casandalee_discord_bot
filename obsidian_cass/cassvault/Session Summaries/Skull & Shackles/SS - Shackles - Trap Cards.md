# Shackles - Trap Cards

## Summary

The party continues life aboard the Wormwood. After distributing loot from clearing bilge rats, they shop at the quartermaster store. During nighttime actions, [[Ser-Toche]] sneaks into [[Master Scourge]]'s quarters, triggers a poisoned harpoon trap, and nearly dies. [[Riviera]] saves him with healing. When [[Slippery Syl Lonigan]] catches Riviera leaving Scourge's room, Ser-Toche assassinates Syl and stages the scene to look like she triggered the trap. [[Holden]] creates a distraction by performing on a table while [[Sha-Feng]] influences [[Conchobar Turlach Shortstone|Conchobar]] with poetry.

## Key Events

- The party sells loot from previous bilge encounters to the quartermaster [[Kroop|Grok]], splitting about 28 gold per person
- Shopping session at the quartermaster store: weapons, armor, potions, and supplies purchased
- [[Sha-Feng]] recites a Chinese poem to [[Conchobar Turlach Shortstone|Conchobar]], successfully influencing the gnome bard
- [[Ser-Toche]] picks the lock to Scourge and Kipper's quarters during nighttime
- Ser-Toche triggers a poisoned harpoon trap, taking 10 damage and 2 points of Constitution damage from poison, dropping to below zero HP
- [[Riviera]] notices Ser-Toche slipped into the room, enters and stabilizes him with a cure light wounds potion, then heals him with a second spell
- [[Slippery Syl Lonigan]] spots Riviera leaving Scourge's room and threatens to report her
- Ser-Toche sneaks up behind Syl and assassinates her with a sneak attack for 13 damage, killing her
- The party stages the crime scene: Ser-Toche retrieves the harpoon from Scourge's room and stabs it through Syl's body to make it look like she triggered the trap herself
- [[Holden]] provides a critical distraction by jumping on a table, performing, and drawing the crew's attention while Riviera and Ser-Toche handle the situation
