# Shackles - Edging Blivious

## Summary

A massive storm strikes the Man's Promise. During the storm, grindylow sea creatures attack the ship, putting multiple crew members to sleep with spells and dragging several overboard -- including Sandara, Rosie, and Kabar. The party fights to repel the grindylows while also keeping the ship from capsizing. Sarah spots a reef ahead and the party scrambles to reduce sail, but the ship runs aground on a coral reef near Bonewrack Isle. Plugg blames Sarah for the grounding and orders the party ashore in a dinghy to find fresh water. The party discovers a village of bird-folk called the Bahari Kofi (tangu-like creatures) who have an uneasy truce with the local grindylows. The Kofi warn that captured crew will be sacrificed to a dark god within two days and describe dangerous "bad people" (ghouls) in the island's interior. The party fills water barrels, trades gunpowder for kumquats, and prepares to cross the island to rescue their captured crewmates.
