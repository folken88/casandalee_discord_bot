## Summary

The party participates in a massive three-ship naval engagement against the Chelish warship Veil of Duskhilm and Bronze Fleet vessels, securing victory through coordinated boarding actions and spellcasting. After the battle, they sail to Tidewater Rock where Storgrim marries Lady Augusta in a ceremonial wedding, and subsequently ventures into the dungeons below to confront her zombified ex-husband, defeating him to recover valuable magical items including the Tidewater Sidepiece ring and a Tallybuckle belt.
