# Speak with Skull — Skull & Shackles 2025-05-29

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=2AERbrEysxQ)

**Characters Present:** Holden, Storgrim, Sha-Feng, Vaughan, Riviera, Bujon, Rhyarca, Sorak

## Summary

The party cleared the final combat encounters in Gol Khazak's mushroom chamber, defeating undead dwarves including the corrupted Lord of Gol Khazak. They recovered important documents including a submersible schematic, navigational charts, and an oath sender (fire-destruction folder). Storgrim identified evidence of Alcenar dwarven technology and an underwater vessel design. The party learned that Sorak's sahuagin tribe knows of similar vessels and that Tidewater Rock fortress is currently under siege.
