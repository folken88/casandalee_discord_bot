# Carrion Crown Campaign Journal - Session Summary

The party began the session at Brook Nook, where they eliminated a nest of tiny evils and ghouls, looting the location thoroughly. While the main group was away, Mayanda and Denva conducted an intensive crafting session at the forge, fueled by heavy drug use, ultimately completing and upgrading several magical items. The party's craftspeople finished multiple projects: Dinvaya completed an Amulet +2 and Ring +2, Kai upgraded a Ring +2, and Josh's character combined the Tyrant's Band with another earring to create a powerful plus-two protection item. Before departing to resupply in Chastel, Meyanda mentioned her cousin was fighting a god—an ominous hint at larger conflicts beyond the party's current scope.

With their equipment upgraded, the party departed eastward from Booknook toward their next objective, approximately eight miles away. As they traveled the main road between Chastel and Southern Ustalav, Kate Blackwood noted the unusual absence of wagon traffic on what should have been a busy trade route—a troubling sign of the undead cult's impact on the region. The party soon discovered Thistle Farm, a small family-owned operation normally staffed by 20-25 hands during peak season. A partially devoured horse carcass lay in the road at the farm's entrance, its blood and guts scattered across darkened earth, indicating it had been killed and consumed by a group of creatures.

Upon approaching the farm's perimeter, the party discovered clay faces embedded in the wheat field, spaced 30-40 feet apart—at least three were visible. Gaspar smashed one with his golden hammer, destroying it. The party cast protective buffs including Cat's Grace, Shield of Faith, and Weapon of Awe before proceeding. They found a farmhouse with its door wide open, interior trashed with splintered furniture, and bloody footprints throughout the dining room—clear evidence of violence and undead activity.

Rather than conduct a methodical building-by-building search, the party decided to blow the scrap horn to draw out creatures and initiate combat. The strategy proved effective, triggering an encounter with multiple undead skeletons. The battle was fierce and decisive: Rodney Danger Smith shattered skeletal necks and exploded skulls with precise arrow fire, Kovira incinerated undead with scorching rays that reduced them to blackened dust and shards, and the melee fighters crushed skeletons with void shard attacks and holy smites. Multiple fast skeleton variants fell to the party's coordinated assault, their bodies exploding and shattering under holy damage and flaming arrows.

The grim discoveries at Thistle Farm revealed the scope of the necromancer's atrocities: approximately 60 victims had been killed roughly three weeks prior, dismembered, and their body parts used to construct the undead creatures now plaguing the region. Among the gore, Kate Blackwood discovered an Inheritor blade sticking out of a pile in the grain silo. The party's worst suspicions were confirmed—the cult's trajectory through wealthy locations including Evergreen Haven manor and Northgard fortress suggested those locations had likely suffered similar fates, with Northgard fortress almost certainly overrun with undead and its inhabitants dead for approximately a month.
