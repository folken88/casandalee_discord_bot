# Iron Gods - Minds are Blown

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=3kTAwGppFHk)

## Summary

The party raids a Technic League facility previously used by Captain Garon, searching locked rooms and looting his body for powerful items including a belt of mighty constitution, headband of vast intelligence +4, a grenade launcher, and a gold-plated key to the palace. They discover a secret room containing a massive techno-magical battery capable of draining magic items. Behind a magically sealed door, they find and kill a captive Illithid that attempted to cast Dominate Monster on Ulfred. They also rescue Rag, a hero from the Battle of Scrapwall, and a catfolk woman from a different tribe than Nomkath, both tortured and experimented on by Garon. The party plans to destroy the facility by dropping the battery into the sewers via Passwall and detonating it.
