# Carrion Crown Campaign - Session Summary: "Muffin Topslapper 2024 09 08"

The party navigated through the treacherous Shudderwood Forest, moving through territory controlled by the Broken Ones werewolf clan—a group they had previously stabilized through violence and monetary support. As they traveled east and southeast with moderate confidence in their direction, the party decided to make camp at the Standing Stones, a historically significant location with known ties to the Whispering Way. This decision would prove fateful, as the stones began to emanate an undulating bluish-green light as darkness fell. When Savage cast *Detect Magic*, she was overwhelmed by the intensity of the magical aura—rated a perfect 10 out of 10. Conetta used her druid abilities to communicate with local wildlife, discovering a red squirrel near the stones, while Dinvaya's detection revealed the source: pure necromantic magic of the highest concentration. Recognizing the danger, the party immediately abandoned the campsite and relocated east to safer ground.

The following morning, the party resumed their journey toward Ascanor Lodge, their intended destination. As they traveled through the woods near a creek approximately one day's journey from the lodge, they discovered a grim scene: the smell of death and multiple humanoid bootprints (8-9 different individuals) scattered about the area, with evidence of people standing and milling around. The party's investigation revealed a series of brutally murdered corpses, each bearing the hallmarks of a coordinated hunting operation. The bodies included a thin, scraggly-bearded man nailed to a pine tree with a 6-inch iron spike through his forearm and wolfsbane stuffed in his mouth, and a farmer who had been shot multiple times and hung from a tree by one hand, also with wolfsbane forced into his mouth. The pattern was clear: someone was systematically hunting and executing people in the forest, using wolfsbane as a deliberate provocation—likely targeting or framing werewolves.

As the party processed this discovery, they discussed their strategy for approaching Ascanor Lodge and their target. Intelligence from a previous team revealed that their quarry was extremely dangerous: when the earlier group attempted to reason with the target, he became hostile, turned invisible or gaseous, summoned a powerful spectral entity, threatened lives, and attacked even when told he was free to leave. The party debated multiple approaches—whether to use deception, attempt negotiation, employ magical countermeasures like *Entangle*, *Babble*, *Lull*, and *Dispel Magic*, or resort to direct confrontation with grappling tactics. They also discussed the possibility that their target might attempt to escape using the same supernatural abilities that had allowed him to evade the previous team.

The party made several notable preparations for their arrival at Ascanor Lodge. Muffin Topslapper equipped judge's robes and applied apple cheek concentrate to appear younger and more authoritative. The group had acquired a pink axolotl companion during their travels and obtained potions of *Disguise Self* to help disguise Wolf Span, one of their companions, in his original human form or other appearances. They also carried Corporal Koval's skin—an item with necromantic properties whose true purpose remained uncertain—along with various other equipment including a 6-inch iron spike and wolfsbane removed from the corpses. With their preparations complete and their strategy debated, the party continued toward Ascanor Lodge, ready to confront whatever dangers awaited them in this dark corner of Ustalav.
