# Session Summary: Riptide Cove Investigation

The party's exploration of Bonewrack Isle brought them to Riptide Cove, where they encountered a massive swarm of grindylows defending their territory. The initial combat was chaotic and brutal—the party set a enormous mound of trash ablaze, creating thick obscuring fumes that hindered both sides. During the battle, party members repeatedly failed their saves against the mist, forcing allies to drag each other to safety while one brave (or foolish) party member swam directly into the lake amid the grindylow onslaught. Despite the confusion, the party emerged victorious, with Storgrim delivering a devastating 30-damage slash that sent one grindylow erupting in blood and viscera, while Sha-Feng's sonic knuckle punch obliterated another creature entirely. After regrouping on a nearby beach to heal, the party observed five minutes of relative calm—their first real respite since arriving at the cove.

With the immediate threat neutralized, Ser-Toche volunteered for a stealth reconnaissance mission, swimming out into the cove around noon with a borrowed Ring of Swimming. Her solo scout proved invaluable: she discovered a complex underwater cove system featuring honeycomb coral formations, ancient volcanic structures, and multiple depth pools. The western entry appeared shallow but dropped suddenly to 40-50 feet, while the eastern chamber showed clear signs of grindylow activity. Riviera followed up with a more thorough exploration, diving deep into the tunnel system approximately 30 feet down and discovering a side passage worn smooth by frequent grindylow traffic. Pushing deeper into the 75-foot-deep honeycomb passages, Riviera found human bones scattered throughout and identified the East/Southeast passage as the primary grindylow thoroughfare.

The most significant discovery came when Riviera located a macabre shrine: a skeletal dwarf corpse decorated by grindylows over many years, layered with multiple sets of armor and equipment, clutching an algae-covered crossbow. The body yielded valuable loot—sealed wack juice, a slime-covered ring, brass knuckles (later identified as the magical Rolling Sea Knuckles), and a glass tube containing a mysterious pink putty substance. As Riviera continued deeper into the completely submerged cave system with its long, winding tunnels, she detected movement approximately 40-50 feet away in the darkness: armed creatures approaching. She wisely retreated to the surface to report her findings.

The party regrouped to plan their infiltration strategy, facing a critical challenge: most party members are human and require light to see underwater, making stealth difficult. The party debated whether to wait an hour or two to allow enemies to lower their guard, or to attack immediately before the grindylows could harm any prisoners they might be holding. Meanwhile, Shaung identified the magical items recovered from the dwarf's corpse—the Rolling Sea Knuckles provided unpredictable bonuses and penalties, while the ring proved to be a lesser Ring of Luck with similarly variable magical properties. The party also discovered that at least two members lacked strong swimming abilities, a significant concern for the underwater assault ahead. As they prepared for what promised to be a dangerous infiltration into the grindylow lair, the party weighed their options carefully, knowing that prisoners' lives hung in the balance.
