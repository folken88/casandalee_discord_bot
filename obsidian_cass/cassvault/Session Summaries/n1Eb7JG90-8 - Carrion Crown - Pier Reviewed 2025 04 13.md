## Summary

Following the Battle of Feldgrau, the party destroys the Whispering Way's cult workshop by fireballing it, collapsing it with silx explosive, and consecrating the wreckage to prevent ghoul fever spread. Kate Blackwood is teleported back to Lepidstadt to report to Judge Daramid. The remaining party rides southeast toward the Soy Vida region, heading for the Kinstripe Ferry and Carrion Hill to chase Whispering Way cultists who fled with a green statue to trade in the swamps. The party also needs to warn Lepidstadt about reactivated Whispering Way agents.
