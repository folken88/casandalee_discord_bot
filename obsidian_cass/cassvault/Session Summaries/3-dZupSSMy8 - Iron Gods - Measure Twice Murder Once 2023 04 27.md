# Iron Gods - Measure Twice Murder Once

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=3-dZupSSMy8)

## Summary

The party regrouped at their safe house in the slums of Starfall with their new ally Blackout, a former Technic League operative. Blackout provided intelligence on the Technic League captains, and the group debated which captain to target next. They decided on Garton (the alchemist) but also discussed Abernathy (a robot expert) as an easier target. Nomkath used Cassandralee's recordings of overheard city conversations to gather intelligence, learning about a missing Technic League captain named Sila. They also used Cassandralee to access the brain-in-a-jar memories of Therus Halyard, uncovering Garton's associates and the revelation that Connor Bane had rescued Val from Garton years ago. The party decided to leave Starfall for a few days while waiting for the bounty hunter Hearsick to return to town.
