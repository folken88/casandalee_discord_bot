# Iron Gods Campaign Journal: Backseat Hammering
## Session Summary - September 1, 2022

The party gathered at their campsite approximately 12-15 miles from the Dominion Hive to plan their assault on the brain-creature infestation plaguing the region. After extensive debate, they rejected stealth and magical fast travel in favor of a direct, aggressive approach: they would drive north in their powerful off-roader vehicle—a machine boasting approximately 1200 horsepower—to strike the northern group of Dominion-controlled creatures before potentially hitting the southern group the same day. Croctaw had previously scouted the area using Commune with Nature spells while in bird form, revealing orc and kassaf patrols positioned on both sides of the river valley, with guard stations concentrated on the north side. The party learned that these creatures were being controlled by small brain-like parasites inhabiting their skulls, which communicated across vast distances as part of a hive mind collective.

The party mounted up and drove north with characteristic loudness, abandoning any pretense of subtlety. They encountered the northern guard group—approximately half a dozen orcs organized in groups of three, accompanied by brain-creature spell casters that operated independently of their host bodies. Combat erupted with devastating efficiency. Ulfred's hammer crushed a skeletal creature's spine after it had already absorbed over 200 HP of damage. Zuma unleashed a full burst of icicle attacks against a black panther-like Dominion creature, striking it five times in the face, eye, and neck until it fell. Tokala's chainsaw dismembered an orc during the melee. Nomkath's magic missiles collapsed an invisible brain skidder creature. Luna's rapid-fire shots brought down a Kassatha within touch range. Isuma unleashed a devastating barrage using Gravity Bow and Rapid Shot, her attacks exploding an invisible brain creature into a grotesque pile of meat and legs when it finally became visible.

The battle raged on as the party coordinated their ranged and melee attacks against the remaining enemies. An orc fighter was reduced to approximately 1 HP before Olbryn and Zuma finished it off. A Dominion-trained displacer beast fell to the party's combined efforts. Multiple additional brain creatures were systematically eliminated through coordinated ranged attacks. When the dust settled, the northern guard group had been completely annihilated, and the party claimed their spoils: two sets of +2 leather armor looted from the defeated Dominion forces, and the corpse of the displacer beast, which they strapped to their wagon for later processing. The hide could be crafted into a cloak, armor, or boots; the eyes—which glowed yellow—could be fashioned into a necklace or charm; the teeth held monetary value; and the exotic meat would fetch a good price. Rusty great axes and worn hide armor from the orc corpses were deemed not worth the effort to carry.

With the northern group eliminated and resources still available, the party prepared to pursue their secondary objective: striking the southern group before day's end. The Dominion's hive mind would soon recognize the loss of its northern sentries, but the party had already proven their combat superiority and showed no signs of slowing down.
