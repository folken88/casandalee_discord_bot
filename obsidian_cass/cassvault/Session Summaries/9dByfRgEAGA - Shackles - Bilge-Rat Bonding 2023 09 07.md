# Session Summary: Shackles Campaign - Bilge-Rat Bonding

The party's second day aboard the Wormwood began with a violent confrontation in the ship's lower decks. After encountering pirates blocking their path, the party engaged them in combat, nearly killing several crew members before deciding to drag the unconscious Tilly Brackett topside as a warning to others. They looted 60 gold pieces from their defeated opponents. When Master Scourge confronted them about the fight making them late for duties, the party explained their circumstances well enough that he reduced their punishment from three lashes to merely a warning—though lashes would still come at dusk.

Throughout the day, the party members strategically dispersed across the Wormwood to build relationships with the crew. One character bonded with Giffer Tibbs through acrobatic displays while swabbing the deck, while another earned Crimson Cogward's favor by recounting dark tales of heroism. Sha-Feng impressed the ship's cook Ambrose with a poem about agricultural labor called "Sympathy for the Peasants," earning an invitation to socialize after hours. Holden attempted to recruit the experienced sailor Cog—a former gem merchant reduced to swab rank—though his poor diplomacy check was salvaged by strong roleplay. That evening, Ser-Toche successfully picked a locked locker in the quartermaster's store, acquiring alchemist fire, antitoxin, a bullseye lantern, and 15 gold pieces. Riviera recovered her fiddleback from Rosie Cusswell, while other crew members bonded over rum and conversation.

The session's tone shifted dramatically when Jack Scrimshaw reported being bitten by something in the bilges while draining water and moving flour. Master Scourge immediately ordered the party—the "new bloods"—into the bilges with a grim ultimatum: find and kill whatever attacked Scrimshaw, or don't return alive. The party descended into the dark, flooded depths of the ship where they encountered and defeated multiple giant spiders and dire rats in brutal combat. Ser-Toche cleaved one spider in half with a devastating blow, while Holden punched a dire rat with such force it was stunned and fell over. Oak bifurcated another bilge spider with a claw attack, and an acrobatic party member stomped another rat's head after jumping on it. The party emerged victorious from their first true trial aboard the Wormwood, having proven their worth through blood and violence.
