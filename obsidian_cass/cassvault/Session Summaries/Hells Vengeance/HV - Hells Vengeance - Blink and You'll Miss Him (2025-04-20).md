## Summary

The party infiltrated St. Ilnius Fountain, a holy Iomedaen site, to defile it and soak the severed dragon head (Parnonorix) in its waters as part of a ritual. They discovered a hidden temple containing five celestial statues that animated as combat-ready archons. The session was dominated by an extended battle against these powerful divine beings, with Bruce's blink spell proving extraordinarily effective at negating massive incoming damage. The party had made significant progress in defeating or severely wounding several enemies by session end.
