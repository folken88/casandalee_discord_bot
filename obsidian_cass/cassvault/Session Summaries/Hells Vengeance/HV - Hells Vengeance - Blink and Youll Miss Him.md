## Summary

The party has progressed to Saint Ilia's Fountain, an Iomedaean holy site that they must defile as part of the Tathlum ritual involving the severed gold dragon's head. They are 14th level and on approximately day two of a five-day deadline to complete the ritual. The party has already fought through much of the site -- defeating a water elemental, exploring caves with bioluminescent mushrooms, and finding living quarters. They prepare buffs (protection from energy communal for fire and electricity, heroism, echolocation, gravity bow) and advance. The session recaps the campaign arc: the Hellknights found Iomedae's sword Hearts Edge, the Glorious Reclamation started over its ownership, the party killed the gold dragon Parnonorix (who personally knew Iomedae), took his head, and are now defiling the fountain. Combat begins with a powerful angel archer across a 40-foot chasm dealing devastating damage.
