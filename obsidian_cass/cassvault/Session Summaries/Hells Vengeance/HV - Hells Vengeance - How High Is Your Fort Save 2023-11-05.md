## Summary

The party assaults Citadel Dinyar, fighting through Glorious Reclamation forces including paladins, squires, and summoned dinosaurs. They face Lord Grais Fan, the high cleric leading the fortress defense, along with an invisible wizard who summoned multiple creatures.
