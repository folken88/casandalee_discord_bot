# Hells Vengeance - Anti-Magic Field-Day 2025 06 01

**Campaign:** Hells Vengeance | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=EU9HgsBwyfM)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Jamal, Draymus, Reese, Bruce

## Summary

Continuing the battle at Saint Ilia's Fountain, the party fights the remnants of the Glorious Reclamation defenders. Chadriel lies dead at -255 HP under black tentacles in the center of the temple. The party debates murdering Draymus as a joke ("do we succeed right now and murder Drus and save ourselves forever?"). Reese kills Bianca with five arrows (four pinning her head and face), Bruce retreats and drinks a cure critical. Jason casts unspeakable horror on Nash the angel shotgunner, whose body dissolves into a purplish pile of mush in golden armor. Jaffael, the angel cleric with a bastard sword resembling Hearts Edge, retreats across a 40-foot chasm. A powerful angel archer appears on the far side and devastates the party with multi-arrow volleys, sending Quap back to hell and dropping Jamal to -12 HP. Draymus uses his black tentacles to drag Jamal's unconscious body around a corner to safety. The party regroups behind cover, debating whether to push forward against the archer or heal up and use word of recall to escape.

  - Characters: Jamal, Reese, Quap

  - Characters: Jamal, Reese, Bruce
