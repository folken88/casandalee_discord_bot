## Summary

The party infiltrated St. Ilnius Fountain, a holy Iomedaen temple, to defile it and complete a dark ritual by soaking the severed head of Parnoneryx (a gold dragon they killed at Citadel Dinyar) in its waters. After discovering a secret chamber containing statues of five celestial beings and a massive mural of Iomedae, they triggered combat when Draymus crossed the threshold. Five greater celestials awakened as animated constructs: Chadriel (archangel with a maul), Bianca (eladrin with an estoc), a gunslinger angel (dual pistols), a shotgun-wielding angel (with sonic rounds), and a cleric angel (Jaffael). The party survived multiple near-death experiences, particularly relying heavily on Bruce's Blink spell to mitigate catastrophic damage. They managed to kill Chadriel and are currently in a grinding fight against the remaining four celestials.
