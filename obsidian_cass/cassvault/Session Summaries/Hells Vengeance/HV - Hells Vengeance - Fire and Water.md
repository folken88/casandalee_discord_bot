## Summary

Following the assault on Citadel Dinyar, the party deals with the aftermath of killing a gold dragon. They debate how to collect bounties on dead Glorious Reclamation members and profit from the dragon's body. Jason summons a contract devil named Teru to obtain an adamantine fireproof skinning dagger so Reese can skin the gold dragon. The party worries about devils learning they have a dead gold dragon. They plan to teleport to the capital (Egorian) with the dragon's head and other loot while leaving bodies for the state to handle. The party also discusses needing to desecrate a sacred fountain as part of their ongoing mission.
