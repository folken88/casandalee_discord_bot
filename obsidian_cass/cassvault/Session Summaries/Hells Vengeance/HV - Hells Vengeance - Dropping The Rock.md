## Summary

The party plans their assault on Citadel Dinyar, the Hellknight fortress held by the Glorious Reclamation. They are hiding in a rope trick somewhere in the woods after previously clearing much of the basement and fighting a gold dragon outside the citadel. The session focuses heavily on strategic planning -- debating teleportation, air walk, bag of holding troop deployment, and "ODST dropping" from the top of the castle. Jason's devils are part of the force. Draymus can teleport the party but not the Hellknight allies due to creature limits. The party considers multiple approaches including sneaking in via the previously-used secret basement entrance and a top-down assault.
