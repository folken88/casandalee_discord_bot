# Iron Gods Campaign Journal - Session: Repeat Offenders (September 22, 2022)

The party awoke in their camp within the formerly hunted area, having defeated the outer patrol enemies the previous day. After a tense first watch where Ulfred's exceptional perception (rolling 41 despite armor penalties) kept the camp secure, the night took an unsettling turn. During the second watch, both Tokala and Nomkath experienced shared prophetic dreams of hiding in a shifting forest while mysterious entities searched for them psychically. Both instinctively shielded their minds and found paths opening before them—a disturbing sign that Dominion forces were actively hunting them through psychic means. The party discussed their findings and resolved to make their move toward the hive the following day, but only after all members had fully rested.

After thorough preparation and spell casting—including Mage Armor, Communal Vision, and Resist Energy (fire)—the party set out toward the Dominion cave. Their initial plan to approach via chariot proved disastrous when Ulfred's driving check failed spectacularly, sending the vehicle rolling sideways into a nearby river. The party was swept downstream by the current and headwind, losing precious time before regrouping and continuing on foot through the forest valley. By approximately noon, they arrived at the Dominion cave entrance with time to spare for final preparations. The party applied extensive buffs including Fly, Blur, Heroism, Long Strider, and Hound's Blood potions, equipping themselves for a frontal assault.

Upon entering the cave, the party discovered a massive 70-foot-wide chamber filled with scrap metal and debris. Hidden beneath piles of junk was an enormous damaged Annihilator robot—a larger, more devastating version of the Hellion chassis they had encountered before. As the party approached, the robot suddenly stood up, triggering immediate combat. Luna opened fire with deadly accuracy, her favorite enemy bonus and rapid shot technique proving effective; while most shots bounced harmlessly off the robot's armored body, a critical hit near its eye sent sparks flying. The chamber itself began to collapse around them as the battle intensified, forcing the party to fight not only the massive mechanical threat but also the crumbling environment itself. The encounter had begun in earnest, with the fate of the campaign hanging in the balance.
