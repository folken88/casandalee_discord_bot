## Summary

The session opened with reincarnating a dead party member (Blackout/Gavrilo). Kroktah cast Reincarnate using 1,000 gold worth of oils funded by Luna's considerable wealth. The reincarnation table returned human, which was both anticlimactic and ironic given Blackout's desire to fit into society. Blackout woke up in the Sewer Sanctum, discovered he was now human (losing darkvision), and processed the change over Brow Brew. The party then distributed Crassus's captured gear--an extensive haul including bracers of armor +4, the bastard sword Hater (+2 human bane), headband of vast intelligence +4, hip fire band, rod of metamagic quicken, Technic League captain badge, com set, death wine potions, and the Panorama Celestial ring (constant true seeing, fly at will, teleports, +5 perception). After much deliberation, the ring went to Ulfred, the quicken rod and hip fire band to Olbryn, and the headband to Nomkath. Luna and Nomkath fenced items totaling 28,601 gold, leaving the party about 22,000 short of Hearsick's 50,000 gold asking price.
