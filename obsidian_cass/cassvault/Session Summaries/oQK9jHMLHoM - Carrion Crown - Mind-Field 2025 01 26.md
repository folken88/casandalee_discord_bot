# Carrion Crown - Mind-Field 2025 01 26

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=oQK9jHMLHoM)

**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

## Summary

CP-USS interrogates a captured Whispering Way cultist who is slowly turning into a werewolf. Kate Blackwood uses detect thoughts to brain-bake the cultist while the party restrains him and triggers his mental traps. They extract extensive intelligence about the cult's forces in Feldgrau, the ritual components being transported south, and the cult's connection to events in Lepidstadt. The session is entirely focused on the interrogation and planning.
