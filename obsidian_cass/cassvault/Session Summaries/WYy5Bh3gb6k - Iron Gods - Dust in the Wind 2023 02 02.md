# Iron Gods - Dust in the Wind 2023 02 02

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=WYy5Bh3gb6k)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Luna, Akradenn, Ulfred

## Summary

The party departs Torch for Starfall via wind walk, leaving their truck behind for repairs and upgrades. They fly across Numeria and get their first aerial view of Starfall and Silver Mount. Upon arriving in the city, they shelter in an Earth Daughters safe house in the slums, where they encounter a decomposing body and prepare for their infiltration of the Technic League's seat of power.

## Key Events

- Meyanda upgrades Mr Brow's chainsaw with a larger speaker system (subwoofer), granting a +4 bonus to Dazzling Display against targets that can hear it. She also repairs broken teeth on the chainsaw.
  - Characters: Mr Brow, Meyanda
  - Location: Torch
- The party leaves the truck in Torch for repairs and charging. Cassandlee drains 200 charges from the truck, bringing herself to 490 out of 1000 charges. She performs a final radar check showing clear skies.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Cassandlee
  - Location: Torch
- The party purchases brown cloaks to disguise themselves as smog clouds during wind walk, since Starfall is covered in pollution. Olbryn's white robes would have been conspicuous.
  - Characters: Olbryn, Nomkath
  - Location: Torch
- Crocodi casts wind walk on the entire party in two castings. The party flies across Numeria, seeing Scrap Wall, Torch's purple beam, and the landscape from altitude for the first time.
  - Characters: Luna, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Ulfred, Akradenn
  - Location: Numeria airspace
- Cassandlee is transformed by wind walk alongside the party, surprising Crocodi because the spell only works on living things with souls, confirming Cassandlee has some form of soul.
  - Characters: Cassandlee, Luna
  - Location: Numeria airspace
- The party gets their first view of Starfall: a polluted city built around the base of Silver Mount, a massive unnatural mound with a jagged stone shard sticking out of it. The city is surrounded by fortified military outposts.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Akradenn, Luna
  - Location: Approaching Starfall
- Nomkath shares knowledge of Silver Mount's history: legends say it is a dead god, a dead monster, or an evil device buried by ancient barbarians. The Technic League rose to power about 100 years ago.
  - Characters: Nomkath
  - Location: Approaching Starfall
- The party arrives in Starfall and shelters in a tiny apartment belonging to an Earth Daughters contact in the slums. They find a heavily decomposed body with plants growing out of it in an adjacent room.
  - Location: Starfall slums
