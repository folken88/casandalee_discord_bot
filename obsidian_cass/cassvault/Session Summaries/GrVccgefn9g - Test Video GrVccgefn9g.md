# Campaign IG - Session Summary

The party received a critical briefing from Dolkha and McGinnis regarding a dangerous underwater cave system that claimed the life—or at least the presence—of the dwarf Conchobar Turlach Shortstone. Conchobar had ventured into the location approximately seven to eight hours prior, expected to return by 2:00 AM, but had not yet emerged. The cave entrance requires water-breathing magic to safely navigate, making it an exceptionally perilous undertaking for unprepared adventurers.

The briefing revealed a grim history: four previous expeditions into the cave had ended in failure. A group of halflings, followers of the mysterious Bry, local brigands and thugs, and others had all attempted entry—none returned successfully. This pattern of disappearances underscored the genuine danger awaiting the party. However, hope came in the form of Joram, a scout who had previously explored the location and survived to report his findings.

Joram's reconnaissance provided crucial intelligence about what lay within. He described encountering a non-magical construct (which he initially called a golem but later corrected), multiple creatures including a large woolly beast, and uncivilized yet intelligent humanoids inhabiting the cave system. Most remarkably, Joram discovered a massive wall constructed entirely of glow kite—a rare sky metal—complete with a central door. Through negotiation and deal-making with the cave's inhabitants, Joram had secured passage beyond this formidable barrier.

To prepare for the expedition, the party consulted with a female dwarf blacksmith in the settlement, who directed them to Joram for water-breathing preparations. Joram agreed to cast an extended water-breathing spell on the party, providing a combined total of 32 hours of aquatic respiration—sufficient time to explore the cave system thoroughly and return safely. With this magical preparation complete, the party now stands ready to descend into the depths and uncover the fate of Conchobar Turlach Shortstone.
