# Carrion Crown Campaign Journal - Session: Wands in the Dark

## Preparation and Mounting Pressure

The party found themselves in a race against time as they learned that a paladin of Iomedae had been captured by vampires in Northgarde, with only approximately two days before their companion would be lost forever—or worse, turned. While a contingent of Iomedae paladins was en route to Northgarde after Farrah Richton identified the location of a holy white metal helmet artifact, the party realized the reinforcements wouldn't arrive in time. After two days of crafting magical items with the help of Denva, including combining multiple cloaks into enhanced versions with improved properties and resistance bonuses, the adventurers made the difficult decision to proceed without waiting for backup. They left a detailed message at the Fasma statue in Northgarde informing the incoming paladins of their eight-person force, time of entry, and the dire situation they were entering.

## Acquisition and Underwater Preparations

Recognizing that a direct assault on the vampire stronghold would be suicidal, the party devised an infiltration strategy involving an underwater approach. They traveled to Lepidstadt to acquire specialized equipment for their aquatic insertion: scrolls of water breathing with capacity for seven to ten targets, potions of monkey fish to aid swimming, air bubble potions, and various other magical aids. The party distributed these resources carefully among their members, including Kate Blackwood, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas, Gaspar, and Kovira. They also secured a murol comb from Willowbrook that granted eight hours of water-breathing capability when consumed, and discussed alternative solutions such as the Touch of the Sea spell and rings of swimming to ensure all party members could survive the river current approach to the fortress's north side.

## The Assault Begins

With their equipment secured and buffs applied, the party made their way to the river north of Northgarde and prepared for their daring underwater insertion. As they entered the water and began their approach against the current, combat erupted. Kai proved devastatingly effective in close quarters, cleanly decapitating a vampire spawn with a powerful bastard sword strike and severing another enemy's head in rapid succession. Rodney Danger Smith provided crucial ranged support, his arrows cutting down multiple zombies and coales in quick succession. The party's careful preparation—combining magical cloaks for enhanced endurance and speed, distributing potions and scrolls, and planning their approach methodically—appeared to be paying off as they fought their way toward their captured companion and the vampire stronghold's secrets.
