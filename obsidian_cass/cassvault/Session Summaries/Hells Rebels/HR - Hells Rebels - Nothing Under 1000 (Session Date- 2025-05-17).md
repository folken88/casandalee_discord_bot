## Summary

The day after the Battle of Kintargo (March 21st, Independence Day for Ravenel), the party attends a public ceremony where they receive commendations from newly reinstated Lord Mayor Jillia. During the festivities, they notice Dottari detectives being urgently summoned and leave the stage to investigate. They discover a corpse of Wario Donager (a respected leather worker and manager of Vespam Artisans) hanging from a lamp post, encased in wax with ritual symbols dedicated to Shax (a chaotic evil deity of murder and sadism). Medical examination at the city morgue reveals the victim was paralyzed via a talon/claw wound to the neck, had his heart surgically removed and replaced with wax containing a dead whipperwill (an omen of death), and matches the wounds of a halfling victim found days earlier.
