# Carrion Crown Campaign Session Summary: "Stay in School"

The session opened with a memorable scene at the Dog Watch Tavern, where Conetta engaged Guard Captain Mid Johnson in a drunken sword-fighting competition—a match where combatants must drink each time they're struck. Despite her heavy intoxication, Conetta dominated the bout with her distinctive rapier featuring a grappling hook attachment, impressing the gathered crowd including Judge Darymid, who arrived at 2:30 a.m. to find Farrah Richton awake and visibly annoyed at the tavern. This chaotic opening set the tone for what would become a high-stakes mission briefing.

At 6 a.m., Judge Darymid summoned the CP-USS team to District House P13 with only 3.5 hours' notice. The party spent the early morning hours sobering up, acquiring supplies, and fortifying themselves with coffee. Upon arrival at the precinct, Kendra Lorrimor had arranged an impressive continental breakfast featuring desert coffee, yogurt parfaits, avocado toast, scones, and eggs—a gesture that provided both sustenance and morale boost before the briefing. Judge Darymid revealed their mission: apprehend the fugitive Estovion at Ascanor Lodge in the dangerous Shudderwood region. The team learned that Estovion had confessed to aiding the Whispering Way under duress and had previously attempted to murder CP-USS. Critically, they were warned of an invisible undead werewolf capable of possessing people that would defend Estovion if he became unconscious.

The briefing expanded when Rissa arrived with additional intelligence from Seus, offering to serve as a messenger regarding Estovion and the Shudderwood region. The party also received a warrant for the capture of another target, Aoon, described as a formidable wizard with knowledge of extraplanar travel, invisibility, form-shifting spells including gaseous form, and a history of escape attempts. Seus' involvement was kept confidential, as the party recognized that revealing his connection could endanger him given Ascanor's previous assassination attempts. The team received mission funds from Judge Darymid and conducted extensive shopping, purchasing shotguns, dispel magic scrolls, amulets, cloaks of resistance, and anti-werewolf supplies in preparation for the dangerous journey.

After finalizing their preparations and debating travel logistics—whether to prioritize stealth or speed through the forest—CP-USS departed Lepidstadt heading south on the South Road toward Shudderwood. The journey would prove perilous, as the party soon encountered bandits on the road. The combat was brutal and costly: Savage was killed early in the encounter, struck down by crossbow bolts. During the chaotic fighting, Kai's attempt at a non-lethal strike against Patch Dangerson went catastrophically wrong, decapitating the target and leaving him at negative 62 HP. Dismas unleashed devastating void ray attacks, with one empowered bolt disintegrating an enemy's throat, neck, and spine after catching him in the mouth. Multiple other combatants fell to the party's coordinated assault, including Road Kings bandits and several noble children caught in the crossfire. The encounter left the party bloodied but victorious, though the loss of Savage weighed heavily as they pressed onward toward their objectives in the Shudderwood.
