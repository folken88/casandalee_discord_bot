## Summary

# Session Summary: "That Sinking Feeling"

The party's exploration of the wreckage-strewn waters near Shackles began with the discovery of a wooden treasure chest, which—after some technical difficulties with the item management system—yielded a substantial haul of supplies including seven tons of metal bars, potions, books, an obsidian stone, a glaive, an Inheritor's Blade, and a composite longbow. With their cargo secured, the adventurers turned their attention to the hulking remains of shipwrecks scattered across the water.

Upon investigating an overturned vessel, the party encountered their first serious threat: multiple zombies clinging to the ship's rigging. Storgrim boldly leaped fifteen feet down to engage them in melee combat, though the barnacle-covered surface proved treacherous, causing his initial rapier thrust to miss. The party rallied to support him, with Riviera providing musket fire, Bujon unleashing a critical hit with his Jolt spell that electrified one zombie into oblivion, and Holden executing impressive parry-and-riposte maneuvers with his rapier. After clearing the undead, the party explored the corpse-laden interior of the wrecked ship, discovering severed body parts, scattered weapons, and magical items among the debris.

The most significant discovery came when Holden found a glowing sword embedded in a corpse's chest. Upon touching it, the blade spoke to him telepathically, demanding to be released and insisting its wielder be healed. Holden managed to suppress the sword's luminescence to avoid alerting nearby enemies and used a potion to revive a mysterious, disoriented man named Oblivious. However, pulling the sword from the corpse caused the hulk to shift ominously, and strange ripples appeared in the water—something far larger was approaching.

A horrific creature emerged from the depths: a massive reefclaw with giant lobster claws, a serpentine body, hideous mandibles, long antennae, and a spiny back. This initial encounter quickly escalated as multiple reefclaws swarmed the party from the surrounding waters. The ensuing battle was desperate and brutal, with party members fighting to prevent themselves from being dragged into the sea. Riviera proved instrumental, firing arrows with deadly precision—including a critical shot through the eye of a reefclaw that was attempting to drag Holden into the water. Bujon engaged the creatures in melee combat while Sha-Feng demonstrated exceptional marksmanship, killing multiple reefclaws with well-placed shots. Through coordinated effort and no small amount of luck, the party defeated the aquatic onslaught, though the encounter left them battered and questioning what other horrors lurked in these cursed waters.
