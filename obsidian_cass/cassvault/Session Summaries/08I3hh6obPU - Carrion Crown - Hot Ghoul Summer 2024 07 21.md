# Carrion Crown Campaign - Session Summary: "Hot Ghoul Summer"

The party found themselves at a compound filled with undead horrors after completing a western skirmish and looting its spoils. Hearing ominous banging and clunking sounds emanating from the north and a kitchen building, the adventurers decided to prioritize the main northern structure. Upon arrival, they discovered the front door sealed shut with iron spikes—a clear sign of the dangers within. Working together, Gaspar, Kai, and Kate Blackwood used adamantine weapons to breach the reinforced entrance, chopping through the barrier to reveal a red-carpeted hallway stretching into darkness.

What followed was a nightmarish combat encounter as the party advanced into the hallway. A skeleton sprinted toward them from 75 feet away, quickly followed by a cascade of undead werewolves, skeletons, and zombies pouring from deeper within the building. Dismas proved instrumental in the early stages, landing a devastating shot through an undead werewolf's rib cage that sent it crashing backward against the wall. Kate Blackwood's exceptional defenses (AC 35) allowed her to weather attacks from multiple werewolves, while Gaspar successfully resisted both ghoul fever and lycanthropy after being struck. Dinvaya summoned a spiritual weapon to strike at the creatures with precision.

As the undead swarm intensified, Sid deployed a Black Tentacles spell to control the hallway and prevent enemies from advancing further. This tactical maneuver proved crucial, allowing Dismas to systematically eliminate the grappled werewolves with Scorching Ray and rapid arrow fire, killing at least three of the creatures. However, the party's greatest challenge emerged when a massive "Fat Boy" creature appeared at the hallway's end, its stench cloud ability forcing melee combatants to retreat and obscuring vision for ranged attackers. The creature was eventually cornered in a bathroom where Elfrip finished it with a Fireball spell, causing it to explode spectacularly.

Beyond the combat itself, the party discovered the kitchen had belonged to a cook who prepared fresh barbarian banana bread and blood caiman tacos using recipes from *Savage Sustenance*. They also recovered a Pharasma-emblazoned apron with magical properties (+5 to cooking checks, +2 to saves versus fire), along with preserved foods and a mysterious ghoul pie with one slice already consumed. Several party members acquired useful items including potions of cure light wounds and remove disease, adamantine crowbars, and a wand of levitate—all essential supplies for surviving the horrors of this undead-infested compound.
