## Summary

# Carrion Crown Campaign Journal - Session Summary

The party established camp in the Shudderwood forest after entering the region, with Farah compiling important questions for their guides Rissa and Kosta about the dangers they would face. As the group settled in for the night, they organized a watch rotation and discussed the oppressive, muggy weather. During the night watch, Joy Bridge detected a disturbing phenomenon around 3-4 a.m.—all forest sounds suddenly ceased for a brief moment before resuming, a strange occurrence that prompted the party to investigate. An Inquisitor cast Detect Evil but found nothing, leaving the group unsettled but unable to identify the source.

The unease proved justified when the party discovered that both Farah and Rissa had wandered away from their bedrolls during the night. Following their tracks, the party found both women standing motionless at the edge of a circle of trees, staring upward into the branches as if entranced. Dinvaya attempted to use magical abilities to break whatever hold affected them, casting Detect Magic and Detect Invisibility, while the party also attempted Dispel Magic, but no magical effects were detected. As the party approached Farah's position, they heard clumping footsteps and spellcasting nearby. Farah urged the group to be quiet, insisting she was listening to something. When they pressed forward, the horrifying truth revealed itself: the forest around them was filled with bodies hanging from the trees.

A voice emanated from the corpses—a chilling message about "The Butcher returns"—identifying the presence of the legendary Butcher of Courtaud, a figure whose dark legacy haunted the region. The party's investigation led them to discover that Farah's grandfather had been connected to this sinister history, having deliberately drowned dozens of soldiers by chaining them with bricks and heavy objects and sinking them into the gray river. The hanging bodies and the supernatural compulsion drawing party members into the forest appeared to be manifestations of this dark past reaching out from beyond the grave, suggesting that the Butcher's influence—and perhaps his very presence—still lingered in Shudderwood.

The party recovered significant treasures from an enchanted chest, including the Widow Maker, a +2 Unholy Great Axe designed for murdering jealous husbands, a +2 one-handed hammer with momentum-enhancing properties, and a Bloodstone Trauma necklace that had belonged to the Butcher's first lieutenant. They also acquired golden teeth extracted from an 11-foot skeleton encountered during combat with undead creatures in the water, along with various other weapons and equipment. These discoveries, combined with the supernatural phenomena and the revelation of Farah's family connection to the Butcher, suggested that the party's journey through Shudderwood would be far more dangerous and personally complicated than initially anticipated, with dark forces and darker family secrets converging on their expedition.
