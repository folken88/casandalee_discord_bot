# Session Summary: Mulligan Crevass

## Discovery and Reconnaissance

After resting overnight at Castaway Stockade following their victory over a ghoul-infected hanged man, the party pressed southward along Bonewrack Isle's coastline. Hacking through dense jungle and following the beach-soil interface to minimize their exposure to the overgrown terrain, the adventurers spotted the crow's nest of a completely sunken ship approximately a quarter mile offshore—a grim reminder of the dangers lurking in these waters. Their destination remained the grindylow lair where their lost comrades were being held captive.

## The Hidden Cove

Following an inlet inland from a southwestern beach, the party discovered a hidden cove on Windward Isle's northeastern side, surrounded by towering 30-40 foot pale stone cliffs choked with thorny vegetation. Riviera used a potion of fly to scout ahead, ascending 100 feet above the canopy to survey the area. Her reconnaissance revealed a vast blue water cove approximately 300 feet across, with crystal-clear sections showing wrecked boats and debris, and darker deep sections suggesting vertical passages into underwater caverns. Most critically, she spotted at least four grindylow hunters on the northwestern beach, with evidence of many more nearby—approximately 20 hunters and 6 shamans total. The beach was littered with boat debris, tools, and skeletal remains. Riviera also discovered a hidden passage: a narrow 1.5-foot gap between two cliffs to the north that could provide access to the west side of the cove without detection. Observations showed grindylows regularly diving into the cove's center and disappearing underwater, confirming an extensive underwater cave system.

## Tactical Positioning and Combat

The party positioned themselves on the cliff with their backs to the wall, using the elevation advantage for maximum defensive benefit. Riviera took a ranged support position 45-50 feet above the beach. Before engaging, the party consumed Whispered Courage whiskey for fear resistance and distributed healing potions among key members. Armed with alchemist fire bottles, pipe bombs, and their standard weapons, they prepared for a desperate rescue operation against overwhelming odds.

## The Battle

Combat erupted with brutal efficiency. Holden opened the engagement with a rapier strike during the surprise round. Storgrim cleaved a grindylow hunter cleanly down through the rib cage with his two-handed axe. Ser-Toche delivered a critical sneak attack for 14 damage, while other party members executed precise strikes—one stabbing a grindylow for 10 damage, killing it instantly. A ranged attacker's shot exploded a grindylow shaman's head, sending its burning corpse into the water. Riviera struck through another grindylow's body with her rapier and kicked it into the cove. Despite the party's tactical advantage and devastating initial strikes, the sheer number of enemies proved overwhelming. Sha-Feng, surrounded by seven grindylows and a shaman, was pulled underwater and reduced to zero or near-zero hit points. The battle continued with the party fighting desperately to rescue their companions while managing the relentless assault from the grindylow forces.
