## Summary

The party operates from their sewer sanctum in Starfall, splitting up for reconnaissance missions. They debate whether to meet with the mercenary Hearsick (who wants 50,000 gold for information), visit Mockery for intel, and scout the palace. Akradenn (Crocodile) flies to the palace district in cockroach form but triggers a magical alarm barrier south of the palace, causing the entire palace to turtle up and patrols to mobilize.
