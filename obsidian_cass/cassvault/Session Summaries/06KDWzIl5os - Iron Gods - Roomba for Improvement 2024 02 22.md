# Iron Gods - Roomba for Improvement

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=06KDWzIl5os)

## Summary

The party continued their assault on the Technic League's primary surveillance facility in Starfall. A massive multi-room battle raged against Technic League forces including borgs, troopers, casters, risen undead barbarians, a void dragon, and the Technic League leader Osman himself. Nomkath caused enormous property damage with repeated Silvex explosions, destroying the control room and a hapless Roomba cleaning robot. Luna was briefly dominated by an enemy hellcaster but Olbryn successfully dispelled it before she could attack the party.

## Key Events

- Nomkath destroyed the Technic League's primary surveillance control room with Silvex explosions, setting a new record for property damage
  - Characters: Nomkath
  - Location: Technic League facility, Starfall

- Luna (Rye's bloodrager) dealt massive damage with crits, killing a gearsman in a single round of attacks
  - Characters: Luna
  - Location: Technic League facility hallways

- Olbryn teleported to rescue Akradenn from Osman using his shift ability, then rewound back to safety
  - Characters: Olbryn, Akradenn
  - Location: North hallway near Osman

- Olbryn dispelled Dominate Person on Luna just before her turn, preventing her from attacking the party
  - Characters: Olbryn, Luna
  - Location: Control room area

- A Roomba cleaning robot became a running joke as it was repeatedly caught in explosions, reaching -157 HP before being officially declared dead
  - Characters: Multiple party members contributed to its destruction
  - Location: Technic League facility

- Nomkath used a copycat wand to disguise himself as a Technic League sniper, confusing Toala briefly before Toala chose to kill the real sniper instead
  - Characters: Nomkath, Toala (NPC)

- Cockatrices (Cassandra's companion) used voltaic organ electrical discharge against enemies trapped in a wall of thorns
  - Characters: Cockatrice (NPC companion)

- A void dragon disintegrated Nomkath for 91 damage, nearly killing him
  - Characters: Nomkath
  - Location: Conference room area
