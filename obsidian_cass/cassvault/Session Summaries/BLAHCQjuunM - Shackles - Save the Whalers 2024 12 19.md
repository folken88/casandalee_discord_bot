# Shackles - Save the Whalers

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=BLAHCQjuunM)

## Summary

The crew of the newly rechristened Pillager's Knife (formerly the TPK) prepared to depart Rickety Squibs and begin their pirate career. Much of the session was spent on logistics: assigning crew positions on a new ship management sheet, buying provisions, debating where to sail, and dealing with the chaos caused by Lil Von the monkey. The crew decided to follow a magical mug's new marker west toward an island roughly 9-10 days away. Sha-Feng cut off his own ring finger to apologize to Storgrim for the monkey's behavior, then reattached it with a cure light potion the monkey brought him. The ship departed with Rickety christening it the Pillager's Knife, and they sailed west for most of the day before spotting a ship (possibly a whaler) beached or stuck between two islands at dusk.

## Key Events

- The party debated their destination, with Sha-Feng wanting to eventually reach Senghor (Shanu Bay) to free slaves and visit family, and others wanting to follow a treasure map
  - Characters: Sha-Feng, Storgrim, Holden
  - Location: Rickety Squibs

- Crew positions were assigned using a new ship management sheet: Storgrim as Captain, Holden as First Mate, Riviera as Navigator, Rhyarca as Pilot, Bujon as Wizard (wind mage), Sha-Feng as Cook, Sandara as Surgeon
  - Characters: All crew
  - Location: Pillager's Knife

- Holden's magical mug revealed a new location marker to the west on an island, and the party decided to sail toward it
  - Characters: Holden (Kip), Storgrim
  - Location: Rickety Squibs

- Lil Von the monkey caused extended chaos: throwing poop and coins at party members, getting drunk on whispered courage whiskey, being blinded by glitter dust, and eventually learning limited Mayonnaise (the party's secret language)
  - Characters: Vaughan (primary handler), Sha-Feng, Storgrim, Riviera
  - Location: Rickety Squibs and the ship

- Sha-Feng cut off his ring finger to apologize to Storgrim for the monkey, then reattached it using a cure light potion that Lil Von himself brought
  - Characters: Sha-Feng, Storgrim, Lil Von
  - Location: Rickety Squibs docks

- The Risen crew (from a previous adventure) integrated well with the existing crew, impressing veteran sailors like Marl with their sailing skills
  - Characters: Risen crew NPCs, Marl
  - Location: Pillager's Knife

- Bujon spent alter winds spells (about 3 per day) to boost the ship's speed
  - Characters: Bujon
  - Location: Pillager's Knife

- At dusk, after sailing ~150 miles, Sarah spotted a ship between two islands that appeared to be stuck on a sandbar with longboats around it
  - Characters: Sarah (NPC lookout), Riviera (navigator)
  - Location: Open sea, west of Rickety Squibs
