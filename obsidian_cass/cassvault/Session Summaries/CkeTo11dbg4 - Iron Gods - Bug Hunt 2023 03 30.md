# Iron Gods - Bug Hunt

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=CkeTo11dbg4)

## Summary

The party explores the Kill Box Underground Market beneath Starfall. Tokala passes out after taking a drug and is revived with "Go Nuts," a stimulant that grants haste and 48 hours of wakefulness but causes Constitution damage and fidgeting. The party shops at the underground market, which includes a drug shop, scroll shop, potion shop, floral shop, and weapon stores, all operating off-the-books away from Technic League oversight. They discover a locked secure area with well-armed bodyguards and scout the market looking for a Technic League captain who is supposed to visit a specialty merchant. Nomkath confiscates Tokala's remaining drugs.
