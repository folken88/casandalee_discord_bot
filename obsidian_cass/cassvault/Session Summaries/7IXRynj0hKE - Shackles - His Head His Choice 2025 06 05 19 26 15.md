# Shackles - His Head His Choice

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=7IXRynj0hKE)

## Summary

The party finishes clearing the dwarven mine with Sorak's assistance, tackling the last room beyond the mushroom farm. They fight remaining undead workers and soldiers, with Bujon using alter winds to blow plague spores out of the mushroom farm after Riviera's gunfire triggers them again. Sha-Feng demonstrates his flurry of blows and trip attacks. Holden contracts the Gol Kazak undead plague and names his rapier "The Flying Guillotine." The party encounters a heavily armored undead soldier and continues battling through the complex.
