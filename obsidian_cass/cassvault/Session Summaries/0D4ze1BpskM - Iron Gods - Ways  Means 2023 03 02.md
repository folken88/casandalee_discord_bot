# Iron Gods - Ways & Means

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=0D4ze1BpskM)

## Summary

The party resurrects Nomkath using Raise Dead, sacrificing a Glazer and pipe bomb to cover the material component cost. They spend the morning planning their next moves in Starfall, with Cassandalee providing an AR-enhanced virtual tour of the city through Nomkath's VR helmet to narrow down the location of Ferris's safe house to north-central Kill Box near the Street of Lights. The party disguises themselves and heads out to investigate the neighborhood before visiting the Mockery, an underground market contact. They also meet with Drowmuck at Starfall Blades and gather intelligence on Technic League captains and city factions.
