# Carrion Cronies Campaign Journal - Session: Cirque Du Soul Wraith

## The Mindscape Incursion

The party found themselves violently pulled into the mindscape when Farah failed a crucial will save against the Vasus possessing her. What began as a mental intrusion quickly escalated into a full combat encounter as the party discovered themselves facing an organized force of cultists, zombies, and Soul Wraiths engaged in some form of ritual. Rather than engage in a prolonged ranged exchange, the party made the tactical decision to hold their ground and force the enemy to advance, allowing them to control the battlefield's tempo and positioning.

## The Battle Intensifies

The scouts identified a diverse enemy force: weaponless zombies mixed with dangerous axe-wielding variants, cultists armed with daggers and swords alongside spellcasters, potion and grenade-carrying alchemists, and the Soul Wraiths at the ritual's heart. Combat proved brutal and tactical—Cheda narrowly parried a mage zombie's inflict moderate wounds strike inches from her face before counterattacking, while the party coordinated flanking maneuvers and discussed spell tactics. Farah took splash damage from an area effect but made her reflex save, halving the damage. The party's spellcasters proved invaluable, with one character casting Wind Wall to neutralize ranged attackers and bomb throwers, while another deployed Cone of Invisibility against enemy spellcasters.

## Mounting Casualties and Heroic Stands

As the battle raged, casualties mounted on both sides. Styx phased in and out of shadow form to deliver a critical 35-damage strike against a Brian, while another Brian nearly fell to a charging blade attack that dealt 43 damage. An Inquisitor among the party activated a prayer ability, granting all allies a prayer bonus, +3 armor class, and +2 to all saves. The party employed multiple buffs including stone skin, resist elements, and infernal healing. Curiosity cast void ball, interrupting the Soul Wraith and dealing acid damage across multiple enemies. The mysterious entity Bemorael, a bone shatter wizard, was completely disintegrated by Farah's disintegrate spell after failing a fortitude save—taking 224 damage to its 117 hit points and being removed from existence entirely.

## The Final Confrontation

The battle culminated in a desperate struggle against increasingly powerful undead, including axe zombies and a megas with vampiric touch abilities. Estovion, a major cultist leader, cast a powerful lightning spell that channeled electricity through his own body into the Soul Wraiths—a contingency that killed him in the process. The party ultimately prevailed, with Silus Gren being beheaded and left on the dance floor in a pool of blood, and Estovan defeated by the party's combined efforts. Among the fallen were Stovie, who died heroically, and numerous cultists and undead minions. The party recovered one red scroll from Estovion's remains, and the Vasus entity temporarily attached itself to Farah, requiring her to pass it to another guide. The mindscape battle had been won, but at considerable cost.
