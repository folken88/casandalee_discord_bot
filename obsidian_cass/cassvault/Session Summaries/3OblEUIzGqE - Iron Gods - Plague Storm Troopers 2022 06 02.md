# Iron Gods Campaign Session Summary: Plague Storm Troopers

The party concluded their fierce battle with a vengeful ghost near the Scar of the Spider's riverbank, where ruined structures and an abandoned boat marked the supernatural encounter. After Maginnis and the others destroyed the intangible specter, its weapons and gear became tangible and fell to the ground, ready for looting. The party collected the ghost's belongings, including its guns and a journal or sketchbook that promised valuable information. Notably, the temperature increased significantly after the ghost's destruction, a mysterious phenomenon the party noted but could not immediately explain.

With morning light, the party turned their attention to investigating the fungal threats in the region. Before venturing into dangerous Dominion hive territory, they debated whether to examine the corpses of fungal monsters for Technic League information. The group also discussed acquiring protective potions from Petros's orchard and made a supply run to Mad Peter's shop, purchasing Thunderchunk doses, protective items like grape lip (against mind-affecting magic), and additional healing potions and wands to bolster their defenses against the fungal threats ahead.

After four days of peaceful travel down the eastern edge of the valley toward the river and mountain range, the party began to notice environmental changes indicating they were crossing into Mi-Go territory. The foliage and animal behavior shifted noticeably, and scouts detected subtle signs of demon trees—the young Geeth—within a mile or two. The party learned from their guides that Mi-Go were intelligent fungal entities capable of traveling through dimensions, and that demon trees served as their allies. Recalling previous encounters where demon trees had grabbed party members and drained their charisma with a reach of at least ten feet, the group decided to travel south into the mountain foothills rather than west into the swamp to avoid known demon tree locations and secure one more night of dry camping.

The scouts' reconnaissance proved crucial when they discovered a glade in the swamp bearing signs of recent, intense combat. A massive fallen tree lay amid a camp setup with tents, a dead tentacled monster, and evidence of explosions. The damage appeared to be a mix of technological and magical destruction, strongly suggesting Technic League involvement. Among the grim discoveries was a dead Technic League captain found on a ledge near a pool of water, apparently killed by an unknown creature lurking in the depths. The party now faced critical decisions about investigating this site and determining what had transpired in this remote glade.
