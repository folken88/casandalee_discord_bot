# Iron Gods Campaign Session Summary: Escape Velocity

The party found themselves in a desperate underground battle against a coordinated force of chaos beasts, migos, and other aberrations within a complex cave system. After initial combat left several party members injured and Nomkath paralyzed by a Hold Person spell, the group made a tactical decision to retreat to a more defensible position. Tokala used restoration magic to free Nomkath from paralysis while the party regrouped and formulated a new strategy rather than continue fighting in open terrain where the enemy's numbers posed a significant threat.

Recognizing the advantage of narrow passages, Maginnis proposed a defensive ambush strategy: the party would funnel the incoming enemies through a tight tunnel corridor with 15-foot ceilings, forcing them to approach in smaller numbers. Akradenn positioned himself as the primary damage dealer, unleashing multiple lightning bolt spells against the advancing chaos creatures—he had already cast four of his fifteen available lightning bolts and planned to use eight to eleven more. Luna employed an Air Walk spell to position herself five feet above the ground, creating a sniper's vantage point to rain down attacks on enemies below.

The ensuing combat was intense and prolonged, with the party employing a coordinated magical assault. Akradenn's lightning bolts proved devastatingly effective, though the party discovered at least one enemy creature possessed resistance to lightning damage. Supporting spellcasters including Olbryn and Nomkath contributed flaming sphere, flame strike, and spiritual ally attacks to the barrage. Ulfred demonstrated tactical flexibility by using his rewind ability to reposition and cast additional lightning bolts against multiple enemies simultaneously.

Throughout the battle, the party maintained discipline despite the chaos creatures' aggressive charges down the corridor. Luna managed to catch a glimpse of interesting structures deeper in the tunnel system, noting that the party should investigate further once they eliminated the current threat. Though the session ended with combat still ongoing, the party's superior positioning and coordinated magical assault suggested they were gaining the upper hand against the aberrations threatening their progress through the Iron Gods campaign.
