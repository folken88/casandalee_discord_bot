# Carrion Crown BHOL - Warehouse Waldo

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=0muw3v70jHk)

## Summary

The BHOL (Blackhand Operatives League) party continued a warehouse assault against their assassination target's forces. The session picked up mid-combat with Stray (Kovira) in spider form fighting a vampire that had survived a disintegrate spell. A second vampire materialized from bats and joined the fight. The party battled Red Runner gang thugs and vampires across the warehouse compound, with Rev (Pinkney) providing psychic commentary in a James Spader voice. Switch identified the gang leader Roland Redeye McRoy and blinded him with glitter dust, while Torkayla killed a vampire with a spell-shot through her DVL rifle. A new player William joined as Lamark the Inquisitor.
