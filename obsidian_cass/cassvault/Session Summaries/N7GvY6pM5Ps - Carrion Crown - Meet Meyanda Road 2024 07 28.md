# Carrion Crown Campaign Journal - Session Summary

## Rest and Resupply at the Compound

After clearing the cultist compound, the party secured the southeastern cabin area near the front gate as their resting place, moving bodies away from the sleeping quarters to maintain some dignity and hygiene. While the group recovered, Gaspar began planning his crafting projects: upgrading his +1 ring of protection to +2 (requiring 3 days and 3,000 gold in materials) and enhancing an amulet. The party then set their sights on Booknook and began the journey along the road toward their next destination, with discussions about navigation and survival checks marking the beginning of their travel.

## The Mystery of the Overturned Wagon

As the party traveled the road east of Willow Mir, they discovered an overturned wagon approximately 1.5 miles from the town, its wheels damaged and supplies scattered across a wheat field. Investigation revealed that the wagon had departed Willow Mir roughly a month prior at normal speed before suddenly accelerating to incredible velocity—suggesting something or someone had taken control. Evidence at the scene indicated that 2-4 occupants had fled on foot into the surrounding fields. Among the scattered supplies, the party discovered a child-sized backpack containing a lesser bag of holding, a holy symbol of Sarenrae, a wand of dancing lights, a popup book about Sarenrae, bedroll, and spoiled trail rations. The discovery raised troubling questions about what had happened to the wagon's passengers and why they had fled so desperately.

## The Arrival of Meyanda

The party continued their journey through the grasslands toward Booknook, eventually camping near the river between the halfling settlement and Willow. As evening fell, Rodney Danger Smith mysteriously heard rumbling in the distance and received a warning from the grass itself that a monster was approaching. When Elfrip flew up to investigate, he spotted a long black line moving across the field at high speed—which turned out to be Meyanda arriving in a large mechanical vehicle, accompanied by techno music. Meyanda revealed that she had been making significant profits from the trade routes opened by the party's clearing of Fort Ustra and offered her assistance and supplies at a 40% discount.

## Equipment Upgrades and Progress Update

The party took advantage of Meyanda's arrival to conduct extensive shopping and equipment upgrades. They purchased holy water, a Relax-U self-care device, an observation drone with high perception and stealth capabilities, and discussed acquiring communication sets for team coordination. Meyanda also agreed to craft a +3 Mithril Shield for the party. Through their transactions, the group sold items and received gold in exchange, further bolstering their resources. During this time, the party assessed their progress in tracking the Whispering Way cult through Shudderwood: they had made up approximately one week of the month-long gap they had been behind, reducing their deficit from four weeks to three weeks.

## Notable Acquisitions and Preparations

Among the items claimed during this session, Elfrip took possession of a child-sized backpack shaped like a Sarenrae cleric with golden feathered wings, while Alfred secured the lesser bag of holding found in the wagon. Kai crafted an elasmosaurus skull helmet from dinosaur bones the party had previously acquired, replacing an older Alton helmet. The party also offered the holy symbol of Sarenrae to any member willing to become a follower of the Dawn Flower, and Elfrip claimed a popup book titled "Tales of the Dawn Flower" as a memento. With their supplies replenished, equipment upgraded, and Meyanda's support secured, the party prepared to continue their pursuit of the Whispering Way cultists deeper into Shudderwood.
