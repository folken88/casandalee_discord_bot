# Speak with Skull & Shackles

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=2AERbrEysxQ)

## Summary

The party continues clearing the dwarven mine complex alongside Sorak and his Sahuagin warriors. Sha-Feng is returned to the party after being captured, and a slave collar is removed from him. The group investigates blast-damaged rooms, discovering evidence of an Alkenstar dwarven expedition that was deliberately destroyed. They fight undead dwarves in the mushroom farm area and encounter the undead Lord of Gol Kazak, a crowned dwarven king wielding two axes including a returning throwing axe. Riviera's gunfire triggers plague-laden spores from the mushroom farm, infecting Holden and Riviera with the Gol Kazak undead plague, though Rhyarca cures Riviera with a scroll of Remove Disease.
