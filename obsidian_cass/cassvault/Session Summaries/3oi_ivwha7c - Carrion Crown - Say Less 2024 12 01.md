# Carrion Crown - Say Less

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=3oi_ivwha7c)

## Summary

After defeating a powerful bone construct and undead crows at Feldgrau, the party withdraws to explore Harrowgate, a ruined Shining Crusade fortress. They rest outside the ruins where Gaspar has vivid dreams of the fortress under siege, and Elfrip and Dismas share prophetic dreams about laying enchanted bricks underground. The party adopts a layered scouting formation with Kovira flying invisible as lead scout and Kai on the ground as close support. They discover a fog-shrouded ruined tower with an eerie stillness and no animal life, containing a glowing pool in the collapsed central stairwell. Kovira detects undead in the area.
