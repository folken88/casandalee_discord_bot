# Shackles - In The Rear With The Beer

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=F3nBl-ofq-s)

## Summary

The party lands on Windward Isle and heads south through dense jungle toward a city, following intelligence about Chellish agents using Half Moon Beach as an extraction point. They find a skeleton with severed legs that crawled to its death. While marching single-file along a narrow jungle trail with Riviera passing around whiskey in the rear, the party is ambushed by giant centipedes and hidden archers. Several party members are bitten and some suffer fatigue from the centipede venom. Sarah, an NPC companion, cuts one centipede in half to protect the healer. Storgrim shrugs off the venom with dwarven fortitude.
