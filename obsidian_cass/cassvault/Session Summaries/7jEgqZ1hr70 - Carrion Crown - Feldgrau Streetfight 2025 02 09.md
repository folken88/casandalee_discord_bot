# Carrion Crown - Feldgrau Streetfight

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=7jEgqZ1hr70)

## Summary

A massive multi-faction battle erupts in Feldgrau as the party fights the Whispering Way cult forces including Orang-Rude (the cult leader), three gunslinger undead (Bet, Pride, and Deadshot), chain-slinging corpse-hook undead, true vampires, and morgs. Allied NPCs Sylvia (a dragon rider) and Vex provide air support, with Sylvia casting a time-manipulation spell granting each party member free self-buff actions. Danger arrives late to the fight but immediately begins firing phase-locking arrows at Orang-Rude from long range using Sylvia's aerial battlefield vision. The gunslingers are under a confusion-type effect causing them to attack the nearest target. Dinvaya gets caught by a chain-slinging undead while repositioning away from a gunslinger.
