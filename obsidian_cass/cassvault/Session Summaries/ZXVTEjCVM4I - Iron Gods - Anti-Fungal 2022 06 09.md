# Iron Gods Campaign Session Summary: Anti-Fungal (June 9, 2022)

The party ventured deep into a plague-infested cave system where they encountered a horrifying array of alien fungal creatures and tentacled horrors. The initial combat proved immediately challenging when a staff-wielding creature cast a confusion spell (DC 20 Will save) that left Maginnis disoriented and vulnerable. A flying tentacled monstrosity attempted to grapple the confused spellcaster, dealing 3 points of constitution damage before being driven back. The creature's carapace was nearly shattered by Tokala's devastating critical hit to its head, though the alien horror remained disturbingly alive despite the grievous wound.

As the party pressed deeper into the foggy battlefield, they faced multiple floating tentacled creatures emerging from the mist. Olbryn performed a masterful feint (rolling a natural 20 for a total of 42), rendering enemies flat-footed and allowing the party to capitalize with devastating attacks. One particularly impressive assault saw a character deliver four sneak attacks for 68 total damage against a stoneskin-protected foe that bled pinkish-brown alien blood. An enemy spellcaster managed to cast a dimensional escape spell, narrowly maintaining concentration despite the chaos of combat.

The party's advance through the tunnel system brought them to a soupy, mildly poisonous slime-filled passage where they encountered more fungal creatures with chitinous armor plating. Tokala unleashed a plasma shotgun at close range, dealing fire and electrical damage to multiple enemies. The party systematically dismantled their foes—Akradenn delivered the final blow to one creature with a precise dagger strike between its armor plates directly into its brain, while Tokala used a chainsaw to horrifically dismember another space fungus creature. Luna achieved a critical sneak attack that finished an aqueous orb-trapped mutant creature that Ulfred had been pummeling while it remained entangled.

Throughout the brutal encounters, the party acquired several valuable items including a black hypogun with lesser restoration capabilities looted from defeated Mi-go creatures, a plasma shotgun that Brow has been carrying despite lacking proficiency, and various other equipment. Healing wands were distributed among the party for recovery, and Croctaw found himself carrying a giant aqueous orb containing the corpse of a dead mutant creature—an unusual and unsettling trophy of their victory.
