## Summary

Following a major combat against undead in the previous session, CP-USS loots and explores a Shining Crusade-era granary built atop ancient foundations in Feldgrau. They discover the building has far older foundations than the granary above it, with a note stabbed to a door reading "F off monsters." The party finds hidden stonework beneath rugs in the floor, revealing a vertical shaft descending 40 feet to an underground passage with running water. They use levitate wands, feather fall, and rope to descend and begin exploring a tomb or burial chamber beneath the town.
