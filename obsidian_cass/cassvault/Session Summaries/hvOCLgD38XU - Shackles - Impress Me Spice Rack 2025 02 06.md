## Summary

The party returns from their fight with Bronze Fleet slavers on Firegrass Island, bringing rescued survivors and severed slaver heads back to Bent Beak's town. After resupplying they head back across the treacherous rope bridge to search for captives reportedly taken by a giant. They track hoofprints of massive buffalo-like creatures and discover a mysterious planted forest of saplings surrounding a single enormous tree, while sensing impact tremors from something large stalking them.
