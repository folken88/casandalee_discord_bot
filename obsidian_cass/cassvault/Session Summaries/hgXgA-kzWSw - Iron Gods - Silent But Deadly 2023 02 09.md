## Summary

The party spends the night in the Earth Daughters safe house in the slums of Starfall, debating whether to use Rope Trick or stay in the apartment. They set watches, deal with a decomposing guard's body in an adjacent room, and wait for their Earth Daughters contacts to return from a meeting. The session focuses on stealth, paranoia, and preparation as the party operates covertly in the heart of Technic League territory.
