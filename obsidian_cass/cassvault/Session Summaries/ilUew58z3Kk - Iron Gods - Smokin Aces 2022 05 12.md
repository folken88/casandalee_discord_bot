# Iron Gods Campaign Session Summary: "Smokin' Aces"

The party's camp was violently disrupted when strange robots attacked under cover of darkness, capturing several party members while Nomkath and Krocta managed to escape. This led to a tense negotiation with Binox, a mysterious figure who controlled the robots, brokered through an unexpected ally named Isuma. The party agreed to trade "entities" in exchange for the safe return of their captured companions. After retrieving their scattered gear from the forest campsite, the party began piecing together a disturbing pattern: they were dealing with forces far larger and more sinister than simple bandits or local threats.

Through encounters and recovered documents, the party learned of a Kassath diplomat who had been kidnapped thousands of years ago by aliens and placed in a false habitat. The diplomat spent nearly a year learning the aliens' ways before discovering the betrayal. When the Dominion of the Black Hell attacked the alien vessel, the diplomat was forced to escape and subsequently spent six years frozen in isolation. The party also encountered evidence of the Dominion's brutal conquest of the Androth spaceship, where they had possessed crew members and forced a mutiny, leaving survivors traumatized and scattered across the region.

Trust within the party fractured when Hearsay's disappearance during watch came under scrutiny. While Hearsay claimed he had gone invisible to scout and plan an ambush, party members questioned whether he had made a deal with Binox to allow their capture as specimens. Tokala, ever concerned with honor, warned Nomkath away from his sister Cassava and suggested the group might need to undergo loyalty trials. The tension reflected deeper divisions: some party members prioritized survival and treasure, while others, particularly Tokala's tribe, valued honor above all else.

The party's exploration yielded significant magical acquisitions, including Maura's Leathers (enchanted studded leather armor with stealth bonuses), two +1 Drow Razors, a Pearl of Power for 3rd-level spells, and a mysterious journal belonging to Alfred Asmara that contained references to the valley and Dominion creatures. They also recovered a glowing Androffan crystal capable of disabling the Minion Hive—technology that had proven useful against other Androffan devices. As the party regrouped and equipped themselves, they remained acutely aware that multiple powerful factions—aliens, the Dominion, and mysterious robots—were converging on their location, and trust among their own ranks was becoming as dangerous as any external threat.
