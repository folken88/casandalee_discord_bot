# Battle for Scrapwall - Session Summary

The Technic League launched a devastating assault on Scrapwall, marching a formidable force of gearsmen robots toward the settlement's gates. The party received only ten minutes' warning before the enemy arrived, forcing them to rapidly prepare defensive positions. Ulfred used stone shaping magic to construct a defensive wall just inside the portcullis gate, while party members scrambled to take advantageous positions across the fortress's ramparts and fortifications. Nomkath secured an elevated vantage point for rifle fire, and the group buffed themselves with spells and potions in preparation for the incoming onslaught. Using arcane eye and clairvoyance spells, scouts detected approximately seven human-sized gearsmen and two massive 22-foot-tall juggernauts advancing through a dust storm, accompanied by repair bots and Technic League soldiers.

When the enemy breached the lower gate, chaos erupted across Scrapwall's courtyard and walls. The party's cannons opened fire, blasting chunks of metal from the advancing gearsmen, while party members engaged in fierce melee and ranged combat from their fortified positions. Algernon dominated a Borg unit with magic, turning the powerful robot against the enemy forces. Rock wielded a massive steel beam as a weapon, using it to devastating effect against enemy casters, including a wizard in red robes whose head was crushed by the overhead swing. Farrah's fire snake spell short-circuited and destroyed a gearman, while Sevrath tore through enemies with silver claws in brutal melee combat.

The battle was characterized by intense and visceral combat, with both sides suffering heavy casualties. Silverhawk allies were decimated by the Technic League's superior firepower and the juggernauts' laser attacks. Notable enemy losses included a large man whose head was blown off by point-blank crossbow fire, a Silverhawk sniper whose head exploded from thermal scope fire, and multiple robot troopers destroyed by the party's combined efforts. The party also acquired significant temporary enhancements during the battle—Rock consumed a glowing ooze canister that granted him a temporary third arm, and Algodon drank a mutagen that gave him flight, bat wings, pounce ability, and golden-plated claws and teeth.

Though the session ended with combat still ongoing, the party had successfully held Scrapwall's gates against the initial assault, demonstrating effective coordination between their own forces and the Silverhawk defenders. The acquisition of the Borg as a controlled ally provided a significant tactical advantage for future engagements. The battle represented a critical turning point in the campaign, with the Technic League's direct assault forcing the party to defend their adopted settlement against overwhelming technological and military might.
