## Summary

The party conducted extensive recon operations in Starfall. Olbryn teleported to Torch (with a mishap landing him on Skywatch tower) to retrieve Kroktah's new Techstone--a magical rock crafted by Cassandalee that enables tech communication, gives bonuses against tech effects, and creates a mindscape of Kasath for rest. Cassandalee activated an upgraded Greater Status spell networking the party for rapid mental communication and mutual touch-spell casting. Nomkath set a trap for the informant Hearsick by making his presence known at the Mercenaries Guild while setting up a dead drop. Kroktah and Nomkath tested the palace barrier--the Technic League badge allowed passage without triggering alarms, while the Starfall officer's badge triggered a subdued response (palace windows shuttering). Nomkath contacted Hearsick via radio, who offered vital intelligence about a "big fish" for 50,000 gold and revealed he knew about the party's new alliances. Cassandalee mapped the entire Technic League social network from communicator logs.
