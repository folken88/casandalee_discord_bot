# Iron Gods Campaign Journal: Problems That Shootin' Don't Solve

The party's expedition into Black Hill Caves culminated in a brutal confrontation with a greater nothalggu creature that had previously tormented them in the virtual realm. This time, facing the monstrosity in physical form, the adventurers systematically destroyed the enslaved brains that granted the creature its intelligence. As each brain fell, the nothalggu became progressively less capable, until finally Ulfred and his companions reduced it to a corpse through sustained melee assault. However, their victory proved incomplete—the creature's body began smoking and crackling with electrical discharge, suggesting something far more dangerous remained active within its biomechanical form.

The party's investigation of the corpse led them deeper into the facility's mysteries. They discovered a massive creature with a powerful electrical organ, and while attempting to pry open its body to harvest components, the organ discharged with catastrophic force. Multiple party members were electrocuted, and Auburn fell to the overwhelming voltage. The party quickly utilized a Scroll of Raise Dead to restore their fallen companion, but the encounter reinforced a grim lesson: the facility's technology and its hybrid creatures posed threats that conventional combat could not easily overcome.

Recognizing that direct violence alone would not solve their problems, the party shifted to a more methodical approach. They harvested a cantaloupe-sized metallic organ from the creature's corpse—a strange biometallic structure connected to lymph node-like tissues—and extracted massive claws with exoskeletal armor plating. These components, they theorized, might serve as keys to the facility's control systems. After deactivating power cells throughout the area, the party discovered a portal control mechanism and began the delicate work of reverse-engineering its activation sequence.

Through careful experimentation and improvisation, the adventurers determined that the console required at least 6d6 electrical damage delivered simultaneously to function. Using a combination of a charged staff, shield, and harvested metallic tendons to improve conductivity, they managed to channel sufficient electrical current through the system. Five of the six control lights activated strongly, with the sixth flickering before stabilizing. The portal shimmered open, revealing a shocking sight: a massive creature on the other side, calmly holding a small tablet. A cold rush of air emanated from the portal, and both Maginnis and Ulfred felt an unsettling gravitational pull toward the unknown chamber beyond. The party now faces a critical decision about what lies on the other side of this mysterious gateway.
