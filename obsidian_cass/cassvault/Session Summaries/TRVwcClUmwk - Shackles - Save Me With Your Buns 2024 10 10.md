# Session Summary: Shackles Campaign - "Save Me With Your Buns"

The party's exploration of Bonewrack Isle took a dangerous turn when they encountered massive, aggressive Assassin Vines in the poison fields north of their landing site. After initial reconnaissance revealed the vines' predatory nature and reach, the party wisely decided to wait for their heavy hitters before proceeding deeper into the infested area. However, their caution was soon tested when they stumbled upon a vine-covered forest location with a wooden chest. As they approached, an ominous crack and rumble emanated from underground—a warning they should have heeded.

Combat erupted when Storgrim's acrobatic ground slam triggered the vines to coalesce into three towering, humanoid creatures approximately twelve feet tall, each wielding root claws with devastating reach. The battle quickly became chaotic and desperate. Multiple party members found themselves grappled and constricted by the vines' crushing strength, with Sha-Feng nearly killed as he was wrapped in coils around his limbs and torso. Storgrim was knocked prone and grappled, taking significant damage. The creatures proved resilient, with some sinking into the ground to reposition and attack from new angles, destroying the stone fence and road in the process.

The party fought back with determination and tactical ingenuity. Vaughan unleashed spell strikes with shocking grasp, dealing electrical damage to the creatures while using flurry of blows with his vindictive harpoon to reposition and protect allies like Sandara. Riviera proved instrumental in the battle, using alchemist fire to ignite one vine before finishing it with a precise rifle shot that disintegrated its charred trunk. Holden attempted an impressive cartwheel through a threatened square—though his acrobatics check failed, his natural 20 arrow shot proved the vine was immune to critical hits. Despite the creatures' regenerative abilities and underground mobility, the party eventually prevailed, killing multiple vines through coordinated attacks and precise strikes.

Among the carnage and scattered bones of previous victims, the party discovered valuable loot: two scrolls, three potions, an unidentified wand, a bronze compass, two spears (including a long reach weapon and a grindylow spear), cure light potions, and approximately 200 pounds of partially charred Assassin Vine material suitable for crafting or alchemy. A wooden chest contained corn and other supplies. The party also debated the logistics of burying their accumulated gold and whether to post guards, indicating their growing wealth and concerns about security on the dangerous island.
