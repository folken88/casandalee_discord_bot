# Hell's Vengeance Campaign Journal - Session: Dropping The Rock

## Basement Assault and Dragon Encounter

The party—Draymus, Reese, Jamal, and Bruce—launched a comprehensive assault on an enemy stronghold, systematically clearing the entire basement of hostile forces. Multiple Squires and other combatants fell to the party's coordinated attacks, with Reese proving particularly lethal; one Squire fell to a lightning-infused arrow shot, while another was decapitated by Aalla's precise strike. The basement operation appeared successful until the party encountered a formidable dragon. After an intense battle that nearly resulted in the dragon's defeat, a high-level wizard intervened with contingency magic or a word of recall spell, whisking the dragon away before it could be finished. The party, recognizing the threat posed by this wizard-dragon alliance, strategically withdrew to regroup.

## Reconnaissance and Strategic Planning

Forced to hide in a rope trick—an extradimensional space that rendered them undetectable—the party conducted extensive reconnaissance of their target: Citadel Dinyar. Jamal ventured out to scout but became hopelessly lost in the deep forest surrounding the citadel; Reese followed to guide him back using sun position and survival skills. From a concealed position approximately one mile southwest of the castle near the river, the party observed the fortress for twenty minutes. They noted that the docks—site of a previous chaotic encounter involving the bagpipes of Madness—had been cleared of ships, though giant blood stains remained as grim reminders of past violence.

## Tactical Preparations and Equipment

The party acquired significant magical resources for the coming assault. Reese obtained a belt that set his Strength to 20 and a Storm Collar composite longbow with a 550-foot range increment capable of firing magical arrows. A potion of Freedom of Movement was secured for infiltration purposes. Most critically, the party acquired the Rock of Silence—a magical item that could maintain a silence spell without consuming standard actions. The plan was to use this item to approach the citadel undetected, though complications arose when a Hell Knight accidentally threw it into the river during the initial placement attempt.

## Assault Strategy on Citadel Dinyar

The party debated multiple infiltration approaches, ultimately settling on a daring aerial assault. Their plan involved teleporting to the top of the citadel's tallest tower, then using mass air walk spells to descend into the structure—a tactic one party member compared to an ODST drop from military science fiction. The party identified the dragon and white-haired wizard as primary threats requiring immediate elimination before they could cast defensive buffs. They discussed using reduce person spells and air bubbles to incorporate Hell Knight allies into the infiltration, though leaving them behind remained an option. With the Rock of Silence lost to the river, the party prepared to rely on invisibility, gaseous form, and other concealment magic—tactics that had proven successful in previous infiltrations of enemy strongholds.
