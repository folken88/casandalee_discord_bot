# Session Summary: "Just Don't Hit The Rope"

The party faced a critical decision when they arrived at Bahari-Kofi Village with grim news: several crew members from the *Man's Promise* had been captured by grindylow during a recent storm and were being held for sacrifice to their dark god within one to two days. With time running short, the adventurers resolved to mount a rescue mission across Bonewrack Isle. Before departing, they gathered supplies—approximately five pounds of gunpowder courtesy of Chaang, questionable fruit for provisions, and Vaughan managed to trade a cannon charge to local villagers for a dozen barely edible kumquats. The party established a careful marching order and began their overland journey across the island's treacherous terrain, guided by Riviera and Sha-Feng's survival skills as they navigated swampy ground and discovered the remnants of old wooden bridges built by previous inhabitants.

As the group pressed southwest through the island's interior, they encountered increasingly hostile wildlife. While crossing a sparse but functional bridge over a murky swamp pond, Riviera's keen perception (rolling 30) detected large creatures moving swiftly beneath the surface—something the rest of the party failed to notice until it was too late. Giant frogs erupted from the water, their massive tongues lashing out at the party members. Combat erupted as multiple frogs attempted to grab and drag party members into the depths. Sha-Feng charged recklessly through their companions to engage the frog that had grappled them, knocking both Bujon and Holden aside before diving into the water for aquatic combat. The party fought valiantly, with Sarah defending Holden and Bujon unleashing attacks, while one frog was driven back by electrical damage. After a fierce battle, the party killed two of the giant frogs, though several others retreated into the murky depths.

Following their victory, the exhausted party pressed onward and spotted a structure built on stilts rising fifteen feet above the swamp on a dry mound to the southwest—a potential location of interest for their continued search. As they traveled further, they discovered the Poison Fields and managed to harvest approximately twenty ready-to-harvest corn cousins and some questionable berries that Sha-Feng identified as potentially useful for poison or food. The party also successfully killed two giant frogs and harvested four massive frog legs—approximately forty pounds of meat—which Sha-Feng expertly roasted on the beach using tinder twigs, creating turkey-leg-sized portions for the group. Along the southeastern shore, they navigated a sandy beach while carefully avoiding signs of giant crabs, whose notched fallen trees marked their territory. Though the rescue mission remained incomplete as the session ended, the party had proven their determination and resourcefulness in the face of Bonewrack Isle's deadly inhabitants.
