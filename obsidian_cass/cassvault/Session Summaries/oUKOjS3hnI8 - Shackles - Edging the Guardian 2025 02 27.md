## Summary

The crew interrogates and executes a captured Bronze Fleet sailor, learning about a massive slave-trading operation coordinated with Cheliax. They tow a captured Bronze Fleet brig back to Goats Head, celebrate with the locals, and sell loot. The crew then sails to an unnamed island in the Arrowhead chain where they discover a cave guarded by a stone golem. The golem puts most of the party to sleep with a Symbol of Sleep. Sha-Feng saves everyone by speaking Azlanti to the golem, designating the party as visitors. They recover valuable loot from crushed previous explorers, including the Tempest Veil cloak, and discover an Azlanti portal.
