# Iron Gods Campaign Journal: Family Drama (January 5, 2023)

The party's harrowing journey from the Scar of the Spider came to a dramatic conclusion when Nomkath and Crocodile disconnected the power from the brain in a jar they had encountered, effectively killing it over the course of many hours. Desperate to escape the desolate plains and return to civilization, the group made the decision to drive through the night rather than risk another camp. McGinnis and Ulfred consumed insomnia powder to maintain alertness during the grueling high-speed journey across the dusty landscape, with McGinnis demonstrating his characteristically reckless driving techniques to his companion. The party arrived in Torch around 4-5 AM in their six-wheeled monster truck, making a loud and conspicuous entrance that sent the town guards into a panic—one guard even fired an arrow at the windshield, which bounced harmlessly off. McGinnis greeted the alarmed guards cheerfully from the window, though the commotion woke Connor at an ungodly hour.

Upon their return, the party learned of troubling developments in town. A mysterious stranger had arrived approximately a week and a half prior, asking questions about McGinnis in bars and casinos but refusing to reveal their purpose. A few days ago, this stranger was killed in a violent shootout with an unknown gang that left six of the gang members dead. The battle had been brutal and professional—bodies were riddled with double-tap shots to the heart and head, and explosives and buckshot had been used liberally. Most alarmingly, a singed, bullet-hole-ridden envelope bearing McGinnis's name was found on the stranger's corpse, suggesting the mysterious visitor had been connected to McGinnis's past in ways yet to be revealed.

The party also discovered that significant events had transpired during their absence. A battle for control of Scrapwall had resulted in the death of Severoth Slade and the scattering of the Silver Hawks, with smoke visible from Torch during the conflict. Additionally, a city guard was found dead in front of The Foundry Tavern with a pool of blood forming around him, indicating violence had touched the town itself. In quieter moments, Nomkath and Meyanda shared a conversation about their shared nature as androids and time travelers, discussing the burden of their enslavement—a poignant moment that revealed deeper connections within the party.

The most significant development came with the introduction of Casandalee, an AI-like construct requiring power to function, to Connor and Val. The party learned that Casandalee had arrived with 100 power units but was now operating at only 75 out of 1000 capacity after minimal energy expenditure during travel. This sparked urgent discussions about establishing reliable charging infrastructure. Connor demonstrated his technomancy abilities by converting magical energy into electrical power to charge Casandalee's systems, and the party began planning how to keep her powered while traveling. Additionally, the party had acquired several important technological artifacts: an inhibitor facet capable of impairing AI functionality, a memory facet, and a compassion facet that was installed into Casandalee—an installation that caused her systems to go dark and her to scream, suggesting the emotional programming had profound effects on her consciousness.
