# Session Summary: The Sun Temple and Its Mysteries

The party's expedition through the deep jungle led them to follow a group of explorers who were hunting for lost treasure. Their trail proved treacherous when a tribe of cobalts ambushed the explorers, devouring most of them and leaving only a single survivor. The party engaged the cobalts in combat, fighting their way through the cobalt settlement and into a waterfall area that concealed a cave entrance. Beyond this natural barrier lay their true prize: a magnificent sun temple, untouched by civilization for centuries, hidden deep within the jungle's heart.

Upon entering the temple's grand chamber, the party discovered massive stone slab doors that opened to reveal a breathtaking sight—a great room dominated by a golden lizard man golem of immense size and craftsmanship. The construct challenged the party with a riddle, which they successfully answered, causing the guardian to step aside and grant them passage deeper into the temple. As they began their search for treasure and traps, the party's initial excitement was tempered by discovery: a statue they believed to be solid gold was merely painted soft metal. When they attempted to chip away the paint with a sword, a golden golem stepped forward to issue a warning before returning to its position.

The party's investigation revealed an intricate magical puzzle woven throughout the temple. Extensive runes in subtle gold fleck writing covered the statues and floor seams—runes of containment, suppression, nondetection, fireproofing, sealing, and soundproofing. The centerpiece of this arcane design was a giant screaming face in the center of a chamber, surrounded by statues with jeweled eyes featuring octagonal gem facets that all faced inward toward the central visage. Using detect magic and see invisible, the party discovered a critical discrepancy: the gemstone eyes reflected the room differently than direct observation revealed. Where the golden face should appear in the reflection, only a black shadow absorbed light.

Through careful observation and experimentation, the party determined that the chamber's appearance changed based on the time of day and the angle of sunlight entering through a hole in the ceiling. The golden face was only visible when sunlight struck it at the correct angle. When Buhan cast obscuring mist to block the sunlight, the face disappeared, confirming their theory. The party's exploration culminated in the discovery of a sarcophagus containing significant treasures: a staff or rod four feet in length, a suit of armor, a devil-themed iron chest with a pit fiend head clasp and glowing red eyes, and various trinkets. Among other acquisitions, the party obtained the Crown of Gol Khazak, which granted them the ability to command undead creatures with the word "Croatan," along with bracers and several items useful for light manipulation and reflection.
