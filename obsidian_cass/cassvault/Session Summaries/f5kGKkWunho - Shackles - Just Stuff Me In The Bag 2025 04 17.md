## Summary

Following an underwater battle with Sahuagin in a bay near a mysterious island, the party regroups aboard their ship after Sha-Feng was dragged away by a shark he hooked with a fishing rod. They examine Sahuagin weapons and gear, particularly sea steel blades that function normally underwater.
