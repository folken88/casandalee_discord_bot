## Summary

# Iron Gods Campaign Session Summary: "Just the Tip"

The party established a new campsite in the Valley of the Brain Collectors area, near a location where Khonnir Baine had previously engaged in a shootout with a ghost. During camp setup, Nomkath took the opportunity to have private conversations with party members about their divine connections and ancestry. This led to a significant character revelation when Nomkath shared the dark history of his bloodline—his ancestors were evil beings who murdered and made terrible pacts for power and wealth, perpetuating cycles of destruction across generations. However, Nomkath and his sister chose to break this cycle, escaping their ancestral society to use their inherited powers for good or neutral purposes instead of continuing the family legacy of evil.

The party engaged in meaningful moral debate about the ethics of their current situation, particularly regarding whether they should keep a captured creature in captivity for tactical advantage and information gathering. This discussion highlighted tensions within the group about compromising their principles for strategic benefit against dangerous enemies. Despite these disagreements, the party remained focused on their objectives and practical concerns.

Recognizing the need for a secure base of operations, the party decided to establish a teleportation circle at their current location. After debating various color options for the magical circle—blue, green, pink, purple, red, yellow, and dark variants—they settled on dark purple to match drow cultural themes. Rather than placing it on the exposed beach, they chose to construct the circle within nearby house ruins. The party then employed Tokala's Plant Growth magic to permanently camouflage the location, encouraging natural overgrowth around the ruins to conceal their new base from discovery.

With their secure teleportation hub established, the party turned their attention to their remaining exploration objectives: investigating a mysterious large pile of meat or assaulting the Dominion Hive. They planned to use Wind Walk to travel quickly between locations and discussed scouting strategies for the hive, considering aerial reconnaissance to avoid detection. However, they acknowledged that previous mental contact with Dominion creatures had already compromised stealth for some party members, making a direct assault risky. The party decided to gather more intelligence before committing to an attack.

Finally, the party resolved to investigate the meat pile first, planning to travel by boat and foot to reach it over the course of most of a day. They debated whether to launch a frontal assault or conduct smaller probing attacks to avoid triggering a full response from whatever creatures guarded the location. During their preparations, they acquired two scrolls of Non-Detection and a potion of the same, valuable resources for their upcoming scouting missions. The session ended with the party ready to depart for their next objective.
