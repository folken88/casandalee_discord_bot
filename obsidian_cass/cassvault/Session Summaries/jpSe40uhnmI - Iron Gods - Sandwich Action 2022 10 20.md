# Iron Gods Campaign Session Summary: "Sandwich Action"

The party encountered a coordinated assault by Kuritone orcs and alien creatures within an underground facility featuring walls of reptilian hide and glowing blue power spheres. Combat began with the party clustered in a chamber while Crocktaw held position fifty feet west down a passage. Alfred won initiative and the party quickly adapted their tactics, with Tokala deploying an entangle spell (DC 18) at the southern doorway to restrict enemy movement. The haste spell that had been active was winding down, leaving only 1-2 rounds of its benefits remaining. A large armored figure wielding a chainsaw emerged as a significant threat, forcing the party to adjust their positioning and defensive strategies.

The battle escalated as the party discovered the true nature of their enemies: Casatha creatures—humanoid forms inhabited by parasitic brain entities that would exit the host bodies upon death. Multiple Casatha fell to coordinated ranged attacks, their brains exposed as they collapsed. The party also contended with invisible enemies, prompting Olbryn to cast See Invisibility on several allies to level the playing field. A critical moment came when Maginnis's weapon misfired due to a critical failure, redirecting shots toward his own allies—most notably striking Olbryn multiple times with a Dominion Glazer, dealing 18-20 fire damage per hit and reducing the ally to -9 HP and unconsciousness.

The party's arsenal proved devastating against the alien threat. Takala wielded the chainsaw weapon with brutal efficiency, severing an orc-like commandant's spine completely in a single pass. An electrified chainsaw proved effective against the brain entities, with one creature destroyed after being frightened and subsequently electrocuted. Crocoda delivered the final blow to a Cassasaur with a tidal surge spell, finishing the creature that Ulfred had left at 1 HP with a flame strike. Throughout the encounter, multiple small brain creatures were systematically eliminated through coordinated ranged and melee attacks.

During the chaos of combat, Toby (a GM character fighting alongside the party) consumed a Thunder Chunk—a bitter-tasting blue crystal that amplified his voice to extreme volume with double range and provided +2 DC to voice-based attacks and spells (or +5 for speeches) for three hours. This acquisition proved useful for the party's continued operations within the facility. The session ended with the party having secured the chamber and eliminated the immediate threat, though questions remained about the nature of the facility, the origin of the brain creatures, and what other threats might lurk deeper within the underground complex.
