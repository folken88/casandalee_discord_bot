## Summary

The party launches a night assault on a Technic League captain's house in Starfall City. They wind walk from their sewer sanctum, infiltrate through the building's sewer pipes in gaseous form, and fight through multiple gearsmen robots including gunslingers, riot suppressors, and a harvester. Luna uses passwall and devastating electrical attacks. The party kills the captain (Abernathy, an android) and causes significant collateral damage to the building and surrounding area. They loot the house and escape with cybernetic parts, potions, and other valuable items.
