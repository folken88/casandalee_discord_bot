# Shackles - Gill Issue 2025 05 01

**Campaign:** Skull & Shackles | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=gPAktuyx4DE)

## Summary

The party, depleted of spell resources after a dungeon fight, negotiates with the Sahuagin leader Sorax to rest and receive healing from his shamans. Sorax offers to have the Sahuagin reincarnate Bujon (who died in combat) in exchange for a magical artifact worth at least 2,000 gold. Storgrim trades an old magic shield to pay for the reincarnation, finding the price absurdly cheap though Sorax believes he has bankrupted the party.

## Key Events

- The party is out of spell resources after dungeon fighting. They need to rest to recover strength. Sorax, their Sahuagin quasi-ally, is also at single-digit HP.
  - Characters: Holden, Storgrim, Sorax
  - Location: Gol Khazak (dwarven ruins)
- Sorax sends his shamans to heal the party. They heal Sorax first (predictably), then Forwami, then Holden. The party notes Sorax's shamans prioritize their own people.
  - Characters: Sorax, Holden
  - Location: Gol Khazak
- Sorax offers to have the Sahuagin reincarnate Bujon, granting him a new body from the sea via the shark god Seulo. He demands a magical artifact worth at least 2,000 gold, believing this is an absurdly high price.
  - Characters: Sorax, Storgrim, Bujon
  - Location: Gol Khazak
- Storgrim trades his old magic shield (valued at approximately 2,357 gold) to pay for Bujon's reincarnation, suppressing laughter at how cheap the price actually is.
  - Characters: Storgrim, Sorax
  - Location: Gol Khazak
- Sorax takes the shield for appraisal, believing he has closed the greatest business deal of his life and bankrupted the surface dwellers.
  - Characters: Sorax, Storgrim
  - Location: Gol Khazak
