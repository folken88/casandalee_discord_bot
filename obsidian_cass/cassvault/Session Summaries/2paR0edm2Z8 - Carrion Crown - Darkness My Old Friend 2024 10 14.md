# Carrion Crown - Darkness My Old Friend

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=2paR0edm2Z8)

## Summary

The Carrion Crown party launched an ambush against a group of vampires and their undead minions in a fortified compound. The session began mid-planning with Kovira preparing a scorching ray and ultimately opened with a devastating magic missile that double-critted, decapitating one enemy and killing another. The party employed a coordinated strategy with ranged attackers going first, followed by melee. Kai proved devastating as a polearm fighter with Combat Reflexes and Cat's Grace, getting up to 8 attacks of opportunity per round. Multiple vampires turned to mist form to escape, but Gaspar's positive energy channels destroyed several in their gaseous state. Danger (an NPC archer) pinned vampires to walls with precise shots. The session featured Tim driving extra NPCs (Danger and Dismas) and the party using silence spells to prevent detection.
