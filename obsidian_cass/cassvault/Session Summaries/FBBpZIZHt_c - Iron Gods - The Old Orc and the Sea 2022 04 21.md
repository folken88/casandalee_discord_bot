# Iron Gods Campaign Journal: The Old Orc and the Sea

After leveling up and resting, the party prepared for an evening expedition despite multiple warnings from NPCs to avoid the river at night. The group—Ulfred, Nomkath, Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, and Luna—cast protective spells including water breathing (lasting approximately 3 hours and 20 minutes), air walk, stoneskin, grease, and touch of the sea. They equipped themselves with a wand of air walk and discussed strategies for water-based combat, including using bait and thrashing to attract large predatory fish. The party was searching for a mysterious globe rumored to be in the deepest part of the river.

Upon approaching the southern shore, the party discovered a debris field stretching 500-600 feet along the coastline. Among the wreckage were manufactured objects including wood, plastic, and metal fragments—evidence of previous adventurers and possibly a large robot. They recovered a shattered oar matching their inflatable raft and a ruined spell book containing detect magic, light, jolt, message, shocking grasp, shield, frigid touch, and scorching ray. A magical cooking set was also salvaged. The discoveries suggested that others had ventured into this dangerous area before them.

As the party ventured into the middle of the river with Ulfred transformed into an air-walking ape form and Tokala playing music to attract creatures, they encountered approximately 7-8 man-sized fish creatures that suddenly surfaced and began attacking. Combat erupted immediately, with the party responding with coordinated offense. Ulfred cut creatures to pieces with melee attacks, Nomkath unleashed a lightning bolt spell that killed multiple smaller fish creatures, and Maginnis provided gunfire support with a plasma pistol. The battle was fierce and chaotic, with party members stacked in the water and creatures thrashing around them.

The party emerged victorious after a brutal aquatic battle. Multiple fish creatures were slain, including a particularly large and terrifying specimen that fell to combined attacks from Croctaw, Tokala, and other party members. Maginnis finished off the last remaining fish with a headshot. Among the spoils, Croctaw tore a chunk of flesh from the large fish's tail as it sank into the depths. The party also recovered prawn chowder that had been saved from a deceased NPC named Meyanda, which granted magical benefits including expeditious retreat, touch of the sea, and jump abilities. The session ended with the party victorious but facing the question of what other dangers lurked in the mysterious river.
