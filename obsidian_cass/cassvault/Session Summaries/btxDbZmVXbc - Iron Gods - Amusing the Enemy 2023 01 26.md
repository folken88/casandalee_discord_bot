# Iron Gods Campaign Summary: "Amusing the Enemy"

After their victory at Scrapwall, the party regrouped in Torch to prepare for their next major objective: infiltrating Starfall, the stronghold of the Technic League. The group spent their downtime wisely, with Tracy learning a powerful spell that could protect and disguise her identity through the use of a mask or full-face helmet. Meanwhile, the party conducted extensive reconnaissance, learning that legitimate trade routes existed between Torch and Starfall, with Iomedaean paladins and independent craftsmen regularly traveling between the towns to avoid Technic League monopolies. The party also discovered that a shipment from the Scar had recently arrived at the Attempting Tonic Shop, though their funds were running dangerously low after their extended campaign.

The party's planning became increasingly sophisticated as they debated their approach to Starfall. They learned that the Technic League maintained strict entry protocols: a 10 gold per-person entry fee and mandatory tech cataloging with a one-hour sale deadline. Rather than submit to these restrictions, the group considered multiple infiltration methods, including joining a caravan from Algernard's Grave as cover, finding back entrances, or locating waste disposal routes to bypass checkpoints entirely. Luna and Connor created detailed maps of Starfall's layout, identifying major districts including the Southern Grid Forge and Northern Sovereign's Watch, while the party recalled information extracted from Ferris Theorus's mind about safe houses and escape routes his team had previously used.

The party's most ambitious plan involved using the *Wind Walk* spell to conduct aerial reconnaissance of a location called "the kill box." They discovered that the city's constant trash burning via incinerators created permanent smog and haze—perfect cover for their magical reconnaissance. The group planned to wear brown cloaks while in Wind Walk form to blend with the smoke, though they discovered a critical limitation: they could not speak verbally while in this form and would need to rely on sign language if staying close together. This discovery forced them to reconsider their communication strategies for the infiltration.

To prepare for their infiltration, the party engaged in extensive shopping and commissioning. They purchased reversible brown cloaks from the Torch General Store for 5 gold each and considered acquiring additional storage solutions, including Bags of Holding (ranging from 2,500 to 5,000 gold) and a Magician's Chest taken from a hanged smuggler. However, they learned that the Technic League's border inspections could detect Bags of Holding, forcing them to plan alternative methods for smuggling their technological equipment into the city. The party ultimately decided to camp outside Torch for the night before attempting their Wind Walk reconnaissance the following day, marking the beginning of their dangerous gambit against the Technic League's most fortified stronghold.
