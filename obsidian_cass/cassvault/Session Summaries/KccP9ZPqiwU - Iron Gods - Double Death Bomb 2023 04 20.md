## Summary

Continuing a street-level operation, Tokala and her Scrapwall orcs captured a fleeing merchant/baron. The scrap witches tunneled into the sewers and then collapsed an entire neighborhood of tunnels to prevent pursuit, causing a massive earthquake that flattened surface buildings and caused civilian casualties. Nomkath sprinted invisibly through the earthquake-damaged streets to the Dead Block safe house. The party cast Non-Detection Communal to hide from Technic League divination. They then distributed Crassus's captured gear--the Panorama Celestial ring (true seeing, fly at will, teleports) to Ulfred, Rod of Metamagic Quicken and Hip Fire Band to Olbryn, Headband of Vast Intelligence +4 to Nomkath. Luna and Nomkath fenced loot totaling 28,601 gold, but the party remained about 22,000 gold short of Hearsick's asking price. Cassandalee confirmed she had mapped the entire Technic League social network from captured communicator logs.
