# Justice Gorls - Theres Sausage In Here 2025 10 31

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown (Justice Gorls) | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=JmtkuM02PAw)
**Transcript Quality:** poor
**Characters Present:** Antoinette Borden (Toni), Kelda Ironglim, Taelys of Starfall, Lirienne Voss, Crisp, Lou Candlebean

## Summary

The Justice Gorls infiltrate the Craggy Sons' underground smuggling lair on the north side of town to rescue remaining grad students and recover stolen archaeological artifacts. The party had previously rescued Professor Martin and some grad students; now they clear the remaining dungeon rooms, fight through waves of Craggy Sons guards, free the last captives, discover a secret room full of smuggled goods and historical artifacts, and share food with the grateful grad students. Halloween session played in costume.

## Key Events

- The party enters the Craggy Sons' underground lair. The GM recaps: they were hired to recover a missing person (the professor) with a small bonus for the grad students. They have already rescued the professor and some grad students, and learned more captives and stolen items are in the Craggy Sons' basement.
- A confused shopkeeper remains above ground; the party had previously entered by pretending to be customers at his shop.
- The entire party is extremely stealthy (Crisp at +12, multiple characters at +8 and +10 stealth). They attempt a group sneak but a guard rounds a corner and spots them, triggering combat.
- Lou rides on Crisp's back. Lou has multiple wheels of cheese and a "never spill goblet" that doubles spell duration.
- Kelda opens combat with a devastating sneak attack on a flat-footed guard, exploiting the unchained rogue's first-round advantage against enemies who have not yet acted.
- Taelys fires her gun, creating a deafening underground gunshot that alerts nearby enemies. This becomes a recurring issue throughout the session.
- Extended combat through multiple rooms against waves of Craggy Sons guards armed with bows and swords. Lou charges enemies with her hook hammer, earning the nickname "murder child" from the guards. Crisp tears enemies apart with claws (confirmed critical: two arrows through both eye sockets of one guard).
- Lou uses a dead guard's body as a shield, carrying him in a fireman's carry while advancing across the room.
- Kelda goes down during the fight (drops to dying) but stabilizes on a Constitution check.
- Toni heals Kelda with a wand of infernal healing, causing Kelda to wake up with boiling blood and momentary demonic sensations.
- Toni spots imprisoned grad students through a locked chain door and calls out to them. The party bribes and threatens the grad students with academic leverage ("I AM THE PROVOST") to get information about what lies beyond a secret door.
- Crisp scratches at a wall, alerting the party to a hidden compartment. Taelys discovers the keyhole by touch, and they open a secret room.
- The secret room contains smuggled goods: coffee, moonshine, 56 redwood planks, linen, six flutes, spades, training dummies, and a romance novel by "Ember Darren" hidden behind the secret door.
- More significantly, the party recovers stolen archaeological artifacts from Professor Martin's dig: the Mask of the Last Watcher, a reliquary, the Crusader's Tinder Box, the Signet Ring of Cast, the Dawnword Curus, the Scepter of Oathfire, and the Cloak of the Fallen Sentinel (Cloak of Resistance +1).
- A bronze locket, slightly warm to the touch, is found. Grad student speculation: "tiny holy ghost."
- The party also discovers pipe bombs, enchanted deer droppings (mild euphoric drug), and various ammunition.
- Taelys has gun jamming issues (misfire mechanic) during combat.
- The party frees the remaining grad students from their cells and shares food with them: sausages, coffee, and chocolate bars. The grad students make coffee with expert efficiency.
- The session ends with the party having cleared the entire Craggy Sons lair. One guard remains technically alive for interrogation. Some artifacts are still unaccounted for.
- The GM previews the underground mercenary job board ("the Job Well") with various illicit contracts, including a "fortune teller off" and a job at a former temple requiring retrieval of a specific item for 3,000 gold.
