## Summary

CP-USS descends into the lower level of Harrowstone Prison to confront the remaining undead threats. After defeating ectoplasmic wax-like ghosts in the flooded elevator chamber, they encounter and defeat a burning skeleton wielding a flaming great axe (killing guest character Deputy Chris in the process). They then face the spirit of the Lopper—a tortured ghost from the oubliette who wields a shadowy version of his old axe—in the prison's torture chamber, ultimately vanquishing him with the aid of positive energy channeling. The session featured significant combat challenges and the introduction of new deputy characters.

## Key Events

- Party descends into the flooded lower level of Harrowstone Prison via rope. Deputies struggle with the slippery descent; Dinvaya falls and takes damage.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Kate Blackwood, Deputy Chris, Deputy Charles
  - Location: Harrowstone Prison - Elevator Shaft
- Party encounters and defeats two ectoplasmic humanoid undead creatures made of melted wax-like material in the water chamber. Creatures attempt to attack the deputies.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Kate Blackwood, Deputy Chris, Deputy Charles
  - Location: Harrowstone Prison - Lower Level Water Chamber
- Party discovers the cursed weapons wielded by Deputies Chris and Charles—a bloodstained hand axe and a cursed hammer—have psychic effects. Deputy Charles experiences intrusive thoughts of violence; Deputy Chris becomes bloodthirsty and refuses to put down the axe.
  - Characters: Deputy Chris, Deputy Charles
  - Location: Harrowstone Prison - Lower Levels
- Party encounters a headless burning skeleton wielding a flaming great axe (later identified as a Burning Skeleton, immune to fire, weak to cold and blunt damage). In a brutal combat, the skeleton decapitates Deputy Chris in a single attack dealing 88 damage, killing him instantly.
  - Characters: Deputy Chris, Gaspar, Kovira, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Finn
  - Location: Harrowstone Prison - Guard Station/Burning Room
- Party defeats the burning skeleton using blunt force damage (particularly from Dinvaya's mace Sunrise). The skeleton explodes upon death, dealing fire damage to nearby combatants. Deputy Charles takes additional damage and nearly dies again but stabilizes.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Finn, Deputy Charles
  - Location: Harrowstone Prison - Guard Station/Burning Room
- New deputy character (Deputy Brian) arrives and retrieves the flaming great axe dropped by the burning skeleton. He becomes cursed by the weapon similarly to the previous deputies.
  - Characters: Deputy Brian
  - Location: Harrowstone Prison - Guard Station
- Party encounters the Lopper—a tortured ghost spirit of a murderer from the prison's oubliette, wielding a twisted shadow version of his axe and wearing a chain with a human skull. The spirit initially drains life from Deputy Brian and heals itself.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Finn, Deputy Charles, Deputy Brian
  - Location: Harrowstone Prison - Oubliette (Torture Chamber)
- Party defeats the Lopper ghost using multiple haunt siphons, channeling positive energy, shocking grasp spells, and Dinvaya's mace Sunrise. The ghost is bound by celestial chains and pulled toward Vesorianna, the friendly ghost who holds the souls of defeated undead.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Finn, Deputy Charles, Deputy Brian
  - Location: Harrowstone Prison - Oubliette
- Deputy Brian recovers from being knocked unconscious and releases his grip on the cursed axe. The party discusses the discovery of water at the bottom of the oubliette pit.
  - Characters: Deputy Brian, Gaspar, Kovira, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Finn
  - Location: Harrowstone Prison - Oubliette
