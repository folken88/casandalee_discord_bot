## Summary

CP-USS clears the guard barracks in Harrowstone Prison's western torture wing (Reaper's Hold), defeating the Mosswater Marauder and recovering the Warden's keys and a protective badge. They discover a torture chamber where the Warden's skeleton lies, and encounter a disturbing illusion of Kendra trapped in an Iron Maiden. The session ends as they prepare to search the remaining areas of Reaper's Hold.
