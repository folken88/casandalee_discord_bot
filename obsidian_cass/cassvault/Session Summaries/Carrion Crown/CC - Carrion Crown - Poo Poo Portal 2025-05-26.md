# Carrion Crown - Poo Poo Portal 2025-05-26

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=vcJllQF0sJQ)

## Summary

CP-USS enters a pocket dimension hidden in the Carrion Hill jail basement, revealing three invisible chaos beasts and a cultist sorcerer. After intense combat involving deafness, confusion spells, and massive damage, the party destroys all threats and escapes before the dimension collapses. They then travel east, encounter friendly elves at the hidden settlement of Eloren, and receive warnings about angry giants disturbed by the Whispering Way's recent passage through the region.

  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
