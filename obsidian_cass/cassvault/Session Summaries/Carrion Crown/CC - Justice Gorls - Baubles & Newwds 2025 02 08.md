# Justice Gorls - Baubles & Newwds 2025 02 08

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown (Justice Gorls) | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LMoybjYh5sI)

## Summary
The Justice Gorls meet for the first time at a bulletin board outside The Rusted Drake Inn on the poor southwest side of Caliphas. A ragtag group of outcasts, they compete over a bounty posting from Lord Valone offering 10 gold per head for a gang that robbed his warehouse. The party travels to the Valone residence, learns the stolen goods include paintings of Mrs. Valone and her uncle's old baubles, and investigates the burned-out warehouse. Through combined knowledge checks they identify the Clickboom Gang -- a group of sorcerers known for using grease and fire spells -- as the likely culprits, and arrange a fake purchase meeting to lure out a gang member.

## Key Events
- The party forms at The Rusted Drake Inn bulletin board, with multiple characters diving for the bounty poster simultaneously; Lirienne's velociraptor Crisp leaps across and grabs a different poster off the board
- The group travels to the Valone residence and speaks to a guard, who tells them Mrs. Valone's chess/heart collection, her uncle's baubles, and several paintings of Mrs. Valone (some nude) were stolen; the warehouse was burned during the robbery; deadline is Friday (3 days away)
- At the warehouse compound, Taelys examines the scorched locks and determines a sustained stream of fire (not an explosion) was used to melt them; Antoinette identifies this as a burning hands spell cast by someone of human height
- Badger (Tor's rat companion) discovers residue of a grease spell on the warehouse floor; Lou licks the grease to help identify it
- The party narrows the suspects from 30 gangs down to 2 (Red Devils or Clickboom Gang), then determines the Clickbooms are the most likely culprits -- a gang of sorcerers known for greasing locations and then igniting them
- Kelda's knowledge local (25) suggests the gang likely has fences and could be lured out by offering a fake purchase job; the party sets up a meeting to buy 300 teddy bears through criminal intermediaries
- At 7:30 PM, the party meets a Clickboom representative outside The Rusted Drake; he arrives theatrically, casts grease on Kelda and Antoinette, and combat begins
- The session ends mid-combat with the Clickboom member greasing the party and fighting breaking out in front of the tavern
