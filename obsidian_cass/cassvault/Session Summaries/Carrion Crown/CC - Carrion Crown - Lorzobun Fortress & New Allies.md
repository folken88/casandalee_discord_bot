# Carrion Crown - Lorzobun Fortress & New Allies

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=vxYI4BKapwI)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dismas, Kate Blackwood, Denva, Rissa (Beast of Lepidstadt)

## Summary

CP-USS travels to and discovers an ancient dwarven fortress now occupied by a disciplined orc garrison of Aori worshippers led by Krein Verir. After diplomatic negotiations using Denva's Aori sigil, the party gains entry and discovers a second shard of the broken sword hidden in a storage chest—the shard immediately attaches to the blade, upgrading it to +2. The party agrees to raid Fort Austra (an undead fortress to the northeast) to clear the region, then prepares to depart via boat down the river.

## Key Events

- Gaspar spots movement in the forest while the party travels uphill following the sword hilt's pull. The movement is from a 6-foot tall armored soldier, not a dwarf. The party buffs and pursues.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kai, Elfrip, Kovira, Dismas
  - Location: Northeastern mountains, foothills of Lorzobun
- The party discovers a massive dwarven-made fortress gate sealed into a mountainside. A half-orc woman named Krein Verir commands the guard. Diplomatic negotiations succeed when Gaspar displays the Aori sigil that Denva possesses.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kate Blackwood, Denva, All party
  - Location: Lorzobun Fortress entrance
- Inside Lorzobun: The party meets Krein Verir (half-orc commander), Vakra (fit warrior), Bam Bam (elderly orc cleric/mage), and Tram (half-orc quartermaster). The fortress is revealed to be an ancient dwarven stronghold now home to a community of Aori-worshipping orcs who have lived here for centuries.
  - Characters: Krein Verir, Vakra, Bam Bam, Tram, All party
  - Location: Lorzobun Fortress
- The sword hilt pulls toward a storage room. After investigation, the party and Bam Bam uncover a second shard of the broken sword hidden in the bottom of an ancient dwarven storage chest, sealed with stone shape magic centuries ago. The shard attaches to the blade, upgrading it from +1/1d4 to +2/1d6.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Bam Bam
  - Location: Lorzobun storage room
  - Timeline: The shard has been hidden since the time of the Whispering Tyrant's rule over Ustalav
- Bam Bam reveals the orc settlement's history: centuries ago, a brutal warrior named Lon rose through their ranks and unified them, converting them to the worship of Aori and ending their violent warlord culture. He passed away decades ago, and the settlement continues in his legacy.
  - Characters: Bam Bam, Party
  - Location: Lorzobun dining hall
- The party learns of threats around Lorzobun: werewolves to the southwest (Shudderwood), Fort Austra to the northeast (undead fortress, scouts never return), vampires at Castle Kronquist to the far east, and an unknown creature with medusa-like abilities to the west across the river.
  - Characters: Bam Bam, Krein Verir, Party
  - Location: Lorzobun dining hall
  - Timeline: Matches established threats in the region
- Party agrees to raid Fort Austra to clear undead and make the river passage safer for the orc settlement's quartermaster Tram, who could then travel to Neria for supplies. The party receives a boat, supplies, and Bam Bam prepares a Phantom Steed communal scroll for them.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, All party, Bam Bam, Tram
  - Location: Lorzobun Fortress
- The party receives the Aori sigil back as a permanent gift, which will allow them to always find their way back to the fortress and provides protection/recognition. They also purchase werewolf-bane greatswords and other specialized weapons at a 20% discount from Tram's shop.
  - Characters: All party, Tram, Bam Bam
  - Location: Lorzobun Fortress
- Party rests at Lorzobun for 12 hours. Kai remains awake due to effects of the Guts potion (48-hour sleeplessness). The broken sword shard now points northwest, indicating another piece exists in that direction.
  - Characters: Kai, Elfrip, All party
  - Location: Lorzobun Fortress
- Party departs Lorzobun in a small fishing boat with supplies, preparing to travel downriver to Fort Austra. Rissa appears to communicate with trees and can be summoned by calling her name to a forest tree.
  - Characters: All party, Rissa
  - Location: River near Lorzobun
