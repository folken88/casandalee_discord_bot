# Carrion Crown - Sugar Were Going Down Swimmin 2024-09-01

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=onaYjllKN0Q)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas, Rissa (NPC/played by Sid)

## Summary

CP-USS investigates the Temple of Salt in southern Shudderwood, following a mysterious pull from Farah in the middle of the night. After discovering an underwater cavern system filled with salt water, they encounter salt-preserved revenants chained to the bottom, a massive ancient leviathan creature (which they successfully calm), and finally confront a scorpion-shaped stone golem guardian in a vast temple chamber dedicated to Calistria. The session ends mid-combat with the Promethean badly wounded.

## Key Events

- Farah wanders into the forest at night, drawn to a circle of trees. The party follows and discovers the Hanging Valley haunt—a location with whispering dead and bodies suspended from trees.
  - Characters: Farah, Rissa, Curiosity, Savage, Kai, Elfrip, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: Shudderwood (Hanging Valley)
- CP-USS discovers the entrance to the Temple of Salt, a Calistrian holy site, via a pool with submerged stairs. Farah bonded with Gaspar's memory reveals the location and warns of salt water dangers below.
  - Characters: Farah, Rissa, Curiosity, Savage, Kai
  - Location: Temple of Salt entrance (Shudderwood)
- Party descends into a massive flooded underground cavern system. Rissa polymorphs into a seal to scout and discovers a 20-foot deep plunge pool with demolished spiral stairs and passages leading deeper into darkness.
  - Characters: Rissa, Farah, Savage, Kai, Elfrip
  - Location: Temple of Salt - underwater cavern
- CP-USS encounters salt-preserved revenants (undead corpses with manacles) rising from the gray saltwater layer. Combat ensues with multiple revenant enemies. The Promethean form proves effective against them due to piercing damage bypassing skeletal defenses.
  - Characters: Rissa, Savage (Promethean form), Farah, Kai, Elfrip
  - Location: Temple of Salt - cavern chamber
- A massive ancient leviathan (brine tooth)—estimated 70-80 feet long—emerges from the gray saltwater. Rissa successfully casts Calm Animals on it, preventing an attack. The party realizes this creature has grown significantly since Gaspar's last visit (~100 years ago, when it was only 20-30 feet).
  - Characters: Rissa, Farah, Savage, Kai
  - Location: Temple of Salt - cavern chamber
- Party crosses the cavern and enters a vast underground temple chamber with fallen pillars, shallow water, and a well-lit area to the south. They spot an enormous black stone scorpion statue—actually a living stone golem guardian.
  - Characters: Farah, Rissa, Savage, Kai, Elfrip
  - Location: Temple of Salt - main chamber
- Farah bonded manifestation allows Gaspar's phantom to appear. He walks toward the scorpion guardian (recognized as a Calistrian symbol by Farah's Knowledge Religion check). Combat initiates. Farah casts Glitterdust on the initially invisible golem to reveal it.
  - Characters: Farah, Gaspar (phantom), Savage, Kai, Elfrip
  - Location: Temple of Salt - main chamber
- The scorpion golem proves extremely dangerous. It grapples the Promethean with pincer attacks and uses tail stinger strikes. The Promethean is severely wounded (pierced through the body). The party realizes through Knowledge Arcana checks that golems are immune to most effects and respond only to their master.
  - Characters: Savage (Promethean form), Farah, Kai, Elfrip
  - Location: Temple of Salt - main chamber
