## Summary

CP-USS arrives in Ravengro to investigate the death of Professor Petros Lorrimor at the request of Judge Darymid. They meet with Lorrimor's daughter Kendra, examine a heavily magical and evil locked chest containing forbidden tomes, and investigate the professor's body at the Temple of Pharasma. They discover evidence the death was staged—Lorrimor was killed by unknown magical means while standing, then fell backward, and his head was deliberately crushed post-mortem to prevent speak with dead. Evidence strongly suggests death by high-level magic, followed by staging with the stone.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Dismas, Dinvaya, Kai, Elfrip, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith

  - Timeline: 4716.12.13 (Professor Petros Lorrimor's death near Harrowstone Prison)

  - Characters: Kendra Lorrimor
  - Location: Ravengro, Lorrimor family home and Temple of Pharasma
- Party casts sanctify corpse on Professor Lorrimor's body to prevent desecration. Party members pool their resources and make plans to hide their CP-USS badges to maintain secrecy about their affiliation.
  - Characters: Dismas, Dinvaya
