## Summary

CP-USS discovered Farah sleepwalking to a haunted grove with hanging bodies near Shudderwood. After investigating, they found an underground temple complex accessed through a flooded cavern. They encountered salt-preserved undead revenants, calmed a massive brine-tooth leviathan creature, and reached a guardian scorpion golem in a temple of Kyria. The session involved extensive underwater exploration and preparation for combat against the construct.

  - Characters: Grandpa, Promethean form, Farah
  - Location: Temple of Salt chamber
