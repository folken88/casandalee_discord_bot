# Carrion Crown - Digging Deeper (2022-04-24)

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=W_zMMcQEtjA)

## Summary

CP-USS arrives in Ravengro to investigate the death of Professor Petros Lorrimor, a renowned wizard who studied necromancy and cults. They meet his daughter Kendra, examine his heavily magical and evil-aura-radiating chest of books, and investigate the body. Their autopsy reveals Lorrimor was killed by high-level magic (not a rockslide accident as claimed) and his head was deliberately crushed post-mortem to prevent speak-with-dead interrogation. The session ends with an angry mob blocking the graveyard during his funeral.
