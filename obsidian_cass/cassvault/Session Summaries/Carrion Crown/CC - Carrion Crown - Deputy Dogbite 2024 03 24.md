## Summary

CP-USS (now Deputy Marshalls) continue investigating the Whispering Way by traveling through Shudderwood toward Ascanor Lodge. They encounter dead giant spiders and a headless cultist corpse in a forest clearing, speak with dead to learn the cult has 40+ undead minions, discover Kate Blackwood is a werewolf (revealing a long-kept secret), witness a psychic haunt of refugees massacred by Farah's grandfather, and battle a powerful spirit woman in the river valley who drains negative levels before being defeated.
