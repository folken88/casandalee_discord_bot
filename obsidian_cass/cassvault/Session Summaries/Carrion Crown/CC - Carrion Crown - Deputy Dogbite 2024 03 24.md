## Summary

CP-USS (now deputized as Deputy Ustalav Marshalls, nicknamed 'Deputy Dogbites') investigated a spider-filled clearing in Shudderwood where paladins were killed, conducted a speak-with-dead ritual on a giant spider to learn about whispering Way forces, and encountered a horrific psychic haunt of refugees massacred by Pharaoh Blackwood's grandfather a hundred years prior. The session ended with combat against a level-draining ghost of a pregnant woman at Malevolence Creek. Transcript quality was poor with significant audio artifacts.

## Key Events

- Party discovers dead Iomedaean paladins killed by giant spiders in a clearing. They bury the bodies with appropriate religious rites and perform Heal checks to determine cause of death (poison and spider bites).
  - Characters: Kai, Gaspar, Dismas, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Kovira, Elfrip
  - Location: Shudderwood, clearing near Silent Path
- Party casts Speak with Dead on a giant dead spider and uses Detect Thoughts. Kate learns the spider reports 20 alive enemies and 40 dead spiders attacked them. The spider reveals these creatures are psychic and enormous (15-20 foot legs). Party learns to potentially communicate with other spiders by claiming to be allies against the spiders' killers.
  - Characters: Kate Blackwood, Dismas, Gaspar, Dinvaya, Kai
  - Location: Spider clearing, Shudderwood
- Kate Blackwood is revealed to be a werewolf, specifically of the Broken Ones clan. She had kept this secret from the party. She reveals her family is civilized and she can use this connection for diplomacy with local werewolves.
  - Characters: Kate Blackwood
- Party witnesses a psychic haunt of Saurian refugees from approximately 100 years ago marching through Shudderwood on an ancient road. The scene shows them approaching a phalanx of soldiers led by a red-haired man in black armor (revealed to be Farrah's grandfather, the Butcher of Chastel), with fire blocking the road. The haunt replays continuously; standing and watching allows the party to rest overnight.
  - Characters: Kate Blackwood, Gaspar, Kai, Dismas, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Rodney Danger Smith, Kovira
  - Location: Raton Valley / ancient road in Shudderwood
- Party encounters a level-draining ghost of a pregnant woman at Malevolence Creek. Dismas takes the initial charge and uses a Litany spell to debuff the ghost. Combat ensues; the ghost uses Phantasmal Killer to drain Kai of 5 negative levels and Dismas of 2 negative levels before being destroyed by Gaspar. Party negotiates restoration costs.
  - Characters: Dismas, Kai, Gaspar, Dinvaya
  - Location: Malevolence Creek, Shudderwood
