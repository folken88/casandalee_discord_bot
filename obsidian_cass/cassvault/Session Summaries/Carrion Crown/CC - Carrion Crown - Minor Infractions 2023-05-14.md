# Carrion Crown - Minor Infractions 2023-05-14

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=QW1M9PCfmIk)
**Transcript Quality:** poor
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

## Summary

CP-USS investigates multiple crime scenes related to the Beast of Lepidstadt's trial. After defeating Ghasts at Sanctuary Hospice and recovering evidence (including six severed heads and surgical tools purchased from a surgery shop), they present findings to the public defender. They then travel to Hergstag to investigate child murders, discovering that a Wraith—not the Beast—killed the children and has created additional wraiths from its victims. One wraith is destroyed, and four child skeletons are found in a hidden cave.

## Key Events

- CP-USS defeats Ghasts at Sanctuary Hospice and recovers evidence: six severed heads, crossbow bolts, a grease bomb vial, and nut butter. They determine the hospice fire was intentionally set using grease bombs.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

He smelled strongly of chemicals.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: Lepidstadt Courthouse holding cells

  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Judge Embreth Darymid
  - Location: Lepidstadt Courthouse

  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: Hergstag
- CP-USS discovers a corpse of a man caught in a bear trap on a hill in Hergstag, suggesting he was fleeing from something. They also find four child skeletons in a hidden cave behind the scarecrow on the hilltop.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: Hergstag, hilltop cave
