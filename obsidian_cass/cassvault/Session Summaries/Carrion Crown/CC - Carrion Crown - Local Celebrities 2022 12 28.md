## Summary

CP-USS recruits Rodney 'Danger' Smith, a local celebrity guide, to lead them from Ravengro toward Lepidstadt via the town of Cortaud. They encounter evidence of wolf attacks on the road, investigate a farm west of Cortaud, and discover a pack of Timberwolves led by a massive winter wolf at an abandoned Smuggler's hideout, which they engage in combat.
