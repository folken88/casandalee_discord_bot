# Carrion Crown - Local Celebrities 2022 12 28

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=7XG-zJJuRQw)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

## Summary

CP-USS travels from Lepidstadt toward Courtaud by carriage, hiring the local celebrity guide Rodney 'Danger' Smith. After an uneventful journey, they discover the town of Courtaud is plagued by a legendary white wolf called 'The Devil in Gray' that recently dragged a town guard named Tiny over the wall. The party tracks the wolves to their den in the ruins near Smuggler's Fin, encounters a pack of timberwolves mixed with bandits, and defeats several in combat, discovering historical artifacts including the blessed mithril breastplate of Trusty Killian from The Shining Crusade era.

## Key Events

- CP-USS hires Rodney 'Danger' Smith as guide for 20 gold (4 gold/day × 5 days) to travel to Courtaud. Danger is a famous local ranger and folk hero.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: Versailles (local town)
- Party encounters abandoned cart on the road with dead horse killed by wolves. A second body (hand and arm) found nearby, identified by Danger as not belonging to Denny LeBlanc. Wolves had passed several hours prior.
  - Characters: Rodney Danger Smith, Gaspar
  - Location: Road between Versailles and Courtaud
- Party arrives in Courtaud (also called 'Cortod' by locals). Town is eerily quiet with shutters closed. Innkeeper reports wolves took a man named 'Tiny' off the wall recently.
  - Characters: All PCs, Innkeeper NPC
  - Location: Courtaud / Cortod
- Through investigation, party learns of historical legend: 'The Devil in Gray,' a legendary white wolf with blue eyes that terrorized the area 30-40 years ago. Local guard 'Big Dick Jimmy' reports seeing Tiny dragged over the wall by a huge white shape with glowing blue eyes.
  - Characters: Big Dick Jimmy (NPC), Party
  - Location: Courtaud jail
- Party tracks wolves westward from town and discovers a farm with blood in the livestock pen and human remains. Wolf tracks indicate 4-5 wolves dragging prey south toward Smuggler's Fin.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Rodney Danger Smith
  - Location: Farm west of Courtaud
- Party follows wolf tracks to ancient ruins near Smuggler's Fin. They discover a smuggler camp with food stores, rowboats, and a corpse (torso only) in the snow. A campfire is still smoldering.
  - Characters: All PCs
  - Location: Ruins near Smuggler's Fin
- Combat with timberwolves and hints of a much larger wolf. Dismas kills one wolf with a single shot. Party defeats approximately 6-8 timberwolves through combat. Wolves flee after suffering casualties. A very large wolf's tracks are found but the creature itself never materializes.
  - Characters: All PCs, Rodney Danger Smith
  - Location: Ruins near Smuggler's Fin
- Party recovers smuggler loot: silver bar (identified as mithril by Dinvaya), two potions of cure light wounds, a blessed mithril breastplate from The Shining Crusade era belonging to war hero Trusty Killian (blessed by demigod Arazni), a Taldane-language book, silver crossbow bolts, and coin.
  - Characters: All PCs, Dinvaya
  - Location: Ruins near Smuggler's Fin
