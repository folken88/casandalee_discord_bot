## Summary

CP-USS arrives in Carrion Hill to investigate aberrations bursting out of townspeople. After a morning battle with harpies and a basilisk-like creature emerging from the sewers, the party begins exploring the ancient sewer system beneath the town. They discover the creatures are not undead but planar aberrations, likely summoned by the Whispering Way cultists who passed through days earlier. The party prepares to venture deeper into the sewers to find the source and encounters invisible enemies in the tunnel system.
