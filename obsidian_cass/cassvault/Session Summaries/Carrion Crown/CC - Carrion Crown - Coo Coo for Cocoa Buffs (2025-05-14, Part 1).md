## Summary

CP-USS battles a massive chaos beast infection in Carrion Hill's brewery, resulting in two PC deaths (Kai and Elfrip). They discover the town's beer is tainted with parasitic worms that have been infecting the populace for two weeks. After regrouping and casting buffs, the party prepares to descend into the prison basement to confront the source of the infection, encountering a caged, lobotomized jailer and hearing a mysterious voice commanding them not to enter.
