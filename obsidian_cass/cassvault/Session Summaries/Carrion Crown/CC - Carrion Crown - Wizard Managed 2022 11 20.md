## Summary

CP-USS explored the Nevermore section of Harrowstone Prison and encountered a dangerous incorporeal ghost wizard (the Splatterman) wielding spectral weapons and casting potent spells. After a lengthy combat involving haunt siphons, dispel magic attempts, and strategic positioning, the party defeated the ghost. They recovered the warden's sword and badge, which Vesorianna used to banish the remaining five prison ghosts. The party leveled to 4th level and gained the gratitude of Ravengro.
