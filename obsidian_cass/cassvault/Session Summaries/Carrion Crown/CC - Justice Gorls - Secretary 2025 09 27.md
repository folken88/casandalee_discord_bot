## Summary

The party rescues grad students and a professor from the wilderness gang 'Skanktown Killeets,' discovering they were being sold into slavery to the Cragfist smugglers in Calipus. After delivering the rescued academics to the university, they investigate the Tong Hammer smithy in north-central Calipus at midnight, where they encounter and eliminate two armed guards in the back alley. While the rest of the party distracts the blacksmith with conversation, they discover a hidden underground smuggling operation beneath the smithy, stacked with mysterious crates.
