## Summary

CP-USS arrives in Carrion Hill to investigate disappearances and discovers the town is plagued by chaos spawn—aberrations that burst from infected townspeople. After recruiting the bard Elodie and learning about the phenomenon from the tavern keeper, the party stakes out the town at night. When a massive aberration attacks, they engage in a desperate battle against multiple invisible tentacled creatures, ultimately defeating them but discovering 15 civilian bodies destroyed from the inside out. The party realizes the Whispering Way may be responsible for leaving these horrors behind as they fled through the area.
