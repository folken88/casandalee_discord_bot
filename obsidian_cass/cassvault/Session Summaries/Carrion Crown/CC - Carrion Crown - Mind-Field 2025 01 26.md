# Carrion Crown - Mind-Field 2025 01 26

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=oQK9jHMLHoM)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

## Summary

CP-USS successfully interrogates a captured Whispering Way cultist through mind-reading and forced intoxication, extracting critical intelligence about the cult's operations in Feldgrau, their ritual components (the Carrion Crown), and leadership structure. The team discovers the cult fears exposure above all else and learns of multiple enemy forces including giants, spellcasters, and specialized undead. They arrange safe detention of the cultist with the Broken Ones werewolves, while a second team narrowly escapes Feldgrau as a fire giant destroys their temporary base.

  - Characters: Sabrisa, Kate Blackwood, Gaspar
  - Location: Shudderwood
