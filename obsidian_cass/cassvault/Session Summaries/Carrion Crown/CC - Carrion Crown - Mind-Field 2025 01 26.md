## Summary

CP-USS interrogates a captured Whispering Way cultist using detect thoughts while intoxicating him, extracting crucial intelligence about the cult's operations in Feldgrau, their artifact gathering (the Carrion Crown), and Auren Vrood's location. The session culminates in the party splitting—one group delivering the cultist to the Broken Ones werewolf clan for safekeeping while the other group flees a fire giant assault on their fortress and reggroups in the forest.
