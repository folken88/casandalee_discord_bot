## Summary

CP-USS assaults Fort Austra and discovers it heavily defended by undead, including vampiric mists, ghoul knights, and dozens of flaming skull heads. After a brutal first engagement where Kovira and Kate are killed, the party retreats to rest. They return fully buffed and begin a second assault, with Elfrip in fire elemental form proving invaluable against the fire-based undead defenses. The session ends with the party pushing deeper into the fort's interior courtyard.
