# Carrion Crown 1-Shot - Neck-Deep in BHOL

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LIl6GzwB2yQ)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

## Summary

A criminal organization known as the Black-and-Operative-League (BHOL) accepts a high-paying assassination contract worth 110,000 gold to kill Antonette Bordon, wife of a mob accountant. The team investigates her family's properties, discovers they operate a winery with enslaved workers and mysterious security, uncovers that a police captain was killed by Antonette (revealed to be a vampire) a year prior, interrogate and mind-wipe a witness, and ultimately assault a warehouse complex where they encounter vampire guards.

## Key Events

- BHOL receives a contract to assassinate Antonette Bordon for 110,000 gold with a 3-day deadline. The job is flagged as potentially involving undead elements.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: BHOL Headquarters, Lepidstadt
- Team gathers intelligence at the Hound's Tooth bar. They learn Antonette Bordon is the wife of a mob accountant, and that the family owns the Blood Kiss Winery and warehouses on the docks. Information reveals the winery has a quarterly 'inspection week' with increased security and no staff.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai
  - Location: The Hound's Tooth Bar, Lepidstadt
- Dismas, disguised as a Paladin of Iomedae, infiltrates Precinct 8 and retrieves police records. Records show Captain Benedict was killed during a raid on the Bordon family approximately one year ago. All officers involved were transferred, retired, or terminated. An observer named Harlon Jackrick left the force immediately after.
  - Characters: Dismas
  - Location: Precinct 8 / Castle Streth, Lepidstadt
- Team locates and subdues witness Harlon Jackrick (now using alias 'Bram Uerick') at his cousin's apartment. Two of three individuals are killed (one by magical ice bolt, one mauled by Gaspar in bear form). Jackrick is interrogated and reveals Antonette Bordon personally killed Captain Benedict by tearing out his throat with her teeth after he yelled at her. She has glowing red eyes, superhuman speed, and appears to be a vampire.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Kate Blackwood, Dismas
  - Location: Harlon Jackrick's Apartment
- Jackrick's memories are modified by Dinvaya using the 'Modify Memory' spell. New false memory implanted: Jackrick believes he murdered his two companions out of jealousy over a romantic interest. All memories of the team and the encounter are erased.
  - Characters: Dinvaya, Harlon Jackrick
  - Location: Harlon Jackrick's Apartment
- Team scouts the Blood Kiss Winery from the air. Observations include: enslaved halfling workers, multiple advanced aegis guards in heavy black plate armor with enhanced perception, a prepared banquet meeting room, and no visible standard security. Guards appear to be searching/inspecting the property.
  - Characters: Gaspar
  - Location: Blood Kiss Winery, east of Calus
- Team buffs up with spells (Mirror Image, Invisibility, Shield, Haste, Cloak of Dreams) and assaults a warehouse complex in Lepidstadt's docks. Combat begins immediately upon discovery. Team encounters multiple thugs/archers and a vampire guard in heavy black plate armor who regenerates and fights with superhuman agility.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Kate Blackwood, Dismas
  - Location: Warehouse Complex, Docks District, Lepidstadt
