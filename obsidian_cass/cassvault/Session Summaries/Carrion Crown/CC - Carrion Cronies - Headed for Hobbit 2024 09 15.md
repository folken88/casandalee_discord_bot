# Carrion Cronies - Headed for Hobbit 2024 09 15

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LyWMKe9gPAg)

## Summary

CP-USS arrives at Ascanor Lodge to arrest Estovion Lazarov on a warrant for conspiring with the Whispering Way. After failed diplomatic attempts with a room full of Iomedaean paladins, the party disguises themselves as Lodge staff to search the premises. They discover Estovion has left the lodge but locate his necklace still inside via magic. Upon returning to confront him, they trigger a chaotic combat in the main hall when his true nature is revealed—he is possessed by or serving as host to a powerful entity. Combat breaks out against possessed noble Silus Graden, summoned hounds, an invisible archer (Delgross), and confusion magic.
