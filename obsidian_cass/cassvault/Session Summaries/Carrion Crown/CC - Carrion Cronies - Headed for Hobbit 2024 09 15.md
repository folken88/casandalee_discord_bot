## Summary

CP-USS arrived at Ascanor Lodge to arrest Estovion Lazarov on a warrant from Judge Darymid. After failed diplomatic attempts with Iomedaean paladins, the party disguised themselves as lodge staff and attempted to locate Estovion within the compound. A locate object spell revealed his necklace (the Artiste Pendant) was still within the lodge. Upon their return to investigate, combat erupted when they encountered Silus Graden (possessed by the Vilkacis) and hunting hounds, with arrows from the hunter Delgross flying through the melee.
