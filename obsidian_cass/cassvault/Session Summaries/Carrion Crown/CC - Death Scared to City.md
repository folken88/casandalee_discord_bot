# Death Scared to City

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=K6J7UgHa5n4)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas, Elfrip

## Summary

CP-USS investigates the Hergstag wraith and discovers it was responsible for the child deaths, not the Beast. After clearing Hergstag, they use Speak with Dead on recovered heads and learn that a man named Grimes burned down Sanctuary Hospice and was purchasing corpses on the black market. Before raiding Grimes' chemic works, a major riot erupts at the courthouse orchestrated by an invisible assassin and masked agitators attempting to lynch the Beast. CP-USS responds with overwhelming force, disperses the crowd, and secures the courthouse. Judge Darymid grants them a search warrant for Fork Stag and Grimes' Chemic Works, giving them until noon the next day to gather evidence before testifying at the Beast's trial.
