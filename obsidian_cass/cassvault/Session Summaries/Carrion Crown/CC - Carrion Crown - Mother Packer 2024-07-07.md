## Summary

CP-USS investigates a ghoul-werewolf outbreak at a historical battery site near Chastel, discovering evidence of demonic corruption and a link to the nearby Willow Meir estate across the river in Ardeal. After defeating numerous hybrid undead creatures, they loot equipment bearing the sigils of Ardeal's wealthy estates and prepare to pursue the outbreak to prevent catastrophic spread. The session contained significant technical issues with the VTT system affecting spell resolution and combat tracking.
