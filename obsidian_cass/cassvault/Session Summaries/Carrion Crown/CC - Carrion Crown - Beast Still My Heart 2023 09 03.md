# Carrion Crown - Beast Still My Heart 2023 09 03

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=3SepYz2735w)

**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

## Summary

CP-USS scouts and battles the Promethean creature atop Schloss Caromarc's tower. After a fierce fight where the Beast sacrifices itself to push the Promethean over the edge, the party recovers the Beast's brain from an armored containment vessel in its chest cavity. They return to Lepidstadt with Caromarc as a prisoner, the Beast's preserved brain, and plans to reconstruct the Beast in a new body. The party reaches level 7 and enters a month of downtime.
