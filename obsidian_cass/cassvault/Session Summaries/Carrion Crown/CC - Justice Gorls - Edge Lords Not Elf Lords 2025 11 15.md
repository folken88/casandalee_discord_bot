# Justice Gorls - Edge Lords Not Elf Lords 2025 11 15

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown (Justice Gorls) | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mMDV36cQiZE)
**Transcript Quality:** poor
**Characters Present:** Antoinette Borden (Toni), Kelda Ironglim, Lou Candlebean, Lirienne Voss, Taelys of Starfall, Crisp

## Summary

The Justice Gorls escort the rescued grad students out of the Craggy Sons' lair and march them back toward town. They share supplies, arm the grad students with spare shortbows and armor, and prepare for the day-long walk. Around 4 AM in an unfamiliar neighborhood, the party is ambushed by "the Elf Lords" -- a gang of young human men (roughly 20 years old) wearing green outfits, using longbows, and speaking in terrible fake elven accents. A chaotic rooftop battle ensues in which the grad students enthusiastically (and poorly) join the fight. The Elf Lords are routed. The party levels up to 5th level at session's end.

## Key Events

- The session opens with the party still in the Craggy Sons' lair, distributing loot and supplies to the grad students. Items given include sausages, coffee, chocolate, and enchanted deer droppings (euphoric drug).
- The grad students are freed from their cells and equipped with spare shortbows, arrows, and studded leather armor for self-defense during the march. When handed kukri daggers, one immediately tries to stab another, and the party threatens them with loss of "program of study" to restore order.
- Kelda's family connection to the university is established: her aunt is Baroness Karen Likenhine, and the family has donated enough for a wing. Kelda leverages this status to intimidate the grad students into compliance.
- The party is heavily overencumbered from gold and loot. They discuss needing to go shopping and visit a bank.
- The march to town begins around 2 AM. It is snowing lightly and dark. Taelys and Kelda have darkvision; the rest do not. The grad students stumble around in the dark, holding knives and tripping over each other.
- Around 4 AM, in a neighborhood the party has never visited, Crisp uses his advanced scent ability to alert Lirienne. She spots 3-4 silhouetted figures rising above rooftops with drawn bows.
- A voice in the dark announces: "We are the Elf Lords. Cough up your funds or take an arrow in the eye." The party responds with profanity.
- Combat breaks out on rooftops on both sides of the street. Lirienne fires a lit arrow onto a roof to illuminate the area. The party debates whether children might be in the buildings before engaging.
- The Elf Lords are revealed to be young human men (roughly 20 years old) wearing green outfits with hoods, using longbows, and speaking with poorly executed fake elven accents. Lou identifies them on a perception check when she climbs onto the roof. One exclaims "Don't touch me, filthy human" before being hit with Lou's hook hammer.
- The grad students join the fight enthusiastically, firing their stolen shortbows at the Elf Lords. Their accuracy is abysmal (most arrows sail into the city blocks away), but a few manage to land hits. One grad student drills an Elf Lord in the chest; the Elf Lords panic.
- Taelys fires her rifle and one-shots an Elf Lord leader. Another Elf Lord screams "Holy [expletive], she's gone!"
- Lou and Crisp climb onto a rooftop together. Crisp has a natural climb speed and runs straight up the wall. Together they savage an Elf Lord: Crisp leaps onto his shoulder, rips part of his face off, then tears out his throat.
- One Elf Lord attempts to flee across rooftops. The party shouts "Stop or we'll shoot." He keeps running. Lirienne shoots him in the back; he body-skids off the roof into a dumpster.
- One grad student (Billy) is shot in the torso by an Elf Lord arrow and goes down dying. Five grad students attempt CPR simultaneously, failing multiple heal checks. Billy is eventually stabilized with the wand of infernal healing, causing temporary glowing red eyes and a deeper voice.
- The surviving Elf Lords flee. The party collects leather armor, bows, and daggers from the fallen. They backtrack to the same area where the Craggy Sons shop was.
- The party levels up to 5th level (leveling from 4th). The GM walks players through the level-up process, including stat increases, new skill points, and class features.
