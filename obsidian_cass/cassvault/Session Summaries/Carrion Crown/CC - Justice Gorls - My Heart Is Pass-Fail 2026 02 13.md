# Justice Gorls - My Heart Is Pass-Fail 2026 02 13

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown (Justice Gorls) | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LXFhhtiHGpE)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Antoinette Borden (Toni), Kelda Ironglim, Taelys of Starfall, Lirienne Voss, Crisp, Aguclandos Lem (Agu), Dr. Tamsin Virelle

## Summary

A two-part session. In the first half, the Justice Gorls attend the funeral of Professor Vellesca Martin at the Church of Abadar in Caliphas. They deliver eulogies, are publicly honored as heroes, and are approached at the funeral by a dark-haired Osirriani woman named Khep Anotep who hires them to investigate her uncle's suspicious death -- ruled a suicide by Calops but which she believes was murder. The party visits Khep's tattoo shop (Scarab Bean), where Toni receives a magical angel wings tattoo granting feather fall/levitation. They consult with Agu about the uncle's smuggling background. In the second half, the party breaks into the uncle's sealed three-story villa, fights through two floors of zombie servants, discovers the entire household was killed and reanimated, and finds a trove of necromancy-related artifacts including a soul anchor bust of Aroden. The session ends mid-exploration with the attic and ground floor still to be fully searched.

## Key Events

### Part 1: The Funeral

- Slass Venton ("Sassy") delivers a handwritten invitation to the party at their Clickboom gang hideout. The letter invites them to the celebration of life for Professor Vellesca Martin, with reserved seating in the first three rows at the Church of Abadar.
- The invitation includes an original charcoal drawing of the professor, signed by 14 grad students. Smudges at the bottom are from tears.
- Slass mentions he has a job for them next week but tells them to stay out of jail in the meantime.
- Dr. Tamsin Virelle (Ashley's new character) arrives with Lirienne. The party learns Tamsin is known in the underworld as "Dr. Tam" -- an off-the-books street doctor who treats criminals for cash, no questions asked. Lirienne and Tamsin recently fought half a dozen zombies together at the sewer docks while retrieving medical supplies.
- The party discusses funeral attire: no armor, no weapons. Concealed items include daggers in boots, Toni's fighting fan (passes as a non-weapon), Lou's quarterstaff (justified as a walking aid), alchemist fire in a pocket, and Toni's collapsible handaxe (Professor Martin's signature weapon -- there are posters of her holding it, so it must be concealed).
- The eulogy at the Church of Abadar reveals Professor Martin's heroism: she rescued a family of four (and their cat) from a burning building, suffering severe burns; she helped Calops catch the Bone Bandit of Bend Avenue with a slide tackle; and she donated 75% of her salary to local charities.
- The party is publicly acknowledged as the heroes who saved Professor Martin from the Skanktown Killers (the gang whose leader Agu beheaded, earning the title "Queen of Skanktown").
- Kelda delivers a speech honoring the professor's research work, ending with "taken too soon from us." A grad student tearfully says "My heart is pass-fail" (the episode title).
- Lirienne gives an awkwardly honest speech about how they "probably shouldn't be here," ending with "We are here" -- which the grad students echo with fists in the air.
- Toni delivers the most polished eulogy: "We should all be honored to have lived in the same time as a hero like Professor Martin."
- Lirienne presents Crisp, who says nothing. Agu delivers a somber speech about death and gratitude.
- The Calops city police are in the front row, crying. The provost of the university is in attendance.
- The party is internally squirming because they are guilty of killing the professor (accepted a contract). Her death was declared random gang violence by authorities. The public is now furious at gangs, and Calops has doubled nightly patrols and is cracking down.

### Part 2: Khep Anotep and the Investigation

- A dark-haired woman with braids (Khep Anotep, pronounced "Kep") approaches the party after the service. She is Osirriani (Pathfinder's Egypt equivalent) and runs a tattoo parlor called Scarab Bean.
- Khep wants to hire the party to investigate her uncle's death. He died two weeks ago. Calops ruled it a suicide, but Khep is certain it was not. She uses divination magic (prediction spells) and has seen that if she goes to her uncle's house herself, she dies; if she goes to Calops, she disappears. She cannot investigate personally.
- The uncle was a smuggler and trader of dangerous and illegal goods, including dark arts artifacts. He was Osirriani, had a public identity as a rare goods merchant, and had lived in Caliphas for years. He kept a low public profile.
- Khep offers payment in gold plus tattoo work. As a show of good faith, she gives Toni a magical tattoo up front: angel wings (black and gray with red tips across the shoulders), granting feather fall/levitation, usable twice per day based on Intelligence modifier. The tattoo is a magical scroll written into skin that leeches spell energy from the caster to activate.
- The tattoo shop contains many magical tattoos for sale: Muscle Mommy (+8 carry strength), Fasmataz Apron (+5 cooking, +2 fire saves), Mother's Insight (+2 Wisdom), Eyes of the Eagle (perception bonus), Mask of Majora (facial disguise), Golden Death (+Dex), Angel Wings (fly 30ft or 60ft), Dead Saint (+2 Int, +1 Will saves), and a Durstan tattoo (+2 Charisma). She also sells rare books.

### Part 3: Consulting Agu

- The party meets Agu at "Panera" (the party's go-to meeting spot) and asks about the uncle's connections.
- Agu explains there is an entire underground smuggling system in Caliphas with established channels, bribes, and secret passages. Calops investigates deaths based on the victim's social standing -- a low-profile smuggler's death would be easy for them to close quickly.
- Agu says the uncle dealt in things "so heinous they would send the Uslavian public into absolute panic." However, Agu's organization has no stake in this particular investigation.
- Agu warns them to be careful what they touch at the house -- it could be a cursed item that killed him.
- Dr. Tamsin's contacts reveal critical intelligence: the cops who responded to the death scene found all the uncle's servants dead. As they were carrying out his body, the servants rose as zombies and attacked them. The cops fled, chained the gates shut, and put it under Sepost (SEUS) jurisdiction -- the city's undead specialist squad, a subdivision of the police that answers to higher city officials like the judge corps.
- SEUS is currently out of town and overdue from a mission. The property is sealed with chains and a "restricted" sign by SEUS order.
- The party visits Darkloaf Bakery, where Lirienne, Tamsin, and Crisp have a permanent discount on free rye bread from a previous adventure at the sewer docks. A young boy at the bakery feeds Crisp bacon strips.

### Part 4: The Anotep Villa -- Ground Floor & Exterior

- The party arrives at the Anotep villa at 9 PM. It is in a medium-nice neighborhood with adobe walls and iron gates. The gates are chained shut with a SEUS restriction sign.
- Kelda picks the padlock easily and they enter the grounds.
- In the yard, old family graves have been recently dug up. They glow with a fading blue magical barrier. Tamsin identifies the barrier as a sanctify spell -- a circle of salt that prevents corpses within it from becoming undead. The wrapped bodies in the graves are old (possibly hundreds of years), predating the Anotep family's residence.
- Toni's knowledge check reveals the house's foundation is pre-Shining Crusade (possibly 1,000+ years old, from the era of Tar-Baphon's rule), built with magic-enhanced stonework. The house itself is 100-200 years old.
- Crisp scouts ahead and peers through a window. A zombie slams against the window glass, shattering it and reaching for Crisp. Initiative is rolled.

### Part 5: The Anotep Villa -- Combat (Ground Floor)

- Lirienne fires two crossbow bolts in rapid succession: one through the first zombie's eye socket (killing it pinned to the window frame), and a second into another zombie sprinting through a doorway (through the eyes, face-planting it on the floor).
- The front door is locked. Toni runs to it; Kelda follows and picks the lock by jamming her lockpick in and shattering it with the butt of her kukri, then ripping the door handle free with brute force.
- An armored zombie guard with shield and spear attacks Toni, immune to her mirror images because undead can sense the living. It stabs her.
- Taelys agrees to hold her fire to avoid alerting Calops (gunfire in a residential area would bring police in minutes). She fights with her handaxe instead, using the professor's collapsible axe -- the same weapon shown on Professor Martin's posters. She takes a zombie's head clean off.
- Tamsin deploys her spiritual quarterstaff ("spooky staff"), striking from two squares away.
- Toni casts burning hands, catching two zombies. The smaller zombie burns up; the armored brute catches fire with its head pinned to its armor but remains standing.
- A zombie bites Kelda during the fight (zombie infection concern noted).
- Crisp assists: tears out a zombie's throat, causing its head to fall off backward.
- The ground floor is cleared of zombies: several servant zombies and one armored guard zombie (35 HP).
- The zombies are dressed as house servants. Spare servant uniforms found in cabinets confirm they worked here, were killed in some attack, and then animated as undead.
- Blue glowing switches on the wall turn out to be magical light switches -- an enchanted lighting system for the whole house. Kelda inspects them for traps, determines they are safe, and activates them. The entire house lights up, causing upstairs zombies to screech.

### Part 6: The Anotep Villa -- Second Floor

- The party rushes upstairs while buff spells are still active. Servant zombies are shuffling around but too stupid to find the stairs.
- More rapid combat: Lirienne kills zombies with precision crossbow shots, Kelda cleaves through skulls, Taelys uses the handaxe, Crisp tears enemies apart, and Tamsin's spiritual staff snaps necks.
- A zombie bites Lirienne during this floor's combat.
- The second floor is cleared. Lirienne tracks the scene:
  - In one room, several people tried to escape by diving out a window. One may have succeeded (blood splatter on bricks below, no body).
  - Large blood pools indicate where servants died and later rose as zombies.
  - A room north of the stairs has been completely ransacked -- every drawer ripped out and dumped, contents flung everywhere. Someone desperately searched this room at maximum speed.
  - A hallway shows evidence of an extended struggle. Multiple bodies hit the ground here and were later removed.
  - A child's room exists but is old; the uncle had no children. The house was bought fully furnished.

### Part 7: The Anotep Villa -- Attic

- Bloody barefoot footprints (an injured person sprinting with an awkward gait) lead from the second floor up a ladder to the attic.
- In the attic, the bloody footprints stumble to a stop. The body collapsed here, emitting significant blood. It was then turned over onto its back. Huge burn marks near the head and neck area indicate acid was poured across the face and into the mouth.
- A second set of footsteps followed the body and performed the acid burning.
- A third set of footsteps came later and removed the body entirely.
- Lirienne's assessment: the first person crawled up here dying (not undead -- the stumbling footsteps of someone in incredible pain). They collapsed near a wooden bust. Then someone else came and mutilated the face with acid. Then a third party removed the body.
- Among scattered junk in the attic, the party finds a wooden bust of Aroden (the dead god) with an articulated jaw. Tamsin identifies it (on a reroll from 7 to 19) as a soul anchor: anyone who dies within 50 feet of it has their soul stored in the bust and can be communicated with via speak with dead. Multiple souls can be stored; you must know the name of the person you wish to speak with. The bust has incredibly powerful preservation wards and is nearly indestructible.
- The uncle likely crawled dying to this bust specifically so his soul would be captured, allowing someone to later speak with him.
- Tamsin takes the bust (8 lbs). The party plans to bring it to Khep so she can help them speak with her uncle.

### Part 8: Loot and Session End

- Scattered throughout the ransacked attic and second floor, the party finds necromancy-related items: scrolls of bone shaker and other necromancy spells, a wand of inflict wounds, scrolls of speak with dead (useful for Tamsin), a scroll of fear, gold, a chest piece, a hair pin, cold iron, a preserved heart (old, likely a purchased artifact), dark bat remains, matutake mushrooms, a cheese elixir (snagged by Taelys), and red dye.
- Everything found in the house relates to necromancy. The uncle was deeply involved in the dark arts.
- The ransackers left behind valuable items (including the soul anchor bust and expensive scrolls), suggesting they were searching for something specific and left in a hurry.
- Kelda makes a fortitude save after being bitten by a zombie. The result is ambiguous -- the GM says "we'll talk about it later."
- The party levels up (all characters reach new level).
- The session ends mid-exploration. The ground floor still has one clutter pile to search, and the party has not yet opened some doors or explored the back yard graves further. Lou and Sarah were absent for the house exploration portion and will rejoin next session.
