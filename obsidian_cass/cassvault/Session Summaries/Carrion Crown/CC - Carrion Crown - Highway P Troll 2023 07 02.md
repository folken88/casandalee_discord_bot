## Summary

CP-USS travels north from Lepidstadt to investigate Schloss Caromarc, where they scout an enemy-occupied gatehouse and discover trolls and goblins living there. After setting an ambush with their stealthiest members, the party engages in a brutal combat against two trolls and numerous goblins. They successfully eliminate all enemies through coordinated tactics including fire damage to prevent troll regeneration.
