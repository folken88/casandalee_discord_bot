**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=i5SKndH2pak)

## Summary

CP-USS travels north from Lepidstadt to investigate Fort Caromarc (a troll-infested gatehouse). A reconnaissance team scouts the area and discovers multiple trolls and goblins occupying the fortress. The party sets an ambush on the road, successfully defeating two trolls and numerous goblins in combat. They capture two goblins for interrogation and discover that an air elemental guards the bridge to the mansion beyond.
