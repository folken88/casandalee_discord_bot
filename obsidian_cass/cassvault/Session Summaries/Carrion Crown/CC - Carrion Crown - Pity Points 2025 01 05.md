## Summary

CP-USS discovers an ancient, hidden temple beneath Feldgrau's granary, filled with shrines to Iomedae, Pharasma, Sarenrae, and Torag. The tombs contain legendary Shining Crusade champions, and the site provides powerful magical benefits including healing and equipment repair. After securing the location and resting, the party scouts Feldgrau to investigate Whispering Way activity, discovering evidence of heavy Undead movement. They witness a massive battle between Whispering Way forces and demon wolves, including a 25-foot fire giant, Paul bearers, and winged demon wolves. The cultists retreat to a manor house on the south side of town. The party considers negotiating with the demon wolves as a potential temporary ally against the greater threat.
