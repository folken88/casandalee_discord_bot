## Summary

The party travels toward Ascanor Lodge to apprehend Estovion. En route, they discover a brutally murdered human corpse nailed to a tree with wolfsbane stuffed in its mouth—killed by Ascanor Lodge hunters as part of a "game hunt" contract. After extensive debate, they spend 1,000 gold to resurrect the victim via Reincarnate, who comes back as a halfling ranger named Muffin Topslapper. Muffin provides minimal useful information before the party travels to Ascanor Lodge itself, arriving at mid-morning and being confined to a shabby cabin by the lodge staff while their business meeting request is processed.
