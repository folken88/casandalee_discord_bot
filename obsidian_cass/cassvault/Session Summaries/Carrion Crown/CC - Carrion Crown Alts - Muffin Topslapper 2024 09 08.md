## Summary

The party traveled toward Ascanor Lodge and discovered a murdered corpse nailed to a tree with wolfsbane stuffed in its mouth. After extensive debate, they cast reincarnate on the body, bringing back a halfling ranger named Miklos Stanic (renamed Muffin Topslapper by the group). They discovered the man had been hired into a hunting contract by Delross at Ascanor Lodge, then spent the remainder of the session arriving at Ascanor and being sequestered in a shabby cabin while the lodge staff (numerous individuals all named Brian) consulted with their superiors.
