## Summary

CP-USS battles the Whispering Way and their Soul Wraith minions in an elaborate mindscape combat on a collapsing ice platform. The party fights cultists, polymorphic enemies, and incorporeal spirits while Farah becomes briefly possessed by an ancient archdevil entity and unleashes devastating disintegrate spells. After a climactic grapple where Trey becomes dominated and nearly kills Farah, the party defeats the final Soul Wraith. Eston, the ancient werewolf captive, sacrifices himself with a final lightning spell, and Farah communes with his dying mind, learning he willingly chose death over imprisonment.
