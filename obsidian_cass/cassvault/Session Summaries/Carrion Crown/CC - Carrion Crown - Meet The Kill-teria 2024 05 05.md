**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=QbuFnnB7Q5k)

## Summary

CP-USS arrives at Ascanor Lodge and begins investigating suspicious activity. They discover the lodge master Eston has prior affiliations with the Whispering Way and recently hosted a secret meeting between cult members (including a necromancer named Auren Vrood) and werewolf leader Mathus Mordrinacht. Through exploration of Eston's private library, they find his journal revealing the Whispering Way stole the heart of a werewolf pack lord and that a mystical artifact called the Dusk Moth is incorporated into the Stairs of the Moon observatory. As they prepare to confront Eston about his involvement, he mysteriously vanishes from the lodge.
