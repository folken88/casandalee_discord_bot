## Summary

CP-USS investigates two estates (Thistle Farm and Evergreen Haven) in Ardeal, discovering them infested with fast skeletal undead created from murdered farmhands. After clearing the undead, they find a Deputy Marshal's badge and a lich-mark coin at Thistle Farm. At Evergreen Haven, Gaspar and Dismas receive a vision from a statue of Pharasma showing a young paladin captain of Northgarde being turned into a vampire, with the message that she will die in three days. The party gains a 5-day blessing and decides to prepare an assault on Northgarde, an island fortress now controlled by vampires.
