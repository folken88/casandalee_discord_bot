## Summary

CP-USS investigates two dangerous criminals (Vorkstag and Grine, the skinsuit murderers) who have been targeting Lepidstadt's judiciary. After discovering that one of them, disguised as law clerk Elias Varner, visited Judge Darymid's home address at the courthouse, the party sets up an elaborate trap at the judge's residence. When Vorkstag and Grine assault the house late at night, a intense combat ensues featuring Farah's phantom steed, Grandpa's devastating axes, and the party's combined efforts to defeat the two criminals and prevent their escape.
