# Carrion Crown - Person Sized People and muted GM 2024 04 21

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=yBICYY8GRYI)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Dismas

## Summary

CP-USS arrives at Ascanor Lodge in Shudderwood to investigate the cult while a pompous noble named Lord Duran Sylvio Arensa insists on joining them to hunt what they initially believe is a werewolf killing elk. The party discovers giant wolf prints indicating a Vilkacis (pack leader-sized werewolf) has been leaving territorial warnings, but instead encounters and defeats three Titan boars in combat. Lord Duran and his comedically dramatic henchmen survive the encounter despite being constantly knocked prone.

- CP-USS arrives at Ascanor Lodge and negotiates with lodge management. They find an invitation to the lodge from a missing guest, indicating cult involvement in disappearances.

- Lord Duran Sylvio Arensa, an arrogant noble with a high-tech rifle and three comedically theatrical henchmen (the Durans), insists on hunting the creature. The party tracks the scene and discovers a massive Vilkacis wolf print (18-20 feet at shoulder), but keeps this discovery from Duran.
  - Characters: Lord Duran Sylvio Arensa, Gaspar, Kai, Kate Blackwood

Denva uses her healing to keep the Durans alive.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Dismas, Lord Duran Sylvio Arensa

  - Characters: Lord Duran Sylvio Arensa
  - Location: Ascanor Lodge
