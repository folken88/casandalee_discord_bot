## Summary

CP-USS departs Lepidstadt as deputized Ustalav Marshalls with a federal warrant to hunt the Whispering Way. After shopping and preparing in the city, they ride south at night and encounter bandits near the Lesser Mree River. They kill most of the bandits, then learn from a survivor (Billy) that the Whispering Way passed through two months prior, heading toward the Shudderwood via the river. The party crosses into Shudderwood, camps east of the river, and discovers tent spike evidence of a recent campsite. They approach an ancient watchtower at the forest's edge and discover a web bridge spanning a 30-foot gap—likely constructed by a giant spider or similar creature.
