# Carrion Crown - EVERY CORPSEWAGON (2023-06-13)

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=NZezENKvJE8)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

## Summary

CP-USS explores the Chymic Works facility and discovers a horrifying basement operation: a chamber filled with ~30 corpses preserved by a Wand of Gentle Repose, and chemical zombies (including one named Costine) who were being kept alive via a mysterious alchemical fluid. After combat, they secure Costine as a witness and uncover extensive evidence of a body-trafficking operation: a cabinet of flayed skins and faces (including one matching the Beast), surgical tools, a ledger of recipients in Caliphas, and documents linking Vorkstag and Grime to the murders. They transport all evidence to the courthouse and morgue, identify one corpse as missing Sergeant Burns (who died of a Phantasmal Killer spell), and prepare for the Beast's trial with crucial exonerating evidence.

## Key Events

- CP-USS enters a flooded basement chamber containing ~30 corpses preserved in near-freezing water with a Wand of Gentle Repose. Multiple chemical zombies emerge and attack.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: Chymic Works - basement chamber
- Costine, a chemical zombie monk with superhuman strength and martial arts skills, surrenders to CP-USS after his companions are killed. He reveals he needs weekly doses of a special fluid to survive and that Vorkstag and Grime were his masters.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Costine
  - Location: Chymic Works - basement
- CP-USS discovers a cabinet containing flayed skins and faces of victims, including a large humanoid skin suit matching the Beast's description (with a scar from a blood caiman bite). Also find a giant hollowed-out Mongrel Man skin used as a disguise/suit.
  - Characters: Kai, Kovira, Gaspar
  - Location: Chymic Works - third floor bedroom
  - Timeline: 4717.01.31 | Lepidstadt | Vorkstag and Grime escape CP-USS at the Chymic Works.
- CP-USS recovers a ledger listing recipients of corpse shipments in Caliphas, surgical tools, alchemical materials (including Old Mage from Numeria), diamonds, and platinum. Evidence strongly suggests systematic body trafficking and murder.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kai, Kovira, Elfrip, Dinvaya
  - Location: Chymic Works - multiple rooms
- CP-USS finds the Mace of Hamdad (a plus-one Mace of Force with thunderstomp crit effect), a Mwangi bone mask granting plus-two intimidate and storm sight, and a mithril bar stamped 'Property of Hamdad' in the upper tower.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kai, Kovira, Elfrip, Dinvaya
  - Location: Chymic Works - upper tower
- Costine is transported to the courthouse basement cells and placed near the Beast. He agrees to cooperate (within limits) in exchange for the alchemical fluid he requires. CP-USS secures extra guards around both prisoners.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kai, Kovira, Costine
  - Location: Lepidstadt - Courthouse basement
- Morgue examination reveals that the murdered guard found in the Chymic Works basement (Sergeant Burns, who went missing two months ago) died from a Phantasmal Killer spell. Evidence confirms the Whispering Way's involvement.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kai
  - Location: Lepidstadt - Morgue
- CP-USS briefs the Beast's defense attorney on all new evidence: the skin cabinet, the alchemical operation, the ledgers proving body trafficking, and Costine's testimony that Vorkstag and Grime controlled the operation. They believe they can clear the Beast of charges from Morasst and Sanctuary.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kai, Kovira
  - Location: Lepidstadt - Courthouse
