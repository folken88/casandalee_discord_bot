## Summary

The party investigates a series of murders in Lepidstadt connected to the escaped criminals Vorkstag and Grine. After interviewing a severed head via speak with dead and gathering information from city guards, they learn that Vorkstag is wearing the skin of Corporal Koval. The investigation leads them to the Dog Watch bar where they conduct reconnaissance, then to the Southeastern Precinct barracks where they obtain Koval's personal effects. Late in the evening, they use detect thoughts on a drunk guard to obtain a mental image of the skin suit, though this escalates into a chaotic bar brawl as the party attempts to extract.
