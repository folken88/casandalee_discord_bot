# Carrion Crown - Stone Cold Killers (2023-07-30)

## Summary

CP-USS explores a series of flooded chambers within a castle complex. They encounter and defeat four Vaguels (flying head fiends), navigate past a pit of giant leeches, clear out a room infested with Micelloids (fungus creatures that spread disease), discover a hidden cache of potions and supplies, and uncover a hidden wall concealing petrified ghouls infected with ghoul fever. They rest and prepare for what appears to be a Basilisk encounter in another sealed chamber.

## Key Events

- Party encounters four Vaguels (flying, winged undead heads) in a sealed chamber guarded by a demonic barrier. They eliminate all four creatures through ranged attacks without breaking the barrier seal.
  - Characters: Kovira, Kai, Dismas, Gaspar, Elfrip, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith
  - Location: Castle - Vaguel Chamber (with water/leech pit)
- Party discovers a room infested with Micelloids (fungus creatures). Combat ensues and multiple party members contract Purple Pox disease (including Kovira, Kai, and Dinvaya). Party defeats all creatures through strategic use of electricity-based attacks.
  - Characters: Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Gaspar, Dismas
  - Location: Castle - Fungal Chamber
- Party accesses a secret potion room (10x10 alcove) containing remove disease potions, invisibility potions, non-detection potions, displacement potions, charm monster wand, and levitate wand. They collect supplies strategically.
  - Characters: Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Gaspar, Dismas
  - Location: Castle - Potion Alcove/Supply Room
- While discovering the potion room, party detects a stone-shaped wall. Upon investigation (with Gaspar's crit hit), the wall collapses revealing 8-9 petrified ghouls compressed inside, infected with ghoul fever. Party engages in brief combat, quickly dispatching the ghouls with channel energy and ranged attacks.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: Castle - Hidden Ghoul Chamber
- Party discovers a fourth sealed silo chamber. Through perception checks (Kovira 30, Dinvaya 33), they identify several petrified corpses in mid-combat poses, with a stone-eating creature (likely Basilisk based on knowledge checks). Visible is a giant blue and gold bastard sword partially submerged in the water, clutched by severed stone arms.
  - Characters: Kovira, Dinvaya, Kai
  - Location: Castle - Fourth Silo Chamber (sealed, with petrified victims)
- Three party members (Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya) contract Purple Pox disease from Micelloid encounters. They receive remove disease potions and will need to make Fort saves over multiple days to overcome it, or risk death by explosive gestation.
  - Characters: Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya
  - Location: Castle - Multiple chambers
- Party rests overnight in secured hallway between the fungal chamber and ghoul chamber. Two watches stand (first watch: unknown, second watch: Kai/others). No disturbances occur. Party members infected with Purple Pox make disease saves (all fail on first night, some succeed on second attempt).
  - Characters: Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya, Elfrip, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Gaspar, Dismas
  - Location: Castle - Hallway between chambers
- Through Arcane in the Membrane consultation, party determines the threat in the sealed fourth silo is likely a Basilisk (not a Medusa), identifiable by the nature of the stone-eating damage and corpse mutilation patterns. *(medium confidence)*
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kate Blackwood
  - Location: Castle - Preparation area
