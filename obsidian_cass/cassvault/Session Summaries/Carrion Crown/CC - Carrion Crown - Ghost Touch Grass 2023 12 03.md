## Summary

CP-USS travels across Numeria via Meyanda's truck, encountering a border guard post at Dravod Knock run by mutated locals with glowing eyes. After a tense but peaceful negotiation (and an impromptu gun demonstration), they proceed west toward Ustalav. The party camps in old Numerian ruins, where they are attacked at night by five Shadow Souls—incorporeal demonic entities that deal cold and negative energy damage. Using spells, magic missiles, and ghost-touch arrows, the party defeats all enemies. The next morning, Meyanda drops them across the river in her amphibious truck and departs for home. The party discovers the broken blade shard points in two directions and prepares to explore the old Dwarven ruins in the nearby mountains.
