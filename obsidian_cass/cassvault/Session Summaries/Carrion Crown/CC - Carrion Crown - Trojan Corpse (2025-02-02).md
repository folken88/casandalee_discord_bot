# Carrion Crown - Trojan Corpse (2025-02-02)

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=XURfRAAaUjI)

## Summary

CP-USS executed an ambush against Feldgrau's Whispering Way cult operations. The team attempted to bait a fire giant with an elaborate trap corpse, but the operation deteriorated when they encountered multiple high-level undead threats including a log boy, flaming skeleton, morg, and ultimately the fire giant itself. Elfrip was killed by enemy gunslingers but rescued by Sirona (a Deva ally). The session ended mid-combat with demon wolves and allies arriving to reinforce the party against overwhelming cult forces.
