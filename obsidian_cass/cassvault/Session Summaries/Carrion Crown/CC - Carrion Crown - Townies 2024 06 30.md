## Summary

CP-USS arrives in Chastel after being led by friendly Broken Ones werewolves to a battle site where the Whispering Way cult fought the Gaelon demon wolves. The party investigates the scene, finding destroyed undead and signs of a major engagement, then travels to Chastel town where they receive a warm welcome and spend the evening at the Broker tavern. The next day is spent shopping, selling loot from Shudderwood, and purchasing supplies and magical items with funds from Kate's parents.

- Broken Ones werewolves guide CP-USS to a remote cliff-side pond where the Whispering Way and Gaelon forces clashed.   - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas, Kate Blackwood

Kate's parents provide an additional 12,000 gold for the cause. Party purchases the 'Stroke Pit' tent (11,680 gold after discount) and various magical items and equipment upgrades.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas, Kate Blackwood
