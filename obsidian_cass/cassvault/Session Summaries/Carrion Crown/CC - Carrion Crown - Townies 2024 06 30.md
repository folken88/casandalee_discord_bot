## Summary

CP-USS arrives in Chastel after investigating a battle site where werewolves fought cultists and undead. They discover a hidden skeleton of an animated undead at the bottom of an icy pool, evidence of a fierce battle between the Whispering Way and local werewolf clans. The party is warmly received in Chastel, where Kate Blackwood is recognized as nobility, and they spend the evening celebrating at the local tavern before shopping and acquiring new equipment with newly acquired funds.
