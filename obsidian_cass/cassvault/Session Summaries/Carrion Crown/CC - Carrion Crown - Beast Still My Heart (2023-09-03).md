# Carrion Crown - Beast Still My Heart (2023-09-03)

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=3SepYz2735w)
**Transcript Quality:** poor
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

## Summary

CP-USS confronts the Promethean atop Schloss Caromarc after scouting it with Elfrip. A massive battle ensues between the party, the Beast, and the Promethean creature. The Beast, severely injured, sacrifices itself by pushing the Promethean off the 300-foot tower to save the party. The party recovers the Beast's brain from an armored container in its chest cavity and returns to Lepidstadt with plans to create a new body for it.

## Key Events

- Elfrip scouts the top of Schloss Caromarc while invisible and flying, discovering the Promethean—a massive six-legged creature with tentacles and elephant trunk-like appendages—sleeping on top of the tower next to three floating black stones with blue crystals and a lightning-powered machine.
  - Characters: Elfrip, Kate (receiving report via detect thoughts), Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya, Rodney
  - Location: Schloss Caromarc (top of tower)
  - Timeline: 4717.04.27 (same day as Keeper Grove battle)
- Party learns from Caromarc that the Promethean has troll eyes and griffin ears for exceptional perception. Party prepares to levitate up the side of the tower with the Beast carrying them while a silence spell prevents the creature from hearing them.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate, Rodney, The Beast
  - Location: Schloss Caromarc (bridge level)
- Combat begins as the party reaches the top of the tower. Kovira is paralyzed by the Promethean's stinger tentacles and lifted toward its mouth. Gaspar uses neutralize poison to free Kai from paralysis.
  - Characters: Promethean, Kovira, Kai, Gaspar, Dinvaya, Kate, Elfrip, Rodney
  - Location: Schloss Caromarc (tower top)
- The Promethean electrocutes both the Beast and itself using the tower's machinery. The Beast absorbs the electricity and regenerates. Both creatures end up heavily damaged.
  - Characters: Promethean, The Beast
  - Location: Schloss Caromarc (tower top)
- The Beast decides to sacrifice itself to eliminate the Promethean threat. It grabs the Promethean and pushes both of them off the 300-foot tower. The party pulls on ropes and uses levitate and feather fall spells to prevent the Beast from falling, but the Promethean falls to its destruction, taking over 100 damage on impact and dying.
  - Characters: The Beast, Promethean, Gaspar, Kate Blackwood, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Rodney
  - Location: Schloss Caromarc (tower exterior)
- Party extracts the Beast's brain—a preserved organic matter in a triple-layered steel armored container—from the Beast's chest cavity. The brain is alive but the Beast's body is dead.
  - Characters: Dinvaya, Gaspar, Josh (examining), The party
  - Location: Schloss Caromarc (tower top)
- Party interrogates Caromarc about the Promethean and discovers he created both it and the Beast. The Beast's brain is exceptional and adapted in ways other brains could not—this is why it was the only one that worked in the Beast's body.
  - Characters: Caromarc, Elfrip, Gaspar, Dinvaya, Party
  - Location: Schloss Caromarc (lower floor)
- Party discovers Caromarc's wooden Golem—a female form carved to resemble his deceased wife, Cerys. Caromarc created this Golem for unknown reasons, possibly to attempt resurrection of his wife's consciousness.
  - Characters: Caromarc, Party, Elfrip
  - Location: Schloss Caromarc
- Party returns to Lepidstadt with Caromarc as a prisoner, the Beast's brain in a container, Golem parts, and Cerys's body in a hearse. They brief Judge Darymid on events.
  - Characters: Party, Caromarc, Judge Embreth Darymid, Kendra
  - Location: Lepidstadt
- Judge Darymid reveals that Caromarc technically cannot be charged with crimes—he is a noble whose estate is independent from Lepidstadt's governance per his deposition agreement. She cannot hold him legally but allows short-term custody pending investigation.
  - Characters: Judge Embreth Darymid, Gaspar, Party
  - Location: Lepidstadt (courthouse)
- Party discusses plans to create a new body for the Beast's brain, either by teleporting to Torch, Numeria (a major magical crafting center) or via long overland journey. Judge Darymid grants the party several thousand gold credit and one month of downtime to prepare.
  - Characters: Party, Judge Darymid, Dinvaya
  - Location: Lepidstadt
- Judge Darymid states she will investigate the Whispering Way's activities and movements across Ustalav to prepare for the party's next operation against the cult.
  - Characters: Judge Embreth Darymid, Party
  - Location: Lepidstadt
- Party reaches level 7.
  - Characters: All PCs
