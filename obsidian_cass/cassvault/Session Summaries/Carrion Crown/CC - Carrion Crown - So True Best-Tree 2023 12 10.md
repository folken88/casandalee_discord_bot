**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=vxYI4BKapwI)

## Summary

The party travels into the mountains of Shudderwood following the pull of a broken sword hilt. They discover a hidden dwarven fortress currently inhabited by a disciplined orc society worshipping Aori, led by the half-orc Kren Verir. After some tense initial moments, they negotiate peacefully and are given hospitality. While exploring the fortress, they recover another shard of the broken blade from a hidden compartment, upgrading the weapon significantly. The session ends as they prepare to take a boat downriver to Fort Austra to clear it of undead.

## Key Events

- The party spots movement in the forest near the mountains and pursues it, discovering a hidden dwarven fortress with large metal doors set into a mountainside.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kai, Elfrip, Kovira, Dismas
  - Location: Mountains of Shudderwood, hidden fortress entrance
- Kren Verir, a half-orc woman in full plate, opens the fortress doors and welcomes the party peacefully after they display a sigil of Aori. She introduces herself as master of Lur-Subun.
  - Characters: Kren Verir, Gaspar, Kovira
  - Location: Lur-Subun fortress
- The party discovers a broken sword shard hidden in a magically-concealed compartment within an ancient stone chest filled with iron bars. The shard is retrieved and magnetically attaches to the broken hilt.
  - Characters: Kovira, Gaspar, Kai
  - Location: Lur-Subun storage room
- The broken blade upgrades from a dagger (1d4) to a shortsword (1d6) upon recovery of the shard. It is now a +2 weapon with additional enchantments.
  - Characters: Kovira
  - Location: Lur-Subun storage room
- The party has dinner with Kren Verir, Bam (an elderly orc cleric/wizard), Tram (a half-orc quartermaster and trader), and Vakra (a very fit warrior). They learn the fortress has been inhabited by disciplined orc warriors for centuries, reformed by a legendary warrior named Lon.
  - Characters: Kren Verir, Bam, Tram, Vakra, Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dismas
  - Location: Lur-Subun dining hall
- The party agrees to clear Fort Austra (a nearby island fortress) of undead to make it safe for the orcs to travel past. In exchange, they receive a boat with sail and supplies, plus a 20% discount on purchases from Tram's forge.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dismas, Kren Verir, Bam, Tram
  - Location: Lur-Subun fortress
- Bam reveals the sword shard was likely hidden by servants of the Whispering Tyrant in an attempt to prevent it from being found by the living, using stone shape magic to conceal it inside the box centuries ago.
  - Characters: Bam, Gaspar
  - Location: Lur-Subun fortress
  - Timeline: relates to Whispering Way investigation timeline
