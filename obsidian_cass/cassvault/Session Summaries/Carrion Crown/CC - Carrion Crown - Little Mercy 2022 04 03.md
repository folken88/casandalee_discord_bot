# Carrion Crown - Little Mercy 2022 04 03

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=7AAR87Qoa5k)

## Summary

CP-USS is sworn in at their new precinct (Precinct 13) and immediately respond to two murders in the city. Both victims show signs of being killed by the same massive, armored figure with glowing red eyes and a horned helmet. The party tracks the killer through the city to the Succubus brothel, where Detective Marsh was murdered and partially dismembered. Following a trail into the sewers, they discover the thieves guild hideout has been massacred—15-20 guild members slaughtered, many subsequently animated as undead cannibals. The party defeats the undead and finds evidence suggesting Detective Marsh was connected to the guild. Mysteriously, they discover both large armored footprints and small halfling-sized footprints at the scene, suggesting multiple attackers or an unknown accomplice.
