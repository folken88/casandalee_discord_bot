## Summary

CP-USS investigates the Chemic Works in Lepidstadt, discovering a facility producing mutated creatures and alchemical concoctions. After defeating a stitched-dog abomination and several mongrel men, they pursue two invisible rogues (Vorkstag and Grine) through chemical vats and into an underground laboratory, where the rogues' superior stealth and invisibility allow them to escape despite the party's numerical advantage.
