## Summary

The party investigates two related murders in Lepidstadt: the brutal killing of an alchemist and his household (Ronaldol), and the discovery of a skinned city guard (Corporal Koval) at the morgue. Evidence points to Vorkstag and Grine, who are now operating in the southeast quarter of the city. The party conducts interviews, brain-bakes a guard to identify the skin-suit, and ends with a chaotic bar brawl at the Dog Watch.
