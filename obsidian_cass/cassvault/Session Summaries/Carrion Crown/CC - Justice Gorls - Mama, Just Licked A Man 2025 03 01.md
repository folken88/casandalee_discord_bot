# Justice Gorls - Mama, Just Licked A Man 2025 03 01

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown (Justice Gorls) | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Cvg4GTDusao)

## Summary
The Justice Gorls deal with the aftermath of their previous interrogation and murder of a Clickboom Gang member in The Rusted Drake's stable, spending considerable time disposing of the body. They use Prestidigitation to clean evidence, have Crisp chew the head off to prevent Speak With Dead, dismember the corpse, and distribute body parts among their backpacks. After shopping for supplies at Cheatum and Steel, they steal a boat and row into Caliphas Bay to dump body parts, then locate the Clickboom Gang's secret underground hideout near the Devil's Cut. After sneaking up on and interrogating a guard (Lou breaks his leg and licks his face to intimidate him), they learn the hideout entrance mechanism and descend into the cave. Combat erupts when Taelys trips over a lantern, and the party fights Clickboom sorcerers who use sleep and grease spells. The party defeats the gang, loots their hideout extensively, and claims the underground cave with its dock, boats, and river access as their new base of operations.

## Key Events
- The party uses Prestidigitation to clean blood evidence from the stable; Knowledge Arcana (17) reveals Speak With Dead requires a head and mouth, so they decide to remove the head
- Crisp chews through the dead gang member's neck over 20 gruesome minutes to sever the head; the party loots studded leather armor, a composite bow, a club ("the clerb"), and 23 gold pieces from the corpse
- The party shops at Cheatum and Steel, buying backpacks, flint and steel, and alchemist fire; they dismember the body and distribute parts among backpacks for transport
- They steal a dinghy from near The Rusted Drake, row into Caliphas Bay, and attempt to dump body parts (some float and must be weighted down)
- Using survival checks, they locate the Clickboom Gang's hideout near the Devil's Cut -- an underground cave system accessible from old ruins near the lakeshore
- Taelys and Kelda sneak up on a lone Clickboom guard; Lou breaks his leg with a hook hammer and lick-intimidates him into revealing the entrance mechanism (a switch -- up opens the door, down sounds an alarm; he initially lies about which is which but Kelda catches the deception with disable device)
- The party descends into the Clickboom hideout; Taelys trips over a lantern alerting the gang, triggering combat
- Combat features Clickboom sorcerers casting sleep spells and grease spells; one burns Kelda and Taelys awake with burning hands, breaking their own crowd control; Taelys shoots one through the chest with her rifle, killing him instantly; Lirienne sinks a crossbow bolt through a chantress's eye
- Lou charges a chantress with her hook hammer; Antoinette charges through the dungeon and drives her sword through a gang member; Kelda finishes the last fighter with a sneak attack
- One chantress charms Antoinette with a befriend spell, causing party confusion, but is eventually killed by Lirienne
- The party extensively loots the hideout, finding: Duristan paintings, a Caliphas officer's cloak (Cloak of Resistance +1), Caliphas Sergeant's plate armor, a Lioness plate (gnome/halfling sized), a thief coat, scrolls, wands, pipe bombs, and stolen goods
- A note found in the hideout reveals the gang already sold some stolen items and couldn't find buyers for the Duristan paintings; they suggest sinking unsold items or selling them back to the owner
- The party claims the Clickboom hideout as their new base, with its underground location, dock access to Caliphas Bay, kitchen with food supplies, and sleeping quarters
