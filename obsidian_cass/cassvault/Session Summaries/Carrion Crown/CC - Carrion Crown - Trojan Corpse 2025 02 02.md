## Summary

CP-USS executes a coordinated attack on Feldgrau after learning the Whispering Way is constructing the Carrion Crown from skull cylinders drilled from a thousand corpses. The party splits: one team creates an elaborate trap using a decoy corpse laden with pipe bombs to ambush a fire giant, while another team prepares to harass the cult's eastern forces. The trap is sprung but triggers a devastating response—multiple high-level Whispering Way operatives including Auren Vrood emerge, killing Elfrip in a devastating sniper attack. Saranrae's Deva form manifests to save him, and demon wolf allies arrive to turn the tide of battle.
