# Justice Gorls - The Queen of Skanktown 2025 04 26

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown (Justice Gorls) | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=t214hHoxUA0)

## Summary
The broker Slass Venton ("Sassy") brings a lucrative rescue contract to the Justice Gorls at their newly claimed Clickboom hideout, introducing Agu as his field representative. The contract from Quarterfo Archives offers 2,000 gold for Professor Martin alive, 2,000 gold for recovered artifacts, and 10 gold per grad student. After managing encumbrance and supplies, the party travels half a day to archaeological Site 17, where they find abandoned snow-covered tents, a murdered grad student, and Professor Martin's journal documenting the excavation of a Shining Crusade burial site linked to a previously unknown knightly order. Following signs of violent kidnapping, the party tracks the captives to Skanktown -- an outlaw camp of 20+ bandits called the Skanktown Killers. Through a surprise assault, the Justice Gorls massacre the camp leadership, with Agu decapitating the gang boss Breg in her first combat. They recover Professor Martin and three surviving research assistants alive.

## Key Events
- Slass Venton ("Sassy"), a halfling crime broker who takes 10%, arrives at the Clickboom hideout with a rescue contract and introduces Agu (Lydia's character) as his representative who will accompany the party
- The rescue contract from Quarterfo Archives (research arm of Cordo, the largest university in Caliphas) offers: 2,000 gold for Professor Martin alive, 2,000 gold for all artifacts from the dig site, 200 gold for proof of death, and 10 gold per grad student (dead or alive, roughly a dozen)
- Sassy offers to help sell Duristan-related items and arrange buyback of Lady Califaso's stolen paintings at a markup; Kelda introduces herself as connected to Iron Glim Smelting
- The party manages encumbrance at base, stashing excess loot (Duristan paintings, oak logs, extra pipe bombs) in the hideout storage; an unidentified scroll turns out to be a Scroll of Ant Haul
- The party travels northwest through Caliphas and half a day on foot to Site 17, navigating with Lirienne's survival (28 total with Kelda's aid)
- At Site 17's camp, they find 8 snow-covered tents (15 people camped here), abandoned for at least 2 days, with signs of people being violently dragged away; one grad student's body is found at the dig site entrance with feet burned off at the ankles and multiple stab wounds
- The party reads Professor Martin's journal, which documents excavating a Shining Crusade burial site containing an unknown knightly brotherhood called the "Order of the Shard" -- warriors who carried the memory of General Arnison's final stand against Tar-Baphon; the journal describes a hidden chamber they planned to return to open
- A manifest lists 20 artifacts across three shipments including ceremonial items, armor, weapons, and relics from the crusader era
- The party opens the hidden chamber behind an offering shelf (Kelda finds the seam with perception 25, Taelys helps with disable device), discovering a repeating crossbow called "Light of the Dawn" and a gnomish hook hammer; Lirienne claims the crossbow as an upgrade
- Following drag marks through the snow, the party tracks the kidnapped researchers to Skanktown -- a bandit camp of the Skanktown Killers, a coalition of 2-3 criminal gangs
- Antoinette uses Igorian diplomacy (intimidate that clouds memory) to interrogate a Skanktown sentry at knifepoint; they learn the Skanktown Killers have Professor Martin and a few grad students, while another gang called the Craig Sons (people traffickers from east Caliphas) took most of the others
- The party launches a surprise attack on Skanktown at 4 AM: Lou charges and hammer-smashes a guard's skull on a picnic table; Antoinette uses spellstrike (shocking grasp through her blade) to decapitate another; Agu sprints into the boss's tent and decapitates Breg in his sleeping bag on her first combat turn; Taelys shoots a sleeping bandit through the head with her crossbow
- Breg's second-in-command Krissa ("Red Cap") orders the remaining Skanktown Killers to stand down, declaring Agu the new leader: the queen of Skanktown
- Professor Martin and three surviving research assistants are recovered alive from a guarded tent
