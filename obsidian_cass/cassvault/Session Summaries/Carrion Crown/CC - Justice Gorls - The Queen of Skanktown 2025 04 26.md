## Summary

The broker Slass Venton (known as "Sassy") brings the Justice Gorls a lucrative rescue contract from Quarterfo Archives: recover Professor Martin alive for 2,000 gold, recover stolen artifacts for another 2,000 gold, or bring back proof of death for 200 gold. Grad students are worth 10 gold each. Sassy leads a new party member, Lydia, to the former Clickboom Gang hideout where the Justice Gorls have set up base. The Clickboom Gang has been wiped out and their bodies thrown in the river. Agu, who works with Sassy, joins the party for the mission. The party also learns about stolen nudes of Lady Califaso and deals in Duristan-related goods.

## Key Events

- Slass Venton ("Sassy"), a small-time crime broker who takes 10%, arrives at the Clickboom Gang hideout (now the party's base) with a rescue contract.
- The contract from Quarterfo Archives offers: 2,000 gold for Professor Martin alive, 2,000 gold for recovery of all artifacts from the dig site, 200 gold for proof of death, and 10 gold per grad student (dead or alive, roughly a dozen).
- Lydia is introduced as a new character who has her own contract and complications -- the person she needs to recover is reportedly missing from an archaeological dig site, possibly kidnapped.
- Agu joins the party as Sassy's representative to ensure the mission is completed.
- The Clickboom Gang hideout is described as an underground cave with a dock, pier, rowboat, and access to the main river -- a base the Justice Gorls took over after the gang was wiped out.
- Sassy offers to help sell Duristan-related items and arrange to buy back stolen paintings of Lady Califaso at a markup.
- Kella Iron Glenn introduces herself as connected to Iron Glenn Smelting (possibly a prominent family).
- Quarterfo Archives is identified as the primary research arm of Cordo, the largest university in the city.
