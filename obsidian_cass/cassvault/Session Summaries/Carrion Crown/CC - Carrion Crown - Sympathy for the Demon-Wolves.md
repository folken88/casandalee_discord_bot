# Carrion Crown - Sympathy for the Demon-Wolves

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=47f8PTJ1QCI)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Dismas

## Summary

CP-USS negotiates a tentative alliance with Adamaris and the demon-wolf clan after witnessing them flee from the Whispering Way. The party learns the cult has taken the pack lord's heart and established a major stronghold in Feldgrau's southwestern mansion. They're directed to investigate a captured cultist at Sarath Bastion who is slowly transforming into a werewolf and going insane from magical safeguards in his mind. The session ends as the party subdues the cultist for interrogation.

## Key Events

- CP-USS discovers the Whispering Way has established a major base in a southwestern mansion in Feldgrau with hundreds of corpses, multiple necromancers (8-10), and significant undead constructs. The cultists have taken the pack lord's heart and moved it south.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith
  - Location: Feldgrau
  - Timeline: 4717.04.19 - Feldgrau outer guard detected; 4717.04.20 - Battle with WW creations
- Kate Blackwood successfully negotiates with Adamaris, the leader of the demon-wolf clan, establishing a wary alliance against the Whispering Way. Adamaris agrees to help fight the cult in exchange for avenging his fallen pack members.
  - Characters: Kate Blackwood, Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya
  - Location: Standing Stones near Feldgrau
- The demon-wolf clan gives CP-USS a captured cultist imprisoned at Sarath Bastion (a ruined fortress southeast of Feldgrau). The cultist was bitten by a demon-wolf and is slowly transforming, with the regenerative factor keeping him alive but his mind being destroyed by both the transformation and the cult's magical safeguards.
  - Characters: Adamaris, Kate Blackwood, Gaspar
  - Location: Sarath Bastion / Standing Stones
- CP-USS travels to Sarath Bastion with one of Adamaris's warriors and discovers the cultist in the ruins. They successfully subdue him through coordinated grappling (Dismas and Gaspar) while preparing magical restraints.
  - Characters: Dismas, Gaspar, Elfrip, Kovira, Kai, Kate Blackwood, Dinvaya
  - Location: Sarath Bastion
- The party learns that a full moon is imminent (approximately 2-3 days away), which will cause the cultist's werewolf transformation to accelerate and fully take hold if not addressed.
  - Characters: Dinvaya
  - Location: Sarath Bastion
