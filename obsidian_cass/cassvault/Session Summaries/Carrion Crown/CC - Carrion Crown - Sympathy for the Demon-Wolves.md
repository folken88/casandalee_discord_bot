## Summary

CP-USS negotiates with the Demon Wolf clan (Adamaris and ~12 remaining members) to form an alliance against the Whispering Way after a three-way battle in Feldgrau. The party learns the cult has taken a captured cultist to Saravath Bastion fortress southeast of the city, where he is slowly transforming into a werewolf and deteriorating mentally from cult safeguards.
