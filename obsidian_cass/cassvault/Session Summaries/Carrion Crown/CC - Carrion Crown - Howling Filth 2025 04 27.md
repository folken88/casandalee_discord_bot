## Summary

CP-USS arrives in Carrion Hill to investigate disappearances and house demolitions, discovering the town plagued by chaos spawn (aberrations) bursting from civilians' bodies. After recruiting the bard Elodie, the party stakes out the town at night and is ambushed by multiple invisible tentacled creatures and a large wendigo-like aberration that fears the entire party. Through coordinated combat involving fireballs, gunfire, and melee attacks, the party eliminates the creatures, though several members take constitution damage from the creatures' blood-draining attacks.
