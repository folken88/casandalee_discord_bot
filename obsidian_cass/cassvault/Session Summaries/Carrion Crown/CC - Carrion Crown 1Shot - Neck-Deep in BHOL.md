# Carrion Crown 1Shot - Neck-Deep in BHOL

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LIl6GzwB2yQ)
**Transcript Quality:** poor
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas

## Summary

A criminal organization called the Black and Operative League (BHOL) is hired to assassinate Antonette Bordon, a wealthy noblewoman and secretly a vampire, for 110,000 gold. The team conducts reconnaissance including bar interrogations, police record theft, and winery surveillance. They discover Antonette is guarded by enhanced aegis guards and that a previous police captain who investigated her disappeared under mysterious circumstances. Late in the session, they engage in combat at Bordon warehouses against armed thugs and encounter a vampire enemy. Transcript quality was poor with frequent audio/technical issues.

## Key Events

- BHOL receives a contract from Moss (their handler) to assassinate Antonette Bordon for 110,000 gold within 3 days, with proof of death required.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: BHOL Headquarters (Bog Root Wine Cellar)
- Prime (Dismas) conducts aerial reconnaissance of Blood Kiss Winery, observing halfling slaves in the vineyard, aegis guards conducting property inspections, preparations for a banquet, and no regular security presence.
  - Characters: Dismas
  - Location: Blood Kiss Winery (east of Calus)
- Switch and other team members gather intelligence at the Hounds Tooth bar. They learn the Bordons own multiple properties including warehouses at the docks, slum holdings, a manor house in the rich district, and Blood Kiss Winery. They discover a 'quarterly inspection' closure is happening this week with extra security arriving.
  - Characters: Kovira, Kai
  - Location: Hounds Tooth (cop bar in Calus)
- Switch infiltrates Precinct 8 (Castle Streth) disguised as an Iomedaean Paladin using Linguistics and Bluff checks. She recovers police records of an incident involving former Captain Benedict, learning that a raid on Bordon operations resulted in the captain's death and all participating officers being transferred, retired, or terminated.
  - Characters: Kovira
  - Location: Precinct 8 / Castle Streth
- The team locates and infiltrates the apartment of Harlon Jeck, the former observer from the failed raid. They find two constables (William Thomas and Toby Marman) and a dishwasher named Bram Urick. In violent combat, they subdue or kill the constables, and Rev uses modify memory to implant false memories in Bram, making him believe he murdered his companions.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: Harlon Jeck's apartment (4 blocks from Precinct 8)
- Bram Urick reveals under interrogation that Antonette Bordon personally murdered Captain Benedict by tearing out his throat with her teeth during a nighttime confrontation at a carriage stop. He describes her as a thin blonde woman with purple glowing eyes and aegis guards with glowing red eyes jogging alongside her carriage.
  - Characters: Dismas
  - Location: Harlon Jeck's apartment
- The Mark identifies Antonette Bordon as a vampire based on Bram's description of her appearance and abilities.
  - Characters: Gaspar
  - Location: BHOL Headquarters
- Late evening combat erupts at Bordon warehouses. The team (including Dismas as a giant spider) is ambushed by an aegis hexer who casts an ice storm cone attack on the party. A vampire enemy emerges from magical fog and engages the spider in melee combat.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Kate Blackwood, Rodney Danger Smith, Dismas
  - Location: Bordon warehouses (dock district)
