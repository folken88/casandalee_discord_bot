# Carrion Crown - EVERY CORPSEWAGON 2023 06 13

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=NZezENKvJE8)

**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya, Dismas, Rodney, Kate, Elfrip

## Summary

CP-USS investigates the Chymic Works, a chemical facility where they discover a basement full of preserved corpses and encounter four ripped chemical zombies (including one named Costine who surrenders). They find extensive evidence of corpse trafficking, including a ledger, multiple preserved human skins in a cabinet, and weapons. The party arrests Costine, secures the evidence, and prepares for the Beast's trial by establishing that vorkstag and grind were framing the Beast using a giant skin suit. Transcript quality was poor with frequent audio errors and speaker identification issues.

## Key Events

- CP-USS descends into the basement of the Chymic Works and encounters four heavily muscled chemical zombies (corpses enhanced via chemical process rather than necromancy). Combat ensues with creatures displaying supernatural strength and martial arts mastery.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya, Dismas, Rodney, Kate
  - Location: Chymic Works basement
- One chemical zombie named Costine surrenders during combat after most of his companions are killed. He is shackled and interrogated.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya, Dismas, Costine
  - Location: Chymic Works basement
- Party discovers a frozen basement chamber containing approximately 30 corpses in cold water, all preserved with wands of gentle repose. Costine confirms these are bodies being prepared for shipment to wealthy clients in Caliphas.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya
  - Location: Chymic Works basement (frozen chamber)
- Party finds corpse of city guard Sergeant Burns with set of Precinct 13 keys hidden inside his body. Burns had gone missing two months prior after responding to the Black Whale prison incident.
  - Characters: Kai, Kovira
  - Location: Chymic Works frozen basement
  - Timeline: Related to 4716.11.28 Black Whale prison riot
- In upper floors of Chymic Works, party discovers cabinet containing multiple flayed human skins and one large aberrant skin suit matching descriptions of the creature seen in Morast. The creature had a crab-like claw arm and multiple eyes.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai
  - Location: Chymic Works upper floors
- Party recovers Hamdad's Mace (plus-one mace of force with thunder slam critical effect), a magical bone mask from Mwangi Expanse, a mithril bar, and multiple valuable alchemical items and drugs including Old Mage (Numerian drug).
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Dinvaya, Dismas, Rodney
  - Location: Chymic Works upper tower
- Party recovers ledgers documenting vorkstag and grind's corpse trafficking business with wealthy clients in Caliphas. Also finds evidence linking the Chymic Works to Sanctuary (severed heads recovered earlier matched names in ledgers).
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai
  - Location: Chymic Works upper floors
- Costine is transported to courthouse basement and held in a cell near the Beast. He is given fluid stipend required to sustain his chemical zombie existence in exchange for cooperation as a witness.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Costine
  - Location: Lepidstadt Courthouse basement
- Party presents evidence to defense attorney establishing that vorkstag and grind were framing the Beast using a skin suit disguise. Evidence includes the suit itself, the cabinet of skins, ledgers, and Costine's testimony about the operation.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kovira, Kai
  - Location: Lepidstadt Courthouse
- Through investigation of missing persons records, Kai identifies that at least one skin in the cabinet (Olga Slovak) belonged to a previously missing person who briefly returned to cancel her alert before disappearing again.
  - Characters: Kai
  - Location: Lepidstadt Courthouse records
