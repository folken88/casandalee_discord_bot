## Summary

CP-USS travels to the Battery east of Chastel to investigate a disturbance reported by Arno Blackwood. They encounter and defeat a wave of undead werewolf-ghoul hybrids that have been infected with a virulent combination of ghoul fever and lycanthropy, apparently created by the Whispering Way or demon wolves. The party discovers the creatures came from Willowmere across the river in Ardeal, suggesting the outbreak threatens the entire region. After looting masterwork equipment, they prepare to travel to Ardeal on magical horses to contain the undead plague.
