## Summary

CP-USS explored a mysterious compound featuring laboratories and preserved specimens. They encountered and defeated zombie warriors in a flooded hallway, then discovered a massive ancient tree—a six-eyed horror (abhoth-like creature) sealed in a silo chamber. After one psychic blast attack caused confusion among party members and threatened a total party kill, they wisely shut and locked the reinforced door and withdrew. The session ended with debate about whether to attempt killing the creature or find an alternative approach.
