## Summary

CP-USS rested at the Temple of Apsu and prepared for travel. They scouted the approach to Feldgrau via a creek bed and discovered a mysterious ancient statue with black flower petals in an offering bowl, tended by crows. Upon approaching Feldgrau, they were ambushed by a massive Undead Storm Giant Graveknight and waves of enchanted crows. The party engaged the creature in combat, suffering significant lightning damage and fear effects before the session ended mid-battle.
