## Summary

CP-USS arrives at the Penitent Hearth Inn after scouting a nearby quarry where they discover evidence of a brutal battle between demon wolves and undead forces (including true vampires). Danger uses speak with plants and animals to gather intelligence about corrupted death in the area. The party investigates the quarry, finding vampire skeletal remains that show signs of thorough destruction by a small but extremely powerful warrior. They then approach the inn and discover it is protected by powerful magic and run by Sylvia and Vex, a mysterious pair who enforce a requirement of true names in exchange for anonymity. After verifying the inn's legitimacy and safety, the party settles in, sells equipment, and prepares for the next leg of their journey.
