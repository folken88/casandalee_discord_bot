## Summary

CP-USS engaged in a massive battle at the Stairs of the Moon between the Broken Ones werewolf clan and the Silver Hides (cult-aligned werewolves led by the warrior Mathys). The party intervened to help the Broken Ones, with intense multi-front combat across a river valley with waterfalls, ice storms, and ash clouds. Multiple near-death experiences occurred (including several characters almost drowning—"swim crits"), but the party ultimately helped repel the Silver Hides and secured an alliance with the Broken Ones clan and their leader Syra.
