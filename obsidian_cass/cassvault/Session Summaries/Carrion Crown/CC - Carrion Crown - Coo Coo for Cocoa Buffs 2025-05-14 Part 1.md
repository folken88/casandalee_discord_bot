# Carrion Crown - Coo Coo for Cocoa Buffs 2025-05-14 Part 1

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=G-ptqu69uc0)

## Summary

CP-USS arrives in Carrion Hill and discovers the town is infested with parasitic tentacle creatures. After a brutal battle at the brewery where Keeper Hive transforms into a massive chaos beast, the party regains control and prepares to descend into the prison basement to investigate the source of the infection. The session ends as they breach the basement door and encounter a massive, lobotomized humanoid jailer guarding the entrance.

## Key Events

- CP-USS arrives in Carrion Hill and encounters Kovira emerging from the swamp after three days of teleporting. They find the town in chaos with guards searching for answers.
  - Characters: Kovira, Gaspar, Rodney, Kai, Kate, Elfrip, Dinvaya, Dismas
  - Location: Carrion Hill
  - Timeline: 4717.04.25 - CP-USS arrives in Carrion Hill

  - Characters: Elfrip, Keeper Hive, Gaspar
  - Location: Carrion Hill Brewery
- Keeper Hive transforms into a massive chaos beast covered in tentacles and armored flesh. The party engages this creature in combat and eventually destroys it with combined efforts.
  - Characters: Kovira, Gaspar, Kai, Kate, Elfrip, Rodney, Dinvaya
  - Location: Carrion Hill Brewery
  - Timeline: 4717.04.25 - Battle with aberrations in Carrion Hill
- The party learns that all brewmasters were infected with parasitic worms that grew from tentacles, and anyone who drank the beer became progressively weaker to the infection. The creatures feed on organic matter and consume evidence.
  - Location: Carrion Hill Brewery
- The party loots Keeper Hive's equipment including a cloak of resistance +2 with gaseous form, beetle-scale leather armor +3 with pass without trace, twin talons (keen sickle daggers +2), and the Keeper's sigil ring—a hundreds-of-years-old ring with tentacle motifs that grants disease/curse/mind-affecting save bonuses and compels keepers to return if they leave.
  - Location: Carrion Hill Brewery
- The party learns from guards that Keeper Mayde went to the prison basement and hasn't returned. The basement door is barred from inside, but someone was seen moving around down there.
  - Location: Carrion Hill
- The party retreats to the swamp to rest and have Dinvaya perform detect evil/disease/curse checks on all members. No party members are found to be infected.
  - Characters: Dinvaya, Kovira, Gaspar, Kai, Kate, Elfrip, Rodney, Dismas
  - Location: Swamp outside Carrion Hill
- Rissa arrives in bird form and joins the party, having completed her schooling and summer break. She receives buffs including bless and heroism.
  - Characters: Rissa
  - Location: Swamp encampment
- The party breaches the prison basement entrance by destroying the locked trap door with the Hungering Axe. They discover a massive, lobotomized humanoid jailer with a metal cage bolted over his head standing guard at a locked door.
  - Characters: Kai, Gaspar
  - Location: Carrion Hill Prison Basement
- When Gaspar displays the Keeper's sigil to the jailer, the creature unlatches a gate mechanism and releases metal claws. A voice from deeper in the basement commands the jailer not to let them in.
  - Characters: Gaspar
  - Location: Carrion Hill Prison Basement
