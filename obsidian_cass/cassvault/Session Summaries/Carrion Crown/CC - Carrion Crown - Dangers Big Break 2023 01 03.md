## Summary

CP-USS tracks a wounded winter wolf and its pack to East Court, a small frozen village devastated days prior. After a brutal combat encounter in which several party members nearly fall, they kill the winter wolf and discover a hidden alcove containing the preserved corpse of an ancient Shining Crusade scout, equipped with magical items including a consecrated undead-hunting crossbow.
