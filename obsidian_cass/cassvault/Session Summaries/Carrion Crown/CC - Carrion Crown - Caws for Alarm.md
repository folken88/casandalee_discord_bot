## Summary

CP-USS departs the Temple of Apsu and travels toward Feldgrau via a hidden creek route. Two scouts (Kai and Elfrip) discover an ancient nondescript statue with offerings of black dusk-wither petals and observe crows making ritual deposits. The party encounters a massive Undead Storm Giant Graveknight with electrical powers, accompanied by a swarm of corrupted black-winged crows. Combat ensues with significant damage dealt to the giant, though Kai falls prone and the party sustains heavy electrical damage.
