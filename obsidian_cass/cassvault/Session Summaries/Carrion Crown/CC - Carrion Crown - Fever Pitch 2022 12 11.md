## Summary

CP-USS investigates a goblin infestation along the river after the ship is attacked by undead goblins afflicted with ghoul fever. After establishing quarantine protocols and healing the infected crew, the party tracks the goblins to a cave system where they engage multiple ghoul goblins in intense combat. The party defeats the creatures, discovers the remains of 34 humans and retrieves magical items including a +1 dark wood bow and a spell prism, before returning to the ship where they manage to save one crew member from full transformation using healing and disease treatment.
