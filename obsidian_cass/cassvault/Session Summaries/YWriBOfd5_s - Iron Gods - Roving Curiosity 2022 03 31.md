# Iron Gods Campaign Session Summary: "Roving Curiosity"

The party's session began with urgent medical attention for two afflicted individuals. Ulfred attempted to diagnose Mad Peter's condition using his Heal skill, considering whether a Break Enchantment spell might remove the magical effects plaguing him. Meanwhile, Luna cast a Restoration spell on Ulfred to restore his drained Wisdom ability score, obliterating the maze-like mental defenses that had been protecting his fractured mind. In a ritualistic act of devotion, Ulfred made a sacrifice to his god Switch by offering a masterwork warhammer, which was pulled into a small portal and vanished. Using this spiritual preparation, Ulfred then cast a healing spell targeting Matt Peter's Wisdom, successfully restoring the man's sanity after months or years of mental deterioration. Matt Peter regained lucidity and became coherent enough to provide the party with crucial warnings and advice about the dangerous river crossing that should not be attempted at night.

Through careful investigation and a remarkable Spellcraft check (natural 20, total 31), the party uncovered horrifying details about Ulfred's past. He had been captured by creatures called "the Butchers" and given to an entity known as "the Flare," who remade and unmade him repeatedly before he escaped through sheer force of will. Ulfred recognized torture methods consistent with drow interrogation techniques on the victim they examined. The party also learned that the Butchers operate large robots with cages to capture people for torture, and that the Forearm Lady had destroyed one of these mechanical horrors just days prior. Most disturbingly, Ulfred experienced prophetic visions of party member Nomkath trapped in a desert surrounded by junk and wreckage, screaming as black tentacles reached for him—a nightmare that another party member had also experienced months ago.

The party's journey through the valley proved eventful and strange. They encountered dead chipmunks scattered across the forest, apparently casualties of a leadership conflict among the chipmunk population, and successfully avoided further pursuit as they traveled toward the androids' location. They consulted with Matt Peter about the locations of various threats and inhabitants throughout the region. The party made several acquisitions during their travels, including a VR helmet of significant value, various magical items like a Cloak of Resistance +1 for Ulfred, and potions of Cure Moderate Wounds. They also obtained an amulet from a tribe member, which the tribe had deemed acceptable for the party to possess, and gave a pendant belonging to a deceased android companion to an android scout as a gesture of goodwill and respect.

As evening approached, the party established camp near a lake and debated their next move. Despite Ulfred's druidic warning against crossing the lake at night, the party discussed various scouting methods to investigate the water: building a bonfire on the shoreline to attract attention, using airwalking combined with wind manipulation to traverse above the lake, or employing a Control Water spell to part the lake itself. The session ended with the party preparing their approach, weighing the risks of reconnaissance against the dangers that lurked in the darkness of the mysterious lake.
