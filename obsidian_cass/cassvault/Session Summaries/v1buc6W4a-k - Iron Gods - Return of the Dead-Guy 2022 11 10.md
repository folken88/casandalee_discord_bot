# Iron Gods Campaign Journal: Return of the Dead-Guy (November 10, 2022)

The party ventured deep into the underground facility beneath Black Hill, discovering a grotesque chamber filled with giant insect thorax sacs oozing pale bluish fluid. During a fierce combat encounter in this 30-foot elbow-shaped chamber, the spellcaster Olbryn was brutally overwhelmed, his hit points plummeting to negative 34 before falling unconscious. However, Alfred's swift intervention with a resurrection spell pulled Olbryn back from the brink of death. In that liminal moment between worlds, Olbryn experienced a haunting vision—silvery chains pulling him through empty space while hearing Alfred's voice and a woman's ethereal singing, suggesting forces beyond their understanding were at work.

Pressing deeper into the facility, the party discovered a nutrient room containing massive feeding sacs. When Olbryn experimentally cast magic missile at one, it exploded spectacularly, spraying foul-smelling pale substance across the chamber. The group's scout, Carla, detected flickering firelight and heat emanating from deeper passages, drawing them toward what would prove to be a critical discovery: a technological portal generating a whitish-blue energy field in a perfect circle, its center revealing points of light that suggested a connection to distant—perhaps even off-world—locations. The party debated cautiously whether to investigate, with Luna demonstrating characteristic boldness by volunteering to peer through the mysterious gateway.

The investigation was cut short when four dormant shotgun spiders guarding the portal suddenly activated, initiating a chaotic combat sequence. The party unleashed a coordinated assault: Nomkath's magic missiles tore through the creatures' armor, Olbryn's shotgun strikes left scorching burn marks, and Luna's call lightning spell devastated the most damaged spider in the hallway's center. Luna even attempted to climb the ceiling for tactical advantage. The battle raged intensely, with Sisuma delivering a devastating burst attack that reduced one spider to negative 86 hit points, while the creatures returned fire with their own energy blasts.

Beyond this immediate threat lay deeper horrors. The party had previously defeated Meyanda, the notorious "Spike Lady," looting her impressive arsenal including a +3 spike chain, +2 mithril alloy chainmail, and multiple protective items that went to Tokala. They had also vanquished a Dominion Titan—a massive crab-like creature—and recovered various magical equipment from its corpse. Most disturbingly, the party encountered doppelgangers of their own members: a fake Olbryn was struck down by Nomkath, while an evil duplicate of either Tokala or Alfred appeared to be downloading information from the facility's systems before defeating the real Alfred in combat. The real Nomkath ultimately triumphed over his evil counterpart through skilled melee combat.

The session concluded with the party's discovery of critical technology and resources: McGinnis acquired the weapon "Justice" (prompting jokes about being "the god slayer"), Ulfred obtained a Wand of Pure Light for healing, and the group recovered power cables and a VR helmet connection to the facility's power system. Most significantly, Eldrin dramatically returned to the party after being taken into space, removing his sunglasses and announcing his return—suggesting the portal's true purpose and the scope of the Iron Gods' reach extended far beyond their underground lair.
