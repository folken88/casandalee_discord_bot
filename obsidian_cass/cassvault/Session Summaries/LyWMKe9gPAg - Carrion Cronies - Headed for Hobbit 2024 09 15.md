# Session Summary: Carrion Cronies - Headed for Hobbit

## The Warrant Delivery Debacle

The party arrived at Ascanor Lodge on 4717.01.24 with a warrant for the arrest of Estovion, who they believed to be associated with the Whispering Way. However, the lodge was hosting a gathering of Iomedae paladins, complicating their plans considerably. The party devised an elaborate scheme to deliver the warrant through their shapeshifting companion Rissa, who transformed into an axolotl to slip past guards and lodge staff (the Brians and Beckys). While party members created distractions—including acrobatic performances and flattery directed at the guards—Rissa attempted to navigate through windows and doors to reach the paladins. The operation was only partially successful; Rissa managed to infiltrate the paladin's meeting but was detected as evil by multiple paladins using detect evil spells. Rather than being forcibly detained, she was gently escorted out by the lodge staff, and the paladins handed her a mysterious warrant of their own—not the party's document.

## A Greater Threat Emerges

Through Rissa's espionage and subsequent conversations with the paladins, the party discovered that the gathering was far more serious than a routine religious assembly. The paladins from the north, east, west, and south had been summoned by an urgent messenger from the south bearing critical information: the primary defenders of Absalom had been utterly decimated by a massive threat. The assembled forces described the losses as catastrophic—all available defenders combined could not compare to what had already been lost. Military command had ordered the paladins to remain in place and await approval before mobilizing, leaving them frustrated and confined to the lodge while a potential world-threatening crisis unfolded.

## Violence at the Establishment

The party's attempt to serve their warrant at an establishment led by Gwyneth, Trey, and others quickly escalated into combat. When one party member transformed into a Promethean form, the establishment's leadership became hostile and ordered an attack. The ensuing battle proved deadly: Trey was killed when possessed Estovion ripped his head clean off, a serving wench fell to arrows, and Lodge Becky was struck down while standing near party members. The combat revealed that the establishment was connected to darker forces than initially suspected, and the party found themselves in a precarious position—confined to their cabin at the lodge with hostile paladins and guards surrounding them, facing the very real possibility of a total party kill if they attempted to break out.

## Escalating Complications

As the session progressed, the party grappled with increasingly difficult choices. They were confined to their room and received clearance to attend a dinner event at 5 PM, but the discovery of the paladins' secret conference about "saving the world" tempted them to continue spying. Party members like Elfrip attempted to sneak into the lodge building to observe the closed-door conference, while others distributed potions of invisibility and disguise self among the group. The tension between their original mission to arrest Estovion and the revelation of a catastrophic threat to Absalom created a moral and tactical quandary. With Lodge Becky dead, multiple combat casualties mounting, and the paladins now aware of Rissa's true nature, the party found themselves in a dangerous stalemate at Ascanor Lodge, uncertain whether to press forward with their arrest warrant or attempt to learn more about the existential threat the paladins were discussing.
