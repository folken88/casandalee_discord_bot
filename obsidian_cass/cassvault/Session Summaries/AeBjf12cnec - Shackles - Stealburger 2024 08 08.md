# Campaign SS Session Summary: "Shackles - Stealburger 2024 08 08"

The party's exploration of Shipwreck Island—a treacherous reef dotted with seven or eight half-intact vessels lashed together into a makeshift pirate settlement—revealed a grim discovery: every inhabitant lay dead from a mysterious plague. While the party searched the derelict ships for supplies and valuables, including a pair of exceptional boots and 3,000 arrows, the Wormwood engaged a Rahadoumi vessel in naval combat in the waters nearby. The enemy ship, *The Pillager's Knife*, proved a formidable opponent, forcing Captain Barnabas Harrigan and his crew into a desperate boarding action.

The subsequent battle was fierce and chaotic, with combat raging across the enemy ship's deck and below-deck corridors. Several crew members fell in the fighting: the skilled sailor Ratline was shot through the eye by a crossbowman and kicked overboard into shark-infested waters, while Jack Scrimshaw was cut down by an enemy officer despite his valiant flanking maneuver. Bujon was slain and would later be mysteriously reincarnated as an Iku-Turso. The party's own members fought with distinction—Holden earned a Panache point from the GM for his bold defense of Captain Barnabas against an assassination attempt, while later performing a spectacular acrobatic swing across the deck that sent an enemy flying overboard. Sha-Feng contributed with arcane support, using *jolt* to damage enemies, while Aretta kicked a soldier overboard and Storgrim threw another into the sea.

The tide of battle turned decisively in the party's favor as they pressed their advantage. Captain Barnabas pursued the enemy command crew below deck, dealing devastating critical hits that broke their resistance. The Rahadoumi Captain was ultimately beheaded by Barnabas himself. Among the spoils of victory, the party recovered masterwork weapons and armor from the enemy's hold, several potions including cure light wounds and levitate draughts, and most notably the artifact longsword *Radiance*. Though the victory came at considerable cost in crew lives, the Wormwood had proven itself a formidable vessel, and the party had secured valuable resources for their continued adventures in the Shackles.
