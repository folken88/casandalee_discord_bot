# Carrion Crown - The Corpse Locker 2025 02 23

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=XRzirhrjCPU)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gaspar, Kovira, Kai, Kate Blackwood, Dinvaya, Dismas

## Summary

Kate Blackwood decrypts a Whispering Way journal overnight, revealing a series of coded letters between two agents designated "A" that chronicle the cult's operations across Ustalav. The party then distributes loot from the Feldgrau battle, with Dinvaya disassembling the Thundering Silencer rifle to upgrade Rev Vadra. Finally, CP-USS explores the Whispering Way workshop in Feldgrau, fighting through skeletons and blade masters before discovering a temple repurposed as a corpse locker filled with hundreds of preserved bodies and necromantic tools.

## Key Events

- Kate Blackwood spends the entire night decrypting the Whispering Way journal using Heroism buff, achieving an effective +6 bonus. The book is triple-encrypted with codes within codes.
  - Characters: Kate Blackwood
  - Location: Feldgrau
  - Timeline: 4717.04.20
- The decoded journal reveals letters from "A" to "A" chronicling Whispering Way operations: the murder of Professor Lorrimor, the Beast trial as a distraction, the assault on Shudderwood werewolves, the vampire outbreak at Northguard, and tracking of CP-USS.
  - Characters: Kate Blackwood
  - Location: Feldgrau
- The letters reveal the Whispering Way sent the vampire outbreak to Northguard to slow CP-USS, and that whoever wrote the letters can deploy that level of destruction in two days.
  - Location: Feldgrau
- The party distributes loot from the Feldgrau battle: Judge and Jury matching pistols go to Dinvaya, Belt of Mighty Con +2 goes to Kai or Gaspar, daggers of invisibility are claimed, and various items are placed in the stroke pit for sale.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kai, Dinvaya, Dismas, Kate Blackwood
  - Location: Feldgrau
- Dinvaya disassembles the Thundering Silencer rifle over two days, purifying and consecrating its components, then upgrades Rev Vadra with the parts, adding +1 to its enhancement bonus.
  - Characters: Dinvaya
  - Location: Feldgrau
- The party discusses reforging Tyrant's Bands into detect undead badges, but the cost (10,000+ gold per badge) is prohibitive.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Dismas
  - Location: Feldgrau
- CP-USS enters the Whispering Way workshop in a manor in southwest Feldgrau. Kai and Dismas search for traps and secret doors while the party fights through skeletons and blade masters in the halls and basement.
  - Characters: Kai, Dismas, Gaspar, Kate Blackwood
  - Location: Feldgrau, Whispering Way Workshop
- Gaspar uses the Scrap Horn to cast fear, causing enemies to flee and provoke attacks of opportunity. The Inquisitor's aura enhances the fear effect.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kate Blackwood
  - Location: Whispering Way Workshop
- The party discovers a temple that the Whispering Way had repurposed as a "corpse locker" filled with hundreds of preserved bodies, surgical tables, mummified remains, and necromantic tools. Preservation spells and construction magic are active throughout.
  - Characters: Gaspar, Kate Blackwood, Kai, Dismas
  - Location: Whispering Way Workshop
- The party discovers the Black Anvil of the Unquiet Engine, a book on creating advanced undead constructs, along with other necromancer tools and a deeply evil book bound in dark leather.
  - Characters: Dismas, Gaspar
  - Location: Whispering Way Workshop
