## Summary

CP-USS camped at the Statue of the founder of Ustalav. Rodney Danger Smith went full Ranger mode--using Speak with Plants to commune with the grass, learning of corrupted death at a rock quarry to the east and a mysterious prisoner in a flooded area. He then spoke with a bat who confirmed a large humanoid with bat wings was seen near the quarry within the last half-moon. The party rode to the quarry where Kovira scouted invisibly from the air, finding a devastated pit with burned skeletal remains including a massive true vampire corpse dismembered and scorched, plus a dead halfling. Gaspar's exceptional survival check (40+) reconstructed the scene: undead had been left as an ambush against demon wolves, and a single incredibly strong 7ft swordsman killed the true vampire one-on-one, pulling out a fang as a trophy. Kate identified dire wolf tracks matching demon wolf size.
