# Carrion Crown - Sympathy for the Demon-Wolves

**Campaign:** Carrion Crown | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=47f8PTJ1QCI)

## Summary

The party decides to seek an alliance with the Demon Wolves werewolf clan against the Whispering Way cult, who are holed up in a southwest manor in Feldgrau with overwhelming forces including five or six wizards, a Charr giant, and significant undead. After resting at the Ara (a Shining Crusade tomb that serves as an ideal emergency teleport sanctuary), the party learns that the location is specially warded to aid good-aligned teleportation and repel evil. They set out east under silence spells to track the Demon Wolves, who had fled the previous battle. Kate Blackwood uses her knowledge of werewolf culture to help guide the approach.
