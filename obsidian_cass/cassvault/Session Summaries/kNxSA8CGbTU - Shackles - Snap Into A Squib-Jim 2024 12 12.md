## Summary

The party travels east along the peninsula on Rickety Squibs' island to reach a wooden watchtower manned by squib jims. They deliver news that the path is clear, share cayman meat (cooked with a natural 20), and camp overnight. During watch they spot a distant red ship in a far-off rainstorm. The session includes discussion of the new ship combat system being built in Foundry, crew roles, and the squabble monkey's surprisingly good gunnery stats.
