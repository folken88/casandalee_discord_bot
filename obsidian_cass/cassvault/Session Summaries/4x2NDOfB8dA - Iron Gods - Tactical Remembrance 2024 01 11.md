# Iron Gods - Tactical Remembrance

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=4x2NDOfB8dA)

## Summary

The party launches their assault on the Technic League compound during a massive coordinated attack. Tokala (guest player Tracy) arrives with his orc army and allied Starfall gangs, using the Scrap Horn to bombard the compound walls with deafening sound. Nomkath stealths up the wall via Air Walk and silently assassinates a sniper, while Kroktah in air elemental form blasts another sniper off the wall with a wind attack. Tokala is telekinetically charged across the battlefield by Olbryn and chainsaws through a sniper. The party fights their way into the compound, with the goal of reaching the dungeon below to confront Osman, the remaining Technic League leader believed to be linked to Unity.
