# Session Summary: "Edging Blivious" - Shackles Campaign

## Mutiny and Mounting Danger

The session opened with a critical realization: Captain Plugg and his officers were orchestrating their own mutiny, using the coded term "lettuce" to discuss their plans. The party discovered they had been systematically isolated through exhaustion and minimal breaks while Plugg filled the ship with loyal crew members. When Bujon made a remarkable Sense Motive check (natural 20), he detected the dark intentions and hostile atmosphere surrounding Plugg's faction, confirming the party's suspicions that they were in mortal danger. The crew faced a grim choice: act against the mutineers or face death at sea.

## The Storm Arrives

Before the party could formulate a response, a massive storm rolled across the Fever Sea. Bujon detected the incoming tempest and attempted to covertly warn the crew, but Captain Plugg and his officers had already spotted it. All hands were called to deck as the ship was forced directly into the storm rather than around it. The crew worked frantically to manage both masts and sails, struggling to keep the vessel turned into the waves to prevent capsizing. Waves created 80-foot drops between peaks and valleys, and the storm intensified with each passing moment. Sarah was sent climbing into the crow's nest to scout for lighter skies, but the ship violently shifted mid-climb, leaving her hanging sideways from the ropes 30-40 feet above the deck. Despite the chaos and danger, crew members who normally despised each other reached out to save one another from being swept overboard, demonstrating unexpected unity against the common threat.

## Grindylow Attack and Casualties

As the party navigated toward calmer waters visible to the east, combat erupted when multiple grindylows attacked the ship, climbing up its sides with tentacle-like appendages. The battle was chaotic and brutal. Sendara collapsed against the rail and was grabbed by a grindylow's tentacles, pulled toward the sea until a crew member shot the creature through the body—but both the grindylow and Sendara fell into the churning waters. Rosie Cusswell tied a rope around herself and threw a lasso down in a desperate rescue attempt. Foremast Jack was dragged over the back of the ship and lost to the sea. One grindylow cast a sleep spell at Vaughan, who managed to resist the magic. Meanwhile, Fipps Chumlett—already a liability—was beaten unconscious, drugged, and thrown overboard to the sharks by Storgrim. The party salvaged what they could from the chaos, including a bronze item providing AC bonuses, divine spell components, death wines (corrupted cure potions), and various treasures from a looted chest: a cup, an axe, pipe bombs, whiskey, and masterwork studded leather armor. Holden collapsed cold on the deck near the ship's wheel, requiring someone to revive him as the storm raged on.
