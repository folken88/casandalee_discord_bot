## Summary

# Campaign SS Session Summary: "Shackles - Caimon Like A Wrecking Ball"

After successfully mutinying against the officers of the Man's Promise, the party arrived at Rickety Squibs to refit their ship and resupply. The crew spent considerable time shopping and negotiating with local merchants, during which they reached level 4 and established themselves as legitimate pirates bound by Besmara's code rather than press-ganged sailors. The merchants of Rickety Squibs, recognizing the party's capabilities, offered permanent discounts at their stores in exchange for solving a pressing local problem. Storgrim addressed the crew, offering any who wished to leave the pirate life compensation and safe passage, though all chose to continue their adventures at sea.

The local problem proved urgent: Squib Jim reported that the western road to the southern garrison had become increasingly dangerous over recent weeks. Giant caiman had been attacking with unusual frequency and coordination, while bandits, orcs, and goblins had begun harassing supply runs. The party agreed to clear the road and ensure safe passage for supply deliveries in exchange for the promised merchant discount. After a night's rest, they set out westward, hiking up steep mountain ridges and descending into areas marked by recent flooding. They discovered a rope bridge constructed by the Squibs spanning a submerged section of the path—a sign that the road's dangers were well-known to the locals.

As the party crossed the flooded area near the bridge, they encountered a coordinated ambush by at least three massive sun caiman emerging from the water. Combat erupted with several party members rolling natural 20s on initiative, giving them a decisive advantage. Storgrim met the first caiman with a ready action, his axe slicing through the creature's armored plating behind its eye. Sha-Feng punched a second caiman directly in the mouth, avoiding its snapping jaws. Riviera's spiritual weapon proved devastatingly effective, driving blades into the middle back of one sun caiman and severing its spine. The party systematically defeated at least six giant caiman in the engagement, with Storgrim, Holden, and Sha-Feng dealing significant damage throughout the battle.

With the immediate threat eliminated, the party harvested the caiman carcasses, obtaining approximately eleven hides weighing 20 pounds each and eleven chunks of meat. They also recovered four sun caiman carcasses from a nearby pond, which Sarah agreed to process into armor materials over eight hours of work, crafting multiple suits from the creatures' armored skin plates and scales. The party obtained money and a fillet of blood caiman meat from one of the larger specimens. The successful clearing of the road and elimination of the caiman threat fulfilled their agreement with Squib Jim, securing the permanent discount and establishing themselves as capable problem-solvers in the region. Ser-Toche also acquired an evil sword named "Ball" during the expedition, which he took as his primary weapon following his advancement to level 4.
