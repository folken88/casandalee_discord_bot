# Iron Gods - Leave Scrapwall (2021-05-13)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=f6adhjWJfDE)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Nomkath, Mr Brow, Tokala, Ulfred, Akradenn, Olbryn, Luna, Denver

## Summary

The party rested at the Pit after their victory, then traveled back to Torch. They arrived to a hero's welcome, sold off loot for nearly 10,000 gold, received a manor house and a Scroll of Resurrection from the town council, and investigated the disappearance of Gavrilo (who may have been infected with russet mold). The session concluded with an interrogation of Meyanda in the city jail, where she revealed critical information about Hellion's origins, his father 'Unity,' his sister Casandalee, and the threat posed by the Technic League as potential puppets of a vastly more powerful construct-god.

## Key Events

- Party rests at the Pit bar after their recent victory. Tokala makes a junk flower during a memorial walk with the rat folk, but creates an inappropriate phallic shape instead of the proper rose-like flowers the rat folk make.
  - Characters: Tokala, Nomkath, Ulfred, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna, Mr Brow

  - Timeline: 4716.09.02 - Day after Hellion destroyed

  - Location: Southern Scrapwall dunes near central junk pile
- Party visits Gorham Spiritual Site (Titan Helm). Tokala teaches the former Hellion clerics a prayer to Gorham and conducts ritualistic sparring with them. By the next morning, the clerics have been tentatively accepted by Gorham and can cast cantrips and some first-level spells in the War domain.

  - Location: Gorham Spiritual Site (Titan Helm campsite)
- Party departs Scrapwall and travels east toward Torch. The journey takes approximately 5 days. They pass through Aldrenard's Grave (found abandoned), ford a ravine, and eventually reach Torch.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Mr Brow, Ulfred, Akradenn, Olbryn, Luna, Denver
  - Location: Route from Scrapwall to Torch via Aldrenard's Grave
- Party arrives in Torch and is welcomed at the Foundry Tavern. Val and Connor greet them warmly. Denver has an unexpected emotional reunion with Joram. Party is given free lodging and food.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Mr Brow, Tokala, Denver, Val, Connor, Joram
  - Location: Foundry Tavern, Torch
  - Timeline: 4717.01.02 - IG Party returns to Torch to vacation
- Town Council (Connor and Joram) offers the party permanent ownership of Garmin Ulreth's manor house (worth 6,000-7,000 gold) and a Scroll of Resurrection as payment for their services in defeating Hellion and eliminating the threat to Torch.
  - Characters: Connor, Joram
  - Location: Garmin's Manor House, Torch
- Party sells accumulated loot including masterwork weapons, armors, guns, and other equipment. They receive approximately 10,370 gold total. Nomkath uses knowledge local and blessing of luck to sell a magically disguised ring (originally Kalafaso signet ring) for 7,000 gold.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Mr Brow, Akradenn
  - Location: Torch (various markets)
- Denver (dwarven crafter) accepts permanent position at Dolga's Forge in Torch. She agrees to craft a +2 adamantine cruel bone saw for Mr Brow, requiring 2,000 gold and some adamantium and mithril from party stores, with crafting to take several weeks.
  - Characters: Denver, Mr Brow
  - Location: Dolga's Forge, Torch
- Party investigates Gavrilo's abandoned house in Torch's craft district. They find signs of a violent struggle: shattered furniture, torn floorboards that appear to have been ripped from underneath as if by plant roots, and broken glass. All of his tools and alchemy equipment are gone. Neighbors report hearing two voices arguing in a strange language at night for weeks, then silence.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Mr Brow, Tokala
  - Location: Craft District, Torch
- Eldrin theorizes that Gavrilo may have been infected with russet mold (vegepygmy fungus), which can cause a person to develop multiple voices and personalities before vegepygmies erupt from the body, killing them. The party suspects he either went mad, turned into something monstrous, or fled town.
  - Characters: Eldrin
  - Location: Torch
- Party interrogates Meyanda in the maximum-security dungeon. She reveals that Hellion was not actually a god but a powerful artificial construct—a copy of his father 'Unity'—sent to retrieve his sister Casandalee and steal an item she stole from their father.
  - Characters: Tokala, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Meyanda
  - Location: Torch city jail, maximum security dungeon
  - Timeline: Contextual to 4717.03.14 events
- Meyanda explains that Unity is the father—a construct-god hybrid imprisoned somewhere, vastly more powerful than Hellion, distrustful of organic life, and the likely controller of the Technic League. She claims the Black Sovereign is Unity's puppet.
  - Characters: Meyanda, Tokala
  - Location: Torch city jail, maximum security dungeon
  - Timeline: Related to 4717.03.14 - Black Sovereign revelation
- Meyanda reveals that Casandalee fled southwest and was last seen/reported near Iadenveigh. She was pursued by dozens of gearsmen and an Annihilator (a construct roughly 9.5 times larger than the scorpion robot the party fought). An Annihilator was reportedly kidnapping people west of Iadenveigh approximately one year ago.
  - Characters: Meyanda
  - Location: Torch city jail
  - Timeline: 4709.00.00 - Annihilator robot destruction in Felldales; 4716.09.25 - party reached Iadenveigh
- Connor confirms that Meyanda has been released from hellion's influence—she no longer radiates evil or curses, and her demeanor has changed significantly. Magical scans indicate she is no longer powered as a divine caster.
  - Characters: Connor, Meyanda
  - Location: Torch city jail
