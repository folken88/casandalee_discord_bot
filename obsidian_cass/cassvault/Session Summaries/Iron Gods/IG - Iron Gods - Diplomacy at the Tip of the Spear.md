## Summary

The party investigated the aftermath of the tentacle tree fight, discovering two dead Technic League operatives killed by the same creatures. They looted technology and drugs from the corpses. Kroktah used Commune with Nature to scan the valley, receiving visions of a Kasatha female with a rifle, dead Technic League members wrapped in tentacles, androids with glowing red eyes, an old man with a limp, and Hirsik scouting alone. The party debated tracking the Kasatha but ultimately decided to backtrack south, cross the river by inflatable raft, and follow an old trail. They discovered a lookout perch used regularly for years. The trail led to the cave home of "Peter," an elderly, mentally damaged human druid who operates as a drug dealer. Peter sells a variety of designer narcotics. Kroktah had a private conversation with Maginnis, apologizing for calling him a coward and acknowledging his combat contributions. The session ended with Kroktah speaking with a chipmunk, killing the alpha chipmunk after a verbal altercation, and the party planning to attempt to heal Peter's mental condition.
