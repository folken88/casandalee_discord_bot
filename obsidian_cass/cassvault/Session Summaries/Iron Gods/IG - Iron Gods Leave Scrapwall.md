# Iron Gods Leave Scrapwall

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=f6adhjWJfDE)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Nomkath, Tokala, Akradenn, Ulfred, Luna, Eldrin, Olbryn, Denver

## Summary

The party rests at the Pit in Scrapwall, attends a memorial for the fallen Redtooth and Whiskifiss, and visits the Gorham spiritual site to teach the former Hellion clerics about honorable warfare. After establishing better relations with the orc clerics and local leadership, they depart Scrapwall and return to Torch. There they receive a permanent manor house as payment for their work, offload collected equipment and weapons for gold, and investigate the disappearance of the alchemist Gavrilo. The session culminates in an interrogation of Meyanda, who reveals critical information about Hellion's origins, his father Unity (a god-like entity imprisoned somewhere), his sister Casandalee, and the true nature of the Technic League as puppets of Unity.
