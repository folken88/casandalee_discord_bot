## Summary

The party goes fishing in the river near their camp, ignoring warnings from NPCs about nighttime river dangers. They encounter a massive aquatic predator (a pike-like creature) and its smaller companions. After a chaotic combat, the party defeats the smaller creatures and scares away the large creature. They salvage items from the wreckage of what appears to be a destroyed raft, including a ruined spellbook, a sonic disruption cannon, a spell prism necklace, mithral chain shirt, and scales from the large creature.
