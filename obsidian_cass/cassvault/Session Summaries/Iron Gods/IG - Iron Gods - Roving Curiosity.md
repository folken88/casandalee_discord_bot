## Summary

The party attempted to heal Peter the drug-dealer druid. Ulfred cast Break Enchantment and Restoration on him. Break Enchantment found no active enchantments. Restoration partially worked, peeling back layers of mental defense, but Peter's condition is severe PTSD from prolonged torture, not a magical curse. A heal check and Olbryn's knowledge arcana confirmed the torture methods resemble drow mental-break techniques. Peter provided information: the Kasatha woman (Zuma) lives north past the tentacle trees with robots, and the androids live to the west. The party then traveled west to visit the Penitent Androids, a community of androids who follow a self-loathing philosophy, viewing themselves as abominations of technology. The party returned the amulet taken from a fallen android earlier in the campaign. The androids opened trade, selling masterwork weapons, magic items, and potions from their "Penitent Pilgrim Patronage" shop. Tokala and Ulfred stayed outside to guard tech gear. After leaving, Kroktah expressed wanting to kill the androids (viewing them as robots), sparking an ethical debate. The party then traveled north toward the river and camped near it. During second watch, Olbryn spotted a brief reddish glow on the water surface hundreds of feet away. The party debated investigating and began planning to air walk over the river to investigate the next night. The session ended with a comical altercation between Kroktah and Tokala involving the "talking spear."
