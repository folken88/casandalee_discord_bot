## Summary

The party resurrects Nomkath using a raise dead ritual and finds Casandalee intact. They locate Ferris Xoud's safe house in Starfall's north-central district, discovering it stripped of supplies but containing useful loot (thieves' tools, rare arrows, badges, magic items) and information about past failed break-in attempts. The party meets with the Mockery (Drowmuck), a resistance leader, who reveals plans for an assassination opportunity: a night market meeting between Technic League Captain "the Shade" and a wealthy smuggler named Baron Drund in the next 2-3 days. The party agrees to gather intelligence and considers taking action against one of the Technic League's leadership.
