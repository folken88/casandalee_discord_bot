# Iron Gods - Family Drama

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=T70NprWiXY0)
**Transcript Quality:** good
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Tokala, Maginnis, Luna, Casandalee, Meyanda, Dinvaya, Connor, Val

## Summary

The party returned to Torch from the Scar of the Spider, arriving at 5 AM after Maginnis drove through the night on insomnia powder. They learned that the Technic League had retaken Scrapwall, killing Sevroth and scattering the Silver Hawks and Redtooth Raiders. The party discussed their growing understanding of Unity, Hellion, and the Technic League, and resolved to take the fight to the Technic League. Casandalee was introduced to Connor, Dinvaya, and Meyanda, and had a compassion facet installed that gave her emotions for the first time. A gang called the Sons of Cortez attacked in the streets of Torch looking for Maginnis, leading to a large street battle the party won decisively. Maginnis then revealed that the dead person the gang was fighting over was his aunt, who had come to Torch looking for him. He departed the party to attend to family business, discovering a second revolver called Revenge hidden in his aunt's casket.

## Combat Encounters

- Street battle in Torch against the Sons of Cortez gang and Scrapwall scrappers. Enemies included pistol-wielding gunmen, riflemen on rooftops, shotgun fighters, and melee scrappers. Kroktah polymorphed two enemies into cats. Olbryn used chain lightning to devastating effect (58 damage to 5 targets). Nomkath operated while greater invisible, killing multiple enemies with sneak attacks. Luna charged into enemies with vital strike. The party won decisively with only minor injuries.
