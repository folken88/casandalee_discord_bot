# Iron Gods Session 02 - Black Hill Caves Habitat

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=TjjzYPVJBR4)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Gillo, Morana, Mcginness, Takala, Valou

## Summary

The party explored deeper into Black Hill Caves, discovering a vast habitat chamber with a metal wall made of unknown advanced alloy. They encountered and defeated two flying medical drones and entered the habitat interior through sealed doors. Beyond the habitat they found a forest environment with hostile four-armed skeletons and a tentacled creature in the walls. After heavy combat and resource depletion, they retreated and returned to Torch where they rested and resupplied. Transcript quality was fair—many garbled NPC names and audio issues, but core events are identifiable.
