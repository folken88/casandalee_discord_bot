## Summary

The party recovered from the Binox incident and examined loot from the desiccated corpse found in the cave. Isuma shared her backstory with Kroktah: she was a Kasatha diplomat who lived aboard the Divinity, learned android technology and language, then discovered her people had been split into two groups - one taught and one imprisoned in a habitat. She fought for their freedom until the Dominion of the Black attacked, causing the ship to break apart. Frozen in a cryopod, she awoke six years ago, was tortured by the Dominion, and escaped. She confirmed the Dominion is still present in the western valley. The party identified magical gear from the corpse (Maura's Leathers, drow razors, a compass/pearl of power), confronted Hirsik about his disappearance during the ambush, and debated trust issues. Nomkath offered to help Tokala train to resist the data jack. The party traveled to the Scar of the Spider riverbank, found a ruined building with a corpse clutching a prototype grenade revolver called "the Bouncer," and encountered a ghost gunslinger. Maginnis dueled the ghost in a dramatic quick-draw showdown, winning multiple initiative rolls thanks to a magical cigar's +5 initiative bonus, and destroyed the ghost.
