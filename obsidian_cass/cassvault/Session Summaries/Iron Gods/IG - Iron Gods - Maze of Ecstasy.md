# Iron Gods - Maze of Ecstasy

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=zVBT0plnNeQ)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Luna

## Summary

The party continues hiding in Garton's lab on the second floor of the Palace of Fallen Stars. They extensively debate "Operation Healburger" -- the plan to cure the Black Sovereign's numerian fluid addiction by putting a heal spell into food. Casandalee confirms that heal would remove the addiction and cravings but cannot remove permanent DNA-altering technological effects or the magical curse component. The party also debates alternatives: Toala challenging the Black Sovereign one-on-one (deemed too risky), squad-vs-squad combat, or invisibly casting heal on him. They settle on running Casandalee's combat simulation of a squad-vs-squad fight against the Black Sovereign and his inner circle to assess difficulty. The party buffs up extensively for the simulation including particulate form (crit immunity), protection from energy, mirror image, haste, heroism (via clam drink), and greater invisibility. The session is largely planning with the simulation beginning near the end.
