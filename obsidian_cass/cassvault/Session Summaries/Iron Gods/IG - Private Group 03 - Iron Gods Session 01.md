## Summary

The party (Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, and Luna) receives a bounty to rescue Councilman Khonnir Baine from underwater caves beneath Torch. After obtaining water-breathing spells from the Temple of Brigh, they navigate a treacherous underwater passage, encounter and negotiate peace with intelligent underground dwelling creatures (described as dark-skinned, knife-wielding humanoids from the Underdark), discover evidence of prior expeditions, and begin exploring deeper caverns in search of Khonnir and a mysterious 'wall of metal.' Combat erupts with gremlins (small creatures with dimensional abilities) that the party agrees to eliminate in exchange for safe passage and information about the disappeared adventurers.
