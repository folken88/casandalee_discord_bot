# Iron Gods - Choke on this Tower

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=1Qjlfp3i8J4)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Tokala, Maginnis

## Summary

Kroktah (Enrique's new kasatha druid character) formally joins the party after a roleplay introduction. The party explores the Choking Tower's remaining upper floors and discovers a heavily warded door crackling with electricity, which Ulfred neutralizes using the heralcom rod. They descend into the tower's basement, finding a well-crafted underground complex. In a warded room sealed with salt lines, they discover a permanent containment circle imprisoning a leukodemon named Kavenchin from Abaddon. The party debates whether to free it, ultimately deciding to leave it imprisoned and finish exploring the tower first. They also discover a massive wind tunnel chamber containing three pairs of glowing eyes (unidentified creatures).

## Key Events

- Kroktah introduces himself to the party as a kasatha druid who followed them into the tower. He was drawn by a vision of a woman connected to his past. He offers to reincarnate Eldrin, and Ulfred senses he is sincere.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Nomkath, Ulfred, Tokala
  - Location: Choking Tower - Upper Floor
  - Timeline: 4716.10.17 - IG Party meets & recruits Kroktah
- Ulfred uses the heralcom rod to neutralize a lightning-trapped door. The rod absorbs 96 points of electrical damage and then sends a small zap to unlock the door, revealing a long winding staircase descending into the tower's basement.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Nomkath
  - Location: Choking Tower - Upper Floor / Basement Entrance
- Kroktah casts barkskin on Tokala, raising his AC to 23. The party buffs up before descending.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Tokala
  - Location: Choking Tower
- The party descends five stories below ground into the rock beneath the Choking Tower. Ulfred confirms they are below ground level. The basement features perfectly stone-shaped tunnels and newer, better-built doors than the upper floors.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Nomkath, Olbryn
  - Location: Choking Tower - Basement
- In a study room in the basement, the party finds an arcane family workbook (+2/+4 spellcraft), a tome of epics, a book of letters for Numeria, an enchanted traveling formula book, and a ring of missile protection (DR 10/magic vs ranged, recharges after 50 points).
  - Characters: Ulfred, Nomkath, Olbryn, Kroktah
  - Location: Choking Tower - Basement Study
- The party discovers a heavily warded door with salt lines across the bottom. Tokala identifies the salt as spirit-warding, similar to his tribe's witch doctors' practices for containing summoned spirits.
  - Characters: Tokala, Ulfred, Nomkath
  - Location: Choking Tower - Basement
- Behind the warded door, the party finds an 80x40 foot chamber containing a 35-foot diameter permanent containment circle glowing with blue rune work. Nomkath hears thousands of flies buzzing from within the circle.
  - Characters: Olbryn, Nomkath, Ulfred
  - Location: Choking Tower - Demon Containment Chamber
- The imprisoned creature reveals itself as Kavenchin, a leukodemon from Abaddon. It is a 14-foot tall creature with goat hooves, feathered wings, a skinless skull head, and surrounded by a swarm of flies. Olbryn identifies it as a servant of Pestilence (one of the Four Horsemen).
  - Characters: Kroktah, Olbryn, Nomkath, Ulfred, Tokala
  - Location: Choking Tower - Demon Containment Chamber
- Kroktah argues passionately for freeing Kavenchin, drawing on his own experience as a captive. The rest of the party urges caution. Nomkath's sense motive detects desperation, boredom, and a sincere desire to leave but not complete trustworthiness.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Nomkath, Ulfred, Tokala
  - Location: Choking Tower - Demon Containment Chamber
- The party decides to leave Kavenchin imprisoned for now and finish clearing the tower before making a decision. Ulfred proposes either convincing it to submit to dismissal or surrounding it and fighting if a deal falls through.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Nomkath, Tokala, Kroktah
  - Location: Choking Tower - Demon Containment Chamber
- Tokala reads the book of war prayers aloud, casting a "super bless" that gives +2 to all saves as a morale bonus for the party.
  - Characters: Tokala, Ulfred
  - Location: Choking Tower - Basement
- The party discovers a massive wind tunnel room (120x60 feet, 30-40 foot ceilings) containing a 30-foot diameter electric fan and three pairs of glowing eyes floating contentedly in the wind current. A control panel manages the air pump systems.
  - Characters: Olbryn, Ulfred, Kroktah, Nomkath
  - Location: Choking Tower - Wind Tunnel Chamber
