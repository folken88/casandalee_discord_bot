## Summary

The party hides in a safe house in Starfall's slums after returning from a secret meeting. The Earth and Daughter druids approach Crocodile with a request to free an imprisoned sister in exchange for alliance, but Crocodile consults the group's leaders (Alfred and Olbryn) before committing. When guards raid the house before a decision can be made, combat erupts. The party defeats the guards through coordinated use of Silence spells (neutralizing the party's own spellcasters), area-of-effect damage, and tactical positioning. They successfully escape into the night under disguise.
