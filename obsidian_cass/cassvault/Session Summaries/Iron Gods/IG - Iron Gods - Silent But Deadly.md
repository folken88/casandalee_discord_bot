# Iron Gods - Silent But Deadly

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=hgXgA-kzWSw)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Tokala, Luna

## Summary

The party spent their first night hiding in a slum apartment in Starfall's kill box, using rope trick and watches. They debated whether to trust the Earth Daughters who had offered them shelter. Kroktah and the Earth Daughters sister returned with a request for help freeing a captured sister, which led to a confrontation with city guards in the night. After a chaotic combat involving a silence spell and the party fighting guards, they fled through the streets using disguises and stealth, eventually finding a new hiding spot about 20 blocks away.

## Key Events

- The party spent the night in the Earth Daughters' safe house. Tokala and Ulfred slept in a rope trick while Nomkath, Kroktah, and Luna kept watch outside. Tokala used dream feast to provide comfortable rest.
  - Characters: Tokala, Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Luna
  - Location: Earth Daughters safe house, kill box, Starfall

  - Location: Earth Daughters safe house, kill box, Starfall

  - Characters: Kroktah, Nomkath
  - Location: Earth Daughters safe house, kill box, Starfall

- The Earth Daughters sister returned during the night asking for help freeing a captured sister from the guards, leading to a combat encounter with Starfall city guards.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Ulfred, Luna, Tokala, Nomkath, Olbryn
  - Location: Kill box streets, Starfall

- After the combat, the party disguised themselves as peasants using Olbryn's disguise other spells and fled through the streets. They rolled exceptionally well on stealth (lowest roll was 16) and escaped 20 blocks away to a dark alley by 4 AM.
  - Characters: Olbryn, Nomkath, Luna
  - Location: Kill box, Starfall

- Luna's knowledge of Starfall streets proved invaluable for navigating escape routes, choosing quieter neighborhoods and avoiding guard patrols.
  - Characters: Luna
