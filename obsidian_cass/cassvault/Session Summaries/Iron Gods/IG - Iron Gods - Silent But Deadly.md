## Summary

The party, hiding in a safe house in Starfall's slums, negotiates with the Earth and Daughters (a group of nature-corrupted eco-terrorists) who demand help freeing a captured sister. After deciding to confer with the group, Croktah wakes the party when guards arrive. A chaotic combat ensues featuring silence spells, invisibility, wild shape, and a dramatic meteor-strike via rocket boots, after which the party escapes into the city using stealth and disguises.
