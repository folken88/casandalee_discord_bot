# Iron Gods - Calamitous Flailing

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=rqMH5tcxQuE)

**Characters Present:** [[Ulfred]], [[Nomkath]], [[Kroktah]], [[Olbryn]], [[Tokala]], [[Maginnis]], [[Zuma]]

## Summary

Continuing from the previous session, the party drove the Nomad south to attack the second Dominion patrol group. Upon arrival they were ambushed with confusion spells -- [[Olbryn]] was confused for most of the battle, repeatedly punching himself and eventually lightning bolting [[Tokala]] and [[Maginnis]]. The party fought another group of intellect devourer-controlled orcs, kasatha, and a second Dominion panther creature. [[Kroktah]] deployed wall of thorns and entangle to control the battlefield. [[Zuma]] was eventually confused by a natural 1 will save. [[Kroktah]] successfully used baleful polymorph on an intellect devourer-inhabited kasatha, ejecting the brain creature. [[Olbryn]] eventually dispelled his own confusion and cast a lightning bolt hitting two orcs for 61 damage. [[Tokala]] used divine power and his chainsaw with vital strike to devastating effect. [[Zuma]] killed the second Dominion panther creature with three icicle shots.
