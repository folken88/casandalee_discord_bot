## Summary

The party completes the robot fight at Robo Rumble, betting on Sky Metal against Dirty Bomb with Casandalee subtly interfering. They win, and Acradenn agrees to ally with them. During the victory celebration, Casandalee detects a large robot strike force headed to assassinate Technic League Captain Elias at a southwest tower outside Starfall. The party urgently gears up, teleports to the fortress with Acradenn, and defends it against a massive robot assault including meridons, a juggernaut, and flying missile-launching robots. After a grueling combat with missile barrages and melee fighting, the party destroys all the attacking robots, securing both Acradenn and Elias as allies.
