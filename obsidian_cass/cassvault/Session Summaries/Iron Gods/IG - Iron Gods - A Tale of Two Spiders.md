# Iron Gods - A Tale of Two Spiders

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=8QEaQN6aOHc)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Tokala, Nomkath, Ulfred, Kroktah, Maginnis, Olbryn, Hirsek

## Summary

The party negotiated with Hirsek, a tiefling mercenary hired by the Technic League as a surveyor, and recruited him after he agreed to abandon his contract. They investigated the Kura Town ruins further, concluding the giant insectoid creatures captured rather than killed most inhabitants. Using Casandalee's datapad and Kroktah's map, the party navigated into the Scar of the Spider river valley. Hirsek revealed he had seen the giant spider-like creatures hiding in caves deeper in the valley. The party camped, sampled Brow Brew and soothe, and prepared to push deeper into the Scar. Josh was in the hospital during this session; Olbryn was present but had limited participation (confused in the prior session's combat, spent much of this session punching himself).

## Key Events

- Kroktah's sense motive (37) confirmed Hirsek is being truthful. She assessed him as a man of his word, though professionally a mercenary who kills for money.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Hirsek
  - Location: Kura Town
- Nomkath privately assessed Hirsek in Hallit (a language Hirsek doesn't speak), noting he lacks a spellbook and wears armor -- not the profile of a Technic League member proper, more of a bounty hunter.
  - Characters: Nomkath
  - Location: Kura Town
- Tokala explained his hatred of the Technic League: they murdered his wife and son. He demanded Hirsek renounce his Technic League ties or be killed.
  - Characters: Tokala, Hirsek
  - Location: Kura Town
- Hirsek revealed his Technic League mission was surveying, not killing -- scouting for future expeditions to find tech. He was paid 300 gold upfront with 2,000 promised on completion. He agreed to join the party and abandon the contract.
  - Characters: Hirsek, Nomkath
  - Location: Kura Town
- Nomkath patted Hirsek down for a Technic League pin. Hirsek was clean.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Hirsek
  - Location: Kura Town
- Further investigation of Kura Town confirmed the giant insectoid creatures captured most inhabitants rather than killing them. Tracks showed civilians and children who fled into the wilderness were pursued and scooped up. The party concluded the creatures are "harvesting people."
  - Characters: Nomkath, Kroktah
  - Location: Kura Town
- The party looted the necromancer's body: Ring of Protection +2 (Nomkath), Amulet of Natural Armor +2 (Ulfred, old +1 to Alburn), Cloak of Resistance +2, two batteries, a flashlight, and a lighter (Hirsek).
  - Characters: Nomkath, Ulfred
  - Location: Kura Town
- Kroktah asked Hirsek if he had seen anyone with four arms in his travels. He had not.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Hirsek
  - Location: Kura Town
- Nomkath studied Casandalee's datapad and identified four geolocation pings: the Choking Tower (~7 years ago), Iadenveigh (~400 years ago), Silver Mount, and the Scar of the Spider. The datapad uses "planet core" and "deep space observer" as reference points for precise location tracking.
  - Characters: Nomkath
  - Location: Scar of the Spider approach
- The party entered the Scar of the Spider river valley. The terrain shifted dramatically to lush vegetation, trees, ferns, and flowing water -- a stark contrast to the dry Numerian plains.
  - Characters: Tokala, Nomkath, Ulfred, Kroktah, Maginnis, Olbryn, Hirsek
  - Location: Scar of the Spider
- Hirsek revealed he had spent a month in the Scar and nearly died multiple times. He noted the giant spiders hide in caves and that "the very ground wants us dead."
  - Characters: Hirsek
  - Location: Scar of the Spider
- The party camped in the valley. Hirsek was introduced to Brow Brew (the mushroom beer from Iadenveigh) and managed to bluff his way through drinking it. Kroktah used Create Drugs to replicate soothe for Tokala, who rubbed it on his face and lost feeling in it.
  - Characters: Tokala, Hirsek, Kroktah
  - Location: Scar of the Spider

## NPCs

- [[Hirsek]] - Tiefling mercenary from Cheliax. Hired by the Technic League as a surveyor for 2,000gp. Skilled with pistol and rapier, uses acrobatics and sneak attacks. Abandoned his contract to join the party. (Harrison's character)
- [[Casandalee]] - Referenced via her datapad which contains geolocation data showing she visited the Scar of the Spider, Choking Tower, Iadenveigh, and Silver Mount at different times
- [[Alburn]] - Drow companion, received Ulfred's old Amulet of Natural Armor +1

## Locations

- **Kura Town** - Continued investigation confirmed the giant insectoid creatures captured rather than killed most inhabitants
- **Scar of the Spider** - A river valley in its own mountain range, accessible from the southeast. Features lush vegetation, flowing water, and a central lake. Mountains on either side. Extremely dangerous -- full of creatures hiding in caves.

## Uncertain Events

- The exact nature and purpose of the giant insectoid creatures capturing people remains unknown. The party speculates they may be giant robot spiders related to Hellion's body design.
- Whether the Scar of the Spider's giant spiders are the same entities as the metallic insectoid creatures that destroyed Kura Town is unclear -- Hirsek distinguishes between them.
- The significance of Casandalee's datapad showing a check-in at Iadenveigh 400 years ago versus the Choking Tower 7 years ago is not fully explored.
