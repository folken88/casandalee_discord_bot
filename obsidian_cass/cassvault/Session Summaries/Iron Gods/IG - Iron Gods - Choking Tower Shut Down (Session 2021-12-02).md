# Iron Gods - Choking Tower Shut Down (Session 2021-12-02)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=FodMUawDJ-4)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The party rested in the Choking Tower's basement and prepared to deal with a bound demon. They attempted a dismissal spell which the demon rejected, forcing them into combat. They freed an enslaved worm-that-walks creature before encountering three fire elemental beings (Azares) who had been trapped in the tower's forge level, serving Furkas Zoud. The party defeated Zoud's remaining robot servants and negotiated with the Azares, who agreed to help them destroy the tower.
