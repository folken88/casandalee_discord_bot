# Iron Gods - Manly Meetings

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LMTbK84MUcI)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Olbryn, Kroktah, Luna, Blackout

## Summary

The party reincarnates Blackout (Enrique's character) who returns as a human, then distributes Krastus's powerful loot. They fence items around Starfall to raise funds, sell a thoracic nanite chamber to a mercenary named Killinator by surgically installing it, and scrape together 44,600 gold to pay Hearsick for intelligence. Ulfred meets Hearsick one-on-one at the Slotbag and receives a map of the Technic League compound, plus arrangements for two meetings: a diplomatic introduction to Technic League Captain Acradenn at an underground robot fighting club called Robo Rumble, and the location of Gartone's secret safe house. The session ends with the party arriving at Robo Rumble in disguise, meeting Acradenn, and beginning to review robot fight cards.
