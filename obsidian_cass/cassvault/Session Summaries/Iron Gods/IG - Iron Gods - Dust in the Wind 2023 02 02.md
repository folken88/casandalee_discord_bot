## Summary

The party uses Wind Walk to travel from Torch to Starfall, observing the polluted Technic League city from above. After a failed stealth attempt exposes Olbryn, they locate a group of Druids (the Earthen Daughter shamans) through Crocodile's scrying and commune with nature spells. The party makes contact with a hostile Druid shaman in the kill box district and establishes a safe house, though discovers the shamans may be dangerously fanatical about population reduction.
