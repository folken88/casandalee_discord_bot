## Summary

The party uses wind walk to travel from Torch to Starfall in disguise as brown clouds. They observe the polluted, fortified city and its geography from above. After landing, they make contact with a druid shaman named Croc (or similar) in the poor Kill Box district by using commune with nature and scrying. The session ends with the party settling into a safe house maintained by these Druids, though concerns about their violent tendencies are raised.

## Key Events

- Party departs Torch using wind walk spell cast by Croc (Crocodile/Croctalé), becoming gaseous clouds wearing brown cloaks to disguise themselves as smog.
  - Characters: Crocodile/Croctalé, Nomkath, Olbryn, Ulfred, Akradenn, Luna, Casandalee
  - Location: Torch to Starfall
- Party flies over Numeria and observes Starfall from above — a massive mountain rising from a flat plain surrounded by black smoke and pollution. Croc detects the mountain is artificial, likely built by druids or tribal culture.
  - Characters: Crocodile, Tokala
  - Location: Starfall vicinity, above the city
- Party communes with nature via a tree to ask about druids maintaining the city's plants. Learns that dozens of people connected to nature care for trees throughout the city, mostly in the Kill Box and center-west areas.
  - Characters: Crocodile
  - Location: Starfall airspace
- Party attempts to use scrying to locate the female druid shaman seen in the commune vision. Scrying succeeds in establishing brief two-way contact; the shaman resists but receives Crocodile's mental image of nature and healing. Connection reveals the shaman's location in south-central Kill Box.
  - Characters: Crocodile, Unnamed female druid/shaman
  - Location: Starfall Kill Box (south-central area)
- Party materializes in an alley in the Kill Box neighborhood and approaches a house with visible plants inside. Crocodile knocks on the door and meets the female shaman with lion-like paw hands.
  - Characters: Crocodile, Unnamed female druid/shaman
  - Location: Starfall Kill Box, small house
- Crocodile discovers a decomposed city guard (wearing city guard armor) inside the shaman's house, preserved by plant magic. The guard has been dead for weeks.
  - Characters: Crocodile, Tokala, Olbryn, Unnamed city guard (deceased)
  - Location: Shaman's house in Kill Box
- Crocodile gives the shaman a gift of high-quality deer feces (treated by a high-powered druid from the Scar of the Spider). The shaman purifies it with magic and accepts, saying she will meet them tomorrow at noon.
  - Characters: Crocodile, Unnamed female druid/shaman
  - Location: Shaman's house
- The shaman leaves to consult with her sisters (other druids). Nomkath uses a gift (described as a space moth object) to overhear their conversation, learning they view the party as strong potential allies and are interested in killing many people.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Unnamed female druid/shaman, Shaman's sisters (other druids)
  - Location: Starfall streets outside the house
