# Iron Gods - Surrounded by Beef

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=o3vd8mpfao0)

## Summary

The battle against Unity's assault on the Palace of Fallen Stars continues. Spartan gearsmen fire plasma at barbarians and party members, flesh-burrowing observer robots drill into victims' bodies dealing con and strength damage, and two repeater tanks (mobile laser platforms) crawl over the balconies blasting barbarians with clustered plasma fire. Rail gun tanks outside the palace fire precision shots through the floor and walls, killing barbarians and threatening the party. Olbryn shifts position to buff the Black Sovereign's inner circle with haste and protection from fire, placing himself in the center of the sovereign's retainers -- "surrounded by beef" with 1,450 HP of fighters around him. Ulfred and Luna fly across the room to engage the repeater tanks. Akradenn uses anti-magic field tactics and joins the melee. Becki (the sovereign's witch) mazes one repeater tank; T'rock and Kevoth-Kul attack the other. Nomkath heals Tech Makul and guards Casandalee near the throne. Mr Brow disintegrates a Spartan robot for 113 damage from the throne area. The party and Black Sovereign's forces systematically destroy both repeater tanks, multiple Spartan gearsmen, and the observer robots. Mockery dives off the balcony to engage the second rail gun tank outside. The session ends with the battle momentarily won -- the remaining robots fleeing, but the Black Sovereign standing confused on the ruins of a shattered rail gun tank asking "Who are you people?"
