# Iron Gods - Just the Tip

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=5KWeM3BKhFQ)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Maginnis, Tokala

## Summary

The party camps in the Scar of the Spider at the site where [[Maginnis]] previously fought a ghost. [[Kroktah]] has private conversations with [[Olbryn]] and [[Ulfred]] about the morality of keeping the Technic League brain-in-a-jar captive, but finds no allies willing to destroy it. The party establishes a teleportation circle at the campsite using tattoo ink (1,000 gp), hidden with plant growth. They discuss strategy for approaching the Dominion hive, consult [[Isuma]] about Dominion strength (dozens of dangerous creatures plus uncountable parasites), and decide to investigate the enormous "meat pile" first. They travel up the river by motorboat into Dominion territory. [[Tokala]] attempts to sense [[Casandalee]]'s presence near the meat pile and receives a disturbing psychic vision of being watched by hostile entities, alerting the Dominion. The party discovers the meat pile is a 200-foot-tall, 400-foot-wide blob of dead alien flesh radiating powerful magic of multiple schools. They enter through a sphincter-like orifice on its surface -- greasing up [[Tokala]] and having [[Kroktah]] (in ape form) push him in, followed by others diving, shifting, or crawling through the intestinal tube. Inside they find rooms with strange starry floors, leafy walls, and swarms of rot grubs which they fight with fire, lightning, plasma shotgun, grenades, and the MGAE Bouncer.
