## Summary

The party returns to Torch after their expedition to the Scar of the Spider, arriving in dramatic fashion via the six-wheeled truck driven by a stimulant-fueled Maginnis. They reconnect with the town and learn that Scrapwall has been retaken by the Technic League, who are excavating powerful tech. The session features extensive discussion of threats (Unity, the Technic League, Hellion's failed plans) and culminates in a major combat encounter when assassins attack The Foundry Tavern. McGinnis departs the party after discovering his aunt was killed by members of the Sons of Cortez—the same family organization responsible for his father's death years ago.
