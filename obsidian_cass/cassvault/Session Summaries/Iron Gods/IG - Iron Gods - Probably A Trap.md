# Iron Gods - Probably A Trap

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=JSvPFBpm6P0)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Nomkath, Olbryn, Ulfred, Kroktah, Blackout, Luna, Casandalee

## Summary

The party reviews the Starfall power structure and plans their next moves. Olbryn teleports to Torch to pick up a "Tech Stone" crafted by Casandalee via Connor, a kasath stone enchanted with magic that allows communication with technology, gives bonuses against tech, and creates a VR mindscape of Kroktah's homeworld of Kasath. Casandalee casts a communal Status spell linking the entire party's health and position awareness, and an upgraded version enabling communication through their tech communicators. Blackout sets a trap for Hearsick using a dead drop message at the Mercenary Guild. The party then assaults a suspicious facility with doubled guards that Mockery had identified, wind walking to the roof and crashing through it when Ulfred (enlarged to Huge size) collapses the structure under his weight. They fight through Technic League soldiers and cafeteria staff in the opening moments. Ulfred's perception reveals the upturned earth around the facility resembles hastily dug graves.
