# Iron Gods - I thought he was a waiter

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=iSoREpx9xKE)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna, Maginnis

## Summary

Maginnis returns to Starfall after a long absence, arriving at the Sovereign Gate with his wagon and meeting Meyanda at the military hospital. He reunites with the party, is introduced to Mr Brow (whom he initially mistakes for a waiter), and is connected to Casandalee's telepathic network (Castnet). The party purchases the Matriarch's Tiara from Montaine for Olbryn. They then drive the truck into the northern Numerian waste to hunt the juvie terask. After tracking it to the battlefield of Grashyhot, the creature bursts from the sand beneath Ulfred's feet. A massive battle ensues: the creature has devastating armor class (42), spell resistance, and a void/gravity breath weapon that pushes back and stuns party members. Maginnis uses Dead Eye deed and clustered shots from his pistol for significant damage. Ulfred fights in giant elemental form. Akradenn uses the move earth ring to uncover the creature when it burrows underground. The killing blow is delivered by Akradenn with a critical hit from his dimensionally-thin force blade through the creature's eye. The party begins harvesting the meat.
