# Iron Gods - Spare Parts Bud

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=DP4tNVeKwTQ)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Luna

## Summary

The party has infiltrated the Palace of Fallen Stars and is hiding in a broom closet, then relocates to Garton's former lab. They plan to kidnap a palace guard for information to feed Casandalee's simulation. Mr Brow uses thought sense to detect nearby minds through walls. Nomkath and Mr Brow stealth out invisible while Olbryn provides spell support. Mr Brow psychically incapacitates a barbarian guard with ego whip (reducing his intelligence to 0, rendering him unconscious), Nomkath drags him back to the lab. They tie him up with silk rope and masterwork manacles, and Casandalee downloads his brain via VR helmet while he experiences a hallucination of being on a desert planet with a kasatha woman berating him. The brain bake reveals extensive palace layout information including the Black Sovereign's inner circle (Cole/Lady Cole his consort, Tech Makul his cybernetically enhanced captain of the guard, Becki the witch, and T-Rock the 11-foot stone giant oracle of Gorum), the kitchen run by the famous Chef Gorgo, a prisoner locked in the guest lounge, a drug parlor, a two-headed 20-foot monster in the basement, ghost rumors, and dark seers from Nidal. Akradenn patrols disguised as the incapacitated guard. The guard is fiercely loyal to the Black Sovereign.
