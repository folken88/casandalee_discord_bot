## Summary

The party enters the Choking Tower, a massive wizard's tower built by Furkas Xoud. After repairing a broken wheel lock to enter, they encounter a deadly invisible ghost-like creature in the foyer that uses Cloudkill and lightning bolt spells, which they defeat after a difficult battle. They explore lower levels of the tower, discovering numerous magical traps, collecting scrolls and magical items, and encountering small maintenance robots that reset the tower's defenses. The session ends as they prepare to ascend further into the tower.
