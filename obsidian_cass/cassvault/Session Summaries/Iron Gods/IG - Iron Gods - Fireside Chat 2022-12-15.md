# Iron Gods - Fireside Chat 2022-12-15

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=lyEcjPIkDDo)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Nomkath, Olbryn, Akradenn, Ulfred, Luna, Croktah

## Summary

The party departs the Scar of the Spider with Casandalee (an AI Core) and heads toward Torch in their electric vehicle. During an 8-day journey, they interrogate the captured Technic League brain (Ferris the wizard) in a shared VR mindscape, extracting information about Starfall, the Technic League's leadership structure, safe houses, and alternate entry points into the city. Croktah uses the VR environment to reconnect with memories of her Kasathan people through Casandalee's recreations. The session heavily features exposition about Unity, the ancient alien AI hidden beneath Silvermount, its plans to escape via a repaired shuttle, and foreshadowing of the party's eventual journey to Starfall.
