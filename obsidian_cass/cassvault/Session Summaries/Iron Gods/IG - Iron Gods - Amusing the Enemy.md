# Iron Gods - Amusing the Enemy

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=btxDbZmVXbc)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Tokala, Luna, Casandalee, Meyanda, Dinvaya, Connor

## Summary

A planning and preparation session as the party readied themselves in Torch for their infiltration of Starfall. They discussed how to enter the city given the Technic League's strict technology confiscation policies, ultimately deciding to use Kroktah's wind walk spell to fly in disguised as brown smog clouds. Casandalee hacked into a captured Technic League spy drone, revealing intelligence about the party being classified as high-value targets and that Scrapwall's garrison was relatively small. The party manipulated the drone's data to send false information about their whereabouts, then prepared disguises and finalized their gear before departing.

## Key Events

- The party planned their approach to Starfall. Connor and Luna provided intelligence about the city's layout, describing the kill box (lawless slums), sovereign's watch (policed wealthy area), grit forge (craftsmen), and the intersector (palace area).
  - Characters: Connor, Luna, Nomkath
  - Location: Foundry Tavern, Torch

- Connor explained that entering Starfall requires 10 gold per person, and all technology must be cataloged and sold to the Technic League within one hour or be considered illegal contraband.
  - Characters: Connor
  - Location: Foundry Tavern, Torch

- The party decided to bypass the gates entirely using Kroktah's wind walk spell, wearing brown cloaks to blend in with Starfall's heavy pollution and smog.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Nomkath
  - Location: Foundry Tavern, Torch

- Connor warned that Technic League captains can cast detect technology and anti-tech field, and that impersonating the Technic League would be very difficult for anyone except Olbryn.
  - Characters: Connor, Olbryn
  - Location: Foundry Tavern, Torch

- Casandalee hacked into a captured Technic League spy drone that had been surveilling Torch for three days. The drone reported to a Technic League captain named Ghartone and had classified multiple party members as level 5 (maximum) priority targets.
  - Characters: Casandalee, Nomkath
  - Location: The Nomad (truck), Torch

- The drone's deleted logs revealed Scrapwall's garrison consisted of only 10 Technic League agents, 4 Borg soldiers, and 15 Black Sovereign troopers, with all gearsmen recalled to Starfall.
  - Characters: Casandalee
  - Location: The Nomad (truck), Torch

- The party chose maximum subterfuge for the drone: removing all evidence of their presence in Torch and letting it report nothing of interest, rather than planting false information that could be detected.
  - Characters: Casandalee, Nomkath
  - Location: The Nomad (truck), Torch

- Meyanda upgraded Tokala's chainsaw (Yagadar Mourn) with a larger speaker system including a subwoofer, adding a bonus to dazzling display against targets that can hear it.
  - Characters: Meyanda, Tokala
  - Location: Torch

- Kroktah spent private time with Casandalee using the VR helmet to experience virtual Kasath memories, with Casandalee acting as a personal GM for the scenarios.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Casandalee
  - Location: The Nomad (truck), Torch

- The truck (Nomad) was moved to Overth's warehouse for storage, repairs, and upgrades while the party was in Starfall. Casandalee trickle-charged off the truck's transceiver to reach 490/1000 charges.
  - Characters: Casandalee, Meyanda, Dinvaya
  - Location: Overth's Warehouse, Torch
