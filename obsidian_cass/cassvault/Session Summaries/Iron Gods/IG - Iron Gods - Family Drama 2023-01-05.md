## Summary

The party returns to Torch after months in the Scar of the Spider and learns that Scrapwall has been taken by the Technic League. An assassin group calling themselves the 'Sons of Cortez' attacks the town, seeking McGinnis for family revenge. After a brutal street battle, McGinnis discovers his aunt was killed and left with a letter revealing their family's vendetta against an Alkenstrar named Cortez (alias Sanchez) who murdered McGinnis's grandfather. McGinnis departs to handle this personal matter, leaving the party to their own plans against the Technic League. Transcript quality was poor due to auto-captioning errors.
