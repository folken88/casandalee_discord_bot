# Iron Gods - An Exchange of Ideas

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=d--4ESwuQoU)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Maginnis, Tokala
**Absent:** Josh (Olbryn)

## Summary

Continuing exploration of the meat pile dungeon interior. [[Isuma]] enters the meat pile and immediately senses the Dominion's presence, confirming they are responsible for this creature. The party fights through rooms containing armored horseshoe crab-like floating creatures (Dominion drones with laser pistols and anti-gravity) and a massive armored green crab creature (Neh-Thalggu/brain collector) that uses confusion spells and has brains of captured sentients mounted in glass domes on its back. [[Isuma]] proves devastatingly effective against Dominion creatures as her favored enemy. [[Kroktah]] uses her Dispel Hostility ability to remove confusion from [[Isuma]] and later [[Nomkath]], establishing this as a critical counter to Dominion mental attacks. The Neh-Thalggu's brain collection is revealed to contain human, half-orc, and Kasatha brains being used to empower the creature. The starry floor contains a vision of a metal object held by tentacles in deep space below the translucent surface. [[Ulfred]] provides communal protection from energy (fire) making the party largely immune to laser weapons.
