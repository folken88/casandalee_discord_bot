# Iron Gods - Revenge of Soup Bitch

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=u10kziD-HQE)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Tokala (Rye), Nomkath (Daemon), Eldrin (Enrique), Ulfred (Kip), Maginnis (NPC)

## Summary

The party explores an underground facility beneath Iadenveigh, fighting through deformed androids, "hungry flesh" tentacle monsters in pipes connected to an android creation foundry, and a deadly android gunslinger/magus named Alaris (referred to as "soup bitch" / "soup lady" from town). Eldrin discovers a surveillance recording of a wizard named Xoud who visited the facility seven years ago with an invisible servant named Sasho. The android foundry has been creating malformed androids for years. Tokala receives a divine vision of a warrior fighting gearsmen. The session's climax is a brutal multi-round battle against Alaris, who proves to be an incredibly skilled combatant with pistols and a sword, dropping multiple party members before Maginnis delivers the killing headshot while prone. Afterward, Ulfred uses faerie fire to reveal Alaris when she turns invisible. The party barely survives.
