## Summary

The party concluded their doppelganger fight in the Dominion mind-space, solved memory-based puzzles to unlock a vault, acquired several powerful magical items including the Space Bone, and defeated a massive Dominion creature (Dweller in Dark Places) in physical combat. Takala was disintegrated in the mindscape but revived, and the party narrowly survived friendly fire from a dominated Maginnis.
