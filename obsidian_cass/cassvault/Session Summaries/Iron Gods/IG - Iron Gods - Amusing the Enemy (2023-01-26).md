## Summary

The party prepares to depart Torch for Starfall after three weeks of rest and resupply. Casandalee intercepts a Technic League spy drone sent by Captain Ghartone and successfully alters its memory to mislead the League about the party's whereabouts and capabilities. The group acquires bags of holding for their equipment, plans to use Wind Walk for covert entry into Starfall, and learns about the city's layout and security measures from Connor and Luna. They enjoy a final meal at their manor before preparing for their assault on Starfall the next morning.
