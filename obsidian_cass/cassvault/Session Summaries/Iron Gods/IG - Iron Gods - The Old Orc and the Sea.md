## Summary

The party, freshly leveled to 10th, executed their plan to investigate the mysterious reddish glow in the river despite multiple warnings from NPCs. They buffed extensively (water breathing, stone skin, air walk, haste, greater invisibility, touch of the sea) and headed out onto the water. Tokala waded in as bait with his chainsaw in "mega noise mode" while others air-walked above. The noise attracted aggressive pike-like fish, which the party dispatched. A massive creature dubbed the "Godfish" — over 100 feet long with sword-sized teeth and a glowing red lure — surged up from the depths and bit Tokala, piercing his stone skin. After sustained damage from the party (chainsaw, hammer, daggers, inferno pistol, lightning bolts, spiritual ally), the Godfish fled into the depths, releasing an enormous ink cloud. The party dove to the river bottom and discovered the wreckage of a destroyed inflatable raft identical to theirs, confirming that Technic League operatives had perished here. They recovered a sonic disruption cannon, a spell prism necklace, a mithral chain shirt +1, Technic League pins, and a ruined spellbook. Kroktah spoke with fish to learn about the Godfish — a juvenile pike confirmed the creature is revered as "Godfish" and claims the entire river and lake as its territory.
