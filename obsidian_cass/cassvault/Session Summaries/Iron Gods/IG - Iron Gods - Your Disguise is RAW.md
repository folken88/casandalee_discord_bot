# Iron Gods - Your Disguise is RAW

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=qRlTifTcpSw)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The party continues Operation Healburger from Garton's lab in the Palace of Fallen Stars. Their stated goals are to find and co-opt Chef Gorgo and locate the numerian drug stash. They decide to search for the drug stash first since the kitchen location is already known. Mr Brow stays behind with a hangover. The party disguises themselves as barbarians using wands and spells (disguise other, alter self), with Luna using her own anonymity ability. They explore the second floor, locating the mess hall, Hall of History (statues), barracks, and harem. They plan to find where the drugs were supplied from, reasoning the Technic League was the source and Garton's lab area might have clues. Brent (Akradenn's player) fully joins the session. The party's approach shifts from pure stealth to bluff-based disguise infiltration since the barbarians appear to have no magical detection capabilities. Nomkath has a +21 bluff and +27 stealth. Luna makes a knowledge local check (19) to recall the palace layout from her time there 10 years ago, drawing a floor map with pink chemicals. The session is heavily focused on planning and reconnaissance.
