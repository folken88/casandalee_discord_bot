## Summary

The party infiltrates the Palace of Fallen Stars in Starfall disguised as barbarians. After successfully navigating multiple guard encounters through bluffing, they locate and steal the Black Sovereign's remaining drug stash from a hidden room. They then meet Gorgo Ransom, the legendary chef of Numeria, who reveals he is a catfolk like Nomkath and agrees to help them execute "Operation Heal Burger" – a plan to cure the Black Sovereign of his addiction to Numerian fluids by imbuing a specially prepared meal with a powerful healing spell.
