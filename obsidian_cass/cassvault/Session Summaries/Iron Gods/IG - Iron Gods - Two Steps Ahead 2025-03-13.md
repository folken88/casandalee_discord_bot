# Iron Gods - Two Steps Ahead 2025-03-13

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=yu7L1splrog)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Casandalee, Meyanda, Mockery

## Summary

The party harvested meat from the killed Juvi-Tarrasque in the desert but were spotted and attacked by a warden drone and artillery from Silver Mount. After a chaotic escape involving failed teleports, they regrouped in Starfall's laboratory. They delivered the meat to the Black Sovereign's chef, who will prepare healing burgers for a banquet tomorrow. The party then conducted a brutal pre-dawn raid on the Black Sovereign's seers, killing all seven or eight of them with silent assassinations and magic before they could interfere with the plan.
