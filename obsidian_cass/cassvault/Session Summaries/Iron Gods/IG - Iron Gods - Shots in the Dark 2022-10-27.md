## Summary

The party encounters a large dragon in the Dominion facility, which initially appears to offer an alliance against the Dominion's leadership. After brief negotiations, combat erupts. The dragon is revealed to be inhabited by an intellect devourer (a Dominion brain creature). Despite the creature's formidable defenses, magical protections, and mirror images, the party systematically defeats it through coordinated firepower and melee attacks. Afterward, the party discusses a mysterious female presence felt during combat and begins investigating a data pad found in the area, speculating it may be connected to Casandalee.
