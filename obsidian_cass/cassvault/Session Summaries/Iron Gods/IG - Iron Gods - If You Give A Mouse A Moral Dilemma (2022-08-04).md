# Iron Gods - If You Give A Mouse A Moral Dilemma (2022-08-04)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=cH-wOnJ6Xq8)

## Summary

The party delivers corpses to Binox in exchange for deactivating Tokala's tracker. Alfred receives extensive cybernetic enhancements (echo locators and wire jack tendons) from Binox. A significant moral debate erupts over keeping the brain in a jar captive, with Croctaw strongly opposing its continued imprisonment while others argue for its tactical value. The party prepares to assault the Dominion hive, planning to use Croctaw's wind walk spell for rapid deployment into enemy territory.
