# Iron Gods - If You Give A Mouse A Moral Dilemma (2022-08-04)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=cH-wOnJ6Xq8)

**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Tokala, Akradenn, Olbryn, Luna

## Summary

The party retrieved a vehicle from the 'meat pile' and drove to Binox's lair to complete a trade deal: four dominion corpses in exchange for deactivating a tracker on Tokala. During their visit, Ulfred underwent extensive cybernetic surgery with Binox, receiving echolocators, wire jack tendons, and experimental leg thrusters. The party debated the morality of keeping the brain in a jar alive, with significant disagreement about torture and pragmatism. They plan to approach the Dominion hive using Wind Walk spells for maximum speed and stealth.
