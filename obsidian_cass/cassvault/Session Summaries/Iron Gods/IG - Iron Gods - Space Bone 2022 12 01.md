## Summary

The party battles through a Dominion mindscape where they encounter and defeat mental copies of themselves, solve memory-based puzzles tied to various skills and knowledge, and discover powerful magical items in a vault. Upon escaping the mindscape, they immediately confront the Dweller in Dark Places—a massive, brainlike Dominion creature—in the physical world, ultimately defeating it after a brutal fight. They discover two exotic weapons: the Space Bone and the Void Shard Ax.
