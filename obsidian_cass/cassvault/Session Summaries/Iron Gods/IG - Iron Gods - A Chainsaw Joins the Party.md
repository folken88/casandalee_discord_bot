# Iron Gods - A Chainsaw Joins the Party

## Summary

The party's extended stay in Torch concludes with several major events. Tokala has a deeply personal conversation with Meyanda about divine connection and Hellion's lingering influence. Sandbill (Garmen Ulreth's former associate) is publicly hanged for his crimes. Olbryn's sister Cuha and Ola arrive from Scrapwall and settle into the party's manor house. Dinvaya and Meyanda complete Tokala's commissioned weapon -- Yagadar Mondin, a +2 cruel adamantine chainsaw incorporating pieces of Dolga's greatsword, Kulgara's chainsaw, and new adamantine teeth. The weapon features blood shields, a biofuel engine requiring organic matter, multiple power modes, and a dramatic "arena entrance" sound mode designed by Meyanda. The party purchases healing wands, then departs Torch heading southwest toward Iadenveigh, traveling past the tar pits and along the river to the Lockstar Hotel, a frontier inn run by a dwarf named Trucknoll Thunderspark. They gather intelligence about Iadenveigh from passing traders, who describe it as a strict, insular community with strange-looking townfolk and serious law enforcement.

## Key Events

### Tokala's Conversation with Meyanda
- **Characters:** Tokala, Meyanda
- **Location:** Torch jail
- Tokala asks Meyanda about Casandalee and Hellion's visions in his mind
- Meyanda describes her own divine awakening: born in fire, smashing out of a glass tube, killing bandits with a silver pistol provided by Hellion, choosing violence in that moment as what drew Hellion to her
- She tells Tokala that divine connection comes from choices, not study -- his connection to Gorum came from choosing to fight rather than flee
- She suggests Hellion's residual influence has scrambled his mind, making it difficult to focus, but he will heal in time
- Tokala thanks her and says she helped him far more than Joram did
- Meyanda apologizes for Dolga's death in their earlier battle; Tokala says Dolga would not hold it against her

### Sandbill's Hanging
- **Characters:** Connor, Sandbill, townsfolk
- **Location:** Torch, public square
- Connor addresses the crowd: this is justice and law, not revenge
- Sandbill is hanged; the town turns out to watch
- Maginnis discusses rope length technicalities with the hangman

### Cuha and Ola Arrive
- **Characters:** Olbryn, Cuha, Ola
- **Location:** Torch, Ulreth Manor
- Olbryn sent word to Scrapwall; Cuha and Ola arrive by caravan
- They are amazed by the manor house and its butler Reginald
- The town stares at three drow together but treats them well
- Cuha reports that Chug has been relocated to the Pit tavern as bartender

### Yagadar Mondin Unveiled
- **Characters:** Tokala, Dinvaya, Meyanda, Ulfred, Connor, Joram
- **Location:** Dolga's Forge, Torch
- **Timeline:** Correlates to party's extended stay in Torch, before departure to Iadenveigh
- Dinvaya and Meyanda (brought in chains from jail to assist) have transformed the forge with cables, screens, and equipment
- Meyanda contributed mechanical design; Joram installed a toaster using the equipment
- Ulfred presents the weapon to Tokala wrapped in blue cloth
- The weapon: +2 cruel adamantine two-handed chainsaw, incorporating Kulgara's chainsaw and Dolga's greatsword
- Features: adamantine teeth (mirror-sharp, cut through steel like kindling), blood shields/gutters to channel spray forward, biofuel engine requiring organic matter (potato-sized amount per several charges), multiple grip positions, dwarven runes from original sword
- Three modes: low (tool/precision), normal (combat), and a third mode with arena entrance sounds loaded by Meyanda
- When activated on max mode, the weapon is deafeningly loud; -10 to stealth for the entire party
- Dinvaya named it Yagadar Mondin ("Stare to the Top of the Mountain" in Dwarven) to honor Dolga
- If sold in Absalom, it would command approximately 30,000 gold

### Shopping in Torch
- **Characters:** Olbryn, full party
- **Location:** Torch, Temple of Brigh shop
- Olbryn purchases: wand of cure light wounds (600g, 50 charges), wand of infernal healing (600g, 50 charges)
- Maginnis purchases: wand of infernal healing (600g)
- Olbryn attempts to find an efficient quiver but fails the availability roll (98 on d100, needed 1-50)

### Departure from Torch
- **Characters:** Full party
- **Location:** Torch to Lockstar Hotel
- Reginald loads the wagon with trail rations, water, coffee pot, tents, and extra bedrolls
- Meyanda charges three dead batteries using the power transfer unit, drawing from the Torch reactor without turning it off
- Party heads southwest, avoiding the Choking Tower forest and veggie pygmy swamps
- Maginnis navigates (survival check 22) choosing the northern route past the tar pits, following the river south to the Lockstar Hotel

### Lockstar Hotel
- **Characters:** Full party, Trucknoll Thunderspark, traveling merchants
- **Location:** Lockstar Hotel, frontier inn on the plains near the river
- Run by a dwarf named Trucknoll Thunderspark; a surprisingly nice one-building inn
- The party rents rooms: Nomkath and Maginnis share a double; Olbryn and Eldrin take two twins; Tokala and Ulfred take the family room
- A large pile of supplies sits outside -- trail rations, cloth, copper and iron bars; Trucknoll is evasive about whose they are ("transactions")
- Nomkath sneaks out to investigate but is spotted by Trucknoll; sees only basic wilderness supplies
- Trucknoll teaches Ulfred proper regional dwarven dialect, starting with expletives
- Ulfred passes a fort save against powerful dwarven liquor
- Traveling merchants share intel about Iadenveigh: strict laws, ranger-type law enforcement, a tough chief who meets every traveler, strange-looking townfolk ("reading amongst themselves too much"), but fair and safe

### Iadenveigh Intelligence
- **Characters:** Maginnis, Nomkath, Eldrin, merchants
- **Location:** Lockstar Hotel tavern
- Merchants came through Iadenveigh recently, heading to Torch
- They confirm the town is strict but fair; the chief meets every traveler personally
- The townfolk are described as strange-looking -- possibly inbred
- The party advises the merchants on avoiding veggie pygmies: stay in sunny areas, avoid dark and damp places, camp away from green growth
