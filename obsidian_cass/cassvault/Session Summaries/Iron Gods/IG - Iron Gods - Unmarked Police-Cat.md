# Iron Gods - Unmarked Police-Cat

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=qPTYLGvJAzI)

**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Luna, Tokala, Casandalee, Mockery

## Summary

With two days before the underground night market, the party split up for downtime activities in Starfall. Tokala, Nomkath, and Luna visited the Slot Bag casino where Luna won 420 gold on the slot machines. Kroktah and Ulfred scouted the northern Killbox, visiting the Scrap Hole junkyard where they learned that Technic League Captain Krastus had returned from Scrapwall with salvaged metal and equipment. Nomkath disguised himself as a city guard using a copycat wand and conducted reconnaissance on the east side of Starfall, learning names and descriptions of several Technic League captains including Ozmyn Zaidow, Zernabith, Krastus, Garton, The Shade, and Elias. Casandalee warned Nomkath about nearby robots and shut down her sensors to avoid detection. The session ended with the party descending into the underground market tunnels, where Tokala consumed an "orange bag" of Numerian fluid and passed out at the entrance to the drug shop.
