# Iron Gods - Battle for Scrapwall

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=AptkPJbY8z8)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Maginnis

## Summary

A special interlude session described as "The Battle for Scrapwall." The party defended the Scrapwall gates against a Technic League assault force consisting of gearsmen, two massive juggernauts (22-foot-tall gearsmen), invisible soldiers, and a flying spellcaster commander. [[Sevroth]] and the Silverhawks fought alongside the party. [[Olbryn]]/Tatia provided telepathic bond and haste buffs while flying invisibly. [[Maginnis]] and Silverhawk shooters manned the walls with cannons and muskets. [[Kroktah]] drank Numerian fluids, grew a third arm, and wielded a massive steel beam from the Scrap heaps. [[Nomkath]] used thermal sight and See Invisible to spot flanking forces. The Technic League sent invisible squads with troopers, a flying caster with mirror image, and the juggernauts to breach the gate. Toby noted the outcome would be canon regardless of result and that Harrison ran the enemies.
