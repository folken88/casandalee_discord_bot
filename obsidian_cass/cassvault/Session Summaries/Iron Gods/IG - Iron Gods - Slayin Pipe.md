# Iron Gods - Slayin Pipe

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=lbMVkD3t-q0)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Nomkath, Olbryn, Ulfred, Kroktah, Blackout, Luna, Casandalee

## Summary

The party establishes a permanent safe house in Starfall by purchasing a scroll of Mage's Private Sanctum from [[Mockery]], scouting the sewers beneath the Grit Forge neighborhood, and discovering an abandoned pipe factory called "Slayin' Pipe." Olbryn casts Permanency on the Mage's Private Sanctum in the sewer junction beneath the factory. The party then wind walks to Scrapwall to retrieve Rust-Risen corpse parts for framing a future attack on Technic League Captain Abernathy. During a night watch, Blackout reveals to Olbryn that he is half-drow and shares that his patron is Zernabeth, a Technic League captain who wants to seize power from Ozmyn Zaidow.
