# Iron Gods - Fire Husbando

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=anEVQ8AkeH0)
**Transcript Quality:** fair

## Summary

Following the successful defense of Elias's fortress, the party debriefs with Acradenn and Elias about strategy. The two Technic League captains confirm they will side with Zerth against Ozmyn, and advise the party to target Gartone next since he is Ozmyn's most loyal supporter and the primary source of the drug used to control the Black Sovereign. The party discusses how to eventually cure the Black Sovereign and returns to the Sewer Sanctum to rest. Nomkath scouts Gartone's safe house location. The party then assaults Gartone's safe house with full buffs, overwhelming Gartone himself in a very quick fight. After the kill, they explore the facility, discovering locked doors, a hidden power battery room, and a magically sealed chamber containing a tentacle-faced creature resembling something from the Dominion of the Black.
