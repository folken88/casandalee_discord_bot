# Iron Gods - Just the Tip 2022-07-07

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=5KWeM3BKhFQ)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Nomkath, Ulfred, Croktah, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The party camps near the Valley of the Brain Collectors and establishes a teleportation circle for future escapes. After moral debates about holding a brain captive, they scout the mysterious meat pile—a massive 400x200 foot mound of dead alien flesh with strange appendages. Upon closer investigation, they discover it's actually a living creature with internal chambers; entering through a sphincter, they fight swarms of flesh-eating maggots and destroy them with elemental magic and explosives.

## Key Events

- Party establishes a dark purple teleportation circle at their campsite in the Valley of the Brain Collectors, costing 1000 gold in materials. The circle will be hidden by druid-cultivated plant growth.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Ulfred, Croktah, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna
  - Location: Valley of the Brain Collectors
- Party travels by boat upriver toward the meat pile location. They observe the massive flesh mound from a distance—approximately 400 feet wide and 200 feet high, composed entirely of gray fleshy matter with pustules, tentacles, and organ-like appendages.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Ulfred, Croktah, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna, Casandalee
  - Location: The Scar of the Spider (meat pile location)
- Upon reaching the meat pile and testing it with electrical damage, the party discovers it emits strong magical signatures and shows signs of being a living creature, not merely carrion. Tentacle marks and chemical burns on the surrounding landscape indicate it fell or landed at this location.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Ulfred, Croktah, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna
  - Location: The Scar of the Spider (meat pile location)
- Party enters the creature's interior through a sphincter opening. They encounter multiple swarms of flesh-eating maggots (rot grubs) that infest victims and deal Constitution damage. Combat ensues with extensive use of fire and lightning magic.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Ulfred, Croktah, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna
  - Location: Interior chamber of the meat pile creature
- Party destroys all hostile rot grub swarms using area-of-effect spells (lightning bolt, wall of fire, burning sands, aqueous orb with lightning) and explosives. The party discovers the creature's interior contains a gymnasium-mat-like floor with glitter-like reflective specks.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Ulfred, Croktah, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna
  - Location: Interior chamber of the meat pile creature
- Party discovers a second sphincter-like opening with a socket mechanism recessed in the wall (described as resembling a locking mechanism). They begin investigating how to open it.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Ulfred, Croktah, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna
  - Location: Interior chamber of the meat pile creature
