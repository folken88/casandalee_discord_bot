## Summary

The party establishes a safe camp in the Valley of the Brain Collectors and constructs a teleportation circle for future escapes. After extensive moral discussions about keeping a captive brain and wielding power responsibly, they journey to investigate a massive mound of flesh on a beach. Upon closer inspection, they discover the mound is a living (or recently living) creature filled with flesh-eating maggot swarms. The session culminates in a prolonged combat encounter inside the creature's body, with the party fighting multiple swarms of parasitic grubs while exploring what appears to be an organic structure with sphincter-like passages and mysterious locking mechanisms.
