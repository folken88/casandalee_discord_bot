# Iron Gods - Choking Tower Shut Down (2021-12-02)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=FodMUawDJ-4)

## Summary

The party explored the lower levels of the Choking Tower, discovering Casandalee's android corpse and learning more about the tower's systems. They encountered and defeated a worm that walks (a dangerous swarm creature), freed imprisoned fire elementals called azares who were trapped working in the tower's forge, and gathered intelligence about remaining threats including a strange robot upstairs. The party is preparing to destroy the tower with the azares' help.
