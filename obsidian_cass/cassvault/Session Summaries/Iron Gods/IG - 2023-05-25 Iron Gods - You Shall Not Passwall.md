## Summary

The party launched a nighttime assault on a Technic League Captain's house in Starfall, using wind walk to bypass defenses. After breaching through the sewer pipes and kitchen, they faced waves of gearsmen and robots. Kroktah's polymorph ability devastated the mechanical enemies, while Luna and the party brutalized the Captain (Abernathy) in combat. The Captain attempted to escape via pass wall but was intercepted and killed by Luna mid-flight. The house was largely destroyed, the Juggernaut detonated with planted evidence, and the party escaped through the sewers with loot and the Captain's corpse.
