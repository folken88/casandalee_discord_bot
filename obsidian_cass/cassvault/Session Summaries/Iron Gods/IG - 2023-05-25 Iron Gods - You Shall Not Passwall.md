## Summary

The party conducts a nighttime assault on a Technic League Captain's residence in Starfall. After discovering sewer pipes lead to the house, they infiltrate via gaseous form but trigger defensive mechanisms. The resulting combat sees party members transform enemies to wood, trigger a massive tornado spell in the neighborhood, and ultimately kill the Captain (revealed to be a disguised cyborg named Abernathy). A Juggernaut ally assists the party, and they escape via the sewers before the Captain's robot body detonates.
