# Iron Gods Session - April 2021

## Summary

The party continues exploring Hellion's underground facility beneath Scrapwall. They fight through clerics' barracks, encounter an ooze in a waste disposal pit, and descend to a lower level where they battle robot sentries buffed by Hellion. Eldrin inserts a glowing crystal into a central computer terminal, activating an "AI Memory Inhibitor" that briefly disrupts Hellion's control over the facility. Hellion threatens the party through the screens. The session culminates in a major battle when Kulgara, a massive armored orc warrior wielding a chainsaw, arrives with a squad of cyborgs. During the fight, Kulgara nearly destroys Tokala's greatsword (Dolga's blade) with her chainsaw, and a small rogue-like figure attempts a flanking attack but is intercepted by Nomkath.
