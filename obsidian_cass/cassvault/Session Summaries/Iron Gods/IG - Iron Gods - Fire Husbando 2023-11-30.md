## Summary

The party meets with Technic League Captains Acradenn and Elias to plan their next moves against Ozmyn and the Black Sovereign. After learning that Ozmyn is under Unity's control, they decide to target Garton's secret safehouse in Northeastern Starfall. Nomkath scouts the facility, finding disabled cyborg monitors and no gears men presence. The party launches a coordinated assault on Garton's chemical laboratory, defeating two Borg Warriors and a mass of rust-risen before confronting Garton himself. Through an ambush using buffed haste and invisibility, Nomkath assassinates the 17th-level Alchemist Garton, with minimal resistance. The session ends as the party discovers mysterious cells with powerful warding magic, including a mind-attack suppression rune suggesting something dangerous is imprisoned nearby.
