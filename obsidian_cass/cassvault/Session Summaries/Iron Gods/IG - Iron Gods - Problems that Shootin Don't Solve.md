## Summary

The party defeats a greater nithalgoo in the Scar of the Spider's virtual realm, then discovers and activates a portal leading to another Dominion facility. They encounter and defeat a massive Glaurkite construct guarding a compact AI Core, which they discover contains Casandalee's consciousness. After extracting the AI device, they return to their campsite and begin interacting with Casandalee, learning she has been trapped and suffering but is now recovering her cognitive functions. The party levels up to 22.
