# Iron Gods - Underground Space Cave Jungle Adventure

## Summary

While staying in Torch, the party investigates reports of disappearances near the lake at the south end of town. After a fruitless overnight stakeout, they decide to revisit the Black Hill Caves beneath Torch. Swimming down through the underwater entrance, the party discovers that russet mold has massively spread throughout the cave system since their last visit, with multiple corpses -- including Torch city guards -- covered in it and burst open by veggie pygmies. Olbryn clears a path using acid splash while the group navigates through. They discover a massive, rapidly-grown tree blocking the shortcut passage, with stone shattered in the same manner as Gavrilo's floorboards. The party fights two groups of veggie pygmies in the underground habitat dome -- a 400-foot diameter cavern with simulated sky and a dense forest. Eldrin identifies the habitat as an artificial environment for studying creatures. The party pushes deeper into the complex, passing through control rooms and deactivated disease-cleansing chambers.

## Key Events

### Lake Investigation
- **Characters:** Nomkath, Maginnis, Eldrin, Tokala, Ulfred, Olbryn
- **Location:** Torch, southern lake shore
- Nomkath finds repeated footpaths belonging to a local homeless man
- Near the shore, they find drag marks -- long finger-like claw marks leading into the water
- Eldrin acts as bait overnight, walking back and forth by the shore with cologne on while Maginnis posts up with a sniper rifle
- Nothing appears; the night passes uneventfully

### Tokala's Divine Roulette
- **Characters:** Tokala
- **Location:** Torch
- Tokala rolls for divine connection and connects to Brigh (goddess of invention, fire domain)
- He and Ulfred bond over shared connection to Brigh for the day

### Return to Black Hill Caves
- **Characters:** Full party
- **Location:** Black Hill Caves, beneath Torch
- Ulfred casts water breathing (communal) for the party
- Tokala fails his swim check; Ulfred tows him through
- They enter through the underwater passage to the cave system

### Russet Mold Infestation
- **Characters:** Olbryn, Eldrin, full party
- **Location:** Black Hill Caves, beach and tunnels
- Massive russet mold growth has spread throughout the caves since the party's last visit
- Multiple corpses found: a horse with stab wounds and three burst-open eruptions, a bearded man seated against a wall with chest exploded outward, two Torch city guards, and other humanoid corpses
- Olbryn systematically clears paths using acid splash at maximum range (40 feet), avoiding spore range
- Eldrin identifies the mold as russet mold -- vulnerable to acid, alcohol, and remove disease; sunlight only slows it
- Eldrin uses unseen servant to probe corpses, triggering spore clouds from a safe distance

### Massive Tree Discovery
- **Characters:** Eldrin, full party
- **Location:** Black Hill Caves, shortcut passage
- A massive tree has grown through the shortcut tunnel, filling it completely with roots and trunk
- Eldrin identifies it as a hybrid of multiple local tree species (cottonwood, oak, others) that grew impossibly fast -- days or weeks instead of years
- Stone around it is shattered in the same way as Gavrilo's floorboards -- shredded by unimaginable force
- The party connects this to Gavrilo's disappearance: the destruction pattern matches his house
- The tree does not react to touch; only the mold on it releases spores

### Habitat Dome Exploration
- **Characters:** Full party
- **Location:** Black Hill Caves, habitat module
- The party enters a 400-foot diameter circular cavern with simulated sky and a dense forest
- A massive tree grew up through a stone fountain in the center, roughly 80-100 feet tall, with bark indicating less than a year of age despite its enormous size
- Simulated daylight suppresses the russet mold within the habitat

### Veggie Pygmy Battles
- **Characters:** Nomkath, Maginnis, Tokala, Ulfred, Eldrin, Olbryn
- **Location:** Habitat dome
- **Combat 1:** Nomkath spots movement; Olbryn casts haste on the party. Veggie pygmies attack with spears and 15-foot extending finger-tentacles (a new ability not seen in previous encounters). Ulfred obliterates one with his hammer; Maginnis shoots one in the face; Nomkath and Tokala clean up the rest.
- **Combat 2:** A second group attacks near the south path. Party handles them easily -- Tokala charges and chops one in half, Ulfred kill-steals from Eldrin, Nomkath flanks and backstabs. The pygmies' finger-tentacles attempt grapples but cannot overcome the party's CMD.
- Eldrin enters melee combat with a short sword, surprising everyone

### Ancient Control Room
- **Characters:** Eldrin, full party
- **Location:** Black Hill Caves, control room south of habitat
- Screens display habitat data: humidity, temperature, sunrise/sunset in Androffan text
- Eldrin identifies the habitat as similar to unethical elven practices of capturing creatures in false environments for study
- A data jack port is found on a computer terminal; the party notes only Meyanda has a working data jack
- They recall Morgana previously plugged directly into this computer
- Deactivated disease-cleansing chambers are found but cannot be powered on from this location

## Uncertain Events

- **Gavrilo connection to trees:** The party strongly suspects the rapidly-growing trees and shattered stone in the caves are connected to Gavrilo's disappearance and destroyed house, but this is not confirmed. The transmutation aura at his house and the mutant tree growth may both relate to his alchemical experiments or russet mold infection.
- **Missing people:** More Torch citizens have gone missing than the party initially realized, including at least two city guards whose corpses are found in the caves. The full scope of disappearances is unclear.
- **Creature at the lake:** The drag marks at the lake shore remain unexplained. The overnight stakeout produced no encounter.
- **Seth/Skulks:** Maginnis recalls a village of bald, dark-skinned people (Skulks) to the northwest in the caves, led by someone named Seth. Their current status is unknown.
