# Iron Gods - Autonomy Threatened 2022 05 05

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=RuE2f07j7hg)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Tokala, Maginnis, Ulfred, Olbryn, Nomkath, Kroktah

## Summary

The party traveled north through the Scar of the Spider valley searching for a Kasatha woman rumored to live beyond the demon trees. During the night, a squad of female android combat robots ambushed the camp, knocking out Tokala, Maginnis, Ulfred, and Olbryn with stun pistols and non-lethal attacks. The robots classified each captive as "acceptable" but labeled Kroktah as "Kasatha - forbidden" and left her behind. Nomkath escaped using greater invisibility. Kroktah (in bird form) and Nomkath tracked the robots to a cave complex, where they encountered Binox, a large four-legged medical robot performing forced cybernetic enhancements. Tokala was surgically augmented against his will, receiving subdermal armor, enhanced strength, and a data jack port on the back of his neck. The party met Isuma, a Kasatha survivor who had lived alone in the valley for six years. Through diplomacy, Kroktah and Isuma negotiated with Binox for the captives' release in exchange for a promise to deliver four "specimens" (Dominion of the Black creatures) in the future.
