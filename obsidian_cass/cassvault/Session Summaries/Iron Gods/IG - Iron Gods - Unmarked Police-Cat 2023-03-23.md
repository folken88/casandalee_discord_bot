## Summary

The party spends downtime in Starfall City before attending an underground market. Ulfred and Crocodile scout the Scraphole and gather information about the Technic League, discovering Krastus's involvement in looting Scrapwall. Meanwhile, Nomkath conducts reconnaissance on the Technic League captains in disguise, Tokala and Luna gamble (with mixed results), and the party eventually makes their way to the night market entrance in the catacombs beneath the city, where Tokala mysteriously passes out upon arrival.
