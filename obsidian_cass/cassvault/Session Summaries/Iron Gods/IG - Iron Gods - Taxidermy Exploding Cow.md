# Iron Gods - Taxidermy Exploding Cow

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=KhN5sW1AWgE)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The party continues planning Operation Healburger from inside the Palace of Fallen Stars. Chef Gorgo explains the three-step plan: serve a light kale-and-turkey-hot-dog lunch to make the barbarians hungry, eliminate the Dark Seers (crones) so they cannot warn the Black Sovereign, then serve him a healburger made from his favorite meat -- the juvie terask. The party debates multiple plans to kill the crones (including filling the room with explosives, which Ulfred vetoes on structural grounds), ultimately settling on a silence-based assassination using Nomkath's scrolls. Akradenn arranges with the remaining Technic League captains to disable teleport wards on the palace. Nomkath locates a catfolk caravan in Starfall who report the juvie terask's last known location northeast of Starfall in an area cleared of giant wasps. The party researches the creature and learns it is colossal, magic resistant, immune to fire, and resistant to acid. After extensive debate about tactics (including the recurring "taxidermy exploding cow" bomb plan), the party decides to drive out in the truck and hunt it.
