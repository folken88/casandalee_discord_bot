## Summary

The party meets with Chef Gorgo in the Palace of Fallen Stars to plan the assassination of the Dark Seers (the Black Sovereign's oracle advisors) and the creation of a heal-burger to cure the Black Sovereign of his addiction to Numerian fluids. To make the plan work, they need to hunt a Juvi-Terask, a colossal beast in the northern wastes near Starfall. After gathering intelligence from Nomkath's kinfolk about the creature's location, the party spends the session planning their approach: they will use the truck with weapons, magical buffs, and ranged attacks to defeat the creature, then return with its meat for the chef to prepare.
