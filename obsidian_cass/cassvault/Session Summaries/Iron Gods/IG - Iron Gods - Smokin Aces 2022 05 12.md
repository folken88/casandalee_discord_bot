## Summary

The party recovers from a robot attack and meets Isuma, a Kasatha survivor of an ancient android ship. They negotiate with Binox and retrieve equipment including magical items. After learning about the threats in the valley from Isuma, they travel to a haunted peninsula ruin and encounter a Pale Stranger ghost—a gunslinger named Maginnis defeats it in a dramatic duel, obtaining sonic revolvers and other loot.
