# Iron Gods - Nothin Wrong With Me

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=CGtozHJN4X4)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Tokala, Luna

## Summary

After their escape from the Earth Daughters incident, the party regrouped in an abandoned block of the kill box and began establishing it as their base. They explored the surrounding area, learning about local gangs (the Junkers and the V's) and the general territory divisions. Nomkath scouted the populated streets in disguise while Kroktah surveyed in cockroach form. The party then focused on locating a Technic League numerian fluids operation near the Red River bar, with Nomkath and Luna staking out the location over 24 hours. Nomkath discovered a hidden entrance (DC 32 secret door) behind the Red River bar and the party prepared to raid it.

## Key Events

- The party established an abandoned block in the kill box as their safe house, confirming the surrounding perimeter was populated but the block itself was deserted and usable.

- The party learned about a faction called the Mockery from local contacts, who could potentially be allies against the Technic League.
  - Characters: Nomkath
  - Location: Kill box, Starfall

- Nomkath and Luna staked out the Red River bar area for over 24 hours, looking for evidence of a Technic League numerian fluids smuggling operation. Olbryn watched from across the street with his superior darkvision.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Luna, Olbryn
  - Location: Red River bar area, Starfall

- After 24+ hours of surveillance, the party observed a group of half a dozen large armored men enter the Red River bar, exit a couple minutes later, go into the alley behind it, disappear for 30 minutes, then leave.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Luna, Olbryn
  - Location: Red River bar, Starfall

- Nomkath crept into the back alley using a darkvision potion and discovered a super well-hidden secret door (DC 32) at the bottom of a metal storage shed behind the Red River. He chose not to open it to avoid triggering alerts.
  - Characters: Nomkath
  - Location: Behind Red River bar, Starfall

- The party regrouped and decided to raid the underground operation that same night rather than wait. Tokala was given the nagadashi (large curved blade) as a walking-around weapon to avoid carrying conspicuous tech.
  - Characters: All
  - Location: Safe house, kill box, Starfall
