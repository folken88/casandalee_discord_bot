# Iron Gods - Battle for Scrapwall (2022-05-19)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=AptkPJbY8z8)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Andreas, Akradenn, Algernon, Vigor, Tatia, Rock, Frog, Sevroth

## Summary

The IG Party defends Scrapwall's gates against a major assault by Technic League forces. Multiple waves of gearsmen, troopers, juggernauts, and specialized units attack while flying enemies approach from above. The party successfully repels most ground forces through coordinated combat, magical support, and clever tactics, though the battle remains ongoing with aerial threats still pending.

  - Characters: Vigor
  - Location: Eastern tower

  - Characters: Vigor, Tatia, Josh (through prophecy)
  - Location: Eastern scrapwall junk heap
- Algernon (beast morph alchemist with three-arm mutation, fly wings, golden claws) engages in melee. He creates a 5-foot pit beneath one trooper using unknown spell. Algernon kills a repair drone and a trapped trooper with devastating full-attack damage.
  - Characters: Algernon
  - Location: Scrapwall courtyard and gates
- One juggernaut lifts the portcullis with its massive strength. Ulfred's stone wall behind it is partially breached. Andreas guns down the already-injured juggernaut; it locks in place holding the gate half-open.

  - Location: Scrapwall main gate

  - Characters: Sevroth
  - Location: Scrapwall gates exterior

  - Characters: Juggernaut, Silverhawks
  - Location: Scrapwall courtyard

  - Characters: Akradenn
  - Location: Scrapwall gates and exterior
- Tatia dominates a cyborg/borg operative (Fortitude save DC 23). The borg, now controlled by the party, is positioned to attack the remaining juggernaut.
  - Characters: Tatia

  - Location: Above Scrapwall (far distance)
- Multiple repair drones appear mid-battle. Algernon destroys one by shattering its fan blades. Additional repair drones attempt to heal damaged gearsmen but are targeted and destroyed by party.
  - Characters: Algernon, Party
