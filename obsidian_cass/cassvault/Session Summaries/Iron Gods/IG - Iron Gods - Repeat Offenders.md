# Iron Gods - Repeat Offenders

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=IhD-q8RPCkk)

## Summary

With both Dominion patrols eliminated, the party rested and prepared for the assault on the Dominion hive itself. During the night, [[Tokala]] and [[Nomkath]] had vivid dreams of hiding in a forest from searching forces -- their subconscious minds instinctively shielding them from Dominion mental probes. [[Kroktah]] cast dream feast on [[Tokala]]. The party decided to drive the Nomad directly to the hive entrance rather than sneak, heavily buffing beforehand with see invisibility, fly, blur, fire resistance, haste, non-detection, feather step, and more. [[Zuma]] navigated with a survival check of 42, calculating their arrival time precisely. Upon arriving at the hive cave, the party encountered a massive Dominion robot resembling a larger version of Hellion's former annihilator chassis -- six legs, a rotating eight-barrel chain gun, and a glowing red eye. The robot ambushed them from a scrap pile and opened fire with a devastating area-effect bullet barrage, then trampled [[Nomkath]]. [[Zuma]] scored a critical hit but noted the robot's strong damage reduction. The session ended with combat ongoing against the robot guardian.
