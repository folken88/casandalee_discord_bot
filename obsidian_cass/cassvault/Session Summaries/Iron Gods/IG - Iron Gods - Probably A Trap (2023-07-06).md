# Iron Gods - Probably A Trap (2023-07-06)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=JSvPFBpm6P0)

## Summary

The party assaults a secret facility in Starfall where Meyanda's successor has been continuing the Rust-Risen research. After establishing communication infrastructure via Casandalee's tech stone and testing the Palace barrier (discovering the officer's badge triggers alarms), they launch a full frontal assault on the facility. The party destroys much of the structure and battles over 20 undead-construct hybrids while the enemy wizard (identified as Krastus) coordinates the defense from within. The session ends mid-combat with the party heavily engaged and Olbryn badly wounded.
