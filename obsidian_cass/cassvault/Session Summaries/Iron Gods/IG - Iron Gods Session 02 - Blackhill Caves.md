## Summary

The party continues exploring the Black Hill Caves beneath Torch. They investigate a gremlin burial ground filled with carefully arranged leaves, then discover a massive wall of glowkite (sky metal), an advanced alloy resembling titanium. Using swipe cards obtained from the Skulks, Morana opens an airlock-style door system into the Habitat module -- a sealed section of alien technology with crushingly thick metal walls and airlock doors that cannot be open simultaneously. Inside, the party fights repair drones (floating white boxes that fire nets and ram), then enters a strange underground forest habitat with alien dirt, foreign trees, no wind, and a simulated night sky. They battle waves of four-armed skeletons (some displaying a rotating purple glow in their eye sockets) and a tentacled creature concealed in the rock wall. Badly wounded and low on spells and bombs, the party retreats through the airlock, speaks briefly with the Skulk leader Seph, retrieves the corpse of Parta (a dead adventurer), and swims back to the surface. They return to Val's tavern (the Foundry Tavern) in Torch, where a repair drone attacks Val at her quarters. Val destroys it with Shocking Grasp. The party reaches level 2 and plans to return to the caves the next day.

## Combat Encounters

1. **Two Repair Drones** (Habitat interior) - Floating white boxes with disc-shaped attachments. One fired a net that pinned Gavrilo to the wall; the other attempted to ram Tokala. Gavrilo hit one with a critical fire bomb. Val finished the first one. Tokala destroyed the second with his axes. The drones self-repair if left alone (one was fully restored when the party returned through the room).
2. **Four-Armed Skeletons, Wave 1** (Habitat forest) - Two skeletons with short swords charged the party. DR 5/bludgeoning. Some displayed purple glowing eye sockets (never more than one at a time). Dispatched by Tokala and Maginnis.
3. **Four-Armed Skeletons, Wave 2 + Tentacle Creature** (Habitat forest) - Large wave of skeletons attacked from multiple directions. Party retreated to cave entrance as chokepoint. A tentacled creature burst from the rock wall and attacked Morana (she escaped with Vanish). Morana cast Enlarge Person on Tokala. Extended combat with many misses on both sides. Maginnis was the most effective fighter with his pistol. Seph later confirmed the skeletons regenerate.
4. **Repair Drone** (Torch, Val's quarters) - A single drone attacked Val when she opened her quarters. It fired a net (missed) then rammed Maginnis, knocking him prone. Val destroyed it with Shocking Grasp.
