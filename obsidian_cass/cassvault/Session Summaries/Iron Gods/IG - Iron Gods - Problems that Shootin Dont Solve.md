**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6bg5MOpO_jM)

**Characters Present:** Nomkath, Ulfred, Olbryn, Takala, Akradenn, McGinnis, Asuma

## Summary

The party defeated a rare variant of nothalgú in the Scar of the Spider's virtual realm and physical space. After discovering an electrical organ and narrowly surviving its discharge (killing Akradenn), they harvested components and used jury-rigged electrical connections to partially unlock a portal. Passing through, they encountered a massive Glaukite construct guarding a compact AI core containing Casandalee's consciousness. After defeating the construct, they recovered the tablet and made contact with Casandalee, learning she has been trapped and conscious throughout. Transcript quality was poor.
