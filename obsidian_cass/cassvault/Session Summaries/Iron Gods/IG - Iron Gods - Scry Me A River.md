## Summary

The party spends a day gathering intelligence in Starfall while maintaining their secret hideout in the sewers. Crocodile scouts the Palace and discovers a magical alarm triggered by high-level spellcasters, while Nomkath and others meet with Mockery to discuss strategy. The party then begins an extended scrying operation on the mercenary Hearsick to locate him and learn what information he claims is worth 50,000 gold.

- Crocodile flies in insect form toward the Palace and triggers a magical alarm barrier several thousand feet away. The entire Palace immediately goes on defensive lockdown, closing doors and windows. The party learns the Palace has sophisticated detection wards keyed to magical power levels.

  - Location: Palace District, Starfall

  - Characters: Blackout, York (NPC)
  - Location: Mercenary Guild, Starfall

Cassandalee reports detecting strange signals on the Gearsmen frequency from buildings in the center of town, near where Nomkath crossed on the way to/from Mockery.

  - Characters: Crocodile
