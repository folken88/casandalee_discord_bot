# Iron Gods - Scry Me A River

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=o5R6oKDOJus)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Nomkath, Olbryn, Ulfred, Kroktah, Blackout, Luna, Casandalee

## Summary

A reconnaissance and intelligence-gathering session in Starfall. Kroktah scouts toward the Palace of Fallen Stars in cockroach form and triggers a magical alarm barrier around the palace district, causing the entire area to go on high alert. She observes the palace guard patterns and supply delivery procedures from outside the barrier. The party meets with Mockery, who warns them about the destruction they are causing and provides intelligence: the Technic League is hiding two captain deaths from the Black Sovereign's forces, the Black Sovereign has a stash of his addictive drug both in the palace and off-site, and guard patrols have been doubled at a specific facility in town. The party debates whether to trust Blackout with the secret of Casandalee's existence but ultimately decides against it after Kroktah and Luna vote no. Nomkath privately consults with Casandalee, who reveals that gearsmen are acting strangely due to Unity's increased influence, and she has detected unidentifiable signals on the gearsman frequency originating from somewhere northeast of the party's location. Blackout's cybernetic ears pick up unexplained music, which Casandalee had hacked into them.
