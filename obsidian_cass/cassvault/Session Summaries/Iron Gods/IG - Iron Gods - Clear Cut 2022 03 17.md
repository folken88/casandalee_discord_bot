## Summary

The party tracked a mysterious woodsman through the forest and discovered a campsite protected by giant wasps. After a brief combat where Croctaw summoned a devastating windstorm to scatter the insects, they continued tracking toward a cliff face. At the cliff, they encountered three aberrant creatures—tentacled, otherworldly beings from another plane that resembled tree-like shapes but were actually intelligent predators. These creatures fed on the party members by draining their life essence and charisma. The battle was brutal: Nomkath and Tokala were nearly killed and heavily drained of charisma, leaving them in despair. The party ultimately defeated all three creatures with fire damage and lightning magic, though not before sustaining serious casualties. After combat, Alfred used restoration magic (powered by a sacrificed tech flashlight to Brigh) to heal the charisma drain on Nomkath and Tokala.
