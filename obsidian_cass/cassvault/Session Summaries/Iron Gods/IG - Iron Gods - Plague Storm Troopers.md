## Summary

The party traveled back to Mad Peter's druid shop to purchase supplies, then headed south through the Scar of the Spider valley toward the Mi-Go fungal territory to investigate what the Technic League was searching for. Isuma guided them safely, avoiding demon trees and the Dominion detection line. The party learned about Mi-Go (intelligent fungus creatures immune to mind-affecting effects, resistant to electricity and fire) and Young Geeth ("demon trees" that are actually extraplanar monsters disguised as trees). Olbryn purchased Thunderchunk (a drug that gives +2 DC to verbal spells, +5 to speeches, and doubles range of voice attacks). The party entered the Mi-Go territory and engaged in combat against Mi-Go and their associated creatures, with the Mi-Go leader using confusion and plague-like abilities. The session featured extended combat with ability damage, blindness effects, and Ulfred tanking with a 35 AC from Iron Skin.
