## Summary

The party engaged in a complex multi-faction battle underground involving a Technic League captain and her bodyguards, a merchant, Scrapwall Orcs with earth witches, and eventually city guards. After defeating the captain and forcing the merchant to flee, the party escaped through sewers with Tokala and the Orcs. The chase continued onto the surface of Starfall where a pitched battle erupted in the city streets involving the fleeing merchant (the "soft man"), Starfall law enforcement, and the Scrapwall Orcs with their earth witches.
