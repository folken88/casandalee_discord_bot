## Summary

The party battled doppelgängers of themselves in a Dominion mind-space, solved memory-based puzzles to unlock a vault, acquired several powerful magical items including the Space Bone, and defeated a massive Dominion creature (Dweller in Dark Places) in physical combat. Tokala was disintegrated in the mindscape but revived, and the party narrowly survived friendly fire from a dominated McGinnis.
