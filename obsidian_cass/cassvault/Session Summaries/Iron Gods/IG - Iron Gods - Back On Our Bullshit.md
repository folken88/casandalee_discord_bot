# Iron Gods - Back On Our Bullshit

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=H8JeHHekNO4)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Tokala, Nomkath, Ulfred, Kroktah, Maginnis

## Summary

The party returns to Iadenveigh after the Choking Tower events, shops for supplies (wands of infernal healing, handy haversack, scroll of raise dead), then travels to Torch. In Torch they settle the Azers at the forge, give Reginald magical cookware and soup recipes, and take Kroktah to explore the underground Kasathan habitat beneath the town. After visiting Dolga's cairn, the party buys horses and departs west toward the Scar of the Spider. En route they discover a destroyed campsite where six experienced warriors were killed by a massive metallic insectoid creature using laser weapons. Josh was absent this session.

## Key Events

- Party returns to Iadenveigh, shops at the community store and the Nexus Market. Purchases include three wands of infernal healing, a handy haversack, a scroll of raise dead, and a ring of force shield for Kroktah.
  - Characters: Tokala, Nomkath, Ulfred, Kroktah, Maginnis
  - Location: Iadenveigh
- The Azers are settled at Dolga's Foundry in Torch where they begin crafting Nomkath's new armor. They are fascinated by the Torch flame but refuse to go underground due to fear of water.
  - Characters: Ulfred
  - Location: Torch
- Tokala gives Reginald the magical cookware and soup samples so he can recreate the recipes. Reginald will eventually have a shop in Torch selling magic soup at cost.
  - Characters: Tokala
  - Location: Torch
- Tokala discusses shared visions with Meyanda about Casandalee. Meyanda explains that Hellion was a direct descendant of Unity, while Casandalee was a prophet/priest of Unity who rebelled. Meyanda has become accepted in Torch and now carries a pistol given to her by Denver.
  - Characters: Tokala, Meyanda
  - Location: Torch
- Nomkath and Kroktah explore the underground habitat beneath Torch. Kroktah recognizes the environment as simulating her homeworld of Kasath and discovers the skeleton of Hetweth, a tribal leader she knew. When she consumes a piece of his preserved flesh in a kasathan ritual, she receives a vision of Hetweth watching helplessly through one-way glass as his brethren shamble as undead.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Kroktah
  - Location: Black Hill Caves (Kasathan Habitat)
- Kroktah is devastated to learn the facility has existed for thousands of years, meaning her entire civilization may be extinct. She retreats into the woods around Torch in animal form to grieve alone.
  - Characters: Kroktah
  - Location: Torch
- Tokala visits Dolga's cairn, pours out a drink for her, and reflects on her sacrifice. He experiences a spiritual connection, feeling warmth and reconnection to Gorum's ideals of glory and battle.
  - Characters: Tokala
  - Location: Torch (Graveyard)
- Maginnis and Meyanda bond over firearms. Meyanda reveals that Justice (Maginnis's gun) was "the gun that set me free" from Hellion's influence.
  - Characters: Maginnis, Meyanda
  - Location: Torch
- Nomkath's new armor is completed by Ulfred and the Azers -- a natural 20 craft check results in a perfectly fitted, silent, semi-reflective suit with fire resistance.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Ulfred
  - Location: Torch
- Party departs Torch with horses heading west toward the Scar of the Spider. Kroktah scouts from the air in bird form while Nomkath uses his telescope.
  - Characters: Tokala, Nomkath, Ulfred, Kroktah, Maginnis
  - Location: West of Torch
- During the night watch, Nomkath and Alburn both simultaneously feel a chill/shiver. Nomkath investigates and catches a faint smell of burning from the west, miles away.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Alburn
  - Location: West of Lockstar Hotel
- Kroktah uses Commune with Nature the next morning and confirms people were within nine miles during the night but are now gone.
  - Characters: Kroktah
  - Location: West of Lockstar Hotel
- Kroktah discovers a destroyed campsite while scouting. Six experienced warriors were killed: one body burned to ash with the skull cracked open and brain removed, another torn in half. A horse was impaled by a leg the size of a building's support beam. Evidence of high-intensity laser weapons and massive insectoid footprints.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Nomkath, Tokala, Ulfred
  - Location: Between Lockstar Hotel and Kura Town
- Nomkath's engineering check identifies blast marks as laser weapon damage. The creature attacked for less than a minute, killed or captured most of the warriors, and departed north-northwest.
  - Characters: Nomkath
  - Location: Between Lockstar Hotel and Kura Town
- The party recalls stories of giant robot scorpions in the region resembling Hellion's spider-like body but much larger. The GM reveals a sketch from Maginnis's sketchbook matching the creature description.
  - Characters: Tokala, Nomkath, Maginnis
  - Location: Between Lockstar Hotel and Kura Town
