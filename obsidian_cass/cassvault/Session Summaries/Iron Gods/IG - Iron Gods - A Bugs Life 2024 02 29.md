## Summary

The party defeats Osman, a powerful Technic League captain, in a brutal combat encounter inside the Starfall compound. After the battle, they discover that Unity has deployed hunter-killer drones that devastate the surface facility with napalm strikes. The party retreats underground to safety, secures loot from Technic League vaults, and eventually lays low in a hidden hospital provided by Zerth (the former Technic League leader). Coca, the party's druid, dies during combat and chooses to permanently depart to rejoin her ancestral kathani spirits rather than be resurrected.
