## Summary

The party successfully captures and interrogates a merchant in Starfall, discovering he was selling information to the Technic League. After a dramatic escape involving earthquake-induced tunnel collapses, they meet with a mysterious contact called 'Blackout,' a contractor offering to work with them against the Technic League. The session concludes with the party meeting Blackout at the Slot Bag casino, where Alfred and Luna both fall victim to poisoned 'Double Death Bomb' poutine fries.
