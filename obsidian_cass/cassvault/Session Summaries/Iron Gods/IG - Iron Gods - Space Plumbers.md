# Iron Gods - Space Plumbers

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=9MLxNaCqLTI)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Tokala (Rye), Nomkath (Daemon), Eldrin (Enrique), Ulfred (Kip), Maginnis (NPC)

## Summary

The party continues exploring the underground facility beneath Iadenveigh. They encounter Sasho, an air elemental bound by a planar contract to the deceased wizard Xoud, who has been guarding a control room for seven years waiting for a master who never returned. After Ulfred swears by Brigh to free him, Sasho reveals that Xoud took Casandalee's android body from the facility. Ulfred casts dismissal to send Sasho home to the Plane of Elemental Air. The party then discovers a nanotech laboratory and a chemical production chamber leaking mutagenic gas that has been contaminating Iadenveigh's water supply for years, causing the town's mutations and birth defects. Over several days, Ulfred neutralizes the poison gas while Eldrin repairs two comm sets and upgrades the arc rifle. The town of Iadenveigh reduces shop prices in gratitude. The party then departs toward the Smoke Wood forest to investigate a burning tower, encountering injured hill giants along the way.
