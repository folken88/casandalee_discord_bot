# Iron Gods - The Devil Wears Masterwork

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mZZ_a9Cy0X0)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Tokala, Nomkath, Ulfred, Kroktah, Maginnis, Olbryn

## Summary

The party continues west toward Kura Town and the Scar of the Spider. After debating whether to track the giant insectoid creature from the destroyed campsite, they press onward to Kura Town, only to find it devastated and abandoned. Kroktah scouts the fortress from the air and discovers undead half-orc warriors lurking in the shadows. The party investigates, determining the town was attacked five to six days ago by at least two giant insectoid creatures that smashed through the walls. A necromancer had moved in afterward and begun raising corpses. The party fights the necromancer and his undead, with Kroktah's Fire Snake dealing a record-setting 192 damage in a single cast. A tiefling mercenary named Hirsek appeared during combat, fighting alongside the party. Josh was absent this session; Olbryn was present but confused for much of the fight.
