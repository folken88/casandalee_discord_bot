# Iron Gods - Aldronard's Rave

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mp760pFKh2c)
**Transcript Quality:** good
**Characters Present:** Connor, Krosk, Val, Yen, Denver, Sauna, Meyanda, Mae Hart

## Summary

A side session run entirely by the GM depicting the NPC "B-team" (Connor, Krosk, Val, Yen, Denver, Sauna, Meyanda, and Mae Hart) assaulting Aldronard's Grave, which had been occupied by a tribe of manticore warriors and shamans. The party launched a coordinated night attack using invisibility, flight, and surprise to systematically overwhelm the defenders. After heavy combat the surviving manticore warriors surrendered or fled, and the fortress was retaken. Prisoners were brought back to Torch for interrogation.

## Key Events

- The B-team assaulted Aldronard's Grave at night, gaining a surprise round through stealth and invisibility. Connor scouted invisibly while Yen used greater invisibility to eliminate wall guards with scorching rays.
  - Characters: Connor, Krosk, Val, Yen, Denver, Sauna, Meyanda, Mae Hart
  - Location: Aldronard's Grave
- Connor cast Haste on the melee fighters (Krosk, Mae Hart, Meyanda) and later used Fire Snake to hit five enemies at once, dealing 34 fire damage to each.
  - Characters: Connor
  - Location: Aldronard's Grave
- Denver's spiritual weapon and musket fire proved devastating at range, including an 82-damage critical headshot that killed a warrior outright.
  - Characters: Denver
  - Location: Aldronard's Grave
- Mae Hart held the southern stairway, delivering massive vital strike crits (73 damage) to charging barbarians who triggered attacks of opportunity.
  - Characters: Mae Hart
  - Location: Aldronard's Grave
- Krosk served as the tank, absorbing dozens of arrows (mostly blocked by Stoneskin) while throwing his returning battle axe at distant enemies for kills.
  - Characters: Krosk
  - Location: Aldronard's Grave
- Sauna attempted diplomacy with the remaining defenders but the manticore shaman refused to surrender. She then charged into melee with smite evil.
  - Characters: Sauna
  - Location: Aldronard's Grave
- The last shaman eventually surrendered. All surviving enemies were taken prisoner and brought to Torch for interrogation about their Technic League connections.
  - Characters: Connor
  - Location: Aldronard's Grave
