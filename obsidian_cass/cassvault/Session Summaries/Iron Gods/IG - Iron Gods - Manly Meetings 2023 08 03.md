# Iron Gods - Manly Meetings 2023 08 03

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LMTbK84MUcI)

## Summary

The party reincarnates their fallen companion Blackout as a human after a costly battle. They acquire valuable loot from a dead Technic League captain (Krastus), sell items for funds, and meet with the informant Hearsick to purchase intelligence. The session concludes with the party preparing for and arriving at an underground robot fighting arena (Robo Rumble) to meet with Accra, a Technic League captain, where they plan to make wagers on robot matches.

- Blackout is reincarnated as a human via the reincarnate spell. The party gathers expensive oils/spell components (1000 GP value) to fund the casting. Blackout accepts the reincarnation and wakes in the sewer sanctum.

- The party negotiates the sale of loot with Hearsick for 44,600 gold pieces (split between fencing stolen gear and performing augmentation surgery on the mercenary Killinator). The meeting occurs via communicator at the Slot Bag tavern.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Nomkath, Luna, Blackout, Olbryn, Akradenn

  - Characters: Ulfred, Hearsick
  - Location: Bank, Starfall (then mentally via VR helmet connection)
- The party visits Robo Rumble, an underground robot fighting arena east of Starfall's palace. They are led by Hearsick and meet Accra, a Technic League Captain and renowned duelist, who invites them to place wagers on robot fights. Accra proposes a bet: if the party's chosen robot wins, Accra will hear their proposals; if Accra's robot wins, the party buys drinks all night.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Olbryn, Blackout, Luna, Nomkath, Akradenn, Hearsick, Accra
  - Location: Robo Rumble, Starfall (underground arena east of palace)
