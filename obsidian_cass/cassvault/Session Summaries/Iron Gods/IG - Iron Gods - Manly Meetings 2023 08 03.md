## Summary

The party reincarnates their fallen companion Blackout as a human, acquiring significant treasure from the defeated Captain Krastus. After liquidating assets and securing funds, they successfully negotiate with Hearsick, a mysterious informant, paying 44,600 gold for critical intelligence including a map of the Technic League compound and information about high-value targets. The session concludes with the party attending an underground robot fighting arena called Robo-Rumble, where they meet Captain Accra and prepare to place strategic bets on combat robots.
