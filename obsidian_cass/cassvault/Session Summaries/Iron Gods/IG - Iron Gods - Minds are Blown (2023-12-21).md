## Summary

The party infiltrated a Technic League facility in Starfall, discovered and freed several tortured captives (including Rag and a priestess of Sarenrae), and looted Captain Garon's body. After setting explosives that accidentally detonated prematurely, they coordinated with allies (Akradon, Elias, Mockery, and Tokala's forces) to launch a major assault on the main Technic League compound. With a dust storm providing cover and mayhem from allies creating a distraction, the party positioned themselves to assault the compound while Tokala's truck burst through the southeastern wall with reinforcements.
