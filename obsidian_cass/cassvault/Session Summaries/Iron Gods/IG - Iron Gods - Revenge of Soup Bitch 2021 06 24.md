## Summary

The party descended into an underground facility and encountered a nanoparticle swarm, deformed androids, carnivorous flesh creatures, and finally a skilled female android combatant (Alaris) who posed a serious threat to the entire party. Multiple characters were dropped to zero HP during combat before Mcginnis killed the android leader with a well-placed headshot. The party recovered significant loot including magical weapons and armor, and discovered evidence of a wizard named Xoud who visited the facility seven years prior.
