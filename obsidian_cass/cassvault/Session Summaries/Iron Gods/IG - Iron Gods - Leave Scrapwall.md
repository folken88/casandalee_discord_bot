# Iron Gods - Leave Scrapwall

## Summary

The party wraps up their time in Scrapwall, attending a memorial for Redtooth and Whiskifiss organized by the Ratfolk, then traveling to the Gorum spiritual site where Tokala teaches the former Hellion clerics the ways of Gorum. After leaving Scrapwall, the party travels five days back to Torch, where they are welcomed as heroes. The town council offers them Garmen Ulreth's manor house and a scroll of resurrection in lieu of 4,000 gold. Dinvaya sets up shop at Dolga's forge. The party sells a massive amount of loot, including a disguised Califaso signet ring (Ring of Sea Strider), netting roughly 17,370 gold total. They investigate Gavrilo's disappearance, finding his house destroyed from within -- floorboards shredded by incredible force, signs of a struggle, transmutation aura, and neighbors reporting arguments in two voices. The session ends with the party visiting Meyanda in the Torch jail, where she reveals key lore about Hellion, Unity, and Casandalee.

## Key Events

### Redtooth Memorial
- **Characters:** Tokala, Ulfred, Dinvaya, Cuha, Eldrin
- **Location:** Scrapwall, south of central junk pile
- The Ratfolk craft "junk flowers" as memorials for Redtooth and Whiskifiss
- Tokala attempts to make one but produces something phallic; the Ratfolk politely note it takes practice
- Cuha sings the funeral song adapted from the mass grave ceremony at Scrapwall Arena
- Tokala tells the Ratfolk they will always have the party's aid

### Gorum Spiritual Site
- **Characters:** Tokala, Eldrin, former Hellion clerics
- **Location:** Titan Helm campsite, eastern Scrapwall
- Tokala teaches eight to ten former Hellion clerics the prayers of Gorum
- Eldrin assists with knowledge despite being largely ignored by the orcs
- The clerics share their stories; Tokala teaches them about honorable combat
- Sigmund is remembered as a respected warrior who refused Hellion's teachings
- By morning, the clerics gain tentative connection to Gorum -- cantrip-level spells and first-level spells in the War domain

### Nomkath Advises the Orcs
- **Characters:** Nomkath
- **Location:** Gorum spiritual site
- Nomkath counsels the orcs on the tactic of letting enemies underestimate them
- Emphasizes that all of Scrapwall is now their clan to protect

### Departure from Scrapwall
- **Characters:** Full party, Sevroth
- **Location:** Scrapwall gates
- Sevroth shakes hands and says he will keep things together as long as he can
- Dinvaya agrees to travel with the party; Cuha and Ola stay at the Pit tavern
- Silver Hawks return to manning the gates and plan permanent power at the Pit

### Return to Torch
- **Characters:** Full party, Val Baine, Connor, Joram
- **Location:** Torch
- **Timeline:** Correlates to approximately 4716.09.16 per timeline
- The party arrives after five days of travel, recognized by townsfolk
- Val Baine greets them warmly; Joram and Dinvaya share a wordless reunion
- Rope Fist gang members look worried when they see the party
- Olbryn (drow) notes the lack of hostility toward him in Torch; Nomkath explains the town is used to strange things
- Connor reveals Garmen Ulreth's assets have been seized; his gangs disbanded or jailed

### Garmen Ulreth's Manor Offered
- **Characters:** Connor, full party
- **Location:** Torch, northwest hilltop
- The town offers Garmen Ulreth's manor house (worth 6-7,000 gold) plus a scroll of resurrection instead of the 4,000 gold reward
- The manor has multiple bedrooms, a dining hall, fireplaces, a sparring room, a library (full of romance novels that disgust Eldrin), and a pool
- Maginnis initially hesitant as he does not plan to stay, but the party accepts
- Olbryn secures jobs at the casino for Cuha and Ola as performers

### Loot Sale
- **Characters:** Nomkath, full party
- **Location:** Torch
- Major sales include: Shark Bite axe (4,000g), composite longbow +1 (2,000g), scatter suit, various masterwork weapons and armor, firearms, breastplate, heavy steel shield
- Dinvaya disguises the Califaso signet ring (Ring of Sea Strider) by removing the family sigil; Nomkath sells it for 7,000 gold
- Total party gold after all sales: approximately 17,370 gold, split six ways (~2,895 each)
- Gold and silver bars donated to Dinvaya as starting stock for the forge

### Gavrilo Investigation
- **Characters:** Tokala, Nomkath, Maginnis
- **Location:** Torch, craft district
- **Timeline:** Several days into their Torch stay
- Connor reports Gavrilo has not been seen in weeks
- His house: door smashed outward, furniture destroyed, floorboards shredded by incredible force, broken glass everywhere, faint transmutation aura
- All tools, books, and gear missing -- taken or carried away
- Neighbors report hearing arguments in two voices (one normal, one very deep) in a strange language, then silence
- Maginnis theorizes Gavrilo drank something that split his personality; party suspects connection to veggie pygmies/russet mold from earlier adventures
- No leads found; investigation left open

### Meyanda Interrogation
- **Characters:** Tokala, Nomkath, Maginnis, Meyanda
- **Location:** Torch jail, maximum security
- Connor reveals Meyanda began speaking recently after months of silence; she felt Hellion's hold vanish when he was destroyed
- Meyanda explains Hellion was a "copy" of his father Unity -- an immensely powerful AI/construct-god
- Unity sent an emissary (his "daughter" Casandalee) who betrayed him and stole something valuable
- Unity then created Hellion as another copy to retrieve Casandalee, but Hellion also rebelled
- Hellion believed Casandalee died southwest of Torch, near Iadenveigh
- Meyanda believes the Technic League are Unity's puppets, and that Unity controls them from within Silver Mount
- She describes annihilators -- building-sized scorpion robots, 9.5 times larger than Hellion's combat chassis
