## Summary

The party concludes their business in Starfall after dismantling the Technic League. The Black Sovereign is now firmly in control and the barbarians are unified. The group then travels to and infiltrates Silvermount (the crashed spaceship Divinity) through Entrance 40 using invisibility, silence, and teleportation to bypass robot guards. After entering the cargo bays, they encounter hostile combat-upgraded Roombas in a pristine maintenance area, which they defeat. They then discover an elevator shaft leading deeper into the ship with access to personnel areas and trains.
