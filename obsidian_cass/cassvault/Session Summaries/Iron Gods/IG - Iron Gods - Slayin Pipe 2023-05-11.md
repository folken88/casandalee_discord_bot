## Summary

The party acquires a scroll of Mage's Private Sanctum and establishes a hidden safehouse in an abandoned pipe factory's sewers beneath Starfall, with plans to use it as a secure teleportation point. They then travel via wind walk to Scrapwall, scout the Technic League garrison at the gates, investigate the destroyed Smilers' lab to gather remains of rust-risen creatures for framing purposes, and return to Starfall to prepare for an upcoming raid on a Technic League captain's residence.
