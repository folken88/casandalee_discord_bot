# Iron Gods - Choke on this Tower (2021-11-18)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=1Qjlfp3i8J4)

**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna, Tokala, McGinnis, Kroktah (new)

## Summary

The party descends into the basement levels of the Choking Tower, discovering multiple horrors including a permanently imprisoned leukodemon named Kavenchin, cells containing unnatural swarms of insects, a shrine to the evil god Zyphus (which they desecrate), and automated magical construct guardians protecting a mysterious laboratory. They defeat the constructs and prepare to enter 'Lab Zero,' a highly secured chamber containing advanced machinery.

## Key Events

- Party meets Kroktah (new character), a skeletal humanoid creature seeking a woman from his past. After initial suspicion, Ulfred determines he is sincere and he joins the party.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Kroktah, Party
  - Location: Choking Tower
  - Timeline: 4716.10.17 | Choking Tower | IG Party meets & recruits Kroktah
- Party unlocks an electrically-trapped door using a heralcom rod, which safely absorbs a massive (96 point) lightning discharge and triggers the door lock.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Party
  - Location: Choking Tower basement levels
- Party discovers a permanent summoning circle in a sealed chamber, containing Kavenchin, a leukodemon from Abaddon. The demon is imprisoned by complex rune work designed to contain (not protect against) something.
  - Characters: Party, Kavenchin
  - Location: Choking Tower basement - sealed chamber
- Party discovers a cell block containing multiple chambers. One chamber has a shattered window and is empty. Another southern chamber is carpeted with millions of small yellow worm-like pupae in a massive, unnatural swarm with no apparent food source.
  - Characters: Party, Kroktah
  - Location: Choking Tower basement - prison cells
- Party encounters an ooze shrine guardian (reverent to Zyphus, god of accidental death). They defeat it in combat and destroy the shrine, desecrating it. The destruction of an unholy weapon grants a full night's rest to divine casters (Tokala, Alfred, Croktah).
  - Characters: Tokala, Alfred, Croktah, Ulfred, Nomkath, Olbryn
  - Location: Choking Tower basement - shrine room
- Party defeats two magical construct robots (silver, four-armed, with integrated spell-like weapon systems) protecting a laboratory entrance marked 'Lab Zero - Technic Recovery.'
  - Characters: Tokala, Alfred, Olbryn, Ulfred, Nomkath, McGinnis
  - Location: Choking Tower basement - hallway to Lab Zero
- Party discovers the door to Lab Zero is a complex technological lock that can be opened with nanites or deep technological knowledge. They prepare to enter.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Party
  - Location: Choking Tower basement - Lab Zero entrance
- Party finds a corpse of a combat-experienced male human wearing advanced Numerian armor (labeled 'KILLA') with a high-tech thermal scope rifle, data jack, and various drugs. Body is dessicated and wind-blasted, estimated 8-10 years dead, cause of death: severe internal injuries/lung damage.
  - Characters: Party, Croktah
  - Location: Choking Tower basement - wind chamber
