# Iron Gods - Dim Lights Thick Smoke

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=PWISZr7yqWk)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Eldrin, Olbryn, Tokala, Maginnis

## Summary

The party battles Furkas Xoud's ghost on an upper floor of the Choking Tower. Xoud opens with a surprise round cloudkill, then follows with black tentacles and a lightning bolt. The party uses wind wall to create a safe breathing zone. Xoud possesses Maginnis via magic jar, turning the gunslinger's deadly accuracy against the party. Tokala is shot down by the possessed Maginnis. Ulfred's magic circle against evil ultimately forces the ghost out of Maginnis's body, and the party counterattacks with sneak attacks, spiritual weapon, and holy smite. Xoud retreats into the walls. The party withdraws downstairs to recuperate, carrying the badly wounded.
