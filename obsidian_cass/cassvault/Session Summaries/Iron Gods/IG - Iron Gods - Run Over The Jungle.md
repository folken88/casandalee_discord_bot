# Iron Gods - Run Over The Jungle

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fiwCvyObw54)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Tokala, Nomkath, Maginnis, Ulfred, Olbryn, Eldrin

## Summary

The party investigates disappearances near Iadenveigh's lake, using Eldrin as bait at the watershore overnight, but nothing takes the bait. Nomkath finds drag marks (claw or finger-like tracks) leading into the water. The party then decides to return to the Black Hill Caves beneath Torch, swimming down with water breathing cast by Ulfred. They discover the caves have been massively overtaken by russet mold since their last visit, with multiple corpses (including Torch city guards) killed and burst open by veggie pygmies. Olbryn uses acid splash to painstakingly burn paths through the mold. They also find enormous, rapidly-grown trees that burst through stone in an unnaturally short time, matching the damage pattern at Gavrilo's destroyed house. The party navigates through the caves to the original dungeon complex, finding the disease-cleansing room deactivated and an underground jungle/forest with veggie pygmies. Combat ensues as the party fights through multiple waves of veggie pygmies with their rubbery tentacle-fingers.

## Key Events

- The party staked out Iadenveigh's lake overnight using Eldrin as bait to lure whatever has been dragging people into the water. Nothing appeared after a full night of watching.
  - Characters: Eldrin, Maginnis, Nomkath, Tokala, Ulfred
  - Location: Iadenveigh - Lakeside

- Nomkath tracked drag marks near the lake shore that looked like long claw or fingertip marks being dragged from shore into the water, no more than a week old.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Eldrin
  - Location: Iadenveigh - Lakeside

- Tokala rolled on his "wheel of divinity" and connected to Brigh, gaining access to Brigh's domain spells for the day. The party joked about Tokala becoming a "second cleric of Brigh" alongside Ulfred.
  - Characters: Tokala, Ulfred
  - Location: Iadenveigh

- The party swam down into the Black Hill Caves using communal water breathing cast by Ulfred. Tokala and Olbryn struggled with swim checks and had to be helped.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Tokala, Nomkath, Olbryn, Eldrin, Maginnis
  - Location: Black Hill Caves - Underwater Entrance

- The party discovered russet mold had massively spread throughout the caves since their last visit, covering corpses of Torch city guards and a horse. Multiple bodies had burst open from veggie pygmy growth.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Eldrin, Olbryn
  - Location: Black Hill Caves - Tunnels

- Olbryn used acid splash (a cantrip) to slowly burn paths through the russet mold, clearing about one square per round. This was slow but effective and kept the party safe from spores.
  - Characters: Olbryn
  - Location: Black Hill Caves - Tunnels

- The party discovered enormous trees that had burst through solid stone in an impossibly short time (days or weeks), matching the damage pattern at Gavrilo's destroyed house. The trees were a hybrid of multiple local species.
  - Characters: Eldrin, Nomkath, Ulfred
  - Location: Black Hill Caves - Near Gavrilo's House Foundation

- Eldrin used an unseen servant to scout ahead, triggering russet mold spores and examining corpses safely from a distance.
  - Characters: Eldrin
  - Location: Black Hill Caves - Tunnels

- The party navigated back through the original dungeon complex, passing through rooms they remembered from their first adventure, finding a disease-cleansing room deactivated and more mold growth.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Eldrin, Maginnis, Tokala, Ulfred, Olbryn
  - Location: Black Hill Caves - Dungeon Complex

- The party entered an underground jungle area with actual sunlight filtering through from above and encountered veggie pygmies. Nomkath spotted one first, and Maginnis took the first shot with the sniper rifle, alerting the rest.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Maginnis, Eldrin, Tokala, Ulfred, Olbryn
  - Location: Black Hill Caves - Underground Forest

- Olbryn cast haste on the entire party at the start of the veggie pygmy fight, granting extra attacks and movement.
  - Characters: Olbryn
  - Location: Black Hill Caves - Underground Forest

- The party fought multiple waves of veggie pygmies with elongating rubbery tentacle-fingers that attempted to grapple. Tokala and Ulfred destroyed them easily in melee while Maginnis and Eldrin provided ranged fire.
  - Characters: Tokala, Ulfred, Maginnis, Eldrin, Nomkath, Olbryn
  - Location: Black Hill Caves - Underground Forest

- Veggie pygmies (multiple waves): Small creatures with 15-foot reach tentacle-finger attacks that attempt to grapple (CMB vs CMD). Individually weak but numerous. Tokala's high CMD deflected grapple attempts easily. Ulfred obliterated them with his hammer. Maginnis used arc pistol and sniper rifle at range. Some explode into spore clouds on death.
