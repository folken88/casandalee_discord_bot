# PG03 - Iron Gods Session 04 - Rescue & Gambling

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=20E5VSgZ-lI)

## Summary

The party successfully revived Khonnir Baine using a combination of electricity and healing magic, though he remains physically scarred and weakened. They celebrated at the Silver Disk Casino (earning modest profits), gathered intelligence about town troubles and a potential Technic League spy, then returned to Black Hill Caves to explore further. They encountered moss-creature guardians in a chemistry lab, survived a deadly spore infection, and discovered masterwork equipment including a magical hammer and ring of swimming.
