## Summary

The party descended into a flooded technological facility where they encountered mutant flesh creatures created by a malfunctioning android foundry. After a grueling combat against regenerating blob-like entities (killed with fire), they discovered surveillance footage showing a wizard named 'Master Zoud' and an invisible ally 'Sahasha' visiting the facility seven years ago. They then faced multiple combat encounters with deformed androids and a highly skilled android woman resembling the town soup maker (Alaris), who proved to be an extraordinarily dangerous magus/rogue/arcane trickster. After a brutal multi-round battle involving repeated near-death situations, Nomkath finally defeated her with a well-placed sneak attack.
