## Summary

The party encounters and defeats a tentacled aberration in the Choking Tower, looting significant magical items including enchanted bracelets and cloaks. They then face the ghost of Furkas Zoud in a prolonged magical duel, ultimately defeating him. A mysterious kasatha druid named Croc arrives claiming to follow the party and seek someone from his past. Meanwhile, Eldrin is killed by the aberration and the party must decide whether to resurrect him.
