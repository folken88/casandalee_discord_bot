## Summary

The party explored deeper into Black Hill Caves beyond the habitat wall, discovering a massive metal structure and a hidden underground forest filled with four-armed skeletons. After intense combat against the undead, a mysterious tentacled creature, and dangerous floating robots, they retreated to Torch exhausted and low on resources. They discovered evidence that Val's father may have ventured into this area, and plan to return better equipped after leveling up.
