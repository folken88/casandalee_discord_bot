# Iron Gods Session 04 - Rescue Gambling Moss Man Woman TV

**Date:** 2020-07-23 | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=20E5VSgZ-lI) | **Duration:** 3h 7m

## Summary

The party heals [[Khonnir Baine]] using Val's electricity and [[Joram]]'s Remove Disease to purge the nanites from his body. That evening, the party receives invitations to [[Garmen Ulreth]]'s casino, the Silver Disk Hall, where they gamble and gather rumors about a Technic League spy in Torch and widespread headaches among the townsfolk. The next morning, [[Dolga]] rewards the party with masterwork gear. Khonnir provides intel about a second sealed entrance to the caves, the purple-haired woman, and a broken elevator that needs a steel safety rod. The party returns to the Black Hill Caves science deck, where they fight vegepygmies (moss creatures) and a tougher vegepygmy wielding a magical hammer of [[Brigh]]. Gavrilo and Tokala are infected by a spore cloud explosion but cure themselves by sprinting to the artificial sunlight in the habitat area. The session ends with the party discovering a chemistry lab with a locked safe full of nanite ooze and potions.

## Key Events

### Khonnir Baine Healed
- **Characters:** [[Khonnir Baine]], [[Val]], [[Joram]], [[Morgana]], [[Gavrilo]]
- **Location:** [[Torch]] - Foundry Tavern area
- **Timeline:** Correlates to shortly after 4716.07.13 (Khonnir rescue)
- Val uses her electricity (Shocking Grasp) on Khonnir while Joram channels positive energy simultaneously. Joram then casts Remove Disease, pulling nanites out of Khonnir's body as glinting particles. Khonnir briefly wakes, speaks to Val, then falls asleep. Joram casts multiple Restorations but Khonnir's stats are severely reduced (3s and 4s). He will carry scars for life.

### Gambling at Silver Disk Hall
- **Characters:** [[Gavrilo]], [[Nomkath]], [[Tokala]], [[Maginnis]], [[Skathe]], [[Garmen Ulreth]]
- **Location:** [[Torch]] - [[Silver Disk Hall]]
- **Timeline:** Same evening as Khonnir's healing
- The party receives red envelopes with silver disk seals inviting them to the Silver Disk Hall with 100gp credit each. Garmen Ulreth greets them personally. Tokala bets all 100gp on black at the roulette table and loses everything. Gavrilo plays poker successfully, using Sense Motive to read opponents, and wins about 60gp profit. Nomkath wins about 20gp profit. Maginnis wins about 25gp. Skathe cashes out immediately, unwilling to gamble.

### Rumors Gathered at Casino
- **Characters:** [[Gavrilo]], [[Maginnis]], [[Skathe]]
- **Location:** [[Silver Disk Hall]]
- Gavrilo hears there is at least one Technic League spy in Torch. Many townsfolk are having headaches and ringing ears, starting around when the torch went out. Skathe hears someone whisper that Garmen Ulreth's "little gang" must be involved in the town's problems. Maginnis hears the gremlins have not been around lately, compared to "rats leaving a sinking ship."

### Dolga's Rewards
- **Characters:** [[Dolga]], party
- **Location:** [[Torch]] - Dolga's Foundry
- **Timeline:** Next morning
- Dolga distributes masterwork gear: masterwork barrel (with Brigh symbol, from a schematic provided by Sanvil) and more bullets for Maginnis; masterwork handaxes with tribal symbols (bravery and wisdom) for Tokala; masterwork thieves' tools with two masterwork daggers for Nomkath; masterwork agile breastplate for Skathe; masterwork longbow for Morgana; masterwork light crossbow for Gavrilo. She also provides a scroll of Raise Dead (worth 6000gp) that the town council had set aside for Khonnir. Dolga reminds them the 4000gp bounty for relighting the torch is still available.

### Khonnir's Intel
- **Characters:** [[Khonnir Baine]], [[Gavrilo]], [[Nomkath]], [[Morgana]], [[Val]]
- **Location:** [[Torch]]
- Khonnir reveals: the torch is technology of "unimaginable power," not magical or natural; the town pays a tax to the Technic League to keep them away, and without torch revenue they cannot make payment within a month; the purple-haired woman entered through a second eastern entrance that she sealed behind her; he had a brown card and a black card but neither opened the sealed entrance; the elevator needs a two-foot steel safety rod to function; there is a chemistry lab on the same floor he was tortured on. Khonnir speaks to Morgana in Androffan (revealing she is an android). He gives Gavrilo and Morgana each a scroll of Mirror Image from his hidden stash.

### Vegepygmy Combat
- **Characters:** [[Gavrilo]], [[Skathe]], [[Nomkath]], [[Tokala]], [[Maginnis]], [[Morgana]]
- **Location:** [[Black Hill Caves]] - Science Deck
- The party returns to the caves. The skulk and gremlin areas are vacated. Gavrilo opens a door revealing a moss-covered room with three small vegepygmies armed with spears. A fourth, much stronger vegepygmy with multi-colored dreadlocks and a glowing silver hammer of Brigh emerges from an adjacent door. The party kills the three regular vegepygmies. The strong one (dubbed "Beefy McVegetable") is defeated largely by Gavrilo's bomb and Nomkath's daggers. Nomkath uses a feint to land a sneak attack. Gavrilo and Nomkath grab the belt and potions off the boss just before his body explodes in a spore cloud. Both fail their Fort saves (back-to-back natural 1s) and take Constitution damage as mold begins growing from their mouths, nostrils, and eyes. Gavrilo uses Knowledge Nature to determine sunlight kills the mold, and they sprint to the habitat area's artificial sunlight to cure themselves.

### Chemistry Lab Discovery
- **Characters:** [[Gavrilo]], [[Morgana]]
- **Location:** [[Black Hill Caves]] - Science Deck, Chemistry Lab
- The party discovers a chemistry lab with superior masterwork-level alchemy tools (+5 to Craft Alchemy). A locked safe with an Androffan readout lists its contents: nanite ooze (black), nanite ooze (blue), potion of Air Bubble, potion of Grease, nanite canister, and potion of Protection from Evil. The safe requires defeating a security system to open.
