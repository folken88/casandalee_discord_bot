## Summary

The party, traveling through the Scar of the Spider valley, was ambushed at night by giant wasps. Kroktah ended the encounter decisively by casting Control Winds, creating a windstorm that blasted the wasps away from the camp and flattened the surrounding forest. The party then tracked a mysterious, highly skilled woodsman northwest through increasingly dense forest. The tracks ended at a low cliff where the party was ambushed by three aberrant tentacle monsters disguised as trees. These creatures grappled and drained charisma through blood-sucking filaments. Nomkath was nearly killed before Kroktah saved him by casting Gaseous Form on him. Tokala and Ulfred destroyed the creatures with the chainsaw and hammer while Maginnis used the inferno pistol to devastating effect. Olbryn contributed lightning bolts and haste. After combat, Ulfred cast Restoration on both Tokala and Nomkath to undo the charisma drain.
