# Iron Gods - Underground Space Cave Jungle Adventure (Session 2021-05-20)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fiwCvyObw54)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The party investigates a mysterious creature luring victims to a lakeside location in an unnamed town. After staking out the area at night with no results, they return to the Black Hill Caves system to explore and clear russet mold infections. They discover evidence of recent catastrophic changes: mutated trees that grew unnaturally fast, numerous corpses infected with the mold disease, and signs of major structural damage. Upon reaching the underground forest habitat and the facility beyond, they encounter and defeat multiple veggie pygmy swarms and a large vine-cat creature, though the extent of the changes and their cause remain unclear.
