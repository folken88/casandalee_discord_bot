# Iron Gods - Two Steps A Head

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=yu7L1splrog)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The party finishes harvesting the juvie terask, but Casandalee detects a Unity surveillance drone 5,000 feet above them broadcasting military-grade encrypted signals. A warden robot fires an artillery shell at the terask carcass, and the party scatters. Olbryn teleports Mr Brow onto the drone via greater teleport, and Olbryn disintegrates it with a massive 136-damage hit, destroying it. The warden fires on its own drone's position, hitting Olbryn and Mr Brow for 102 damage each. Meanwhile, Ulfred's teleport ring misfires badly, sending him and Akradenn 62 miles off course and 3,000 feet in the air before they teleport back. Olbryn catches the falling Mr Brow with a wall of force, then casts fly on him. The party retreats: Olbryn teleports most of the group back to Garton's lab in the palace; Nomkath and Casandalee escape in gaseous form in the truck with Meyanda. They fight off pursuit probe robots with the chain gun. Back in Starfall, Nomkath delivers 100+ pounds of terask meat to Chef Gorgo in disguise, bluffing past guards. The party plans the healburger timeline: Chef Gorgo needs spells cast on the meat the next morning, then cooks in the afternoon. They discuss timing the Dark Seers assassination relative to the banquet, contact Mockery via radio (he agrees to help), and rest. Casandalee manipulates the Dark Seers' dreams overnight to prevent prophetic visions of the attack. The party then assaults the Dark Seers at dawn: Nomkath and Mockery silently assassinate four sleeping crones in their bedrooms, Akradenn opens the chapel door, and the remaining two are killed by chain lightning (from Olbryn) and Mr Brow's explode head spell (his first successful use of it).
