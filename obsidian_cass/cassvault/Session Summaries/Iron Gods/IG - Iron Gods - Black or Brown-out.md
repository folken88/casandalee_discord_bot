# Iron Gods - Black or Brown-out

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=k3-S1RU32vc)

## Summary

Continuation of the assault on the Technic League facility (likely Krastus's Rust-Risen production site). The session is almost entirely combat. The party fights numerous Rust-Risen (undead cyborgs with blind sense and ranged firearms) and a powerful enemy wizard with heavy buffs including invisibility, displacement, mirror image, and disintegrate. Blackout takes a sniper position behind a toilet and uses Casandalee's blind sense relay through the Status spell to bypass the wizard's displacement and mirror image. Luna cuts through multiple enemies with devastating melee attacks. Olbryn casts chain lightning to clear groups of Rust-Risen. Nomkath uses Destroy Robot on a Thrasher-type gearsman. Kroktah uses Control Winds to create windstorm conditions. The wizard fires disintegrate at multiple party members and summons Black Tentacles. Olbryn is knocked unconscious (at -12 HP) but stabilizes. The wizard attempts to flee south but is pursued through hurricane-force winds.
