## Summary

The party spends downtime in Starfall City gathering intelligence on Technic League captains and exploring the city's underworld. Nomkath disguises himself as a guard to scout for captain information while others gamble and visit the Scrap Hole junkyard in the north. They discover the Technic League captain Krastus has taken materials from Scrapwall to the Scrap Hole, and uncover information about several TL captains. The session concludes with the party preparing to infiltrate an underground Night Market in the city's catacombs beneath a collapsed building, where they plan to attend an illegal auction.
