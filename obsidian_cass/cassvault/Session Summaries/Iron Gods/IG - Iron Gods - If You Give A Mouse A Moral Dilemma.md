# Iron Gods - If You Give A Mouse A Moral Dilemma

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=cH-wOnJ6Xq8)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Tokala
**Absent:** Hans (Maginnis)

## Summary

Session focused heavily on the moral conflict over the Technic League brain-in-a-jar. [[Kroktah]] secretly attempts to destroy the brain while [[Tokala]] sleeps, first trying to dispel magic on it from the air, then wild-shaping into a mouse to sneak into [[Tokala]]'s backpack. She casts warp metal to damage the brain's container but [[Tokala]] hears her casting and discovers the mouse. [[Isuma]] watches the entire sequence without intervening. The confrontation leads to an extended party debate about the morality of keeping the brain captive versus its tactical value. [[Isuma]] delivers a powerful speech about Kasatha freedom culture and her own enslavement by the Dominion. The party also discusses recharging their vehicle's power supply, plans to deliver Dominion corpses to [[Binox]], and travels east across the Scar of the Spider in their newly acquired vehicle (the "Patty Wagon"), with [[Isuma]] driving.
