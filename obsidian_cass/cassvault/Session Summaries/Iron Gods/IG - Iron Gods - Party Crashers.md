# Iron Gods - Party Crashers

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=ThWfwymcpbA)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The party plans their infiltration of the Palace of Fallen Stars to confront the Black Sovereign. Casandalee reports all gearsmen have retreated to Silver Mount. The party debates whether to kill or cure the Black Sovereign, ultimately leaning toward the "healburger" plan (smuggling a heal spell into food). They discuss the palace layout using Casandalee's holographic projection from Luna's memories and Mockery's knowledge. The party has no diplomacy skills. They identify the drug entrance (a window into Garton's former lab on the second floor), buy supplies from Mockery at 50% discount (wands of invisibility, see invisibility, disguise other), get tattoo upgrades from Toala, and plan the approach: Nomkath and one other will stealth/fly to the palace, break in through the Technic League window, while the rest approach disguised as barbarians. Brent (new player) joins the session but his character has not been formally introduced yet. Enrique/Mr Brow is absent.
