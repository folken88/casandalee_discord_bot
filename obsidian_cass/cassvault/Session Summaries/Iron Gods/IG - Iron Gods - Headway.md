# Iron Gods - Headway

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=K-bfzsLFAAk)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

Enrique's new character, Mr Brow (Augustus Teabrow), a halfling psychic with an abnormally large head, approaches the party at their hospital safe house in Starfall. He uses telepathic bond to contact Nomkath, evades Isuma's surveillance with an audiovisual hallucination decoy, and is escorted inside. Mr Brow reveals he has been watching the party from afar throughout their campaign in Starfall, admires their work against the Technic League, and wants to join them. Casandalee acts as intermediary, protecting the party's minds from Mr Brow's passive mind-reading while evaluating his sincerity. Nomkath vouches for him after telepathic communication. Meyanda shares her life history with Mr Brow via castnet. Mr Brow reveals he has noticed all the gearsmen leaving the city and heading toward Silver Mount. Akradenn recognizes Mr Brow as the rarest type of spellcaster: a psychic. The party accepts him. Meanwhile, the party is getting tattoos from Toala and leveling up.
