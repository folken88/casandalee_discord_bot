# Iron Gods - Tactical Remembrance

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=4x2NDOfB8dA)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Olbryn, Kroktah, Luna, Tokala

## Summary

The party launches the assault on the Technic League compound. Tokala (Tracy guest-playing) arrives with his orc army, Dinvaya, Isuma, Meyanda, and the nomad truck equipped with the scrap horn to create a massive distraction on the southeast wall. The allied Technic League captains (Zerth, Acradenn, Elias) have convinced two-thirds of Technic League forces citywide to stand down. Under cover of Kroktah's dust storm over the entire city, the party infiltrates over the north wall. Nomkath silently assassinates a tech sniper on the wall, while Kroktah as an air elemental blasts a second sniper off the wall; Tokala is telekinetically charged by Olbryn and chainsaws the fallen sniper. The party discovers the slave pits are completely empty. They begin clearing the compound surface, heading for the gate controls and the central stairway to go underground after Ozmyn.
