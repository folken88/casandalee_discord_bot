# Iron Gods - A Bugs Life

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=lZlZ_YiSJ04)

**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The session concludes the battle against Osman, head of the Technic League. Cockatrice/Kroktah escapes the Roomba she was trapped in, but is ultimately killed by Osman's blade dash (hit twice for approximately 360 total damage). Ulfred is also killed by Osman but his contingency ring activates, teleporting his body via shadow steed to Beva on the surface, where she casts Breath of Life to revive him at 0 HP. Nomkath lands a critical flanking sneak attack on Osman, debuffing his AC. Casandalee dispels Osman's stoneskin. Luna confirms a critical hit on Osman thanks to Nomkath's AC debuff, slamming him against the wall and knocking him out of flight. Nomkath delivers the killing blow, jamming a dagger into the back of Osman's skull and whispering a message to Unity in Androffan. Casandalee urgently connects to Osman's data port to download information from his dying brain, then warns the party that Unity has targeted the compound. Unity bombards the Technic League compound with napalm from hunter-killer drones, forcing everyone underground. The party levels to 15. Loot includes Osman's null blade, his Technic League badge (plus 4 protection/natural armor/resistance), hex grid breastplate (+10 touch AC), and a nanite hypo gun with Breath of Life capability.
