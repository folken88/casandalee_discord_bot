## Summary

The party meets with Chef Gorgo at the Palace of Fallen Stars to plan how to cure the Black Sovereign of his addiction to Numerian fluids using a disguised Heal spell in a meal. They learn the Sovereign's favorite rare meat is from a Juvie Teras (a colossal beast similar to but smaller than a true Tarrasque), requiring them to hunt this creature in the northern waste near Starfall. The session primarily consists of the party gathering intelligence from Nomkath's catfolk relatives about the beast's location, researching its capabilities, and developing multiple combat and infiltration strategies.
