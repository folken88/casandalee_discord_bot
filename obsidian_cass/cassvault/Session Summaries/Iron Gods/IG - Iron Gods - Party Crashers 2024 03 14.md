## Summary

The party infiltrates the Palace of the Black Sovereign in Starfall after dismantling the Technic League's power structure. They scout Garon's laboratory, bypass magical wards, and successfully bluff their way through multiple palace guards using disguises. The session ends with the party hidden in a cyborg barracks, debating their approach to confronting the Black Sovereign—whether to challenge him to a duel, ambush him with a healing spell to cure his Numerian fluid addiction, or pursue assassination.
