# Iron Gods - Fireside Chat

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=lyEcjPIkDDo)
**Transcript Quality:** good
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Takala, Maginnis

## Summary

A roleplay-heavy session with no combat. The party prepared to leave the Scar of the Spider and return to Torch. They hooked [[Casandalee]] to a high-capacity battery and she revealed extensive lore about [[Unity]], the Divinity starship, the Technic League, and her own history as Unity's first cleric who rebelled. The party debated what to do with the AI Core and discussed [[Kroktah]]'s existential crisis about technology. During the eight-day drive home in the Nomad, [[Nomkath]] interrogated the brain-in-a-jar prisoner (the Technic League wizard Theorus) using Casandalee's VR environment, with [[Kroktah]] disguised as the traitor Hearsick. They extracted intelligence about Starfall, the Technic League's structure, the Black Sovereign's status, possible entry points to the city, safe houses, and learned that Kasathian pods may exist within Technic League territory.

## Key Events

- Party connected [[Casandalee]] to a high-capacity battery (100 charges) with instructions to ration it over 10 days. Casandalee immediately attempted to start the Nomad truck and deployed scanning equipment. [[Nomkath]] enabled a "child lock" to prevent her from controlling the vehicle.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Casandalee
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - campsite

- Casandalee scanned the valley and confirmed no Unity-aligned tech remained. She detected Androffin signals in the eastern reaches of the Scar of the Spider, which the party identified as [[Binox]].
  - Characters: Casandalee, Nomkath
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - campsite

- Casandalee confirmed that the tracking transmitters on [[Takala]] and [[Ulfred]]'s tech implants were still turned off.
  - Characters: Casandalee, Nomkath, Takala, Ulfred
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - campsite

- Casandalee revealed major backstory: The Androffins built the starship Divinity and traveled the stars, kidnapping specimens including Kasathians ([[Asuma]] and [[Kroktah]]'s ancestors, approximately 9,000 years ago). The Divinity was attacked by the Dominion, its central AI was briefly touched by them, and it fled through space to crash on Golarion, becoming Silver Mount. That AI is [[Unity]].
  - Characters: Casandalee, Nomkath, Kroktah, Takala, Ulfred
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - campsite

- Casandalee explained that Unity spent thousands of years underground, concluded the planet should be under its total control, and has been working toward that end. She was Unity's first cleric in her last Android iteration, was called across incredible distance to Silver Mount, met Unity face to face, recognized it as evil, and rebelled. Unity sent her out with an AI Core to spread its influence; she hid it instead and was killed.
  - Characters: Casandalee, Nomkath, Takala, Ulfred
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - campsite

- Casandalee confirmed [[Hellion]] was a copy of Unity placed on an AI Core and sent out, but Hellion also rebelled by starting its own religion rather than spreading Unity's influence.
  - Characters: Casandalee, Nomkath
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - campsite

- Casandalee explained Unity's remaining plan: repair one of Divinity's original shuttles (a spaceship capable of interplanetary travel), construct a housing for itself aboard, and become mobile. The Technic League is likely being used to gather fallen Divinity parts for this purpose.
  - Characters: Casandalee, Nomkath, Takala
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - campsite

- [[Kroktah]] had an existential crisis, questioning her purpose and expressing frustration with the party's reliance on technology. [[Nomkath]] offered her the option to leave. Kroktah became depressed and withdrawn but ultimately stayed.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Nomkath, Ulfred, Takala
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - campsite

- [[Takala]] sent the two rescued Kuratown Orcs to find Kroktah's tribe, with instructions to join them if they wished.
  - Characters: Takala, Kroktah
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - campsite

- [[Asuma]] chose to stay behind in the Scar of the Spider with Peter to finish cleansing the valley of Dominion and Migo remnants. She promised to visit the party in Torch later. She performed a traditional Kasathian farewell to [[Kroktah]] and promised to introduce any Kasathians she found.
  - Characters: Asuma, Kroktah, Nomkath
  - Location: Scar of the Spider

- Party traveled approximately 8 days from the Scar of the Spider to Torch in the Nomad, stopping at Mad Peter's camp (where Kroktah obtained more "deer dung" herbs) and the Lockstar Hotel. They passed the former site of the Choking Tower (now gone) and paid tribute to [[Eldrin]]'s memory.
  - Characters: all PCs
  - Location: Scar of the Spider to Torch road
  - Timeline: ~4716.12.12 to ~4716.12.20

- [[Nomkath]] interrogated the brain-in-a-jar prisoner (Technic League wizard Theorus) using a three-way VR connection: VR helmet, Casandalee's AI Core, and the brain. Casandalee served as host for the virtual environment, creating a cityscape resembling Androffin architecture.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Casandalee, Kroktah, Takala, Ulfred
  - Location: Casandalee's VR environment (during travel)

- [[Kroktah]] disguised herself as the traitor [[Hearsick]] (a tiefling Technic League operative) inside the VR environment using her shapeshifting abilities (disguise check 35 with Nomkath assisting). Nomkath posed as a fellow Technic League member negotiating with Theorus.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Nomkath
  - Location: Casandalee's VR environment

- Casandalee scanned Theorus's mind, finding no direct evidence of Unity control but noting his burning desire to control technology as a "red flag of a Unity stooge." She pulled surface memories showing him storming out of a meeting, packing, and leading an unauthorized expedition out of Starfall at night.
  - Characters: Casandalee, Nomkath
  - Location: Casandalee's VR environment

- Intelligence extracted from Theorus: Garton is one of the Technic League captains (described as ugly, fat, and a mage). The gearsman robots in Starfall operate semi-independently and cannot be reliably commanded. Ozmyn is head of the Technic League. The Black Sovereign does whatever Ozmyn tells him. "Everyone is everyone's enemy in the Technic League." A Technic League member named Sila found and dissected a Kasathian from a stasis pod. Theorus's memories revealed a way over the city wall using flight devices, and locations of safe houses.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Casandalee
  - Location: Casandalee's VR environment

- [[Kroktah]] remained in the VR environment after interrogation, asking Casandalee to recreate Kasathian people using memories stolen from [[Asuma]] by the Dominion. Casandalee created a full immersive recreation of a Kasathian weather-reading ceremony with Asuma and other Kasathians. Kroktah joined the ceremony and spent extended time among virtual recreations of her people.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Casandalee
  - Location: Casandalee's VR environment
