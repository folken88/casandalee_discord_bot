# Iron Gods - Autonomy Threatened (2022-05-05)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=RuE2f07j7hg)

## Summary

The party is ambushed by advanced female android warriors at their campsite and four members (Alfred, McGinnis, Ulbryn, and Tokala) are captured and taken to a robot facility in a cave system. The remaining free members (Croctaw, Nomkath, and Hirsick) pursue their companions, discovering a Kasatha named Isuma who has been hiding in the caves. Through negotiation with the facility's central AI (Binox), they secure the release of their companions, though Tokala has been surgically enhanced with a data port and reactive armor plating. The party recovers and begins the journey back to their camp.
