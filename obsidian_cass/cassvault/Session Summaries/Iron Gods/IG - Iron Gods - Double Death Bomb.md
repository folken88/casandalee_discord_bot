## Summary

The party, split between two groups, navigated the aftermath of a major earthquake in Starfall caused by their pursuit of a merchant. Nomkath and Tokala led Scrapwall Orcs on a tactical extraction, collapsing tunnels to escape pursuit, while the main party regrouped at a safe house. They analyzed intelligence on Technic League forces, made contact with a mysterious figure called "Blackout" at the Slot Bag casino, and encountered poisoned food that incapacitated Ulfred and Luna.
