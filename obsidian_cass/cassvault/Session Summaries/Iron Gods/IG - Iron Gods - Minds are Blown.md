# Iron Gods - Minds are Blown

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=3kTAwGppFHk)

## Summary

Continuing in Gartone's safe house after killing him last session, the party explores locked rooms and loots Gartone's body. They find a belt of mighty constitution +4, bracers of Grenadier, a cloak of the wild man, a grenade launcher identical to Maginnis's old weapon, a headband of vast intelligence +4, Gartone's enhanced Technic League Captain badge (+2 AC and saves), a technic chain shirt +3, and a set of keys including an ornate gold-plated key that Nomkath determines belongs to something in the Palace. The party opens the magically sealed door and discovers a tentacle-faced creature resembling the Dominion of the Black, pinned to the wall by force fields. The creature attempts to dominate Ulfred but he resists. The party attacks the restrained creature. The party also discovers Gartone's hidden power battery that drains magic items and plans to destroy it by dropping it into the sewers via pass wall.
