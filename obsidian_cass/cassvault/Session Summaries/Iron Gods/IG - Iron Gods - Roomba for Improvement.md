## Summary

The party continues the massive assault on the Starfall Technic League compound. Combat rages across multiple hallways and chambers as the party fights waves of undead barbarians, risen hellcasters, Tech League troopers, and a Tech Witch (level 14 Arcane Trickster). Ulfred's wall of thorns provides critical battlefield control, trapping enemies and forcing a high-level caster to waste turns dispelling it twice. Nomkath repeatedly drops explosives (silx/C4/pipe bombs) into the same area, causing structural damage to the facility. Akradenn decapitates Captain Grin after she is revealed to be the void dragon when her polymorph is dispelled. Osman, the head of the Technic League, enters combat via blade dash, cutting through multiple party members in a single turn with his null blade (a lightsaber-like weapon that hits on touch AC). Cockatrice (Kroktah's wild-shaped cockroach form) is killed by Osman and sucked into a malfunctioning Roomba. Orren teleports Akradenn to safety using dimensional shift. The session ends mid-combat with Osman still fighting.
