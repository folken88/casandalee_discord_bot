## Summary

The party continued their assault into the Dominion hive after dealing with the entrance robot. They pushed deeper into the cave system, encountering groups of intellect devourer-controlled orcs and kasatha in narrow corridors. [[Kroktah]] deployed entangle to bottleneck the enemies in a narrow 10-foot passage. [[Tokala]] used dazzling display with his chainsaw to shake the enemies, and the party set up a defensive formation with melee fighters blocking the corridor and ranged fighters behind them. [[Nomkath]] switched from daggers to the Dominion glazer rifle for ranged combat. [[Maginnis]] confused by Dominion psychic attacks shot [[Olbryn]] in the back with the glazer for 23 fire damage while targeting a brain creature. [[Zuma]] landed devastating critical hits, killing multiple inhabited kasatha and orc hosts. [[Tokala]]'s spiritual weapon proved effective with force damage. The party observed glowing blue power spheres and walls resembling reptilian hide inside the hive -- [[Ulfred]] identified them as some kind of power reactor connected to the facility. The entangle spell and narrow corridors allowed the party to control the flow of combat effectively despite the constant threat of confusion spells from the intellect devourers.
