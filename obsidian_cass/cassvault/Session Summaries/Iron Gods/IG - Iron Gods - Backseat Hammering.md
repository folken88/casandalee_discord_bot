# Iron Gods - Backseat Hammering

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6xDnQ30nhcs)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** [[Ulfred]], [[Nomkath]], [[Kroktah]], [[Olbryn]], [[Tokala]], [[Maginnis]], [[Zuma]]

## Summary

The party prepared to assault the Dominion of the Black's territory in the Scar of the Spider. [[Kroktah]] flew to the edge of Dominion territory as a bird and cast multiple commune with nature spells, learning the locations of two patrol groups of dead-but-animated orcs and kasatha controlled by intellect devourers along the Dominion boundary. The party then drove the six-wheeled Nomad transport to the northern patrol location with [[Maginnis]] driving. They engaged and defeated the northern patrol group, which consisted of several orcs and kasatha piloted by brain creatures (intellect devourers), plus a displacer beast-like Dominion creature. [[Zuma]] provided critical intel about the intellect devourers and proved devastating against the Dominion creature. [[Tokala]] threw [[Ulfred]] into melee using telekinetic charge. [[Olbryn]] killed a fleeing invisible brain creature with magic missiles. The party decided to proceed to the southern patrol group in the same day.
