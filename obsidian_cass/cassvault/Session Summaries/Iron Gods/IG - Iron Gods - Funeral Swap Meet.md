# Iron Gods - Funeral Swap Meet

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=7ZwEfBIAbGQ)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Eldrin, Olbryn, Tokala, Maginnis, Kroktah

## Summary

The party returns to the room where Eldrin was killed by the abyssal creature, finding deeper darkness still filling the space. Nomkath enters invisibly to drag Eldrin's body out and discovers he is dead. The party fights and kills the tentacled abyssal creature, recovering magical reach bracelets, a cloak of resistance +2, and a ring of protection +2. They distribute Eldrin's gear among the party and explore Furkas Xoud's lavish bedroom chambers, finding significant treasure including a book of war prayers, magical slippers, an elven gown, and a staff of lightning bolt that has become a permanent planar conduit for electricity spells. They discover a vacuum-sealed closet containing a desiccated corpse with a Technic League pin. Kroktah (Enrique's new character, a kasatha druid) introduces himself to the party, having followed them into the tower.
