# Iron Gods - Operation Healburger

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=69D-Kmr1O7s)
**Transcript Quality:** good
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The session picks up after the Dark Seers assassination. The party loots the Dark Seers' quarters, discovering a powerful Shadows Refuge armor set and weapons from a Nidal assassin -- including a void vortex bow and a +4 keen shadow burst longsword. Nomkath takes the shadow sword; Olbryn considers selling loot toward his "birthright" tiara. The party hides the bodies, cleans up, and sets a do-not-disturb ruse on the Dark Seers' area. Nomkath disguises himself and delivers terask meat to Chef Gorgo, who plans to make 18 healburgers (four heal spells from Ulfred, two from Meyanda, each multiplied into three burgers). Gorgo explains everyone will get healburgers so the Black Sovereign's portion is undetectable. The party contacts Mockery, who agrees to attend the banquet. They use Zerth's connections to gain access to the banquet as guests. At the feast, the Black Sovereign eats his healburger and has a visible reaction -- standing up confused, looking around the room as if seeing it for the first time in decades. He bellows "YOU" at the guest table. Then Unity attacks: a projectile blasts through the palace floor near Mr Brow and Nomkath, advanced Spartan gearsmen float up through the windows, and swarms of flesh-burrowing insect robots bore into barbarians. The party engages Unity's forces while protecting the Black Sovereign.
