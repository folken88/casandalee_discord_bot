## Summary

The party encounters and recruits Mr. Brow, a powerful halfling psychic who has been secretly observing them in Starfall. After proving his trustworthiness and sharing memories of his activities (including assassinating Technic League Captain Sterlender), Mr. Brow joins the party via Casandalee's mental network. The group discusses their next major objective: dealing with the Black Sovereign, who is currently drugged and controlled by the Technic League. They debate whether to kill him, heal him of his addiction, or replace him with a new leader, ultimately leaning toward attempting to cure his addiction and restore him to leadership.
