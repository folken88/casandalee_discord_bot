**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=qRlTifTcpSw)

## Summary

The party, disguised as barbarians, infiltrates the Palace of Fallen Stars in Starfall to locate the Black Sovereign's drug stash and recruit the legendary chef Gorgo Ranshead to help execute Operation Heal Burger—a plan to cure the Black Sovereign of his addiction to Numerian fluids by imbuing a meal with a powerful healing spell. The session featured extensive use of Bluff checks, discovery of a high-level Nadali assassin's corpse and the artifact sword Umbrell Scourge, and recruitment of a skilled chef who speaks the Catfolk language.

  - Characters: Nomkath, Akradenn, Ulfred, Luna, Olbryn, Mr Brow

  - Timeline: 4717.03.14

  - Characters: Akradenn, Ulfred, Nomkath
