# Iron Gods - You Shall Not Passwall

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=ukwDTsvqUDA)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** Nomkath, Olbryn, Ulfred, Kroktah, Blackout, Luna, Casandalee

## Summary

The party assaults Technic League Captain Abernathy's house in the Sovereign's Watch neighborhood of Starfall. After planning an infiltration via wind walk through the sewer pipes, Kroktah uses her decomposition ability to destroy the metal plumbing beneath the house, blasting open an entry point through the kitchen floor. The party fights through multiple gearsmen and an augmented gearsman. Abernathy, an invisible wizard with heavy defensive buffs, attempts to flee using a Passwall spell to tunnel through his own roof. Luna strips his Greater Invisibility with a critical hit, and Kroktah summons windstorm-force winds that tear apart the building and surrounding area. Nomkath pursues via Air Walk while Blackout takes a sniper shot up through the Passwall tunnel. Luna flying-charges Abernathy and kills him in midair. Casandalee warns Nomkath she must detonate the gearsman she had secretly taken control of before Unity detects her, and the party plants Rust-Risen remains to frame the attack.
