# Iron Gods - Choking Tower

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=q6x4_x7tEUA)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Tokala, Nomkath, Maginnis, Ulfred, Olbryn, Eldrin, Kroktah

## Summary

The party enters the Choking Tower for the first time. Ulfred repairs a broken wheel-key using make whole, allowing the party to unlock the massive front door. Inside, the tower is immaculately clean (maintained by roombas) but heavily trapped with fire jets. Nomkath scouts ahead and encounters the ghost of Furkas Xoud, the tower's former owner, who attacks with cloudkill and lightning bolts. A brutal fight ensues in near-zero visibility. After defeating the ghost (temporarily), the party explores the first and second floors, finding a library with useful scrolls, a trapped reading room with sleep gas, and multiple fire traps on the stairs. They also encounter and destroy repair robots that reset traps and re-lock doors. Kroktah (Enrique's new character) is introduced to the party, having come to the Choking Tower seeking Casandalee's android body. The party discovers Casandalee's corpse and discusses what to do about Eldrin's body and the ghost's inevitable return.

## Combat Encounters

- Ghost of Furkas Xoud: Used cloudkill, lightning bolt, and black tentacles. Partially incorporeal, extremely difficult to target through the cloudkill fog. Required a DC 30 perception to spot through the combined fog/incorporeality. Maginnis used Gavrilo's noxious veil to see through the fog and directed fire. Nomkath scored critical hits. Ulfred used his wraith blade and spiritual weapon. The ghost regenerates after being destroyed (possibly within hours).
- Repair robot: Strange construct with mismatched legs held together by translucent energy. Had an arm that could shut doors, lock locks, and rearm traps. Destroyed by Nomkath and Tokala.
- Additional repair robots/roombas: Multiple small cleaning robots stomped by Tokala throughout.
