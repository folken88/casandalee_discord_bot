# A Chainsaw Joins the Party

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=dvxygR-l2q0)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The party spends a week in Torch recovering from recent battles, where Tokala's greatsword is reforged by Dinvaya into a powerful adamantine chainsaw called Yagadar Mordin ("Stairway to Heaven"). After testing the weapon and departing Torch, they travel north through Numeria's frontier, stopping at the Lockstar Hotel where they witness hill giants peacefully trading for supplies. They then arrive at Iadenveigh (Yadenveigh), where the town guard captain Redfang hires them to investigate livestock disappearances on the northern farms, suspecting either an unusual predator or the mysterious Annihilator robot mentioned in earlier encounters.

## Key Events

- Tokala's greatsword is reforged by Dinvaya (priest of Brigh) and Meyanda into a unique adamantine chainsaw weapon. The weapon combines Tokala's shattered heirloom sword with Kulgara's old chainsaw, reinforced with adamantine plating and Dwarven runes. It features dual power system (battery + organic fuel), blood shields, and multiple modes including a dramatic high-RPM mode with arena entrance sounds.
  - Characters: Tokala, Dinvaya, Meyanda
  - Location: Torch - Dolga's Foundry
  - Timeline: 4717.03.14 (post-Black Sovereign cure)
- The party departs Torch after approximately one week of rest and resupply. Reginald (their butler) has provisioned the wagon with trail rations, water, tents, and other supplies. Three charged batteries for Tokala's chainsaw are prepared by Meyanda using the torch's power reactor.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna, Maginnis, Reginald
  - Location: Torch
- The party encounters five hill giants at the Lockstar Hotel who are collecting payment/supplies in what appears to be a protection deal with the dwarf proprietor Trucknoll. The giants are peaceful and simply gathering goods they've apparently negotiated for, leaving after loading their satchels.
  - Characters: Maginnis, Nomkath, Tokala, Hill Giants (5), Trucknoll
  - Location: Lockstar Hotel - frontier location between Torch and Yadenveigh
- The party arrives at Yadenveigh and meets Redfang, the town's captain of the guard and legendary huntsman. After identifying themselves, they are invited to meet with Redfang in the high home and offered beer (Brow Brew - local mushroom/grape fermented beverage). Redfang hires them to investigate livestock disappearances on the northern farms.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Tokala, Mcginnis, Eldrin, Ulfred, Alfred, Redfang
  - Location: Yadenveigh - High Home
- The party investigates bear tracks and claw marks near the town wall. They find unusual fur samples with brown, gray-white, and blue-tinted hairs mixed together—unusual coloring for a normal bear. They plan to visit a farmer named Jevick whose farm is outside the northeast gate.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Mcginnis, Redfang
  - Location: Yadenveigh - Northern forests near town wall
- The party discovers a gearsman (human-sized robot) impaled on a sharpened log outside Yadenveigh's north gate, with multiple spears driven through its chest. It has apparently been there for a very long time and serves as a warning/monument.
  - Characters: Tokala, Nomkath, Mcginnis
  - Location: Yadenveigh - North gate area
