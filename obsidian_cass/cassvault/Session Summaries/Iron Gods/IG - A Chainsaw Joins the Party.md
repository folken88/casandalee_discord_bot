## Summary

The party spends a week in Torch after acquiring Tokala's new chainsaw weapon (Yagadar Morden). They depart Torch heading south toward Iadenveigh, stop at Lockstar Hotel where they encounter hill giants conducting a monthly 'transaction' with the innkeeper, then arrive in Yadenvay to meet with Sheriff Redfang about livestock disappearances on northern farms.
