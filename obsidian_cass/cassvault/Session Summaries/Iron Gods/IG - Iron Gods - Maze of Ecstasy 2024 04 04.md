## Summary

The party conducts a combat simulation with Casandalee to test strategies against the Black Sovereign before attempting to challenge him in his palace. The simulation reveals the Sovereign's formidable defenses, including shield link with an Oracle, high-level spellcasters (particularly the witch Beski), and significant damage reduction.
