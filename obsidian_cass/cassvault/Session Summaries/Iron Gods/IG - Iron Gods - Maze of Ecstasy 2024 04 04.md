## Summary

The party conducted a combat simulation against the Black Sovereign and his forces within the Palace of Fallen Stars to assess their chances in a direct confrontation. The simulation revealed the fight would be extremely difficult, with the Black Sovereign having significant damage reduction, shield link abilities, and powerful allies including a high-level witch (Beski). The party discussed alternative approaches including the 'Heal Burger' plan to cure the Black Sovereign of his Numerian fluid addiction, and concluded that plan may be preferable to direct combat.
