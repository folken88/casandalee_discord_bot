# Iron Gods - Dust in the Wind

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=WYy5Bh3gb6k)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Kroktah, Olbryn, Tokala, Luna, Casandalee

## Summary

The party departed Torch and traveled to Starfall via Kroktah's wind walk spell, experiencing an aerial view of Numeria for the first time. They observed Silver Mount and the massive pollution-choked city of Starfall from the air before descending into the kill box district. The party materialized in an alley, applied disguises, and began exploring the lawless slums. Kroktah communed with a local Earth Daughters druid/shaman who could sense her true nature. The party found a safe house in an abandoned block of the kill box and began establishing it as their base of operations.

## Key Events

- The party left the truck at Torch for repairs and departed via wind walk. Casandalee's soul was tangible enough to be affected by the spell, surprising Kroktah since it only works on living things with souls.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Casandalee
  - Location: Torch, airborne

- During the flight, the party saw Scrapwall, the destroyed Choking Tower site, and Silver Mount for the first time from the air. The massive structure appeared unnatural, possibly an ancient burial mound covering something enormous.
  - Characters: All
  - Location: Airborne over Numeria
  - Timeline: 4717.02.04 - IG Party journeys to Starfall in disguise

- Nomkath recognized from tribal lore that Silver Mount may be a dead god, a dead monster, or an evil device buried by ancient barbarians. The Technic League's growth over the last hundred years correlated with increasing pollution and terror.
  - Characters: Nomkath
  - Location: Airborne, approaching Starfall

- The party entered Starfall from the southwest over the kill box, wearing brown cloaks to blend with the smog. Tokala was spotted by civilians who called him an "orc wizard" but he intimidated them into silence using crude gestures despite being in cloud form.
  - Characters: Tokala
  - Location: Kill box, Starfall

- The party materialized in an alley in the kill box, applied disguises, and began exploring. Luna used disguise self via her helmet. Olbryn disguised as a peasant.
  - Characters: Luna, Olbryn, Nomkath
  - Location: Kill box, Starfall

- Kroktah, in cockroach form, encountered a local Earth Daughters druid/shaman who could sense her true druidic nature through a communion similar to speaking with a tree. The shaman seemed to trust Kroktah because of their shared connection to nature.
  - Characters: Kroktah
  - Location: Kill box, Starfall

- The party established an emergency escape plan: Olbryn can teleport himself plus four medium creatures. Kroktah and Nomkath volunteered to stay behind and escape independently if needed.
  - Characters: Olbryn, Kroktah, Nomkath
  - Location: Kill box, Starfall

- The party found an abandoned block in the kill box to use as a safe house base of operations.
  - Characters: All
  - Location: Kill box, Starfall
