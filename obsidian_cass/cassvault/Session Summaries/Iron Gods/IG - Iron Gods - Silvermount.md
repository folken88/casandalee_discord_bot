# Iron Gods - Silvermount

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=RQmM1OHc1-E)
**Transcript Quality:** good
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The session opens with the aftermath of Operation Healburger. The Black Sovereign Kevoth-Kul is fully cured, dead sober, and in peak health -- looking like he is 35 despite being 80-90 years old due to the residual effects of Numerian fluids. He quickly defeats all challengers to his rule, ripping heads off and kicking skulls down stairs. The Technic League is effectively dissolved: Elias has been absorbed as the army's wizard, Zerth has disappeared, Nalkii will never be found, and Akradenn remains with the party. The Black Sovereign makes Tokala, Mr Brow, Ulfred, Luna, Nomkath, and Olbryn War Chiefs of Numeria. The party selects items from the sovereign's vault (Mr Brow takes the Beak of the Void Raven helmet; Akradenn takes the Goremouth Warbell gauntlet). Olbryn has purchased the Matriarch's Tiara. The party then turns its attention to infiltrating Silver Mount to confront Unity. Casandalee briefs the group using the truck's holographic table, showing that the crashed ship Divinity is 8 miles long with most of it buried underground. She explains Unity is a ship navigator AI that has become divine through its followers, and that eliminating or converting Unity's followers within Silver Mount could weaken its divine power. The party decides to enter via Entryway 40, a lightly guarded portal 400 feet up a sheer cliff face. The plan involves disabling guard robots without destroying them so Casandalee can spoof their signals, using anti-tech fields to disrupt communications, and looping the video feeds. The session ends with the party preparing to enter Silver Mount.
