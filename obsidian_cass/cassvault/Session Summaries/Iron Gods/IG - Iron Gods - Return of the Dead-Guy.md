# Iron Gods - Return of the Dead-Guy

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=v1buc6W4a-k)
**Transcript Quality:** fair
**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Olbryn, Kroktah, Maginnis, Takala, Asuma

## Summary

The party resurrected [[Olbryn]] after his death in the previous session, then explored deeper into the Scar of the Spider facility. They discovered a Dominion portal to outer space and a plasma-like energy barrier blocking a hallway. After defeating shotgun spiders guarding the portal, [[Kroktah]] burrowed beneath the energy barrier and found Androffin tech cables connected to Dominion machinery. The party drained the power source by charging batteries, shutting down the fire barrier. They then entered a Dominion mind-space chamber containing stolen memories, where their minds were pulled into a psychic realm. There, the party fought doppelganger versions of themselves in a prolonged PvP-style combat encounter that was not fully resolved by session end.

## Key Events

- [[Ulfred]] used a Scroll of Raise Dead to resurrect [[Olbryn]], who had died in the previous session at negative 34 hit points. Olbryn heard singing (identified as Cuha/Kua) while being pulled back by silvery chains.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Olbryn
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - nutrient room

- Party discovered a Dominion portal (technology-generated, appearing as a white donut of energy showing stars and space through it) and a plasma energy barrier (described as fire burning inside stars) blocking a northern hallway. Shotgun spiders were dormant near the portal machinery.
  - Characters: Takala, Nomkath, Kroktah
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - portal chamber and fire hallway

- Party defeated four shotgun spiders guarding the portal area. [[Asuma]] and [[Maginnis]] provided ranged fire support. [[Ulfred]] contracted a disease (two CON drain) from a spider attack.
  - Characters: Takala, Nomkath, Ulfred, Maginnis, Asuma, Olbryn
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - portal chamber

- [[Kroktah]] investigated the portal by flying through it as a bird but nothing happened - it appeared to only function for Dominion creatures. [[Nomkath]] used a severed Dominion claw on the portal console, triggering a psychic push and the distant sound of a woman screaming in pain (potentially Casandalee).
  - Characters: Kroktah, Nomkath
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - portal chamber

- [[Kroktah]] burrowed beneath the plasma barrier in badger form, discovering the floor was made of living flesh (dead/dying scales and tissue with nerve endings and blood vessels). She found Androffin tech cables connected to Dominion machinery beneath the floor and dragged cables back to the party.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Nomkath
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - beneath fire hallway

- [[Nomkath]] connected a VR helmet to the exposed cable and discovered a battery system running two devices, with 18 hours of power remaining. The party drained the power by charging multiple batteries (high-capacity batteries, shield battery, weapon batteries), eventually shutting down the plasma barrier after approximately 10 hours of drain.
  - Characters: Nomkath, Maginnis, Takala
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - fire hallway

- Party looted the Dominion torture creature from a previous fight, identifying: a +3 spike chain, +2 mithril chainmail, amulet of natural armor +2, ring of protection +2, and cloak of resistance +2. All items had inward-facing spikes.
  - Characters: Olbryn, Takala
  - Location: Scar of the Spider

- Party entered a chamber with purple-glowing walls covered in constantly shifting writing (in Aklo, which no one could read), tiny glowing creatures, and a pool of dark water. The room contained Dominion stolen memories from millions of beings.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Takala, Ulfred, Nomkath, Olbryn, Maginnis, Asuma
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - memory chamber

- [[Kroktah]] failed a Will save while scouting the memory chamber in bird form and was mentally captured. The remaining party entered to rescue her, with several members (Nomkath, Olbryn, Asuma, Maginnis) also failing Will saves and being pulled deeper into the room. [[Takala]] and [[Ulfred]] held them back via rope in a tug-of-war.
  - Characters: Kroktah, Takala, Ulfred, Nomkath, Olbryn, Maginnis, Asuma
  - Location: Scar of the Spider - memory chamber

- The party's minds were pulled into a Dominion psychic realm where they were split into groups and forced to fight doppelganger versions of each other. The doppelgangers updated in real-time, gaining new scars and abilities. Combat was extended PvP-style with the party fighting copies of themselves.
  - Characters: Takala, Ulfred, Nomkath, Olbryn, Kroktah, Maginnis
  - Location: Dominion mind-space

- Session ended mid-combat with the doppelganger fight still in progress. Several fake party members were destroyed (fake Olbryn, fake Ulfred/Takala nearly down) while fake McGinnis remained active.
  - Characters: all present PCs
  - Location: Dominion mind-space
