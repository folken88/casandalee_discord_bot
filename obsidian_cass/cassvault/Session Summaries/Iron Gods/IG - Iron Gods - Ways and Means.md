# Iron Gods - Ways and Means

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=0D4ze1BpskM)

## Summary

The party resurrected Nomkath using a sacrifice to Brigh, then located and explored Ferris's safe house warehouse in Starfall, discovering equipment, magical items, and evidence of his involvement with the Dominion of the Black. They met with the Mockery (Drowmuck), who provided intelligence about an upcoming illegal night market auction where Technic League Captain "The Shade" plans to meet smuggler Baron Drund, presenting an assassination opportunity.
