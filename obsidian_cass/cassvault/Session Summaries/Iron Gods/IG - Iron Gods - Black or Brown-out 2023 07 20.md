## Summary

The party engaged in a massive combat encounter against a powerful wizard (Krastus) commanding Rust-Risen robots and other constructs in a facility. The fight lasted approximately eight rounds and involved significant tactical positioning, with Olbryn casting chain lightning and dispel magic, Nomkath removing key buffs from the wizard, and Luna pursuing the wizard. After a desperate final exchange where Nomkath dispelled the wizard's Bear's Endurance buff, Luna delivered the killing blow. The party looted the facility and escaped via sewers and Pass Wall, with Croktah casting Control Weather to cover their tracks with a 30-hour thunderstorm.
