## Summary

The party reunites in Starfall at a military hospital compound after Maginnis returns from his travels. They prepare for 'Operation Healburger'—a plan to hunt a massive Juvi-Tarrasque-like creature to obtain meat for a special healing burger that will cure the Black Sovereign of his numerian fluid addiction. After traveling via Meyanda's truck into the northern wastes, they locate and engage the colossal beast in a brutal combat encounter, ultimately killing it after it burrows underground and uses a devastating void-energy breath weapon.
