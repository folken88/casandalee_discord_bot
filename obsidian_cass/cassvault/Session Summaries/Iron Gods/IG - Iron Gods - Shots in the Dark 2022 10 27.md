## Summary

The party encounters a dragon housing an intellect devourer brain that initially poses as an ally against the Dominion. After tense negotiations and failed diplomacy, combat erupts. The party defeats the dragon-brain hybrid after a brutal multi-round battle, with support from an unseen benevolent presence they suspect may be Casandalee. The party discovers a data pad similar to one Casandalee might inhabit.
