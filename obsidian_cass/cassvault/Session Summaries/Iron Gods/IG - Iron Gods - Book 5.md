## Summary

The party is mid-combat in underground tunnels beneath Starfall, fighting a Technic League Captain and her bodyguards alongside Scrapwall Orc allies and their Earth Witches. After killing the Technic League Captain, the party splits — Tokala leads the Scrapwall Orcs to pursue a fleeing merchant ("the soft man") while Nomkath leads the rest of the party to escape through the sewers. The Orcs tunnel up through the earth into a market district, causing massive collateral damage including collapsing buildings, and engage the merchant's remaining bodyguards and Starfall lawmen in a chaotic street battle. Tokala uses a chainsaw to devastating effect, dealing 93 damage in a single hit.
