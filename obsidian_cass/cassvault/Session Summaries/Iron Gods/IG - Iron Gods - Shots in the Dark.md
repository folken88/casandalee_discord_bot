# Iron Gods - Shots in the Dark

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=rmqEYxPPD2A)
**Transcript Quality:** moderate
**Characters Present:** [[Ulfred]], [[Nomkath]], [[Kroktah]], [[Olbryn]], [[Tokala]], [[Maginnis]], [[Zuma]]

## Summary

Josh was absent for this session, so [[Olbryn]] was run as an NPC. The party continued fighting deeper into the Dominion hive. [[Maginnis]] started the session still confused, and [[Kroktah]] was also confused from the previous session's combat. [[Olbryn]] attempted to dispel the confusion on [[Kroktah]] and [[Maginnis]] but failed both caster level checks. Dominion creatures attacked from the east through an iron wall barrier, with shotgun blasts and a nathulgu (large Dominion creature) breaking through. [[Kroktah]], while confused, cast air geyser on [[Olbryn]], slamming him into the 10-foot ceiling and dropping him prone. [[Nomkath]] experienced a powerful psychic event: the Dominion nearly took full control of his mind, but a warm, guiding presence helped him find his way back to his body, filling him with joy and relief. This presence (implied to be connected to Casandalee or another benevolent force) granted him an immediate action. [[Ulfred]] charged down a hallway using his rocket boots and fly spell, activating Eagle Soul for damage immunity on impact. [[Tokala]]'s spiritual weapon scored a critical hit on a nathulgu, destroying it with Dolga's spiritual great sword. A nathulgu cast a massive lightning bolt down the hallway hitting [[Nomkath]], [[Maginnis]], [[Olbryn]], and [[Kroktah]]. [[Zuma]], while confused, acted normally and advanced to close range to fire on touch AC, decimating enemies with her icicle shots.
