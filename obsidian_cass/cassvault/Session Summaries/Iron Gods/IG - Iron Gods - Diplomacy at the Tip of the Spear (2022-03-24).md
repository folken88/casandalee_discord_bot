# Iron Gods - Diplomacy at the Tip of the Spear (2022-03-24)

**Campaign:** Iron Gods | **Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=iwDb-bfEhFs)

**Characters Present:** Ulfred, Nomkath, Mr Brow, Olbryn, Akradenn, Luna

## Summary

The party tracks a mysterious individual through the Scar of the Spider valley and discovers Peter, a drug-addled hermit druid living in a cave. They purchase various alchemical substances and learn about local inhabitants including androids and a Kasatha with a rifle. The session ends with Croctaw having an absurd encounter with a hostile alpha chipmunk, whom she kills before attempting to interrogate other chipmunks about the old druid's condition.

Party loots equipment and Alfred performs surgical extraction of cybernetic components.
  - Characters: Ulfred, Nomkath, Akradenn

  - Characters: Nomkath, Tokala, Akradenn, Olbryn, Ulfred, Luna
  - Location: Peter's cave in southern valley

  - Location: Peter's cave
