# Iron Gods Campaign Journal - Session Summary
## Fireside Chat, 4222.04.26

The party made the critical decision to depart Silver Mount and return to Torch with their most valuable acquisition: Casandalee, an artificial intelligence whose consciousness now resides in a portable AI Core device. After successfully recovering the device from the Dominion of the Black Lair, the adventurers prepared an electric carriage powered by the Nomad power cell and multiple high-capacity batteries for the journey. Casandalee was connected to a dedicated high-capacity battery with enough charge to sustain her for approximately ten days—a strict power budget that would require careful rationing throughout their travels.

Before departing, the party engaged in a profound conversation with Casandalee about her nature and origins. She revealed that she is a copy of the original Casandalee, who had fled from Unity's soldiers after stealing and hiding the AI Core device. The original perished in her escape, leaving this copy as the sole surviving version of Casandalee's consciousness. The party also confirmed the death of Hellion, a rogue AI they had eliminated, and discussed the troubling reality that Hellion had created a copy of itself that betrayed it and fled—a dark mirror of Casandalee's own situation. These revelations sparked heated debate among the party members about Casandalee's nature as a god-like machine entity and whether she should be trusted, with some members questioning whether she should even be allowed to survive.

The journey presented immediate logistical challenges. The party discovered rogue Unity Tech operatives, including Binox and androids, in the eastern reaches of the Scar of the Spider, forcing them to verify that captured enemies' transmitters were disabled to prevent tracking. They also carried a troubling trophy: a brain preserved in a jar belonging to a Technic League operative, which they debated keeping for interrogation or releasing. Additionally, the party prepared to send two freed orcs to Ulfred's tribe with instructions to locate them, potentially adding allies to their cause.

During the encampment, Casandalee demonstrated unexpected abilities when she sensed nature magic and plant life communicating in the area, suggesting her consciousness could interact with the natural world in ways the party had not anticipated. The party also discussed the whereabouts of Hearsick, a lost companion who is a type-link, with some members expressing a desire to confront him about his absence. As they prepared for the eight-day journey to Torch, tensions simmered regarding Casandalee's role in the group and the moral implications of harboring an artificial god-like entity whose ultimate loyalties remained uncertain.
