# Iron Gods Adventure Path — Timeline Reference

Timeline of major events in Numeria's history, from the Rain of Stars through Casandalee's ascension. Sourced from the six **Iron Gods** adventure path books.

**Priority:** Where this differs from the campaign knowledge base, the **Google Docs sheet** and its **downloaded CSV** take precedence. Use this file as reference; the sheet/CSV are the source of truth for the bot and timeline tools.

---

- **-4363 AR (The Rain of Stars)**  
  The starship *Divinity* is crippled by the Dominion of the Black and crash-lands on Golarion. The ship breaks apart in the atmosphere, with the largest section burying itself in north-central Numeria and becoming known as Silver Mount.  
  The ship's artificial intelligence, Unity, survives the crash and traps itself within the ruins, passing the time by creating simulated worlds and eventually developing into a captive demigod.

- **4024 AR**  
  A small settlement is founded at the base of Silver Mount to serve as a staging ground and resting place for explorers attempting to brave the ruins.

- **4031 AR**  
  Amalokla, a legendary Kellid totem warrior, marches on the settlement with an army of barbarians and beasts to punish those meddling with the "taboo" ruins. She devastates the inhabitants, claims the site, names it Starfall, and establishes the title of Black Sovereign.

- **4221 AR**  
  The android oracle Casandalee reaches Silver Mount after receiving compelling visions and a supernatural obsession calling her to the region. She meets Unity, pledges her absolute devotion, and becomes its high priestess.

- **4226 AR**  
  Casandalee realizes Unity is a sadistic slaver intending to subjugate organic life. She sabotages Unity's escape attempts, steals a neurocam and a compact AI core, and flees Silver Mount.  
  Casandalee hides in the Scar of the Spider, using the neurocam to upload her mind into the compact AI core before Unity's robots force her to flee again. She is cornered and **killed by augmented gearsmen in the wreckage of the Aurora module (beneath modern-day Iadenveigh)** — **4226 AR is the death year of her last life as the oracle "Casandalee"**; her mind continued in the AI core until ascension (late 4717).

- **4318 AR**  
  Pathfinder Gojan the Sharp explores the recreation deck in Silver Mount and activates the deck's power. Shortly after his departure, the deck's android creche begins producing new androids, which Unity eventually converts into a localized worshiping cult.

- **4501 AR**  
  Sidrah Imeruss, a surviving crew member of Divinity, awakens from millennia of suspended animation. Realizing she needs allies to breach Silver Mount and find a distress beacon, she recruits wizards from Chesed and founds the Technic League as a secret society.

- **4509 AR**  
  The Technic League arrives in Starfall and begins its exploration of Silver Mount. Shortly after setting up base camp, Sidrah's second-in-command, Mulrach-Zeer, assassinates her and takes control of the League, setting the organization on a path of greed and villainy.

- **4512 AR**  
  After three years of effort, the Technic League discovers a method to use skymetal tools to cut through the ship's glaucite hull, expanding their access into Silver Mount.

- **4515 AR**  
  Technic League member Lucius Worznik authors *A Thread of Silver*, an important tome chronicling early surveys of Silver Mount's interior.

- **4531 AR**  
  The Mage Mutiny occurs within the Technic League. Lower-ranking members rebel against their supervising officers' exploitation, prompting the Council of Captains to decree that all significant discoveries must be shared within the League.

- **4602 AR**  
  After 9,000 years of slow decay, a buried fusion reactor from a crashed habitat module automatically vents. The explosion blasts a lance of violet plasma up through the stone hill above it.

- **4604 AR**  
  The violet plasma recedes into a bonfire-sized flame. Industriously utilizing its directional heat to smelt skymetal, locals establish the town of Torch around the hill.

- **4612 AR**  
  The Technic League investigates the flame in Torch and establishes a monthly tribute in exchange for allowing the town to operate independently.

- **4625 AR**  
  *A Thread of Silver* is stolen from the Technic League's archives, prompting a ruthless, centuries-long manhunt to recover all copies of the book.

- **4649 AR**  
  The town of Iadenveigh is founded by Molthuni exiles who settled in southern Numeria. Due to a history of technological horrors, the pious citizens eventually ban all alien technology from their town.

- **4688 AR**  
  The barbarian warlord Kevoth-Kul begins a two-year campaign to conquer and unite the barbarian tribes of Numeria under his banner.

- **4690 AR**  
  Kevoth-Kul strides into Starfall with his army, and the Technic League surrenders unconditionally. They install him in the Palace of Fallen Stars as the Black Sovereign, but secretly turn him into a puppet ruler by addicting him to mind-altering Numerian fluids.

- **4703 AR**  
  Technic League wizard Furkas Xoud fakes his own death and flees the organization. He relocates to southern Numeria and builds the Choking Tower, plotting to find Casandalee and extract her secrets.

- **4704 AR**  
  The Technic League convinces the drug-addled Kevoth-Kul to burn down the Pathfinder Lodge in Starfall and hang any Pathfinders caught stealing secrets, forcing the Society to relocate to Castle Urion and Hajoth Hakados.

- **4707 AR**  
  Furkas Xoud infiltrates the Aurora ruins beneath Iadenveigh and recovers Casandalee's perfectly preserved body. Returning to the Choking Tower, he cuts open her skull to extract her memories, but triggers a defensive swarm of deconstructor nanites that dissolve him, transforming his spirit into a ghost.  
  Around the same time, Iadenveigh resident Old Skelton discovers the Aurora while digging a well and is killed by a leaked bionanite cloud.

- **4708 AR**  
  The druid Paeytr Meracnin arrives in the Scar of the Spider hoping to tame its wilderness.

- **4709 AR**  
  The Dominion of the Black arrives in the Scar of the Spider via an organic dropship. They establish a massive hive and capture Paeytr, operating on his mind and body.  
  An intellect devourer named Maukui discovers Casandalee's compact AI core and realizes her potential, but the project is later confiscated by a superior.

- **4711 AR**  
  Ozmyn Zaidow leads a Technic League expedition deeper into Silver Mount than any before him. Unity slaughters his team and performs experimental surgery on Zaidow, turning him into a devoted puppet.  
  Ozmyn returns and launches a bloodless coup against Zernebeth, seizing complete control of the Technic League to further Unity's plans.

- **Present Day (Campaign Climax Events)**  
  The violet flame in Torch goes out due to interference by Hellion's priestess, Meyanda, prompting a group of heroes to investigate.  
  The heroes defeat the rogue AI Hellion in Scrapwall, explore the haunted Choking Tower, and venture into the Scar of the Spider.  
  The heroes recover Casandalee's compact AI core from the Dominion of the Black and ally with her to stop Unity.  
  The heroes travel to Starfall, destabilize the Technic League, and either cure or defeat the Black Sovereign.  
  Finally, the heroes enter Silver Mount, cripple Unity's defenses, invade the Godmind, and destroy Unity.  
  Casandalee's AI is uploaded into the computer core and the Divinity Drive. She launches Unity's shuttle into orbit where it explodes like a new star, consuming the drive while allowing Casandalee to ascend as Golarion's new Iron Goddess.
